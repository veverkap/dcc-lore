# Parasitic Infestation

# [[Parasitic Infestation]]
The collective worms/parasites that infest victims (system-announces "You have been infested with a parasite!"), read memories, and attempt mind control. They speak as a collective and physically inhabit hosts like [[dungeon crawler carl]] and the circus performers.
