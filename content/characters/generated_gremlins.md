# [[Generated gremlins]]
Small NPC mobs swarming the truck and garage area; some were created or manipulated via [[emergency gremlin scroll]]. They interact with the team (throw a wrench, hand a note) and provide comic and chaotic elements to the scene.

## Additional Observations
# Generated gremlins
Mass-produced gremlin mobs hired or used to work on the truck and act as distractions; some were given or ate [[Emergency Gremlin scrolls]] and are involved in the invoice prank and the warm note delivery.
