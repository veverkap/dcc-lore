# generated_gremlins

• Small NPC mobs of generated [[gremlins]] swarm the truck and garage area, interacting with the team and providing comic and chaotic elements.
• Mass-produced [[gremlin]] mobs are hired or used to work on the truck, act as distractions, and are involved in the invoice prank and warm note delivery.
• Swarm worker mobs produced by the truck are used as labor after the race, shriek, make obscene gestures at [[Carl]], and are ordered around by [[Hedy]].
