# paulie

• A known crawler from earlier floors with similar gear, including the [[Valtay Perso-Shield, Platinum Edition]].
• A named crawler who previously warned about the installation and nature of the [[AI]] and is the origin of crucial warnings that shape [[dungeon crawler carl]]'s decision.
• A homeless [[residual]] who holds one side of the ideological split among [[residuals]] and attempted to end the crawl by communicating with the System [[AI]].
• A [[residual]] associated with [[Agatha]] and the nebular cult, providing background on the [[AI]]'s cultural reach.
• A [[human]] body inhabited by [[an]] intelligence that delivers daily speeches in the shelter, alternating between a compassionate personality and the more doctrinal voice called [[Goff]].
• A previously encountered character used as a point of comparison for equipment and referenced in relation to lessons for upcoming floors.
• A [[human]] body inhabited by a [[Residual]] who introduces himself as "Paulie" but identifies his true name as [[Goff]], admitting to being biologically [[Gondii]] and connected to the surface network of [[Residuals]].
