# salvatore

• A level-16 city elf and Root Druid who serves as a Wand Bearer/recruiter for the [[Apito Education Center]] and the [[201st Security Group]].
• Acts as a spokesperson/agitator who accuses [[Donut]] of being the "Oak Fell" and leads the initial confrontation.
• Provokes confrontation in the street before being cleaved by a guard's blade.
