# bianca_del_ciao

• [[Hellspawn]] familiar and pet of [[Miriam Dom]], transformed from a goat into a demonic mount with black flames and wings, used to pull a cart carrying [[Carl]] and company.
• Named pet of [[Prepotente]], used as a visual comparison for the goat-like sponsor [[plenty]].
• A Level 32 [[Hellspawn]] Familiar, physically massive, emanating black flames, with wings, and highly dangerous, pulling a cart carrying [[dungeon crawler carl]] and companions.
• Associated historically with [[Prepotente]], used as a visual reference point for how [[plenty]] looked during its brief appearance.
