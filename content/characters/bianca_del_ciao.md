# Bianca Del Ciao
Hellspawn familiar and pet of [[Miriam Dom]]. Formerly a goat, transformed by enhanced pet-biscuits into a demonic, growing mount (described as size of a draft horse at level 32) with black flames and wings; used to pull the cart carrying Carl and company.
