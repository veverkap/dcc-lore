# orren_the_liaison

* Liaison who temporarily held the [[Gate of the Feral Gods]] after [[dungeon crawler carl]] gave it up on the sixth floor to prevent misuse.
* Hooded Syndicate liaison who conducts meetings over [[dungeon crawler carl]]'s possession of the [[Gate of the Feral Gods]], represents Syndicate interests, and enforces Dungeon rules.
* Liaison present during contract negotiations with [[Borant]], skeptical about ad hoc license exceptions, and interacts with [[Quasar]] over legal clauses.
* Liaison who tried to give a warning to [[dungeon crawler carl]] about sponsor manipulation and other dangers, part of the chapter's background intelligence.
* Liaison who informed [[dungeon crawler carl]] that [[an]] incident involving [[Harbinger]] and [[Maggie My]] had been "dealt with", cited as background authority on liaison matters.
* Liaison and negotiator appearing on the broadcast with [[Cascadia]] to explain the system [[AI]] takeover and the negotiated settlement, identified as a former manager figure (Desperado Club).
* Liaison who summons [[dungeon crawler carl]] to the [[Liaison's Office]], holds meetings with the [[Syndicate]] and other parties, uses a playable ball (the [[Orren's Ball]]) to create a [[Zero Zone]], and mediates questions about the [[Illegal Non-[[Valtay]] Implant]] and the [[Failsafe]].
* Workaholic NPC/administrator figure present in the meeting, negotiates with [[Gary]] about the continued operation of [[The Crawl]], and reacts anxiously to events.
* Character who owns or occupies the office the protagonist appears in after the time-slowed scene, noted as arriving in "[[orren the liaison]]'s office" immediately following the installation event.
* Named participant in the broadcast dialogue who acknowledges that the stampede was a known possibility and defends prior choices in the situation.
* Syndicate Liaison who interrogates [[dungeon crawler carl]] and [[Donut]] in [[an]] office at the [[desperado club]], claims to be [[an]] independent consultant working with the [[Syndicate]] government and the [[Controlling AI]], investigates conflicts between kua- tin and [[AI]] reports, and reveals the punitive [[Third-Party Teleport Ban]].
* Liaison present in the interview room who enforces meeting rules, claims office authority, and threatens the presence of [[hunters]] in the nearby dance floor.
* Fishbowl-headed liaison present on the current floor who briefed [[dungeon crawler carl]] and [[Donut]] about upcoming changes, aware of internal liaison factions, and tries to discreetly warn [[crawlers]] about procedural and political dangers.
* Liaison previously encountered by the group who attempted to warn them about unspecified concerns, referenced by [[dungeon crawler carl]] when considering media appearances.
* Liaison whose now-empty office is in the back area of the top floor, mentioned as someone who advised [[Astrid]] and is connected to the club staff areas.
* Syndicate liaison who teleports and interacts directly with [[dungeon crawler carl]] and legal counsel, pulled [[Carl]] from the memory-floor, negotiated with the [[System AI]], seized the [[Residual]]'s loot, and enforces Syndicate decisions (including withholding the [[Gate of the Feral Gods]]).
* [[Victory]]'s communicator contact who attempts to access diagnostics and reports being locked out by the speaking [[AI]] because he's being "Hunted", mentioned by [[Prime Minister Victory]].
* Referenced as a prior hooded/reaper-like figure used for comparison to [[Mordecai]].
* Used as a point of comparison when [[Mordecai]]'s head creaked; mentioned in passing to describe [[Mordecai]]'s motion/voice.
* Ally communicating directly with [[Carl]] during the judge sequence, instructing [[Carl]] to ask precise questions of the [[Plenty]] leadership about tunneling integrity and the escaped gods.
* Participant in the discussion with [[Carl]] and others, acknowledging the riskiness of [[Carl]]'s actions and that they were aware of the possibility of consequences.
* Crawler trying to message the group but currently blocked from sending messages, asking about [[Carl]]'s class choices.
