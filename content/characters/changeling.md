# Changeling

# Changeling
A shapeshifter category described by [[Mordecai]]: beings that change form when their guildhall is moved, adopting a mob-type appropriate to the current dungeon floor. Mordecai explains he became a [[Changeling]] after floor changes and that his form changes with each relocation.

## Additional Observations
# Changeling
An unnamed changeling referenced as being somewhere on the [[Ninth Floor]]. Carl worries about this individual’s safety due to [[Juice Box]]’s efforts to reach and target their allies.

## Additional Observations
# changeling
One of the three basic shapeshifter archetypes in the dungeon (alongside [[mimic]] and [[doppelganger]]). Noted to be violently illated by contact with a [[shadow mimic]].
