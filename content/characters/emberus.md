# emberus

Here is the consolidated markdown list:

* **Emberus**: Major fire god who smashes popped bubbles and rampages across the surface; currently searching for [[Orthrus]] in this chapter and is credited with massive destruction (burning villages and derricks).
* **Emberus**: God of fire and ash referenced in quest text; believes his son's puppy [[Orthrus]] is inside [[Bubble 18]] and will smite quest failers one-by-one if the pup is harmed or the quest fails.
* **Emberus**: Sun and Ash god (level 250) encountered in the chapter; described as a massive, burning humanoid (and later as a huge floating head) whose presence makes nearby air deadly.
* **Emberus**: God of Sun and Ash who personally recruits [[Carl]] as [[an]] adherent; provides divine boons (e.g., temporary +25% constitution) and issues deity rules and quests, including two major system quests: "Find Out Who Killed My Son" and "Kill [[Hellik]]."
* **Emberus**: Deity worshipped by some [[hunters]] and guards in [[Zockau]]; kills of Emberus worshipers trigger divine penalties such as placing actors into [[Penitence]], doubling tithing, and the risk of a [[Smite]].
* **Emberus**: God worshipped by [[Pater Coal]] and the [[Satyr Jeweler]]; lends the church's name to enchanted items (e.g., the [[Enchanted Pauper's Ring of the Steadfast Emberus]]) and backs the [[Temple of Emberus]].
* **Emberus**: Deity contacted by [[Carl]] via the deity tab; [[Carl]] claims to have proof about Emberus's son's killer and arranges a meeting in "Club [[Scolopendra]]."
* **Emberus**: Deity appearing inside [[Club Scolopendra]] in a [[human]]-sized, furious form; he questions [[Carl]] about the death of his son and emits deity effects (e.g., granting immunity to [[Sheol]]-based attacks while present).
* **Emberus**: Deity who transforms from [[human]] into a massive half-faced monstrosity; targeted and pinned by ranged bolts, becomes briefly vulnerable, then explodes; his fall triggers a global system announcement.
* **Emberus**: God worshipped by [[Quemada]]; has [[an]] associated shrine visible in the room (the [[Emberus shrine]]).
* **Emberus**: God worshipped by [[Carl]] whose quests and threats drive major plot choices; has explicitly threatened [[Carl]] with death if certain tasks (like killing [[Hellik]]) are attempted; central to [[Carl]]'s dilemma about worship and betrayal.
