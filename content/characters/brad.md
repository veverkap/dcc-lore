# brad

• A survivor in the New Queen Anne settlement who resents [[Beatrice]]'s behavior.
• Present during the Syndicate/Raider incursion and considers intervening before [[Lexis]] neutralizes threats and claims custody of [[Beatrice]].
• Lives with [[Beatrice]] in New Queen Anne, regrets not entering the dungeon, and witnesses the Syndicate/producer intervention that results in [[Beatrice]]'s abduction and the shooting of a [[sac]] pirate.
• Mentioned as [[Beatrice]]'s ex-boyfriend and used as a point of comparison by [[Donut]] when describing [[Lazarus-A-Bang-Bang]]'s appearance and demeanor.
