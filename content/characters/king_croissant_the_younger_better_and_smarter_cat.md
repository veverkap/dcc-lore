# king_croissant_the_younger_better_and_smarter_cat

* King Croissant, the Younger, Better, and Smarter Cat: a tiny summoned kitten totem notable for a surprising heroic-singing teleportation ability that removes Alpha [[Carl]] and itself temporarily from the field.
* A tiny summoned kitten totem called "the Younger, Better, and Smarter Cat" that sings and teleports with Alpha [[Carl]].
* A totem card that appeared among the hydra's deck and was referenced as a cat-like entity.
* Timed out before needing to be killed and dissipated when [[Donut]] discarded cards.
