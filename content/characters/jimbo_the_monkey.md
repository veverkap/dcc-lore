# jimbo_the_monkey

• [[Jimbo the monkey]] is referenced in the system announcement mockery following the failed quest about adoption, and is part of the flavor text in the floor's announcements.
• [[Jimbo the monkey]] is also referenced in the system quest failure taunt.
• Jimbo is a pet monkey offered as part of the reward for killing [[Orthrus]] in the world quest "Get [[Orthrus]]".
