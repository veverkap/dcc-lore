# Tigrans
A race/species of occupants of the APV (including [[Dario]] and [[Nico]]) involved in the defense of [[Penelope]] and the confrontation with Carl.

## Additional Observations
# [[Tigrans]]
A pair of fake tiger-people (NPCs) occupying a truck/garage; responsible for killing mercenaries and a gremlin earlier, they become charmed by [[Donut]]'s [[All Eyes on Me]] and are the intended targets for [[Disintegration Trap]]s.
