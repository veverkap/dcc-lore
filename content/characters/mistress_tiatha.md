# mistress_tiatha

* [[Mistress Tiatha]] is a game guide NPC who provides early-game guidance about stairwells for borough and city bosses.
* She is a named authority figure with visibility into game mechanics and events, offering blunt tactical suggestions and behaving like a careless sponsor.
* She is a manager figure for other [[Crawlers]], offering suggestions and being criticized for not being reliably helpful.
* She is a knowledgeable NPC about charm mechanics, providing information about NPC constitution and charisma.
* She is a source of information about the Syndicate's influence on cultures and mundanity in the game world.
* She is a manager for performers, including [[Elle]] McGib, and provides knowledge about NPC charm mechanics.
* She is a non-combatant NPC who appears drunk and vocal about crawl history and failsafes during the announcement.
* She is a magic-user instructing [[Imani]] on battlefield enhancement spells using [[an]] upgraded magic room.
* She is a craftsman/specialist who provided the Capacitor Wire used in [[Carl]]'s plan.
* She is a curator of a pile of recovered jewelry and items, including Petey Motteux's Little Secret.
* She is a knowledgeable NPC who believes the "Negative ROI" effect is actually Separation Anxiety.
* She is a drunken guild member who asserts the procedure "has happened before" and provides a grudging vote of confidence to [[Mordecai]]'s plan.
