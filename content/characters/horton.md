# horton

• A mushroom performer/guitarist who plays "Wonderwall" and is linked to the final act's music, leaving stage after a major explosion.
• A mushroom-liked NPC/performer providing comic interaction and visual flavor during the talent show.
• A mushroom-themed performer playing [[an]] acoustic guitar, performing "Wonderwall" during the ballroom sequence and part of the acts leading up to the final performance.
• A musician-type crawler who died in the fight and is lamented by the party, also referred to as the "Wonderwall guy".
