# Uzzi
[[Mordecai]]’s brother who died as part of the chain of events tied to [[Odette]]’s interference and the rigged game.

## Additional Observations
# Uzzi
A skyfowl warrior and member of Odette's party. Chosen as the involuntary vessel for the [[Dart of Ophiotaurus]]'s summoning, [[Uzzi]] is struck in the chest, contained by the [[Circle of Containment]], and consumed by the forced appearance of the deity [[Dodola]], resulting in the message "Crawler Uzzi has fallen."

## Additional Observations
# [[Uzzi]]
[[Uzzi]] is a proud skyfowl warrior member of the party and the brother of [[Mordecai]] in spirit. He is the intended target of the [[Dart of Ophiotaurus]]; after being struck he is forced into the involuntary summoning that kills the storm god, leading to his death announcement ("Crawler Uzzi has fallen") and enabling the party's escape.
