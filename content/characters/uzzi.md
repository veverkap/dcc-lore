# uzzi

• [[Mordecai]]'s brother who died as part of the chain of events tied to [[Odette]]'s interference and the rigged game.
• [[Skyfowl]] warrior and member of [[Odette]]'s party, chosen as the involuntary vessel for the [[Dart of Ophiotaurus]]'s summoning, struck in the chest, contained by the [[Circle of Containment]], and consumed by the forced appearance of [[Dodola]], resulting in the message "Crawler Uzzi has fallen."
• Brother of [[Mordecai]] in spirit, intended target of the [[Dart of Ophiotaurus]], and forced into the involuntary summoning that kills the storm god, leading to his death announcement ("Crawler Uzzi has fallen") and enabling the party's escape.
• [[Skyfowl]] warrior and party member, proud and protective of his brotherly bond with [[Mordecai]], selected as the vessel for the [[Dart of Ophiotaurus]] and forcibly contained inside the [[Circle of Containment]] to complete the summoning, his involuntary death triggers the puzzle's completion and the appearance of the [[Key Mote]].
