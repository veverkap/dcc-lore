# zarogoth

• A generated mob described as fat, moving shadows leaking oil, mass-produced by war mages via a generator/antenna.
• Also known as Sludge [[Ghouls]] or [[Sausage]] Golems, attacks in swarms.
• Obese black creatures with a painful ray attack and liquid interiors that must leak past 51% to die.
