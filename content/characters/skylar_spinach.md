# skylar_spinach

• [[Skylar Spinach]] is a rare mob captured as a Totem Card, a splitter [[ghommid]] that grows stronger with each iteration, listed as Level 66 in card form.
• It is a [[ghommid]]-splitter totem card that separates into two entities ([[Skylar]] and [[Spinach]]), used by [[Donut]] to attack the puppet reindeer, and demonstrates a split/head-and-tail mechanic.
• The totem provides high dodge and distraction, and when it splits and times out, its parts can become separate temporary cards for the remainder of a battle.
• It is favored by [[Mongo]] and [[Samantha]] interactions and can be used to harry flying reindeer puppets in the simulation.
• The totem can separate into two stronger temporary cards when it times out, acting as a high-dodge distraction in battle.
• It is a fast team card/character mentioned as one of the few in [[Carl]]'s deck capable of high speed.
• The totem was temporarily fused with [[Lazarus]] via the Golden Combo card and is part of the party's roster and referenced as part of previous combinations.
• It is a level-variable splitter [[ghommid]] with multiple afflictions (Iced, Terrifier, etc.) and each primary iteration grows stronger when split and is non-corporeal.
• The totem was used by [[Donut]] and combined with [[Lazarus]], when the combined form dies, it splits into multiple, more powerful versions of [[Skylar]] [[Spinach]].
• It is a party member with sufficient speed to be valuable in the team's attempts to accelerate card play.
• The totem is a named totem/character mentioned in passing as being ignored by others in the group of totems.
