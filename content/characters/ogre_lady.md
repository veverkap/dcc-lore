# ogre_lady

• A named ogre enemy, Ogre Lady, was encountered and killed prior to [[dungeon crawler carl]] waking, possessing the [[Magical Staff Card]].
• Her dying eyes haunt [[dungeon crawler carl]]'s dream.
• The [[Magical Staff Card]] animated dead turkeys into [[zombies]].
