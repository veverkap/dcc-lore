# donita_grace

• Crawler whose last message warns of [[an]] overwhelming [[Krakaren]] infestation at [[Station 24]].
• Practices spells, including the hole spell and [[Clockwork Triplicate]], and scouts corpses for route information.
• Posts a warning about stop 24 ("Holy shit. Holy shit. Guys. Don’t go to stop 24...") and signals the severity of the [[Krakaren]] hatchlings.
• Sends warnings to others, including a deceased-crawler warning about Station 24.
