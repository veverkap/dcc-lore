# mana_genita

• Goddess referenced by the enchantment on [[Princess Donut]]'s tiara, associated with spellcasting and worshippers who may automatically cease hostility toward those wearing items tied to her.
• Described as [[an]] obscure deity associated with spells and worshippers whose automatic hostility can be removed by the [[Enchanted Tiara of Mana Genita]].
• Also described by [[Mordecai]] as [[an]] obscure deity associated with spells, with the tiara removing automatic hostility by worshippers of Mana Genita.
