# commandant_kane

* [[Commandant Kane]] is the great nephew of [[Wynne]] and the commander of the dreadnaught [[Wasteland]].
* He is connected to the Dirigible Gnome family and larger aerial politics.
* He holds one of the pocket watches linked to the [[Gate of the Feral Gods]].
* He is the father of [[Bonnie]] and the commander of the town's defenses.
* He is found dead in [[Bonnie's House]] with a [[Letter from the Glass Wizard]] and a [[pocket watch]].
* He is associated with the daily collateral ritual and the fate of the town.
* He is mentioned as the person who will judge the state of the hostage during negotiations.
* He is the leader of the town (Hump Town) and has a pocket watch that is one of the watches [[Carl]] seeks.
* He is reported dead by his daughter during the assault on the floating house.
* He holds one piece of the three-part artifact for the [[Gate of the Feral Gods]].
* He is a high-level dirigible gnome (Lootable Corpse, Level 55).
