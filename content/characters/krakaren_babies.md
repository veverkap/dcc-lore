# krakaren_babies

• Small [[krakaren]] mobs, referred to as "[[Krakaren]] Babies", are post-stage-three threats found at various [[Station 24]]s.
• They are absent from the tracks during [[Carl]]'s run and the rescue.
• Their presence affects crawler movement and defensive planning on the line.
