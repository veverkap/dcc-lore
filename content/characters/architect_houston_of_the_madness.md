# architect_houston_of_the_madness

* [[Viceroy]] and primary antagonist operating the surgical apparatus (the Grease Monkey)
* Attached to the [[changeling]] via the machine and killed during the melee
* His death triggered system announcements and trap consequences
* A [[Viceroy]] outworlder and leader associated with "the Madness."
* Accused of torturing [[Arief]]'s brother and coordinating outside observation/cheating in the operation that led to the massacre.
* Leader of the [[Madness]] faction who might be provoked if rival leaders attempt to usurp throne control.
* A feared actor referenced by [[King Rust]] who has been using the season's rules to expand his arsenal.
* [[An]] influential attendee whose robes undulate and reacts with visible excitement to [[Juice Box]]'s transformation.
* [[An]] alien scholar-type and self-described [[Viceroy]] of knowledge who provides explanations of trans- dimensional entities and studies access to such creatures.
* [[An]] individual likely operating in the [[Madness castle]]'s operating theater, suspected to be in the surgical theater room.
* A named figure whose operating theater is mentioned as having been cleaned by a robot later picked up by the crew.
* A named NPC whose surgical theater once held a white [[Cleaner Bot]] that [[Carl]] looted; referenced regarding model age and shop availability.
