# quetzalcoatlus

* [[Quetzalcoatlus]]: ghostly, non-corporeal wife/empress figure tied to the Crypt of [[Anser]]; a ghostly antagonist whose resurrection and fleshing are a concern for multiple parties.
* [[Quetzalcoatlus]]: ghost queen tied to local lore; mentioned by Juice Box when [[Carl]] probes [[changeling]] goals and artifacts.
* [[Quetzalcoatlus]]: named beast referenced as a creature that might have been given flesh by a Flesh Mechanic spell, used as part of the explanation for [[Wynne]]'s ghoul-like condition.
* [[Quetzalcoatlus]]: ghostly creature the Principals are hunting for its plant-altering spell; central to [[Henrik]]'s plan to create a food source to stave off genetic defects among Juice Box's people.
* [[Quetzalcoatlus]]: glowing green ghost/pterodactyl boss that appears and launches powerful aural attacks inside the Akula, damaging party HP and disrupting the mission.
* [[Quetzalcoatlus]]: giant beast whose corpse contained a partial map of the sixth floor used by survivors like [[Gwen]] and [[Tran]].
* [[Quetzalcoatlus]]: named beast whose body yielded gate coordinates that were used to map the floor; mentioned as a prior loot source for navigational data.
* [[Quetzalcoatlus]]: mob type referenced as the ghost-boss counterpart seen on a previous floor; used here as a visual comparison for [[Sierra]]'s massive pterosaur form.
* [[Quetzalcoatlus]]: non-corporeal undead guardian/ghost tied to the Crypt of [[Anser]]; survivors seek to resurrect her into flesh; she is described as birdlike and screaming through the tomb.
* [[Quetzalcoatlus]]: ghostly, non-native creature with a unique plant-altering spell; the focal prize of the Principals' hunt because its spell might solve a biological crisis for Juice Box's people.
* [[Quetzalcoatlus]]: named ghost mentioned as having been usurped by [[Psamathe]] in the necropolis lore; part of the supernatural stakes tied to using the Winding Box and opening portals.
* [[Quetzalcoatlus]]: glowing green, pterodactyl-like boss ghost that appears and screams at the party inside the Akula, performing [[an]] aural attack that damages health and causes hull-wide tremors; identified as a major threat.
* [[Quetzalcoatlus]]: deadly flying reptile credited with killing [[Henrik]] and his team; referenced on the loot UI for [[Henrik]]'s corpse ("Killed by a very pissed-off Quetzalcoatlus").
* [[Quetzalcoatlus]]: large boss creature in the lower part of the Necropolis/temple, targeted by the team via the planned lightning electrification of the interior waterways.
* [[Quetzalcoatlus]]: large fallen creature whose corpse contained a partial map of the Sixth Floor; referenced during loot of the subterranean level.
* [[Quetzalcoatlus]]: named beast (corpse) from which gate coordinates were looted on a previous floor; its coordinates helped map the ninth floor and inform travel routes.
* [[Quetzalcoatlus]]: pterodactyl-like mob type referenced as the ghost boss from a prior floor; the chapter's vampiric boss shares the same species archetype but is a flesh/undead variant.
* [[Quetzalcoatlus]]: previously encountered vulture goddess from the fifth floor; referenced for comparison to [[Nekhebit]].
