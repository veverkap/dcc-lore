# Terror the Clown

# Terror the Clown
A level 10 clown-on-stilts mob encountered in the street. Extremely tall, eyeless, with a tooth-filled mouth and oversized butcher knives; described as a former stilt-clown turned bloodthirsty monstrosity. Drops [[Clown Meat]], a [[Big Top Ticket]], [[Gold]], and wearable props (e.g., [[Red Wig]], [[Little Hat]]) and weapons.
