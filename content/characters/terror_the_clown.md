# terror_the_clown

• A level 10 clown-on-stilts mob encountered in the street, extremely tall, eyeless, with a tooth-filled mouth and oversized butcher knives.
• Former stilt-clown turned bloodthirsty monstrosity.
• Drops [[Clown Meat]], [[Big Top Ticket]], [[Gold]], wearable props (e.g., [[Red Wig]], [[Little Hat]]), and weapons.
