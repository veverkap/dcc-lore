# herot

* [[Herot]] is a crawler and cookbook author who wrote the 16th edition of a crawler cookbook and proposed the "Worn Path Method" for interacting with [[NPCs]].
* He is also [[an]] author of influential essays on NPC nature and party composition, and is quoted for advice on surrounding oneself with trustworthy companions.
* His theories are read and critiqued by [[Crawler Ossie]] and referenced by the narrator.
* He confirms the rule that stairwells may be gated but cannot be removed, and is cited for [[an]] essay on crawler survival statistics.
* He is [[an]] in-lore thinker/writer who theorized about NPC awareness and the metaphysics of the dungeon.
* He is [[an]] advisor to [[Dungeon Crawler Carl]] on waking [[NPCs]] and elites, and his counsel influences [[Carl]]'s thinking about system interactions and strategy.
* He is the author of [[an]] essay on the "Worn Path Method" and written ramblings referenced by [[Crawler Ossie]].
* He is a former crawler and author who devised [[an]] exploit with [[Menerva]] to help awakened mobs and [[NPCs]] survive.
* He is a notable figure mentioned by [[Akuma]] and recognized by some characters, associated with seasons and NPC crowds.
* He is a figure trapped inside the [[Pineapple Cabaret]] with limited powers but possessing a shop interface and a standing order behavior.
* He is [[an]] agent inside the [[Pineapple Cabaret]] with access to the dimensional [[Holding Area (Dimensional Holding Area)]] and the ability to pull people out.
* He is [[an]] off-screen individual [[Carl]] asks [[Pontiff]] to deliver a message to as a contingency.
* He is a cookbook author and floor designer trusted by [[Carl]], creator or operator related to the [[Pineapple Cabaret]]/holding procedures and whose choices affect [[NPCs]] like [[Pontiff]].
