# prime_minister_victory

• [[Prime Minister Victory]] watches the broadcast of the [[Ascendency Game Rules]] and the events on the [[Twelfth Floor]].
• She commands staff (including [[orren the liaison]]), responds to reports about the [[Crawlers]] and [[Scolopendra]], and struggles with the [[AI]]'s proclamations and the political fallout from the [[mantis]] and [[Hive Home]].
• She is a high-ranking political contact who speaks to [[dungeon crawler carl]] and confirms the severity of the crisis: rogue [[AI]] actions, destroyed systems, and mobs/gods appearing outside expected zones.
• She is a named political figure present on the broadcast who criticizes [[dungeon crawler carl]]'s recklessness during the unfolding events and participates in the live discussion.
• She orders diagnostics on the [[Grigori]] NPC and requests [[Chaco]] be pulled in for debrief.
• She interacts with her staff including [[Orren]] and [[Leve Billings]].
• She is mentioned as a possible person the team could bring to the [[Eleventh Floor]] as part of the prize.
• She is invoked in the rant of the dungeon [[AI]]/voice, mentioned as part of the entities the [[AI]] curses while asserting dominion over the game.
