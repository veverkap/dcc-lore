# gucci_the_maltese

* [[Imani]]'s small companion, a Maltese [[dog]] named [[Gucci]], was observed being held during the rooftop disturbance.
* [[Gucci]] was also seen being clutched by the giant [[Imani]] avatar in the lobby scene and was present during the exit.
* A white Maltese [[dog]], Gucci, is present in [[Apartment 310]] and does not have X'ed-out eyes like the [[human]] shells.
* Gucci is a [[dog]] belonging to [[Imani]] who barks loudly during the apartment encounter and is mentioned to have sewn eyes.
