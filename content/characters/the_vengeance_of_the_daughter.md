# the_vengeance_of_the_daughter

• [[The Vengeance of the Daughter]]: a thematic arc heading grouping Chapters 16–29 under the label.
• [[An]] in-game quest issued by [[Tsarina Signet]] to assault [[Fort Freedom]] and eliminate the leaders of the [[Naiad Confederacy]], with a reward of advancement to part two and increased willingness of assault survivors to follow the player (+20%).
• A system-issued quest that tasks players to help [[Tsarina Signet]] assault [[Fort Freedom]] and eliminate leaders of the [[Naiad Confederacy]], with a reward of progression to part two and increased willingness (20%) of survivors to follow orders.
• A quest-complete system message marking the end of [[an]] initial assault and granting follow-up benefits (minor party affinity).
• A new quest/System announcement outlining the next objective: help [[Signet]] take revenge on her half-sister [[queen [[imogen]] of the high elves]] and break the [[elves]]' hold on the [[Hunting Grounds]], with major rewards (loot from the [[castle of the high elves]] and rumored protection versus [[Scolopendra]]).
• A program/contract enforced by [[sensation entertainment incorporated]] referenced by [[Zev]], considered [[an]] active program affecting crawler obligations and eligibility for other programs.
• A named example of a secondary program referenced by [[Mordecai]] when explaining the use of control sigils, representing a scripted showrunner-controlled entity or program.
• A system- level quest completion notification that appears at the end of the chapter, marking the resolution of the current objective and providing the meta outcome of the encounter.
