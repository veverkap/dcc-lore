# Magistrate Featherfall
Named magistrate referenced on the text of the [[Gate Pass]] found in [[GumGum]]'s inventory. Mentioned as a black cleric capable of controlling undead in the town — a potentially relevant local authority for the party's investigation.

## Additional Observations
# Magistrate FeatherFall
Named magistrate of the Skyfowl village. The party attempts an audience but finds his office magically locked and learns he "only comes out at night nowadays." Suspected by [[dungeon crawler carl]] to be connected to the murders (Silver quest suspicion), though no direct evidence is confirmed in this chapter.

## Additional Observations
# Magistrate Featherfall
The town magistrate whose corpse is discovered hung and mummifying in a bone dreamcatcher; displayed in-game as "Lootable Corpse. Former Magistrate Featherfall. Dark Cleric. Level 32." Found to have been dead prior to Carl and Donut's explosion; used as a dark fetish by [[Miss Quill]]'s operation.
