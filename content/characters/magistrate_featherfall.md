# magistrate_featherfall

• Named magistrate referenced on the text of the [[Gate Pass]] found in [[GumGum]]'s inventory, capable of controlling undead and potentially relevant to the party's investigation.
• Magistrate of the [[Skyfowl]] village, found to be magically locked in his office and only comes out at night, suspected by [[dungeon crawler carl]] to be connected to the murders.
• Corpse of Magistrate [[Featherfall]] discovered hung and mummifying in a bone dreamcatcher, found to have been dead prior to [[Carl]] and [[Donut]]'s explosion and used as a dark fetish by [[Miss Quill]]'s operation.
• Name appears on the [[Gate Pass]] found in [[GumGum]]'s inventory, mentioned as the local authority whose signature can keep city guards from interfering.
• Reclusive magistrate who only comes out at night, suspected of involvement in local crimes and the presumed authority figure [[Carl]] and [[Donut]] seek.
• Found hung and mummified in a bone hoop in his quarters, revealed to have been dead/ mummified long before [[Carl]]'s blast.
