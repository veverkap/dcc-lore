# princess_formidable_of_the_skull_empire

* A [[princess]] of the [[skull clan]] who sponsored [[Katia]] and is likely connected to the Skull Empire succession.
* Named sponsor for [[Katia]] and likely a member of the Skull Empire's royal line; possibly connected to succession questions involving [[Prince Stalwart]] and [[Prince Maestro]].
* Sent [[an]] emergency worship scroll that led [[Katia]] to worship [[Eileithyia]].
* Sister of [[The Maestro]] and associated with the [[skull clan]].
* Ruler of the [[skull clan]] who previously placed a benefactor box containing the [[Bolt of Ophiotaurus]].
* The rebellious daughter of [[King Rust]] who commandeered a warship and disclosed that the [[skull clan]]'s orbital [[Monitor Station (Venus Orbit)]] contains a fail-safe.
* A level 1 orc, formerly second in line to the throne of the [[skull clan]], who wields [[an]] arm-mounted energy weapon.
* Heir in the [[skull clan]] who would assume control if the new orc king were killed or otherwise removed.
* Youngest sister in the royal line of the [[skull clan]] who sponsored [[Katia]] and whose political position may matter for future floors.
* Benefactor who sent [[an]] emergency silver box to the group, tying into the chapter's political and sponsorship intrigue.
* Influential leader of the [[skull clan]] who advocates triggering the system [[Failsafe]] to prevent perceived [[AI]] corruption.
* Clashes with other council members over the proposed extinction action.
