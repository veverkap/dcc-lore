# jikininki_janitors

* Janitor-ghoul [[NPCs]] that shuffle through train cars with brooms and dustbins, moaning and chattering with their teeth.
* They board at specific stations and leave passengers alone if ignored.
* They are non-aggressive if left alone and move between cars, tied to station spawn patterns.
* They have a chittering, clicking mouth and shuffle through cars.
* [[Jikininki]]: 7-foot-tall, emaciated, suit-clad humanoids with serrated nails, voracious appetites, and conductor-style hats labeled "Janitor". Level 17.
* Found in rail cars, including [[Car 15 - Janitor's Lair]], and drop bones, gold, and small trinkets.
* Tall, emaciated humanoids in tattered suits, often serving as common, voracious threats on the floor.
* Avoid interfering with people who leave them alone and are notable for their constant chattering/clicking mouths.
* Shuffle through the train holding brooms and dustbins, serving as [[scavenger]]/janitor foes in the cars.
