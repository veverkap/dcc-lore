# Jikininki Janitors
Janitor-type ghouls that shuffle through train cars holding brooms and dustbins. Moaning, mouth-chattering NPC/monster types that often board at specific stations and are described as leaving passengers alone if ignored.

## Additional Observations
# Jikininki Janitors
Janitorial ghoul-type NPC monsters that shuffle along the train with continuous chattering teeth ("click, click"). Non-aggressive if left alone; move between cars and are tied to station spawn patterns.
