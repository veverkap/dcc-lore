# jikininki_janitors

• Janitor-ghoul [[NPCs]] that shuffle through train cars with brooms and dustbins, moaning and chattering with their teeth.
• They board at specific stations and leave passengers alone if ignored.
• They are non-aggressive if left alone and move between cars, tied to station spawn patterns.
• They have a chittering, clicking mouth and shuffle through cars.
