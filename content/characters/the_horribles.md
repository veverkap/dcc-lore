# the_horribles

* Crowd/spectator group present in the stands whose members rise together and then vanish; their masks (e.g., the [[Horribles mask]]) litter the field.
* [[Horribles]] are the former mobs/avatars who sit in the bleachers as silent observers and witnesses to the arena slaughter.
