# inle

• Deaf and mute sea-associated god, [[an]] Orisha, partially mad from time in the Nothing.
• Repairing his shrine grants immunity to many health-seeping conditions and +50% Constitution.
• He will not ascend and is destined for the Nothing.
• His image appears at [[Shrine of Inle]].
