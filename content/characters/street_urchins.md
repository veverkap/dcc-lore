# street_urchins

• Minor hostile mobs, known as Street Urchins, are encountered near dead [[crawlers]] on the map during the escape, appearing as red-dot threats in the party's route.
• Street Urchins are also hostile small mobs that surround corpses on the map during the evacuation.
