# the_divider

• A level-17 neighborhood boss monster described as a two-headed sea-creature/whale with massive, bloated body and multiple manhole-sized holes.
• Found dead in the abandoned swimming pool and exploded in the fight when [[dungeon crawler carl]] fell into its corpse, showering allies with gore.
