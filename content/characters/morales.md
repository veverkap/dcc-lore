# Morales
A former crawler elf, Level 60 Plumbing Specialist (healer class) who nonetheless wields a large saber. Tattooed member of the 103rd Recon Legionnaires and The Tenner Wraiths; afflicted by the [[Skanked]] debuff during the fight.
