# chandra

• [[Princess]] Chandra, Esquire, a [[Naga]] lawyer and recent claimant of the title, is the primary schemer in the chapter.
• She is aboard the [[Ventilator]] and has asserted [[Widow's Rights]] to claim [[Carl]] as husband.
• She has arranged conservatorship over [[Donut]]'s earnings.
• She maneuvers against rival groups like the [[Donut Holes]] and legal counsel [[Quasar]].
• She is politically aligned with her uncle, the [[Sultan Protectorate of the New Reformer]], and uses [[Taurin]] [[mercenaries]] to enforce her plans.
