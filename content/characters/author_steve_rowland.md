# author_steve_rowland

• A named, Level 50 Forest Troll dancer onstage at the [[Penis Parade]], described as a buff, green-skinned troll gyrating with a club while NPC patrons throw coins.
• Interacts with [[Samantha]].
• One of the male strippers present in the saferoom; slow-dances with [[Britney]] during the Christmas celebration.
• Briefly injured during his performance on the [[Penis Parade]] stage; his name appears as [[an]] in-game NPC label.
• Grabbed by a crab and pulled through a portal into hell, screaming, in a notable death moment witnessed by the group.
