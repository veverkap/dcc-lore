# hobgoblins

• Named humanoid guards observed dead at Stop 75, likely part of the crashed interdiction teams; used as environmental detail on which stations were overrun.
• Repair-oriented humanoid mobs spotted around Station 75 with explosive tactics; suspected to use the flatbed cars in the yard and to deploy explosives in fights.
• Mob species that use explosives and operate repair shops/flatbed cars in the trainyard area around [[Station 75]]. Responsible for collateral damage in station caverns.
