# brambles

• The [[Brambles]] are [[an]] advancing environmental hazard that overruns towns, forcing population movement, and drives evacuation plans (e.g., casting [[Zerzura]]).
• The [[Brambles]] are a hostile plant/entropic force that chases [[elves]] and crosses battle lines earlier than expected, contributing to a collapsing defensive situation.
• The [[Brambles]] spread across the map, covering it over a few days, and force player relocation and strategic timing to avoid being trapped.
