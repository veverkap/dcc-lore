# Tipid

# Tipid
A Crest crew member and operator aboard the freighter [[Homecoming Queen]] (formerly a garbage freighter). Monitors the crawl, reminisces about past losses (including [[Horatio]]), and helps shepherd fifty thousand hidden troopers for on-boarding. Works closely with [[Rosetta]] and follows advice from [[Doctor Hu]].
