# Parade of Horribles
[[Parade of Horribles]] — named concept from the chapter: literally "a parade featuring a progression of people wearing comic and grotesque costumes" and, figuratively, "a rhetorical device presenting a series of terrible results." In a Dungeon Crawler Carl reading this functions as a named spectacle or mob-like encounter (a procession of grotesque figures/floats) and as a trope describing cascading catastrophes.

# Parade of Horribles
A term defined as both (1) a literal parade featuring grotesque costumes and monster floats, and (2) a rhetorical device listing a sequence of terrible outcomes to argue against an action. In this chapter it is presented primarily as a conceptual rhetorical device. See also [[fearmongering]] and [[catastrophism]].
