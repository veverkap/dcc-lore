# otto

• [[Otto]] is part of the backstory explaining the origin and commercialization of the [[Red Terror Place of Family Adventure]] fauna.
• [[Otto]] is a registered guest of [[scolopendra]] and is associated with the [[Otto Banking Unit]].
• [[Otto]] is referred to as "Otto the slime guy" and was voted by viewers to be the manatee "stand-in" in the arena.
