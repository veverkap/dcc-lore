# magnificent_troy

• [[An]] orc-announcer who co-hosts the live broadcast with [[Kevin]] during the [[Special Event: Boss Battle Stream (Beta Opt-in)]], providing play-by-play and explaining boss mechanics.
• A member of the protagonist's group, often sarcastic and boisterous during combat, who observes and mocks [[Quan Ch]] and participates in the skirmish.
• A field correspondent who refused to descend to the surface, prompting [[Chaco]] to take the assignment.
• [[An]] orc-like on-air commentator for the special event broadcast, providing play-by-play context and explaining mechanics during the streamed boss fight involving [[Lusca]].
• A second on-screen commentator paired with [[Kevin]] whose reactions and banter fill the broadcast during the encounter with [[Lusca]] and the Juvenile Octo-Sharks.
• [[An]] orc announcer (drunk, tuxedoed) partnering with [[Kevin]] to narrate the live Survival Royale Special Event and provide commentary and exposition about the demon eviction and combatants.
• A teammate and combatant who provides running commentary and mockery during the fight, interacting with physical props (e.g., mimicking [[Quan Ch]]).
