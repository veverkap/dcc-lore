# vicente

• A named champion previously defeated by [[dungeon crawler carl]] and company; the [[elves]] reference him as their champion who "blossoms on Her tree" after his death.
• His killing factors into the city [[elves]]' narrative of righteous sacrifice.
• Lootable corpse found in the alley: a city elf (Level 16) killed by [[Donut]] with [[an]] assist by [[dungeon crawler carl]] and credited in the kill log.
• Carried ~1,500 gold and a [[scroll of meat hooks]] scroll; wore a black shirt with a patch for the [[201st Security Group – Magical Ops]].
• A former champion of the [[Apito]] cult whose death is referenced by the city [[elves]] (head blown off by [[dungeon crawler carl]]).
• His defeat is used as a recruitment/propaganda point by the [[201st Security Group]].
