# pierre

• [[Grapple]] porter NPC (Level 25) who runs the [[Yellow Line Reward Room]] and dispenses [[Prize Case]]s and suitcases to [[crawlers]].
• Non-combatant, speaks slowly, and manages the suitcases that players may select as one-per-crawler rewards.
• Dispenses one prize per crawler per line; facilitates the suitcase draw that releases the [[Fire Ants]] trap.
• Operates the [[Yellow Line]] Reward Room and dispenses [[Prize Case]] suitcases.
