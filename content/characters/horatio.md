# horatio

• A long-dead friend of [[Tipid]] recalled by [[Tipid]] as part of his personal history and motivations for participating in the crawl.
• A fellow crawler and friend of [[Tipid]] who died during the dungeon exit sequence when only one person could leave using the [[Key]], leaving behind a wife and child.
• The central emotional point of [[Tipid]]'s guilt.
