# stalwart

• [[An]] actor praised by [[King Rust]] for building a large army this season, mentioned as a force contributor whose efforts increased the threat to [[dungeon crawler carl]]'s side.
• Named warlord of the [[orcs]], still a factional power despite [[King Rust]]'s death, referenced to explain ongoing orc political structure.
• War Chief of the Bone Clan whose death was announced by a system message, with his corpse destroyed beyond collection.
• Deceased orc noble whose death precipitated succession events in the [[skull clan]] and motivated actions around the yacht transaction.
• Younger orc boss killed by the [[war mages]] during [[Faction Wars]], distinguished from [[an]] older orc killed earlier by [[Rosetta]].
• Named crawler killed by [[Akuma]] prior to the events in this chapter, with his death tied to the stolen [[Gate of the Feral Gods]].
• Younger orc boss killed during [[Faction Wars]] by the [[War Mage Rebellion]].
• Character referenced as having been killed by [[Akuma]] during the events surrounding the [[War Mage Rebellion]] and the theft of the [[Gate of the Feral Gods]].
