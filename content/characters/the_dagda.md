# the_dagda

* Deity whose temple connects into [[Club Vanquisher]]; the group moves from the [[Temple of the Dagda]] into the club as part of their breach.
* Deity referenced via the "temple of [[The Dagda]]" that had previously been used to test [[black nimbus]] teleport behavior; part of divine geography and lore.
* Seasonal temple deity (weather and "masculinity" god) whose temple serves as the canonical entrance to [[Club Vanquisher]] on the ninth floor; worship attracts fighter-clerics and seasonal sponsorships.
