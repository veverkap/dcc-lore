# bogdon_ro

• A [[human]] crawler (Legatus) who appears on the top-ten leaderboard.
• A named hunter mentioned in chat as someone others were pursuing.
• Former top player-killer whose head is mounted on [[Vrah]]'s carapace and assists in attacking [[Vrah]] during [[dungeon crawler carl]]'s counterattack.
• A subject of questioning for the Popovs, with [[Maxim Popov]] commenting that Bogdon Ro "got what he deserved".
• A top-ten target mentioned in chat, previously being hunted by [[Langley]]'s team when they were killed.
• A high-profile player killer whose detached head is also mounted on [[Vrah]] and participates in the unexpected attack against her during the fight.
