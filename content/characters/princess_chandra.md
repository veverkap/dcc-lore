# princess_chandra

• A named figure whose marriage certificate is delivered to [[Carl]] via the [[Legendary Fan Box]]; the certificate is referred to as the narrator's marriage evidence.
• A [[Naga]] lawyer described as antagonistic to [[Quasar]], referred to as "[[Donut]]'s lawyer", and implicated in instigating attacks against [[Quasar]]'s family.
• Has filed legal actions (e.g., challenging a marriage certificate) and disappeared after a hit was placed on her.
