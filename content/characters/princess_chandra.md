# Princess Chandra

# [[Princess Chandra]]
A named figure whose marriage certificate is delivered to [[Carl]] via the [[Legendary Fan Box]]; the certificate is referred to as the narrator's marriage evidence.

## Additional Observations
# Princess Chandra
A Naga lawyer described as antagonistic to [[Quasar]]. Referred to as “Donut’s lawyer” and implicated in instigating attacks against [[Quasar]]’s family. Has filed legal actions (e.g., challenging a marriage certificate) and disappeared after a hit was placed on her.
