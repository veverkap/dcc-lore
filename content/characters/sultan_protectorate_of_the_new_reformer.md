# sultan_protectorate_of_the_new_reformer

* The self-styled title taken by [[princess [[chandra]] esquire]]'s uncle after the coup, referred to as the new ruler of the [[Naga]] sector.
* The title allowed [[Chandra]] to accept a noble title.
* Referenced leader of the [[sultanate]] whose survival (and that of other family members) is tied to getting off the ninth floor; mentioned as a target that must be killed to exit that layer.
