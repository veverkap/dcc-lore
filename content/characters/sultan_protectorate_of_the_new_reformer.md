# Sultan Protectorate of the New Reformer

# [[Sultan Protectorate of the New Reformer]]
The self-styled title taken by [[princess chandra esquire]]'s uncle after the coup. Referred to in the chapter as the new ruler of the Naga sector whose rise allowed Chandra to accept a noble title; the exact person remains unnamed but the title is central to Chandra's newfound political cover.

## Additional Observations
# Sultan Protectorate of the New Reformer
The self-styled title taken by [[Chandra]]’s uncle after the coup that replaced the former king. He is the political figurehead whose seizure of power created the unstable context enabling [[Chandra]]’s legal maneuvers.
