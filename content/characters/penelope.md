# penelope

* A pig companion crawling with [[dungeon crawler carl]], wearing enchanted gear, and receiving buffs and potions.
* Also called "[[Penny]]", a pig associated with emotional dialogue and held by [[Taranis]].
* A level 5 Yorkshire Sow, partner to [[Nico]] and [[Dario]] in a throuple, and magically protected by [[Taranis]].
* Previously affected by the [[Touch of Maw-Loo]] / [[Snowdrop Curse]].
* A pig- person racer who is the focus of [[Nico]]'s protectiveness.
* Present on the hill, exposed to hail and at risk from environmental damage.
* A sapient Yorkshire pig given temporary crawler status after being fed [[an]] enriched pet biscuit.
* Communicates via chat and craves the consumable [[Splooge]].
* Speaks in- caps in the chapter about a breakup and a lie, influencing [[Taranis]]'s decision to spare lives.
* Belongs to [[Nico]] and [[Dario]], described as magically special due to being affected by the [[Touch of Maw-Loo (Snowdrop Curse)]].
* Protected by [[Taranis]] such that anyone who harms her will be marked for death by the thunder god.
* A pig- character racer present on the hill while teams debate strategy during the boss fight.
* [[Nico]]'s pig companion who becomes soaked and injured by the hail during the encounter.
* The pig kept inside the APV (nicknamed [[Penny]]) whose panicked squeals are tied to lightning flashes.
* The pig owned/ guarded by the [[Tigrans]], deliberately avoided harm by [[Donut]] during the charm.
* A pig the narrator is concerned about rescuing, with her status checked against system notifications.
* A pet/ client in the [[Pet Stables]] mentioned alongside [[Rend]], [[Gonk]], and [[Simoom]].
* A named pig in the stables who interacts with [[Rend]] and draws the attention of [[Porky]].
* A pig present in the stable scene that [[Samantha]] interacts with, causing reactions from [[Gonk]] and [[Simoom]].
* A pig present at the floats who interacts with [[Carl]] (eating a flower and responding to a snack call).
