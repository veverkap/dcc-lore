# Tsarina Signet
A revealed Elite NPC: "Half Naiad, Half High-Elf Summoner. Level 60." Covered in living, animated tattoos; emits very high Charisma/attraction effects (Puppy Dog-like), and is associated with the circus as a wanted/traitor figure.

## Additional Observations
# Tsarina Signet
A half-high elf, half-naiad elite previously encountered on the third floor; referenced for similarity to the glass statue of [[Lika]] and as part of the broader circus quest storyline.

## Additional Observations
# Tsarina Signet
Half-naiad, half high-elf summoner and an elite (Level 60) allied with local defenders. Uses summoning/ward spells (keeps rain off) and leads a small force that includes [[Clint Smashgrab]] and other allies. Offers [[dungeon crawler carl]] choices about the captured [[Bravvo]].

## Additional Observations
# Tsarina Signet
Also referred to as Signet, an exiled half-daughter of the Naiad leading a local force in the jungle. She organizes the assault plans, commands a mixed force of elves and creatures (including [[Areson the Wise]] and [[Edgar]]), and issues the quest that drives the chapter.

## Additional Observations
# Tsarina Signet
A target of [[Samantha]]'s plea. Described as the granddaughter of [[Princess Yungsten]] of the [[Naiad Castle]] and someone with power to restore [[Samantha]]'s body; Carl and [[Donut]] plan to visit her during the assault on the [[Naiad Castle]].
