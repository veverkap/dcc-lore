# corky_corcunda

* [[Corky]] ([[Corcunda]]), a named crawler, was confirmed knocked out and drugged inside another team's garage, prompting [[Dong]]'s frantic rescue attempt and a potential confrontation with the Lady Dominators.
* [[Corky]]: captive spotted in another team's garage, said to be drugged and on a team with "weird ladies"; sparks [[Dong]]'s urgent actions
* One half of [[Corcunda]] after the Split Personality spell; remained at the club and became close to [[Dong Quixote]]
* Womantaur teammate who is unconscious during the crash and comes to rest near [[dungeon crawler carl]] after the wreck
* One half of [[Corcunda]] who remained at the club as a dancer and formed a close relationship with [[Dong Quixote]]
* Womantaur team member found unconscious after the crash; survives with injuries and is nearby [[Carl]] when they come to rest
* A critical ally for [[Carl]] whose safety is a major concern; [[Carl]] fears that the [[womantaurs]] or rival teams might try to kill Corky
* The missing other half of [[Corcunda]] who left the [[Penis Parade]] team to search and is now in danger during upcoming races; reunification with [[Porky]] is urgent
* A contained [[fleshmancer]] subject held in the garage (target for rejoining by [[Grigori]])
* Recalled by [[Dong]] as the man he loved; described in a memory from the [[Desperado Club]] where Corky comforted [[Dong]] during a siege and framed a philosophy about dying together being meaningful
* Target of the rescue plan: [[Prepotente]] intends for [[Bianca]] to snatch Corky from the back seat once the opposing shield is defeated
* A [[half-mantaur]] passenger found unconscious and "strung out" in the back of the truck; later begins to rejoin with the other half and regain consciousness alongside [[Porky]]
* One half of a split [[mantaur]] pair travelling with the party; exposed, injured, wrapped in plastic, and temporarily fused back together with [[Porky]] by magic and physical restraint
