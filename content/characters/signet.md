# Signet

# Signet
A half-naiad elite NPC and summoner introduced on the third floor. [[Signet]] met [[dungeon crawler carl]] and [[Donut]] in the street, used swirling tattoos and attempted a blood-magic sacrifice to animate a "battle squad" of Mold Lions tied to [[Grimaldi’s Traveling Circus]].

## Additional Observations
# Signet
Also referred to as Tsarina Signet; a high-level (level 60) antagonist who captured [[Donut]] and cast the heirloom spell [[Water Lily]]. Claims a personal history with the circus and [[Grimaldi]] and demands [[dungeon crawler carl]] fulfill a promise (kill [[Heather]]) to secure release terms. Uses family heirloom magic and can perform a summoning.

## Additional Observations
# [[Signet]]
Also called Tsarina Signet. An elite caster who summons massive paper‑like tattoo monsters via blood magic. She appears at the circus with a retinue of 2D "tattoo" minions and directs the assault on the Big Top.
