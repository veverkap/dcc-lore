# bathin

• Identified by the HUD as one of the chief engineers of the [[plenty]]; introduced when [[dungeon crawler carl]]’s interface briefly returns and displays Bathin’s description.
• A named caprid in the herd, described with a jowly [[face]], who has a strong vocal reaction during the herd's panic and who earlier explains the [[Justice Light trap]].

• A caprid on the panel with a jowly [[face]] who emits a pronounced scream and had earlier been explaining the [[Justice Light trap]] to [[Carl]]. One of the goats who fainted during the panic.
