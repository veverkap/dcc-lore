# grigori_the_placid

• A tall, gaunt [[human]] Level 93 [[fleshmancer]] hired as a mercenary by [[Team Meadow Lark]], who worships [[Gula]] and is reserved and poised in social situations.
• Renowned flesh sculptor in the dungeon who introduces himself as more than a "[[fleshmancer]]" and engages with [[Samantha]].
