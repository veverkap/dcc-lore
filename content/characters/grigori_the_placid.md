# grigori_the_placid

* A tall, gaunt [[human]] Level 93 [[fleshmancer]] hired as a mercenary by [[Team Meadow Lark]], who worships [[Gula]] and is reserved and poised in social situations.
* Renowned flesh sculptor in the dungeon who introduces himself as more than a "[[fleshmancer]]" and engages with [[Samantha]].
* [[An]] NPC who was shown being released from [[AI]] control and stumbling toward the [[crawlers]]' garage, subject of requested diagnostics by [[orren the liaison]] and of interest to [[Prime Minister Victory]].
* A tall mage/[[fleshmancer]] specialist who will attempt the dangerous joining ritual, discusses [[Split Personality]] and [[Meld]] processes, and will learn to drive for a race contingency.
* A character present in the garage at the start of the chapter alongside [[Hedy]] and [[Jamal]], mentioned as being left there while others move to the [[Safe Room]].
* A tall [[human]] character strapped into the race vehicle who appears uncomfortable and out of place, noted to have struggled with driving lessons (mentioned alongside [[Hedy]]).
* Passenger in [[Carl]]'s vehicle who reacts physically to the heat (vomiting into a deep fryer) during the factory run.
* A tall mage trapped in the truck who vomits when exposed to the cocaine cloud and assists in on-the-spot healing and support.
* A [[mantaur]] healer/spellcaster traveling with the narrator, performs emergency magics to keep the split [[mantaur]] halves patched together, chants a multi-part spell to fuse the halves, and directs the crew during the ceiling-track segment.
* A crawler who is on the floor injured (scalp wound) and apologizes for [[an]] oversight about deity worship, referenced in relation to the appearance and actions of [[Grull]].
* A contemplative team member who reflects on survival and the nature of death after the race, speaks with philosophical concern about the afterlife.
* [[An]] associate present with [[Samantha]] in her room, mentioned as someone who will come out and relate events tied to [[Samantha]]'s situation.
* A parade participant shown riding atop [[Gonk]] during the procession.
* A dungeon-born NPC and [[fleshmancer]] who is taken over by a macro [[AI]] to narrate the parade exposition, commandeers [[Chaco]]'s microphone atop [[Lamashtu the Donkey]] and delivers the core historical narrative about the [[Primals]], the [[Eulogist]], and the Decision.
* [[Grigori]] stands behind [[Carl]] with arms crossed during the arena confrontation, a named ally present at the scene.
