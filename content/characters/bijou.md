# bijou

• Tiny non-corporeal fairy mob that physically grabs a tendril of light from a god and attaches it to a worshiper's heart.
• Facilitates connections between adherents, temples, and other worshipers.
• Can traverse floors and is visible to certain skilled observers.
• Small fairy-like beings captured by the [[Bijou Trap]] and released when [[justice lights team]]'s trap deactivated; their connecting threads tied multiple planes together before deactivation.
