# Bijou

# [[Bijou]]
Tiny non-corporeal fairy mob that physically grabs a tendril of light from a god and attaches it to a worshiper's heart. Facilitates connections between adherents, temples, and other worshipers; can traverse floors and is visible to certain skilled observers.

## Additional Observations
# Bijou
Small fairy-like beings captured by the [[Bijou Trap]] and released when [[justice lights team]]’s trap deactivated; their connecting threads tied multiple planes together before deactivation.
