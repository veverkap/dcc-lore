# zhang

Here is the consolidated list of unique factual points about Zhang:

• Zhang is a level-7 crawler and companion of Li [[Jun]] who participates in Death Watch.
• Zhang is a friend/associate of Li [[Jun]] and possibly among the group at Station 101.
• Zhang is Li [[Jun]]'s best friend and a level-28 Earth Mover who brings a one-charge Shrinking Magic Wand.
• Zhang is a caster/crew member who mans the Def Leppard cart cockpit controls and fights alongside Li [[Jun]], Li [[Na]], and [[Mongo]].
• Zhang is a party member who helps operate the portal cart and oversees turning off the portal after the [[Nightmare]] departs.
• Zhang is a crawler who receives a celestial box and a cape, granting broad stat bonuses and a risky once-per-hour "Roll the Dice" effect.
• Zhang is a crawler and companion of [[Carl]], owner of Zhang's Cape of Odds, which grants broad stat bonuses and a risky once-per-hour "Roll the Dice" effect.
• Zhang is a character expected to be upset about Li [[Jun]]'s death and likely to blame himself rather than others.
• Zhang is a crawler who comforts [[Donut]] after Li [[Jun]]'s death, expressing pride and respect and helping manage party morale in the aftermath.
• Zhang is a contact who verifies Li [[Na]]'s response and relays that she is "hunting."
• Zhang is a crawler mentioned in conversation who partnered with Li [[Na]] and used a biological vehicle described as [[an]] octopus-like creature.
• Zhang is a caster allied with Li [[Na]] and others, using spells to affect mushroom guards and change timers on their aggro/behavior.
• Zhang is a performer juggling knives with Li [[Jun]] and [[Na]].
• Zhang is a crawler accompanying Li [[Jun]] and Li [[Na]] in the caravan and involved in the talent show entries.
• Zhang is a party member who used two zaps on [[Carl]]'s wand, returning the Shrink Wand afterward and helping with the countdown to the ascension event.
• Zhang is a crawler who is paired with Li [[Na]] in this heat and is mentioned regarding upgrade choice (biological).
• Zhang is a named crawler/NPC who provides intelligence to [[Carl]], reporting that Li [[Na]] had killed many other teams.
• Zhang is a crawler who reports wins and expresses concern that remaining teams might be conspiring against his group.
• Zhang is a distraught party member who is grieving losses and is contemplating killing Li [[Na]] to stop her.
• Zhang is a named individual whose survival is tied to the success of the plan to use the Pineapple Cabaret for curing Li [[Na]].
• Zhang is a crawler and friend of Li [[Na]] who physically presses the pet-carrier button to send Li [[Na]] into the carrier.
• Zhang is a crawler participating in the event who receives notifications about team disqualifications and achievements.
• Zhang is a party member mentioned as having the minimal stables space after the guild lost the level 5 stables.
• Zhang is a crawler who joins the departing group, engages in banter with [[Carl]] about rank, and receives affection from [[Donut]].
