# shi_maria

Here is the consolidated markdown list:

* [[Shi Maria]] is a unique monster and demi-god [[spider]] encountered by [[Samantha]]. Known by many alternate names (e.g., The Screamer, The Bedlam Bride) and listed in the bestiary (the [[Bahamas book]]).
* Shi [[Maria]] is a powerful demi-god arachnid boss with a closed fifth eye that can inflict permanent blindness or insanity.
* She is a dominant [[spider]]-card totem in [[Donut]]’s deck, known for devouring enemies and manipulating the flow of fights.
* Shi [[Maria]] is a team card/creature noted for high dodge and speed.
* She is a powerful totem/creature card in [[dungeon crawler carl]]'s faction deck, discussed as dangerous and considered for combination or removal.
* Shi [[Maria]] is a named totem/character referenced for power comparison, with [[Lazarus-A-Bang-Bang]] being compared to her.
* She is a summon/unit that [[Donut]] can create, mentioned as a potential triplicate via [[Clockwork Triplicate]].
* Shi [[Maria]] is a powerful totem ([[spider]]) summoned during the fight, able to bite off hydra heads and withstand fire.
* She is a summoned combatant from [[Donut]]’s deck who is described as eating corpses quickly during the skirmish.
* Shi [[Maria]] is a card- based entity/ persona that forcibly attaches to [[dungeon crawler carl]] as a totem, voices control inside his mind, and compels him to tattoo himself with [[eye of the bedlam bride]].
* She is a tall summoned minion/card with grotesque long legs, used in combination with [[Alpha Carl]] and ends up physically combining with [[Carl]] via a system combination.
* Shi [[Maria]] is a goddess/NPC card tied to a quest, with [[Donut]] ripping her card earlier but the quest to bring her to a city remaining active with [[Carl]].
