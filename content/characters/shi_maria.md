# Shi Maria

# Shi Maria
A unique monster and demi-god spider encountered by [[Samantha]]. Known by many alternate names (e.g., The Screamer, The Bedlam Bride) and listed in the bestiary (the [[Bahamas book]]). Resides in the swamp near the southern coast/mouth of the big bay and is capable of devouring crawlers whole.

## Additional Observations
# Shi Maria
Also known as "The Screamer," a colossal, unique demi-god spider with a human-like head. Specializes in illusion and fear magic (notable spells include [[Unending Insanity]] and [[Lights Out]]). Vulnerable to Psionic attacks; able to ensnare victims and purportedly animate bodies.

## Additional Observations
# Shi Maria
A powerful demi-god arachnid boss (aliases include MaeMae, The Bedlam Bride, The Screamer, The Widow Endless). Described as a Level 140 City Boss and a Reaper Spider variant with a closed fifth eye that can inflict blindness or insanity. She bargains with [[dungeon crawler carl]] to be turned into a card and escorted to [[Larracos]] in exchange for help finding her husband.
