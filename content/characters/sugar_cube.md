# sugar_cube

• A white Persian-cat likeness used as one hydra head, connected to [[Donut]]'s family, and briefly destroyed before regenerating.
• The colloquial name for the high elf castle (from the sixth floor), where the ballroom where carnage occurs is located, and which was later transferred to the ninth floor and used by the [[lemig sortition]].
• A white Persian-cat likeness on the hydra (one of [[Donut]]'s relatives) used as a head with its own attacks and presence in the boss composition.
