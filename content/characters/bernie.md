# bernie

• The named mole-like pet, Bernie, perches on [[Rosemarie]]'s shoulder and interacts with patrons in the club.
• Bernie is a black-and-white fuzzy mole creature that reacts physically to [[Damascus Steel]] and shows emotional reactions during the scene.
• Bernie has a distinctive fur pattern and acts like a pet.
