# osvaldo

• A [[Curupira]] ranger who looted [[Imogen]]'s memorial crystal, causing conflict with [[Prepotente]] and others.
• A Brazilian crawler who looted the memorial crystal from Queen [[Imogen]]'s body; owner of the hawk [[Gimli]] and implicated in prior conflicts.
• A level-60 [[Curupira]] ranger who made his first appearance on the floor leaderboard; fought alongside or against other top [[crawlers]] after the prior- floor events.
• A crawler who originally held the Memorial Crystal '[[Apito]]' before it ended up with [[Prepotente]].
• A crawler who publicly commended by [[Zev]] for a "stunning sacrifice of his hired mercenary".
• A small, red-haired level 72 crawler of the race/ class [[Curupira]] who rides on [[Bruna]] the Slaughter Gnu for Team [[Flamengo]].
• A competitor who moved ahead of the narrator's group and is following [[Florin]]'s route.
• A member of the racing field mentioned among those who passed through parts of the park alongside [[Florin]] and others.
• A crawler standing outside Garage Three with [[Filipe]], drinking a Busch Light and joking about missing beers.
• A teammate who holds the reins of his gnu during the starting lineup; positioned between teams and used tactically by [[Carl]] to maneuver into place.
• A crew member in [[Carl]]'s vehicle, reacts verbally to events in the cab and steers toward a larger doorway in the factory.
• A teammate who tends to the giant gnu [[Bruna]] and angrily recounts how the Unicorn cheated to win the race.
• A character who previously possessed the Memorial Crystal ([[Apito]]), kept it in his inventory, and reported it inactive until it suddenly shone during Faction Wars.
• A crawler whose garage remains closed and who cannot go to the Cabaret because he worships a god; his intentions about taking a deal are uncertain.
• A fractious crawler on the team considering risky options (e.g., dropping down the elevator shaft); communicates tactical intent with [[Carl]].
• A competitor who lied to survive and managed to cross the finish line with [[Filipe]] with a minute to spare, impacting the final standings for Heat Six.
• A crawler who refuses to take [[an]] Outreach Guild deal; directly confronted by [[Carl]] and whose choice affects final-heat compositions.
• A rival crawler and one of the fastest in the dungeon; positioned on a gnu/mount, he refuses cooperation with [[Carl]] and faces a timed engagement where roof demolition is used to trap him.
• A crawler who attempted to exit via the rooftop portals to the eleventh floor; used the Launch spell and carried explosive and poison ordnance that nearly killed [[Bruna]].
