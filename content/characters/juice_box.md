# juice_box

Here is the consolidated list of unique factual points about Juice Box:

• Juice Box is a [[changeling]] prostitute NPC employed at the inn [[The Toe]].
• She can rapidly shift forms in public, displaying titles like "Ratkin Brood Mother Attendant" and "Mouser Dame".
• Juice Box is a mischievous [[changeling]] who wrangles [[changeling]] children and distributes juice boxes.
• She is a member of [[Carl]]'s group and receives instructions to keep children in the safe room and control personal space during operations.
• Juice Box is a local townsperson who interacts with [[Carl]] and the rock-boded [[Chris Andrews 2]].
• She is a [[changeling]] who can alter forms and influence meetings, and is central to discussions about the new [[Shanty Town]] markets, allegiance, and defensive plans.
• Juice Box is a leader of [[team retribution]] and a [[changeling]] figure with many recorded lives (claims ~400).
• She is a key actor who teleported away with the war mages after [[Stalwart]]'s death and is involved in coordination and inquiries with [[Ferdinand]] and the adjutants.
• Juice Box is a healer/[[Fleshmancer]] with a sensed connection to [[Tagg]] inside [[Louis]].
• She is a caster/operative who activates the transplanted organs during the [[Louis]] graft and coordinates magical timing for the surgical window.
• Juice Box is a warlord and major player who coordinates squads of [[changelings]] to pose as basilica guards.
• She is a character who disappeared along with [[justice lights team]] and was implicated in prior events.
• Juice Box is a [[changeling]]/dirigible gnome woman who cares for orphans in [[Hump Town]].
• She is a [[pazuzu]] NPC grieving the death of a brother and is present in Hump Town and involved in the group's larger social context and rescue coordination.
• Juice Box is a [[changeling]] girl in [[human]] form present in the safe room, showing signs of NPC-like memories and instability.
• She is a crawler companion who comforts [[dungeon crawler carl]] and offers personal anecdotes.
• Juice Box is a [[changeling]]/ prostitute crawler who agrees to be sent through the gate and later flees to warn others.
• She is a named romantic partner who broke up with [[Louis]] in the aftermath, enabling plot developments in [[Samantha]]'s narration.
• Juice Box is a psychotic [[changeling]] NPC who became a warlord after the system intervened to stop her killing spree.
• She is a leader of a rival team signaling distress in chat and is mentioned as a competitor in the all-or-nothing outcome imposed by the Peeling Phase ruleset.
• Juice Box is a named NPC referenced among the standard [[NPCs]], suspected to be in trouble after being dumped in [[Sheol]] and noted to have transformed previously into [[an]] [[Unwashed]]-like form.
