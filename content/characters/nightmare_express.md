# nightmare_express

Here is the consolidated list of unique factual points about the [[Nightmare]] Express:

• The [[Nightmare]] Express is a distinct train route that runs in a figure-eight pattern, known as the "Iron Tangle".
• It has longer cars and wider tracks than other trains.
• The train spawns hostile mobs like the Cave Mudge Bonker and Shock Chomper.
• It intersects multiple lines, including the Red and Yellow lines.
• The train runs a long circuit, with stations 11-435 described as a three-day trip.
• It displays anomalous looping/time-reset behavior affecting crew and objects.
• The train has a known end-of-line behavior around stop 435.
• It has a repeating-but-weird loop property.
• The train contains specialized cars, including employee quarters, porter rooms, and the engine car.
• It has open-top freight cars carrying large monsters.
• The train is driven by a coal/boiler-driven engine marked "666".
• The train is used as a central mode of transport in the chapter, stopping at stations and hauling multiple cars.
• It is used to attempt traversal through portals and for transporting party members and cargo.
• The train is a target route the party intends to reach, with stops at 283 on the Purple and Mauve lines.
• It is regarded as a dangerous route, with the party considering it as a tactical option but also regarding it as a risk.
• The train is a named steam train that runs a looping route through stations, including Station 83 and Station 283.
• It is temporarily commandeered by Dungeon Crawler [[Carl]].
• The train is used as a mobile base, allowing [[Carl]] to travel between stations and push debris with its cowcatcher.
