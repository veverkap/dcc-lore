# nightmare_express

* The [[Nightmare]] Express is a distinct train route that runs in a figure-eight pattern, known as the "Iron Tangle".
* It has longer cars and wider tracks than other trains.
* The train spawns hostile mobs like the Cave Mudge Bonker and Shock Chomper.
* It intersects multiple lines, including the Red and Yellow lines.
* The train runs a long circuit, with stations 11-435 described as a three-day trip.
* It displays anomalous looping/time-reset behavior affecting crew and objects.
* The train has a known end-of-line behavior around stop 435.
* It has a repeating-but-weird loop property.
* The train contains specialized cars, including employee quarters, porter rooms, and the engine car.
* It has open-top freight cars carrying large monsters.
* The train is driven by a coal/boiler-driven engine marked "666".
* The train is used as a central mode of transport in the chapter, stopping at stations and hauling multiple cars.
* It is used to attempt traversal through portals and for transporting party members and cargo.
* The train is a target route the party intends to reach, with stops at 283 on the Purple and Mauve lines.
* It is regarded as a dangerous route, with the party considering it as a tactical option but also regarding it as a risk.
* The train is a named steam train that runs a looping route through stations, including Station 83 and Station 283.
* It is temporarily commandeered by Dungeon Crawler [[Carl]].
* The train is used as a mobile base, allowing [[Carl]] to travel between stations and push debris with its cowcatcher.
* The Nightmare is a named train/engine used by Dungeon Crawler [[Carl]] and companions as their primary moving base on the tracks.
* It has a key-controlled cab and passenger cars, and can be boarded or approached by mobs.
* The Nightmare is a key location/vehicle referenced as the party's primary steam engine/train, used for travel on the subway tracks.
* It has a passenger car, engine cab, and is referenced alongside other trains.
* The Nightmare is a named train line on the floor's map that forms part of the Syndicate logo pattern and the railway/wormpath system.
* It is a target for survivors to capture and use to travel into the yard from Station 436.
* The Nightmare is a hidden railway location referenced as the parking place for [[an]] asset called the Nightmare, with no [[ghouls]] and connected to nearby stations and safe rooms.
* It is a fallback/evacuation reference point in the chapter.
* The Nightmare is a named train line/loop on the floor's map referred to as the "Nightmare's loop", used by [[Mordecai]] to identify patterned train routes that form the Syndicate logo.
* Named cab/train used as mobile base, where characters return after station excursions and where some interpersonal conflicts occur.
* Named locomotive/creature that idles at the station, serving as the boiler/engine for the rescue train, with riders clinging to its boiler.
* Named train/route with heavy mob presence and war mages, planned to be caught at a transfer station.
* Used by [[dungeon crawler carl]]'s party as a mobile base and referenced when returning from station excursions.
* Hidden railway train or parked vehicle free of [[ghouls]], with accessible rest areas/safe rooms, used as a logistical landmark in rescue planning.
* The gnomish Drop Bear, a named beast/vehicle-associated creature, is parked in the large garage of the uprooted flying house.
* It remains lashed down while the house lifts into the air.
