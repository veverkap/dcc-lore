# dromedarian

• Local camel-like NPC people in the bowl operate tracked carts, run patrols, and sell weaponry such as bazooka tubes.
• Camel-like [[NPCs]] make up town residents and guards, including ordinary [[dromedarians]] (Waster Patrol) and [[changelings]] in disguise, like [[Henrik]].
