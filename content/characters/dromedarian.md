# Dromedarian
Local camel-like NPC people in the bowl who operate tracked carts, run patrols, and sell weaponry such as bazooka tubes.

## Additional Observations
# Dromedarian
Camel-like NPCs that make up town residents and guards. Some are ordinary dromedarians (Waster Patrol) while others have been revealed as [[Changelings]] in disguise; examples include named figures like [[Henrik]].
