# herman_the_fleet

* [[Herman]] the Fleet is a brother connected to the circus family who was in [[Larracos]] during the attack; his fate is currently unknown according to [[Signet]].
* [[Herman]] the Fleet is [[an]] Elite ogre (Ogre Strongman, Level 70) acting as a desk-bound officer in the antechamber; he was revealed to be a suicide bomber covered in explosive satchels before being killed.
* [[Herman]] the Fleet is another brother in the same family as [[Areson]] and [[Apollon]]; he was in [[Larracos]] during the events and his fate is unknown.
* [[An]] ogre offshoot of the three-headed progenitor [[Di-we]] who resides in [[Larracos]].
* [[An]] ogre derived from the [[nodling]] progenitor [[Di-we]], said to be located down below in [[Larracos]] and represented among [[Signet]]'s tattoos.
