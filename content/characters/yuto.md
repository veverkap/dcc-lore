# yuto

• A named red Oni who was part of the onstage group "team Yokai" at the Lollipop, sang "Careless Whisper" in the chapter, and is a male demon performer and member of the karaoke troupe.
• [[An]] Oni from team Yokai who supports [[Carl]] onstage by patting him on the back.
• A large red demon / Oni from team Yokai who is struck by the dead [[gremlin]] and later defended by allies (including the Minister of Blood-Letting and skeletons).
