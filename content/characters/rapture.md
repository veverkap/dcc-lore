# rapture

* A level 95 womantaur, freckled and red-haired, partner to [[Genesis]], practices domination magic, and displays rough, tobacco-choosing behavior.
* Driver of the womantaur convertible, primary target of [[Donut]]'s planned [[Danforth bolt]] attack.
* One of the two figures conversing with [[Nico]] and present at the scene near the parked vehicles and shields.
* A racer with red curls who participates in the group at the hilltop and comments on handling the situation with the bosses.
* A rival/ally figure who proposes a temporary alliance with [[dungeon crawler carl]]'s group and helps in the battle, skeptical of trusting others.
* A member of the Lady Dominators present during the garage scene, mocking others and reacting to events around [[Dong Quixote]].
* Driver or passenger of a pursuing muscle car who flashes devil horns at the truck; observed during the race sequence alongside rival teams.
* Ally who fires a flamethrower during the race and tends to [[Genesis]] after the crash.
* Seen in the passenger seat of a muscle car during the race; gives a taunting gesture (devil horns) toward the truck team.
* Ally who wields a flamethrower (used to clear skulls above a pursuing vehicle) and assists [[Genesis]] with healing after the crash.
* A crawler/teammate who moved quickly to their garage after the race; involved in post-race actions and grief reactions.
* A member of another team seen walking from a garage toward a derelict doorway; part of the race's broader field visible in the pit stop.
* One half of a duo (womantaur/stripper) who, along with [[Genesis]], drives the GTO with [[Corky]] and refuses to surrender him; taunts the party during the garage escape.
* One of the occupants of the damaged [[GTO]] who is alive but afflicted with the "Strung Out" debuff from the cocaine cloud in this chapter.
