# Changelings

# Changelings
A shapeshifting race that changes by first becoming a generic version of a race then altering appearance; contrasted with [[Doppelganger]] and [[Mimic]] mechanics and exemplified by [[Juice Box]].

## Additional Observations
# Changelings
Shapechanging NPC/mob race that can mimic creatures they have touched. Infiltrated camel ranks in the town; their culture includes sects like the [[Changeling Principal]] and an obsession with collecting a "full library" of forms. Killing a changeling in human/true form yields a valuable head/brain component.

## Additional Observations
# Changelings
Shapechanging allies of [[dungeon crawler carl]] infiltrating enemy areas; some have made it into the castle and are part of the broader tactical picture.
