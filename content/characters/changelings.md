# changelings

• A shapeshifting race that changes by first becoming a generic version of a race then altering appearance, exemplified by [[Juice Box]].
• Shapechanging NPC/mob race that can mimic creatures they have touched, with a culture including sects like the [[Changeling Principal]] and [[an]] obsession with collecting a "full library" of forms.
• Allies of [[dungeon crawler carl]] infiltrating enemy areas, with some members part of the broader tactical picture.
• Altered or shape-shifting beings allied with [[Signet]], [[Kiwi]], and [[Big Tina]], brought into the [[castle of the high elves]] and teleported to the [[Ninth Floor]] to form part of the party's army.
• Squad-type NPC [[mercenaries]] capable of taking basilica guard forms, used strategically to deter gods while the [[celestial ascendency]] is pending.
• Shapechanging [[NPCs]] present among [[camels]] earlier but notably absent after the Wasteland impact, with story ties to the necropolis and water immunity.
• Vulnerable NPC children, including [[Bonnie]] and [[Ruby]], intended to be relocated down floors by [[dungeon crawler carl]] and company, with their relocation central to the debate over using [[Zerzura]] versus a gate.
