# gideon

• A crawler (tank class) who contacts [[dungeon crawler carl]] with field reports about feral gods and alerts them to a monster carrying a large whip or chain whip.
• Leader of [[an]] assault team, [[an]] alternative assignment for attendees like [[Britney]].
• Member of the larger operation, setting up in the woods to observe and assault the castle front if needed.
• Remote watcher hidden near the castle, providing battlefield reconnaissance and reporting on mounted [[elves]], [[mantises]], and disruptions involving the [[Twister]] airship.
• Deceased crawler who led a team that died earlier in the assault, his loss weighing heavily on [[dungeon crawler carl]].
• Crawler recruited to help the party, who later died, affecting group morale.
• [[Human]] tank-class crawler seen in chat, reporting sightings of gods/[[demons]] clashing and alerting [[Carl]] about a monster carrying a large whip or chain whip.
• Distant crawler who reports combat [[experience]] with vampiric creatures, appearing in comms describing a near-fatal encounter and showing player reactions across the map.
