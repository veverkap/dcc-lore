# colonel_ret_james_ian_dinniman_1938-2023

• The author's father, Colonel (Ret) James [[Ian]] Dinniman (1938-2023), was a hero to his country and to the author.
• He died around the time the author was writing chapter 45.
• His death was a significant personal event and the reason for the author's dedication.
• He is presented as a personal legacy distinct from the author's books.
