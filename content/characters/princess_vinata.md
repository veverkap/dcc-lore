# Princess Vinata

# Princess Vinata
The sultana avatar of the [[sultanate]] in-game, a giant white naga with six arms, red pupil-less eyes, and a golden breastplate bearing a flowered-branch symbol. In-game moniker: "the Whore" (portraying the historical "Sepsis Whore"); she exerts a powerful, charismatic presence that unnerves [[dungeon crawler carl]].

## Additional Observations
# [[Princess Vinata]]
A naga warlord who whispers strategic and personal threats to [[dungeon crawler carl]], offering political marriage/alliances and suggesting covert action. Calm, persuasive, and politically ambitious.

# Princess Vinata
The sultana of the naga ruling house and central antagonist of the mission (referred to as the Sepsis Whore). [[Princess Vinata]] is famed for mysteriously disappearing with her elite honor guard near the climax of [[Faction Wars]] events and is believed to be linked to hidden escape routes tied to [[Club Vanquisher]] and city-area teleport restrictions.
