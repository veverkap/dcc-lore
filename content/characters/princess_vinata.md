# princess_vinata

• The [[sultana]] avatar of the [[sultanate]] in-game, a giant white [[naga]] with six arms, red pupil-less eyes, and a golden breastplate bearing a flowered-branch symbol.
• Exerts a powerful, charismatic presence that unnerves [[dungeon crawler carl]].
• A [[naga]] warlord who whispers strategic and personal threats to [[dungeon crawler carl]], offering political marriage/alliances and suggesting covert action.
• Calm, persuasive, and politically ambitious.
• The [[sultana]] of the [[naga]] ruling house and central antagonist of the mission (referred to as the Sepsis Whore).
• Famed for mysteriously disappearing with her elite honor guard near the climax of [[Faction Wars]] events and is believed to be linked to hidden escape routes tied to [[Club Vanquisher]] and city-area teleport restrictions.
• The deceased [[naga]] [[princess]] whose leadership and "Great Consensus" philosophy shaped the faction's plans.
• Her death occurred during the temple event tied to the season's climax.
