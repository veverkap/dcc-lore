# experience

• Named mob encountered in the park: a four-legged, wolf-sized body with a large [[human]] head (bearing resemblance to Che Guevara) wearing a magical red beret, described as a monstrous take on pre-Colombian Taino folklore, aggressive, magical, and loot-dropping (including a red beret and a card).
