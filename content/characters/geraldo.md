# [[Geraldo]]
Another level-70 monk seal mob introduced near the chapter's end; the second seal approaching after the first encounter with [[Yago]].

## Additional Observations
# Geraldo
Geraldo is the Level 70 Monk Seal Picket Sentry summoned from the [[Monk Seal Totem]] by [[Donut]]. He appears with a health bar, a 60-second timer, and a tiny flag icon linking him to the party; he responds verbally and refuses to attack the Squonk in the chapter.
