# ghouls

• Common hostile undead mobs that stream from stations and home toward large gatherings of [[crawlers]]; a primary threat on the railway and subject to system spawning behavior.
• Undead-liked mobs generated by the ghoul system; their mass deaths are used strategically to pop soul crystal and overload the system.
• Primary aggressive mob type swarming stations and tracks; variants include Wrath Ghouls and Blister Ghouls.
• Widespread undead/monster variant streaming along tracks and being funneled by robot rooms onto conveyors to be shipped to the Abyss.
• Undead-liked spawns produced by ghoul generators and by kills of living [[Krakaren]]; encountered mixed into waves and created persistent threats near ghoul generators.
• Common undead-liked mobs filling the trainyard and tunnels, contributing to the heavy waves of enemies the [[crawlers]] must navigate.
