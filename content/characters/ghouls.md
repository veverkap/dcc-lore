# Ghouls
Common hostile undead mobs that stream from stations (e.g., station 72) and home toward large gatherings of crawlers; a primary threat on the railway and subject to system spawning behavior.

## Additional Observations
# ghouls
Common undead mobs encountered near stations and stairwells throughout the chapter; held at bay by defenders like [[Imani]] during the group's movement.

## Additional Observations
# Ghouls
Enemy mobs referenced as targets during the skirmish; [[Katia]] claims [[Donut]] was aiming bolts at the ghouls when she was hit.

# Ghouls
Undead-like mobs generated by the ghoul system; their mass deaths are used strategically to pop [[soul crystal]] and overload the system.

## Additional Observations
# Ghouls
Primary aggressive mob type swarming stations and tracks; variants include [[Wrath Ghouls]] and [[Blister Ghouls]]. Frequently generated in large numbers and responsible for sieges of platforms and stairwells.

## Additional Observations
# [[Ghouls]]
Widespread undead/monster variant streaming along tracks and being funneled by robot rooms onto conveyors to be shipped to the Abyss. Low XP but numerous; constitute the main enemy wave in several stations.

## Additional Observations
# Ghouls
Undead-like spawns produced by ghoul generators and by kills of living Krakaren; encountered mixed into waves and created persistent threats near ghouls generators.
