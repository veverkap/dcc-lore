# genesis

* [[Genesis]] is a level 95 Lady [[Mantaur]] Dominator who worships [[Enyo]].
* She appears with [[Rapture]] and inspects the protagonists, including [[dungeon crawler carl]], [[Donut]], and the rest of the party.
* She is a womantaur who participates in the battle, kills the [[engineer]] NPC [[Niels]] with a grappling hook, and dons a kangaroo costume herself.
* She is a member of the garage group who shouts from inside the closed garage door and is part of the tension surrounding [[Dong Quixote]]'s condition.
* She is a teammate who receives a healing potion from [[Rapture]] after the crash and is found folded over in [[an]] unnatural pose.
* She is a womantaur who introduces herself as Genesis, wears a white racing suit and cowboy hat, speaks with a Southern accent, and worships [[Enyo]] rather than [[Grull]].
* She is one of the two figures standing at the top of the hill, paired with [[Rapture]], and is engaged in discussion with [[Nico]].
* She is a womantaur racer present at the hilltop, discussing tactics and the temporary truce while watching the fight with [[Prison Pocket]]/[[Party Foul]] and interacting with other racers.
* She is a lady [[mantaur]] ally who delivers a lethal grappling-hook strike that kills the [[engineer]] NPC [[Niels]], participates in melee actions against the boss, and helps in the assault plan.
* She is a member of the Lady Dominators' group heard shouting from inside the garage during the scene, part of the small faction social dynamic.
* She is [[an]] injured teammate who receives a healing potion from [[Rapture]] after the finish-line crash and is folded over herself in [[an]] unnatural pose when rescued.
* She is a crawler who, alongside [[Rapture]], scoops up [[Dong]] and goes to their garage following the wreck.
* She is a rider seen in the passenger seat of the Lady Dominators, described as scream-laughing and making obscene gestures during the race.
* She is paired with [[Rapture]] in the same garage group, observed at the pit stop and moving toward the race area.
* She is a partner to [[Rapture]] who drives the golden-tired GTO and asserts ownership of [[Corky]] and another "gimp," pulling the car away to the starting blocks.
* She is another occupant of the crashed [[GTO]] suffering the "Strung Out" condition and at risk due to the loss of containment in the crash.
