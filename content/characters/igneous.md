# igneous

• Igneous: a race classification that grants high constitution but imposes penalties (e.g., inability to use chat or inventory without damage), physically altering appearance and sometimes personality after transformation, associated with increased strength and personality changes, and confers high constitution and specific handicaps (e.g., inventory/chat penalties).
