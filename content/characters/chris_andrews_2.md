# chris_andrews_2

• Level 6 crawler and [[Brandon]]'s brother, with a quiet and taciturn presence.
• Occupied the throne room of the [[Akula]]'s bridge, triggering the Bubble Notification and declaring him the liberator of the [[Water Quadrant]].
• Identified as "Crawler #324,116" and displayed as a level 35 [[Igneous]] Zulu Warrior, later revealed to be a host body occupied by the [[Infiltrator]] parasite controlled by [[Maggie My]].
• Successfully occupied the Bridge of the [[Akula]] and liberated the [[Water Quadrant]].
• Present in town when the party discovers the parasitic takeover, central to the crisis involving [[Maggie My]], [[dungeon crawler carl]], and [[Donut]].
• Appears in the quest chat as trapped/enslaved earlier, but later reports moving and disconnecting wires, with [[an]] [[Enslaved]] marker in the group list.
• Shown in a loot/corpse log as [[an]] assist on kills in the area, indicating previous player activity around the discovered loop and stairwell.
