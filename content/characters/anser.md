# Anser
The emperor for whom the Crypt of Anser was constructed; his court and wife were entombed, and he is central to the lore of the quadrant's undead threat.

## Additional Observations
# Anser
A historical or quasi-mythic figure tied to the tomb and the oppression of local peoples (bactrians, dromedarians, glabers). [[Anser]]'s legacy motivates some attackers' genocidal rhetoric.
