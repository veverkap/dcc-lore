# anser

• The emperor for whom the Crypt of Anser was constructed, entombed with his court and wife, and central to the lore of the quadrant's undead threat.
• A historical or quasi-mythic figure tied to the tomb and the oppression of local peoples ([[bactrians]], [[dromedarians]], [[glabers]]), whose legacy motivates some attackers' genocidal rhetoric.
• The figure whose tomb was found by [[Gwen]] and [[Tran]], depicted as a goose skeleton wearing a golden crown and tied to subterranean tomb lore.
• The emperor whose tomb and court were sealed in the crypt, whose death created the Necropolis/crypt situation central to the quadrant's dangers.
• A historical or mythic oppressor who subjugated the [[bactrians]], [[dromedarians]], and [[glabers]] and is linked to the tomb and local lore.
• The named goose whose sarcophagus (Tomb of Anser) contained a skeleton with a golden crown, [[an]] explored relic tied to sixth-floor mapping lore.
