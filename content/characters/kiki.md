# Kiki

# Kiki
Quasar’s long-time friend and fellow attorney, described as a heavy drinker and a dromedarian. Represents clients such as [[Daniel Bautista]] and is quickly recruited by [[Quasar]] to assist when [[Tempest]] is attacked. Uses legal and personal contacts to respond to threats.
