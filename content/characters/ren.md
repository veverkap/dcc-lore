# ren

* [[Ren]] is a performer who explains how a large [[meatball]] can tank attacks and draw incoming missiles during the talent show.
* [[Ren]] is a combatant using a flamethrower in the fight, providing notable area-of-effect damage against [[mantises]] and [[elves]].
* [[Ren]] is a [[dog]]-woman crawler who questions [[Carl]] about how the team acquired a faction-wars spot and later reveals to have a "Pet Show Champion" trophy mark in the chapter.
* [[Ren]] is a named ally who responds in chat, volunteering to be ready near the Capitol exit to intercept [[Quan Ch]].
* [[Ren]] is the leader of a squad allied with [[Carl]], coordinating remotely and wary of teleport-trap defenses.
* [[Ren]] is a member of the opposing squad in the Capitol fight, wielding a flamethrower and summoning totems, including a large worm and a mystic protection shell.
* [[Ren]] is a crawler who chooses to sacrifice her remaining options on this floor, euthanizing her pet [[Garret]] to reset its level and weaknesses, and drops her gear to [[Dungeon Crawler Carl]].
* [[Ren]] is a crawler who detonates a building with a magical wrist bracer and whose actions force [[Dungeon Crawler Carl]] and the group to move.
* [[Ren]] is a crawler mentioned as having bowed out early from [[an]] encounter, referenced in group discussion about choices and survival.
* [[Ren]] is referenced in [[Carl]]'s thoughts as someone broken by hopelessness, part of [[Carl]]'s personal motivations and memories.
* [[Ren]] is a fallen ally who previously provided consumable cards, including [[Midnight Express]] and [[Glow Up]].
* [[Ren]] is a teammate mentioned as the owner of a [[Flamethrower]] that the totem urges [[Dungeon Crawler Carl]] to examine.
* [[Ren]] is a named crawler/warlord referenced as having set off teleport traps at the Capitol building, involved in earlier conflict with [[Carl]]'s team.
* [[Ren]] is a companion to [[Khulan]], part of [[Carl]]'s backstory references for spellbook acquisitions.
* [[Ren]] is a deceased character referenced as the original owner/association for the newborn pet, with several group members insisting any name should honor [[Ren]].
* [[Ren]] is a participant/commentator on stage acts, awkwardly explaining mechanics of [[Garret]] and [[Tserendolgor]]'s performance.
* [[Ren]] is a [[dog]]-woman crawler who questions how [[Dungeon Crawler Carl]] purchased a faction-wars spot and is found with a small trophy mark related to a pet show in the aftermath.
* [[Ren]] is a crawler with a background as a model who underwent a transformation into a [[dog]], referenced in squad gossip.
* [[Ren]] is [[an]] ally in chat who confirms readiness to intercept [[Quan Ch]] near [[an]] exit in the [[Capitol Building]]/[[Cuba Area]].
* [[Ren]] is the leader of a small squad allied with [[Carl]], coordinating with [[Carl]] via chat, hiding with her team while preparing to confront [[Quan]].
* [[Ren]] is a member of the squad defending the room at [[El Capitolio Nacional de Cuba]], wielding a flamethrower, summoning totems, and using protective mystic cards.
* [[Ren]] is a [[dog]]-woman crawler who is grieving, sacrificial, and pragmatic in this chapter, giving up gear and cards to help others and preparing to use a wrist bracer to deny the showrunners a fight.
* [[Ren]] is [[an]] ally/enemy figure referenced in the escape and tied to the building's destruction, with [[Carl]] considering marking her with the [[Ring of Divine Suffering]] but not doing so.
* [[Ren]] is a deceased ally who previously gave [[Dungeon Crawler Carl]] consumable cards, including [[Midnight Express]].
* [[Ren]] is a member of the group whose equipment is inspected by [[Shi Maria]] while attached to [[Dungeon Crawler Carl]], specifically having a flamethrower that [[Shi Maria]] examines.
* [[Ren]] is another crawler referenced in relation to traps and combat on prior floors, linked to the [[Lord of the Flies]] achievement indirectly.
* [[Ren]] is a companion whose flamethrower [[Carl]] examines while under control, mentioned in the inventory-scrutiny sequence by [[Shi Maria]].
