# Chuluuna

# [[Chuluuna]]
Strength-based human of [[Ren]]’s squad who can turn his skin to stone and serves as the primary tank; reportedly able to see invisible foes. Husband to [[Khulan]].

## Additional Observations
# [[Chuluuna]]
Large Mongolian crawler in Ren's squad. Casting spells when the worm attacks; he is killed and dismembered during the battle when his totem consumes him.
