# chuluuna

• Strength-based [[human]] of [[Ren]]'s squad who can turn his skin to stone and serves as the primary tank; reportedly able to see invisible foes. Husband to [[Khulan]].
• Can withstand heavy damage and see [[an]] invisible attacker (likely [[Quan]]).
• Large Mongolian crawler in [[Ren]]'s squad who casts spells (including [[Chain Lightning]]).
• Was swallowed/maimed by the worm [[Basquiat]] during the fight and whose corpse features in the aftermath.
