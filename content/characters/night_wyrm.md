# night_wyrm

• Demigod worshipped by the [[Guild of Suffering]], connected to divine- suffering artifacts and the culture that leaves marks like the [[Night Wyrm’s Nasty Little Web of Suffering]].
• Source of local knowledge/ maps referenced by [[dungeon crawler carl]]; provides information about unknown red-tagged monsters and failed attempts to clear a room.
• A disembodied, hissing voice that issues mission directives and rewards, praising the party for killing a mark and commanding delivery of the body to the guild.
• A performer in the [[Desperado Club]] identified as part of the family dynamic with [[Astrid]], [[Damascus Steel]], and [[Anaconda]]; the Night Wyrm is referenced as a performer and as having a lair area in the club.
• A [[Samantha]]- like demigod referenced in the Engaged Lock Box text and the voice behind the Guild Job, owning the Guild Job and communicating the assassination contract and family-themed rhetoric.
• A source of information on the club map or lore, reporting that nobody knows the nature of the red-tagged monsters behind the sealed door.
• A significant named figure referenced as the lair-holder on the top floor and described by [[Astrid]] as her husband, [[Hamed]], connected to top-floor politics inside the [[Desperado Club]] and referenced in relation to secret passages.
• A voice that issues the quest-style command "The mark is dead…" and directs the reward for returning bodies, associated with the [[Guild of Suffering]] and framed as a commanding, serpentine/deific presence.
• Implicated in the murder of [[Astrid]] and described as the husband and a possible member of a guild (tied to the [[Guild of Suffering]]), mentioned in [[Carl]]’s theory about the family.
