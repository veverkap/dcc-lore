# Night Wyrm

# Night Wyrm
Demigod worshipped by the [[Guild of Suffering]]. Not a standard pantheon member; connected to divine-suffering artifacts and the culture that leaves marks like the [[Night Wyrm’s Nasty Little Web of Suffering]].

## Additional Observations
# Night Wyrm
The Night Wyrm is a Samantha-like demigod mentioned as the entity behind the [[Engaged Lock Box of the Night Wyrm]] and connected to the [[Guild of Suffering]]. The box and its tasks are tied to the Night Wyrm's influence.

## Additional Observations
# Night Wyrm
Source of local knowledge/maps referenced by [[dungeon crawler carl]]; provides information about the unknown red-tagged monsters behind the locked door and the failed attempts to clear that room.

## Additional Observations
# Night Wyrm
A disembodied, hissing voice that issues mission directives and rewards. In this chapter the [[Night Wyrm]] praises the party for killing a mark and commands delivery of the body to the guild.

## Additional Observations
# Night Wyrm
A performer in the club who is identified as part of the family dynamic with [[Astrid]], [[Damascus Steel]], and [[Anaconda]]; the Night Wyrm is referenced both as a performer and as having a lair area in the club.

## Additional Observations
# [[Night Wyrm]]
A Samantha-like demigod referenced in the Engaged Lock Box text and the voice behind the Guild Job. The Night Wyrm (or its family) owns the Guild Job and communicates the assassination contract and family-themed rhetoric; tied to the [[Guild of Suffering]].

# Night Wyrm
A source of information on the club map or lore; cited as reporting that nobody knows the nature of the red-tagged monsters behind the sealed door.

A significant named figure referenced as the lair-holder on the top floor and described by Astrid as her husband; Astrid gives his personal name as Hamed. Connected to the top-floor politics inside the [[desperado club]] and referenced in relation to the secret passages.
