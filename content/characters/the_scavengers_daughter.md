# the_scavengers_daughter

* [[The [[Scavenger]]’’s Daughter]] is a deity who awakens and triggers temporary floor-wide benefits, announced by system messages.
* She is a named entity that enhances [[dungeon crawler carl]]'s fire attacks and vinegar-infused potions.
* She is a hostile or fearsome entity within the dungeon narrative, connected to [[dungeon crawler carl]]'s flashback/ dream state.
* She reacts to the strange light and is part of the assembled party observing the beacon.
* She is [[Carl]]'s vessel/homebase that receives a divine proximity buff when [[Yarilo]] comes into range.
* She is [[an]] alias/title of [[Eris]] at the chapter's start, indicating her awakening and surge of power.
* She is a system announcement that triggers when [[dungeon crawler carl]]'s [[Essence Bar]] becomes full, signaling the availability of [[The Daughter’’s Kiss]].
* She is a named deity whose "opening" and "closing" of awareness trigger realm-wide system messages, granting temporary benefits.
* She is a named divine or powerful entity whose effects appear in the interface, providing active buffs.
* She is [[an]] alternate title/manifestation used for [[Lucia]] in the chapter, connected to her sudden, uncanny behavior.
* The [[Scavenger]]'s Daughter is a named crawler/NPC present at the scene, initially described as having eyes remain open and filling with power as events unfold.
* She is [[an]] entity whose soul power lingered within [[dungeon crawler carl]], tied to a whispered need for [[Carl]] to stay alive and sane.
* She is a divine/boon-granting presence referenced directly ("The [[Scavenger]]'s Daughter closes her eyes") and also the namesake for [[Carl]]'s [[Scavenger's Daughter patch]] which alters buffs/debuffs when gods appear.
* She is a named deity/title referenced in the system announcement ("The [[Scavenger]]'s Daughter has opened her eyes") and linked contextually to the deity presence centered on [[Harpocrates]] in the chapter.
* She granted a temporary benefit protecting the player's genitals from being chopped; she looks away from the gore and her granted benefit later fades but becomes permanent for the remainder of the floor.
* She is the child reportedly found and taken by [[Warlord Akuma]]'s team inside the [[Castle of the Semeru]]; cited as a reason the team left the castle.
* She is [[an]] entity reportedly found and taken by other parties; speculated in the scene to possibly be the missing child of [[Samantha]] and relevant to [[Samantha]]'s guilt and motivations.
* She is a named NPC/Crawler referenced as having closed her eyes and lost the benefit that was protecting her; her state affects the players' ability to avoid certain boss attacks.
* She is a named presence at the scene whose eyes remain open and who is implied to be affected by the deity's arrival, mentioned in proximity to the summoning of [[Ysalte]].
* She is a named entity/totem/deity-like presence that interacts with [[dungeon crawler carl]]'s interface: she "has enhanced your fire attack," looks away during the fight, and temporarily enhances vinegar-infused potions.
* She is a named minion/totem referenced by the system announcement; described as being "fed" and having wrath unleashed—implies [[an]] active summoned entity or empowered card.
* She is a deity whose "opening her eyes" grants power and can be activated for effects; tied to [[Carl]]'s [[Scavenger]]'s Daughter patch and referenced as providing deity-level benefits.
* She is a named NPC who briefly "closes her eyes" and loses the benefit protecting others; tied narratively to the events enabling the final attacks by [[Scolopendra]] if she dies or is abandoned.
* She is a named summoned/created entity or weapon referenced by [[an]] automatic notification: "The [[Scavenger]]'s Daughter has been fed. Unleash her wrath." Treated as a named beast/asset that must be activated by the player to use during the race.
* She is a crucial, named NPC referenced as central to any plan for keeping denizens of the [[Pineapple Cabaret]] safe and enabling escape to the planet's surface; her father is also noted as important to the scheme.
* She is a deity referenced in-chapter when the narrator is told "You are in the presence of a deity." [[Scavenger's Daughter]] is described as having opened her eyes and filling with power, establishing divine presence on the floor.
* She is a mythic offspring referenced in multiple versions of the [[Scavenger]] storyline; described as [[an]] entity with the power to erase and replace the current pantheon by taking gods' powers for herself.
