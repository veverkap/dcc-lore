# low_thi

• Named crawler (Level 25) who fatally stabs [[Wynne]] and earns the achievement "Cockblock" for interrupting [[dungeon crawler carl]]'s quest to free the gnome.
• Member of the tomb raider group who volunteers as "pterodactyl duty" to distract the boss [[Quetzalcoatlus]] while others complete the connector objective.
• Crawler (identified as Crawler #4,778,551, Level 25) who destroys the catacomb map and kills [[Wynne]], accidentally triggering a Quest failure and earning the in-game achievement “Cockblock.”
