# drek

• Small, level-6 demonic baby mobs that swarm platforms and attempt to board cars on the [[Red Line]], characterized by pack behavior, climbing pillars, and biting.
• Found at [[Sirin Station - 81]], they are a primary mass-threat in the Red Line system.
• Can latch onto victims and are a key threat at [[Sirin Station (81)]].
• Named hostile, referred to as one of the creepy babies on the train, and representative of the dangerous mobs on transit lines.
