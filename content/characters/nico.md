# [[Nico]]
A level 91 Tigran racer associated with [[One Fine Pig]]. Presented as a muscular tiger-person with strong emotional attachment to his partner(s) and protective of [[Penelope]]. Engaged in discussion with [[Genesis]] and [[Rapture]] at the hilltop.

## Additional Observations
