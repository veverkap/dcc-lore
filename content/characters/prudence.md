* Mother of two bear cubs, including [[Randy and Todd]], present in the guildhall and saferoom.
* Named NPC referenced in [[Samantha]]'s offhand threat and part of the group's social dynamics.
* Deceased character who described the intended "Water Ballad" performance for [[Big Tina]].
* Creature/NPC with cubs given access to the guildhall for safety by [[dungeon crawler carl]].
* Bear NPC who sits with her cubs during the group's stop and tends to [[Samantha]] in her lap.
* Source of commentary about the wand carried by [[Tina]] and implied to have knowledge about its abilities.
* Mother of the two bear cubs, found passed out and snoring in the guildhall.
* Bear cub or associated creature under the team's protection during the conflict.
