# engineer

• Anonymous operator of the engine/engine-train, never seen by [[Vernon]] or the crew.
• Unnamed operator of the engine car on the [[Nightmare]] Express.
• Unseen, locked engineer NPC who occupies the [[Engine Train]].
• Requires [[an]] engineer's key to access the engine car.
• Possibly non-looping and central to train mystery lore.
• Players cannot easily contact or access the engineer.
