# Engineer
Anonymous operator of the engine/engine-train. Never seen by [[Vernon]] or the crew; the engine car is heavily locked and requires an engineer’s key. Mentioned as a persistent but mysterious presence.

## Additional Observations
# Engineer
The unnamed operator of the engine car on the Nightmare Express. The [[Engineer]] is mentioned as unseen and inaccessible (engine car locked with an engineer’s key), possibly non-looping, and central to train mystery lore; players cannot easily contact or access the engineer.
