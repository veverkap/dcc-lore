# pox_slug

• Small slug creature spawned when a boil from [[SlugPox]] erupts, begins at Level 1 and grows in level with subsequent bursts; initially non-hostile to players but aggressive and disgusting in nature.
• A small monster produced when [[SlugPox]] boils erupt, scales in level over time, and can latch onto targets; considered a parasitic minion.
