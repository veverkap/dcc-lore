# robot_donut

• A robotic companion, sometimes called "[[Princess]] [[Donut]] the Queen Anne Chonk", owned by [[dungeon crawler carl]].
• Modeled as both a real crawler device and a marketed toy on [[Veriluxx New Toys Showcase]].
• A previously owned or referenced robotic companion mentioned as a missing asset the team cannot use for their current disguise or plan.
• [[Carl]]'s popular robotic pet/streaming personality, central to the commercial appearance on [[Veriluxx New Toys Showcase]] and the target of a toy replica (the Veriluxx [[Donut]] doll).
• Provides comic relief, interacts with the hosts [[Gravo]] and [[Liddi]], and is present during the trailer explosion.
