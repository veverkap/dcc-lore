# popov_brothers

* [[Popov brothers]] are a duo of named players referenced by [[Odette]] as part of inter-team conflicts on previous floors.
* They are named entertainers/figures mentioned in CrawlCon autograph sessions as VIP duo partners for limited likeness sessions with [[dungeon crawler carl]].
* They are referenced as nodlings, a multi-headed race, with two heads each, mentioned by [[Donut]] and [[dungeon crawler carl]] in comparison to [[nodling]] biology.
* They are a pair of popular [[crawlers]] often treated as two individuals for appearances and events, mentioned as heavy participants at the con, sharing autograph duties with the protagonist.
* They are a pair of [[crawlers]] who died, were transformed into babies, and subsequently removed from the game; later reported to be okay by [[Zev]].
* They are a pair of sibling [[crawlers]] frequently scheduled for many events at CrawlCon, noted as effectively being treated as two panelists.
* They are a scouting group referenced as having scouted another area, described as being too far south to join the current action in time.
* They are a pair of [[crawlers]] who died at the Masquerade and subsequently transformed into babies and were expelled from the game; later reported to be okay by [[Zev]].
* They are a pair of characters previously encountered in the room (their playing cards remain).
* They are a [[Nodling]] duo ([[Illusionist]] and Bogatyr) who appear on the top-ten leaderboard and in the recap footage.
* They are a two-headed [[Nodling]] duo (Visionary and Bogatyr) featured in the recap footage fighting another [[human]]; shown as a combined top-ten entry.
* They are a paired crawler entry on the leaderboard ("[[Dmitri]] and [[Maxim]] Popov – [[Nodling]] – [[Illusionist]] and Bogatyr – Level 30") listed in the broadcast.
* They are a two-headed [[Nodling]] duo represented as a single top-ten entry; they appear on the leaderboard and are shown aboard a pirate ship in the recap footage.
* They are twin-headed [[Nodling]] [[crawlers]] who specialize and appear in the top-ten; displayed on the recap broadcast fighting a leather-clad [[human]]; one half becomes a Visionary and the other a Bogatyr.
* They are referred to collectively as the Popovs; a two-headed ogre pair who protect and stand over [[dungeon crawler carl]] during a mass heal. Both are fatally impaled by a flaming sambhur mount during the chaos.
* They are a family/ group of [[crawlers]] who enter the ballroom with wide eyes and join the crowd; part of the supporting attendees and allies around [[dungeon crawler carl]].
* They are a family/ group of [[crawlers]] present and participating in the ballroom banter and planning, seen reacting during the confrontation with [[Sir Ferdinand]].
* [[Dmitri]] Popov is a crawler who received the Enchanted Stick of [[Cascadia]]'s Screams and was eager to meet [[Donut]]; did not receive a notice of [[an]] appeal on his gift.
* [[Dmitri]] Popov is one half of the Popov brothers; the more outgoing twin who happily signs autographs and jokes with fans.
