# warlord_empress_dnadia_of_the_prism

• Powerful leader of the [[Prism]] who proposed multiple [[Emergency Action Item]]s targeting [[crawlers]], the [[Princess Posse]], and the NPC population on [[Larracos]].
• Saccathian ruler with tentacles, multiple built-in shields, and water/ice-themed magics.
• Incapacitated and keeps her health artificially lowered to deny certain effects.
• Target of [[Carl]]'s marking and interrogation.
• Proposed multiple high-impact [[Action Item]]s, including [[Emergency Action Item]]s, aimed at deleting [[NPCs]], disallowing [[crawlers]], and removing the [[Princess Posse]], which were all rejected by the system.
