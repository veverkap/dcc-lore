# prince_maestro

* Prince [[Maestro]], son of [[King Rust]], focus of controversy, and former Skull Empire royal.
* Disowned and killed by [[valtay]] in retaliation for a failed assassination attempt.
* Former member of the Skull Empire line of succession.
* Killed by [[valtay]] in actions tied to the [[valtay]].
* Controlled [[Grull]] during a prior encounter.
* Sponsor of [[Maggie My]] and implicated in courtly attempts to eliminate [[Carl]].
* Provided the [[Celestial Grenade]] used in this chapter.
* Example of a franchisee tied through corporate loopholes to larger powers (the [[skull clan]]).
* Target in the benefactor/bolt backstory; his body was inhabited by [[Grull]] at one point.
* Disgraced former prince of the [[skull clan]] previously involved in high-profile media and spectacle.
* Deceased member of the Skull Empire royalty referenced in relation to [[princess [[formidable]] of the skull empire]] and the family's internal power dynamics.
* Formerly second in line for the Skull Empire's succession who was disowned and killed by the [[valtay]] in retaliation for a failed assassination that killed [[Manasa]].
* Member of the Skull Empire's ruling family invoked as a sponsor tied to [[Maggie My]] (and indirectly to [[Chris Andrews 2]]).
* Named sponsor/gift-giver: credited with providing the celestial grenade used in the chapter (originally intended as a sponsor prize).
* Crawler mentioned as [[an]] example of franchise loopholes and acquisitions; noted as having been franchised to [[an]] entity later acquired by the [[skull clan]], illustrating corruptible ownership structures in the franchise system described by [[Zev]].
* Named orc example used to describe the type of orc kin to [[Baroness Victory]] and other orc characters referenced in the chapter.
* Member of the Skull Empire who at one point inhabits the body of [[Grull]]; a named noble connected to the bolting/bolt narrative.
* Royal relation of [[Baroness Victory]] referenced to contextualize her background; part of the larger nobility tied to the [[Princess]] Posse.
* Named prince whose body was occupied by [[Grull]]; associated with the plot threads around the bolt and the Skull Empire.
