# Krakaren Juvenile Clonelings

# Krakaren Juvenile Clonelings
Small, tentacled octopus-like mobs (level 14–17 initially, later seen as level 20–23 and human-sized) whose blood is caustic and whose tentacles burn on touch. Central antagonists pouring into stations and the breach that [[dungeon crawler carl]] plugs with an [[Interdiction Cart (Def Leppard)]].

## Additional Observations
# Krakaren Juvenile Clonelings
Monkey-sized, tentacled stage monsters (level 14–17 early, later up-leveled to ~20–23) that spawn from stage-three DTs; their blood is caustic, tentacles burn on touch, and they scream. Primary invaders funneled into the [[Portal to the Abyss]] at [[Station 36]].

## Additional Observations
# Krakaren Juvenile Clonelings
Named juvenile Krakaren mobs (level 14–17) that are monkey-sized, tentacled, tentacle-burning, acid-blooded creatures who were prevalent around station 24.
