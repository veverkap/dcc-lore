# krakaren_juvenile_clonelings

• Small, tentacled octopus-like mobs (level 14–17 initially, later seen as level 20–23 and [[human]]-sized) whose blood is caustic and whose tentacles burn on touch.
• Monkey-ized, tentacled stage monsters (level 14–17 early, later up-leveled to 20–23) that spawn from stage-three DTs; their blood is caustic, tentacles burn on touch, and they scream.
• Named juvenile [[Krakaren]] mobs (level 14–17) that are monkey-ized, tentacled, tentacle-burning, acid-blooded creatures.
