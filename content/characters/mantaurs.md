# mantaurs

• [[ManTauRs]]: A referenced worker group/species mentioned by [[Madison]] as misbehaving, tied to the Iron Tangle employee population and social dynamics.
• Large, double-stacked humanoid beasts ([[mantaur]] creatures) encountered guarding the employee line, capable of summoning or triggering deities when mortally triggered.
• Large, double-stacked boss-type [[NPCs]] ([[mantaur]] creatures) encountered guarding the employee line entrance, hostile, multi-limbed, and able to be baited into [[portal]] teleports when sufficiently weakened.
• Boss creatures that served as summoning vessels for [[Grull]], killing them granted bronze boss boxes to party members.
• Bipedal [[mantaur]] enemies encountered guarding the employee line, two are baited/attacked to trigger a summoning event; one is used to initiate the involuntary summoning that calls [[Grull]] (via the [[mantaur]]'s timed invocation).
