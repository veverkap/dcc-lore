# dwight

• Named Sparkling Unicorn and one member of [[Team Sparkles]] (Level 87 Enchanter), rides the [[Avernus Creeper]] mount, and has a surly, aggressive personality.
• Driver/pilot of [[Team Sparkles]], arrives in a vine vehicle with a cannon, and taunts [[dungeon crawler carl]]'s team after the finish.
• Partners with [[Lucienne]] and rides the [[Avernus Creeper]] mount in the race.
• Engaged in a verbal altercation with the guck elemental [[Masterplan]].
• Has a valuable horn and [[an]] atypical unicorn personality.
• A unicorn spectator/participant visible on the hill who cheers when the opposing kangaroo explodes.
• Screams down at the [[Dominators]] alongside [[Lucienne]], contributing to the chaotic atmosphere before the race kickoff.
• A rival or problematic creature who is intended to be slowed down in the race.
• A competitor being pursued by [[Florin]] and considered likely to take a different route than the narrator's group.
• A racer mentioned in passing; [[Carl]]'s group did not stay to see Dwight cross the finish line.
• Violently stabs [[the DJ]] in the [[face]] with his horn at the climax of the scene.
• Drunken bar patron who accuses [[Carl]] and [[Donut]] of killing [[Lucienne]], throws a corpse-[[gremlin]] that inadvertently hits [[Yuto]], and becomes a focal point in the melee.
• [[An]] unconscious party member who was dumped in front of his garage but expected to awaken shortly.
• A racer who fails to move at the starting line and is noticed by [[Donut]] during the opening scramble.
• Driver associated with team [[Free Love]]; faces complications after the death of [[Lucienne]] and uncertain ability to hire a mercenary for the race.
• A lone racer seen sitting in his vine near the starting area.
• A vehicle (or vehicle construct) with a noted "self-driving upgrade" that allows it to float down in sync with the racers.
• A crawler briefly seen sleeping in a vine and later occupying spot number one; noted by [[Donut]] as having two garage keys.
• Racer whose vine pulls a wheeled cart and who has attached a spinning missile launcher; antagonistic toward [[Carl]]'s team and uses a GPS avatar modeled after [[Lucienne]].
• A crawler who deploys a giant cannon mounted on a vine and a missile cart; his offensive setup is neutralized early in the bout and he is killed when [[Donut]] severs his head.
• Character from whom [[Donut]] looted the remaining two keys; mentioned in relation to party inventory and garage access.
• Individual from whom [[Carl]]'s group looted the massive magic missile launcher that is later mounted on the truck.
