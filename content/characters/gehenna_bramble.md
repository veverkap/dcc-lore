# gehenna_bramble

• [[Gehenna Bramble]] is a spell-effect/rule cast as punishment for failing the world quest, spreading outward from Odious Creeper locations.
• It transforms plants into 10-meter-high, thorn-covered bramble walls that boil victims' blood on contact.
• The [[brambles]] cannot be poisoned or permanently killed and will avoid stairwells.
• They are aggressive and can overtake areas on a timer, described as unusually violent in some chapters.
• The [[brambles]] spread outward at [[an]] approximate rate of 4.5 km/h, transforming plants into animated walls with thorns that cause instant blood boiling and explosion.
• They cannot be poisoned or directly countered and avoid stairwells by 5 meters.
