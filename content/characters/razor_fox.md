# razor_fox

• A specific mob that appears in this chapter, afflicted by the [[DTs]] (stage three), with a transformed form as a hippo-sized tentacled monstrosity.
• A named creature encountered prior to entering the portal, mentioned alongside the [[bugbears]] as part of the group's recent departure.
• A named mob entry encountered in DT stage three, displayed as "Razor Fox. Level 22. Warning: This mob is suffering from the DTs. It is in stage three of three."
• The DT-altered form is a hippo-sized tentacled monstrosity with a round mouth, and killing it can produce hazardous effects.
• A named creature type mentioned alongside [[Bugbears]] as part of the party's earlier encounters before entering [[Hungry Eyes Vendor Village]].
