# prison_pocket

• A Level 170 province boss, a giant marsupial associated with the species [[Macropus Dominus]], subject to the boss gate mechanic [[Your vehicle may not pass past this point until this boss monster is dead or removed.]].
• Speaks with a fake Australian accent, swallows [[NPCs]]/[[gremlins]] in his pouch to heal, and is the source of the [[Enchanted Juvenile Macropus Dominus Skin]] when [[an]] [[engineer]] corpse is recovered.
• A named boss introduced as a giant, fur-covered marsupial (scientific/name tag shown as Macropus Dominus), blocking racers with a pouch full of small, mostly-dead joeys and secreting a caustic pouch oil.
• Heals when it consumes wounded creatures from its pouch and is paired across the road with the boss [[Party Foul]].
• Drops the [[Enchanted Juvenile Macropus Dominus Skin]].
• Fought during the race, with [[dungeon crawler carl]] credited with dealing 95% of the damage, and the team receiving Platinum Boss Boxes for the kill.
• One of the obstacles on the road, referred to as a demon kangaroo boss that eats [[gremlins]]/joeys to heal, has a pouch full of goo and corpses, and is the primary target of the party's infiltration and sabotage.
