# geyrun

* Named as the master associated with [[Orthrus]], implicated in [[Orthrus]]'s backstory and connection to the godly hierarchy on the floor.
* Son of [[Emberus]] who was murdered; his death is the subject of a god-quest given to adherents of [[Emberus]].
* Deceased god whose murder is the focus of [[an]] [[Emberus]] quest; former owner of the two-headed puppy [[Orthrus]], and central to [[dungeon crawler carl]]'s investigatory tasks.
* Murdered son of [[Emberus]] whose death is central to the feud between [[Emberus]] and [[Hellik]], mentioned in [[Hellik]]'s speech.
* Entity referenced in divine correspondence and intelligence trade; mentioned as [[an]] object of information in godly negotiations.
* Named individual whom [[Samantha]] mentions possibly being indirectly or directly responsible for killing, referenced in her confession.
* The murdered son of [[Emberus]] whose death is the ostensible cause of [[Emberus]]' wrath; his killing is the central mystery referenced by the quest update [[Find Out Who Killed My Son]].
* Owner of the two-headed puppy [[Orthrus]]; his death drives the investigation players must undertake.
* Referenced as the murdered son in the chapter's quest update ("Find out who killed my son"); the quest invites questioning [[Amayon]] about [[Geyrun]]'s death.
