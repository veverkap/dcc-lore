# hellspawn_familiar_goat

* Companion/monster unleashed by [[Prepotente]] inside the Club Vanquisher
* Devoured clerics and triggered a "Holy Crusade Bounty"
* The Hellspawn is a terrifying, late-stage transformed goat familiar that is horse-sized, multi-breasted, pitch-black with glowing red eyes and steam.
* A large, black-flamed transformed goat-liked mount associated with the goat group, described as a horse-sized, heat-crackling creature that signals the group's dangerous nature.
* Covered in phantom black flames and multiple [[human]]-like breasts, affiliated with the goat/transformed group seen across the [[noodle]].
