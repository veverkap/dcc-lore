# juvenile_octo-shark

• Minion mob of [[Lusca]], described as lamprey-like with tentacles, labeled as "Juvenile Octo-Shark. Level 30."
• Spawn in thousands and engage in massive litters, leading to cannibalistic feeding frenzies.
• Exploited by [[Carl]] using a [[Potion of Bloodlust]].
