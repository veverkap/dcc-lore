# zeynep

• A level-52 "Boring Ol' Mage" crawler who intervenes with a Mute spell to stop [[Emir]] from attacking [[Carl]] and helps de-escalate the situation.
• A level 58 crawler whose corpse is present inside the [[Madness castle]] operating theater.
• A crawler found dead and grotesquely operated on at one of the surgical tables in the [[Surgical Theater of Architect Houston]].
• A level-52 player crawler who is part of a small crawler group on the dance floor that [[dungeon crawler carl]] asks to leave for safety.
