# burcu

• A level-59, badger-headed [[Porsuk]] and Swashbuckler who appeared in the top ten list.
• Previously encountered by [[dungeon crawler carl]] during the [[Butcher's Masquerade]] and present in his chat history.
• Known from prior events (Butcher’'s Masquerade) and appears on the top list of [[crawlers]]; part of the social web around the characters.
• Crawler whose whole team appears to have been absorbed by [[madness]] according to the [[Find Crawler]] skill; currently unresponsive to messages.
• Present in the operating theater with [[Architect Houston]]; contributed [[an]] entry to the [[Book of Voodoo]] and was sketching fireball trap placements.
• Tortured, tongue/voice box cut, bound by magical restraints (switch-controlled), unable to cast until freed; briefly knocked unconscious by a [[Wand of Nighty-Night]] used by [[dungeon crawler carl]].
• Level-59 newcomer on the leaderboard with a badger-headed [[Porsuk]] race appearance and the [[Swashbuckler]] class.
• Previously encountered by [[Carl]] at the Butcher'’s Masquerade and involved in a fight after [[Imogen]]'’s death.
• Known to [[dungeon crawler carl]] by reputation; referenced as a potential avenger of [[Emir Akbas]].
• A now-deceased crawler who had accused [[Carl]] of killing her father, mentioned as part of lingering mysteries.
