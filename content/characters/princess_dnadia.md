# princess_dnadia

* [[Princess D'Nadia]] is a Saccathian (tentacled, squid-faced) guest from the [[prism]] who regularly appears on [[Danger Zone with [[Ripper]] Wonton]]. She publicly mourns [[Manasa]] after the attack and leads vocal condemnation on behalf of her realm.
* She is a sponsor figure who gifted a [[Benefactor Box]] to [[Donut]] and signed the unique [[Prism Industries Capacitating and Focusing Goggles. Special High-Fashion Edition. "The [[Princess]] [[Donut]]."]] to him.
* She provided [[Donut]] with sunglasses that revealed threat information about [[Hekla]] and sent benefactor boxes to [[crawlers]] like [[Donut]] and [[dungeon crawler carl]]'s group.
* She is a named benefactor who sent [[an]] incremental upgrade to [[Donut]]'s sunglasses and is associated with the [[Prism]] Kingdom.
* She is a high-profile sponsor or personality referenced as sponsoring multiple [[crawlers]] and is used in conversations to indicate prestige of certain sponsors.
* She is a leader of the rival faction "[[Prism]]" and a prize bidder in [[Faction Wars]].
* She is a tentacled royal from the [[Prism]] Kingdom and a member of the [[Saccathians]] (Sacs).
* She is a close friend of [[Manasa]] and reacts emotionally to her death during the broadcast.
* She is a named NPC/sponsor figure who provided loot that enabled [[Donut]] to acquire sunglasses instrumental to in-game discoveries.
* She is a minor but plot-relevant social contact who gifted sunglasses to [[Donut]] that helped reveal hostile intents.
* She is a named aristocrat referenced by [[Dr. Hu]] and [[Porthus]] as another unstable political actor whose behavior contributes to the chaotic backdrop of the season.
