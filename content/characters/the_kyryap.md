# the_kyryap

* Named source of a level 13 snare trap encountered in the hallway; referenced as the set designer/author of that trap.
* The Kyryap is a named claiming entity referenced by the [[Tracking Tag]]'s message, presenting [[an]] external threat tied to tracking/assassination mechanics.
* The Kyryap is a named source of a level 13 snare trap encountered in the club hallway, responsible for trap-setting within the dungeon.
* The Kyryap is a hostile entity that "has claimed you" via a [[Tracking Tag]], implying a threat that may persist until floor collapse.
* The Kyryap is one of the powerful dungeon entities present in the ongoing slaughter at Club [[Scolopendra]] after the breach of [[the Nothing]].
