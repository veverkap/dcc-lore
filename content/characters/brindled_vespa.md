# brindled_vespa

• A massive hornet-like mob that bursts from a [[grub]] pupa in the hallway encounter, described as having arms and clawed fingers that spits corrosive white goo.
• Central antagonist in the frozen combat clip shown during [[Death Watch]].
• Population was halved and acid damage was reduced by a [[System Announcement]].
• Fought by [[Lucia Mar]] in the broadcast.
