# pollyslog

• Level 22 monstrous fish-like creature with large, acid-secerting fingers, described as strong and bulky.
• Interacts with train doors leading to dismemberment hazards.
• Large, green- skinned, fish-liked monster species with [[milk]]-white eyes and acid-secerting fingers, described as strong and not very bright.
• Reacts violently if prevented from boarding.
• Large, green, fish-liked monster (Level 22) that secretes acid from its fingers, encountered at a platform and interacts violently with the [[Gangway Chock]] and doors.
