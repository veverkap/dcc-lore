# crawler_paz_lo

* A Crawler credited by a system message with slaying the goddess [[Ysalte]], triggering global consequences and earning a divine "marked for death" status from other deities.
* Crawler #8,199,454, Level 45 [[human]] [[Santero]] who worships [[Ogun]] and wears glowing silver plate armor, appearing nervous in the encounter.
* Slayer of [[Ysalte]], triggering global deity reactions and loot generation, and marked for death by deities as a consequence.
* Former crawler turned into a god-like card, possessing upgraded equipment, and referred to as "The God Predator".
* Card ([[Paz]]) was in the party's possession and was ultimately ripped by [[Donut]], removing the card from play.
* Level 101 former crawler, bound to a card, and considered morally compromised after Sister Ines' actions.
* Card was looted after the goddess' death, and the party ripped it to prevent further harm.
* [[Paz]] is [[an]] armored [[Santero]], a necromancer-cleric hybrid and tank, who uses strong healing/protection spells and a signature defensive ability called [[Turtle]] to draw aggro.
* He is a paladin member of [[Carl]]'s party, drawing undead aggro with his holy armor and receiving temporary strength boosts as unsettled are killed.
* Paz is a local or party member directly addressed by [[Asojano]], who identifies him as a "[[Santero]]" and asks him to repair the shrine in the center of town.
* He is a crawler and ally in the group who helps attempt shrine repair and defends the door, suffering from the smiting-like debuff (flaming anvil marker) and is told to seek the All Healer inside the club after infection.
* Paz is a character who was grievously injured by events, a former worshipper of [[Ogun]] who had his healing/benefit removed and then was permanently debuffed by the deity.
* He is a crawler who has been transformed into a card (implied), referenced by [[dungeon crawler carl]] and [[Donut]] with concern over his fate after being turned into a card by [[an]] unknown hand.
* Paz is a character referenced in group history, imprisoned earlier and tied to events the party is dealing with (mentioned during debrief and relating to past murders).
* He is a victim referenced in accusations against the [[catgirl]], with a [[Paz Card]] item tied to this character and associated grievances.
* Paz is a source of information about crab sizes, referenced by [[dungeon crawler carl]] when comparing the migrating [[crabs]].
* He is a crawler who was stabbed with a team flag by [[Sister Ines]], mentioned as part of recent traumatic events.
* Paz is [[an]] armored totem summoned by [[Sister Ines]], recognizable by a hand-shaped burn on his [[face]] inflicted by the god [[Ogun]].
* He is a man known to [[Ren]] and mentioned in passing, with [[Ren]] asking [[dungeon crawler carl]] to make sure [[Sister Ines]] doesn't go down the stairs so as not to lose Paz's card.
* Paz is a being (a weird version is summoned by the "goddess lady" in this chapter), captured temporarily by [[Katia]] in a shield jail thing.
* He is a figure who ended up as a card and who killed the god [[Ysalte]] during the [[Floor 8]] events.
* Paz is a crawler mentioned in passing in relation to [[Katia]] and the killing of a goddess, part of the backstory that impacts NPC reactions such as [[Arief]]'s despondency.
* He is a crawler who killed the goddess [[Ysalte]] during the prior floor's conflict, mentioned in the context of that fight.
* Paz is a paladin-class crawler whose presence draws undead aggro (noted as aggravated by the presence of a [[Santero]]).
* He is a teammate confronted by [[Asojano]] and asked to repair the shrine, hesitant and central to the negotiation about helping the Orisha.
