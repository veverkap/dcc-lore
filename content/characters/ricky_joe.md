# ricky_joe

• A juvenile Fathom dwarf (level 2), son of [[Eunice]], who had his arm bitten off by [[Mongo]] and was rescued by [[dungeon crawler carl]].
• Holds the dead salamander [[Benjamin]] on a stick.
• One of [[Eunice]]'s children.
• Mentioned as a one-armed son of [[Eunice]].
• Claimed a female [[Skyfowl]] was visiting the city [[elves]], prompting suspicion that [[Miss Quill]] might be involved in the plot.
