# arief

• War Leader Arief, a Level 80 [[Semeru]] Dwarf, commander of the [[larracos]], greets [[dungeon crawler carl]] and [[Donut]] and requests diplomatic rituals.
• Arief is [[an]] awakened NPC dwarf who guides the protagonists through [[Larracos]], providing exposition about the city's ringed levels and local history.
• He acts as a scout/companion during the descent and shares looks and impressions with [[dungeon crawler carl]].
• Arief guards the door during the meeting and is a guild member who guards the door during the [[changeling]] small wedding.
• He is the controller of the giant [[robots]]/diggers when [[Juice Box]] is away, responsible for operating the mech assets under the [[Dream castle]].
• [[Arief]] is mentioned as having [[an]] uncle on the Jatismara war council.
