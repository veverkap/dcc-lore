# shock_chomper

• A level 20 snake-liked, red-maned mob (described as "lesser [[nagas]] with mommy issues") encountered on the [[Nightmare Express]] and [[Yellow Line]] trains, with [[an]] electrical attack that can be interrupted by melee hits.
• Drops fangs and small amounts of gold when looted.
• Found on trains, can be interrupted by melee strikes.
