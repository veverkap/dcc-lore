# onikuma

• A named bear mount ridden by [[Rosetta]] that performs attacks in the arena, leaping onto groups of mobs.
• Has a never-ending supply of fleas, mentioned by [[Chiyome]].
• Was splattered with goo during the race, cited by [[Chiyome]]'s report.
• Can be ridden by [[Rosetta]] to jump and attack groups of razor-backed monkey mobs.
• Associated with the dead razor foxes; briefly crossed the finish line alive and then blinked out, suggesting mounts can survive separate from riders.
• A desirable mount option, referenced by [[Hedy]] as a potential acquisition if available in cul-de-[[sac]] houses.
• A bear mount in [[Carl]]'s garage that snuffles on the ground and interacts with pets like [[Rend]] and [[Mongo]] during the staging.
• A giant bear mount ridden by [[Rosetta]] during the procession.
