# henrik_the_changeling

• Juice Box's brother and a desperate Principal who is hunting [[Quetzalcoatlus]] and one holder of a piece needed to assemble [[Gate of the Feral Gods]].
• Accused of torturing a gnome to further his goals.
• Named as Juice Box's brother and a desperate Principal who sought to forcibly use [[Wynne]] to render [[Quetzalcoatlus]] corporeal.
• Possesses one piece of the three-part artifact that can open [[Gate of the Feral Gods]].
