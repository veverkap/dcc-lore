# vrah

Here is the consolidated list of unique factual points about Vrah:

• Vrah is a mantis hunter mentioned by [[Ian]] as a recurring, lethal hunter on the hunting floors.
• Vrah is a mantis-liked alien who led [[an]] aerial unit that decapitated [[crawlers]] on a trail and displayed brutal looting behavior.
• Vrah is a hunter connected to the mantis hive and is the sibling of [[Xindy]].
• Vrah is a notorious hunter who decapitates victims and whose presence poses a major threat to the crawler [[Carl]].
• Vrah is a named mantid or mantid-affiliated hunter who possesses [[an]] Antidote Ring and specific resistances, making her difficult to defeat by poisoning.
• Vrah is a leader of a mantis hunting team, large and powerful, equipped with a crossbow and a grotesque carapace decorated with attached heads.
• Vrah is [[an]] enemy/hunter referenced in relation to the fight, and [[Carl]] received [[an]] achievement for shoving [[an]] arrow into Vrah during conflict.
• Vrah is a large mantis female who storms the hallway and seeks [[Edict]].
• Vrah is a character currently infected and deciding whether to attempt to ride out the infection or cut it away.
• Vrah is a named individual used as a point of comparison in dialogue, indicating a known figure within [[Carl]]'s social milieu.
• Vrah is a hunter who managed to summon [[Emberus]] to Zockau early in the floor, enabling some local [[hunters]] and guards to worship the god.
• Vrah is a mantis-liked hunter who enters the ballroom during the masquerade, drawing attention and foreshadowing conflict between [[hunters]] and [[crawlers]].
• Vrah is a giant mantis-liked crawler encountered in the ballroom, who confronts [[Carl]] and threatens revenge on him and his friends.
• Vrah is a hunter who acts as [[an]] enforcer in the ballroom, shouting at other [[hunters]] and enforcing order around the prize event and protection seal.
• Vrah is a cured crawler linked to the birthing incident, present among the crowd and subject of crabbed comments about lineage.
• Vrah is a hunter moving among the crowd and later seen approaching the prize counter, part of the hunter contingent creating pressure on the [[crawlers]].
• Vrah is a leader of a group of [[hunters]] coordinating [[an]] alternate plan, working with her mother and having agents inside the castle.
• Vrah is a named bug/ mark target in the ballroom fight, severely wounded and ultimately finished off by [[Mongo]] and [[Kiwi]].
• Vrah is a bug-lady hunter who sought vengeance after her sister was killed, defeated on the Butcher's Masquerade by [[Mongo]] and [[Kiwi]].
