# bactrians

• A named race/faction referenced in the context of losing collateral and having their town bombed by [[Gnomes]].
• Mentioned as part of the geopolitical/dungeon ecosystem.
• A named race/faction mentioned as being in a three-way stalemate with the [[Dromedarians]] and the [[Gnomes]] atop the Necropolis of [[Anser]].
• Named enslaved group referenced by [[Crixus]] as builders of the tomb who were subjugated by [[Anser]]; part of the region's historical lore.
