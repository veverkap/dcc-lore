# pearson

• Pearson is a [[changeling]] who helps [[Carl]] and the [[crawlers]] hold a door against [[crocodilian]] guards, acts as a liaison for evacuation plans, and carries a hand-drawn map for coordination.
• Pearson is a [[changeling]] who impersonates [[Signet]] to gauge the castle guards' responses and leverage [[an]] expectation that [[queen [[imogen]] of the high elves]] will want to deal with [[Signet]] personally.
• Pearson is a [[changeling]] who appears similar to [[Signet]] (robed woman form) for distraction and possible physical contact by [[queen [[imogen]] of the high elves]].
• Pearson is a [[changeling]] who is instructed to maintain a particular [[human]] form and is warned away from the castle by elf guards.
• Pearson is a [[changeling]] who is formerly disguised as a [[dromedarian]] bartender and currently in cretin form while helping hold a door, coordinating evacuees, and acting as [[an]] intermediary between [[Carl]] and trapped townspeople.
