# Pearson
Pearson is a changeling (previously disguised as a dromedarian bartender) who helps Carl and the crawlers hold a door against crocodilian guards. Acts as a liaison to mobilize town residents for evacuation plans and carries a hand-drawn map for coordination.

## Additional Observations
# Pearson
A changeling who is impersonating [[Signet]] as part of the group’s deception; the guards hesitate upon seeing the changeling.

## Additional Observations
# Pearson
A changeling allied with the crawlers who is impersonating another character to draw attention. [[Pearson]] is ordered to maintain a particular human form and is warned away from the castle by elf guards.
