# meatus

• A gross, titular godlike entity described as a giant penis; fought during the events and was ultimately smooshed through the [[Li [[Na]] chain]].
• Meatus is referenced as a god whose smoky image forms from the [[Celestial Godslayer Box]] before dissipating; the appearance affects the party ([[Rosetta]] notes a lingering taste) but no physical reward emerges.
