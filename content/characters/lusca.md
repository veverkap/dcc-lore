# lusca

• Octo-Shark Brood Mother Queen, the Level 82 City Boss, attacks and swallows the submarine [[Akula]] during the chapter.
• Described as a massive sharktopus mouthbrooder that gives her babies about ten minutes to feed, triggering a countdown mechanic [[Ten-Minute Timer]] on-screen.
• Central antagonist of the streamed [[Special Event: Boss Battle Stream (Beta Opt-in)]].
• City boss: a colossal octo-shark god (feral god) whose mouth holds thousands of Juvenile Octo-Sharks.
• The party wounds and ultimately fatally cleaves through her brain region during the sequence, triggering major world effects when released into the realm.
• Swallows the [[Akula]] and is grievously wounded by [[Carl]]'s [[Protective Shell]] spell but not immediately slain.
• Named as [[an]] octo-shark creature speculated to be the cheat/fast-win method for the [[Akula]] quadrant.
