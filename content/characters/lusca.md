# Lusca

# Lusca
Octo-Shark Brood Mother Queen, the Level 82 City Boss that attacks and swallows the submarine [[Akula]] during the chapter. Described as a massive sharktopus mouthbrooder that gives her babies about ten minutes to feed, triggering a countdown mechanic ([[Ten-Minute Timer]]) on-screen. Central antagonist of the streamed [[Special Event: Boss Battle Stream (Beta Opt-in)]].
