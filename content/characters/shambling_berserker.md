# shambling_berserker

• A Level 12 undead abomination built from sewn-together body parts, quadrupling in power in darkness, slow until enraged, and killed by [[Mongo]] and the party when daylight returned.
• Also referred to as a "Shrilling" or "mini-Grinder", a mindless forest undead used as fodder in the mass outbreak described in [[The Creeping Apocalypse]], numerous and variable in size, less threatening individually but dangerous in quantity.
• A level-12 undead neighborhood boss composed of sewn-together body parts, described in the chapter's bestiary-style entry and noted as especially dangerous in darkness.
• A horrific monster type formed when vampire victims' body parts meld together rather than reuniting into a single corpse, presented as a dangerous possible outcome when glamoured fragments reassemble poorly.
