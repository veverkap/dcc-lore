# Homecoming Queen
A massive former garbage freighter (owned by [[Long Haul Biological Waste Management Solutions]]) repurposed by Crests to smuggle fifty thousand troopers and mercenaries into the on-boarding flow. Acts as a mobile staging area in orbit for the Princess Posse and allied teams.
