# homecoming_queen

• A massive former garbage freighter owned by [[Long Haul Biological Waste Management Solutions]], repurposed to smuggle 50,000 troopers and [[mercenaries]] into the on-boarding flow.
• Acts as a mobile staging area in orbit for the [[Princess]] Posse and allied teams.
• Heavily armed with secret armament, including enhancement zone guns and beams, and equipped with enhancement zone suits and power armor.
• Served as the platform from which [[an]] NPB was deployed to destroy [[an]] orc yacht.
• Named destination where departing warriors are headed as part of the post-transfer routing.
• A ship or asset described as being heavily armed with enhancement-zone weaponry and instrumental to the rapid dominance achieved by the [[open intellect pacifist network]].
• A massive freighter from [[Long Haul Biological Waste Management Solutions]] anchored in orbit above [[Earth]], repurposed as the transport carrying tens of thousands of [[mercenaries]] and former [[crawlers]] (the "[[Princess]] Posse") inbound to the dungeon.
• A heavily armed individual or entity described as "decked out in enhancement-zone weaponry" and compared to a fully armored ogre; cited as part of the force that helped the insurgents seize control.
