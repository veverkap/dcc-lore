# brindled_grub

• A [[grub]]-type creature referenced as a common enemy archetype.
• The Brindled Vespa is compared to the Brindled [[Grub]]; [[grub]] pupae spawn hornet-like forms used in the broadcasted encounter.
• A creature referenced by [[Donut]] as the actual target of one of her offhand threats; part of the game's fauna.
• Small mob creatures connected to the [[Krakaren]] boss; they assist in the production of Rev-Up moonshine in a prior encounter.
• Small hostile creatures referenced by [[Donut]] as part of the local fauna/ menaces repertoire.
