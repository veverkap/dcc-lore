# the_feral_god_meatus_has_entered_the_realm

• The Feral God [[Meatus]] has entered the realm, triggering strategic alarms and limiting certain abilities like [[Scavenger]]’s Daughter.
• [[Meatus]] is a loud, two-part god composed of a [[gremlin]]-like host and [[an]] enormous sapient "dink" attached to it, antagonizing [[Harpocrates]] and central to a godfight.
• [[Meatus]] is a combatant engaged in a clash near the Dream castle, grappling with gods in the larger battlefield fray.
• The crawler trapped inside the giant rolling ball of [[crawlers]] that [[Harpocrates]] is desperate to recover is referred to as [[Meatus]].
• [[Meatus]] is held by [[Harpocrates]] as part of the ball/shaft construct and is described as having "lost his rigidity" during the fight, observed near Li [[Na]]'s tower.
• [[Meatus]] applies a grotesque temporary curse when he comes into range and is seen trapped and dissolved beneath the Astral Paw during the mass-slam sequence over Shanty Town/[[Larracos]].
• [[Meatus]] is a grotesque godlike figure described as a giant stinky penis, participating in the divine brawl during Faction Wars and later processed through the Li [[Na]] chain.
• [[Meatus]] is referenced in connection with the Lorena Bobbitt Box and the smoky form produced by the Celestial Godslayer Box, appearing as the smoky god image before the smoke is smashed and dissipates.
