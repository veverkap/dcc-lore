# mantaur_creatures

* Large, powerful NPC/monster groups guarding platforms, described as neighborhood bosses, that impeded access to [[Station 60]] and caused groups to fall back.
* Powerful neighborhood-boss [[mantaur]] [[NPCs]] guarding platforms, notably blocking [[Station 60]], who resisted crawler groups and physically kept defenders out.
* Large neighborhood-boss mantaur creatures guarding platforms, powerful melee defenders, and obstacles for retreating groups.
* A centaur-like summoning vessel used to host and call forth [[War God Grull]], with two mantaur bodies central to the plot.
* A screaming creature seen being carried by a cheetah-like mount during the chaotic god arrival.
* Horned humanoid mobs encountered in groups, with a music-triggered group berserking mechanic and participating in chaotic hallway fights.
* The mantaur species/race, with males having a secondary torso with four arms, and female variants ([[womantaurs]]) presenting as four-legged or with a secondary torso and hands, typically worshiping [[Grull]], though [[Genesis]] and [[Rapture]] worship [[Enyo]].
* A manufactured mantaur race (half-[[human]]/half-[[human]] genetic construct) used as transit engineers in the Tangle system, known for physical strength, testosterone-fueled behavior, and service as [[the tangle]] train engineers.
* [[Mantaur]] and [[ManTauR]] are referenced as separate entities, with the latter providing critical context about trains being sent through portals with only engines returning.
* [[Mantaur guy]]: Previously encountered NPC/creature referenced by [[dungeon crawler carl]], who explained aspects of train teleportation mechanics (engine cars vs. remaining cars).
* Named-but-unspecifed NPC referenced by [[Carl]], who previously explained how engine cars and train cars behave at the end of the line (context for the [[Abyss]] dumping trains).
