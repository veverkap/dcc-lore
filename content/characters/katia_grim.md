# katia_grim

* [[Katia Grim]] is a crawler attuned to Crawler #9,077,265, central to this chapter.
* She is a moral concern for [[Donut]] and dungeon crawler [[Carl]], urging them to flee while actively moving [[NPCs]] to safety.
* [[Katia]] cannot access certain clubs/saferooms due to being a quest host.
* She is a party member who purchased the Training Room with a coupon and explores her personal space door during the chapter.
* [[Katia]] is assigned to train the Catcher skill and occupies one of the combined personal rooms alongside [[Princess]] [[Donut]] and dungeon crawler [[Carl]].
* She is a [[doppelganger]] crawler who rose into the top ten after recent events and is a permanent part of [[Carl]]'s three-person team.
* [[Katia]] is a recipient of multiple loot boxes, including a Gold Savage Box and the Enchanted Wrestling Belt of the Great Gorgo.
* She is a party member who acquires rooms using a coupon and is assigned training goals.
* [[Katia]] is a worshipper of [[Eileithyia]] and possesses the Engaged Lock Box of the Night Wyrm.
* She is romantically involved with [[Donut]] and coordinates with dungeon crawler [[Carl]], [[Mordecai]], and [[Daniel]] [[Bautista]].
* [[Katia]] is the General of the [[Princess]] Posse who communicates with dungeon crawler [[Carl]] during the lobby lockdown.
* She is a party member who explores her personal room and is assigned training priorities.
* [[Katia]] is a [[doppelganger]] crawler who is on the leaderboard and was featured on the recap show.
* She is a Crawler who possessed a piece of the Gate of the Feral Gods and identifies dungeon crawler [[Carl]] as the true owner.
* [[Katia]] is the holder of the purple tiara/crown and the Engaged Lock Box of the Night Wyrm, and is close to [[Donut]] and central to the plan involving the box's boons.
