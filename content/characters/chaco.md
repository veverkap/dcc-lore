# chaco

Here is the consolidated markdown list:

• Pterolykos, Song Bard, level 66, NPC host of "The Prize Carousel" segment, appears in [[Carl]]'s room to run prize selection, panics when [[Mordecai]] attacks, and delivers prize descriptions during the show.
• Crawler involved in a widely noticed on-stream incident tied to a trolling vote; referenced as a target of grudges and part of the explanation for bounties and grief.

• Named individual referenced as the target of [[Mordecai]]'s temper; implicated in a prior conflict that nearly led to expulsion.
• Vendor or craftsman running a counter where performers get upgrades; [[Mordecai]] instructs getting upgrades at Chaco's counter.
• Crawler running a prize counter at the event; readies himself for possible confrontation as [[hunters]] close in.
• Attendee at the far end of the room who disappears along with the judges when the chamber protection collapses.
• Ally mentioned by [[Odette]] who was involved in the containment and killing of a goddess (leading to in-game consequences); later included in trusts [[Odette]] set aside.
• Wolf-headed pterolykos bard in [[Odette]]'s party; uses bard magic (including the Lightning [[Rod]] song) and ultimately worships [[Dodola]] to enable the use of the Dart of Ophiotaurus on [[Uzzi]].
• Former [[skyfowl]] turned game-show host (winged wolf) who arrives as a field correspondent, theatrically reporting and attempting live interviewing during the council.
• Warlord originally expected to appear in the meeting but was absent after being "punched in the [[face]]"; friend of [[Odette]] and implied to have colluded on meeting logistics or host substitutions.
• Crawler-associated individual who blinked away in the broadcast, momentarily leaving behind Lamashtu the donkey; sought for a debrief by Prime Minister [[Victory]].
• Pterolykos song bard NPC (Level 66) and host of "The Prize Carousel" segment; appears via teleportation to run the fan-box prize selection, interacts with [[Mordecai]] and the party, and is present when the system enacts the Naughty time-out on [[Mordecai]].
• Crawler referenced disparagingly for a trolling move tied to a vote and implicated in events connected to [[Mordecai]]'s death by others' speculation.
• Vendor referenced as the seller of the shrink wand [[Carl]] owns.
• Wolf-headed pterolykos bard in the party; plays the tar and casts music-based magic (including the Lightning [[Rod]] effect), wields the Dart of Ophiotaurus, and ultimately worships [[Dodola]] and uses the dart against [[Uzzi]] before casting the Circle of Containment, precipitating the puzzle's activation and the god's death.
• Crawler who killed [[Dodola]] during the containment incident tied to [[Odette]]'s illegal deal; later mentioned as a beneficiary of [[Odette]]'s trust set-asides.
• Announcer chosen by the [[AI]] to act as Grand Master of the parade; delivers the parade rules and starts the ninety-minute build timer.
• Formerly active crawler and parade announcer encountered riding Lamashtu the Donkey; described as ill and terrified during the eleventh-floor climax; receives a healing from [[Carl]] while unconscious and serves as a focal NPC during the float presentations.
