# Heather the Bear

# [[Heather the Bear]]
Also called Heather the Roller-Skating Bear. A Level 19 Neighborhood Boss formerly part of Grimaldi’s Traveling Circus attraction. Transformed into a worm-infested, roller-skating monstrosity whose parasite worms could burrow into victims and speak in their minds. After being set aflame and then hit with a custard outcome from a scratcher ticket, the worms were purged and the elderly bear briefly regained lucidity before dying.
