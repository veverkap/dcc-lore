# heather_the_bear

• [[Heather the Bear]], also known as [[Heather]] the Roller-Skating Bear, was a Level 19 Neighborhood Boss and former attraction at [[Grimaldi]]'s Traveling Circus.
• She was transformed into a worm-infested, roller-skating monstrosity, with parasites that could burrow into victims' minds.
• After being set aflame and hit with a custard outcome from a scratcher ticket, the worms were purged, and the bear briefly regained lucidity before dying.
• [[Heather]] the bear was freed by [[dungeon crawler carl]] in a past event, referenced by [[Signet]] as analogous to previous acts of liberation during the circus rescue.
