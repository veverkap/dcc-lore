# dismember_the_war_mage

* The named war mage antagonist, [[Dismember]], commands [[Fleshers]] and builds powerful spells from the other side of the barrier on the Dismemberment Limited.
* [[Dismember]] seeks to obtain bones/skins to iterate his creations and is referred to as "[[Dismember]]" during the confrontation on the train.
* As the masterminder of repeated attacks and resleeving cycles, [[Dismember]] is responsible for summoning [[Fleshers]] and setting up the train's deadly mechanics.
* [[Dismember]] can summon [[Fleshers]], animates skin to form minions, and threatens to flay and re-sleeve victims.
* Named war mage encountered and killed on a train, decapitated by [[dungeon crawler carl]].
* His death triggers conversion of [[Fleshers]] into Bereft Minions and is associated with the Dismemberment Limited line.
* His head becomes the party's loot, [[War Mage Head]].
