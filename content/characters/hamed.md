# hamed

• Identified as the new manager of the [[desperado club]]; revealed to be the real name of the Night Wyrm and the leader of the [[Guild of Suffering]]. Enforces a crawler ban and staffs the club with heavy security.
• Leader of the [[Guild of Suffering]] and father of [[Anaconda]] and [[Damascus Steel]]; associated with the Desperado Club takeover after [[Astrid]]'s death.
• Antagonistic figure connected to multiple plots in the chapter: orchestrated events involving [[Astrid]] and the stripper group, and feared by guards. His machinations are central to the group's mistrust of the casino setup.
• [[Hamed]] is [[an]] NPC despot mentioned as leading a failed dungeon revolution for early indentured [[NPCs]] and as part of NPC offshoot politics.
• Possibly in [[Orren]]'s office; [[Carl]] wonders whether [[Hamed]] knew about the [[mimics]], implying [[Hamed]] is a person of interest on the floor.
