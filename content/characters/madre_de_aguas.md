# Madre de Aguas
A level 125 city boss serpent ("Mother of Waters") that can control local fauna; was charmed by [[Sister Ines Quiteria]] but was freed when [[Samantha]]'s teleportation broke the charm.

## Additional Observations
# [[Madre de Aguas]]
An important NPC whose zombification and subsequent fall triggered a large-area system announcement; her death/undead state caused a major demon ([[Amayon]]) to slip into the realm.
