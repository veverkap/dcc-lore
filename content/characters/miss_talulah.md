# Miss Talulah
A named individual of the species [[Screeching Death Manatees]], [[Miss Talulah]] is a Level 90 enemy encountered at the cave entrance. Described and classified in a system announcement as a [[Special Guest Creature]] for the 10th and 11th floors, she is dispatched by [[Donut]] using [[magic missile]].

## Additional Observations
# [[Miss Talulah]]
A named individual instance of the species [[Screeching Death Manatees]]. Presented in the chapter as “Level 90 Screeching Death Manatee,” she attacks the race convoy and is killed instantly by [[Donut]]’s magic missiles. Her death produces gore and sticky water that affects the road.
