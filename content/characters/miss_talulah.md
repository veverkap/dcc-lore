# miss_talulah

• A named individual of the species [[Screeching Death Manatees]], [[Miss Talulah]] is a Level 90 enemy encountered at the cave entrance, dispatched by [[Donut]] using [[magic missile]].
• A named individual instance of the species [[Screeching Death Manatees]], she attacks the race convoy and is killed instantly by [[Donut]]'s magic missiles, producing gore and sticky water that affects the road.
