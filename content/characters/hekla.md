# hekla

Here is the consolidated list of unique factual points about Hekla:

* Hekla is a crossbow-firing Valkyrie leader and founder/icon of the faction [[Brynhild'’s Daughters]].
* She is [[an]] Icelandic Crawler and leader of the team [[Brynhild'’s Daughters]].
* Hekla is a high-ranking crawler, level 28, 33, and 24, and is portrayed as practical, cunning, and interested in recruiting [[Donut]] into her group.
* She is a powerful shieldmaiden and leader of a crew of "daughters" (part of [[Brynhild'’s Daughters]]).
* Hekla is a top-10 Crawler (Shieldmaiden/Amazonian) shown skewering monsters and appearing on the recap and leaderboard.
* She is a contact who scouts train-platform tactics and monster migration patterns, reports monster disembarkment every five floors, and collects route intel.
* Hekla is a named crawler mentioned in conversation as possessing a signature move ("[[Donut]] Kick").
* She is a fellow "daughter" crawler mentioned by [[Katia]] who found a similar room with two [[robots]] in another warren, corroborating the odd robot-room pattern across stations.
* Hekla is a leader figure of the all-female group called the [[Daughters]], skeptical of certain recruits and concerned with group composition.
* She is a physically imposing fighter who devised the plan to use a subway train on the [[Vermillion line]] to rescue trapped [[crawlers]] at [[Station 101]].
* Hekla is a ferocious fighter and [[archer]] in the group who mans a crossbow during the run and monitors the line maps.
* She is involved in a violent confrontation on the train, is shot and subsequently healed by [[Silfa]], and is accidentally killed by [[Katia]]'s activated skill, earning [[Katia]] a bounty.
* Hekla is a former leader of [[Brynhild'’s Daughters]] who is killed on the train, and her corpse is used to create clockwork minions via [[Donut]]'s spells.
* She is a former owner of magic gear (crossbow buffs, bolts) whose equipment characteristics are referenced when [[Katia]] uses the auto-crossbow.
* Hekla is a hostile player or antagonist whose attempt to kill [[Katia]] was detected thanks to items like [[Donut]]'s sunglasses.
* She is a referenced crawler/team who was sponsored (or at least financially involved) with [[Odette]] or her circle, discussed as part of prior sponsorship and controversy.
