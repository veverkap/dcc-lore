# Former Circus Lemur
A level 8 mob descended from the ruined Grimaldi troupe: red-furred, knife-throwing lemur creatures wearing painted human skull masks. Part of the circus-related enemies attacking the street.

## Additional Observations
# Former Circus Lemur
