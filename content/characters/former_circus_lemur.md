# former_circus_lemur

• A level 8 mob descended from the ruined [[Grimaldi]] troupe: red-furred, knife-throwing lemur creatures wearing painted [[human]] skull masks.
• Named in kill logs (e.g., killed [[Grace Bautista]]).
• Acts as a hostile mob in the circus encampment and part of the traps that felled other [[crawlers]].
