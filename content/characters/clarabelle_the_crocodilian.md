# clarabelle_the_crocodilian

• A [[crocodilian]] bouncer who manages entry at the station-only entrance bar, enforces club rules, offers paid private security, and shares gossip about managers and banned players.
• Controls access to the station-only entrance bar/club, handles security offers, and interacts with [[dungeon crawler carl]] and [[Donut]].
• Previously stood guard at the [[desperado club]] entrance and was used as a point of comparison for [[Club Vanquisher]]'s vestibule.
• Offers paid private security services tied to club management promotions.
• Shares ground-level gossip about managers and banned [[crawlers]] like [[Lucia Mar]].
• NPC guard of the Desperado Club, referenced for comparison to [[Club Vanquisher]]’s entrance area and guardian styles.
