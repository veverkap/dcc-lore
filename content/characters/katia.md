# katia

Here is the consolidated list of unique factual points about Katia:

• Katia is a crawler and party member who accompanies [[Carl]] and [[Donut]] on their adventures.
• She is a skilled mapper and strategist, able to plot multi-line transfers using the Ochre Line map.
• Katia has the Pathfinder skill, which provides expanded map visibility and scouting abilities.
• She is a powerful crawler who can put large objects into her inventory and retrieve them.
• Katia is a shapeshifter who can change her form to adapt to different situations, including taking on a she-hulk form.
• She is a skilled fighter and uses her abilities to support her party in combat.
• Katia is a mechanic and tinkerer who can repair and upgrade equipment.
• She is a caster who can use spells like Hanzo and Crowd Blast.
• Katia is a high-level party member who has risen from level 44 to 52 and has gained rewards and skills such as Steely Skin and upgrades to Find Crawler.
• She is a named top-ten competitor and has been mentioned as a future guest on [[Odette]]'s show.
• Katia is a local ally who has left the saferoom alongside [[Louis]] and [[Firas]].
• She is a member of [[Carl]]'s group who has been involved in various adventures and has gained [[experience]] and skills.
• Katia is a quiet and earnest party member who is sensitive to her fan reception and has struggled with her identity and new physiology.
• She is a powerful crawler who has been involved in key battles and has used her abilities to support her party.
• Katia is a named ally who has been mentioned as someone who has left [[Carl]] and the group.
