# howling_gulls

• Their scream attack is amplified when used as homogeneous soul essence in the [[Scavenger Daughter]]'s patch, producing much stronger area effects.
• Filling the [[Scavenger Daughter]] patch with a single species (e.g., all Howling Gulls) greatly amplifies the power and [[residual]] effects of the [[Daughter's Kiss]] special attack.
