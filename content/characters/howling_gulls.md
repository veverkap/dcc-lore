# Howling Gulls

# Howling Gulls
A mob species encountered while grinding. Their scream attack is amplified when used as homogeneous soul essence in the [[Scavenger Daughter]]'s patch, producing much stronger area effects.

## Additional Observations
# Howling Gulls
A species of mob whose souls interact with the [[Scavenger Daughter]] patch mechanic; filling the patch with a single species (e.g., all Howling Gulls) greatly amplifies the power and residual effects of the [[Daughter's Kiss]] special attack.
