# colonel_rosetta

* Commanding officer inside the castle coordinating internal defenses.
* Requests "edifice explosion suppression" and oversees deployment of [[crawlers]] and mages.
* Searches for compromised personnel like [[Lonnie]].
* Former merchant/journalist who returned to the dungeon to aid the [[Princess Posse]].
* Leaderly figure on the floor; distrustful of the [[Apothecary]] and loyal to [[Porthus]].
* Former host of the documentary series [[Shadow Boxer]].
* Author of the ninth edition of the [[Dungeon Anarchist’'s Cookbook]].
* Currently alive and active in the fight for the castle of [[Larracos]].
* Rosetta is a crawler and interviewer who probes [[dungeon crawler carl]]'s past during the recorded session.
* She is the author of a later edition note and appears to have contributed to exchanges in [[Milk's Cookbook]].
* Rosetta is a host/producer whose show previously featured [[Carl]]'s group and provided a hidden message that influenced [[Donut]]'s choice in the arena.
* She is a contact who provided secret messages on [[Shadow Boxer]] and had shown television programs set in Miami as a clue.
* Rosetta is a named NPC/ally referenced as missing along with the charity and sponsor tied to the benefactor box; implied to have been involved in arranging the Miami location.
* She is a ninth-edition crawler who wrote about the absolute rule that stairwells are the only way to move between floors and recounted the Faction Wars portal incident involving multiple factions and [[NPCs]].
* Rosetta is a nervous Crest crew member aboard [[Homecoming Queen]] who assists [[Tipid]] with shuttle operations and is involved in the plan to smuggle former [[crawlers]] and [[mercenaries]] into the on-boarding process for the Faction Wars.
* She is a key guild member and alchemist in the [[Guildhall]] who needs to be out fighting to refill her blood bar and is involved in decisions about base defenses, the [[hangar access point]], and the alchemy table.
* Rosetta is a fast, lethal combatant who uses a tech-based invisibility cloak to launch from the ground and execute a swift decapitation of [[King Rust]] using a whip.
* She is a crawler and informant who theorizes how the [[sultana]] and her court used deity mechanics to move between floors undetected and provides historical/ contextual knowledge about gods like [[Theia]] and [[Khepri]] and about temple mechanics inside [[Club Vanquisher]].
* Rosetta is a member of the group advising [[Donut]] to escape the Nest tower due to the presence of the god [[Harpocrates]] and warning about [[an]] imminent level-20 [[Sonic Boom]].
* She is a party member who posts a condolence message ("Rest well, comrade") in response to [[Justice]] Light's death and is noted as remaining in the safe room and converted to a hired mercenary status.
* Rosetta is a companion who returns from market with vegetables and identifies the presence of a potential [[fleshmancer]]; informs the group about available [[mercenaries]] and undead details relevant to [[Rend]].
* She is a contact who previously discussed "protective" measures for parties stuck in the system and is referenced by [[Lexis]] and used as background for the unfolding insurgent situation.
* Rosetta is a collaborator on the cleaner-bot plan who warns about shop-interface complications and is working with [[Mordecai]] on feasibility.
* She is a guild member who produces the small glowing [[cactus]] and explains how the [[Deadly Bloom]] dread trap on it will be used to indicate [[Li Na]]'s fate and status to the rest of the party.
* Rosetta is a party member who provides explanations tied to the [[Scavenger's]] matters and confirms that no deity updates (from [[Emberus]]) occur while in the location where the group currently is.
* She is a member of [[Carl]]'s party who reports on the constraints of the [[safe room]] (it only opens when the heat starts) and is tasked with evacuating people back to safety but notes limitations under the attack's timing.
* Rosetta is a source of information about how the catchall holding area (the [[Nothing]]) functions and its practical use by floor designers.
* She is a crawler with knowledge of finance and the Skull Empire; Rosetta explains the relationship between Harry Tisch, [[Odette]], and the orc economy.
* Rosetta is a character who hypothesizes that the visualization is a "tunnel node," interpreting the on-screen dots as communication pinholes.
* She is a rider of [[Onikuma]] the bear; described wearing a glittering red gown and a magical Gurkha hat.
* Rosetta is a party member who provides equipment solutions (notably sourcing [[Claw Caps]] and showing shop inventory) and is present in the safe room and assists [[Donut]] and [[Carl]].
