# Colonel Rosetta
Commanding officer inside the castle coordinating internal defenses. She requests "edifice explosion suppression," directs searches for compromised personnel like [[Lonnie]], and oversees deployment of crawlers and mages from within the [[the castle]].

## Additional Observations
# Colonel Rosetta
Central crawler and former merchant/journalist who returned to the dungeon to aid the [[Princess Posse]]. Leaderly figure on the floor; distrustful of the [[Apothecary]] and loyal to [[Porthus]]. Former host of the documentary series [[Shadow Boxer]] and author of the ninth edition of the [[Dungeon Anarchist’s Cookbook]]. Currently alive and active in the fight for the castle of [[Larracos]].
