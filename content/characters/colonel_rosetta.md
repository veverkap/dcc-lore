# colonel_rosetta

• Commanding officer inside the castle coordinating internal defenses.
• Requests "edifice explosion suppression" and oversees deployment of [[crawlers]] and mages.
• Searches for compromised personnel like [[Lonnie]].
• Former merchant/journalist who returned to the dungeon to aid the [[Princess Posse]].
• Leaderly figure on the floor; distrustful of the [[Apothecary]] and loyal to [[Porthus]].
• Former host of the documentary series [[Shadow Boxer]].
• Author of the ninth edition of the [[Dungeon Anarchist’'s Cookbook]].
• Currently alive and active in the fight for the castle of [[Larracos]].
