# shade_gremlins

• A mob species created in a bizarre event, described as small goblin-like monsters bearing the [[face]] of [[Henry]].
• Responsible for killing residents and prompting last messages like [[Brandon]]'s during the third-floor collapse.
• Introduced as a named mob type in the club conversation, allegedly spawned en masse by a succubus witch.
• Hostile mobs that killed [[Brandon]] while he protected his team, used as part of team backstory.
• Overran parts of the third floor, responsible for deaths referenced in [[Brandon]]'s final message and general floor collapse combat.
• A horde of small goblin-like monsters born of a succubus-related incident, described as numerous and associated with [[Henry]]'s [[face]].
• A hostile monster type referenced in [[Brandon]]'s final message as the attacking force that overwhelmed his position on the third floor.
• A mass-spawned goblin-liked mob born from a succubus encounter, described as numerous, green, and grotesque, part of [[an]] incident recounted by [[elle mcgib]].
