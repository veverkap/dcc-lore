# olga

• A level 75 [[Grulke]] frog mercenary, part of [[Team Helix]], outspoken about past race events and losses such as [[Helado]].
• Traveling with [[Carl]] and [[Donut]], clarifies species distinction with [[Donut]]'s remarks.
• Mentioned as someone [[Quemada]] and others seek to protect.
• Companion who mutters about bargaining and payment multipliers during the visit to [[Upgrades]].
• Driver of the team's truck during the race, receives commentary from [[Donut]] and helps position the vehicle at the starting line.
• Activates defensive equipment, announcing "Activating the shield", and deploys the visible new shield around the group during the pre-race chaos.
• Driver and crew member in [[Carl]]'s vehicle, responsible for navigation and vehicle control, reports on threats ahead and explains background about [[Quemada]] and the fallen ice fairy [[Helado]].
• Suffered the system debuff [[Gingered]] during the ambush and was rendered unconscious/in a fugue until clerical healing, replaced as driver by [[Finley]] temporarily.
• Rendered unconscious during the race and whose survival is a concern for companions such as [[Finley]] and [[Donut]].
• [[An]] NPC mercenary whose body is described among the casualties after the APV's counterattack.
• A resurrected corpse (summon) occupying the driver's seat, described as headless and leaking gore but still following voice commands, brought back by [[Donut]]'s [[Second Chance]] and later extended by [[Clockwork Triplicate]].
• A clockwork automaton (repeatedly replaced) used as a driver in [[Carl]]'s vehicle, versions of [[Olga]] leak fluids or pop cogs as they time out during the race.
