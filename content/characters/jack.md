# jack

• [[Jack]] is [[an]] elderly male resident who uses a walker, wears a Cincinnati Bengals hat, and has a reputation for bothering female residents.
• He is shown to have violent/erratic behavior, precipitating danger and a shouted warning from the party.
• His pinned hat and death trigger the summoning of the Rage Elemental.
• He is a crawler shown in a gross, memorable clip, and his actions precipitate the headline "Trapped! Meadow Lark and The Royal Court of [[Princess]] [[Donut]] in Peril!"
• He is referenced for earlier chaotic behavior, including peeing on a wall and wearing [[an]] orange Bengals hat.
• He is part of the town's prior antics and is used in [[Donut]] and [[Carl]]'s banter and recollection.
• He is referenced in a memorable on-stream incident ("when Jack pissed on the wall") that the group recalls, connecting the narrator to [[Elle (Mrs. McGibbons)]].
• He is invoked metaphorically when characters compare godly anatomy to "Jack's penis".
• He is a named individual referenced in a flashback moment (urinating on a wall) tied to [[elle mcgib]]'s memory of being saved.
• He is a past companion whose crude action ("pissed on the wall") is remembered by [[elle mcgib]] as part of the rescue that bonded her to the narrator.
