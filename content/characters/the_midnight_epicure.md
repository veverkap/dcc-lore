# the_midnight_epicure

* Invoked by the caprid panel as "The [[Epicure]]" / "The Midnight [[Epicure]]", a deity-like concept referenced during the goats' proclamations and tied to [[Prepotente]]'s worship.
* Also called the Caprid Who Devours the Sky, a folkloric/deity-like figure whose goat-head symbol became a ward against nocturnal evils; tied to the goat-head patch motif.
* A deity worshiped by [[Prepotente]] that embodies [[an]] apocalyptic consumption motif, protecting prey animals and being the source of [[Prepotente]]'s quest to hold the [[Memorial Crystal ([[Apito]])]].
* A giant effigy/head constructed from black dahlias for the parade, symbolizing vengeance and revolt against predators; part of the twin symbolism alongside the [[Unwashed]] in [[Carl]]'s presentation.
* Has a satirical goat iconography that has become a ward against night evils, appearing on the black denim upgrade patch awarded by [[plenty]].
