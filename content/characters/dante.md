# dante

• A named character listed in the chapter index, linked narratively to sections covering [[Tempest's Floor 8]].
• A [[crocodilian]] crawler, former Game Guide/Guildhall Instructor in the [[Trap Master guild]], Class: Thief, Top Level 89, and author of the Third Edition of the [[Dungeon Anarchist’s Cookbook]].
• Took a deal at the start of the 11th floor and was killed 14 seasons short of exit while temporarily reassigned as [[an]] [[Ice Castle]] guard for refusing orders.
• Closely associated with [[justice lights team]] and the [[Lockpicking guild]].
• A former neighbor of [[justice lights team]] during his indentureship as a merchant.
• Refused [[an]] order to kill innocents in [[an]] ice castle and was erased by a beam from the [[AI]], his defiance and death profoundly shaping [[justice lights team]]'s moral stance.
