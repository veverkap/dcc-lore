# lucienne

• Named [[mandagot]] and smaller partner of [[Dwight]] on [[Team Sparkles]] (Level 81 Fink), a rodent-like, high-pitched-voiced creature who worships [[Diwata]] and is hostile toward certain characters.
• Small French rodent associated with [[Team Sparkles]], previously riding atop [[Dwight]]'s head and later submerged in the vine-vehicle, acts vocally during the altercation.
• Companion of [[Dwight]] on [[Team Sparkles]], appears mostly unhurt after the finish and complains about a cracked helmet.
• A [[mandagot]] (Level 81 Fink) and the smaller partner of [[Team Sparkles]], described as a rodent-like, high-pitched, aggressive creature that worships [[Diwata]] and carries a racing helmet.
• A small French rodent associated with [[Dwight]] on [[Team Sparkles]], described helmeted and partly submerged in vines, participates vocally in the exchange with the guck elemental.
• Companion of [[Dwight the unicorn]] on [[Team Sparkles]], appears mostly unhurt after the collision, complains about a cracked helmet and future retribution.
• Character who is screaming at the Dominators during the confrontation, part of the group's surrounding commotion as the race start approaches.
• Competitor allied with [[Dwight]] that [[Florin]] was instructed to pursue during the race.
• Driver for [[Team Sparkles]] who, after being charmed/summoned, ends up on the hood of the truck and is forcibly pushed through the finish-line barrier, exploding into the vehicle in a brutal scene.
• Slain unicorn racer whose corpse and cracked racing helmet are described in the truck, source of gore that [[Carl]] deals with after the race and whose helmet is recovered by [[Donut]].
• Person whose taste/scent lingers with [[Carl]] and figures into his recollection about deity interactions and prior conversations.
• Character referenced as being killed, provoking a drunk accusation from [[Dwight]] and contributing to the violent uproar in the bar.
• Named crawler noted as dead, with her death directly impacting [[Dwight]]'s ability to drive in later heats.
• Late character whose likeness and voice are used as the holographic fairy avatar for [[Dwight]]'s GPS, invoked to unsettle opponents.
