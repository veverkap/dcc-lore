# Moon Reaper
A janitor-type mob native to the 15th Floor and Sheol; described as spiritlike, hooded reaper creatures whose "Reaper Gaze" can inflict effects such as [[Jaunt Sickness]]. Generally non-hostile unless attacked and feared even by powerful denizens.

## Additional Observations
# Moon Reaper
A named mob type: a janitor-class spirit creature that occupies the [[15th Floor]] and sometimes wanders other floors devouring everything in its path. Possesses a dangerous gaze attack capable of inflicting conditions such as [[Jaunt sickness]]. Described as feared even by the princes of hell.
