# moon_reaper

• A janitor-type mob native to the 15th Floor and [[Sheol]], described as spiritlike, hooded reaper creatures.
• Their "Reaper Gaze" can inflict effects such as [[Jaunt Sickness]].
• Generally non-hostile unless attacked, and feared even by powerful denizens.
