# skarn_kid

* A young pilot, known as the [[Twister]]'s pilot, who charges other kids for flights and is a minor but named member of the community.
* Briefly referenced as a young crew member responsible for mooring the airship to a tower during [[Sledge]]'s extraction, mentioned by [[Louis]].
* A level-3 [[changeling]] juvenile NPC orphan in [[Hump Town]], looked after by [[Flint]], with [[an]] [[Enchanted Gnome FarSeerer]] telescope and local intelligence on the [[Wasteland Castle]] and gnome activity.
* Runs a telescope-for-fee business, interacts with [[dungeon crawler carl]], and references saferoom misinformation from [[Flint]].
* Transforms into a gnome to care for [[Bonnie]], provides comfort, and hosts her in his home.
* Present among children in the staging area, interacting with peers like [[Ruby]] and showing compression sickness effects on [[changelings]].
* Referred to as the "Skarn kid," responsible for mooring [[an]] airship to a tower during the evacuation.
* Plays with [[Louis]]'s new airship in the hangar and is part of the wider cast present around the party's base.
* Can shapeshift to hide his true age and is identified as the actual identity behind the listed [[Gangue]].
* Shifts form to resemble a [[Chee]]/[[Holger]] and was banned from manning the door guns for the mission.
* Peers out to assess enemy reactions during the bombing run.
* Has shape-changing abilities and briefly becomes octopus-like to shield [[Bonnie]].
* Emotionally affected by the eruption and instructed to shelter in [[Area 52]] saferooms and [[Larracos]] if needed.
* Named individual referenced alongside [[Bonnie]] by [[Louis]] as being absent from the failed operation.
* Lives in [[Hump Town]], owns [[an]] [[Enchanted Gnome FarSeerer]] telescope, and patrols.
* Runs a telescope-for-fee business, visited by [[dungeon crawler carl]], and advised about saferooms.
* Transforms into a "creepy [[human]] child form" and mentions guidance from [[Flint]].
* Rented a telescope and helps with access to town services; present among the crowd and mentioned in relation to the pocket watch rental.
* Asks [[Carl]] about returning to watch movies after the town is defended.
* Transformed into a gnome form to care for [[Bonnie]] and is present among the [[camels]]/[[dromedarians]] and supportive of orphaned survivors in [[Hump Town]].
* Named companion referenced alongside [[Bonnie]] as part of the [[changelings]] group [[Carl]] is trying to protect.
* Sits with other kids, including [[Ruby]], and interacts with the bear cubs, attempting to moderate their bickering.
* Named survivor referenced as being on the [[Ninth Floor]] along with [[Bonnie]] and part of the group that had suffered compression sickness during the previous floor's events.
