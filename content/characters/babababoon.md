# babababoon

• Level 17 exclusive mob, a grotesque [[human]]-faced baboon hybrid that causes chaotic behavior.
• Suffers from the DTs, in stage two of three, doubling strength and removing reasoning for intelligent mobs.
• Created by crossing [[an]] earth baboon with a violent population, noted as dangerous and idiotic chaos incarnate.
• Encounters occur in transit stations and on tracks near the [[Nightmare]].
• Displayed as "Babababoon. Level 17." in the system UI and afflicted by the [[DTs]] withdrawal mechanic.
• Notable for stage-based behavior changes and extreme melee strength.
• A baboon/[[human]] hybrid mob type encountered at higher levels, including a level 45 [[Babababoon]] wearing a Santa hat.
• A named mob species (Level 17) introduced in the transit station, described as deranged, simian humanoids created by crossing earth baboons with violent jail populations and suffering stages of the [[DTs]].
• Appears in stage-2 "DTs" withdrawal and is highly destructive and unpredictable in encounters with the party.
