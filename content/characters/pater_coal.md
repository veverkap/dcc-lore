# pater_coal

• Hobbledehoy High Cleric Supreme of [[Emberus]] (level 101), performs sacrament on [[dungeon crawler carl]] in [[Emberus High Temple of the Ending Sun]], grants [[Martyr’'s Path]] floor boon, and reveals quest-related hints about [[Geyrun]]'s death.
• Escorts protagonist from [[Temple of the Dying Sun]] and urges getting protagonist to a healer; present during the Club Vanquisher scene.
• Acts as [[an]] intermediary for faith-backed services and loans, discussing and approving interest-free loan in the shop scene.
• High cleric of [[temple of emberus]] at [[Club Vanquisher]], referenced by [[dungeon crawler carl]] as [[an]] example of someone taken over by [[AI]].
• Named figure from [[temple of emberus]] referenced as a previous instance of [[System AI]] taking over or mimicking [[an]] entity.
• Instructed [[Carl]] to find by a notification from [[Emberus]]; referenced as someone [[Carl]] needs to talk to.
• Hobbledehoy high cleric and [[Emberus]] escort who finds and tends to protagonist in [[Club Vanquisher]]; performs public sacrament in [[Emberus High Temple of the Ending Sun]] and relays god-quest details about [[Geyrun]] and items tied to [[Katia]] and [[Donut]].
• Secretly helped [[dungeon crawler carl]] "buy" a ring at [[Club Vanquisher]]; mentioned in relation to [[an]] overloaded ring debuff.
• Cleric figure encountered leaving [[Temple of the Dying Sun]] and escorting narrator out into [[Club Vanquisher]]; acts as caretaker and guide in the club area during the events of the chapter.
• Facilitates [[an]] interest-free loan from [[Temple of Emberus]] and negotiates the trade-in of jewelry for enchanted gear.
