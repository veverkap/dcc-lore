# Wrath ghouls (province boss)
Described as a province boss composed of multiple wrath ghouls tied together into a giant mouth‑covered monster that fills a station room (seen in some station 36s and similar to station 48 bosses). Cited as an almost impossible foe that can wipe parties quickly; referenced in party reconnaissance via messages with Imani and Elle.
