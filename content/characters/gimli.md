# gimli

• [[Gimli]]: a stone hawk with the ability to turn people to stone, introduced onstage by a Brazilian crawler, and later eaten by [[Bianca]].
• Owned by [[Osvaldo]] and later died at the end of the Butcher’s Masquerade.
• Mentioned in relation to the memorial crystal incident, where [[Osvaldo]] intended to break a crystal in front of the goat for killing [[Gimli]].
