# mrs_mcgibbons

• [[Mrs. McGibbons]] is a ninety-nine- year-old resident rescued by [[Carl]] and the team; she is healed with a potion after being hurled into the ceiling by the elemental's gravity reversal.
• [[An]] elderly member of the group whom [[dungeon crawler carl]] helps carry into a room during the encounter with the [[Rage Elemental]] and the recap footage.
• [[An]] elderly survivor (about 99 years old) encountered in a people-[[bucket]] during the evacuation; she is confused by the dungeon, emotionally says goodbye to [[dungeon crawler carl]], and briefly holds [[Donut]].
• A top-10 ranked Crawler (also referred to in the broadcast as a Frost Maiden); noted for rising levels between recap episodes and for appearances on the recap show.
• A crawler referenced in the recap show who appears to be the same figure later listed as [[Elle McGib]]; she is shown in footage as a "Frost Maiden" and has risen in level during recent progress.
