# sugar_hermits

• Migrating mob creatures that swarm the track during the race, described as female, skull-wearing, tentacled creatures that feed on brains and bones.
• They attach skull-like shells to their bodies and can latch onto racers and [[mercenaries]].
• They can rain from the sky, explode on impact, and swarm [[Tem]]'s skull.
