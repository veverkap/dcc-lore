# luke_of_the_lemig_sortion

* A goblin representative from the [[lemig sortion]] who laughs at Juice Box’’s self-awareness claim and suggests evacuation to the [[High Congress]].
* He is a goblin-aligned representative from the faction [[lemig sortion]].
* He is dismissive of Juice Box’’s self-awareness claim and later considers evacuation for his people via the [[High Congress]].
* Representative of the [[lemig sortion]] with a surfer-dude voice, expressing fear and pessimism about the meeting.
* A Level 64 Lemig goblin, identified as a general in the [[Democratic Sortion]], whose death is central to the quarrel between factions.
* Friend of [[commander stockade of the lemig sortion]], whose death provokes Stockade's emotional collapse.
* A caterpillar-like combatant possessed or driven insane by [[Shi Maria]]'s influence, suffering grievous self-harm and triggering adjudicative limitations.
