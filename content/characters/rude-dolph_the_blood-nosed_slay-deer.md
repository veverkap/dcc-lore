# rude-dolph_the_blood-nosed_slay-deer

• A level-100 Eastern United States Legendary Creature encountered in the mock battle simulation.
• Classified as a Puppet-Master: invulnerable until its eight puppet reindeer are killed.
• Emits a deadly nose laser and has a triangle-in-circle marker.
• Intangible until its eight puppet reindeer are killed; uses a powerful laser nose attack and a lion-like roar.
• Summoned in a V formation with gore-covered puppets and a mall Santa head in its mouth.
