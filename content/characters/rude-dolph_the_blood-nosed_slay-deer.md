# [[Rude-Dolph the Blood-Nosed Slay-Deer]]
A level-100 Eastern United States Legendary Creature encountered in the mock battle simulation. Classified as a Puppet-Master: invulnerable until its eight puppet reindeer are killed. Emits a deadly nose laser and has a triangle-in-circle marker.

# Rude-Dolph the Blood-Nosed Slay-Deer
Eastern United States Legendary Creature introduced as a puppet-master boss in the simulation. Intangible until its eight puppet reindeer are killed; uses a powerful laser nose attack and a lion-like roar. Summoned in a V formation with gore-covered puppets and a mall Santa head in its mouth.
