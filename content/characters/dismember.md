# dismember

• Named war mage encountered and killed on a train, decapitated by [[dungeon crawler carl]].
• His death triggers conversion of [[Fleshers]] into Bereft Minions and is associated with the Dismemberment Limited line.
• His head becomes the party's loot, [[War Mage Head]].
