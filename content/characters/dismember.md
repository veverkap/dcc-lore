# Dismember
Named war mage encountered and killed on a train (decapitated by [[dungeon crawler carl]]). His death precipitates the conversion of Fleshers into Bereft Minions and is associated with the Dismemberment Limited line; his head becomes the party’s loot ([[War Mage Head]]).
