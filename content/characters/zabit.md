# zabit

• [[Zabit]] is a level 66 hunter and Beastmaster of the Atoll race, who speaks via projected text and sign language.
• He owns a trap module bandolier and purchases invisibility-related items.
• He was killed by his own trap after [[dungeon crawler carl]] cast [[Tripper]].
• He buys prizes, including a drop shield token and invisibility potions.
• He uses a projected speech device and wears a trap module bandolier.
• He explains hunter inventory and prize box rules to [[dungeon crawler carl]].
• He is a named hunter present among those surrounding the prize counter and interacting with the party's [[NPCs]] and [[crawlers]].
• He uses invisibility and traps (notably a belt/landmine setup) in [[an]] attempt to escape, but is killed when his own trap [[Tripper]] detonates.
