# pitbull_lady

• The Pitbull Lady is the stepmother figure in [[dungeon crawler carl]]'s past, tied to poisoning his father and family revelations, including [[Asher]].
• She is referred to as the stepmother who poisoned [[Carl]]'s father, recognized in the hydra's visions, and identified as the source of poison imagery by [[Donut]]/[[dungeon crawler carl]].
• She is also called "pitbull lady" in conversation.
