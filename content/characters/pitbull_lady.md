# Pitbull Lady
The stepmother figure in [[dungeon crawler carl]]’s past (referred to as the "pitbull lady" or stepmother). Her likeness appeared in the hydra and was tied narratively to poisoning [[dungeon crawler carl]]’s father and to family revelations (including [[Asher]]).
