# glamoured_creeper

• A variant of creeping undead that forms when reassembled undead fragments reach a threshold (≈70%).
• Mentioned by [[Mordecai]] as a prior emergent threat related to the current events leading into [[The Creeping Apocalypse]].
• A creature type referenced by [[Mordecai]] as the expected evolution of certain self-building fragments once they regain enough mass; related to but distinct from the new [[odious creeper]].
