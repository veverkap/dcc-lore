# slit

• Feral Minor Demon (named) Slit, a makeup-encrusted, chain-whip-wielding demon of huge scale (Level 200) released near [[dungeon crawler carl]]'s group, and subsequently ripped in two by a higher feral god.
• Slit is a harem demon from the Nothing, and is referenced as having been summoned and laser-focused on [[Samantha]].
• Slit is the sister of [[Minge]], and is mentioned in backstory about the demon family.
• Slit is a kaiju-sized feral demon (Level 200) who has been encountered multiple times, and is described as makeup-encrusted, arena-sized, and wearing [[an]] S&M outfit.
• Slit has attempted to kill [[Samantha]] in the past, and is used as a benchmark for demon-power discussion.
• Slit has been killed by a god, and her death is used as context for the danger of the Nothing's monsters.
