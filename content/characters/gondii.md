# gondii

• A category of parasitic creatures that reactivate dead bodies, retain memories, and maintain bodies for long periods.
• Can be classified as "Biologically, I am gondii" to indicate non-[[human]] origins and host inhabitation.
• A named parasitic worm species suspected of causing sudden control/animation of troops.
• Reactivates dead bodies when entering them shortly after death.
• A biological designation used by occupying [[Residuals]] to claim identity ("Biologically, I am Gondii").
