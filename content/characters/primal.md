# Primal

# [[Primal]]
Blank-slate race choice historically used in Dungeon Crawler World. Visually unchanged, grants unlocked higher-tier skills to train to 20 but deducts -1 to all base stats and removes the +10 stat bonus humans get. Lore: ancestral progenitors of the universe; feared and mysterious.

## Additional Observations
# Primal
An underlying entity type said to exist beneath the masks; the narrator notes that removing a mask would reveal what some call a [[Primal]].
