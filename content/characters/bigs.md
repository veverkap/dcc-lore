# bigs

Here is the consolidated markdown list:

• [[Bigs]] is a crawler who gathers with others outside the privacy bubble during the broadcast moments, referred to as "Bigs the sluggalette".
• Caster/fighter riding [[Gonk]] who endorses attacking the enemy and participates in the group's combat decisions.
• A large slug-like ally sent on reconnaissance into the throne room; received orders from [[dungeon crawler carl]] to hold the room and was the first to occupy the throne room in this chapter.
• A slug-like companion who sits atop the [[meatball]] character's head; travels with [[Rend]] and the party through the tower stairwell.
• A party member who rides atop [[Gonk]] and banters with the group during the assault on the [[Reaver castle]].
• A member of the group who distrusts [[Li Na]] and references her actions against [[Sneaky Tyler C]] as evidence of malicious intent.
• A named sluggalette who socializes with [[Samantha]], helps listen to her recap, and is invited to [[Samantha]]'s bachelorette party; described as messy but liked.
• A companion or ally commanded by [[dungeon crawler carl]] to get to the RV during the [[Scolopendra]] encounter; involved in the party logistics for escape/plan execution.
• A survivor present in the damaged Big Shot Chicken truck who coordinates with [[Carl]] about evacuation and survivors; reports losses like [[Hedy]].
• A crew member described as a mercenary rather than a pet, asking why he didn't receive a hat; distinguished from pets like [[Mongo]] and [[Rend]].
• A companion who comments on keeping the junk from the [[Legendary Fan Box]] and compares it to barracks living; physically moves through the piled items.
• A team companion who follows [[Samantha]] outside, laps poured beer, and participates in the group's outdoor commotion.
• A slug member of the crew who returns to the garage with [[an]] [[Inebriated]] debuff and calls out to [[Imani]] in the scene.
• A party member who becomes physically ill (vomiting) during the gathering; tended to while the cleaner bot shrills.
• A sluggalette friend of [[Samantha]] who listens to the recap and is involved in social moments (invited to [[Samantha]]'s bachelorette party).
• [[An]] associate or ally called by [[Carl]] ("Bigs, get to the RV!") during the chaotic baiting sequence; involved in the party's movement and extraction.
• A survivor leader-type in the Big Shot Chicken truck who coordinates pickup and asks about mounts and trucks; follows [[Carl]]'s instructions to gather survivors.
• A party member who asks why he didn't receive a hat; [[Carl]] clarifies Bigs is technically a mercenary, not a pet eligible for the pet-only accessories.
• A teammate who reacts positively to [[Carl]]'s costume, approaches to sniff it, and leaves a trail of orange goo on the garage floor. Portrayed as physically expressive and tactile.
• A pet/companion who laps spilled beer and follows [[Samantha]] and others into the crowd; part of the group's animal entourage.
• A slug member of the party who returns to the garage with [[an]] [[Inebriated]] appearance; part of the arriving group that includes [[Samantha]], [[Mongo]], and [[Rend]].
• A team member who rode atop [[Jamal]]'s head after the race, heckling the [[gremlins]].
• A [[sluggalos]] child/figure perched on [[Dong]]'s head, combative and loyal to her group's code. Swings a small hatchet in the chapter and provides blunt, comic commentary while also displaying familial attachment.
• A companion carried by [[Bucket Boy]] whose head hatchet sags as she surveys the scene; part of [[Carl]]'s group.
• A named team member present among the [[mercenaries]] and allies in the garage scene.
• A previously born slug associated with [[Carl]] (from [[an]] earlier floor) referenced as part of [[Carl]]'s rationale for keeping slugpox active; mentioned for narrative context.
• Rode with [[Samantha]] on [[Old Shuck]]; wears a tiny Gurkha hat adapted to [[an]] eyestalk and benefited from Gurkha hat skill boosts.
• [[An]] enthusiastic participant in the final assault, cheering on the slaughter of the masked elites; speaks lines of triumph during the raid from the transport bays.
• Insists on driving and orchestrates reckless behavior (including running a deep fryer) during the arena entrance.
