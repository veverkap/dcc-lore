# svern

• Svern – [[Changeling]] Principal (Level 49): a named mob guarding [[Town Hall]], whose head yields a luminescent prize and whose class/role is explained by [[Mordecai]].
• The [[changeling]] principal's corpse is kept by [[Carl]]'s group and is revealed during inspection of the town leadership as proof of [[changeling]] activity.
• The party kept the [[changeling]] principal's corpse, and [[Leon]] discovers it among [[Henrik]]'s remains during inspection, using it as "proof" of [[changeling]] activity in the town.
• [[Svern – [[Changeling]] Principal]]: a named [[changeling]] guard (Level 49) encountered and killed inside [[Town Hall]], reverting to a faceless humanoid form with a glowing, luminescent head that is the vital target per lore.
