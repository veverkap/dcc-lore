# tally

• A named non-combatant NPC who identifies as a [[bopcas]] and serves as the caretaker of the [[Safe Room]] fast-food restaurant, preparing food, managing rental/sleeping spaces, and moving between floors as the dungeon collapses, speaking with a Slavic accent.
• Runs the safe-room restaurant and answers questions related to [[Safe Rooms]] and purchasing in-dungeon services, providing guidance on items like crafting tables and personal purchases.
• A furry companion and former resident of [[an]] off-world rescue/relocation program who watches the premiere of [[Dungeon Crawler World: Earth]] with the narrator, providing exposition about earlier shows and contrasting [[borant corporation]]'s approach with the [[squim conglomerate]].
• Has been persuaded by [[Donut]] to give extra food (five plates of salmon) and is a minor supporting character mentioned in passing.
