# clarabelle

* A lizard-faced, [[crocodilian]] bouncer and employee of the [[desperado club]] who enforces entry rules and notes that the club's pet room/playroom is out of order after a recent incident.
* Occupies a small backroom area connected to the [[desperado club]] and is reachable via a portal.
* Enforces floor-specific entry rules, knows secret access points, and acts as a gatekeeper for access to the [[Hunting Grounds level]].
* A [[crocodilian]] door guard at the [[desperado club]] who silently admits [[dungeon crawler carl]] and [[Donut]] into the venue.
* Club attendant who blocks entry until bribed and interacts with [[Samantha]].
* A guard at the door of the [[Desperado Club]] who enforces the new manager's rule banning [[crawlers]] and reports that the surviving [[cretins]] and sai were airlocked and sent to the [[mercenary markets]].
* A witness or informant who previously explained the aftermath of the Desperado Club incident that impacted [[Toyotomi]] and other Sai.
* A source who previously explained aspects of the [[blood bar rules]] to the narrator, including how blood bars behave inside clubs like [[Club Vanquisher]].
* A [[crocodilian]] bouncer who sits in the entrance vestibule of the [[desperado club]] and enforces the new manager's rule banning [[crawlers]].
* A staff member at the [[desperado club]] who waves the team through and handles club traffic with minimal fuss.
* The guard at the door of the Desperado Club mentioned by [[dungeon crawler carl]].
* Bouncer/attendant who controls access to the [[Desperado Club]] entrance and recognizes the group, flags some as banned or without passes, and defuses a potential confrontation by refusing to stop them when she sees [[Carl]] leading them.
