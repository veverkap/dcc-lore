# edgar_the_tortoise

• [[An]] NPC/[[tortoise]] who provided a detailed castle map used by the group to plan their approach to the [[castle of the high elves]] and its ballroom.
• Slow mage and scout with level-15 [[Part of the Background]] skill; useful for stealth scouting and advanced map-making via [[Sense Movement]].
• A member of the assault force described among the summoned creatures; submerges into the water during the approach on [[Fort Freedom]].
• [[An]] NPC who provided a detailed castle diagram used by [[dungeon crawler carl]] and the group to plan their strategy around the [[castle of the high elves]] and the ballroom layout.
• A [[tortoise]] figure who once aided [[Signet]] with memories; owner/technician linked to the goodwill interface capacitor on [[Edgar]]'s map.
• [[Tortoise]] NPC who previously used tattoo supplies to place magical tattoos (referenced to explain the function of the [[Enchanted Prison Tattoo Kit]] used to mark [[Tsarina Signet]]).
