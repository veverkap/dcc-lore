# brandy

* [[Brandy]]: a named NPC, [[engineer]]'s associate, and level 75 demon who is pregnant, safe, and protected by the party; also a member of the Iron Tangle workforce and keeper of a compartment on the train.
* Brandy: a train [[AI]]/[[engineer]] associated with the party's train, remains idle at stations, and interacts with [[Carl]]'s plans and train operations.
* Brandy: a member of [[Carl]]'s group who remains near the parked [[Nightmare]] at [[station 41]] and appears content to idle while others move about.
* Brandy: a level 75 demon party member who argues with [[Madison]] in the furnace room, enforces contract rules for [[demons]], and is protective/combative.
* Brandy: the train named [[Brandy]] (also referred to as the [[Nightmare]] when turned around) used by [[Carl]]'s group, parked at [[Station 41]] and idled while the party visits the saferoom and club.
* Brandy: [[an]] ally mentioned as the one who will guide setting up the [[Nightmare]] to explode; [[Brandy]] is referenced as part of the bomb/mission support team.
* Brandy: a named NPC/employee referenced by [[Widget]] and [[dungeon crawler carl]] as the train [[engineer]]'s partner, described as alive and expecting children, and mentioned in exchanges about the slain [[engineer]] [[CrackJack]] and train operations.
