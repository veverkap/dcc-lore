# crawler_coolie

* Provides first-person mission-style entries describing a planned suicide attack from [[an]] [[Orbital Ship]] and later a failed detonation.
* Mentioned in [[Carl]]'s thoughts as the author of the 19th edition of the cookbook and as someone who had a last view similar to [[Carl]]'s during the transfer sequence.
* Credited with a passage in the [[Cookbook]] that informed [[dungeon crawler carl]] about the [[Production Trailer]] forcefield and the effect of the disco ball dust.
* Author of the 19th edition of a cookbook and a character who previously died after [[an]] assassination attempt on admins; evokes the space-transfer view that unsettled [[dungeon crawler carl]].
* Sacrificed everything to attempt to kill two admins, referenced as [[an]] example of extreme actions by [[Carl]], who compares his own choices to what [[Coolie]] or [[Priestly]] might have done.
