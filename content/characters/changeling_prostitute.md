# changeling_prostitute

* A shapeshifting NPC encountered at [[Spit and Swallow]] who alters appearance (e.g., attempting [[Jessica]] Rabbit)
* Noted interaction with [[Katia]]
* A shapeshifter category described by [[Mordecai]]: beings that change form when their guildhall is moved, adopting a mob-type appropriate to the current dungeon floor.
* [[Mordecai]] became a Changeling after floor changes and his form changes with each relocation.
* One of the three basic shapeshifter archetypes in the dungeon (alongside Mimic and [[Doppelganger]]).
* [[Changelings]] are extremely harmed by contact with Shadow [[mimics]].
* [[An]] unnamed Changeling is referenced as being on the Ninth Floor, with [[Carl]] worrying about their safety due to Juice Box's efforts to target their allies.
* Mob/NPC class referenced repeatedly as the entities whose corpses fill the [[Akula]] bridge, including [[Henrik]].
* Appears as "Level 50 [[Changeling]] Principal" on loot UI.
