# shambling_berserkers

• A previously encountered mob-type referenced by [[Carl]] as analogous to the new frankenmonster formed by festering [[ghouls]]; used for comparison in size and menace.
• A prior-floor enemy referenced for comparison to the giant frankenmonster at [[Stop 48]], notable for violent, berserk behavior.
• Also called Shrillings or mini-Grinders in the chapter, these are smaller, undead- type mobs spawned or present throughout the forest as part of the post-vampire carnage; accompany the larger [[odious creeper]].
