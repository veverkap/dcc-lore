# firas_m

* A level-22 [[Hammersmith]] crawler encountered at [[Spit and Swallow]]; one half of a duo with [[louis santiago]] who are unhelpful, intoxicated members of the town.
* Firas is a [[Hammersmith]]-class crawler.
* He is a member of [[Carl]]'s party.
* Firas is a melee-focused crawler who carries [[an]] intelligence-enhanced mace.
* He is known for his drunkenness and confusion about dungeon mechanics.
* Firas is a skilled caster who can use the high-level spell [[Puddle Jumper]].
* He is a level 35 crawler.
* Firas is a member of the attacking force involved in the throat-cutting ploy.
* He participates in the missile launches and combat.
* Firas is a party member who assists in the deception and staged killing of [[Henrik]].
* He is a member of the group that retreats from the burning Town Hall.
* Firas is a crawler who helps manage the house/ balloon rig and assists in instructions to protect [[Orthrus]].
* He is a member of the group that travels to Pandinus after the quadrant is defeated.
* Firas is a crewmate who helps operate the uprooted flying house and deploys chum bombs.
* He is a party member who discusses the house name [[Twister]] and stands with the group as events unfold outside.
* Firas is a friend of the party who died during the events surrounding the Butcher's Masquerade.
* He is a named totem card mentioned as part of the hydra/totem situation.
* Firas is a character mentioned as having suggested the name "Party Planner" for the [[Twister]].
* He is a member of the group that participates in the staged murder of [[Henrik]].
* Firas is a crawler who helps secure the plane/house and assists in instructions to protect [[Orthrus]].
* He is a member of the group that participates in the fight against the Odious Creeper.
* Firas is a level 35 [[Hammersmith]]-class crawler who shares anecdotes about the [[changelings]] and [[Bonnie]].
* He is a named companion mentioned as part of the group that left the saferoom prior to the events in the chapter.
* Firas is a member of the raid team that participates in planning and movement.
* He is a crawler who assists with operations and follows directives from [[Carl]] to guide [[Samantha]] through the map.
