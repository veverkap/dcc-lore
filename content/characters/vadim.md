# vadim

• One of the two survivors from the water quadrant who refuses to return, warning of horrors on the ocean floor.
• Crawler in [[Carl]]'s team on the [[Akula]], falls when the vessel shifts, and later enters the bridge with [[Katia]] and [[Tran]] to attempt escape and loot corpses.
• Crawler who panics and [[seals]] himself in a room above the bridge of the [[Akula]], forcing the team to coordinate a rescue/escape while monitoring his oxygen and escape options.
• One of two survivors from the [[water quadrant]] present at the land-side landing, warns of deep-ocean horrors.
• Teammate of [[dungeon crawler carl]] who falls through the sub's interior early in the scene and later joins the others on the bridge to loot corpses and reach the escape hatch.
• Crawler who panics and retreats to [[an]] upper room on the [[Akula]], [[seals]] himself in, and eventually uses [[an]] escape hatch, communicating with [[Carl]] while trapped.
