# emir_akbas

* [[Emir]] Akbas, a level-55 [[Ghazi]] crawler of the Zebani race, was encountered on the dance floor, drunk, violent, and responsible for initiating a melee with [[Carl]] early in the chapter.
* A burly male who had been part of the group with [[Zeynep]] on a previous floor (the Desperado Club); noted for a past drunken confrontation with [[dungeon crawler carl]] and absent from the current group in the castle.
* Mentioned as the likely name of [[Burcu]]'s father, possibly also a Zebani and a crawler tied to that mystery.
