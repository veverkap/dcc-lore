# viscount_fog

• [[Viscount Fog]]: a deceased hunter renowned for slaying undead, former master of [[Sierra]], and star of the in-dungeon television program [[Blood Hunter]].
• Hunted [[Miriam Dom]] and fought with [[Prepotente ([[Pony]])]], ultimately dying at sunset from the compounding curse effect [[Cursed Light Walker]].
• Trained his mount [[Sierra - Northropi Vampire]] to fight at his side and hunted the vampiric threat until his demise.
• Attacked [[Miriam Dom]] and [[Prepotente]], his counters and wards causing the spell backlash that led to the current crisis.
