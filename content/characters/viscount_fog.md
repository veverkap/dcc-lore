# [[Viscount Fog]]
A deceased hunter renowned for slaying undead; referenced in Sierra's backstory as her former master who hunted vampires and ghouls.

## Additional Observations
# Viscount Fog
An elite vampire hunter and star of the in-dungeon television program [[Blood Hunter]]. He hunted [[Miriam Dom]] and fought with [[Prepotente (Pony)]], ultimately dying at sunset from the compounding curse effect labeled [[Cursed Light Walker]].

# Viscount Fog
A now-deceased famed hunter of vampires and undead who trained his mount [[Sierra - Northropi Vampire]] to fight at his side. Mentioned in the boss flavor text as having hunted the vampiric threat until his demise.
