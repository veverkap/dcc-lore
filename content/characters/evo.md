# evo

• Briefs panelists about other guests, including [[Princess D'nadia]] and [[Ripper Wonton]].
• Program director of [[Danger Zone with [[Ripper]] Wonton]] and a member of the [[Forsoothed ([[Soothers]])]] ([[Soothers]]).
• Welcomes guests [[dungeon crawler carl]] and [[Princess Donut]].
• Explains the composition of the show and its regular panelists.
