# amayon_prince_of_sheol

* Demonic prince who claims knowledge about who killed [[Carl]]'s son; offers information in exchange for actions (seeking Soul Crystal—[[Apito]] and permission to break a truce by dealing with the High Cleric of the Watch)
* Offers information about [[Carl]]'s missing son in exchange for actions involving [[Amayon]]'s brothers, tied to the Temple of [[Issitoq]] diplomatic objective
* [[Amayon, Prince of Sheol]]
* [[dungeon crawler carl]]
* [[Amayon]] is a Demon Prince of [[Sheol]], associated with [[Sheol]] royalty and a temporary fire alteration buff.
* He is a level 250 demon prince, one of the four princes of [[Sheol]], currently expelled and inhabiting the body of the Madre de Aguas snake.
* He has cast a wide-area portal to inhale souls in the Cuba region to return home, and is the central subject of the quest-triggering System Announcement.
* He is a caster who raises his hands and shouts in [[an]] unknown language to cast a spell.
* He is a massive demon who appears in the realm, bringing [[Sheol]] fire, and his arrival generates a major Area Message and a quest opportunity to question him about the murder of [[Geyrun]].
* He is a demon lord who was enslaved by [[Ysalte]] in this world, initially dropped into the open air and expands to enormous size.
* He is a caster ally who raises his hands to shout and attempt a spell to affect the sky, his casting is crucial to the group's plan.
* He is a demon lord who was freed and powered-up by billions of consumed souls to cast a world-ascension spell, destined to ascend through the portal to wage war on his brother.
* He is a [[Sheol]] demon who arrived during a demon eviction event and became involved with Sister Ines on Floor 8.
* He is a massive demon who repeatedly punches himself and interacts with [[Carl]] and [[Samantha]], ultimately begins casting the spell to return himself home and debates alliances regarding his murdered child.
