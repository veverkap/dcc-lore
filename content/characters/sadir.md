# sadir

• A [[null]] hunter contracted via the Walk-On business to abduct natives and track high-value marks, including named animals like [[Contessa Purrington]] and [[Gravy Boat]].
• Experienced tracker struggling under social prejudice, partners with [[Gennrik]] on a mission to capture a high-value cat (named [[Gravy Boat]]).
• Operates under Syndicate rules (implants scrambled), uses a [[Hotsheet]], and worries about family back in the [[Filt system]].
