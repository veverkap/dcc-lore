# akuma

* [[Akuma]]: Named war mage, leader of the war mages, and leader of the War Mage Rebellion.
* Steals the [[Gate of the Feral Gods]] from [[Elle McGib]] and fights in the clearing.
* Associated with the war mages, dislikes [[Agatha]], and is involved in factional tension.
* Given full system access and named sole Warlord of the war mages after [[Agatha]]'s removal.
* Led the faction's late intervention during the Faction Wars and is blamed for the theft of the [[Gate of the Feral Gods]].
* Referred to as "Akuma the war mage" among the Ascendency participants.
* Signed the anonymous note handed to [[Dungeon Crawler Carl]] by a generated [[gremlin]], offering clandestine help and instructions.
* Killed [[Stalwart]], stole the [[Gate of the Feral Gods]], and disappeared after taking a castle and ordering [[Carl]] to kill [[Agatha]].
* Responsible for actions like the theft of the [[Gate of the Feral Gods]].
* Signed the anonymous warm note handed to [[Carl]] by a generated [[gremlin]], claiming the ability to smuggle friends to the [[Pineapple Cabaret]].
* Killed [[Stalwart]] and stole the [[Gate of the Feral Gods]], central antagonist referenced in the chapter's backstory.
* Leader of the War Mage Rebellion, involved in the theft of the [[Gate of the Feral Gods]] and occupation of [[Larracos]].
* Elf-like war mage affiliated with the War Mage Rebellion, involved in the theft of the [[Gate of the Feral Gods]] and occupation of [[Larracos]].
* Restrained and slowly losing health due to anti-magic containment, provides exposition about the dungeon's reserves and the existence of [[Herot]], [[Menerva]], the [[Pineapple Cabaret]], and the dragonwork of the dungeon's [[Framework]] and [[Macro AI]].
* Provides key intelligence about the [[Pineapple Cabaret]], mentions [[Herot]] by name, and gives conflicting info about threats like the [[Ogre Imperium]] and a [[Shadow mimic]] infestation.
* Crawler who previously gave cryptic instructions about killing teams and warned about gods entering certain places, a figure whose statements [[Carl]] distrusts.
* Crawler contacted by [[Carl]] in-chat, responds tersely and is preparing to leave the floor.
* Source claiming that [[Samantha]]'s sand-ooze daughter is of interest to the war mages.
* Proposed using the [[Wheel of Fortune]] and its "Nothing" spot to access a dimensional holding area for extraction rather than a [[Cleaner Bot]] method.
* Participant worried that sending worshippers of gods into the [[Pineapple Cabaret]] would trigger a smite, involved in deliberations about the holding area.
