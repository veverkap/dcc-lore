# maxim_popov

* The quieter Popov twin, prone to growling threats during the convention appearance, and reacts strongly to certain fan questions.
* One of two fat, year-old ogre [[nodling]] yearlings found among the dead, initially unconscious, and later transferred off-floor to a kinder facility.
* The more stoic Popov twin, easily offended and sharp-tongued, involved in the controversy about their race and choices.
* One of two baby ogres (a [[nodling]] yearling) found among the battlefield gore, initially unconscious, and later deemed ineligible and transferred off-floor as per game rules.
* Mentioned as associated with [[Dmitri]] and appearing in future convention plans; part of the side cast referenced by [[elle mcgib]].
* Left- headed, melee-focused member of the two-headed [[nodling]] duo, identified as a melee-class [[Bogatyr]] and wielder of a flaming [[Meteor Hammer]].
* Twin brother and partner to [[Dmitri]].
* Named as someone the narrator was supposed to meet at a convention table; mentioned in passing during exposition by [[elle mcgib]].
* Left-side twin of the two-headed [[nodling]] pair encountered by [[dungeon crawler carl]], a melee-focused crawler classed as a [[Bogatyr]].
* Wields a flaming chain weapon referred to as [[Maxim's meteor hammer]] and serves as the more gruff, protective half of the pair.
* Paired with [[Dmitri]] in a comparison to illustrate conventional ogre archetypes referenced by [[Carl]].
