# maxim_popov

• The quieter Popov twin, prone to growling threats during the convention appearance, and reacts strongly to certain fan questions.
• One of two fat, year-old ogre [[nodling]] yearlings found among the dead, initially unconscious, and later transferred off-floor to a kinder facility.
• The more stoic Popov twin, easily offended and sharp-tongued, involved in the controversy about their race and choices.
• One of two baby ogres (a [[nodling]] yearling) found among the battlefield gore, initially unconscious, and later deemed ineligible and transferred off-floor as per game rules.
