# krakaren_prime

• [[Krakaren Prime]]: original source of the [[Krakaren]] collective mind, spawns multiple clones, and grows stupider over time.
• Powerful in-dungeon brewer/being from the Nothing, created the Hell-kissed Conscription potions, and is banished and preparing for domination.
• Entity stuck in the Nothing, with [[Paca]] as [[an]] agent, referenced in relation to the Gate of the Feral Gods.
• Level 235 Floor boss, tentacled, tree-perching monstrosity, massive, emits green mist, and is enraged by the party's actions.
• Reacts angrily to the arrival of [[Scolopendra]], described as the "Miss [[Krakaren]] Prime" the dungeon [[AI]] referenced.
• Appears in the carnage following the breach of the Nothing, specifically within Club [[Scolopendra]].
• Named among powerful non-deity boss monsters within the sapient mobs grouping, cited as [[an]] example of a high-tier mob entity.
• Massive mob perched atop the towering white tree in the arena, [[Carl]] assumes it is the banished entity that had been on the eighteenth floor.
