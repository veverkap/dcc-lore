# Krakaren Prime

# [[Krakaren Prime]]
The original source of the Krakaren collective mind. Mentioned as the progenitor that spawns multiple Krakaren clones; part of an anti-science, collectivist entity that grows stupider over time.

## Additional Observations
# [[Krakaren Prime]]
Powerful in-dungeon brewer/being from the Nothing who created the Hell-kissed [[Conscription]] potions; described as banished and preparing for domination (source of the potion given to [[dungeon crawler carl]]).

## Additional Observations
# Krakaren Prime
An entity stuck in the Nothing for whom [[Paca]] is an agent; referenced in relation to the [[Gate of the Feral Gods]].
