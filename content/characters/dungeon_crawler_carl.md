# dungeon_crawler_carl

Here is the consolidated list of unique factual points:

• [[Carl]] is the protagonist and narrator of the series.
• [[Carl]] is a Level 13 crawler who selects the [[Primal]] race and the Compensated Anarchist class.
• [[Carl]] is a celebrated crawler whose death is nearly televised as a "Death Challenge!" event.
• [[Carl]] is a frontline melee fighter who coordinates with [[Donut]] in combat.
• [[Carl]] is a crawler who loots and fights in the dungeon, manages inventory, and gains skills and achievements.
• [[Carl]] is a party leader who coordinates rescue and tactical actions against threats on the necropolis.
• [[Carl]] is a crawler who detonated a bomb in the temple and carries items like the [[Emberus]] Ring.
• [[Carl]] is a narrator who organizes survival plans for the Butcher's Masquerade and coordinates with other [[crawlers]].
• [[Carl]] is a crawler who fights alongside [[Donut]] and commands tactics during the encounter with the Mold Lions.
• [[Carl]] is a crawler who leads the party through the Abyss Station and the surrounding catwalks.
• [[Carl]] is a party leader who organizes the infiltration of Town Hall and pursues quest objectives.
• [[Carl]] is a crawler who opens benefactor boxes, ingests a [[Valtay]] neural enhancer, and crafts explosives.
• [[Carl]] is a narrator who coordinates rescue and defense efforts, communicates with teammates, and builds missiles.
• [[Carl]] is a crawler who pilots the wicker basket of the Vahana and leads the plan to retrieve watches.
• [[Carl]] is a protagonist who interacts with studio hosts and the PR admin, holds items like the novel Stinger, and participates in the fifth-floor quadrant selection.
• [[Carl]] is a high-level crawler who opens loot, interacts with teammates, and appears on the floor leaderboard.
• [[Carl]] is a crawler who prototypes vehicles and explosives, leads construction of the royal chariot, and appears as "[[Carl]]" on the floor leaderboard.
• [[Carl]] is a narrator who coordinates the plan to immolate the ooze core, uses the Sticky Feet ability, and manages party actions during the basement confrontation.
• [[Carl]] is a protagonist who leads the party, orders depth charges, and directs [[Gwen]] and others during the encounter with the city boss [[Lusca]].
• [[Carl]] is a crawler who receives multiple achievements and loot, wields influence over the events that accidentally released entities, and interacts with system announcements.
