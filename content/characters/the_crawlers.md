# the_crawlers

* The player group responsible for summoning and transforming [[Scolopendra]] and central to the Ascendency broadcast observed by [[Prime Minister Victory]].
* Responsible for summoning and interacting with entities such as [[Scolopendra]] and have departed for the [[twelfth floor]].
* Term used in the [[Syndicate Announcement]] to describe surviving humans who choose to enter and progress through the [[World Dungeon]] levels seeking to reclaim their lost matter and resources.
* The player-characters / contestants participating in dungeon runs and public events like [[CrawlCon]], including figures such as [[dungeon crawler carl]], [[Donut]], and others who must comply with vessel security and scheduling.
* Group term for [[Carl]]’s faction/race of survivors operating within the dungeon environment, claiming many towns and often unable to be mayors except in special cases (e.g., Zockau), with the club enforcing separation between [[Crawlers]] and [[Hunters]] on specific floors.
