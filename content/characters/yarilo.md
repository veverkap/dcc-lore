# yarilo

• A banished god of lust whom [[Ghazi]] intended to summon, but was actually contacted by [[an]] impersonator.
• Banished god of lust referenced in the [[Mad Dune Mage]]'s letter as having been sought via a portal opened with the [[Winding Box]] and [[pocket watch]].
• A god of lust in the masquerade origin tale whose actions led to violence and the sanctification of the ritual, resulting in the creation of the [[Protection Seal]] and banishment to the [[Nothing]].
• A named god referenced as having been banished to the [[Nothing]]; invoked as part of in-world lore about gods, parties, and a "harem" that was imprisoned.
• A Feral God/Deity whose entry into the realm is announced in the chapter, with presence too distant to trigger certain item effects (e.g., [[Scavenger’'s Daughter]]).
• A god (god of lust) who was banished and is hostile toward [[Samantha]], mentioned as the deity [[Samantha]] impersonated and implicated in the [[Butcher'’s Masquerade]] backstory.
• A god encountered in pursuit of [[Samantha]], derogatorily described as a god of lust/odd behavior; observed to give up chasing in the chapter.
• A named combatant on the move again during the floor's battle; mentioned as [[an]] active threat.
• A god who is the target of a conscription potion by a war mage; the potion causes the caster to explode, illustrating the dangers of forced conscription.
• A god mentioned as approaching alongside "the ball"; expected to arrive roughly in tandem with [[Harpocrates]] and the warball described by [[Donut]].
• The god of lust appearing in-range of the party; his presence triggers a temporary blessing on the [[Scavenger’'s Daughter]] and he is actively engaged in direct combat with [[Harpocrates]].
• A feral god of lust (and relative of [[Emberus]]) who is beaten to death by [[Harpocrates]] in Shanty Town, [[an]] event that provokes divine anger and a system-wide announcement.
• A figure who emerged from [[the Nothing]] chasing [[Samantha]]; he later died in the conflicts recounted in the [[Faction Wars]] recap.
• A banished god of Lust referenced as the intended target of [[Ghazi]]'s summoning, with the entity summoned in place of [[Yarilo]] being actually [[Psamathe]].
• A deity referenced in [[Eris]]'s taunt about what "[[Mom]]" did to him; a previously established figure invoked to warn or threaten, used as part of [[Eris]]'s family-history remarks.
• A god of lust referenced in the masquerade origin story whose transgression led to the slaughter that made the ritual necessary.
• A deity referenced in-character as hosting bad parties; used as a cultural touchstone in dialogue when comparing party catering and taste.
• A being who arrived "from the [[The Nothing]]", chased [[Samantha]], and was later reported killed during the events on the floor.
• A deity referenced indirectly via a "Yarilo party" anecdote told by [[Samantha]] in which a sacrifice was made over a trivial food preference, illustrating the extremes of [[Chalchiuhtlicue]]’s followers.
• A feral god and child of [[Taranis]] who was banished to the Nothing by [[Apito]], with his emergence from the Nothing coinciding with the activation of the [[Memorial Crystal ([[Apito]])]].
