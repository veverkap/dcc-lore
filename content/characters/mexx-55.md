# mexx-55

• A floating, jet-black, metallic disk [[AI]] that briefs [[dungeon crawler carl]] and [[Donut]] upon arrival in a small rental trailer, announcing leasing details, program assignment, and giving instructions for the holo/segment.
• A floating production avatar used in the studio trailer, initially manifesting visually but speaking in a gruff, orc voice when the production crew intervenes, acting as part of the show's in-trailer control/interface connected to [[Maestro]]'s production.
