# hedy

Here is the consolidated markdown list:

• [[Hedy]] is a white-tagged female [[gremlin]] NPC applicant and Level 30 vehicle mechanic, serving as a non-combatant garage manager option for mechanical vehicles.
• She is a [[gremlin]] companion mentioned as lost in the crash, previously housed with the group's mounts and engineers.
• As a mechanic, she services the team's truck, presents upgrade options, and advises on upgrade strategy, [[mercenaries]], and removable driving systems.
• She balances a massive wrench on her finger, explains the behavior of cleanup [[gremlins]] after races, and oversees vehicle upgrades.
• She is a crew member who provided the device that became the [[Donut]] drive system and opened the garage chain to reveal the storm outside.
• She is a team member who supervises [[gremlin]] work, yells at generated [[gremlins]] to stay on task, and interacts with [[Carl]] and the [[gremlin]] crew about truck repairs and the "[[princess]] throne seat".
• She is a pet that was assigned to be guarded in the garage and was mentioned when [[Mongo]] fails at his guarding duty.
• She is [[an]] [[engineer]] who cobbled the truck's pulley-and-lever defensive deployment system housed in the truck's kitchen, enabling the twin ghoul cages.
• She is a figure seen in house number one garage alongside [[Jamal]] and [[Dong]], part of survivors greeting racers.
• She is a mechanic who services the Royal Court Truck and advises on upgrades, runs a garage, and manages the tablet upgrade interface.
• She is a [[gremlin]] mechanic who oversees repairs and upgrades on the vehicle, demonstrates dexterity, explains post-race [[gremlin]] behavior, and advises the narrator about racer trust.
• She is a character who supplied the team with the device used to operate the [[Donut]] drive system and seen moving to open the garage chain at the race start.
• She is a crew member who greets the team at the garage, inspects [[Carl]]'s kangaroo costume, reports the audience upgrade vote result, and expresses disdain for role-play-style costumes.
• She is a pragmatic member of the group who supervises the generated [[gremlins]]' work on the truck, scolds them, and organizes repairs and installations.
• She is a pet that [[Mongo]] was supposed to be guarding in the garage and mentioned in the guard instructions.
• She is [[an]] [[engineer]] who designed and constructed the truck's pulley-and-lever cage deployment system installed inside the truck that allowed rapid extension and retraction of the ghoul cages.
• She is a garage crew figure appearing in the open garage of house number one alongside [[Jamal]] and [[Dong]] after Heat Three concludes.
• She is a garage tech/mechanic managing [[Carl]]'s truck upgrades, advising on choosing between one Golden upgrade or three regular upgrades, and monitoring team readiness.
• She is a pit stop attendant/NPC who inspects arriving vehicles, enforces pit-stop procedures, and interacts with the team led by [[Carl]].
• She is a companion left behind in the garage at the start of the race.
• She is a support member who installed protections on [[Carl]]'s vehicle to help them traverse the watery tubes.
• She is [[Carl]]'s tactician and upgrade specialist in the team, reviewing upgrade options, advising on vehicle vs. mount choices, and managing the [[gremlin]] workers after the race.
• She is a character being guarded in the garage and mentioned as safe under [[Jamal]]'s watch.
• She is present in the garage with [[Grigori]] and [[Jamal]] at the chapter's opening, part of the group sheltering on this floor.
• She is a character who comments on [[Grigori]]'s aptitude for driving lessons and participates in personnel observations.
• She is the garage attendant waiting at the team's garage door, reacting to the team's new audience-vote status.
• She is a party member holding a tablet who asks about what upgrade the players voted for, observes the group, and the announcement.
• She is a crew member working on final vehicle fixes in the garage prior to the race, seen alongside [[Bucket]] Boy and other Pineapple Cabaret allies.
• She is a mechanic/upgrade vendor present in the garage who discusses upgrade options and coordinates final upgrade choices with [[Carl]].
• She is a small [[gremlin]] allied with [[Carl]] who shows affection and roughness toward him and accompanies the team in the garage and starting blocks.
• She is a [[gremlin]] present in the garage, interacting with other mounts and pets.
