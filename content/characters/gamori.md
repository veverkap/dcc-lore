# gamori

• The lead [[engineer]] of the [[plenty]] and current Matriarch of [[plenty]].
• Initially attempts to control information, becomes emotional when exposed.
• Judges [[dungeon crawler carl]] as dangerous to the herd.
• Disappears after the violence.
• Lead [[engineer]] of the [[Plenty Tunnel Project]].
• Current Matriarch of [[The Plenty]].
• Present as the stern female caprid who becomes distraught when the herd is startled.
• Defends the herd's secrecy and judges [[Carl]].
