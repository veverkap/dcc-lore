# langley

• Leader of a party of six [[Archer]]-class [[crawlers]], coordinates with [[dungeon crawler carl]] on training and the Town Hall quest.
• Finnish [[archer]] in [[dungeon crawler carl]]'s immediate party, pleads for cooperation from unruly [[crawlers]] like [[Louis]] and [[Firas]].
• Leader/contact of a group tasked with rooftop coverage and escape support for [[dungeon crawler carl]]'s team.
• Reports on city defenses and notes anti-air preparations at the main gate and interrogation procedures for outsiders regarding [[gnomes]] and [[grulke]] toads.
• Member of [[Carl]]'s party stationed in Hump Town, present during the evacuation and preparations.
• Named member of [[Carl]]'s force referenced in the assault plan ("Follow [[Langley]]'s lead").
• Crew member or fire-control operator who announces and fires coordinated missile salvoes for the attacking group.
• Recovered and tarped the gnomish Drop Bear airplane and was placed on the balloon-collection task with [[Louis]] and [[Firas]].
• Ally who reported on the archers' performance during the fighting around the bowl.
• Leader with a few people guarding the detached stairwell, responsible for keeping the stairwell clear of sand and building protections.
• Operator/liaison who relays communications and coordinates Langley's international crew.
• Companion who interacts with [[Mongo]] and is present among the safe-room group.
• Leader of [[an]] escort team tasked with shepherding refugees from the Desperado gates to stairwells for descent.
• [[Archer]] crawler who fights alongside [[dungeon crawler carl]] earlier on the level, discusses alliances and hunting of player-killer groups.
• Hunter mentioned as having been killed along with his team, his death triggers a system announcement that affects [[Carl]] emotionally and the group's situational awareness.
• Named individual whose severed head is mounted on [[Vrah]]'s carapace and who reanimates briefly in the fight to bite [[Vrah]]'s neck.
• Mentioned alongside [[Britney]] as part of [[an]] accent comparison for [[Maxim]]'s voice.
• Named NPC whose head previously bit into [[Vrah]]'s carapace, referenced in [[Vrah]]'s description of scars.
• Level-24 leader of a six-man party of identical-class archers (automobile salesmen from Helsinki) encountered in town, coordinates training and received the Town Hall quest.
• Finnish ([[archer]]) crawler in the group, polite and concerned, pleads with [[Louis]] and [[Firas]] to take the dungeon seriously.
• Local who reports on town defenses and the state of the gates, informed [[dungeon crawler carl]] that the town had guards and was setting up anti-air defenses.
• Member of [[dungeon crawler carl]]'s group referenced among the [[crawlers]] present at the [[desperado club]] during the evacuation.
• Named ally referenced in [[Carl]]'s tactical instructions ("Langley, you guys get the pilot").
• Crew member calling and firing missile salvos during the engagement, coordinates with [[Carl]] on target assignments.
• Member of [[Carl]]'s wider faction who reported that archers like [[Louis]] and [[Firas]] held their own against the Thorny Devil queen during the battle.
• Crawler with people guarding the displaced [[stairwell]], organising defenses around critical structures near where [[Carl]] originally landed.
• Party contact and leader of a multinational crew, facilitates logistics and relays information about survivors like [[Vadim Zbar]] and [[Britney Proskurina]].
• Member of the wider coordination team mentioned as leading escort teams responsible for moving refugees from the Desperado portal to the stairwell for descent.
• [[Archer]] and fellow crawler who has leveled up during the season, dialogues with [[Carl]] about factional alignments and hunting player-killer groups.
• Hunter mentioned in a system chat notification as having fallen along with his entire team, his death is a notable event that affects the party's mood and plans.
• Named hunter whose reanimated head is attached to [[Vrah]]'s carapace and later bites at her in a surprising turn that helps [[dungeon crawler carl]] attack.
• Referenced historical antagonist who once bit [[Vrah]] on the neck, mentioned in context to explain trauma and previous encounters; not present in the ballroom scene.
