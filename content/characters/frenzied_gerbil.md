# frenzied_gerbil

• The [[Frenzied Gerbil]] is a feral, fast, and vicious rodent encountered on mid-level floors, such as the fifth or seventh floors.
• It is a recurring mob type, used as live ammo inside [[Live Ammo Ball]]s, and is responsible for massive aerial and ground attacks.
• [[Frenzied Gerbil]] is a low-level but dangerous mob (Level 11) stored in Live Ammo Balls, capable of generating swarms to assault Hump Town and igniting when passing through fire.
