# Frenzied Gerbil

# Frenzied Gerbil
Mob archetype referenced in the chapter. The [[Frenzied Gerbil]] is the creature type of [[Ralph]]—a feral, fast, and vicious rodent encountered on mid-level floors (e.g., the fifth or seventh floors).

## Additional Observations
# Frenzied Gerbil
A recurring mob type (Level 11 in this chapter) used as live ammo inside [[Live Ammo Ball]]s and responsible for the massive aerial and ground attack; previously appeared as the boss [[Ralph]].

## Additional Observations
# Frenzied Gerbil
[[Frenzied Gerbil]] is a low-level but dangerous mob (Level 11) stored in Live Ammo Balls; gerbil swarms are generated en masse to assault Hump Town, burrowing into flesh and igniting when passing through fire.
