# haxor_the_destroyer

• Haxor the Destroyer is a Level 50, named [[Dreadnought]] merchant encountered in the men's bathroom on the [[Hunting Grounds level]]. He is killed by [[dungeon crawler carl]] and carries a small amount of loot, including a [[Credit Chit]].
