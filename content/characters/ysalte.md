# ysalte

* The goddess Ysalte, also known as the Downward Spiral, The Inevitable, and the Vinegar Bitch, is [[an]] Earth-and-Water deity worshiped by the [[Semeru]] [[dwarves]].
* She is a trickster god who appears unpredictably near the gates of [[Larracos]] and is feared by [[crawlers]].
* Ysalte is a level 250 goddess of Hopelessness and Insanity, temporarily summoned for a special boss event on the eighth floor, and is invulnerable on that floor.
* She wields a pickaxe and a slightly enhanced version of Sister Ines' deck, and grants a temporary world effect.
* Ysalte is sponsored by Pontifex Shine of the nebulars and is known for her ability to seize control of other powerful beings, including [[Amayon]].
* She is also referred to as the goddess of Hopelessness and Insanity, and is associated with deaths, including the death of Sister Ines.
* Ysalte's presence applies a temporary status effect that alters potions, and she is feared and hated by gods and [[demons]].
* She is a central figure in the chapter's major system events, and her death triggers major system announcements and generates a memorial artifact.
* Ysalte is also referenced as a character who was slain during the events, and her death inflicts a lasting penalty on her worshippers.
* She is mentioned in various contexts, including as a god who marked [[Louis]] for death, and as a character who was squandered by [[Carl]].
