# carls_stepmother

• Young woman who appears as the father's caretaker/soon-to-be-wife in the recording, identified by a pitbull/[[dog]] tattoo on her neck (matching the urn name [[Chance]]).
• Hides and retrieves syringes and morphine, injects [[Carl's Father]] with a lethal dose, and comforts [[Asher]].
• Younger woman with a pitbull tattoo on her neck (visible in a wedding photo and noted by [[Donut]]).
• Appears tired, in scrubs, hides and retrieves narcotics from flowerpots, and ultimately injects [[Carl's Father]] with [[Morphine]].
