# Carl's Stepmother

# Carl's Stepmother
Young woman who appears as the father's caretaker/soon-to-be-wife in the recording. Identified by a pitbull/dog tattoo on her neck (matching the urn name [[Chance]]). She hides and retrieves syringes and morphine (some hidden in flowerpots), injects [[Carl's Father]] with a lethal dose, and comforts [[Asher]].
