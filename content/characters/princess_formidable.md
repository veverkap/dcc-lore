# Princess Formidable
Ruler of the [[Skull Empire]] who provided a benefactor box on the fourth floor containing the bolt later used by [[Katia]].

## Additional Observations
# Princess Formidable
The [[Skull Empire]]'s outspoken representative on the Syndicate Council who urges immediate detonation of the system via the [[Failsafe]] to prevent an AI takeover; she commands a cruiser in orbit above [[Earth]].

## Additional Observations
# Princess Formidable
A prominent off-dungeon figure referenced as having been forcibly brought by the [[AI]] from her spaceship to the [[Desperado Club]]. Cited as an example of who might be selected as a stand-in for the [[Eleventh Floor Arena]].
