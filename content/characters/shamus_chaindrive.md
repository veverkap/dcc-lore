# shamus_chaindrive

• Legendary bugbear treasure hunter and treasure-seeing antagonist in lore; creator/owner of the sentinel automaton [[Ruckus]] and seeker of the [[Gate of the Feral Gods]] in the [[Necropolis of Anser]].
• Bugbear treasure hunter and historical antagonist responsible for constructing or commissioning the automaton sentinel [[Ruckus]].
• Obsessed with the [[Gate of the Feral Gods]] and seeker of the [[Necropolis of Anser]]; known for using constructs and a submarine to pursue artifacts.
• Bugbear submarine [[captain]] who, like [[Ghazi]], came to the region to seek treasure in the [[Necropolis of Anser]].
• A previously-encountered bugbear (submarine [[captain]]) mentioned as part of [[dungeon crawler carl]]'s past experiences; noted as having been reduced to a head in a jar on [[an]] earlier floor.
