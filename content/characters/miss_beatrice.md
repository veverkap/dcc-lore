# miss_beatrice

* Miss [[Beatrice]] is a background NPC who previously insulted [[Billy Maloney]]'s sisters and is referred to as "[[Bea]]" in dialogue.
* She is a named character referenced via her therapist (the Dr. Metcalf hologram) and earlier interactions with [[Donut]].
* Miss [[Beatrice]] is also referred to as "[[Bea]]" and is a neighbor who reacted angrily to police presence at the apartment complex and has prior interactions concerning apartment access.
* She is mentioned in passing related to [[Dr. Metcalf]] and past events with the narrator.
* Miss [[Beatrice]] is a social acquaintance of [[Carl]] and others in previous context.
* She is referenced in a joke involving contact lenses and a drawer.
* [[Beatrice]] (also called [[Bea]]) is [[Carl]]'s ex-girlfriend and the primary owner/handler of [[Princess Donut]]. She is a fragile [[human]] survivor emotionally attached to a cat (nicknaming it [[Ferdinand]] while its actual name is [[Gravy Boat]]).
* Beatrice is a named character referenced as [[an]] "obvious choice" for repurposing due to ties to the "Vengeance of the Daughter" storyline.
* She is mentioned as part of the interview with [[Odette]] alongside [[Carl]] and [[Donut]], contributing to the increased attention [[Carl]] is receiving.
* Beatrice is associated with the interview controversy alongside [[Dungeon Crawler Carl]]; fans repeatedly question [[Carl]] about her and related events.
* She is referred to as "[[Bea]]" or "Miss Beatrice" in the chapter, and a photograph of her appears in a fan-box and triggers personal reflection for [[Carl]].
* Beatrice is a traumatized [[human]] survivor living in New Queen Anne who is emotionally attached to [[Gravy Boat]] and suffers visible scratches and mental strain.
* She is revealed to be alive by [[Odette]], creating a major personal plot development for [[Carl]].
* Beatrice appears on [[Odette]]'s program looking shell-shocked, drugged, and with memory gaps after being kidnapped and trafficked.
* She is mentioned as part of the anecdote and as someone who might use personal grooming (ties into the [[Enchanted Pedicure Kit of the Sylph]] context).
* Beatrice is a central off-screen NPC in [[Carl]]'s backstory, linked to social-engineering and drama (blocked accounts, orchestrated messages) and implicated in hiding family details from [[Carl]].
* She is mentioned as a co-competitor with [[Donut]] in a show (a video clip used by production), part of [[Donut]]'s on-air material.
* Beatrice is a referenced figure from [[Carl]]'s past whose friend [[Trixie]] is discussed in [[an]] anecdote about obsession and violence.
* She is mentioned as having vacationed in the Bahamas and as [[an]] influence on [[Donut]]'s geography knowledge and tastes.
* Beatrice is a local woman whose social interactions and hidden knowledge (including seeing messages and hiding [[Asher]]'s existence from [[Carl]]) play into the emotional fallout.
* She is recognized by [[Donut]] during the hydra encounter, and one of her faces is used by the hydra.
* Beatrice is a minor character referenced in relation to past media-watching (a scene where [[Donut]] turned a movie off), indicating social interactions among the group.
