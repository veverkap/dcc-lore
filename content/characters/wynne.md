# wynne

* Dirigible Gnome Flesh Mechanic (Level 50) found chained beyond the quadrant boundary in the subterranean chamber, holding the Necropolis of [[Anser]] map prior to his death.
* Holds the key to gaining access to the [[Wasteland]].
* Has the ability to make [[Quetzalcoatlus]] temporarily corporeal.
* Was resurrected by [[Donut]]'s [[Second Chance]] spell and presented as the Commandant's uncle.
* Is a central figure in the town's collateral arrangement with the [[gnomes]], with his resistance to cooperation and drug tolerance complicating the code that keeps the town safe.
* Is physically represented in the plot by [[Katia]]'s disguise.
* Has the ability to make [[Quetzalcoatlus]] temporarily corporeal, a key figure Juice Box sought to reach so her brother could touch the creature.
* Is a gnome collateral whose body/ghost is central to the daily code that prevents the town's destruction; his refusal to cooperate has forced the [[dromedarians]] to drug him.
* Is [[an]] incapacitated gnome uncle figure central to the negotiation ploy; impersonated physically by [[Katia]] in the cart and named in the message to [[Commandant Kane]].
* Is a gnome mentioned as part of [[Henrik]]'s original plan (used to give flesh to a ghost); referenced in the torn journal fragment describing attempts to physically touch a ghostly creature.
