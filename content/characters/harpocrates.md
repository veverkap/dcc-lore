# Harpocrates
A deity summoned by an escaped feral god (noted after [[the feral god meatus has entered the realm]]’s arrival) whose appearance is announced by system messages and impacts tactical decisions.

## Additional Observations
# Harpocrates
The deaf god of silence referenced in the chapter; an entity that violently punishes loudness and is engaged in a confrontation with [[the feral god meatus has entered the realm]].

## Additional Observations
# Harpocrates
A godly, mute harpo-like deity pursuing the enormous crawling ball of flesh; central antagonist in the scene who is being distracted by battlefield antics and music (see [[Donut]] and [[the feral god meatus has entered the realm]]).

## Additional Observations
# Harpocrates
Named crawler who was distracted during battle but is now redirecting toward the tower; part of the ongoing fight on the floor.

## Additional Observations
# [[Harpocrates]]
A pursuing god that may target players (especially Donut) and whose behavior could change depending on whether associated gods are freed from the ball.

## Additional Observations
# Harpocrates
A deity whose arrival is announced by the system ("You are in the presence of a deity"). When he is present, nearby players receive temporary skills, such as a level 15 [[Pied Piper]] skill; he is poised at the city's edge and is described as threatening but restrained by defenses.

## Additional Observations
# [[Harpocrates]]
A god/kaiju who appears outside the tower—huge, slim, wearing white-and-gold robes, notable for having had his genitalia stolen. His presence draws attention and is confronted by changelings and ogre guards near the tower.

## Additional Observations
# Harpocrates
A deity conscripted into the conflict (marked with [[Under Orders]]). He wields the ball and shaft (held by the shaft of [[the feral god meatus has entered the realm]]), smashes city blocks, and is listed as a member of the [[Princess Posse]].

## Additional Observations
# [[Harpocrates]]
A much-larger god fighting across Shanty Town, wielding a massive ball/shaft and delivering the killing blow to [[Yarilo]]; he is currently involved in the combat zone near [[Larracos]] and the [[Reaver Castle]].

## Additional Observations
# Harpocrates
A large construct or controllable asset tied to [[Li Na]] and the operation. In the chapter, [[Harpocrates]] is ordered to stand still but is sensed to be resisting the command.

## Additional Observations
# Harpocrates
A giant entity that tosses a large ball into the funnel of [[Castle of Larracos]] to trigger the draining and the raid; his action initiates the critical sequence of events.

## Additional Observations
# Harpocrates
A benefactor/godlike figure whose benefit granted the [[Pied Piper]] skill to [[dungeon crawler carl]]; Harpocrates remains present and observing near the Larracos hole. The chapter notes that the benefit from [[Harpocrates]] becomes permanent due to [[Random Effect: Eris]].
