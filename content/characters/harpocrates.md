# harpocrates

* A deity summoned by [[an]] escaped feral god, announced by system messages, and impacting tactical decisions.
* The deaf god of silence, violently punishing loudness, and engaged in a confrontation with the feral god.
* A godly, mute harpo-like deity pursuing the enormous crawling ball of flesh, central antagonist in the scene.
* A named crawler who was distracted during battle but is now redirecting toward the tower, part of the ongoing fight on the floor.
* A pursuing god that may target players (especially [[Donut]]) and whose behavior could change depending on whether associated gods are freed from the ball.
* A deity whose arrival is announced by the system, granting nearby players temporary skills, such as a level 15 Pied Piper skill.
* A god/ kaiju who appears outside the tower, huge, slim, wearing white- and- gold robes, notable for having had his genitalia stolen.
* A deity conscripted into the conflict, wielding the ball and shaft, smashes city blocks, and is listed as a member of the [[Princess]] Posse.
* A much-larger god fighting across Shanty Town, wielding a massive ball/ shaft, and delivering the killing blow to [[Yarilo]].
* A large construct or controllable asset tied to Li [[Na]] and the operation, ordered to stand still but sensed to be resisting the command.
* A giant entity that tosses a large ball into the funnel of Castle of [[Larracos]] to trigger the draining and the raid.
* A benefactor/godlike figure whose benefit granted the Pied Piper skill to dungeon crawler [[Carl]], remaining present and observing near the [[Larracos]] hole.
* A god who screams in anguish over the loss of his severed organ, curses the "Champion of [[Nekhebit]]," and then disappears from the realm.
