# wall_monitors

• [[Wall Monitors]]: hostile entities that congregate near gangways between lines, representing local threats.
• Lizard-like mobs that can fly short distances and attack from gangways, some of which are lost to a black-hole effect around the pit near the [[Abyss]].
• Congregating mobs that block gangways between lines, presenting as environmental hazards on route.
• A lizard-class species native to the [[Abyss]], tied mechanically to the [[Extinction Sigil Tattoo]] effects.
• Named mobs that flying [[crawlers]] shot at and sometimes got pulled into the [[Abyss]] due to a black-hole effect near the pit.
