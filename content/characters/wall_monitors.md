# Wall Monitors

# [[Wall Monitors]]
Named hostile entities referenced as congregating near gangways between lines (e.g., the gangway to the [[Mindaro line]]), representing local threats the party and allied groups must fight through.

## Additional Observations
# Wall Monitors
Lizard-like named mobs encountered by crawlers who can fly short distances and attack from gangways; some are lost to a black-hole effect around the pit near the [[Abyss]].

## Additional Observations
# [[Wall Monitors]]
Hostile or defensive mobs that are congregating near gangways (reported by [[daniel bautista]]). Represent environmental threats when moving between lines such as [[Mindaro line]].

## Additional Observations
# Wall Monitors
A lizard-class species native to the abyss; were exterminated on a floor (triggering the [[Extinction Event]] achievement). Tied mechanically to the [[Extinction Sigil Tattoo]] effects.

## Additional Observations

## Additional Observations
