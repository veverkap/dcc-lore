# Danger Dingo

# [[Danger Dingo]]
A level-5 dog-like monster with aggressive behavior and an AI that simulates a rough Australian accent. Large and ferocious with distinct corpse-paint-like fur; drops items such as [[Poor Dingo Pelt]] and gold.

## Additional Observations
# Danger Dingo
Large canine mob type kept in kennels within the boss chamber. [[Danger Dingo]] units are initially under kobold control (red on the minimap) until Carl feeds one a [[Pet Biscuit]], turning its dot white and freeing it from slavery.
