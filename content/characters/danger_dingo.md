# danger_dingo

• A level-5 [[dog]]-ike monster with aggressive behavior and [[an]] [[AI]] that simulates a rough Australian accent.
• Large and ferocious with distinct corpse-paint-like fur; drops items such as [[Poor Dingo Pelt]] and gold.
• Initially under kobold control (red on the minimap) until [[Carl]] feeds one a [[Pet Biscuit]], turning its dot white and freeing it from slavery.
