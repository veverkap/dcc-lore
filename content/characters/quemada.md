# quemada

• A level 96 flaming fire fairy, part of [[Team Helix]] and a worshipper of [[Emberus]], who attempts to renegotiate the price after [[an]] agreement.
• A fairy worshipper of [[Emberus]] who approaches [[Carl]] in Hungry Eyes Village, describing a divine vision to slay [[Hellik]] and raises a fee to protect companions like [[Olga]] and [[Finley]], with smoky wings and physically smaller than [[Elle]].
• A companion who gives [[Carl]] pep talks and continues conversationally oblivious to some of the immediate divine disruptions, present physically during the chapter's events.
• A small fire fairy teammate who is emotionally unstable, questioning reality and crying while floating near [[Carl]] and [[Donut]], adding to the group's tension before the race.
• A fire fairy in [[Carl]]'s party suffering a panic disorder after the death of [[Helado]], emotionally volatile during the race, repeatedly invoking [[Emberus]] and requiring calming from [[Finley]] and restraint by the [[Porsuk mercenary]].
• A knocked-out fire fairy mercenary carried by [[Finley]] during the pit stop, later confirmed killed by the mercenary death announcement.
• [[An]] NPC mercenary who poofed out in a flare and left a scorch mark among the dead [[mercenaries]].
