# Concierge Shark
High-level aquatic mob (noted as Level 41) described as jet-black tiger-shark–like predators with glowing red eyes; attracted to blood and common in water-themed dungeons. Nicknamed the "Death's Welcoming Committee" Shark.

## Additional Observations
# [[Concierge Shark]]
A named mob corpse discovered in the flooded alley (Level 41). Described as recently dead and subsequently used by [[dungeon crawler carl]] to disperse soul buildup via [[Daughter’s Kiss]].
