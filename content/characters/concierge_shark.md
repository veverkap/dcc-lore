# concierge_shark

• Level 41 high-level aquatic mob, described as jet-black tiger-shark–like predators with glowing red eyes, attracted to blood, and common in water-themed dungeons.
• Nicknamed the "Death' Welcoming Committee" Shark.
• Found dead and suffocated in the flooded alley, used by [[dungeon crawler carl]] to disperse soul buildup via [[Daughter’'s Kiss]].
• A Level 41 mob type in the necropolis waters, large, jet-black tiger-shark–like creatures with glowing red eyes that are attracted to blood and serve as common underwater death-dealers.
• Used by [[Semeru Warden]] to discharge accumulated soul energy.
• Labeled in-[[Larracos]]’s flooded alley as [[an]] identifiable creature.
