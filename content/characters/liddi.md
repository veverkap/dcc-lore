# liddi

• Female host of the [[Veriluxx New Toys Showcase]], also referred to as "Professor Liddi" during the taping.
• Dresses in a mismatched superhero costume.
• Claims to have helped bring the [[Robot Donut]] toy to life.
• Interacts with [[dungeon crawler carl]] and [[Robot Donut]] on-camera.
• Warm to [[Robot Donut]] on camera and helps steer the commercial when [[Carl]] improvises.
