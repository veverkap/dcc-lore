# ruckus

• The giant cyborg buzz-ard boss, [[Ruckus]], roosts near the town nightly, described as half biological, half mechanical, and avoided by waster patrols.
• It is a Level 55 Borough Boss, a gigantic spring-operated Chicken Hawk Sentinel with steampunk armor and a massive tethered multi-buzzsaw weapon.
• [[Ruckus]] is mentioned as the source of a silver boss box granting loot such as [[Surefires]].
• It is destroyed by coordinated [[Missile (two-stage rocket)]] strikes and vehicle maneuvering after pursuing the party.
