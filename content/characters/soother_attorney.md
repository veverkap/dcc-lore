# Soother Attorney
An alien virtual attorney (referred to as a "soother") who conducts outreach guild deal negotiations with crawlers. Described with large saucer-like eyes, unemotional demeanor, and the habit of bringing snacks to the meeting. Presents formal indenture offers and enforces the outreach guild rules in the meeting room (a [[Zero Zone]]).
