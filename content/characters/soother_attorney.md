# soother_attorney

• [[An]] alien virtual attorney, referred to as a "soother", conducts outreach guild deal negotiations with [[crawlers]], presenting formal indenture offers and enforcing guild rules in the meeting room (a [[Zero Zone]]).
• The soother is described with large saucer-like eyes and [[an]] unemotional demeanor, often bringing snacks to meetings.
• The soother meets with [[Porthus]] in a [[Zero Zone]] to offer indenture deals, providing a list of outreach guild offers and explaining selection mechanics and pre-collapse allowances (e.g., items allowed, but no weapons).
