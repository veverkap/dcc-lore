# mukta

• [[Mukta]] is [[an]] Administrator who messages [[crawlers]], overrides scheduling, and initiates transfers.
• She booked and ran the interview program, and is responsible for scheduling appearances.
• She enforces participation and issues orders to [[dungeon crawler carl]] and [[Princess Donut]] during the broadcast.
• She threatens acceleration (punishment) if guests refuse to comply with the show's requirements.
• She is a kua-ting party member who was eaten by one of [[Lucia Mar]]'s dogs, [[an]] incident remembered by [[Zev]] and [[dungeon crawler carl]].
