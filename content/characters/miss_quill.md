# miss_quill

* Miss [[Quill]] – [[Skyfowl]]. Level 30. Assistant to the Magistrate. Guards access to Magistrate [[FeatherFall]]'s chamber, refuses to disturb the magistrate for non-[[Skyfowl]] victims, and reacts dramatically when presented with Dead Hooker (Quest Clues).
* Mastermind and true power behind the town's corruption, masquerading as [[an]] assistant to the magistrate. Runs the [[krasue]] operation, collects feathers, and sends victims to Remex the Grand for transformation.
* Dark cleric/necromancer figure who cast spells through Remex and others to siphon soul power, orchestrated the creation of [[krasue]] and [[an]] army, and planned the three-part spell called The Final War.
* Used soul crystals to cast spells, contributing to the floor's instability and the creation of dangerous soul-crystal effects.
* Old [[Skyfowl]] lady from a previous floor whose apartment contained over a thousand stuffed monsters, many of which were repurposed by other [[crawlers]].
* Key antagonist in the chapter and real power running the settlement, initially suspected assistant to the magistrate but revealed to be the mastermind controlling the [[krasue]] and the city.
* Deceased NPC/Crawler whose stuffed creatures and glass display cases are central to a local quest, with her death and apartment full of plushies driving much of the investigation in the chapter.
* Powerful dark cleric and conspirator central to the chapter's plot, coordinating soul-siphoning operations, using forgeries, and planning to complete the multi-night spell The Final War to reclaim the Over City for the [[Skyfowl]].
* Used a Soul Crystal to cast a spell that contributed to the floor's instability, connected to the broader soul-crystal problem on the floor.
* Named boss referenced in loot history, killed by [[Carl]] and rewarded with a Bronze Boss box.
* [[Quill]] mentioned as prior content contributing to [[Carl]]'s loot.
