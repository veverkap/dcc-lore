# gremlin_barkeeper

* The [[gremlin]] who runs the Lollipop bar enforces house rules about outside drinks, pours beer, and reacts to the party's antics.
* A generic referenced creature type.
* Accuses the [[Tigrans]] of having killed "your own gremlin" earlier, establishing prior violence.
* A mob whose goo is licked up by a pig after activating its shield; presented as a fallen creature in the garage.
