# mitch_fiorelli

* Level 120 croupier for the [[Wheel of Fortune]] in the [[Desperado Club]]; enforces game rules and demands payment and tips for letting people jump into the Nothing spot.
* Named as the new croupier at the [[top-floor casino]], formerly a wall [[rat]]; referenced by [[Pontiff]] in relation to portal testing.
* [[Mitch]] is the mimic antagonist, [[an]] Adolescent Shadow Mimic at level 120, who absorbed the real NPC Mitch (a [[Wall Rat]]) and acts as [[an]] Enforcer sent by <<Redacted>> to attack the Cabaret contacts.
* [[Mitch Prime]] is identified as a [[Wall Rat]] NPC who was eaten by [[Mitch]].
* The real [[Mitch]] is a [[Wall Rat]] NPC who was consumed by [[Mitch]].
