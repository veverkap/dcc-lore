# dmitri

• Character referenced in passing as having made a poor race choice that combined himself with his brother, part of the backstory about rival groups.
• Right- headed member of the two-headed [[nodling]] duo, identified as a mage-class (Visionary) and expressed a desire to meet [[Donut]] and recounted his lost cat Kapitan Whiskers, one half of the twin team with [[Maxim]].
• One of the Popovs, seen being escorted away, casually bids dungeon crawler [[Carl]] goodbye and references [[Donut]] in passing.
• Named character referenced in backstory (notably for accidentally selecting a troublesome race and related drama), mentioned in alliance/convention context.
• Right-side twin of a two-headed [[nodling]] pair encountered by dungeon crawler [[Carl]] in the green room, identified as a mage class, a Visionary, gentle and sentimental, remembers [[an]] orange cat named Kapitan Whiskers and expresses a desire to meet [[Donut]].
• One of the twin Popov brothers who calls out to dungeon crawler [[Carl]] and is escorted from the production room, serves as a brief personal contact for dungeon crawler [[Carl]] amid the chapter's confinement and procedural events.
• Mentioned as [[an]] example of a typical ogre-like entity in the game, used for comparison to the spiked Ogre.
