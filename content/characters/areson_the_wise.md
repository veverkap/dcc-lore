# Areson the Wise
Level-45 ogre strongman encountered near the bridge. Resembles other ogre strongmen (e.g., [[Apollon the Mighty]]) but is a distinct character aligned with the town defenders.

## Additional Observations
# Areson the Wise
An elite ogre (seven-foot strongman) encountered in the jungle who delivers a dead hunter to [[dungeon crawler carl]] and is part of [[Tsarina Signet]]’s contingent.

## Additional Observations
# Areson the Wise
Referenced as Signet’s bodyguard and used as a comparison to the nodlings’ species traits; provides background context on nodling biology and splitting on death.
