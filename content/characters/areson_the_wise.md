# areson_the_wise

• Level-45 ogre strongman encountered near the bridge, distinct from other ogre strongmen like [[Apollon the Mighty]].
• Seven-foot strongman encountered in the jungle, delivers a dead hunter to [[dungeon crawler carl]], and is part of [[Tsarina Signet]]’s contingent.
• Bodyguard of [[Tsarina Signet]], provides background context on [[nodling]] biology and splitting on death.
• Physically similar to other ogre strongmen, appears alongside [[Tsarina Signet]] and is present among elites in the area.
