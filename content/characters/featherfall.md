# featherfall

• Implicated antagonist in the Sex Worker questline; suspected lead baddie coordinating undead and city [[elves]].
• A deceased figure central to the backstory; once in control of local powers and involved in the ritual that created Remex and powered the [[Swordsmen]].
• A formerly powerful [[Skyfowl]] figure involved with control of the town [[Swordsmen]], instrumental in the events that led to the manifestation of Remex the Grand.
