# pawna

• A goddess of peace, described as [[Grull]]'s sister and arch enemy, associated with the rare potion [[Pawna's Tears]] that grants +5 to any chosen skill or spell.
• Invoked in dialogue, such as "What in Pawna's name?" and used as [[an]] insult towards a cleric's mother.
• The goddess of peace and sister/arch enemy of [[Grull]], with her name appearing on the legendary potion [[Pawna's Tears]] found in [[Carl]]'s [[Legendary Deity's Box]].
