# station_mimics

• [[Station Mimics]]: Six named entities referenced by [[Madison]] that are ravenous and cranky, part of the station system's hazards and passenger routing mechanics.
• [[Station Mimic]]: A massive city-boss mimic occupying the trainyard near [[station 433 terminus]], described as a potato-shaped blob the size of multiple stations that can transform into buildings and spawn legged pieces when damaged.
• Large, train-station-sized [[mimics]] that swallow people, belch walking mouth minions, and pose a boss-level threat, referred to by [[daniel bautista]] and warned about by [[dungeon crawler carl]].
• Named city-boss variant that turns stations into mouths, reported devouring passengers at station 433 and producing walking mouth minions, active threat in transit tunnels.
• Classified as a city boss that splits into mobile pieces if damaged.
