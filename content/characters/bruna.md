# bruna

• Named companion mentioned by [[Osvaldo]] as being tough; part of [[Osvaldo]]'s team or mount.
• Fast-moving racer who rushes ahead at the start of the chapter; part of the racing melee in the factory course and a visible presence among the competitors.
• Giant gnu associated with [[Osvaldo]]; patted affectionately after the race and part of the team's cast of mounts/companions.
• Named mount or vehicle that other racers are riding (e.g., [[Osvaldo]] and [[Filipe]] are astride Bruna).
• Named unicorn/vehicle used as a mount in the heat; [[Osvaldo]] sits on Bruna's back at the starting line.
• Opponent NPC/crawler whose shield temporarily protects her but is then breached by team tactics; she recovers quickly and re-enters the lobby fight.
• Named slaughter gnu mount under threat during the rooftop sequence; target of a grenade and multiple poison dart bombs; saved temporarily by a trio of [[potion balls]] and aided by summoned [[emergency gremlins]] performing medical repairs.
