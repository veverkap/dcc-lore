# Diwata/Circe
A powerful mantis-aligned deity/figure ("Diwata/Circe") who blended in with mantises inside the castle, gave hunters back their magic, and is directly involved in the enemy's strategy. Targeted by [[Samantha]] and disrupted when soul crystals were used.

## Additional Observations
# Diwata/Circe
A goddess figure (Diwata) also called Circe in the text; present in the castle assisting mantises and giving hunters their magic back. Described in a flaming mantis birthing form at one point. Central antagonist whose presence prevents the party from breaking the [[Peace Seal (anti-violence seal)]] easily.
