# diwatacirce

• A powerful mantis-aligned deity/figure, [[Diwata]]/Circe, blends in with [[mantises]], restores [[hunters]]' magic, and is involved in the enemy's strategy.
• Targeted by [[Samantha]] and disrupted by soul crystals.
• Described as a goddess figure ([[Diwata]]) or Circe, present in the castle, assisting [[mantises]], and giving [[hunters]] their magic back.
• Appears in a flaming mantis birthing form.
• Prevents the party from breaking the [[Peace Seal (anti-violence seal)]] easily.
