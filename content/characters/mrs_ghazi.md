# mrs_ghazi

• A named sand ooze, identified as a "Sand Ooze" and a "Level 52 Borough Boss", is a minion of [[Psamathe]].
• It exhibits devotion to a [[human]] and unusual behavior for its species.
• It houses its glowing core under a recliner in [[Ghazi' Workshop]].
• It is the central antagonist in the basement/ooze chamber sequence.
