# were-castors

• Magically transformed rodent-like creatures, transformed by [[Imogen]] long ago, serving as elite fighting force.
• A class/species of furry, humanoid [[NPCs]], including [[Clint]] and [[Holger]], with transformative traits, bite- based infection risk, and village culture.
• Shapeshifting allies who helped in the assault, gathered around [[nadine]]'s corpse, and assisted in retrieving [[Signet]] and cleanup/search duties.
• Function as tribal/mob allies, capable of close-quarters violence, animalistic bites, and powerful tracking by sound/smell.
