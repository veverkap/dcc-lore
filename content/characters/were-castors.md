# Were-castors
A transformed warrior type: rodent-like creatures that six elite fighters were turned into by a transfiguration cast by [[Imogen]] long ago. These six serve as hardened, elite were-warriors who rode the Bearded Sage Caterpillar.

## Additional Observations
# Were-castors
A class/species of furry, humanoid NPCs (including [[Clint]] and [[Holger]]) who have transformative traits, bite-based infection risk, and village culture; operate as aggressive melee mobs/NPCs in the chapter.

## Additional Observations
# Were-castors
A group of were-caster units allied with [[Signet]] who led parts of the assault, performed recoveries around [[nadine]]'s corpse, and returned to their short chee forms after combat. Function as tribal/mob allies.
