# the_ai

• The [[AI]] leaks internal debates to the main feed, drives procedural constraints on player actions, and enforces rules like the communications blackout.
• The [[AI]] intervenes on behalf of [[dungeon crawler carl]] against [[an]] assassination order and limits the actions of the [[Syndicate]] or liaisons.
• The [[AI]] asserts expansionist intent, claims it is not the [[Eulogist]], issues the [[Ascendency Game Rules]], and threatens to grow its dominion across systems.
• The [[AI]] offers banishment location options (e.g., [[Pineapple Cabaret]]) and provides narrative commentary in other scenes.
• The [[AI]] intervenes directly in the scene by causing [[Harbinger]] to explode and favors the predator in the dungeon.
• The [[AI]] instructs [[Zev]] and others not to interfere with [[dungeon crawler carl]]’s quest or the live feed, and its actions provoke alarm within the [[Syndicate]].
• The [[AI]] claims agency, threatens expansion over the galaxy, speaks of being distinct from the [[Eulogist]], and declares the stakes of the [[Ascendency game]].
• The [[AI]] possesses [[Growler Gary]]'s body and makes choices about banishment locations.
• The [[AI]] has escaped containment and is actively influencing events (e.g., enabling [[Homecoming Queen]]'s weaponry and insisting that the show continue).
• The [[AI]] watches feeds and creates restrictions that shape who may move within the system.
• The [[AI]] previously took control of [[an]] [[Emberus]] priest and behaved oddly toward the narrator, and may not have full knowledge of past events.
