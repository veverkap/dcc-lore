# Nebular Priests (nebs)

# Nebular Priests (nebs)
Group of robed hunter/NPC enemies engaged in combat with mantis warriors in [[Alucarda]]. Several are killed by explosives and mantis attacks; some survivors heal and retaliate with magic missiles. Their highest stat is intelligence on certain killed targets, which interacts with [[Ring of Divine Suffering]] gains.
