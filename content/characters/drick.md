# drick

• [[An]] older, scarred, albino moon elf and former crawler who used a [[Plasma saw]] as his main weapon and is controlled by a [[valtay]].
• A panelist and ally who comments on the [[nebulars]]' beliefs and gives [[Carl]] a subtle acknowledgment during the arena discussion.
• A bald Dream elf iteration who is a recipient of a [[Valtay life extension]] and has a [[Valtay]] worm in the brain, recently passing a referee exam but inexperienced as [[an]] adjutant.
• A psychotic and self-promoting adjutant candidate.
• [[An]] "infected elf outworlder" who serves as [[an]] adjutant to [[Ferdinand]] and has a brain parasite distinct from the protagonist's rock friend, hosting a [[valtay]].
• A Dream elf adjutant for [[team retribution]] who appears in the club vestibule carrying a tablet and acts as [[an]] intermediary in negotiations.
• A Dream elf adjutant associated with the NPC team (Team Retribution) who appears in the trenches to observe the assault and is capable of teleporting or going invisible to warn allies.
• [[An]] adjutant for [[team retribution]] who stands with [[Baroness Victory]] during broadcast updates and is characterized as slightly inebriated and opinionated.
• A party calling a foul for alleged illegal interference related to the newly emergent [[war mages]] and pursuing a hearing/quorum to challenge their status.
• [[An]] adjutant present with [[Victory]] and [[Tart]] at the [[Reaver castle]] approach.
• [[An]] adjutant carrying [[Ferdinand]] on his bald head and speaks briefly with [[Carl]] about next-floor plans.
• [[An]] older, scarred, albino moon elf and former crawler noted for a high body count in past seasons, who discusses tactics and the mantid threat with [[Uptown Hal]] and [[Sydnee Iglacia]].
• A panel participant and commentator who interacts with [[dungeon crawler carl]] during the show and provides perspective on nebular beliefs and the "Ballad of Survival."
• A bald Dream elf iteration who is [[Valtay]]-infected (contains a [[Valtay]] worm in the brain) and a former crawler and consultant who passed the referee exam and is a candidate adjutant.
• Another adjutant candidate described as "psychotic" and eager to make a name for himself, contrasted with [[Odette]]'s choice.
