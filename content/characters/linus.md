# linus

• A soother alien tourist who won "extra access" due to being the only visitor present; wears a T-shirt and fanny pack, and is timid around [[elle mcgib]] and [[Donut]].
• A member of the crawler group at the table who makes a comment about music preferences (Creedence Clearwater Revival).
• A persistent fan-group guy associated with [[elle mcgib]] whose presence in the garage bothers [[Imani]].
• A crawler present at the table who comments about music and joins the group conversation.
• Described as "[[Elle]]'s fan group guy" who hangs around the garage and makes [[Imani]] uncomfortable; motivates [[Imani]] to stay in the safe room.
• Deceased brother of [[Minus]]. A former biological license [[engineer]] for [[the Dream]] who purchased citizenship and a ship.
• A member/fan in the group described as wearing a shirt with a moving all-over print of [[Elle]] and sitting beside [[Grigori the Placid]] in the Lollipop.
• A strange, partially-corporeal tourist character who interacts inappropriately with [[Donut]]; insured custody and behavior is enforced by [[Elle]].
• A young crawler being babysat by [[Bautista]] outside the stables and being celebrated before restrictions prevent him from entering town during parts of the race.
• A crawler operating a tea station at the farewell, pouring tea for others like [[Karac]] and interacting with attendees including [[Prepotente]] and [[Imani]].
• [[An]] impostor agent among the survivors who is revealed and emotionally collapses when exposed; connected to a "real Linus" who had posted a revealing essay.
• [[An]] [[infiltrator]] figure mentioned earlier whose suspicious presence rattled [[Carl]] and the group.
