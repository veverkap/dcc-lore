# Rat Hooligan
A Rat-Kin mob archetype referenced by Mordecai's infobox. Described as the "smartest, fastest, and ugliest" Rat-Kin class — physically strong with a working grasp of magic, distinct from [[Rat Brute]] and [[Rat Shaman]] (mentioned as comparisons). The term is used as Mordecai's class identity.
