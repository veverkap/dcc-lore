# Katia Grimmsdottir

# Katia Grimmsdottir
Called "The Scavenger’s Daughter" and an important crawler/heiress. Wakeful recipient of [[Eileithyia]]'s boon; chooses to be made pregnant and is carried away from the dungeon after being restored to human form and fitted with cybernetics.
