# agatha

Here is the consolidated markdown list:

• Crawler #7,450, elderly, homeless woman who was the first to enter the maze entrance; known for her shopping cart, blue IKEA bag, and for spraying a giant happy-[[face]] symbol on the wall with metallic silver spray paint.
• Elderly survivor encountered in the arena, injured and bloodied, who demands possessions back; noted to be level two (and effectively level four after boss XP).
• Fellow crawler mentioned as having cleared and killed grubs in the neighborhood; credited as killer on a lootable corpse.
• Elderly crawler who is missing from the group and notably absent from the televised footage; presumed lost or dead by others in the shelter.
• Named individual mentioned briefly as absent during the evacuation; sought by [[Brandon]] and others while the group coordinates movement.
• Level-four crawler encountered pushing a squeaking shopping cart containing salvaged goods (including tech manufactured by [[Valtay]]); interacts briefly with Dungeon Crawler [[Carl]] before descending to the next floor.
• Woman referenced by Dungeon Crawler [[Carl]] as someone he considered possibly connected to the [[Valtay]]; [[Carl]] decides to avoid further involvement with her.
• Older crawler encountered at the stairwell pushing a squeaking shopping cart and at level 8 without a chosen class; notable for the pink flamingo in her cart and her mysterious descent.
• Familiar crawler encountered at the stairwell, now level 8 and still [[human]] without a chosen class; pushes a shopping cart with a pink flamingo and moves down the stairwell despite [[Imani]]'s attempt to stop her.
• Homeless, shopping-cart-pushing woman mentioned by name during [[Orren]] the Liaison's interrogation as someone the Syndicate checked as a potential outside contact – ultimately ruled not to be in the [[crawlers]]' bubble during the incident.
• Named [[Residual]] referenced as a different kind from the speaker [[Paulie]] ([[Goff]]); mentioned as having previously interacted with Dungeon Crawler [[Carl]] and as [[an]] example of the variety among the [[Residuals]].
• Agent number 22, a long-lived, non-[[Valtay]] operative living undercover on Earth (stationed in Wenatchee), who scavenges supplies, infiltrates other shells, and participates in the recruitment conflict against [[Apothecary]].
• Level 23 [[residual]] – a hyperspatial alien lifeform inhabiting a planetary host body – currently targeted by powerful factions and explicitly warned against by [[Zev]] and the Syndicate; previously compared to a [[residual]] met at a homeless shelter.
• Disparaged comparison target in the conversation – described as "the smelly homeless lady with the squeaky shopping cart."
• [[Residual]] who became the warlord of the War Mages team; described as the rarer kind of [[residual]] aligned with the Nebulars and the worship of the [[Eulogist]], making her [[an]] adversary to the [[Apothecary]].
• Co-warlord unexpectedly assigned to the war mages; her appointment and motivations are unclear and central to political confusion.
• Warlord mentioned by Warlord [[Akuma]] as [[an]] enemy who can jump to the Waiting Room and reappear outside stairwells; implicated in dangerous actions that endanger everyone.
• Target mentioned as the subject of [[Akuma]]'s orders to [[Carl]]; implicated in wider plots connected to the castle and the war mages.
• Older crawler who appears pushing a squeaking shopping cart with a pink flamingo; is level 8 and still listed as [[human]] without a chosen class; walks past the group into the stairwell.
• Familiar crawler encountered pushing a squeaking shopping cart; currently level 8 and still labeled [[human]] without a chosen class; disappears down the stairwell during the sequence.
• Shopping-cart-pushing homeless woman mentioned by Dungeon Crawler [[Carl]] and suspected briefly by [[Orren]] the Liaison as a possible outside source of information; explicitly stated not to be in [[Carl]]'s bubble at the time of the incident.
• Distant crawler seen in another glass room, associated with a shopping cart visual in the spiral of pods.
