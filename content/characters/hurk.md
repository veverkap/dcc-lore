# hurk

• A gleener (fish-like alien) and veteran CrawlCon panelist who floats in a virtual tank and speaks with a formal accent.
• Designer of the [[desperado club]] and a perennial panel presence.
• Serves as a judge in the daycare art contest and mentors the narrator briefly.
• A gleener judge at [[CrawlCon]]'s daycare art contest.
• Mentors and advises [[dungeon crawler carl]].
• Casual, often intoxicated or distracted, and familiar with convention politics and factions like [[plenty]] and the [[Apothecary]].
