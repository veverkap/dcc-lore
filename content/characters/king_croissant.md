# King Croissant

# [[King Croissant]]
A tiny summoned kitten totem (called "the Younger, Better, and Smarter Cat") that sings and teleports with Alpha Carl; presented as an adorable but effective card-to-tempo totem.

## Additional Observations
# King Croissant
A totem card that appeared among the hydra's deck and was referenced as a cat-like entity. Mentioned as having timed out before needing to be killed, and later dissipated when [[Donut]] discarded cards.
