# the_sledge

* A level-35 rock monster bodyguard, often referred to as "[[Sledgie]]", who accompanies [[Donut]] and [[Bomo]] and provides protection spells.
* Casts a protective magic on the party at the [[desperado club]] and accompanies [[Donut]] into restricted areas.
* A large rock-monster bodyguard who carries [[Donut]] and later pats her gently.
* A tuxedoed, level-35 rock monster (cretin) bodyguard paired with [[Bomo]] who protects the narrator's party and comically dances with [[Donut]].
* A rock-monster ally who accompanies [[Donut]] into the Desperado Club and casts a magic protection spell on the party.
* A potential NPC hire from the Desperado Club mentioned as a prospective tank replacement; referenced alongside [[Bomo]].
* A large bodyguard creature assigned to [[Carl]]'s group who stands guard at the Desperado Club; interacts nonverbally with [[Donut]].
* A crawler/ally who remains in the safe room alongside [[Bomo]] to perform tasks during the masquerade; referenced as part of the group's operational plan.
* A rock-monster companion who casts a magic protection spell for the party and accompanies [[Donita Grace]] into the [[desperado club]].
* A rock-monster ally used as muscle and protection in the club, who casts or benefits from protective spells and physically watches party members like [[Donut]].
