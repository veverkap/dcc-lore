# damascus_steel

• [[Damascus Steel]]: NPC and stripper, familiar [[face]] in the [[desperado club]] scene, and assistant lead dancer at the [[Penis Parade]].
• Level 75 Ifrit, metal-feeling, scarred, and out-of-focus, with stubby horns and white, pupil-less eyes.
• Emotionally affected by [[Astrid]]'s death and knows the protagonist.
• Claims familial ties to [[Astrid]] and pleads with [[dungeon crawler carl]] to hire the Penis Parade crew as [[mercenaries]].
• Son of [[Hamed]] and [[Astrid]], brother to [[Anaconda]], and sibling of [[Anaconda]].
• Known fighter, previously encountered by [[Donut]], and still alive.
• Member of the [[Guild of Suffering]] and connected to [[Donut]] and [[Katia]].
• Has a family history tied to the [[desperado club]] and pleads with [[dungeon crawler carl]] to hire the dancers to save them from reassignment.
