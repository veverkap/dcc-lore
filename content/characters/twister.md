# twister

* A named flying/balloon house rig used by [[Louis]] and [[Firas]] to ferry people and equipment, with stability affected by crosswinds.
* A locale/feature in the chapter used as a takeoff area, referenced during flight planning as the Twister descends back toward the [[pazuzu]] village.
* A named air- based threat referenced among targets anti-air guns are calibrated to destroy.
* A floating house/aircraft used for heavy explosives and air support, hovering over the battlefield and carrying the team's ordnance.
* A ship where a notable earlier event occurred (fifth floor memory), referenced in recollections tied to past encounters and loot.
* A house/airdevice and its net used by the team, involved in stabilizing and landing operations during the storm.
* A named location/phenomenon referenced as a place the party must drop off the edge of and pull up from during flight, avoiding central dangers to reach [[Orthrus]].
* A vessel/ platform from which [[Louis]] can deploy wide-area potion effects like "Tent the House" to distribute [[Uncle Morty's Insta Lawn-Kill and Undead Repellant, Extra-Strength Edition]] at range.
* A floating house/airborne platform used as a mobile base and air support in the raid, carrying shields and enough explosives to level the town.
* A vessel/ship that functions as a meeting point and transport for some party members (e.g., [[Samantha]]), planned to be used as [[an]] escape route if the raid goes sour.
* A vessel (the ship on the fifth floor) referenced in memories triggered by conversations, used as a locational touchstone in recalled past events involving other [[crawlers]].
