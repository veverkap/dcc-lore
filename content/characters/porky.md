# porky

* The nickname for the second half of [[Corcunda ([[Corky]])]], required to complete quest part two.
* The other half of [[Corcunda]] produced by the Split Personality spell, dislikes [[Dong Quixote]], and left to pursue singing.
* A named figure referenced as someone who supposedly hates [[Dong]] and is part of the social tensions in the bar.
* A target of the charisma/singing plan, the team hopes that a successful performance will charm [[Porky]] into their guild.
* The left half of the bisected entity [[Corcunda]], a [[half-mantaur]] NPC in a white containment/gimp suit with many zippers, encountered in the [[Stage Performance Guild]] and willing to join a rescue to reunite with [[Corky]].
* A hulking, one-eyed crawler in a containment suit, emotionally attached to [[Dong Quixote]] and enraged at [[Carl]] for proceeding with the rejoin, present guarding/recovering the subject.
* A [[half-mantaur]] guild figure who stands behind the narrator during the [[Safe Room]] conversation, shares a history with [[Dong]] (the scarf anecdote), and is named as one of three [[mercenaries]] the narrator can bring on a dangerous mission.
* A named mount/companion (wearing a white gimp suit) associated with [[Dong]], noted for interacting with [[Penelope]] in the stables and for being present in the race vehicle.
* The other [[mantaur]] half that appears to merge/reunite with [[Corky]] in the back of the truck, speaks reassuringly and helps stabilize the injured [[mantaur]].
* The other half of the split [[mantaur]] pair, [[Porky]] has exposed guts and is being held and wrapped alongside [[Corky]] until [[Grigori]]'s fusion spell completes and they become [[Corcunda]].
* A referenced character in dialogue about past reconciliations with [[Corcunda]] ("you and Porky hadn't made amends"), implying prior relationships relevant to the [[mantaur]]'s choices.
