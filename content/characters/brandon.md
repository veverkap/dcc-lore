# brandon

Here is the consolidated markdown list:

• Wields a massive [[War Hammer]] and helps construct and place heavy sections of the makeshift fortress nicknamed [[The Speedbump]].
• Uses brute strength in melee against the [[Ball of Swine]] and its constituent [[Tuskling Knight]]s.
• Leader of a crew of [[crawlers]] operating on the floors; suspected to be waiting near the first-floor collapse.
• Leader figure among the group in the storm shelter; discusses strategy for descending to the [[third floor]] with [[dungeon crawler carl]] and volunteers to scout the way.
• Helps shepherd residents into the Safe Room; administers potions and mutters angrily about [[Jack]].
• Party member and builder who advised on the launcher design and physics, worked with [[Chris]] on construction, and helps deploy tactics against the [[Rage Elemental]] and grubs.
• Survivor who helps push improvised people-[[bucket]] transport with [[Chris]] and [[Imani]].
• Guides his group via a scorched hallway to [[an]] exit, offers to pick up salvage, and shares [[an]] emotional farewell with [[dungeon crawler carl]].
• Friend and correspondent of [[dungeon crawler carl]] who sent a long message about selection, race choices, and loot distribution after floor recaps.
• Deceased Crawler who sent a final message found in the "Messages from deceased [[Crawlers]]" folder; describes holding a line against Shade [[Gremlins]] on the third floor and provides a personal plea to reconcile with his brother.
• Brother of [[Chris]] and former member of the old caretaking crew; died protecting the team from Shade [[Gremlins]].
• Left a message for [[Chris Andrews 2]] prior to the reveal of the parasite takeover; used as a conversational touchpoint.
• Deceased Crawler whose final message appears in [[Carl]]'s "Messages from deceased [[Crawlers]]" folder; killed by Shade [[Gremlins]] on the third floor; his last message reveals personal regrets and the floor collapse.
• Young Crawler who died protecting others; his choices influenced other [[crawlers]]' race/class picks and his death is mourned by acquaintances like [[elle mcgib]].
• Named individual in the party's circle who advised [[Imani]] about retaining coupons; referenced in dialogue about how to handle unwanted items.
• Friend of [[dungeon crawler carl]] who sends a final chat message from a dying third-floor encounter; his death-message informs [[dungeon crawler carl]] of the collapse and requests he relay apologies to his brother [[Chris]].
• Brother of [[Chris]] and former member of the old caretaking crew; died protecting the team from Shade [[Gremlins]]; his last written message is referenced as a poignant character moment.
• One of the brothers working at [[Meadowlark Elder Care]] who occasionally shares food with [[Agatha]] and is part of the local surface context used to attract [[an]] entrance.
