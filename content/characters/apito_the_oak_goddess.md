# apito_the_oak_goddess

* [[Apito]], the Oak Goddess: a deity whose worshippers were abandoned by elf mothers, prompting [[Nekhebit]]'s jealousy and curse.
* Apito is the Oak Mother, a revered deity in local elf religion and the spiritual figure behind the Apito Education Center and the "Plantation" vision.
* Apito is mentioned as the deity whose worship displaced [[Nekhebit]] in old elf lore and appears in the crossbow's item lore.
* Apito is a named deity example mentioned in the cookbook notes illustrating powerful sponsored gods that can appear in the crawl, tied to the broader "Celestial Ascendency" sponsorship mechanics.
* Apito is the other parent of [[Grull]], forming part of the god-of-war's origin lore.
* Apito is a higher deity mentioned in relation to [[Diwata]]'s lineage and the larger pantheon ([[Diwata]] is "one of Apito's brood").
* Apito is credited with putting the ballroom protections into place and is invoked by [[Theobold]] when explaining the security measures to dungeon crawler [[Carl]].
* Apito is also called the Blessed Oak Mother or the Oak Mother, and is the goddess whose ritual established the Butcher's Masquerade.
* Apito forms the guestlist herself and is tied to the ritual that may grant a boon.
* Apito is a powerful figure who "forms the guestlist herself" and is tied to the ritual that may grant a boon.
* Apito is mentioned as a target of [[Nekhebit]]'s grudge and is part of the [[Emberus]] quest.
* Apito is a deity mentioned in [[Ysalte]]'s backstory as having threatened her with the Nothing, prompting her flight to [[Sheol]].
* Apito is depicted in the stained glass at Club Vanquisher's door and is mentioned in the context of the window that the rams interpret as their god.
* Apito is referenced by [[Hellik]] as "our mother and sister, Apito," alleged to be corrupted and part of the divine political intrigues mentioned in the chapter.
* Apito is a god who had [[Carl]] listed as "friend of the church" despite wider hostility, and is linked to the temple events.
* Apito is a deity referenced by [[Carl]] as connected to [[Krakaren]] and other god-scale events, mentioned in conversation about divine involvement in the arena chaos.
* Apito is a legendary figure referenced by the [[AI]] as [[an]] example of how gods in the Ascendency have been killed in myth.
* Apito is the Oak Mother, revered in local elf mythology, and is cited by [[Mordecai]] as the tree goddess worshipped by [[elves]] and as the origin of the fanatical beliefs motivating groups like the City [[Elves]].
* Apito is a deity invoked in the crossbow's lore as the deity favored over [[Nekhebit]], part of the myth explaining the Fae split referenced in the weapon description.
* Apito is a named deity referenced in the cookbook notes as [[an]] example of a powerful god that can be summoned, part of the roster of gods treated as dangerous summons.
* Apito is a higher-level deity in the pantheon, with [[Diwata]] described as "one of Apito's brood," situating Apito within divine lineage and pantheon dynamics.
* Apito is a goddess referenced as the originator of the protections applied to guests at the masquerade, mentioned by [[Theobold]] as responsible for the queen's protection mechanics.
* Apito is a deity-liked figure referenced in the stage announcement who "wishes for us to mingle with our enemies," invoked as the spiritual justification for the Masquerade's rules and rituals.
* Apito is a powerful external figure/entity who controls or oversees the ritual/ party called the Butcher's Masquerade, said to form the guestlist herself and grant (or fail to grant) boons at the event.

* Apito is a legendary figure invoked by the [[AI]] ("Will you kill me, much the way Apito did in the legends?") used as a mythic reference point for killing gods.
