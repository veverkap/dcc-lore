# gravy_boat

• Nicknamed yellow cat from [[Princess Donut]]'s backstory, associated with [[Marjory]] and [[Donut]]'s youthful antics.
• Feral/stray cat central to the settlement incident in [[New Queen Anne]], targeted for containment by Syndicate agents and ultimately knocked out and placed in a [[Bounty Cube]].
• Named crawler/player mentioned in social banter, possible target of [[Juice Box]]'s ire.
• NPC/Person the group intends to consult for assistance, previously considered as someone [[Juice Box]] might convince to help.
• Notorious, fast stray cat central to multiple plot beats, hunted by Syndicate walk-on teams and later found by [[Bea]] and [[Brad]] in the collapsed city.
• Took part in killing a creeper and is noted as [[an]] eccentric presence.
• Fellow crawler mentioned by [[dungeon crawler carl]], referenced in passing when describing recent events involving them.
• Named NPC referenced in the context of Faction Wars and audience reaction, mention causes a gasp among the party.
• Named crawler referenced in banter with [[Donut]] and [[dungeon crawler carl]] about truce naming, part of the social fabric of the [[Princess]] Posse.
• Named NPC/faction-related entity mentioned in connection with pending action items, its mention elicits a reaction from [[Donut]].
• Named cat associated with [[Marjory Williams]], referenced in the conversation about abuse and the cat being harmed previously.
• Present in the apartment who "never woke up" during the scene, mentioned by [[Carl]] as part of the survivors left behind on the couch.
