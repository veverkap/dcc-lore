# swamp_wights

• Undead mobs referenced in explosives/infusion notes, vulnerable to potion-infused smokes.
• Class of undead monsters targeted by infused smoke/bomb tactics, as described by [[Crawler Sinjin]] and [[Crawler Forkith]].
• Vulnerable to certain infused smoke or bomb tactics, as noted in alchemical notes within [[The Dungeon Anarchist’’s Cookbook]].
