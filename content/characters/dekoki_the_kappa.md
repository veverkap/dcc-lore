# dekoki_the_kappa

* [[Kappa]] NPC who becomes paralyzed, spills water from his bowl-head, and falls into [[Donut]] near the end of the performance, contributing to the chaotic finale.
* [[Dekoki]], a Level 84 [[kappa]] performer, is part of team Yokai and keeps her head-bowl water sealed with wrap and rubber bands while singing karaoke.
* She is a named [[kappa]] performer onstage at the Lollipop, described as a Seductress.
* She regains mobility after the Minister of Blood-Letting pours beer into her head-bowl and apologizes to [[Donut]], requiring help reapplying Saran Wrap.
* She is a karaoke regular from Team Yokai, performing onstage while her team cheers her on.
* She pats [[Carl]] on the back before his performance.
* She is the source of plastic wrap used to seal [[mantaur]] brains, mentioned as the wrapping material used on [[Corky]] and [[Porky]].
