# Parvati / Alexandro

# Parvati / Alexandro
Agent number 21 who originally used the human name Parvati and later abandons that shell to assume the identity Alexandro. A compatriot of [[Agatha]], also a long-lived operative involved in Earth-season recruitment and infiltration at the Mumbai site. Reports killing and inhabiting a Borant-employed human in order to proceed inside their transport.

## Additional Observations
# Parvati / Alexandro
Agent number 21 who peeled away alongside [[Agatha]]. Originally known as [[Parvati]], she kills and infiltrates a human host and thereafter adopts the name [[Alexandro]] to conceal her recovered guise. She reports a forward team on the [[Mumbai site]] and coordinates with other agents.
