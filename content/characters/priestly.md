# priestly

• Cookbook author referenced by [[dungeon crawler carl]] who loved the city of [[Larracos]] so much it drove him mad; used as a cultural touchstone in the chapter's description of the city.
• Author of the 14th edition cookbook and a respected source of dungeon info (noted as someone who would have acted differently than [[Carl]] in moral dilemmas).
• Former crawler and author of the "Note from Crawler Priestly" entries, broken mentally by the dungeon after witnessing the destruction of [[Larracos]] and its [[NPCs]].
• A conscript crawler whose diary entries open the chapter (notably a doomed entry about the Skull Empire battle).
• Cookbook author referenced in relation to past players' lack of knowledge about war-time mechanics (specifically the ability to conscript mobs).
