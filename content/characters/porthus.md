# porthus

• A wealthy sponsor-figure arguing with [[Dr. Hu]] about the risks and costs of backing a [[human]] crawler; skeptical about the crawler's chances and worried about political fallout involving groups like the [[Valtay]] and [[kua-tin]].
• A High Elf crawler whose class is Rogue, Top Level 89, and survived indentureship after taking a deal on the 11th floor; worked as a Game Guide and author of the second edition of the [[Dungeon Anarchist’s Cookbook]].
• A militant leader who ordered installation of enhancement-zone weaponry on the [[Homecoming Queen]] and publicly announced the formation of a new militant group warning Syndicate governments to comply with demands.
• A higher-profile figure mentioned as publicly distancing himself from [[Colonel Boomer]] after a controversial incident; tied to crawler community politics.
• A former crawler and ally who recognizes [[Volteeg]] as the cookbook author, tries to motivate him to act, and is involved in later [[Operatic]] operations and decisions.
• A named individual referenced as not being in the dungeon this season and explicitly not the sponsor of [[Theia]] in the current context.
• A crawler referenced as someone close to [[justice lights team]]; mentioned as one of the few who had not been told certain intimate details of [[Justice]]'s past.
• The architect of the off-dungeon scheme to purchase ships and finance mass escape to the [[Plenty system]] with the help of the [[Open Intellect Pacifist Network]].
• A character who knows [[justice lights team]]'s plan and is noted among those who understand the trap mechanics being used.
• A named decision- maker in the backroom of crawl sponsorship; speaks with [[Dr. Hu]] about going "all in" on a particular [[human]] crawler and weighs PR and political consequences for sponsors and factions.
• A high-elf crawler and former Rogue (Class: Rogue, Race: High Elf; Top Level: 89) who survived indentureship and worked as a Game Guide after taking a deal on the 11th floor; author of the second edition of the [[Dungeon Anarchist’s Cookbook]].
• A character listed as missing and wanted by [[Syndicate Security]].
