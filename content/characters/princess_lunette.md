# princess_lunette

• [[An]] incomplete flesh golem (a tattoo made flesh) used as a body for [[Samantha]] to inhabit, identified as the body of [[Signet]]'s mother and described as a naiad-like construct.
• The full-sized, pure naiad body produced by [[Signet]]'s final spell, intended as a vessel for [[Samantha]] to inhabit.
• The body necessitates use of a [[kua-ting rebreather]] due to its physiology (gills).
• Treated as a named flesh construct and used in [[Samantha]]'s plan to regain a corporeal form.
