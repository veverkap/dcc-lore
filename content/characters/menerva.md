# menerva

• A former schoolteacher and crawler who begged [[Porthus]] to "Go. Run." and is trapped on the [[Tenth Floor]] with uncertain fate.
• A former collaborator with [[Herot]] who helped [[engineer]] the escape pocket that became the [[Pineapple Cabaret]].
• A person of interest referenced in conversation, with [[Rosetta]] reacting to the mention of Menerva, implying potential allegiance.
• The NPC in charge on the [[Seventeenth Floor]] overseeing the construction and occupation of the [[Backstage Death Maze]].
• Building a house in the [[Eastern Canopy]] with her husband, preparing to retire.
