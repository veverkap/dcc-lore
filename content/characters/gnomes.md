# gnomes

• A race or mob type mentioned in the chapter blurb of [[Book IV, The Gate of the Feral Gods]], grouped with [[Camels]], [[Geese]], and [[Shapeshifters]].
• Involved in large-scale hostile actions, including bombing towns and impacting nearby [[crawlers]] and castles.
• Responsible for the floating [[Wasteland Castle]] and bomb warfare, operating the [[gnomes]] and using weapons like the [[Gnomish Knock-Knock]].
• Mentioned as the attackers who destroyed a nearby town after learning its collateral changed.
• Listed among the races/creatures that appear in the forthcoming [[Book IV, The Gate of the Feral Gods]].
• Have dirigible forces and red hats prominent on the island.
