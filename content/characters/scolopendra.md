# Scolopendra encounter rules
A collection of explicit dungeon rules tied to the presence or fate of [[Scolopendra]]: killing [[Scolopendra]] will not immediately win the game (exit still requires the 18th floor); killing her halts level timers, prevents future deal offers after the 12th floor, triggers Ascendency battles, and causes the dungeon to collapse 12 hours after an Ascendency winner is crowned. If [[Scolopendra]] is not killed and the floor collapses with her inside, the boss is removed and the dungeon becomes impossible to exit. Additionally, a specific fail rule: if [[Scolopendra]] dies or is abandoned upon a collapsing floor, all remaining attacks will trigger simultaneously.
