# monk_seals

• [[Monk seals]]: Hostile or capture-target beasts on the beach, asked to be captured by [[Sister Ines Quiteria]] as a local mob objective.
• [[Monk seals]]: Monstrous/ mob-type encountered on the beach, asked to be captured as part of a cooperative objective.
• [[Monk Seals]]: A species/faction of seal mobs on the coast, described as tough, intelligent, tanky melee fighters devoted to a martial/seal religion and trained in Caribbean-style fighting.
• [[Monk Seals]]: A named mob/species faction on the beach, engaged in the conflict described by the quest [[The Chowder War]], with singing and losses central to the escalating battle.
