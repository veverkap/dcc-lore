# reef_explorer

• [[Reef Explorer]]: a Level-65 ghost-class mob wearing a deep-dive suit, summoning bodyguards (including [[Swordfish Interlopers]] and a rare squid-like totem) and utility cards (notably many [[Precision]] cards).
• Killed via magic ([[Raul]] the crab and party actions).
