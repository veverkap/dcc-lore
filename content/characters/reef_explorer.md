# [[Reef Explorer]]
The deckmaster mob encountered at the base of the dirt mound. A Level-65 ghost-class mob wearing a deep-dive suit; summoned multiple bodyguards (including [[Swordfish Interlopers]] and a rare squid-like totem) and utility cards (notably many [[Precision]] cards). Could not play more cards in the fight due to circumstances.
