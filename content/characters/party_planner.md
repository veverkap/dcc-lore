# party_planner

• The Party Planner is a vessel/transport used by [[Louis]], [[Katia]], [[Daniel]] [[Bautista]], and others for travel to the base.
• It is a named, low-ceilinged aircraft that flies fast and low over the plains and serves as the staging area for the assault.
• It is [[an]] area/instance where [[Donut]] is positioned and circles the [[Naga]] stronghold as part of the operation and contingency plan.
• It is a named zone or structure on the level, just below the invisible ceiling, with a green glow from [[an]] energy beam.
• It is the airship used by Dungeon Crawler [[Carl]] and his team, featuring cabins, propellers, shields, and forced to limp back to Area 52 after the volcanic eruption.
