# cave_mudge_bonker

• A level-19 monster species encountered on the [[Nightmare Express]], described as squat, gray-skinned, pig-like, and war-like, carrying wooden clubs with nails, linked to the [[Cave Mudge society]].
• Also known as "Bonkers", these creatures are four-foot, pig-like, and wield spiked wooden clubs.
• They are part of the broader [[Cave Mudge society]] and one of the primary threats on the route, dropping crude clubs and low-value loot.
