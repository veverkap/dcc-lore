# Forsoothed
A common alien soother species presented as TV hosts in the chapter (the hosts [[Gravo]] and [[Liddi]]). Function as program presenters and cultural intermediaries for products like the [[Robot Donut]] toy on [[Veriluxx New Toys Showcase]].

## Additional Observations
# Forsoothed
Alien race also called "Soothers." Represented on the program by [[Evo]], described as thin, white, hairless beings with large black eyes who drift while walking. Noted cultural contacts with humans and involvement in Syndicate affairs.
