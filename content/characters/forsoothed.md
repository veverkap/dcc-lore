# forsoothed

• A common alien soother species presented as TV hosts, represented by [[Gravo]] and [[Liddi]], function as program presenters and cultural intermediaries for products like the [[Robot Donut]] toy on [[Veriluxx New Toys Showcase]].
• Also known as "[[Soothers]]," this alien race is represented by [[Evo]], described as thin, white, hairless beings with large black eyes who drift while walking, with cultural contacts with humans and involvement in Syndicate affairs.
