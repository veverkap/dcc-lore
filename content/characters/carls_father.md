# carls_father

• The dying man in the hospital bed, gaunt and lucid, is verbally cruel and is injected with a lethal dose of [[Morphine]] by [[Carl's Stepmother]].
• He is referenced as a deceased or absent figure connected to the [[1965 Harley-Davidson FLH Panhead Electra Glide]] and the labeled flower pots in the spliced yard.
• He is recalled by [[Dungeon Crawler Carl]] as a figure who grew a mustache for motorcycle trips.
