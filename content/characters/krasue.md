# Krasue

# Krasue
A Level 16 mob encountered in the alley: described as a flying, disembodied female head with dangling organs and ravenous/vampiric tendencies. The in-text entry notes they must be killed by destroying both the head and the remaining corpse to fully defeat them.

## Additional Observations
# Krasue
Undead ghost-like foes described as three floating head/torso things that likely killed [[GumGum]] and are implicated in the deaths of prostitutes who fall from the sky. Referred to as "ghosts" by the system and as typical hench-creatures often associated with bigger undead threats (e.g., a lich).

## Additional Observations
# Krasue
Nighttime vampiric corpses that split between a living daytime form and a detached, headless/nighttime monstrous form. In this chapter they are the primary monsters in the magistrate's quarters: some hang as corpses (Xs on the map), others roam (red dots), and the chapter explains that destroying their bodies is required to truly kill them.

## Additional Observations
# [[krasue]]
A type of undead/flying servant engineered by [[Miss Quill]] — used as lieutenants and eyes/ears in battle. Their creation yields spikes of soul power that feed the [[Soul Crystal]].
