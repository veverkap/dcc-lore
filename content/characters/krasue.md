# krasue

• A Level 16 mob encountered in the alley, described as a flying, disembodied female head with dangling organs and vampiric tendencies, requiring both head and corpse to be destroyed to fully defeat.
• Undead ghost-like foes, three floating head/torso things, implicated in the deaths of prostitutes and likely killed [[GumGum]].
• Nighttime vampiric corpses that split between a living daytime form and a detached, headless/nighttime monstrous form, requiring destruction of their bodies to truly kill.
• A type of undead/flying servant engineered by [[Miss Quill]], used as lieutenants and eyes/ears in battle, generating spikes of soul power that feed the [[Soul Crystal]].
• A grotesque mob encountered in the alley, floating, disembodied female heads trailing organs and viscera, requiring both head+organs and the rest of the corpse destroyed to be killed.
• Undead/ghost creatures encountered in the alley, responsible for grisly mutilations and associated with the larger mystery, described as hench-creatures often tied to undead forces.
• Constructed flying lieutenants used by [[Miss Quill]] as eyes, ears, and soul-power generators, generating surges of soul energy that feed the [[Soul Crystal]] and [[Remex the Grand]].
