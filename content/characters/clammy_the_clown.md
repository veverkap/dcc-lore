# Clammy the Clown
A Level 9 circus mob encountered at the entrance to [[Grimaldi's Big Top]]. Described as a fat, wide clown with pointed teeth and long, yellowed fingernails; one of the resurrected clown clones maintained by [[Ringmaster Grimaldi – Pestiferous Vine]].
