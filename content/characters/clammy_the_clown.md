# clammy_the_clown

* A Level 9 circus mob encountered at the entrance to [[Grimaldi's Big Top]].
* Described as a fat, wide clown with pointed teeth and long, yellowed fingernails.
* One of the resurrected clown clones maintained by [[Ringmaster [[Grimaldi]] – Pestiferous Vine]].
* A transformed clown mob encountered at the [[Big Top]] physically blocks the exit as the parasites exert influence, representing the infected performers inside the circus.
