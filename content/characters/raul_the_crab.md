# raul_the_crab

* [[Raul the crab]]: a summoned crab totem allied with [[Donut]] and [[Lazarus-A- Bang-Bang]], performs explosive entries and magical attacks, and uses a spell-imbued claw attack to sever a [[Reef Explorer]]'s limb, releasing ghostly water and killing the mob.
* [[Raul the crab]]: can be modified by cards like [[Greased Lightning]].
* [[Raul the crab]]: referenced as part of deck composition, potentially problematic simultaneous summons with [[Geraldo the Monk Seal]].
* [[raul the crab]] is a named Red Maníseros Land Crab, a Sun-Kissed Cloud Rank Warrior (Level 70), and a powerful fighter with a short meditation-based attack.
* He is a summoned crab totem who requires time to cast spells and was frozen and killed by snare cards during practice.
* Raul is a team member who provides a water spray attack used to quench [[Carl]]'s burning wounds during the battle.
* He is a crab totem summoned by [[Carl]], often called Raul the crab, and is central to the emergency event, his biological act needed to power [[Amayon]]'s take-me-home requirement.
* Raul is a crab summoned/collected as a card on Floor 8, acquired by [[Carl]]'s party.
* He is a summoned, crab-legged combatant who ascended in level and rareness (to mythic) and intervenes in the stairwell fight, holding [[Lucia]].
* Raul is a crab companion who fights near the tower, suffering severe lightning damage during the chute slide and nearly killed by [[Gustavo]] 3's attacks.
* He is a named ninja-crab mob encountered atop a truck, practicing chants and spells, captured and converted into a common card by [[Carl]] and [[Donut]].
* Raul is a totem card described as a crab, included in the party's standard totem roster.
* He is a summoned crab totem under [[Donut]]'s control, casting the once-per-fight spell [[Bris]], acting obsequiously, and serving as a ranged damage/tank totem in practice fights.
* Raul is a character previously represented as a card whose permanent freeing is discussed by the party.
