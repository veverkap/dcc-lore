# raul_the_crab

• [[Raul the crab]]: a summoned crab totem allied with [[Donut]] and [[Lazarus-A- Bang-Bang]], performs explosive entries and magical attacks, and uses a spell-imbued claw attack to sever a [[Reef Explorer]]'s limb, releasing ghostly water and killing the mob.
• [[Raul the crab]]: can be modified by cards like [[Greased Lightning]].
• [[Raul the crab]]: referenced as part of deck composition, potentially problematic simultaneous summons with [[Geraldo the Monk Seal]].
