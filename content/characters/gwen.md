# gwen

Here is the consolidated markdown list:

• [[Gwen]] is a crawler contact who reports battlefield observations and warns about activity in the water quadrant.
• She is a local NPC who comments on catastrophic events and provides a civilian perspective on the battle's fallout.
• [[Gwen]] is a leader of a team working on digging out and sealing the stairwell beneath the flooded castle remains in the [[Necropolis of Anser]].
• She is a crawler who reports discoveries to the party and her message triggers the inventory system warning and the decrystallization event.
• [[Gwen]] is a crawler who can access Club Vanquisher memberships and scouts local social/faction situations in [[Pandinus]].
• She is [[an]] ally and remote operator contacted by [[dungeon crawler carl]] who handles external coordination and is told to move players to saferooms when [[Lusca]] attacks.
• [[Gwen]] is a party member who comments on strange loots and the possessed head [[Samantha]] and joins others near the crafting room.
• She is a squad member assigned by [[dungeon crawler carl]] to operate the drain and attempt to fully drain the necropolis; later returned to a saferoom.
• [[Gwen]] is a leader of a team that looted gate coordinates off the body of [[Quetzalcoatlus]] on the previous floor; referenced as the origin of mapping information used by the party.
• She is [[an]] Inuit woman in the group noted for facial and hand tattoos; defends her tattoo aesthetics in a discussion with [[Donut]].
• [[Gwen]] is a crawler who took severe wounds from a pulse rifle but was healed by the system and returned to active duty.
• She is a teammate paired with [[Daniel]] for back-door entry and looting during the temple engagement.
• [[Gwen]] is a crawler present among the returning attendees at the masquerade.
• She is a crawler who moves to support the group protecting [[Lucia Mar]] and the prize counter when [[hunters]] approach.
• [[Gwen]] is a crawler who can access certain clubs (such as the [[Club Vanquisher]]) and provides reconnaissance about local happenings in towns like [[Pandinus]].
• She is [[an]] ally coordinating external evacuation and timing; tasked with moving tomb raiders and others to saferooms while [[Carl]] handles the fight aboard the [[Akula]].
• [[Gwen]] is a crawler who died in the boss encounter; mourned by survivors including [[Donut]] despite being characterized as mean.
• She is a named friend referenced in relation to [[Tran]]'s injuries; mentioned as having been lost at the end of the previous floor.
