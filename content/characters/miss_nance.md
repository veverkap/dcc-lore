# miss_nance

• A second funeral bell sergeant assigned as a guard escort to [[Donut]] in [[Point Mongo]], named by [[Donut]] and involved in initial town defense scenes.
• One of the mushroom guards left in town; given orders by [[Donut]] and overseen by [[Mordecai]] to help defend and evacuate the area if necessary.
• Another funeral bell sergeant flanking [[Donut]]; participates in town guard formations and is one of the named sergeants in [[Point Mongo]].
• A mushroom guard posted in town under one of the mushroom caps; receives direct orders from [[Donut]] and [[Mordecai]] to continue setting up defenses and to escort [[Mordecai]] to a safe room if necessary.
