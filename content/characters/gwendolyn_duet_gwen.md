# gwendolyn_duet_gwen

• A level-27 player (self-styled "Boring Ol' Fighter") in a nearby bubble who communicates with [[Carl]]; describes her zone as containing the [[sandcastle]] and hostile creatures including giant snakes and spiders.
• A crawler in chat who attempted to coordinate retreats to train lines and criticized players who disregarded warnings about the imminent structural collapse and [[Krakaren]] betrayal.
• Leads breach and siege efforts against a beach necropolis, coordinates building siege ladders/catapults, reports on a borough-boss-level bird harassing operations, and scouts submarine activity.
• A combatant who fights feral [[pazuzu]] and earns recognition in the liberation announcement; appears in the party that helps secure the necropolis and is specifically named in the bubble liberation notification.
• A mushroom-based singer NPC who died during the Butcher's Masquerade.
• Leads a team breaching walls at a necropolis and reports harassment by a borough boss bird and sightings of a submarine crew working on a water-bound vehicle.
• A spear-wielding crawler who fights alongside [[Katia]] and [[Tran]] during the sandstorm and the feral [[pazuzu]] attack; later explicitly congratulated by the bubble system for liberating the subterranean quadrant.
• A deceased friend of [[Tran]] referenced in his messages; cited as part of his grief.
