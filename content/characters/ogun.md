# ogun

* [[Ogun]]: major dungeon god worshipped by [[Paz]] Lo and [[Anton]] Lopez, associated with blacksmiths and protection, grants weapon bonuses and resistance to charms
* God of Blacksmiths with a broken shrine in the thorn room (Shrine of Ogun), repairing which yields no extra benefits but failing to do so risks smites on his worshippers
* God of Blacksmithery and Armor, level 250 and invulnerable while present, described as a buff, dark-skinned smith with a war hammer, white glowing eyes, and a celestial anvil
* God who shed blood in [[Yemaya]]'s temple and insulted her, his actions part of the context for godly intervention
* God who disfigured [[Paz]] (the burn that revealed part of his skull)
* God referenced in dungeon crawler [[Carl]]'s memory in relation to a past failed Black Nimbus casting meant to block divine intervention
* God of Blacksmiths and one of the seven shrine options, repairing his shrine offers no additional benefits and may smite non-worshippers
* God of Blacksmithery and Armor summoned into the room, indifferent and punitive, removes [[Paz]]'s healing ability, crushes [[Anton]] to death, then departs
