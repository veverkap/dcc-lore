# the_luau

• [[The Luau]] is [[an]] enormous, fat, pink-skinned orc deckmaster (boss) encountered in practice, dressed in Hawaiian gear and accompanied by a conga-playing [[piglet]]; she summoned a [[Mo'o]] and was defeated quickly by the team.
