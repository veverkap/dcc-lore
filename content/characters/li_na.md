# li_na

Here is the consolidated list of unique factual points about Li [[Na]]:

* Sister of Li [[Jun]]
* Crawler with demonic Changbi racial trait
* Uses multiple magical chains (some that immobilize, toss wind blades, or ignite)
* Intelligent, direct, and a strong tactical voice
* Provides brutal crowd control
* Suggests using the Def Leppard cart to bait a province boss into the portal
* Member of the crawler party that helps manipulate and keep a mutilated [[mantaur]] alive for use in the summoning gambit
* Instructs others to punch the [[mantaur]] to start the summoning
* Party member conversing with Li [[Jun]] while the group handles the portal cart and the aftermath of the train detonation
* Level-30 Changbi classed as a Slave Driver
* Pale, horned, and uses multiple chains with varied effects (fire, wind blades, immobilization)
* Tactical, blunt, and instrumental in the plan to lure a province boss into the portal
* Leader of a team that ran to the Desperado Club to procure expensive supplies for the party
* Mentioned as having sourced critical materials
* Observer and ally who watches over [[Donut]] in the Desperado Club
* Present during [[Carl]]'s interactions with [[Damascus]]
* Friend named by [[Samantha]] as part of her bachelorette party roster and social network
* Contact mentioned in passing during [[Carl]]'s attempts to coordinate movements and operations related to the Desperado Club
* Crawler who provides [[an]] alternate reference date (March 20) used by [[Louis]]
* Trusted by others for timekeeping
* Member of [[Carl]]'s network who joins the consensus to assist with the stairwell plan
* Named hunter who moved into town alongside Li [[Jun]] and others after the creeper battles
* Scout with a racial trait that reveals god affiliations of nearby entities, providing battlefield intel on hunter composition and worship
* Ally reporting demon swarms and battlefield status; communicates needs for assistance
* Crawler who uses dreads (auras) to control crowds
* Activates the dread "Dark Purpose" amplified by the antenna/wire network to devastating effect, then cycles to "Defile Soul" and "Bleeding Horror" to rapidly level them
* Crawler who wields blood-based dreads
* Communicates with [[Carl]] during the breach, has the dreads "Blood Horror" and "Succulent Decay," and possesses "Damned" status in-game
* Ally riding [[Harpocrates]] like a mount and coordinating with other teams to assault the Reaver Castle
* Remote ally who is commanding/controlling certain assets like [[Harpocrates]] from a tower
* Crawler whose chain towers mark thousands of enemies on the floor
* Stopped answering communications and told [[Zhang]] that she was "hunting"
* Floating crawler with ethereal chains and a black-purple glow, visibly marked and hostile
* Rendered unable to heal after the Li [[Na]] chain incident
* Has installed Li [[Jun]]'s celestial-quality eye into herself and intends to use the original potion on [[Harpocrates]] and others
* Conscripted the god [[Harpocrates]] and is actively controlling him in the battle against [[Yarilo]]
