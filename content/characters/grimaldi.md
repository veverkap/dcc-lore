# grimaldi

* Owner and ringleader of Grimaldi's Traveling Circus, transforming into a vine during the cataclysm and exerting control over infected performers and creatures via spores and mold-covered parasites.
* City boss and circus family member infected and central to the drama inside the Big Top, communicating mentally through the Parasitic Infestation and tied to [[Signet]] by family history.
* Figure who killed parasites and freed circus performers after the attack, taken by [[Signet]] and with a story described as "mostly done" in the current narrative.
* Associated with [[Donut]] in prior lore, part of the characters' backstory references.
* Potential target for the potion by [[Signet]], mentioned in cautionary context regarding who should be allowed to use the benefactor item.
* Planted NPC/being integrated with [[an]] "all-tree node" when arriving in the world, implied to be merged with a tree and possibly not active as a separate combatant.
* [[Signet]]'s circus ringmaster husband who was turned into a vine by [[Scolopendra]] and is now connected to the All Tree.
* Figure who killed parasites at the circus and liberated performers, taken by [[Signet]] afterward.
* Named figure who once associated with the female character who lived in the village with the were-casters, part of background lore explaining alliances and departures.
* Crawler who might be subject to [[Signet]]'s attention and possible use of "I take it all back" if informed.
* Figure planted beside [[an]] all-tree node when arriving in this world, with roots intertwined with a tree and becoming one with it, with [[Signet]] remarking that the tree has survived prior infestations, implying Grimaldi's continued or at-least-stable presence.
* Named by [[Signet]] as someone she regrets not saying goodbye to, presumably a person or family tied to [[Signet]]'s past.
