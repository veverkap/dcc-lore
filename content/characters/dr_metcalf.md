# dr_metcalf

• Provides race briefing, map, path assignment, hazards, time limit, and other race details to the team.
• Announces distance, track type, tasks, special rules, hazards, and timing for the next race.
• Relays system rules and reacts antagonistically to the presence of the talking pet chair [[Dorota]].
• Calls out when the team's invulnerability buff is about to expire.
• Presents the "Limited details on the next race" announcement containing distance, track type, tasks, special rules, hazards, and time limits.
• Makes audible announcements to racers, including the activation of the [[Starting block shield]].
• Frequently sarcastic and antagonistic toward the team's other equipment, such as the [[Smart Companion Royal Pet Throne]].
• Monitors defensive timers and invulnerability windows.
• Provides route analysis and options for the team, describing paths to the [[pit stop]] and terrain hazards.
• Offers logistical detail used by [[Carl]] and the party to plan their race route.
• Identifies multiple paths, including the [[River of Sloth]] and the giant loop.
• Issues the "Limited details on the next race" announcement describing the track, hazards, and rules.
• Transmits map data and commentary to the team during pre-race preparations.
• Provides real-time navigation and environment analysis.
• Marks containment status with a blinking light.
• Issues formal race announcements.
• Appears on the dash with a smug expression during the race.
• Emits a terse beeped message supportive of aggressive sentiment toward upgrade-holding opponents.
• Loads and times the potion-delivery shotgun and other ordinance.
• Assists [[Carl]] with weapons control and tactical execution during the starting-line confrontation.
• Responds verbally when [[Carl]] calls for her.
• Loudly complains while the [[sluggalos]] pile into the truck.
