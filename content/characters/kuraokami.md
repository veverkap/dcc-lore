# Kuraokami

# Kuraokami
A goddess worshiped by [[Sinjin]] in this chapter. Her worship imposes specific rules (daily corpse-touching with ice, five-day boon conditions) and allows the deity to send messages to worshippers. Failure to follow the rules leads to debuffs, loss of benefits, falling from grace, or being smited.

## Additional Observations
# Kuraokami
An ocean goddess with a temple that doubles as the entrance to [[Club Vanquisher]]. Her temple is active in the area and the party tries to avoid antagonizing her to prevent divine retribution.
