# kuraokami

• A goddess worshiped by [[Sinjin]] with specific rules (daily corpse-touching with ice, five-day boon conditions) and consequences for non-compliance (debuffs, loss of benefits, falling from [[grace]], or being smited).
• [[An]] ocean goddess with a temple that doubles as the entrance to [[Club Vanquisher]].
• Her temple is active in the area and the party tries to avoid antagonizing her to prevent divine retribution.
• Failure to follow her worship rules can lead to invasive messages and risk of being smited.
• Damaging her temple risks severe divine repercussions.
