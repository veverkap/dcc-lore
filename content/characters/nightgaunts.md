# nightgaunts

* A mob type referenced via the "[[Nightgaunt]] Precipice" choice
* [[Carl]]'s cloak specifically enrages Nightgaunts, making them dangerous adversaries for him
* A demon-[[Enchanted Nightgaunt Cloak of Stoutness|like]] creature whose hide composes the Enchanted Nightgaunt Cloak of Stoutness
* Real [[Nightgaunts]] will likely react negatively if they see someone wearing the Enchanted Nightgaunt Cloak of Stoutness
