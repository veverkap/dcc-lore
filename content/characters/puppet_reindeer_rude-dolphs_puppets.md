# puppet_reindeer_rude-dolphs_puppets

• The eight gore-covered servant reindeer, animated by [[Rude-Dolph the Blood-Nosed Slay-Deer]], act as the boss' puppets and must be killed before the main puppet-master is vulnerable, including named individuals like [[Blitzen]] and [[Comet]].
