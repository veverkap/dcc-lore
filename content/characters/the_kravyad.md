# the_kravyad

* [[The Kravyad]]: a named boss monster reportedly present at employee stops, including [[station 435 [[kravyad]] station]], and potentially a threat for parties attempting employee-area solutions.
* Guards employee transit portals, including [[station 435 [[kravyad]] station]], and may punish memory/time when using certain portals.
* [[Kravyad]]: a dark-magic force or boss that prepares employees for the portal back to base, described as [[an]] obstacle to [[face]] if attempting the employee-portal route.
* A worker or species managed by [[Madison]] as part of the Iron Tangle workforce and tied to station/employee systems.
* Blue, six-armed, [[naga]]-like employee-creature used by the Iron Tangle as part of a productivity program: hypnotizes [[dwarves]] and other workers, consumes select workers as payment, and is lifebound to [[an]] [[employee portal]].
* A multi-armed snake-woman NPC encountered by other [[crawlers]], capable of hypnotic spells that were neutralized by [[daniel bautista]], and who threatened to kill herself, endangering nearby portals.
* A named mob/creature that [[daniel bautista]] and others attempted to paralyze; the paralysis failed and the [[Kravyad]] died, which closed the portal and caused logistical problems.
* A named threat type mentioned as being "in charge of employee return" and implied to guard the employee teleport/portal.
* [[Naga]]-like, blue, six-armed creatures used by the Iron Tangle for labor control, hypnotizing and eating [[dwarves]] as payment, and tied to the employee-portal mechanic.
* A multi-armed snake-woman NPC encountered on the subway platforms who attempted hypnosis and later threatened self-harm to deny [[crawlers]] portal access.
* A named boss/creature engaged by survivor groups; [[an]] attempt to paralyze the [[Kravyad]] resulted in her death and closed a portal, causing logistical problems for survivors.
* A named foe referenced as being "in charge of employee return" and implied to be a guardian/boss associated with the employee portal/transport process.
* A named boss monster reportedly guarding employee stops (e.g., Station 435), mentioned as a potential lethal obstacle for those attempting certain portal routes.
* A named worker group/species mentioned in [[Madison]]'s responsibilities (benefits manager for all the [[Kravyad]]).
* A named class of Iron Tangle creatures used in [[human]]-resources: [[naga]]-like, blue, six-armed beings that hypnotize and feed on [[dwarves]], control employee rotations, and are lifebound to the [[Employee Portal]].
* A large named mob/creature targeted by players; [[an]] attempted paralysis resulted in its death, causing the portal to close and stranding many [[crawlers]] without [[souvenir hats]].
* A force in charge of employee return through the portal, using "dark magic" and causing memory side effects for employees returning through the portal.
* A named boss monster said to guard certain employee portals (mentioned as a possible threat at [[station 435 kravyad station]]).
* A worker class/species referenced by [[Madison]] as part of the Iron Tangle workforce; subject to HR oversight and benefits.
* A blue, six-armed [[naga]]-like employee-boss that manages [[an]] exploitative worker program—hypnotizes [[dwarves]], consumes some as payment, and controls [[an]] [[employee portal]] lifebound to itself.
