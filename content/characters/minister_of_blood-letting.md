# Minister of Blood-Letting

# [[Minister of Blood-Letting]]
A level 98 skeleton performer who wears pope-like garb and a necklace of live bats. Leader-type figure of the team called the [[Bleak Congregation]] and a memorable karaoke act at [[The Lollipop]].

## Additional Observations
# [[Minister of Blood-Letting]]
A named skeleton performer who takes the stage at the [[Lollipop]]. Described as a Level 98 Dark Ghoul Papist, wearing pope-like attire and a live-bat necklace; member/leader of the faction [[Bleak Congregation]].
