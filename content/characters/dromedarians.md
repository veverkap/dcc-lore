# dromedarians

Here is the consolidated markdown list:

• [[Dromedarians]] are a camel-like, NPC race common to arid regions, described as strong, water-storing warriors who form town society and are in a stalemate with the [[Bactrians]] and [[gnomes]].
• They are skilled at surviving arid climates and are present as townsfolk, guards, and workers in [[Hump Town]], with a lethargic cultural description when settled but historically brutal warriors.
• They patrol the wasteland, take in orphans like [[Skarn]] after gnome bombings, and are repeatedly referenced in local lore and bomb histories.
• They operate anti-air and bazooka capabilities, run the nearby settlement, sell weaponry, and keep outskirts mob-free with patrols.
• They are also [[an]] enslaved group cited by [[Crixus]] as complicit in historical horrors under [[Anser]], central to local animosities.
• They man anti-air guns and repair town defenses in Hump Town, with many fighting and falling during the gerbil onslaught.
• They can also assault the APV with spiked baseball bats, with one being killed by the APV shield and the others tending to the bodies and vehicle.
