# loita_the_administrator

* [[Loita]] the administrator, previously killed by [[dungeon crawler carl]], contextualizing current Syndicate scrutiny.
* Representative of the [[Bloom]] party, tied to [[Hekla]] and potentially claiming control of [[Katia]]'s account.
* Tiny [[kua-tin]] admin and temporary PR agent who enforces rules on inventory and studio conduct, directly interacting with [[Odette]], [[Lexis]], [[Donut]], [[Katia]], and [[dungeon crawler carl]].
* Outreach representative who may attempt to take over [[Katia Grim]]'s account, linked to rival political party/representation dynamics described by [[Zev]].
* Administrative/ system contact who notifies [[crawlers]] about sponsor deals, briefing details for promotional obligations, benefactor boxes, and guest-program scheduling, enforcing protocol and expectations.
* Sponsor representative who explains toy design constraints to [[Carl]] and [[Donut]] and defends the practice of making damaged prototype dolls self-destruct.
* Higher-level social-media/production figure and strict manager who communicates with [[Donut]] and the team, enforces discipline, and threatens consequences (e.g., taking [[Mongo]] away).
* Small kua- tin admin and on-site sponsor/handler for [[dungeon crawler carl]] on this program, occupying the production trailer's green room, expressing extremist antipathy toward dry cultures and [[dungeon crawler carl]]'s people, and critically injured/killed in the trailer explosion.
* Kua- tin warlord previously encountered (and assassinated) by the protagonist, referenced as a source for knowledge about "The Great Consensus."
* Representative who is positioned to potentially take over outreach duties for the team, associated with [[Bloom]] and having prior ties to [[Hekla]]'s representation.
* Tiny kua- tin admin who acts as the on-set PR/administrator during the [[Odette]] interview segment, enforcing show rules (e.g., inventory windows), carrying a rebreather, and bearing the Bloom pin.
* Representative who may attempt to take over the party's account, linked to [[Bloom]] and rumored to have represented [[Hekla]] previously.
* Tiny kua- tin admin who acts as the team's on-set PR/administrator in this chapter, enforcing studio rules (e.g., inventory restrictions), wearing a rebreather-like device, and displaying the Bloom pin.
* Administrative/systemic figure who communicates sponsor details and schedules benefactor obligations and programs for approved [[crawlers]] (including [[Carl]], [[Donut]], and [[Katia]]).
* Contact who messages about incoming benefactor/sponsor items and coordinates deliveries for team members like [[Donut]].
* Sponsor/program representative who explains safety/marketing constraints on the toy-robot versions of [[Donut]] and enforces self-destruct features for prototypes.
* Manager/producer figure with authority over promotions and appearances, issuing orders to [[Donut]] and running the show presence, threatening penalties if disobeyed.
* Small kua- tin admin assigned to [[Carl]]'s production trailer, politically zealous and hostile toward [[human]]/dry culture, invoking [[Bloom]]'s ideology and the [[Borant Corporation]]'s security priorities, directing on-set protocol, and watching the taping remotely before being mortally wounded in the explosion.
