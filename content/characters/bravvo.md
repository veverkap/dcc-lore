# bravvo

• Unaffiliated level-50 [[yenk]] hunter shot from the sky and found tied across saddles on a massive caterpillar, severely injured and nearly dead.
• Hunter encountered and killed by [[dungeon crawler carl]] and [[Donut]], with equipment including enchanted black leather boots, 30 [[Good Health Potion]]s, 30 [[Good Mana Refill]]s, a [[Magical Longsword]], and 9 [[levitate]]s.
• Hunter's death granted [[dungeon crawler carl]] a single stat point and yielded limited loot.
• Hunter claimed to be hunting [[dungeon crawler carl]] when found.
• Hunter's highest stat was dexterity; killing him granted [[dungeon crawler carl]] a single stat point.
• Hunter wore black enchanted leather boots and carried potions and a magical longsword that granted +2 constitution.
• Hunter was looted by [[dungeon crawler carl]] and [[Donut]].
