# jacobus

• The Reverse Tooth Fairy, Jacobus, is a Level 90, tiny but muscular Danish fairy mob with currency-demanding tooth-stealing behavior, permanently summoned due to [[Carl]]'s [[Scavenger’’s Daughter]] patch.
• Jacobus replies in Danish, interacts with [[Samantha]], and has dental surgery skills.
• He is a small reverse tooth fairy-liked NPC referenced and named by [[Imani]] during the interrogation scene, involved in close interactions with [[Akuma]].
• Jacobus buzzes around [[Carl]] and [[Imani]], speaks unpredictably (including untranslated lines), and exhibits a manic demeanor.
• He is a crawler who runs after [[Imani]] to assist in the effort to save [[Dong Quixote]].
• Jacobus is a small fairy who buzzes around [[Bigs]] and [[Dong]], even peering into [[Dong]]’s mouth before being shooed away by [[Imani]].
• He is a tiny red starfish creature that [[Imani]] carries, temporarily changed and squirting water, shown during the curing scene.
• Jacobus is a starfish companion held by [[Imani]] who emits water during the interaction at the [[Desperado Club]], with a minor supporting presence during the wheel negotiation and emotional moments.
• [[Imani]]'s reverse tooth-fairy, Jacobus, is presently in starfish form and being kept in the safe room.
