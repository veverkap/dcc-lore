# finley

• A level 81 [[Porsuk]] mercenary in [[Team Helix]] with green-enchanted claws, who flirts with [[Samantha]].
• Named companion mentioned by [[Quemada]] as someone to keep safe with raised fees, part of [[Carl]]'s close circle.
• Favored ally of [[Donut]], mentioned in the party's conversational dynamics.
• Character who attempted to seduce [[Samantha]], provoking banter among the party.
• Teamed with [[Carl]] in the truck, commenting on performances and upgrades, and aiding in on-the-move vigilance during the race.
• Performed calming interventions for [[Quemada]] and interacted closely with the party during the race.
• Small froglike crew member who took over driving duties for [[Olga]] and was present in the vehicle during the assault.
• Mercenary allied with the team who carried unconscious allies into town seeking a temple, later killed by [[an]] APV.
• NPC mercenary encountered and killed in the street, showing defiant protection of his friends before being struck down.
• Previously used corpse with missing legs, available for summoning/driving.
• Character whose death the narrator remembers, used as [[an]] emotional touchstone for the cruelty of the floor.
• Character who died on [[an]] earlier floor, part of the narrator's traumatic memories influencing present decisions.
