# semeru

• The volcanic level/region of Semeru, where [[Larracos]] sits, was historically fertile and home to the [[dwarves]] until devastation by [[Scolopendra]].
• The Semeru are a named people/species referenced in the chapter, with a ruined stronghold described as the "castle of the Semeru".
• A pair of Semeru are seen operating a massive dwarven digger atop the wreckage.
