# gumgum

• A Level-5 orc NPC who begs for help, discovered piling dead sex workers in the street, and requests investigation into nightly deaths.
• Found dead in the alley with chest ripped open, likely killed by [[Krasue]].
• Inventory contained a [[Gate Pass]] and a [[Mysterious Letter]] tied to the investigation.
• Tortured by city [[elves]], used in their justification for purging "unworthy filth", and drew attention to the Oak Fell.
• Recognized irregularities in the scheme surrounding [[Miss Quill]] and the soul harvesting.
• Mentioned as someone who cared for dead prostitutes and was tasked with a quest by [[dungeon crawler carl]] and [[Donut]].
• Found as a lootable corpse with a [[Gate Pass]] and a [[Mysterious Letter]].
• Noted as someone who noticed suspicious activity related to the soul- collection operation run by [[Miss Quill]] and [[Featherfall]].
