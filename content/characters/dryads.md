# dryads

• The tree/forest NPC population inhabiting the [[Small Dryad Settlement]] are hostile to [[dungeon crawler carl]] due to a troublemaker warning and brand.
• Forest spirits allied with the trees cast spells to call for aid against the [[Gehenna brambles]], potentially summoning [[Diwata]].
• The [[Dryads]] are a plant-associated race present in the settlement, mentioned in town rules as escorts allowed to bring [[Macaques]]/monkeys into town.
