# La Ciguapa
A legendary mob encountered near a kapok tree; described as a hairy woman with backward feet who lures opponents with charm. Noted entry in the [[Bahamas book]]. Vulnerable to familiars and pets, dropped two cards when killed by [[Mongo]].
