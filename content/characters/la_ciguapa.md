# la_ciguapa

• A legendary mob encountered near a kapok tree, described as a hairy woman with backward feet who lures opponents with charm.
• Vulnerable to familiars and pets, dropped two cards when killed: the [[Damnation]] card and the [[Glow-Up]] card.
