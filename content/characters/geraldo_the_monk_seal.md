# geraldo_the_monk_seal

* A newly collected squad member, a monk seal, mentioned as the first recruit after acquiring a second card.
* A candidate to test in the practice arena, mentioned by [[dungeon crawler carl]] as a named ally to evaluate combat abilities under new card rules.
* A higher-level entity from prior encounters, used as a comparison target to gauge [[Skylar Spinach]]'s strength.
* A named totem/card in the squad's possible summons, with questions raised about interactions if summoned with [[Raul the Crab]].
* A seal totem summoned by [[Donut]], energetic and theatrical in battle, exclaiming and attacking nearby threats.
* A newly acquired squad member, a monk seal, referenced at the chapter opening as the second card capture to join the group.
* A summon/companion that [[Carl]] plans to test in the [[Practice Arena]] under the new [[Card System (Deck Combat)]] rules.
* A named totem/card in [[Carl]]'s potential deck lineup, with uncertainty about interactions if summoned simultaneously with other totems (e.g., [[Raul the Crab]]).
* A totem summoned by [[Donut]], energetic monk-seal totem who charges into fights, shouting and flipping, used as a combat piece against [[crabs]] and [[zombies]].
* [[Geraldo]] is a Level 70 Monk Seal Picket Sentry summoned from the [[Monk Seal Totem]] by [[Donut]], with a health bar, 60-second timer, and flag icon linking to the party.
* He appears in various scenarios, including a mock battle, as a melee fighter/tank, and as a summoned creature used by [[Donut]] in combat.
* Geraldo can be killed and resurrected by [[Uzi Jesus]] if the appropriate spell is used.
* He is fast enough to contribute to speeding up the deck and is used in [[Donut]]'s test of the [[Magical Staff Card]].
* Geraldo is a named totem/card mentioned in the social/deck context, noted for finally tolerating [[Raul the crab]] and having a relationship with [[Lazarus-A-Bang-Bang]].
* He is a unit/target used in [[Donut]]'s test of the [[Magical Staff Card]], causing summoned entities to start fighting each other during the trial.
* Geraldo is a monk-seal totem allied with [[Carl]]'s side, summoned by [[Donut]] at one point and crashing into the collapsing floor during combat.
* He is a second named monk seal of equal level to [[Yago]] that appears on the beach after the first seal is defeated.
* Geraldo acts as a defensive totem with a visible timer and health bar, accepts utility buffs like [[Stout]] and [[Time Extend]], and mirrors the team's flag iconography.
* He is a monk seal totem card summoned during the simulation, portrayed as a playful but stunned combatant who intervenes after [[Shi Maria]] defeats [[Rude-Dolph the Blood-Nosed Slay-Deer]].
* Geraldo is a monk seal totem in [[Donut]]'s deck, acting as a melee tank and fighting poorly alongside [[Raul the crab]] due to mutual hostility.
* He is a party member described as fast enough for quick attacks, contributing to the team's speed considerations in deck construction.
* Geraldo is a summoned monk-seal totem used by [[Donut]] that performs flashy attacks in the choice chamber match.
* He is a totem/character mentioned as tolerating [[Raul the crab]] and getting along with [[Lazarus-A-Bang-Bang]].
* Geraldo is a creature or summon referenced as a target of [[Clockwork Triplicate]] experimentation; [[Donut]] used the staff/card on him during a test fight.
* He is the team's familiar monk seal totem referenced as the counterpart to the summoned [[Yago]], used as a point of comparison in the narration.
* [[Geraldo the Seal]] is a totem summoned by [[Donut]] that attempts melee contributions in combat.
* [[Geraldo the Seal]] is destroyed by Monobrow [[Sam]]'s death ray early in the encounter.
* [[Geraldo the Seal]] is a combatant that engages the hydra and temporarily aids [[Carl]] and party.
