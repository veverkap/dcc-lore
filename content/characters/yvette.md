# yvette

• Teenage daughter of [[Frank Q.]] and [[Maggie]].
• Forced to wear the [[Enchanted Night Wyrm's Ring of Divine Suffering]] to mark the protagonist.
• Choked to death by [[Maggie]] as a result of the ring/ mark situation.
• Tragically killed after being made to use the [[Enchanted Night Wyrm's Ring of Divine Suffering]].
• Suffered from marking [[Dungeon Crawler Carl]].
• Died, with her death being a key emotional revelation in the chapter.
• Mentioned in [[Carl]]'s thoughts, with her mother's actions motivating some of [[Carl]]'s emotional state and feelings about the [[Ring (Marked for Death)]].
