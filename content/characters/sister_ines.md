# sister_ines

Here is the consolidated list of unique factual points about Sister Ines:

• Sister Ines is a nun-class crawler who leads a small team (Team Sister Ines).
• She is a poet laureate/bard-healer type who buffs allies and uses a trident.
• She has a troubled past, being a former convict for mass poisoning in a convent and nicknamed "El Segador de La Habana" (The Reaper of Havana).
• She possesses a racial/skill-based charm effect (Toxo-plasma variant) that compels others to protect her and makes them forget.
• She is a cleric/nun who can detect if a mob is undead and casts supportive holy magic that can remove debuffs.
• She is emotionally invested in the sacred/legend debate surrounding creatures.
• She is a healer and party member who repeatedly removed the Terrified debuff from [[Paz]] and fired bolts while aiding the group's rush to the temple.
• She is a cat-woman figure who confronts [[Ogun]] and attempts to protect [[Paz]] and [[Anton]].
• She is a revealed murderer from prior events and her actions remain part of the group's emotional context in this chapter.
• She is a named crawler who is a potential rival for the key and is feared by [[Carl]] and [[Donut]] because if she reaches the stairwell before them, she could take the key and progress to the ninth floor.
• She is a nun-liked crawler present on the floor and a possible [[keyholder]] for the Cuba zone's exit.
• She is a named crawler mentioned as someone who should not be allowed to go down the stairs; connected to the card/exit politics and the preservation of other players on the floor.
• She is a powerful antagonist present in the area who is attempting to control the locale [[Amayon]] by summoning [[demons]].
• She is a deceased character whose corpse dissolved and became the shell that summoned [[Ysalte]].
• She is a referenced leader whose team previously captured a version of the [[Yago]] monk seal totem.
• She is a nun-liked cat-girl crawler on Floor 8 who acted like a serial killer and ultimately allied with the [[Sheol]] demon [[Amayon]] before being put down by [[Louis]].
• She is a cat-girl disguised as a nun who became violent/insane during Floor 8 and ultimately allied with the [[Sheol]] demon [[Amayon]] before being killed by [[Louis]].
• She is mentioned by [[Osvaldo]] as one of the acquaintances connected to the recurring death pattern tied to [[Ysalte]] and memorial crystals.
