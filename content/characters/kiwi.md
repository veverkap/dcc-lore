# Kiwi
Kiwi is the Mongoliensis Pack Leader (Level 60) and the alpha of the female raptor pack encountered outside [[Point Mongo]]. She confronts and engages with [[Mongo]] during the scene and emits the call that causes the "Deer in the Headlight-ed" effect.
