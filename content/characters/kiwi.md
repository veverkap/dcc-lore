# kiwi

Here is the consolidated markdown list:

• [[Kiwi]] is the [[Mongoliensis]] Pack Leader (Level 60) and alpha of the female raptor pack encountered outside [[Point Mongo]].
• Mother of [[Big Tina]] and a named velociraptor who is temporarily charmed into minion status of [[Princess Donut]].
• A smaller monkey-like character prone to eating odd objects and is being restrained by companions.
• A fast, named velociraptor mount used by [[Princess Donut]].
• [[An]] [[ursine]] NPC/crawler-mother figure who cares for [[Tina]] and shepherds a pack of dinosaurs.
• A smaller dinosaur accompanying [[dungeon crawler carl]]'s group.
• A named participant in masquerade preparations whose costume uses bubbles and who interacts with other team members and pets.
• A velociraptor (the [[mongoliensis]]) referenced as the mother of [[Tina]] and as [[an]] example of [[an]] animal already magically affected.
• A velociraptor who is transformed into a bear by the [[i take it all back]] and returns to the stage at [[Donut]]'s command.
• A female [[mongoliensis]] dinosaur who befriended and later mated with [[Mongo]].
• [[An]] [[ursine]] character (bear woman) who was previously a dinosaur and reverted back; pregnant at the time of the chapter.
• Originally a velociraptor pack leader, now returned in a pregnant [[ursine]] form and caring for troops.
• A [[changeling]] associated with grooming duties (doing [[Samantha]]'s hair) and with imminent childbirth.
• A creature wheeled into the room with newborns at the start of the scene; part of the group's immediate medical context.
• Parent of babies (mentioned alongside [[Mongo]]'s offspring) referenced as part of the NPC/creature continuity the party expects to reconnect with on higher floors.
• A female dinosaur observed at the edge of town among the lady [[Mongos]]; makes a distinctive call and then retreats into the jungle, indicating presence of nearby dinosaurs.
• Mother of [[Big Tina]]; [[an]] [[ursine]] who survives in a dinosaur form and attempts to restrain and protect the community from her daughter's violence.
• A pink velociraptor, identified as "[[Mongoliensis]] Pack Leader – Level 60." Formerly vampirized, she is cured and becomes a temporary minion of [[Princess Donut]] after being affected by [[Charm Mongoliensis]].
• A smaller avian creature, including a clockwork version present in the pub.
• A fast, larger raptor companion of [[Princess Donut]] and [[Mongo]].
• [[An]] [[ursine]] guardian and mother of [[Tina]]. She manages the dinosaur group that follows [[Tina]] and intervenes to protect others from [[raptors]]; intimately connected to [[Tina]]'s backstory and wellbeing.
• A smaller dinosaur in the pack who returns to [[Carl]]'s side and inspects the corpse of [[Iota]] like a predator inspecting prey.
• A dinosaur companion who remains outside the town limits with [[Tina]] and other dinos while the party negotiates entry.
• A member of the backup crew for [[Donut]]'s talent show entry and part of the group involved in staging the performance involving [[Tina]].
• A named companion/performer referenced in masquerade costume preparations; physically interacts with other characters (e.g., attempts to eat or play with them).
• Another of [[Carl]]'s [[Mongos]] (female, used alongside [[Mongo]]). Acts in raids to hold back threats like [[Big Tina]] and participates in fights and looting.
• A velociraptor referenced as a mother figure ([[Tina]]'s mother) and as [[an]] example of a creature previously charmed (compared to [[Ferdinand]]). Mentioned as "[[mongoliensis]]."
• A velociraptor initially among [[Donut]]'s pack who is transformed by the [[i take it all back]] into a bear ([[ursine]]) and returns to the stage; emotionally connected to [[Mongo]].
