# eunice

• A pregnant Fathom dwarf (level 30) intervenes in the street, attacking [[Salvatore]] with a rolling pin.
• She becomes a focus of combat and is briefly frozen and injured by elf magic.
• She is similar to the dwarf who served tea on the ride and is the dwarf mother of [[Ricky Joe]].
• She is a level-30 pregnant Fathom Dwarf who uses a rolling pin to disrupt the elf recruitment scene and is later targeted and frozen by [[an]] Icer elf's attack.
