# juicer

• Referenced prior boss used as comparative lore for how DT-affected creatures bulge and appear; invoked to contextualize the appearance of stage-three DT monsters.
• Referenced as a prior boss encounter (the "Juicer boss fight") from which the party looted tools and wrenches; mentioned to explain some of [[dungeon crawler carl]]'s acquired gear.
• Referenced earlier in the book/series as a second boss; used as a visual comparison for the bulging, veiny appearance of stage-three DT creatures encountered in the [[Abyss]].
• A newly encountered borough boss that [[Carl]] and [[Donut]] defeated; described as a fused/drugged enemy with a crucial weakness involving a high-pressure blood vessel that [[Donut]] exploited.
