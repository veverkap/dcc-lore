# leon

• Named Level 44 Dirigible Gnome Negotiator and Commisar of the dreadnaught [[Wasteland]]. Performs [[changeling]]-detection touch and inspects the group's "hostage," agreeing to the boarding deal.
• Ambassador and leader of the attack on the necropolis, commanding [[Wasteland]]'s forces, directing ground and air actions, and inspecting staged bodies in the ruse.
• Politically minded, suspicious, and ritualistic, performing [[changeling]]-detecting spells on the party and inspecting bodies.
• Negotiates boarding and hosts the interrogation that the party must pass.
• Coordinates the bombardment operation and movement of the [[Vahana]], giving orders to attackers and inspecting staged bodies.
