# eryops_gigantis

• Large, bottom-dwelling amphibian that can shoot lightning and root in place.
• Notoriously difficult to move or kill.
• Inviting one into your home means it never leaves.
• Associated aliases: River Squatter, Mud Kato.
• Related mechanics: rooted constitution bonus, lizard-class interactions.
