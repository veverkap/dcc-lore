# primals

• The ancient, largely vanished beings responsible for the civilization that produced artifacts like the [[Eulogist]]; only remnants remain and their technologies or gods (like [[Scolopendra]]) inform current myths and dangers.
• [[Primals]] refers to the original high-level beings whose minds were sampled and aggregated into the system [[AI]]; the text treats Primals as the source-sapience behind many NPC avatars.
