# Eclipse Pyxies
A demon mob type resembling fat, fur-covered guinea pig-like creatures with bat wings and jagged teeth. Stuffed toy versions are used by [[daniel bautista]] and can transform into real, flying attackers during the ruse in [[Club Vanquisher]].
