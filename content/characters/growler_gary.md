# [[Growler Gary]]
A level-25 gnoll bartender working the [[Downward Dog]] station bar. Drunk, repeatedly resurrecting, and ultimately used as the source of gnoll hands to activate [[Interdiction Rapid-Response Railway Repair Cart]] consoles. Central to the chapter's plan because his body parts satisfy a magical activation requirement.
