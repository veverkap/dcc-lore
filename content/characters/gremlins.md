# gremlins

• Small repair creatures that crawl over the vehicle after a race to perform bulk repairs, described as not truly talking, doing cleanup work, then disappearing and leaving final upgrades to [[Hedy]].
• A prevalent NPC race in [[Hungry Eyes Vendor Village]], forming the majority of the local population and appearing as vendors, patrons, and signage mascots.
• Summoned NPC helpers produced by the [[Emergency [[Gremlin]] scroll]]; they carry toolboxes, have distinct appearances, and explode when their summoning times out.
• Small NPC creatures that serve as bartenders and DJs at the [[Lollipop]] and act as mechanics/assistants for racers (e.g., fixing [[Tran]]'s vehicle).
• Small hostile or mischievous creatures referenced as swarming when [[Mongo]] was released; treated here as named mob-type entities present in the scene.
• Small helpers filling the garage and attaching flowers and decorations to the floats, mounts, and vehicles.
