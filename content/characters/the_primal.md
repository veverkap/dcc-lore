# the_primal

* Referenced as [[an]] [[infant]], resurrected, and enslaved being whose recruitment or reawakening is central to the agents' plans; rescuing or influencing the [[Primal]] is a key objective for [[Agatha]]'s faction.
* Blank-slate race choice historically used in Dungeon Crawler World, granting unlocked higher-tier skills to train to 20, but deducting -1 to all base stats and removing the +10 stat bonus humans get.
* Visually unchanged.
* Lore: ancestral progenitors of the universe; feared and mysterious.
* [[An]] underlying entity type said to exist beneath the masks; removing a mask would reveal what some call a [[Primal]].
