# nodling

• The Nodling is a two- or three-headed ogre-like race, with the ability to split into new creatures when they die, effectively granting [[an]] extra life.
• They start life as toddlers before rapid growth.
• Their death process can split into progeny that emerge as toddlers and grow quickly.
• The original body is used for tattoos in [[Signet]]'s culture.
• [[Dmitri]] and [[Maxim]] are Nodlings.
