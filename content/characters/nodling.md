# Nodling

# Nodling
The two-headed ogre-like race to which [[Dmitri]] and [[Maxim]] belong. Noted for splitting into new creatures when they die (effectively an extra life) and for starting life as toddlers before rapid growth.

## Additional Observations
# Nodling
A multi-headed ogre-like creature that died and split, later turned into a tattoo and given flesh; referenced as causing confusion due to duplicate instances.

## Additional Observations
# [[Nodling]]
A multi-headed race (typically two or three heads, but can range from two to six). Their death processes can split into progeny that emerge as toddlers and grow quickly; the original body is used for tattoos in [[Signet]]'s culture.

## Additional Observations
# Nodling
Species/race of the two-headed ogre twins ([[Dmitri]] and [[Maxim]]). Notable racial trait: when a nodling dies it splits into new creatures that emerge as toddlers and quickly grow—effectively granting an extra life.
