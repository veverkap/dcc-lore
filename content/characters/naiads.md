# Naiads

# Naiads
River-dwelling beings mentioned as inhabiting waterways on the [[Hunting Grounds]] floor. Treated as dangerous in the chapter; the narrator warns to "stay out of the water" because of the [[naiad confederacy]].

## Additional Observations
# naiads
Water-entity NPCs previously engaged by the party; [[Signet]]'s prior encounter with the naiads is connected to the larger [[the vengeance of the daughter]] plot.

## Additional Observations
# [[naiad confederacy]]
Water-tied humanoid race banned from the settlement by rule; a naiad creature briefly attacks from the river and drags a corpse away during the confrontation.

## Additional Observations
# Naiads
The aquatic folk associated with [[Signet]] and the river around [[Fort Freedom]]. Many are oppressed by the local ruling class, were affected during the "nine-tier attack," and appear as casualties in the battle.

## Additional Observations
# [[naiad confederacy]]
Water-entity guards and locals occupying parts of the river. Numerous naiads are found dead after the conflict; some served as summoned guards via traps and summoning devices.
