# naiads

• River-dwelling beings inhabiting waterways on the [[Hunting Grounds]] floor, treated as dangerous and part of the [[naiad confederacy]].
• Water-entity [[NPCs]] previously engaged by the party, connected to the larger [[the vengeance of the daughter]] plot.
• Water-tied humanoid race banned from the settlement by rule, with some briefly attacking and dragging away a corpse.
• Aquatic folk associated with [[Signet]] and the river around [[Fort Freedom]], many oppressed by the local ruling class and affected by the "nine-tier attack".
• Water-entity guards and locals occupying parts of the river, with some found dead after the conflict and others serving as summoned guards via traps and summoning devices.
