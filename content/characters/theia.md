# Theia

# [[Theia]]
A goddess of light in the [[celestial ascendency]] pantheon who grants spells including [[Travel the Path]] and has a unique recruit mechanic: turning prospects into motes of light that fit in a worshiper's pocket for transfer to the [[Temple of Theia]]. Historically connected to the Ascendency (Samantha's mother) and sponsored by the [[open intellect pacifist network]] in this season.

## Additional Observations
