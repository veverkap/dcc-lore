# theia

• [[Theia]]: a goddess of light who grants spells including [[Travel the Path]] and has a unique recruit mechanic, turning prospects into motes of light that fit in a worshiper's pocket for transfer to the [[Temple of Theia]].
• Sponsored by the [[Open Intellect Pacifist Network]] and historically connected to the Ascendency ([[Samantha]]'s mother).
• Associated with the Open Intellect's agent [[Rectrix]] and has relational ties to [[Carl]] following the raid.
• Named as [[Samantha]]'s mother, whom [[Samantha]] contemplates confronting but finds [[an]] impostor [[skyfowl]] ([[justice lights team]]/other) in her place.
