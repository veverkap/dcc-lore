# drakea

• A prolific annotator of the crawler book (22nd Edition) who documented the [[Naga]]-run season with emotional, hate-filled notes and a brief personal endnote vowing revenge; referenced by [[dungeon crawler carl]] when reading backmatter.
• A crawler who wrote extensive, emotional notes (22nd edition) about the [[Naga]]-run season and vengeance; one of the most annotative voices in the book [[dungeon crawler carl]] reads.
• A famed crawler-author of the Dungeon Anarchist’s Cookbook (22nd ed.) and the namesake of several crafted items ([[Drakea’s Enchanted Kerchief of Disorder]]); remembered by [[Mordecai]] and tied to the [[naga]] season.
• A crawler referenced by a short note appended to [[Volteeg]]'s entry; appears as [[an]] editor or commentator (22nd Edition) on the cookbook notes.
• A referenced crawler who, according to [[Mordecai]], was killed by one of his own traps after showrunners intervened; [[an]] example of the dungeon's deadly manipulations.
• A player or in-world figure with extensive notes on the [[Nagas]] and the faction's history; referenced as having run in past game seasons and left documentation about [[naga]] governance and behavior.
• A deceased figure mentioned in a personal note by [[dungeon crawler carl]]; remembered for hating the [[naga]] and referenced emotionally in the closing note about consequences for [[naga]] people.
• A crawler who appended a 22nd Edition note reflecting on [[Ikicha]]'s last entry, reiterating the danger of false hope and framing the dungeon as a staged environment populated by puppets, while still nurturing a personal hope symbolized by a mental "fire".
• A later-edition editor/commenter on [[Priestly]]'s diary entries, appearing in a note appended to the chapter, offering blunt commentary on [[Priestly]]'s fate.
• A referenced crawler who was allegedly killed by one of his own traps due to showrunner interference; used as [[an]] example to illustrate systemic cruelty and manipulated danger.
• A historic crawler referenced as having attempted a similar plan in the past and ultimately failing when the [[Nagas]] interfered, used as a cautionary example by [[Mordecai]].
