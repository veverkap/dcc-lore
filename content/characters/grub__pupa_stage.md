# grub__pupa_stage

* A specific lifecycle stage of the [[Grub]] mobs shown on the minimap with a timer indicating metamorphosis or countdown to the next phase.
* Common dungeon mobs that swarm hallways
* In this chapter, a mass of [[Grub]]s assaulted the corridor and many died, leaving a line of Xs on the map and later appearing as a variant with a pupa timer
* Sessile pupa sacs block a hallway and give birth to hostile [[Brindled Vespa]]s.
* Pupa sacs are shown in the scene during [[Death Watch]], with one tearing open to release a hornet.
