# krakaren_crotch_dumpling

• Name given by the system/[[AI]] to a spawned brood creature after a stage-three monster exploded; produces thousands of tiny, fast-growing parasitic offspring that can rapidly become a nest hazard.
• Small parasite/mob hatched from the exploded stage-three DT creature; described as rice-grain sized but rapidly growing and numerous, originating from [[Krakaren]] biology and forming nests.
• Named monster entry produced by the system's mock-motherly description after a stage-three DT creature exploded and hatched thousands of rice-grain-sized clone parasites.
• Small parasite mob that hatches from a DT-stage creature explosion; described as rice-grain-sized rapidly growing clones that require immediate killing to prevent infestations.
