# di-we

• A three-headed [[nodling]] and the progenitor ogre who died protecting [[Signet]] from agents of her sister.
• His original body is used as [[an]] ogre tattoo on [[Signet]].
• His knowledge is distributed among the offshoots [[Areson]], [[Apollon]], and [[Herman]].
