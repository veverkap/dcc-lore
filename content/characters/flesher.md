# flesher

• A sentient strip of skin created by the War Mage spell "You're Not Done Yet."
• Seeks bones to cover and animate skeletons into Symbiotes.
• Can form new war mage-controlled constructs if allowed to mature.
• Can devour and resleeve [[crawlers]] and Symbiotes like [[Levi the Seventh]].
• Frequently used by war mages as minions.
• Can smother and melt victims' bodies.
• Can animate and swarm the ball.
