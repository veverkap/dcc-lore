# louis

Here is the consolidated list of unique factual points about Louis:

* Louis is a crawler who arrived in the dungeon by driving a converted van and wields a legendary spell called "Cloud of Exhaust".
* He is paired with [[Firas]] and is known for misunderstanding his power and needing [[Mordecai]]'s intervention.
* Louis is a party member who participates in the Town Hall breach and casts a cloud/smoke spell during the operation.
* He is wounded in the explosion and is helped by [[Carl]] and [[Donut]], and later directed to saferooms and grinding with archers.
* Louis is a member of [[Carl]]'s immediate crew who helps pilot and manage the biplane/drop bear during takeoff and flight preparations.
* He is a crawler who assists [[Firas]] with transport runs, helps find the bubble world to open the portal, and coordinates movement of survivors to the sixth floor.
* Louis is a named ally who has helped recover the flying house called the [[Twister]] from [[changeling]] refugees.
* He is a member of the guild who demonstrates a move with a thrown "potion bandolier" and jokes about tattoos.
* Louis is a crawler who killed Sister Ines' bard and was confronted by [[Ysalte]] for that action.
* He is a member of the party who marries Juice Box in a private "small wedding" ceremony at the Desperado club.
* Louis is a pilot who takes the Party Planner on a detour over Shanty Town and operates from the main airport above the castle.
* He is a member of [[Carl]]'s team who becomes trapped in the Reaver Castle and is rescued by [[Samantha]].
* Louis is a general and current host whose chest has been gutted and whose survival depends on preserving organs currently linked to Epitome [[Tagg]].
* He is a crawler who was saved by [[Samantha]] and later became host to the Dream warlord until the group used the flesh golem to replace it.
* Louis is a pudgy crawler who leveled up to 30 and is a friend of [[Katia]]'s.
* He is a member of [[Carl]]'s group who helps pilot and manage the uprooted flying house and is concerned about the falling house and [[Orthrus]].
* Louis is a crawler who helps [[Katia]] and later helps wrangle refugees, and is described as somewhat blundering.
* He is a member of [[Carl]]'s team who helps recover the flying house called the [[Twister]] from [[changeling]] refugees and helps coordinate group logistics.
* Louis is a level 35 crawler who specialized into Extermination Professional and is engaged to be married.
* He is a hunter referenced on the crude map and a target of intense animosity by some (notably members of Dream).
* Louis is a participant in the recap raid who floated above and helped direct the attack alongside [[Firas]].
* He is a member of the group who volunteers to carry jugs of Uncle Morty's Insta Lawn-Kill and Undead Repellant, Extra-Strength Edition and deploy them from the [[Twister]].
