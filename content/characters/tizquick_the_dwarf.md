# tizquick_the_dwarf

• [[Tizquick]] the dwarf is a named dwarf seen leaning out of the window of the [[Nightmare]] train after the fight.
• He is [[an]] ambient NPC present at the scene as [[crawlers]] and trains arrive.
• He appears piloting or aboard the [[Nightmare]] engine and leans out to survey the aftermath after the battle in [[trainyard]].
• He leans out of the [[Nightmare]] when it stops, witnessing the aftermath in [[trainyard]].
