# tizquick_the_dwarf

* [[Tizquick]] the dwarf is a named dwarf seen leaning out of the window of the [[Nightmare]] train after the fight.
* He is [[an]] ambient NPC present at the scene as [[crawlers]] and trains arrive.
* He appears piloting or aboard the [[Nightmare]] engine and leans out to survey the aftermath after the battle in [[trainyard]].
* He leans out of the [[Nightmare]] when it stops, witnessing the aftermath in [[trainyard]].
* A dwarf conductor for the Mango line found collapsed and crying in the hallway, sympathized with by [[dungeon crawler carl]].
* A dwarf sitting at the entrance to colored-line portals at [[Station 60]], recommended for information about line colors and portal routing by [[dungeon crawler carl]].
* A dwarf who helps away teams find the correct colored line and participates in station operations.
* A dwarf crawler standing vigil at [[Station 60]], helping other [[crawlers]] navigate between colored lines.
* A dwarven [[engineer]] who visited the [[Nightmare (train)]], mentioned in conversation with [[Fire Brandy]], with a personal backstory including a killed daughter.
* A dwarf who appears aboard the [[Nightmare]] and leans out the window to observe the aftermath of the [[mimic]]'s death.
* A dwarf conductor for the [[Mango line]] found huddled and weeping at [[Station 60]], tagged and recognized by [[dungeon crawler carl]].
* A dwarf who helped drive the train and elected to stay with [[Fire Brandy]] aboard the [[Nightmare]], committing to detonate the train at the bottom of the pit.
* A dwarf who assisted [[elle mcgib]]'s away team in finding the correct colored line and reaching the exploded station 72.
* A dwarf who died alongside [[Fire Brandy]] when they revealed their NPC nature and crashed the [[Nightmare]], referenced as part of prior cautionary lore.
* A dwarf conductor for the Mango line encountered near [[Station 60]], mourning for a daughter that was "never real", displaying visible grief that moves [[dungeon crawler carl]].
* A dwarf NPC at the entrance to colored-line portals near [[Station 60]], suggested contact to learn colors/ports needed to reach [[Yard F]] or related stations.
* A dwarf ally who assists away teams and contributes to exploration and station recovery efforts on the floor.
* A dwarf who helped drive the train and chose to stay with [[Fire Brandy]] aboard the [[Nightmare]], committing to detonate the train to maximize the blast before hitting the bottom of [[The Abyss]].
