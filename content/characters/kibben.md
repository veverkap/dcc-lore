# kibben

• A Level-50 High Elf Stablemancer responsible for handling performer animals at the castle, overseeing the separation and safety of creatures like [[Big Tina]] and other show animals.
• Allegedly stole [[Sir Ferdinand]]'s rhinoceros mount [[Simoom]], prompting complaints and a demand for satisfaction.
• Former stablemaster of the high elf castle, caring for mounts and pets like [[Simoom]] and [[Rend]].
• Called in to calm [[Big Tina]] during the labor emergency at [[Bucket Boy Clinic]].
• Present trying to calm animals like [[Big Tina]] and help maintain order around the tent area.
• Level-50 High Elf Stablemancer who manages the castle's animals and handling, speaking to [[Donut]] about animal separation and safety for the acts.
