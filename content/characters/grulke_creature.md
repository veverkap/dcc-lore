# grulke_creature

* A summoned level-15 frog/toad mob used as a diversion to draw attention away from the group, summoned via the stuffed [[Grulke infantry figure]].
* A rare race of militaristic toad warriors
* Noted for leaping ability, tongue attacks
* History of mercenary decline
* A mob/creature referenced during town interrogations; locals are questioned about sightings of [[Grulke Toad]]s and [[gnomes]], implying they are notable threats or points of interest.
