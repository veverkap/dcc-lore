# radoslav

• [[Radoslav]] is a Level 75 bugbear NPC, a member of [[free love]], and survivor of the crashed red [[Pontiac minivan]]. He mourns the teammate [[Alevtina]] and engages with [[Donut]] and [[dungeon crawler carl]] about the race rules and the prize of freedom.
• He is a bugbear allied with [[Carl]]'s party, rides in the food truck during the race, and speaks about bugbear customs and honors fallen friends like [[Alevtina]].
• He is a social bugbear NPC resident of a nearby garage in the [[Team Roundabout]], provides beer from a magically refilling cooler, and converses with [[Carl]] and [[Chiyome]].
• He is a teammate present in the cul-de-[[sac]] who offers a beer to the [[mantaur]] pair and makes [[an]] offhand remark about them being "dumbasses" after they leave.
• He is a companion to [[Jasha]] who watches the scene from a nearby driveway while holding a beer.
• He is a member of [[free love]] who helps [[Jasha]] extract [[Gluteus]] from the wreckage and tends to survivors after the crash.
• He is [[an]] injured (burned arm), grieving the death of teammate [[Alevtina]], and discusses the race's stakes and the promise of freedom if they win.
• He is [[an]] awakened NPC bugbear riding in the food truck, described as a musician (pan flute) and follows bugbear cultural norms about bodies of the dead.
• He is grateful to [[Carl]] and [[Donut]] for assistance during the race.
• He is a friend of [[Carl]] referenced as killed in earlier events; cited alongside [[Jasha]] to illustrate past actions.
• He is a bugbear NPC who offers beer to [[Carl]] and companions, relaxed, runs a magical cooler in his van, and chats with [[Chiyome]] and [[Donut]].
• He is a member of [[Carl]]'s group who offers beer and makes flippant remarks about the [[womantaurs]].
• He is a crawler companion to [[Jasha]] who stands with a beer and watches the events unfolding between garages.
• He is a spectator/crawler observed alongside [[Jasha]] in the garage area waving to [[Carl]]'s group.
• He is a teammate of [[Jasha]] who arrives in a battered van at the pit stop and takes a garage spot across the street from [[Imani]] and [[Elle]].
• He is a teammate working alongside [[Jasha]] on last-minute repairs and expected to be close behind on the track.
• He is a companion of [[Jasha]] who helps retrieve a unicorn post-race.
• He is one of the pair sitting in [[Garage Four]] chatting with [[Jasha]] and the others.
• He is one of the group of [[crawlers]] watching events unfold outside the garage alongside [[Jasha]], [[Osvaldo]], and [[Filipe]].
• He is a companion of [[Jasha]] who remains with him in front of their garage during the farewell in [[Hungry Eyes Village]].
