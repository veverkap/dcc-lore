# uzi_jesus__gold_edition

* A gold-plated, powered-up variant of Uzi [[Jesus]] that appears mid-battle, wielding twin golden Uzis and dealing massive "Bullet Hell!" style damage in support of dungeon crawler [[Carl]].
* [[Uzi Jesus]] is a named totem/card with dangerous ranged capabilities, capable of lethal attacks and unreliable resurrection.
* It is also referred to as "Heyzoos" and has been summoned by [[Donut]] during a mock battle, acting as a healer.
* The totem has been part of various combinations, including [[Lazarus- A-Bang-Bang]] and a powerful but unreliable resurrecting card in [[Donut]]'s deck.
* It has also been referenced as part of a merge that improved the party's lineup, and as a named card-creature collected by [[Carl]]'s party on Floor 8.
* In some instances, it is depicted as a small robot/figure with a toy Uzi, speaking and participating in scenes.
* It has been mentioned as part of [[Donut]]'s totem set, sharing skills and memories with other totems.
