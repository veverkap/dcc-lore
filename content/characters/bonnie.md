# bonnie

* A juvenile dirigible gnome (Level 5) who runs a lemonade stand and is the daughter of [[Commandant Kane]]. She is obsessed with her pet [[Sausage]], spoiled, and under a spell that dilates her pupils.
* Carries a necklace containing a [[Soul Crystal (F-Quality)]] and wants to set up a lemonade stand.
* Has a traumatized past, including the death of her father at the hands of [[Denise]].
* Is a gnome tinkerer who disassembles [[spider]] automata and is important to the group's success on later floors.
* Is a gnome crawler who operates a ballista system in aerial combat and is a priority to protect during the skirmish.
* Is a small gnome child who mans [[an]] upgraded gun, tracks incoming missiles, and shoots down [[seekers]] during the bombing run.
* Is a gnomish farseer who reacts to the smell and sight of [[the feral god [[meatus]] has entered the realm]] and attempts to scout from the open door.
* Is a child NPC passenger protected by [[Skarn]] aboard the [[Party Planner]].
* Is a named individual referenced by [[Louis]] as someone who was thankfully not present during the bombing attempt.
* Is a juvenile dirigible gnome introduced as [[Bonnie]] (Level 5) who runs "The [[Sausage]] Memorial Fund Lemonade Stand" and is emotionally attached to her pet [[Sausage]].
* Is a child survivor found in the floating house who received a potion from her father and possesses a small purple- hued gem used as the house power source.
* Is a gnome kid who wants to install upgrades on the [[Twister]] and is part of the flying-house/child pilot subplots.
* Is a small gnome [[changeling]] mentioned as one of the children [[dungeon crawler carl]] worries about when considering the effects of [[Zerzura]].
* Is a small gnome character working on a disarmed [[spider]] automaton and is needed for survival of the ninth floor.
* Is a gnome crawler allied with the team and operates a ballista system in aerial combat.
* Is a crawler referenced in [[Sledge]]'s report ([[Prudence]] died protecting two babies; they're hugging Bonnie).
* Is a named NPC/player noted as being on the [[Ninth Floor]] (not one of [[Mordecai]]'s attendants) and is contrasted with attendants who have compression sickness.
