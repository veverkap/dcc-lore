# Paper Monsters

# [[Paper Monsters]]
Summoned, flat paper constructs forming part of Signet’s assault force (paper soldiers, paper cutouts, etc.); they enter the water and fold/dissolve as the engagement unfolds.

## Additional Observations
# Paper Monsters
Summoned, animate paper constructs used in the assault on [[Fort Freedom]] (also called paper soldiers/cutouts). Include varied forms (flying, swimming, draped over the castle) and perform as both transport and combat units.
