# paper_monsters

• Summoned, flat paper constructs forming part of [[Signet]]'s assault force, entering the water and dissolving as the engagement unfolds.
• Summoned, animate paper constructs used in the assault on [[Fort Freedom]], including varied forms and performing as both transport and combat units.
