# Tootsie

# [[Tootsie]]
A named pet in the encounter: a level-25 Night Weasel Scout. Invisible until damaged, agile, six-legged, ferret-like with oversized teeth. Tootsie is a scout/seekers-type used by hunters to paralyze and disable targets.
