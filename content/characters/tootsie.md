# tootsie

• A named pet, a Level 25 Night Weasel Scout, is ferret-like, six-legged, and highly toothed.
• It is fast and capable of paralyzing prey.
• It is invisible until damaged.
• It is a scout/seeker-type used by [[hunters]] to disable targets.
• It is a pet of [[Hunter Zabit]] and was encountered in the Selva.
