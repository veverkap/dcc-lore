# lucia_mar

* A guest performer mentioned as having "two mongrels" and poor crowd skills; one of her dogs mauled [[Odette]]'s producer during a previous show.
* A highly capable young crawler observed by Dungeon Crawler [[Carl]] during recap episodes, noted for athleticism and powerful gear.
* A powerful crawler shown on the recap tearing through the dungeon and accumulating player-killer skulls.

* A crawler who maintains multiple pets (has two dogs) without issue, used to illustrate exceptions to general pet limits.

* A top-ranked crawler on the Current Leaderboard ([[Lajabless]], Black Inquisitor General).
