# lockjaw

• Gnoll mercenary affiliated with [[madness]], separated from his unit after the [[Prism]]'s defeat, and ordered to stay behind for questioning.
• Assigned to sit in and hold a throne room for six hours to contest ownership, supplied with provisions by [[dungeon crawler carl]], and ordered not to leave until the timer expires.
