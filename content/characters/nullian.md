# nullian

* A species known as "the [[Null]]" or "Nullians" that traffic in creatures.
* They brought a [[Nightgaunt]] to Earth, leading to the "Visitor" legend.
* A social/racial classification for individuals who lack full syndicate rights and [[face]] discrimination, frequently referenced as a lived identity and motivator for participating in Walk-On operations.
