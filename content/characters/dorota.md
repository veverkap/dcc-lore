# dorota

• Crew member who offers to calm or soothe [[Princess Donut]] during the convoy's high-speed escape.
• Intervenes to save [[Donut]] when a skull creature attaches to her [[face]], tearing the thing off and killing it.
• Offers calming suggestions to [[Donut]] during the high-speed run.
• Violently rips a skull-creature off [[Donut]] during a facehugger attack, credited by [[Donut]] as saving her from a lethal effect.
• Complains about weight capacity and insists on seating arrangements, interacts with [[Donut]] and the vehicle crew during the race setup.
• Briefly stops complaining once the vehicle seat adjusts during the race.
• Complains during the frantic race, helps occupy the passenger area, and is present during the River of Sloth encounter.
• Offers commentary and advice during the pre-race sequence.
• Calls for others to take their seats ("My [[princess]]! Please get back in your seat!") and is present among the truck's occupants.
• Physically manipulates a manual window handle for [[Donut]]'s vehicle when others cannot reach.
• Appears to be integrated with the driving apparatus.
