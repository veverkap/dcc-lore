# queen_consort_ugloo_of_the_skull_empire

* Queen Consort [[Ugloo]] of the Skull Empire, a photographic subject from the "Summary Judgement" loot, identified as the mother of Prince [[Stalwart]] and part of the scandalous photos given to [[dungeon crawler carl]].
* Named member of the [[skull clan]] royal family, rumored to have been aboard [[Prince Stalwart]]'s yacht when it was reportedly destroyed during the escalation described on [[Danger Zone with [[Ripper]] Wonton]].
* Half-sister of [[Baroness Victory]], whose death and family ties are mentioned as context for the orc judge's impartiality claims.
