# ghommid

• [[Ghommid]]: catch-all species name for other-realm spirit creatures that settle where the veil is thin (cemeteries, etc.).
• Generic term for undead/contagion-affected townsfolk and mobs in the town around [[Pueblo de los Olvidados]], which can teleport into saferooms and are influenced by [[an]] Orisha like [[Asojano]].
• Term covers many one-of-a-kind and varied forms of other-realm spirit creatures that inhabit thin-veil locations (cemeteries, etc.).
