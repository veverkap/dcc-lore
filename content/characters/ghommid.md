# Ghommid

# [[Ghommid]]
A catch-all species name for other-realm spirit creatures that settle where the veil is thin (cemeteries, etc.). In this chapter, a Ghommid settlement has been corrupted into ravenous, unsettled undead.

## Additional Observations
# ghommid
Generic term for the undead/contagion-affected townsfolk and mobs in the town around [[Pueblo de los Olvidados]]. These creatures shuffle, groan, and can teleport into saferooms; many are influenced by an Orisha like [[Asojano]].

## Additional Observations
# Ghommid
Catch-all term for other-realm spirit creatures that inhabit thin-veil locations (cemeteries, etc.). In this chapter, an entire settlement of ghommids has been corrupted into violent, unsettled undead; the term covers many one-of-a-kind and varied forms.
