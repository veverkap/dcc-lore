# yemaya

* Goddess of Rivers, river mother, and siren who appears from a fountain, granting temporary boons and mystic cards.
* Associated with [[Eileithyia]] and has a shrine that, when repaired, causes her to ascend and grant special cards and boons.
* Level 250 and invulnerable while in her own temple, where she teleports [[Bomo]] to the ninth floor as a boon.
* Has a twin-tailed siren form and is a retainer of [[Eileithyia]], granting temporary effects and mystic rewards.
* Her shrine is one of the seven thorn-room choices, and repairing it will cause her to ascend and grant special cards and boons.
* Has a personal temple where she appears, with system "temple appearance rules" applying, including deity invulnerability and special powers.
* Has been referenced in various contexts, including as a deity whose shrine rebuilding triggered similar re-ascension system messages, and as a goddess whose form was compared to a Starbucks logo.
