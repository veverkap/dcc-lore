# ghommids
Generic non-corporeal undead mob type that swarms the graveyard at night. Emit an ethereal blue glow and wail loudly; many are stunned by the modified smoke/stun payloads while some are immune or resist.

## Additional Observations
# Ghommids
Swarming undead-like mobs outside the temple and in the settlement; their condition is tied to the state of the shrines and the boss. Liberating them is a quest objective; repairing [[yemaya]] cures the surviving ghommids.
