# ghommids

• Generic non-corporeal undead mob type that swarms at night, emitting [[an]] ethereal blue glow and wailing loudly.
• Some ghommids are stunned by modified smoke/stun payloads, while others are immune or resistant.
• Liberating ghommids is a quest objective, and repairing [[yemaya]] cures surviving ghommids.
• Ghommids' minds are altered by [[Asojano]]'s presence, and they teleport into saferooms when aggressive.
• Non-corporeal undead mobs that surge at night, typically slow but faster at night, vulnerable to modified [[Stun Bomb]] and smoke-based area effects.
• Include variants such as hissing fox-like and donkey-headed versions.
• Common hostile mob type besieging the settlement, with infection/affliction central to the room's stakes, and curing them tied to completing the quest and choices in the [[Celestial Thorn Room]].
