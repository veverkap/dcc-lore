# moon_sambhur

• A type of level-50 white stag mount encountered in the high-elf procession.
• A stag-type mount species (level ~50) used by the high [[elves]], named examples include [[Herschel]] and [[Hjort]], described as bone-white with milky eyes and steaming breaths.
