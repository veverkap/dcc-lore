# lepus

• A semi-intelligent species, the ancestral form of the [[Red Cornet]], historically widespread and hunted; used for worldbuilding of the [[Cornet]]'s origins.
• The broader species from which the cornets devolved, historically hunted by the [[skull clan]] during expansion; adapted echolocation and lost sight when driven into lightless caves.
• Historically hunted by the [[skull clan]] and adapted echolocation-based attacks in cave environments; referenced for in-universe species context.
