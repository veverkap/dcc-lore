# Jikininki

# [[Jikininki]]
Seven-foot-tall janitor-themed ghouls encountered in car 15 (map labeled [[Janitor's Lair]]). Level 17 in this chapter; emaciated, suit-clad humanoids with serrated nails and voracious appetites. Wore conductor-style hats labeled "Janitor" and served as a more elite threat than [[Drek]].

## Additional Observations
# Jikininki
Level-17 ghoul-like janitorial mobs found inside a rail car labeled [[Car 15 - Janitor's Lair]]. Tall, emaciated, white-skinned creatures with serrated fingernails, dressed in threadbare conductor suits and hats labeled “Janitor.” Drop bones, gold, and small trinkets.

Janitor ghoul-type monsters (referred to as janitors) that shuffle through the train holding brooms and dustbins. The [[Jikininki]] avoid interfering with people who leave them alone and are notable for their constant chattering/clicking mouths.
