# camels

• Camels: mentioned as one of the creature types featured in [[Book IV, The Gate of the Feral Gods]], treated as in-world beasts/mobs in the chapter's teaser, listed alongside [[Gnomes]], [[Geese]], and [[Shapeshifters]] as highlights of the book's fauna.
