# anton

* A wary, tattooed rogue-crawler in [[Sister Ines]]'s group, quiet and observant, and one of the two team members who worship [[Ogun]].
* Grumpy and joked about by [[Donut]] for being "unemployable" with his tattoos, present during saferoom and group scenes.
* Carries a monkey crossbow and reacts quickly to traps and enemies in the cemetery, providing ranged support during the encounter.
* Party member present during the [[Asojano]] encounter, practical and pragmatic, and helps calm [[Sister Ines]].
* Inspects [[Bomo]]'s armor, helps assemble shrine pieces, and comments on combat, trips on a hidden curb during the run toward the temple.
* Present in the thorn room, initially lured by [[Oshun]]'s charm to attempt repairing her shrine, suffers debilitating debuffs from the boss and is rescued/ treated by allies.
* Killed by [[Ogun]] when the deity steps on his head, previously implicated in earlier events (including the death of [[Elian]]).
* Corpse discovered in [[Yemaya]]'s temple, looted by [[Sister Ines]] during the chapter, and referenced as a real-world death important to the protagonists' emotional stakes.
* Deceased individual whose death weighs on the narrator, referenced as part of the emotional backdrop for the party after the temple incident.
* One of the worshipers of [[Ogun]], quiet, watchful, and skilled at scouting/ trap-finding.
* Party member and ranged damage dealer who uses a monkey crossbow, present during the assault on the corrupted town and reacts quickly to traps and ambushes.
* Pragmatic member of the saferoom group who suggests tactical actions, calms others, and carries access privileges such as a [[Club Vanquisher]].
