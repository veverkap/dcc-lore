# Minus
Protagonist in this interlude, brother of [[Linus]]. Recruited by [[Syndicate security forces]] to undergo a memory-injection impersonation and be attached to the [[fairy crawler]] as part of a suicide mission aimed at destabilizing the crawlers and triggering the [[Ascendency game]].
