# commander_stockade_of_the_lemig_sortion

• Named victim shown being devoured on [[Odette]]'s broadcast; identified as the last of thirty-five to be eaten when the Desperado pit was aired.
• A named warlord from the [[lemig sortion]] who was noted as one of the 35 devoured in the Desperado Club aquatic incident.
• The Lemig warlord (described as a caterpillar) accused of using the [[Summon Ally]] spell to invite [[dungeon crawler carl]] while he was incapacitated.
• Former commander of the [[lemig sortion]] mentioned as having died earlier, used as contextual background for political ripple effects outside the dungeon.
• The late leader of the Democratic Sortion, recalled by [[dungeon crawler carl]] as having exhibited erratic, suicidal behavior similar to what [[King Rust]] briefly displays.
