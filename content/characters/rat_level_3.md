# rat_level_3

* Common dungeon mob encountered on the way to the [[Encampment]]. Larger, fatter than earlier rats and described as having devoured corpses; fought repeatedly by [[dungeon crawler carl]] and [[Donut]].
* [[Rat]]: Common low-level corridor mob, used as a makeshift container for volatile items by [[dungeon crawler carl]].
* Shown devouring corpses in recap footage, used as a plot point in [[Hekla]]'s origin montage.
* Small mob mentioned in the [[Hekla]] recap sequence, devouring a man and used by [[Hekla]] to establish a notable moment in her origin story.
* [[Rat Queen]]: Former local power whose death allowed bloodworms to overrun part of the tunnels, affecting access to certain clubs mentioned by [[Xander]].
* A [[Rat-kin]] mob archetype referenced by [[Mordecai]]'s infobox, described as the "smartest, fastest, and ugliest" [[Rat-kin]] class, physically strong with a working grasp of magic, distinct from [[Rat Brute]] and [[Rat Shaman]].
