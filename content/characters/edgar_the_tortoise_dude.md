# edgar_the_tortoise_dude

* [[Edgar]] the [[tortoise]] dude: a scout/operative on Group Three responsible for scouting and clearing traps on the approach route to the [[naga]] castle prior to the stealth insertion.
* A talking [[tortoise]] in [[Tsarina Signet]]'s retinue who sits with the group during the meeting at camp.
* A talking giant [[tortoise]] and Shell Mage who cast the large protection spell that hid the assault force.
* A named [[tortoise]] among [[Signet]]'s summoned creatures; part of the amphibious force that disappears into the water during the approach to the castle.
* [[An]] ancient [[tortoise]] present in camp who tends to rituals tied to moonlight; he assists [[Signet]] and reacts solemnly to [[nadine]]'s death.
* A [[tortoise]] who performs the blood-and-ink tattooing ritual by using a needle-like implement and the remains of fallen beings.
* Responsible for creating living tattoo likenesses for [[Signet]] and others.
* A [[tortoise]] attendant in the field hospital who monitors patients and explains that [[Katia]]'s treatment must not be interrupted or [[an]] early wake could be fatal.
* A partner to [[elle mcgib]] who participated in the incursion into Blood Sultanate territory and reported back on troop absence.
* A scout/technician who scans enemy positions and is tasked by [[dungeon crawler carl]] to report any movement.
* A watcher in the team who senses and reports movement in the castle, aiding [[dungeon crawler carl]]'s situational awareness.
* [[An]] NPC with the level-15 skill [[Sense Movement]] who can notice upward momentum between floors, indicating someone has worshiped a god for the first time.
* A scout/operative who will report sightings (e.g., of [[Tagg]]) to [[elle mcgib]].
* A member of the crawler group whose sudden recollections of past lives and mingled fear and wonder are noted during [[dungeon crawler carl]]'s brief empathic sweep.
* A [[tortoise]] companion carried by [[Rosetta]] who brings a tattoo kit and other supplies; he interacts directly with [[Donut]] and the defenders.
* A practitioner who prepares and applies the tattoo ink and extracts spells for inscription.
* A [[tortoise]] adjutant who examines [[Donut]]'s tattoo and comments on its lack of sentience; carried off by [[Rosetta]] after the tattooing scene.
* A healer and staffer in the medical tent who performs odd tasks and provides cultural commentary during the birth scene.
* Participant in the room working on saving [[Louis]]; references past ownership of a tattoo later turned into a flesh golem and helps organize the group's approach.
* Member of the party who provided the body of [[Princess Lunette]] earlier in the story and calls parts of the operation (declares "Heart has stopped"). Connected to [[Signet]]'s backstory.
* A talking [[tortoise]] in [[Tsarina Signet]]'s entourage. Part of the diverse group gathering in the clearing to prepare for the assault on [[Fort Freedom]].
* A talking giant [[tortoise]] and a Shell Mage who cast the large protection spell used by [[Signet]]'s forces. Moves with the assault team toward [[Fort Freedom]].
* [[An]] ancient [[tortoise]] ally who appears after the battle with visible gore on his jaws. Responds to [[Signet]]'s question about moonlight and leaves to retrieve his walking stick, indicating ritual or practical duties in camp recovery.
* A [[tortoise]] who performs ritual tattooing using a needle-like stick and the remains of the dead to create moving, living tattoos on [[Signet]]. He is described as methodical and reverent during the tattoo ceremony.
* Responsible for providing the map used to assess the theater/stage layout; referenced by [[Donut]] as having been accurate about the catwalk placement.
* A named NPC turtle who was part of the group transferred off the ninth floor and is expected to return on the twelfth floor.
* [[An]] NPC/[[tortoise]] who provided a detailed castle map used by the group to plan their approach to the [[castle of the high elves]] and its ballroom.
* Slow mage and scout with level-15 [[Part of the Background]] skill; useful for stealth scouting and advanced map-making via [[Sense Movement]].
* A member of the assault force described among the summoned creatures; submerges into the water during the approach on [[Fort Freedom]].
* [[An]] NPC who provided a detailed castle diagram used by [[dungeon crawler carl]] and the group to plan their strategy around the [[castle of the high elves]] and the ballroom layout.
* A [[tortoise]] figure who once aided [[Signet]] with memories; owner/technician linked to the goodwill interface capacitor on [[Edgar]]'s map.
* [[Tortoise]] NPC who previously used tattoo supplies to place magical tattoos (referenced to explain the function of the [[Enchanted Prison Tattoo Kit]] used to mark [[Tsarina Signet]]).
