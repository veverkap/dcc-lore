# tupa

• A god or goddess worshipped by [[Osvaldo]] and [[Filipe L]], affecting access to certain locations or venues.
• Associated with memorial crystals; [[Osvaldo]] had quests to collect memorial crystals for [[Tupa]], and the god's behavior factors into the corrupted-crystal mystery.
