# Tupa

# Tupa
A god or goddess worshipped by [[Osvaldo]] and [[Filipe L]]; worship affects which locations or venues those characters can access (e.g., preventing entry to the [[Pineapple Cabaret]]).

## Additional Observations
# Tupa
A god associated with memorial crystals; [[Osvaldo]] had quests to collect memorial crystals for [[Tupa]], and the god's behavior (including sponsorship changes) factors into the corrupted-crystal mystery.
