# remex_the_grand

• A famous crawler whose actions created leverage and spectacle, used to explain how dangerous spells like [[Zerzura]] attract universal attention.
• [[An]] NPC/entity central to the third-floor assassination/quest sequence, intended target of the dynamite-triggered explosion and shown suffering in the news recap after the detonator event.
• A named lich suspected to be hiding above the [[Swordsmen]] depot/warehouse, central antagonist for the chapter's quest confrontation.
• A crawler who previously used [[Zerzura]] on the [[Scolopendra]] levels, with miscasts and losses providing cautionary precedent for the current plan.
• A monstrous in-between entity, described as Miss [[Quill]]'s husband, attempting to bring himself fully back to life and casting the curse animating [[krasue]] and other horrors.
• A named undead instance of a [[Soul Leech Capacitor]] and self-identified "tenner", encountered by [[dungeon crawler carl]] and [[Donut]], providing exposition about [[Miss Quill]], [[Featherfall]], the creation of [[krasue]], and the three-part spell [[The Final War]].
• A referenced example of a dangerous soul-affected creature ("Remex the [[skyfowl]]") previously encountered on another floor, used as a comparison to explain different flavors of withering- or soul-based entities.
• A crawler who previously cast [[Zerzura]] during a crawl and suffered catastrophic consequences when the spell skipped floors on the [[Scolopendra levels]], with his [[experience]] used as a cautionary example by [[Quasar]].
