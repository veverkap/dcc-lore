# eris

Here is the consolidated markdown list:

• [[Eris]], Goddess of Chaos, is named as a deity who is repulsed by the [[Krakaren]] and is not interested in them.
• [[Eris]] is a powerful, sponsored deity that can be invoked into the dungeon, and is cited alongside [[Apito]] as [[an]] example of a dangerous deity to encounter.
• [[Eris]] throws parties where animals are roasted, and is part of the pantheon context around [[Diwata]].
• [[Eris]] is a deity who appears on the battlefield, affects player-item interactions, and casts large-scale ritual spells.
• [[Eris]] is a goddess who performs the [[Ceremony of Chaos]] to claim the Ascendent throne and creates mass-summoned witnesses.
• [[Eris]] is a powerful entity present among the opposing forces, and is feared as a potential single-shot threat to the raiding force.
• [[Eris]] is a goddess who appears resized and interacts directly with [[dungeon crawler carl]] and [[Warlord [[Princess]] [[Donut]] (The Oak Fell)]], granting random effects and making permanent certain god effects.
• [[Eris]] is a goddess who appears amid the clash involving [[the feral god [[meatus]] has entered the realm]] and other deities, and is announced by the system at the chapter's end.
• [[Eris]] is a goddess who appears in this chapter, capable of manifesting a huge serpentine/snake-tailed form and producing reality-affected effects.
• [[Eris]] is a deity who is referenced alongside [[Apito]] as [[an]] example of a powerful sponsor/god tied to Celestial events and treated as invulnerable outside high floors.
• [[Eris]] is a goddess who appears as the [[Scavenger]]'s Daughter in this chapter, manifesting massive, serpentine/snaked-tailed form, laughing and taunting the players, and triggering reality-altering system effects.
• [[Eris]] is a deity who suddenly appears in Hungry Eyes Village, referred to as the "[[Scavenger]]'s Daughter", displaying chaotic magic, offering [[Carl]] a path to power, and manipulating events.

• [[Eris]] is a referenced ally whose endorsement lends credibility to the risky plan involving the [[Pineapple Cabaret]].
