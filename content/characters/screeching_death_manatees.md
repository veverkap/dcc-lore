# screeching_death_manatees

• Named hostile fauna tied to [[The Caves of the Screeching Death Manatees]] environment.
• Species originates from [[Red Terror Place of Family Adventure]] and is explicitly labeled as hostile, themed fauna.
• Species name used in-system for the grotesque uplifted manatees (e.g., [[Miss Talulah]]).
• Introduced as dangerous mounts/obstacles on the course, exemplified by [[Miss Talulah]].
• Species name is tied to the horror-theme branding of [[Red Terror Place of Family Adventure]].
