# sultana

• The in-game crown-[[princess]] figure and playable leader of the [[sultanate]], portrayed as untouchable and never killed during seasons, role-playing a historical figure called the Sepsis Whore.
• Present with [[dungeon crawler carl]] and [[Donut]] during the warlord council, part of the subterfuge requiring separation of allies.
• A title used in [[Prepotente]]'s rant, referring to the [[naga]] ruler (alias/role connected to [[Vinata]]), identified as [[an]] enemy and central to [[Prepotente]]'s vengeance.
• A named figure referenced indirectly, with her "real-life boyfriend" reportedly killed by [[elle mcgib]]'s team, a detail expected to influence her reactions.
