# satan

• Referred to as the "satan demon guy" who attempts to cast spells during the fight, targeted by spells and interactions.
• Targeted by the spell "Why Are You Hitting Yourself" to disrupt his casting.
• The country boss and owner of [[Satan's Water Park]], a kaiju-sized male hedgehog.
• A mountain-sized hedgehog boss-creature residing in the park, whose body connects to many tubes.
• A massive, sentient entity present at the attraction, reacting to the loop and causing destruction.
