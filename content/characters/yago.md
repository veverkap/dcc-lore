# Yago

# [[Yago]]
Named mob: "Yago. Monk Seal Picket Sentry." Level 70 monk-seal tank mob encountered on the rocky beach. Abilities listed on its totem: Kung Fu Master, Body slam, Earthquake, Deflector, and multiple additional skills. Origin: Cuba. Summon duration and stats appear on its totem card.

## Additional Observations
# Yago
Monk seal totem ("Monk Seal of the Earth Order") allied with [[Ysalte]] in this encounter; presented as a level-95, earth-magic–buffed version that smashes through the floor and pursues targets.
