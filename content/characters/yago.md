# yago

• [[Yago]]: Level 70 monk-seal tank mob with abilities Kung Fu Master, Body slam, Earthquake, Deflector, and additional skills, originating from Cuba.
• Drops a totem card ([[T'Ghee Card]]) when defeated.
• Summoned by [[Ysalte]] as a level-95, earth-magic-buffed version that smashes through the floor and pursues targets.
