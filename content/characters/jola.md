# jola

• [[Katia]] gifted [[Jola]] to [[Donut]] as a mythic creature totem from Iceland.
• [[Jola]] is a named totem/card that gets along with [[Lazarus-A-Bang-Bang]] and others in the deck-meta discussion.
• [[Jola]] is a giant Christmas-themed cat totem summoned by [[Donut]] that engages [[dungeon crawler carl]] and is later crippled/snared by [[Judge Lucian]]'s cards before timing out.
• [[Jola]] is a large card/minion that [[Donut]] discarded because it was "too big for this place".
• [[Jola]] is a named totem/character who gets along with [[Uzi Jesus]] and [[Asojano]].
• [[Jola]] is a massive Yule Cat totem that intervenes during the fight with [[The Visitor]], described as a house-sized Persian cat with a lit candle on its head.
• [[Jola]] is another summon/unit similar to [[Shi Maria]] referenced as a potential result of [[Clockwork Triplicate]].
• [[Jola]] is [[Donut]]'s summoned totem (giant Christmas cat) that engages the hydra and is targeted by snares and explosive traps during the battle.
• [[Jola]] is a summoned Yule Cat totem (giant cat) that arrives in [[Donut]]'s dispatch, speaks Icelandic lines, casts the Fashion Critic! effect to strip [[Quan]]'s clothes, and consumes garments.
• [[Jola]] is a powerful summoned totem sent by [[Donut]] that appears large and non-corporeal, using spells that forcibly remove and consume clothing, targets [[Quan]] and interacts aggressively with other combatants.
• [[Jola]] is a discarded minion/card referenced by [[Donut]] as "too big for this place", removed from play earlier in the encounter.
• [[Jola]] is a character referenced as having almost eaten [[Carl]] in a prior incident, cited as [[an]] example of totem unpredictability.
• [[Jola]] is a large card/minion that [[Donut]] previously discarded as "too big for this place", referenced as part of deck management.
