# doctor_hu

• A strategist/scientist whose predictions about [[AI]] behavior and turn-off of safety protections are referenced by [[Tipid]] and others.
• [[An]] analyst/researcher whose predictions about [[AI]] behavior, removal of protections, and political fallout are referenced by [[Tipid]] as having foretold current events.
• A named [[human]] (or [[human]]-aligned) operator involved in high-level funding and strategic decisions, associated with debates over resource allocation and sponsor strategy.
• A researcher and activist associated with the [[crawlers]] and the [[Open Intellect Pacifist Network]], credited with advocating laws granting new [[crawlers]] citizenship and system-zone privileges.
• A strategist and prophet-like figure whose predictions about [[AI]] behavior, protections being turned off, and eventual consequences were accurate and influential for characters like [[Tipid]].
