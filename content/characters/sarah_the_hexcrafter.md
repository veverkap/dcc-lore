# sarah_the_hexcrafter

• A named hexcrafter who was killed and described as "spread like jelly" on the playing field after the encounter.
• A hexcrafter who stands in the Abrams tank's turret, casting spells to empower the main gun against [[Krakaren]].
• Part of the casualty list in the arena, with her body described as spread across the playing field after the tank was hit.
