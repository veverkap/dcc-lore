# chickadees

• Smaller avian mobs mentioned alongside the [[Skyfowl]]; also notably absent from the night's events.
• A smaller, dwarf-like bird species encountered in the shopping floor, described as waist-high and similar to juvenile [[Skyfowl]].
• Described as the smaller "midget [[Skyfowl]]" that [[Miss Quill]] planned to spare during [[The Final War]]; presented as the intended protected class in her genocidal plan.
