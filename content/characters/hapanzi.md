# hapanzi

• Named (tentatively) as the husband/god-figure in [[Katia]]'s memory sequence; declares [[Shi Maria]] trapped and whose pronouncements affect the characters' interpretation of events.
• Partner to [[Shi Maria]], transforming into a [[human]] form in [[Katia]]'s dream sequence, portrayed as calming ("Worry not, my children") and tied to the [[spider]]-god memory stream.
• The [[human]] husband of [[Shi Maria]] who appears (and reappears) in the replayed memory, implicated in the possession/switching events surrounding [[Shi Maria]] and [[dungeon crawler carl]].
