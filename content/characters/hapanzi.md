# Hapanzi

# Hapanzi
Named (tentatively) as the husband/god-figure in [[Katia]]'s memory sequence; described as a god who declares [[Shi Maria]] trapped and whose pronouncements affect the characters' interpretation of the events.

## Additional Observations
# [[Hapanzi]]
Male spider and partner to [[Shi Maria]], appearing in Katia’s dream sequence and transforming into a human form in that vision. Portrayed as calming ("Worry not, my children") and tied into the spider-god memory stream.

## Additional Observations
# Hapanzi
The human husband of [[Shi Maria]] who appears (and reappears) in the replayed memory; implicated in the possession/switching events surrounding [[Shi Maria]] and [[dungeon crawler carl]].
