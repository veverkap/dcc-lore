# crawler_sinjin

* Crawler [[Sinjin]], [[an]] earlier contributor to [[The Dungeon Anarchist’s Cookbook]] (15th edition), noted a sapper trick: infusing smoke curtains with healing potions to harm undead.
* Crawler [[Sinjin]], a historical contributor to the explosives section of [[The Dungeon Anarchist’s Cookbook]] (15th Edition), described sapper-table infusion techniques that affect undead.
* Crawler [[Sinjin]], [[an]] author of [[an]] entry in the explosives chapter of [[The Dungeon Anarchist’s Cookbook]] (15th Edition), documents a craft technique using a [[Sapper'’s Table]] to infuse bombs and smoke with alchemical reagents to affect undead and other mobs.
* A crawler whose journal entry appears at the start of the chapter, recounting his disastrous decision to worship the goddess [[Kuraokami]] and describing the worship rules and consequences (boons, debuffs, falling from [[grace]], and smiting).
* Reports daily-ice worship requirement and warns others after being smote in his party.
