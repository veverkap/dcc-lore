# nekhebit

• A long-forgotten vulture goddess referenced in the lore of the unique crossbow, associated with vengeance and curses against elf traditions.
• Resurrected on the floor, triggering a system message and "Quest Complete!" notification; portrayed as scarred, old, and vengeful.
• Mentioned by [[Emberus]] as having returned and holding [[an]] ancient grudge, with temples to investigate.
• Named deity/ entity referenced by [[Hellik]] as connected to corruption and rot afflicting the pantheon.
• Named in the system message as the deity for whom [[Warlord [[Princess]] [[Donut]] (The Oak Fell)]] becomes Champion.
• Patron or source of [[Donut]]'s Champion title and associated divine effects.
• Long-forgotten vulture goddess referenced in the lore of the Enchanted Repeating Crossbow of the [[Scavenger]] Mother of Mothers, described as jealous and terrible.
• Cursed certain elf lineages and gave the crossbow to a last warrior guardian.
• Resurrected vulture goddess announced by [[Odette]] as her sponsored deity, depicted as [[an]] aged, scarred Egyptian-style vulture goddess.
• Primordial goddess mentioned by [[Emberus]] as having returned to the halls and holding a grudge against [[Emberus]]' family.
• Associated with temples and quest conflict.
• Patron/ deity-like name in the title "Oak Fell Champion of Nekhebit" attributed to [[Carl]] by [[Mordecai]], implied to be [[an]] entity or divine sponsor associated with Oak Fell rewards.
• Old vulture-like goddess in dungeon myth, called the [[Scavenger]] Mother of Mothers, portrayed as a life/protector deity who was the last of the old gods to fall, mother of three (including [[Apito]] and a centipede).
