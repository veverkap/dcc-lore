# Tom
Deckmaster boss turkey (Level 75), titled a Bereft Shaman of [[Meleagris Gallopavo Rex]]. Casts persistent mystic cards (e.g., the card that created the "Idolized" state and the Ralphie effect) and is physically obliterated by Carl's [[Daughter's Kiss]].
