# bautista

• Bautista is [[an]] owner of animals assisting in the fight, with some of his animals present on the upper beams during the raid.
• Bautista can change shape into a multi-armed creature using a ring, helping to dig through rubble toward the lower floors.
• Bautista is a crawler in the party with [[Louis]] and [[Britney]], noted in upgrade-choice discussions.
• Bautista is referenced as [[an]] item creator or owner whose "toys" used summoning mechanics, with [[Mordecai]] comparing [[Donut]]'s new Emergency [[Gremlin]] scrolls to Bautista's similar summoning/fix items.
• Bautista is a member of the last-surviving team alongside [[Louis]] and [[Britney]], whose status triggers [[an]] audience vote.
• Bautista is a member of the team who moves toward the back of the bar to watch [[Carl]]'s stage debut.
• Bautista is [[an]] ally present in the bar fight who helps secure the unconscious unicorn and protect other [[crawlers]] during the melee.
• Bautista interacts with [[Linus]] the tourist, described as unamused by [[Linus]]'s behavior and noted for having a trending tea.
• Bautista babysits [[Linus]] and teaches him to hand out tea at the volunteer gathering in Hungry Eyes.
• Bautista prepares and serves tea to friends in Hungry Eyes Village, runs the Bautista House of Tea, and offers a special blend that grants a constitution buff to vehicles or mounts.
• Bautista serves food/drink to the crowd, pouring a bowl for [[Donut]], and is present during the unmasking and group gathering on This Floor.
