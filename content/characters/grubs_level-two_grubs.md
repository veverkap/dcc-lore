# grubs_level-two_grubs

• Small janitor-type mobs generated by the [[Waste Disposal System]].
• Level-two grubs are harmless individually (give no XP) but can level up when corpses exist.
• Their kills can trigger additional spawns per the [[Corpse-Triggered Spawn Rule]].
