# sledge

• [[Sledge]]: a rock-liked creature, referred to as "[[Sledgie]]", with knowledge about events; has a new teleportation spell [[Zerzura]] and is considered too valuable to risk on immediate expeditions.
• A rock monster companion, nicknamed "[[Sledgie]]", who is kept from accompanying the shopping run and is comforted to remain in the safe room.
• A caster assigned to [[Donut]] whose presence and ability to cast the [[Zerzura]] spell factor into [[dungeon crawler carl]]'s decision about leaving the floor early.
• A named figure referenced as the origin of a powerful ability – the "Sledge's spell" – which has a timing restriction tied to the floor's last day.
• A pilot/controller of the airship [[Twister]], communicating brief status updates from the castle roof and coordinating aerial actions.
• A remote ally/crawler providing battlefield updates and attempting to reach castle towers; noted for commentary like "Surviving is winning" and reporting on frontline conditions and casualties.
• A caster tasked with a long ritual (one minute to chant, five minutes to cast) that will transfer characters to the [[Ninth Floor]]; he exchanges banter with [[dungeon crawler carl]] about videogame scores.
• A system-announcement voice or mechanic that issues a countdown ("Five seconds. Good-bye.") during the encounter, indicating a timed system effect.
• A cretin and member of the Personal Guard who travels with [[dungeon crawler carl]]'s party toward the southern front.
• A member of the operation who reports incoming missiles and battlefield events; relays strike information during the assault on [[Reaver castle]].
• A member of the party noted to be mixed in with the ball (defensive formation); previously separated from [[Bomo]] and others.
• A rock monster present in the chapter; [[Donut]] sits on its shoulder and it clutches a repurposed controller for a missile system.
• A creature or informant who reported that [[Chris]] sat next to [[frank]] and subsequently killed him; serves as a witness/account in the narrative.
• A bodyguard-like figure present in the Desperado Club while [[dungeon crawler carl]] and [[Donut]] discuss plans; stands nearby during conversations.
• A large pet/creature (nicknamed "[[Sledgie]]") kept at the base; sometimes locked up when the group goes shopping; fond of the group's games and electronics.
• A companion in [[dungeon crawler carl]]'s group noted for playing arcade games (e.g., the Frogger machine); reacts emotionally (disapproval) and interacts casually with other party members.
• A rock monster bodyguard (also called "[[Sledgie]]" affectionately by [[Donut]]) who assists with tasks like brushing [[Donut]] and enforcing quiet in the saferoom.
• A rock monster present in the personal space, noted for staring off into space and one of the group's companions.
• A named crawler/automaton operator referenced as the "real Sledge" used in [[dungeon crawler carl]]'s trap plan; also represented in the fight by clockwork replicas.
• A companion (referred to as "the Sledge") present with the group during the pub scene and travel between towns.
• A named crawler assigned to assist [[Donut]]; referenced in relation to [[Carl]]'s timing and a planned spell (the [[Zerzura]] spell) that requires Sledge's presence to cast.
• A caster whose spell ([[Sledge's spell]]) has a strict timing limitation (castable only on the last day of the floor); discussed in tactical planning by [[Katia]] and [[dungeon crawler carl]].
• A crawler who has become obsessed with a video game and is shown teaching a [[changeling]] child how to steal cars; present in the guildhall and part of the social scenes before the raid.
• A crawler assigned to lead [[changelings]] through tunnels toward the castle's secret entrance; reports heavy mantis presence and decides to approach by airship when tunnels are too dangerous.
• A remote/ support crawler fighting [[mantises]] outside and attempting to enter towers; provides battlefield status updates to [[Carl]] (mentions guard turrets, bears, [[gnomes]], and frontline conditions).
• A caster cooperating with [[dungeon crawler carl]]; tasked with a long casting ritual inside the castle to transfer or protect party members (one-minute chant, five-minute cast) and is warned about transfers and zombie attacks.
