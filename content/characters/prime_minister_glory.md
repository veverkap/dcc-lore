# Prime Minister Glory
Described as the boss of the entire Syndicate and unusually visible on the broadcast next to [[Kevin the announcer]]. Target of interest for [[Mordecai]] who plans to steal his newsletter.

## Additional Observations
# Prime Minister Glory
Syndicate Prime Minister, inhabiting a Gleener body, who argues against triggering the [[Failsafe]] due to the catastrophic political and economic fallout. Represents the cautious, institutional response on the Syndicate Council and warns about the consequences of system destruction.

## Additional Observations
# Prime Minister Glory
Head of state who personally communicated through [[Zev]] to issue an order regarding [[Agatha]]; indicates high-level concern from the [[Syndicate]].
