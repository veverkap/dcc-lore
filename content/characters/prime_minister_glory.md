# prime_minister_glory

• Described as the boss of the entire Syndicate and unusually visible on the broadcast next to [[Kevin the announcer]]. Target of interest for [[Mordecai]] who plans to steal his newsletter.
• Syndicate Prime Minister, inhabiting a Gleener body, who argues against triggering the [[Failsafe]] due to catastrophic political and economic fallout.
• Head of state who personally communicated through [[Zev]] to issue [[an]] order regarding [[Agatha]]; indicates high-level concern from the [[Syndicate]].
• Implicated in [[an]] attempted assassination via the control panel override; referenced by the system announcement that created [[Executive Order 11905]].
• High-profile Gleener who serves as the boss of the entire [[Syndicate]]. Noted for [[an]] unusual appearance on the recap broadcast; targeted by [[Mordecai]] for intelligence gathering.
• Presides over emergency Syndicate council sessions and argues against immediate use of the [[Failsafe]] due to catastrophic economic and political consequences across the galaxy.
• A Gleener body inhabited by a [[Valtay]] assistant, who argues against pulling the [[Failsafe]] due to catastrophic economic and social consequences across the galaxy.
