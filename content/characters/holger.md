# holger

* A mullet-haired were-castor companion to [[Clint]] and the party, wielding a sickle-shaped blade, quick in melee, and immune to certain paralysis effects.
* Ally and member of the group guarding the captured [[Akland]], claiming to sense spells being cast.
* One of [[Carl]]'s companions present in beaver form while [[Carl]] loots the dead elf, observing events with [[Clint]] and [[Carl]].
* A were-beaver companion who tracks and comments on language, travels with the party, and is referenced for his beaver form and personality.
* A mulleted companion in [[Carl]]'s party who explains aspects of the [[all tree]] and the local terrain, present during the regroup after the ambush.
* A beaver-form companion who is swallowed, vomited, and knocked unconscious by [[Claude Sludgington the Fourth]] early in the fight, later recovering near [[Carl]].
* A grieving beaver NPC who mourns the deaths of caretakers [[nadine]] and [[Clint]], reacting emotionally to the carnage and asking questions about worthiness and remains.
* A member of [[Signet]]'s group who reacts emotionally to the animated tattoo of [[nadine]] and acknowledges the presence of [[Clint]] within that memory, also assisting with tasks like trap retrieval and dam building.
* [[An]] adult were-castor and ally who cherishes a protective bond with [[Clint]] and advises caution to the children.
* A member of [[Carl]]'s squad present in the trench, observed in beaver form and commenting on novel buffs such as [[Total Ear Protection]].
* A crawler who moved with [[Dong]] into the darkness and joined another group during the battle sequence.
* A were-castor who opted to stay behind to protect children and others, his half of a teleport trap given to [[Dong Quixote]] to bring a mount and riders through instead.
* A member of [[Carl]]'s traveling group present on the transport to the [[Reaver castle]].
* Defensive coordinator assisting in fortifications and issues incoming warnings during the fight at the [[Reaver castle]].
* [[An]] NPC mentioned as one of the characters who were removed with the city of [[Larracos]] and expected to reappear on a later floor.
* A mullet-haired were-castor NPC who travels with [[Clint]] and the party to [[Point Mongo]], carrying a sickle-shaped blade and a sharpened stick, fighting quickly and viciously in close quarters, and claiming immunity to certain paralysis effects.
* Ally standing guard during the interrogation of [[Akland]], claiming to be able to sense spells being cast.
* Party member currently in beaver form alongside [[Clint]], present while [[dungeon crawler carl]] loots [[Akland]]'s remains.
* A companion crawler who can take a fuzzy humanoid or were-beaver form, travels with [[dungeon crawler carl]]'s group, provides cultural commentary, and participates in tracking and combat.
* Member of the group accompanying [[dungeon crawler carl]] and [[Donut]], offering exposition about the [[all tree]] and local terrain.
* [[An]] ally who is briefly vomited and swallowed by [[Claude Sludgington the Fourth]], emerges unconscious and slime-covered before recovering near the end of the skirmish.
* A beaver companion who mourns the death of [[nadine]] and reacts emotionally to the battlefield losses, reports casualties to others and interacts with the camp after combat.
* A member of the party who reacts emotionally to the animated tattoo, recognizes the appearance of [[Clint]] in the tattoo, and helps coordinate defensive work (e.g., dam building with [[were-castors]]).
* NPC seen among the group waiting at the town edge, greeted by [[Donut]] alongside [[Signet]] and [[Areson]].
* [[An]] NPC mentioned among those transferred away or absent until later floors (alongside [[Edgar the turtle]] and [[Big [[Tina]] the dinosaur]]).
