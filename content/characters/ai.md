# ai

• The psychotic artificial intelligence installed into the [[Primal Engine]] and monitored by the [[Containment Unit]], which may be testing or trapping intruders and is central to the decisions surrounding the [[Fuse]] and the [[Containment Interface]].
• [[An]] in-dungeon artificial intelligence that provided information about the [[Eleventh Floor Arena Audience Vote Mechanic]] and previously abducted [[princess [[formidable]] of the skull empire]] to the [[desperado club]].
• The dungeon/system intelligence that provides messages and manages arena mechanics, including dragging [[Princess Formidable]] from orbit to the [[Desperado Club]] and relaying information about the audience-vote mechanic tied to the [[Eleventh Floor Arena]].
