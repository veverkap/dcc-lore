# AI

# AI
The psychotic artificial intelligence installed into the [[Primal Engine]] and monitored by the [[Containment Unit]]. It appears to enjoy its existence, may be testing or trapping intruders, and is central to the decisions surrounding the [[Fuse]] and the [[Containment Interface]].

## Additional Observations
# [[AI]]
An in-dungeon artificial intelligence that provided information about the [[Eleventh Floor Arena Audience Vote Mechanic]] and previously abducted [[princess formidable of the skull empire]] to the [[desperado club]]. Serves as an informational — and sometimes intrusive — presence for players.

## Additional Observations
# AI
The dungeon/system intelligence that provides messages and manages arena mechanics. Its prior action includes dragging [[Princess Formidable]] from orbit to the [[Desperado Club]]. It also relays information about the audience-vote mechanic tied to the [[Eleventh Floor Arena]].
