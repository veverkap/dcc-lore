# fannar

• Mentioned in mockery by [[Eva]] ("Fannar left me for one of his students").

• Named as [[Katia]]'s ex (boyfriend/husband) who disclosed sensitive information about her.
• A man from [[Katia]]'s past shown in her dream/memory, a former colleague involved in events that ruined [[Katia]]'s life.
• [[An]] older, balding professor and [[Katia]]'s ex who appears in the dream-memory confronting the memory-ghost of [[Katia]].
