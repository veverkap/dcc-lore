# Chin’Dua
A drunken draconian hunter encountered leaning against a planted [[Barrel Bomb]] outside [[The Scuttlebutt]]. Stat-block note: "Chin’Dua – Draconian. Level 50. Striver." Carl marks [[Chin’Dua]] with the [[Ring of Divine Suffering]], activating the [[Left to Fester]] debuff.
