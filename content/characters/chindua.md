# chindua

• A drunken level 50 [[Draconian]] hunter named Chin'Dua was encountered leaning against a planted [[Barrel Bomb]] outside [[The Scuttlebutt]].
• Chin'Dua was marked by [[Dungeon Crawler Carl]] with the [[Ring of Divine Suffering]], triggering the [[Left to Fester]] debuff.
• Chin'Dua's severed hand appears among the trophy drops in the Hunter-Killer box.
