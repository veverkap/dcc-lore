# krakaren_monsters

• Large enemy creatures inhabiting the [[abyss]] and pit, targeted by the [[Nightmare]] detonation and the group's plan to overload the ghoul system.
• Octopus-like invaders, ranging from juvenile clonelings to [[human]]-sized stage monsters, driving the system collapse.
• Primary targets intended to be killed by the [[Nightmare]] explosion and the collapsing ghoul system.
