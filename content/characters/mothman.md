# mothman

• Monster type referenced as appearing at station 88 and associated with transit entries; one of the varied platform threats on the [[Nightmare]] Express route.
• Monster species appearing at station 88 along with a few [[Skinned Mollies]]; described as slow but visually unsettling.
• Boards at station 88 according to [[Vernon]]'s account.
