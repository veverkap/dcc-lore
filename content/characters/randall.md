# randall

• [[Randall]] is [[an]] elderly man who wanders into the chamber with a walker after the battle.
• [[Randall]] is [[an]] elderly resident who is flung from the train and subsequently killed/disintegrated by the Rage Elemental.
• [[Randall]] is a former member of [[Imani]]'s squad who transformed into a support-magic gnome-like lutin and attempted to defuse a totem possession, but was killed during the encounter.
