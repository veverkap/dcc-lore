# Luca Di.
Luca Di. is a Crawler (ID #10,284,471) and a self-identified Pastamancer who authored an entry ("A Banquet Fit for a Princess") recounting rescuing his mother and gifting [[Princess Donut]] a train hat on the [[Fourth Floor]]. He references the [[Bubble Level]] and the healing of his mother, [[Giulia DiMarco]], by a [[Benevento]].
