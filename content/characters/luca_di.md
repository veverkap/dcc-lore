# luca_di

• Luca Di. is a Crawler (ID #10,284,471) and a self-identified Pastamancer who authored [[an]] entry ("A Banquet Fit for a [[Princess]]") recounting rescuing his mother, [[Giulia DiMarco]], and gifting [[Princess Donut]] a train hat on the [[Fourth Floor]].
• He references the [[Bubble Level]] and the healing of his mother by a [[Benevento]].
• He donated his train hat on the [[Fourth Floor]] and his journal entry recounts rescuing his mother [[Giulia DiMarco]] in the dungeon.
• He references helping and being saved by [[Donut]] and [[Carl]] and lists the associated spell [[Temporary Macaroni]].
