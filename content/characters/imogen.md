# imogen

* Imogen is the half-sister of [[Signet]], current ruler of the high [[elves]], and former Sorcerer General who cast transfigurations, including the creation of the [[Were-castors]].
* She is implicated in past conflicts, including the seizure of [[Chee]] lands, and is linked to transfiguration-style assaults.
* Imogen is a powerful caster who can use red lightning and reanimated tattoos to control the battlefield.
* She is the target of [[Signet]]'s vengeance and is expected to receive a boon from [[Apito]], possibly immortality.
* Imogen is a significant figure in the Butcher's Masquerade storyline and is associated with the memorial crystal for the goddess [[Apito]].
* She is a country-level elite boss whose fate impacts the season's climax.
* Imogen is a violent NPC who has attacked a village and used a Transfiguration spell.
* She is the sister of [[Signet]] and is present near the stage, trying to grab [[Signet]] as she is pulled into the Nothing.
* Imogen is a boss/encounter figure whose death triggered disputes over looting and is associated with the Butcher's Masquerade party boon.
* She is a significant figure whose sudden appearance impacted the convoy and is credited with triggering the "Oh shit! A country boss!" achievement for [[Carl]].
* Imogen is a named boss whose defeat rewarded [[Carl]] with the legendary boss box.
* She is a crawler who was killed on the previous floor, precipitating a fight involving [[Prepotente]], [[Burcu]], and [[Osvaldo]].
* Imogen is a referential figure invoked to describe voice tone similarities and is [[an]] earlier influential NPC/figure in lore.
* She is another crawler whose death contributed to the completed [[seals]] on [[Katia]]'s box (killed along with [[Eva]]).
* Imogen is a character mentioned as having died at a moment tied to [[Osvaldo]] collecting a memorial crystal, her death part of the context for the corrupted-crystal quest thread.
