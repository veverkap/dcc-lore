# lucia

Here is the consolidated list of unique factual points about Lucia:

• Lucia is a powerful defender who could not hold a line against [[ghouls]] at a ghoul generator.
• She is a fighter who could not hold the line against [[ghouls]] at [[station 72]], used to illustrate the severity of the threat on the tracks.
• Lucia is a crawler who has not done interviews for a while and is considered "unpredictable" by [[Zev]].
• She is a hunter/antagonist in the chapter currently occupying the [[Safe Room]]; described as alternating between a "pretty woman" form and a skeleton form and capable of setting traps that can teleport intruders.
• Lucia is a hunter who uses teleportation tricks from within saferooms (pre-set teleportation destinations and escape teleports).
• She is responsible for much of the town's carnage in [[Alucarda]] and for teleporting at least one crawler into a trap where [[Vrah]] ambushed.
• Lucia is a dangerous antagonist who has survived [[an]] encounter and is responsible for killing attendants in a temple (leading to the [[Gold Apostate Box]] achievement).
• Her actions caused the desecrated temple mechanic to trigger.
• Lucia is a crawler/Hunter who can teleport [[crawlers]] out of a regular [[Saferoom]].
• She is mentioned by [[Mordecai]] as context for saferoom behavior and teleportation tricks.
• Lucia is a notorious crawler who attacked [[Donut]] and others.
• She wields [[an]] escape teleport spell and may be responsible for a trail of slaughter across towns; perceived as dangerous and possibly experiencing reality differently than others.
• Lucia is a crawler who has had her inventory swapped with [[Florin]] during [[Eris]]'s temporary effect.
• She is a crawler who frequently buys teleport traps and anchors on the market and is feared by other squads; noted for purchasing teleport traps and alcohol.
• Lucia is a dangerous hunter who uses teleportation tricks from saferooms to attack others.
• Her teleport trap and escape tactics trigger much of the chapter's confrontation and she ultimately escapes after clearing much of the town.
• Lucia is a [[Lajabless]] (shapechanging species) attending the Masquerade; appears frightened and unpredictable.
• She is a crawler who displays rapid, near-instantaneous personality switches: one moment sobbing and childlike asking for "my [[mom]]," the next swaggering, violent, and vengeful.
• Lucia's head may be housing other children (a plot detail that prevents [[hunters]] from using her to break the seal).
• She moves through the ballroom casually and drinks tequila during the scene.
• Lucia is a frightened party member cowering behind a [[crocodilian]], covering her ears during the chaos in the ballroom.
• She is a crawler who apparently teleported away before the boss fight, using escape teleports set at stairwell locations; not present for the fight.
• Lucia is a high-demand crawler who purchases teleport trap anchors and alcohol on the market; feared by some squads for aggressive behavior.
• She is a crawler who has refused a deal and may switch personalities unpredictably.
• Lucia is a teammate of [[Florin]] who moved with them from the [[Desert Track]] to [[Satan's Water Park]].
• She is a crawler who has tens of thousands of children in her head (storyline element) whom the team is determined to protect.
• Lucia is a crawler who disputes movement options with [[Florin]] during the crisis.
• She is a crawler who is described as being controlled by someone else and is uneasy during the visit to the [[Desperado Club]].
• Lucia is a crawler with a skull-faced hag form who wields a golden lasso that creates portal-like effects, used to cut [[mimics]] in half during combat.
• She is a crawler who briefly appears in a "beautiful-woman form," pats [[Carl]] on the head, then leaves the room; implied to have been in a different form earlier.
• Lucia is a crawler who partners with [[Florin]] and is part of the garage check-in messages.
