# asher

• Asher is [[dungeon crawler carl]]'s half-brother, a small boy (about six years old) who is shy and frightened.
• Asher's [[face]] appears as one of the heads on the Reminiscence Hydra, deeply affecting [[Carl]] emotionally during the fight.
• Asher's presence is emotionally impactful and a central motivation for [[Carl]] during the battle.
• Asher's disappearance is on [[Carl]]'s mind, mentioned as part of the larger context of missing people in the chapter.
• Asher is a person from [[Carl]]'s life whom [[Carl]] thinks about in the chapter when reflecting on loneliness and absence.
• Asher is [[Carl]]'s deceased brother, whom [[Carl]] recalls and vows to avenge and make proud as he ascends into the portal.
• Asher's likeness appears as one of the hydra's heads, and his inclusion ties into personal trauma and the dungeon's use of familiar victims.
