# gustavo_3

• The other [[rottweiler]] companion of [[Lucia Mar]] shown on the premiere, noted by the broadcast naming (the "3" indicating lineage of anonymous entrants without surnames).
• Participates in combat sequences and fires lightning attacks.
• Is impaled by [[Mongo]]'s Porcupine Defense quills and is ultimately destroyed by [[Rend]]'s Wrecking Ball attack.
• Devours victims (including [[an]] NPC and later [[Samantha]]) and is referenced by [[Lucia Mar]] as particularly dangerous.
• Snaps down on and savagely mauls [[Samantha]].
• Is affected by [[Lucia Mar]]'s [[Rubber]] effect during the ghoul-station sequence.
• Named "Gus" in speech and is paired with [[Cici]] and owned by [[Lucia Mar]].
