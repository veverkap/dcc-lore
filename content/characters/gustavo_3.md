# Gustavo 3

# Gustavo 3
The other rottweiler companion of [[Lucia Mar]] shown on the premiere. Noted by the broadcast naming (the "3" indicating lineage of anonymous entrants without surnames). One dog later gains a lightning-bark ability.

## Additional Observations
# Gustavo 3
The second rottweiler accompanying [[Lucia Mar]] on the televised recap. Participates in combat sequences.

## Additional Observations
# [[Gustavo 3]]
The smaller rottweiler paired with [[Cici]] and owned by [[Lucia Mar]]. In the recap he snaps down on and savagely mauls [[Samantha]]; later sits chewing on the victim’s head.

## Additional Observations
# Gustavo 3
A rottweiler pet (called Gus in shorthand) belonging to [[Lucia]]. He fires lightning attacks, is impaled by [[Mongo]]'s Porcupine Defense quills, and is ultimately destroyed by [[Rend]]'s Wrecking Ball attack.
