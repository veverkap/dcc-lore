# [[The Maestro]]
Named as the host of the assigned program [[death watch extreme]]. Mentioned by [[Mexx-55]] as the show host; no direct appearance in the chapter but invoked as the upcoming on-screen talent.

## Additional Observations
# The Maestro
Orc host of the televised stage show. Brash and theatrical, he mocks crawlers, reveals footage to the audience, and gifts items (e.g., [[Bates zip-up tactical boots]] to [[dungeon crawler carl]] and a [[Legendary Skill Potion]] to [[frank]] / [[Maggie My]]). Claims lineage tied to the [[Skull Clan]].
