# the_hoarder

* The [[Hoarder]] is a 15-foot-tall, grotesquely obese woman formed from a trash heap, vomiting insect minions, and was defeated by [[dungeon crawler carl]] and [[Donut]].
* The [[Hoarder]]'s final moments were replayed during interview clips to question combat behavior and teamwork between [[dungeon crawler carl]] and [[Princess Donut]].
* The [[Hoarder]] was referenced as a first boss and used as a point of comparison when observing street vendors and city life.
* The [[Hoarder]] was the first boss [[Carl]] fought, described as "nothing more than a sad woman", and was used as a moral comparison for what [[Carl]] did to [[Penelope 3]].
* A boss-type enemy, [[Hoarder]], killed by [[dungeon crawler carl]] and companions, yielding a single persistent loot drop (a [[Neighborhood Map]]).
* A named boss with significant broadcast impact, referenced in conversation as [[an]] epic battle ("that epic battle with [[Hoarder]]").
* A previous boss used as a tactical reference point, with a weakness involving [[bugs]] emerging from her mouth, which could be exploited by killing a bug before it fully emerged.
* A past boss fight reused in the dungeon, illustrating the pattern of reusing collected materials and trophies, part of context for why recreated loved ones are anticipated.
