# jaygee

• A hunter whose severed hand was recovered as a trophy from a [[Gold Hunter Killer Box]]; referenced by [[dungeon crawler carl]] when taunting any of JayGee's associates.
• The death of JayGee produced a trophy (the mottled gray hand) and triggered a gold raid reward.
• JayGee was the victim of a trap left by [[dungeon crawler carl]].
