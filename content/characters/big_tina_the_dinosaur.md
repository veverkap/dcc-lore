# big_tina_the_dinosaur

* Big [[Tina]] the dinosaur, a named NPC dinosaur referenced as among the group of [[NPCs]] not present until later floors.
* A large pink-feathered dinosaur implicated in attacks on riverside towns like [[Point Mongo]]. Described as wearing a "pink boa," prone to getting into trouble, and dangerous to [[ursine]] creatures. Central suspect in [[The Recital.]] investigation.
* Named killer responsible for slaying [[Fodera]]; invoked as the entity that killed certain [[ursine]] [[NPCs]] during the dinosaur-ripped rampage.
* A powerful [[ursine]] enemy observed orchestrating the reassembly of severed body parts into stronger reformed creatures; identified as the source/leader behind the mass of reanimated fragments in town.
* A boss giant dinosaur referenced as going "off the rails" and building [[an]] undead army; implicated in the vampirism spread within the forest floor.
* A formerly vampiric Allosaurus noted for having built [[an]] army of undead monstrosities from forest slaughter remnants. Mentioned in the quest text for [[The Creeping Apocalypse]] as a key figure in the prior vampirism events.
* A giant allosaurus pet present in the woods that intervenes by eating mushroom guards and otherwise threatens chaos if unmanaged.
* A giant dinosaur/performer used in [[Donut]]'s act, handled by the stable staff and displayed in secure cages prior to her performance.
* A giant allosaurus ballerina rescued on a quest; daughter of [[Kiwi]] and formerly a bear, altered by the attack of final boss [[Scolopendra]].
* A named figure referenced in comparison to [[justice lights team]]'s feather tufts; currently noted as remaining in the FUPA.
* A familiar dinosaur- like combatant whose roar is heard as city defense towers fall during the chaotic assault around [[Larracos]].
* A target for rejoining the FUPA; was a cause of commotion that led to flaring events at the [[castle of larracos]].
* A giant allosaurus who reacts loudly to [[Mongo]] and is emotionally invested in the group and the children present near the medical tent.
* A large, pink- feathered dinosaur (described as wearing a "pink boa") suspected of attacking towns (including [[Point Mongo]]). Carries a wand (now depleted) and has [[an]] aggressive temperament, especially toward [[ursine]] creatures. Associated with territorial raids and chaos on the river.
* Named killer of [[Fodera]]; implied to be the large dinosaur or hostile entity responsible for mass slaughters in the town.
* A formerly-[[human]]/dancer child transformed into a murderous, vampiric dinosaur (allosaurus) that now rampages through the hunting grounds, actively hunting [[ursine]]. Central target of the quest "The Recital" and capable of creating glamoured minions like the [[Glamoured Fragment – [[Ursine]] Claw (Left)]].
* A powerful animated [[ursine]] spellcaster whose activity is central to the scene. [[Big Tina]] appears to be calling dead body parts to a collection point and is associated with reanimated, composite minions.
* A boss-class giant dinosaur whose rampage and undead/undercutting abilities are tied into the vampirism outbreak in the forest; linked to the broader plot the party must investigate.
* A once-vampiric Allosaurus who had been building [[an]] army from the forest slaughter leftovers. Mentioned as related to the current crisis that spawned [[odious creeper]] and undead monstrosities.
* A giant allosaurus summoned/controlled by some players. Described as a berserker who can break free and devour mushroom guards; her presence affects nearby combat and crowd control.
* A massive, stage-performing dinosaur (also called [[Tina]]) wearing a pink boa and wielding [[an]] inert wand; enchanted during the show to perform a dance and cast a secondary charm through the wand.
* A giant allosaurus ballerina rescued during a quest; mother of [[Kiwi]] and formerly a bear until changed long ago during [[an]] attack by [[Scolopendra]].
