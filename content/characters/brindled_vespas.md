# brindled_vespas

• [[Brindled Vespas]] are a massive, deadly species used as a comparison for picking a pet.
• They are level-eight hornet-like mobs that can be found on the [[Second Floor]].
• They can be knocked from the air with a [[Slingshot]] and finished with [[Donut]]'s magic missile.
• [[dungeon crawler carl]] and [[Donut]] grind for [[experience]] on the [[Second Floor]] by fighting them.
