# ralph

• [[Ralph]] is a named level 11 Neighborhood Boss, a tiny, enraged champion gerbil.
• He is described as a "Frenzied Gerbil" boss who bursts from a small metal cage and attacks players until a [[Danger Dingo]] intervenes.
• He is later revived and stunned before being stomped by [[dungeon crawler carl]].
• [[Ralph]] is a named boss from the second floor, previously killed by [[Carl]] (squished), and referenced as a frenzied gerbil boss that influenced the System [[AI]]'s obsession.
