# Ralph
A named level 11 Neighborhood Boss presented as a tiny, enraged champion gerbil. [[Ralph]] is described as a “Frenzied Gerbil” boss who bursts from a small metal cage and attacks the players until a [[Danger Dingo]] intervenes; later revived and stunned before being stomped by [[dungeon crawler carl]].
