# asojano

* Asojano, a Level 130 City Boss Orisha, is the "Lord of Smallpox" who radiates a disease aura and causes townsfolk to go mad. He requests that [[Paz]] repair his shrine to leave town.
* Asojano is a former god/orisha who clears a path and performs a ritual to "reawaken" his shrine, radiating a sickly green light and creating protective barriers.
* He is the central antagonist of the thorn room, using disease-giving attacks and being defeated in battle.
* Asojano is a named entity mentioned as having been chased off by the party prior to [[Ogun]]'s arrival, implying a hostile presence.
* He is a named NPC the party encountered previously, mentioned by [[Donut]] when grumbling about costs and loot.
* Asojano is a named totem/card mentioned as potentially problematic if summoned alongside other cards in combat.
* He is a powerful, aggressive totem-card allied to [[Donut]] that can cast nasty debuffs, but is potentially untrustworthy with subtle control issues.
* Asojano is a teammate who, along with [[Raul]], was frozen and killed by snare cards in practice fights.
* He is a previously-seen totem card referenced as the other half of the combo that created [[Lazarus-A-Bang-Bang]], known for a large shell-made wand and distinctive reveal.
* Asojano also appears in a gold-edition form ("Lord of Blight – Gold Edition") during the fight, approaching the downed [[Quan]] and applying debuffs and boils before the final death.
* He is a totem referenced as part of the strengthened combo (alongside Uzi [[Jesus]]) after permanent alterations, mentioned when discussing the party's enhanced roster.
* Asojano is a named card-creature on Floor 8 who was combined with Uzi [[Jesus]] into [[Lazarus]].
* He is a Level 130 City Boss, [[an]] Orisha known as the "Lord of Smallpox" who appears in the town, disease incarnate and non-corporeal, and his presence is warping townsfolk into madness and his shrine must be repaired to free the town.
* Asojano is [[an]] orisha/former god figure who cleared the doorway and stands at the midpoint between the party and the temple, performing a ritual to create the "Sun of Reawakening" and claiming he is stronger inside the temple.
* He is the Lord of Smallpox and a city boss on the floor, central antagonist of the thorn room choice (repairing his shrine will win the quest or force a boss fight), and can cast disease effects and is vulnerable to coordinated attacks.
* Asojano is a named card/totem mentioned as likely to be difficult to control in combat, part of [[Carl]]'s concerns about deck behavior in real fights.
* He is listed among the six totem cards available to the party, described as [[an]] orisha totem, and present in the roster of potential summons alongside [[Geraldo]] and Shi [[Maria]].
* Asojano is a powerful, somewhat hostile totem card that can apply nasty debuffs (though arena safeties can block some effects), strong in battle but difficult temperamentally for [[Donut]] to control.
* He is a team member noted for poor decisions in combat (frozen and killed by snare cards in training).
* Asojano is a previously known totem referenced alongside Uzi [[Jesus]], originally carried a large wand made from shells, and [[Donut]] compares [[Lazarus-A-Bang-Bang]]'s skills and constitution to those of Asojano.
* He also appears as a gold-edition variant ("Lord of Blight – Gold Edition"), approaches and applies debuffs to [[Quan]] during the fight before vanishing alongside Uzi [[Jesus]].
* Asojano is a totem referenced as part of a powered-up combination, described as a stronger version used in earlier fusions.
* He is a named card-creature mentioned as part of the card-collection on Floor 8 and combined into or associated with [[Lazarus]].
