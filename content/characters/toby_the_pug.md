# Toby the pug

# [[Toby the pug]]
A pet mentioned by the narrator in the chapter. Cited humorously as depending on reader reviews for food—"Toby the pug will starve to death"—and described as needing "five or six meals a day." Serves as a lighthearted, non-dungeon character used to motivate reader engagement.

## Additional Observations
