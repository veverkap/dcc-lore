# crixus

• A sniper aboard the [[Wasteland]] who expresses ideological hatred toward the subject peoples ([[bactrians]], [[dromedarians]]) and helps coordinate the defense.
• Engages directly with the [[Vahana]] crew during the ascent.
• A gnomish sniper who at one point threw a live ammo ball that missed.
• Participated in attacks on [[Carl]] and at one point threw a live ammo ball.
• Sniper among the attackers with strong ideological motivation against the enslaved groups and a role in anti-ship combat during the assault.
• A named Dirigible Gnome sniper.
