# warlord_agatha

• Leader/proposer for the [[war mages]] who proposed emergency action items that altered the rules during the Peeling Phase, including granting access to [[Conscription]] and changing defeat consequences.
• Former Warlord of the [[war mages]] who has been system-announced as having abandoned her post and removed, triggering Warlord-level command changes.
