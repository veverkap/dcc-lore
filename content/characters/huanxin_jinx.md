# huanxin_jinx

* A six-eyed [[Grixist]] sponsor and plotter who arranged sponsorship and influence around [[Eileithyia]] and seeks to recruit attendants for the Twelfth Floor Ascendency games, CEO of [[Icon Industries]].
* A [[grixist]] heiress and recurring sponsor who drives the storm goddess [[Dodola]] when present, negotiates sponsorship cuts and repeatedly raises demands.
* A sponsor figure/CEO known for cloning herself and having been banned for cheating, sponsored the goddess [[Eileithyia]] and is tied to exit/attendant deals involving [[Katia]] and [[Donut]].
* A figure referenced for prior intelligence, told [[dungeon crawler carl]] that "they'd never let us be safe".
* [[An]] heiress currently on the twelfth floor driving the goddess [[Eileithyia]], mentioned as [[an]] exemplar of the [[Grixist]] type and as having no living relatives besides a child clone.
* [[An]] antagonist with a mortal enmity toward [[Odette]] mentioned in relation to court politics and contestant placement, involved in prior recruitment or manipulation attempts around [[Odette]] and [[Katia]].
* A crawler or agent driving the god [[Eileithyia]] in the basilica events, tied into the god-release and sub-pantheon situations.
* A named entity referenced in relation to a plan or arrangement concerning [[Daniel]] and [[Katia]] ("if this thing with [[Huanxin Jinx]] doesn't work out").
* A goddess who will arrive shortly to perform the ceremony after [[Katia]] consumes the [[Orchid of Eileithyia]].
* A [[grixist]] who is also CEO of [[Icon Industries]] and sponsor of [[Eileithyia]], appears as a goddess form and is ultimately killed in the tent by a bolt fired from [[Enchanted Repeating Crossbow of the [[Scavenger]] Mother of Mothers]].
* A powerful, six-eyed sponsor and CEO-type figure who sponsored [[Eileithyia]]'s retainer arrangement, a [[Grixist]] and head of [[Icon Industries]], plotted to resurrect [[Yemaya]] as a retainer and actively recruited [[Katia]]'s help in exchange for protection and favors on the twelfth floor.
* A benefactor figure who promised to take [[Katia]] to the 12th Floor using the [[Crown of the Sepsis Whore]] and to protect her.
* A [[grixist]] heiress and the sponsor driving the storm goddess [[Dodola]], negotiates payment percentages for intervention and repeatedly increases her demands.
* A player/guild leader who promised to escort [[Katia]] to the 12th floor using the [[Crown of the Sepsis Whore]] and who sought the Ascendency throne and revenge against [[Odette]].
