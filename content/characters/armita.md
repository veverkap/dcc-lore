# armita

• Armita, a close friend of [[Odette]], became [[an]] adjutant (Celestial Attendant) and was trapped in the dungeon, later killed after [[Odette]]'s confession and dealings with [[Huanxin]].
• Armita is [[Odette]]'s friend and former party member who works as a demigod attendant and go-between for sponsors in the Celestial Ascendency, arranging contact with sponsor [[Huanxin Jinx]] and negotiating bribe terms.
• Armita acts as a go-between for sponsor negotiations, repeatedly altering the demanded cut, precipitating the party's desperate actions.
