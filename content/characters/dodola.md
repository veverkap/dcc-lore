# Dodola

# Dodola
A goddess who was trapped in containment and killed during a rigging plot involving [[Huanxin]] and [[Odette]]; central to Odette’s confession about the past.

## Additional Observations
# Dodola
A storm goddess summoned by the party's [[Summoning Box]] and driven by sponsor [[Huanxin Jinx]]. She is forced into a reduced form by the [[Circle of Containment]] and the [[Dart of Ophiotaurus]], trapped with the [[Puzzle Disk]], and killed when the contained lightning spell detonates. Her death produces the key that opens the stairwell to the [[Eleventh Floor]].

## Additional Observations
# [[Dodola]]
[[Dodola]] is the smaller, female storm deity summoned at the scene and revealed to be a sponsored god driven by [[Huanxin Jinx]]. She initially intervenes against [[Adad]], then marches on the party; she is ultimately forced into a reduced form inside the containment, killed by the final explosion of the level-20 lightning effect, and dissipates into ash.
