# dodola

• A goddess who was trapped in containment and killed during a rigging plot involving [[Huanxin]] and [[Odette]].
• A storm goddess summoned by the party's [[Summoning Box]] and driven by sponsor [[Huanxin Jinx]].
• Forced into a reduced form by the [[Circle of Containment]] and the [[Dart of Ophiotaurus]], trapped with the [[Puzzle Disk]], and killed when the contained lightning spell detonates.
• Her death produces the key that opens the stairwell to the [[Eleventh Floor]].
• A goddess referenced in [[Odette]]'s backstory who was driven by [[Huanxin]] and later trapped and killed during a containment incident.
• [[Dodola]] is the smaller, female storm deity summoned at the scene and revealed to be a sponsored god driven by [[Huanxin Jinx]].
• She initially intervenes against [[Adad]], then marches on the party; she is ultimately forced into a reduced form inside the containment, killed by the final explosion of the level-20 lightning effect, and dissipates into ash.
• Sponsored storm goddess summoned by the party’s [[Summoning Box]]. Drives storms and lightning; under the control of sponsor [[Huanxin Jinx]] when present.
• Forced into the [[Circle of Containment]] with the [[Puzzle Disk]], becomes the deity killed by the final lightning explosion, causing the "deity fallen" system announcement.
