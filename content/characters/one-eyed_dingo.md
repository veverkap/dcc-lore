# One-Eyed Dingo
A distinct [[Danger Dingo]] in the chapter—missing an eye and injured—who eats a [[Pet Biscuit]], flips allegiance, leaps upon and swallows [[Ralph]], and is later killed when the boss bursts out of its head.
