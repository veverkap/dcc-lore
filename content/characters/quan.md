# quan

• A cheating, high-level crawler who wields a celestial robe granting flight, lightning attacks, and a shield; antagonizes [[dungeon crawler carl]] and assaults [[Orthrus]].
• Named crawler encountered in the bubble whose arm [[Carl]] ripped off; wore multiple rings including [[Rockard's Ring of Sniping]]; had a reputation for [[glory]]-stealing.
• One-armed individual who will likely attend [[The Butcher's Masquerade]] and is a potential source of conflict for the party.
• One-armed, flying crawler antagonist referenced as a target to be taken out before he can get a key and move to the [[ninth floor]].
• Member of the party who sets traps (nail traps, silent alarms) around exits; mentioned as assisting with defenses near the saferoom.
• Primary antagonist in this chapter – [[an]] incoming crawler who may be invisible, targets stairwells, and holds keys that determine fights and teleports.
• Antagonist PvP crawler in the battle at the capitol; uses snare and mass heal cards and multiple totems; ultimately suffers massive debuffs and dies here.
• Crawler whose body is now lootable after a ceiling crack; his cards remain inaccessible but his gear can be taken by others.
• Killed crawler whose inventory [[dungeon crawler carl]] loots; carried [[an]] enormous array of magical weapons, coins, and valuables (including a celestial cloak and strange items like a nickel-filled sock).
• Sadistic crawler on [[Floor 8]] who stole [[dungeon crawler carl]]'s key, forcing a lethal confrontation in which [[dungeon crawler carl]] killed him without receiving a player-killer penalty.
• Crawler who provided [[Carl]] with funds (about five million) referenced in the dialogue.
• Previous wearer of the [[Cloak of the Benevolent Champion]]; his use of the cloak demonstrated custom-tailored abilities (flight, bolt attack, shields, cloaking) and provided context for strategy about who should wear it next.
• Antagonistic half-elf crawler (sometimes called Quan Ch) who wields a celestial- tier robe granting flight, lightning attacks, and shield protection.
• Crawler whose limb was severed by [[Carl]] during a confrontation; previously attempted lightning attacks.
• One-armed individual [[Carl]] expects might attend the masquerade and potentially cause trouble; listed among the interpersonal tensions to watch.
• Previously encountered "cheater guy" in the group's backstory; mentioned during [[Donut]]'s recounting of card-game-related lore.
• Direct adversary: [[Carl]] and [[Donut]] intend to reach the stairwell and "take out" [[Quan]] to prevent him from obtaining keys and reaching the [[Ninth Floor]].
• Party member who set low-level traps and a silent alarm near [[an]] exit; his traps were disabled by [[Imani]] before the party's reconnaissance.
• Opponent crawler referenced as stealthy and dangerous; suspected of disabling teleport traps and able to go invisible.
• Antagonistic crawler (player killer) engaged in the capitol fight; uses cards like [[Mass Heal]], summons totems, absorbs damage with shields, is targeted by the group, stripped by [[Jola]]'s Fashion Critic!, and ultimately dies with many debuffs after [[dungeon crawler carl]] returns the [[For Daddy (ring)]].
• Deceased or downed crawler whose corpse can now be looted ("Quan's body could now be looted"); earlier conflicts with [[Ren]] are mentioned in the chapter's regretful reflections.
• Defeated crawler whose inventory [[Carl]] loots; carries vast weaponry and wealth, including [[Quan's celestial cloak]], a nickel sock, and other powerful items recovered by [[Carl]].
• Crawler from whom [[dungeon crawler carl]] claims to have earned almost five million; mentioned during a discussion of pooled funds and purchases like the [[barracks upgrade]].
• Named crawler referenced via [[Quan's cloak]] in [[Carl]]'s loot, which is under consideration for inspection by [[Mordecai]].
• Sadistic crawler on [[Floor 8]] who stole [[Carl]]'s key and provoked lethal retaliation; described as a petty and cruel player.
• Named crawler/warlord referenced by his dropped loot, especially [[Quan's Cloak]] and the sapient weapon looted from him ([[Jefferson]] the nickel sock); his defeat provided key items and achievements to [[dungeon crawler carl]] and allies.
• Previous wearer of the [[Cloak of the Benevolent Champion]]; mentioned as having used the cloak selfishly; his prior actions inform the party's decisions about how and when [[Donut]] should equip the cloak.
• Sadistic crawler who stole [[Carl]]'s key, forcing [[Carl]] into lethal conflict; described disparagingly by [[Quasar]].
