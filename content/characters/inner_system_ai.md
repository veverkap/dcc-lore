# Inner System AI
A vast, benign artificial intelligence near the center of the galaxy that projects an enhancement zone and predates Syndicate society. Worshipped by the [[nebulars]]; its existence motivates the Nebs' attempts to gain influence over Syndicate governance and crawls.
