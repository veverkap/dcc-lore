# king_rust

* King [[Rust]] is the monarch of the [[skull clan]] who recently arrived in earth orbit and is noted for remaining quiet amid controversy surrounding [[Prince Maestro]] and the viral [[Pork Boy Snick]].
* He is the ruler associated with the [[skull clan]] and has spent money on a deity sponsorship for the upcoming floor, potentially targeting [[dungeon crawler carl]]'s party via sponsored god mechanics.
* King [[Rust]] is the orc leader of the [[skull clan]] mentioned in the context of succession and political maneuvering.
* He is a referenced royal whose children are embroiled in internal violence and is mentioned by sponsors [[Dr. Hu]] and [[Porthus]] as part of the larger geopolitical chaos surrounding the crawl.
* King [[Rust]] is a high-ranking orc leader (father of [[Prince Stalwart]] and [[Maestro]]), arriving on the battlefield mounted and wearing parade regalia.
* He is the orc warlord opposing [[dungeon crawler carl]] and the posse, mounted on a large swamp yak and accompanied by [[an]] adjutant.
* King [[Rust]] is the large orc leader of a powerful corporate government and commander of a vast horde who arrives to negotiate and reveals his daughter [[princess [[formidable]] of the skull empire]]'s plan to trigger the [[System Failsafe]] from [[an]] orbital [[Monitor Station (Venus Orbit)]].
* He is a slain leader whose severed head and corpse are inventoried by [[dungeon crawler carl]] and scanned by [[Rosetta]] to trigger credit transfer.
* King [[Rust]] is a recently killed ruler whose decapitation and death drive major political fallout in the chapter.
* He is a deceased king whose body was referenced in [[Donut]]'s taunt and [[dungeon crawler carl]] actually has the corpse in his inventory and recovered magical items from him.
* King [[Rust]] is a savage orc leader whose arrival to the dungeon brought a harem of courtesans into the game; his actions and bargains with the [[AI]] explain the status of certain outworlder survivors.
* He is a monarch who publicly disowned [[Prince Maestro]] and attempted to quell criticism of royal behavior following controversies related to the crawl and war games.
* King [[Rust]] is a named figure who has recently spent money on a deity sponsorship and is mentioned as part of potential targeting connected to deity sponsorships on the upcoming floor.
* He is a figure rumored to be in the dungeon and mentioned among the Skull Empire's displaced rulers.
* King [[Rust]] is a named leader of the Skull Empire mentioned in relation to royal succession and the empire's internal politics affecting characters like [[Prince Stalwart]].
* He is a monarch of the [[skull clan]] referenced in discussion of the royal family's status, noted as "supposedly in the dungeon" with uncertain whereabouts.
* King [[Rust]] is a named ruler referenced in the sponsors' conversation; his children are described as trying to murder each other, indicating palace instability tied into crawl politics.
* He is referenced as [[an]] outside influence that might attempt to bribe or whisper to advocates, mentioned by [[Quasar]] as a potential corrupting force from which a lawyer might take orders.
* Then-Prince Rust, [[an]] orc who killed [[Brooks]] with a whip, was later decapitated by [[Colonel Rosetta]].
