# epitome_tagg

• A wealthy, bald dream-elf warlord representing the Dream, publicly aloof but emotionally volatile when provoked.
• Leader associated with the epitome family, known to favor fighting near the front and wear a distinctive helmet.
• A rival leader implied to be likely to attempt to usurp throne control and displace [[Lockjaw]].
• A warlord figure who fled into the forest and whose absence implies their warlord may be inside the ball.
• One of the two Reaver warlords identified inside the ball alongside Warlord [[Fang]] of the Reavers.
• A warlord who participates in the warlord conversation and reacts to developments like the war mage rebellion and rule changes.
• A warlord whom the team expects to drop when a countdown finishes.
• A biological material that appears mixed into [[Louis]] Santiago's replacement organs.
• A famous CEO and warlord of the Dream who is revealed or suspected to be a cyborg-like presence inhabiting [[Louis]]'s biomechanical organs.
• [[Tagg]]'s formal/epitome persona occupying [[Louis]]'s body as a symbiotic presence.
• A hostile entity killed during the faction engagement, with his death announced by a system message crediting [[Mongo]].
• A figure involved in faction motivations, accused of wanting to find a specific crawler ([[Louis]]) and indifferent to hunter casualties or gear.
• A [[human]] (or comparable) player figure at the meeting who dismisses Juice Box and the notion of additional teams.
• A rich participant from the Dream mentioned among the powerful players on Floor 8 whose survival is uncertain.
• A notable figure "from the Dream" mentioned among the wealthy/high-profile participants affected by Floor 8's danger.
