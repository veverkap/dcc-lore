# harbinger

• A massive, holo-displayed caprid liaison from the [[Plenty]] with rows of red eyes and a deep, demonic voice, linked to past assassination attempts involving [[Maggie]] My and the Skull Clan.
• Claims authority over crawl integrity, recalls Dungeon Crawler [[Carl]]'s attorney, bans [[Rosetta]] from future interviews, and intends to have [[Carl]] and his caprid "disposed."
• A remote observer insisted upon by [[Zev]] to watch the team virtually during onboarding.
• A massive goat liaison and protective presence in Dungeon Crawler [[Carl]]'s entourage, monitoring the interview and capable of stopping broadcasts if privileged information is revealed.
• A figure who unexpectedly blinks and disappears during the broadcast, causing surprise among attendees.
• A powerful actor referenced as being "in on this" and having ordered [[Carl]] killed, with their presence or absence affecting [[Orren]] the Liaison's statements.
• A named enforcer or agent referenced by Growler [[Gary]] as having been sent to eject or punish the protagonists.
• A giant goat liaison and protector figure aligned with the [[Plenty]] who appears in the studio armed with a large pulse rifle and confronts [[Gamori]] before being explosively destroyed.
• [[An]] official liaison who enforces crawl integrity during interviews, interrupts and terminates segments, and declares intentions to remove individuals from the crawl.
• A high-level observer who watches events virtually and exerts influence over proceedings.
• A massive goat liaison who enforces broadcast boundaries, monitoring the interview for privileged information and capable of halting the broadcast if rules are violated.
• A character who is referenced as having gone missing from the room right before [[an]] emergency call.
• A colleague mentioned by [[Orren]] the Liaison as [[an]] alternative liaison who might have reacted differently to the [[Residual]] incident.
• A named figure mentioned as a potential candidate to be dragged into the Eleventh Floor Arena.
• A tall, satanic demon-goat figure referenced for comparison to the current magistrate breed.
• [[An]] external [[AI]] referenced as having been stopped by the system.
