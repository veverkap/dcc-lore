# Boomer

# Boomer
Grizzled elf contact and pilot who coordinates docking and assault logistics with [[York]]. Keeps a rough, veteran demeanor, issues orders about power armor (including [[Cronus suits]]) and drop craft, and jokingly trades insults about poetry with [[York]].

## Additional Observations
# Boomer
Former crawler turned commander leading the [[106th Bloody Leeches]] during the southern-front engagement; tasked with coordinating with [[Tran]] and [[dungeon crawler carl]] in the planning room.

## Additional Observations
# [[Boomer]]
Also referenced as Colonel Boomer; commander who criticizes not taking the Naga castle and who reports incoming flyers to the command net during the attack.
