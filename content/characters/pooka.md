# pooka

• A neighborhood boss monster described as fuzzy and goblin-like, distributing race-specific potion vials through a slot at five-stop tunnel portals, and transforming into a giant goat when attacked.
• Operates behind station doors exchanging race-specific vials (Rev-up Vitamin Shots) to monsters in a recurring five-stop cycle on the lines.
• Distributes addictive "vitamin shots" manufactured by the [[Krakaren]], portrayed as a caricature of the "[[Plenty]]" in the floor's political cartoon motif.
• A caprid race (goat-like) used on the floor as drug distributors, handing out addictive substances manufactured by the [[Krakaren]].
• Interacts with the Rev-up Vitamin Shots system.
