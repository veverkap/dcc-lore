# Pooka

# [[Pooka]]
A neighborhood boss monster described as fuzzy and goblin-like. Sells/distributes race-specific potion vials through a slot at five-stop tunnel portals; can turn into a giant goat when attacked.

## Additional Observations
# Pooka
Caprid/goblin-like distributors depicted on the floor handing out addictive drugs manufactured by the [[Krakaren]]; visually associated with goats and the [[Plenty]] metaphor.
