# Little Günter

# Little Günter
Named summoned hominid creature produced by the [[Spellbook of Run, Little Günter, Run]]; a small, fat child-like creature with one health that stores a triggered attack and runs toward a designated target once activated.

## Additional Observations
# Little Günter
A summoned child-like entity produced by the spell [[Run, Little Günter, Run]] who distracts and is used strategically against [[Prison Pocket]].
