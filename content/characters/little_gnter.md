# little_gnter

• Named summoned hominid creature produced by the [[Spellbook of Run, Little Günter, Run]]; a small, fat child-ike creature with one health that stores a triggered attack and runs toward a designated target once activated.
• A summoned child-ike entity produced by the spell [[Run, Little Günter, Run]] who distracts and is used strategically against [[Prison Pocket]].
• A summoned kid (from the [[Run, Little Günter, Run]] spell) who is used as part of [[Carl]]'s tactic against [[Prison Pocket]]; receives a delayed-impact strike from [[Pontiff]]'s mallet that flips the boss.
