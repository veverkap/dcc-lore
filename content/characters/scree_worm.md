# scree_worm

* A parasitic worm that infested [[Chris]] (also tied to names [[Frank]] Q/[[Maggie]] My saga).
* Routed from [[dungeon crawler carl]]'s body after a healing protocol and then eaten/crushed by [[Mongo]].
* One of the alien races mentioned in lore notes, used to illustrate how non-[[human]] intelligences can inhabit bodies or divine shells in the dungeon.
* Distant cousins of the parasites, also known by the name [[Infiltrator]].
* A race of parasites known by the name [[Infiltrator]], part of the taxonomy explaining how parasites annex living hosts.
* [[the Scree]]: [[an]] alien/parasite faction/species that can inhabit bodies, similar to how aliens may drive gods.
