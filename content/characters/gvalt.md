# G'valt

# [[G'valt]]
A dour, long-faced Bactrian camel alien and fellow CrawlCon judge. Former playwright whose label includes the drama "Session of Love." Appears intoxicated or incapacitated during the contest.

## Additional Observations
# G'valt
A dour, Bactrian camel alien seated beside [[dungeon crawler carl]] during the art-judging panel. His label includes "Session of Love" referencing a stage drama he authored that is performed on the [[Ninth Floor]]. Often snorts, sleeps during panels, and is treated with mild derision by [[Hurk]].
