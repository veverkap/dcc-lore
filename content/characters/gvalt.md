# gvalt

• A dour, long-aced Bactrian camel alien and fellow CrawlCon judge.
• Former playwright whose label includes the drama "Session of Love".
• Often appears intoxicated or incapacitated during the contest.
• Seated beside [[dungeon crawler carl]] during the art-judging panel.
• His label includes "Session of Love" referencing a stage drama he authored that is performed on the [[Ninth Floor]].
• Often snorts, sleeps during panels, and is treated with mild derision by [[Hurk]].
