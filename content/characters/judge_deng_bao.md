# judge_deng_bao

* Judge Deng Bao, [[an]] orcish adjutant and seasoned judge with [[experience]] on high courts, known for impartiality and legal expertise.
* Deng Bao, a [[grixist]] or orc adjutant assigned to the war mages, is present in [[Larracos]] and involved in unraveling their plans.
* Judge Deng: [[an]] adjutant who accompanies [[King Rust]], produces a tablet with video for the adjutants to watch during the encounter, and participates in the guarded negotiation.
