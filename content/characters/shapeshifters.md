# shapeshifters

• Shapeshifters are a creature class or mob type referenced in [[Book IV, The Gate of the Feral Gods]].
• They are highlighted as a notable inclusion alongside [[Camels]], [[Gnomes]], and [[Geese]].
• Shapeshifters are included in the teaser list for [[Book IV, The Gate of the Feral Gods]] and denote a class of in-world entities with transformation abilities.
