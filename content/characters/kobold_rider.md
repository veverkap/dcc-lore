# Kobold Rider

# [[Kobold Rider]]
A level-5 mob entry in the chapter that describes the mounted kobold enemies encountered by [[dungeon crawler carl]] and [[Donut]]. The entry explains kobold behavior and their mounted units, and it prefaces the ensuing combat with [[Danger Dingoes]].

## Additional Observations
# Kobold Rider
A higher-tier kobold variant seen in the center of the cheering group. The central [[Kobold Rider]] is better dressed (fur coat, spiked boots), holds the small metal cage that releases [[Ralph]], and dies in the explosion from the [[Boom Jug]].
