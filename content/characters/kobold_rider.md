# kobold_rider

• A level-5 mob entry in the chapter that describes mounted kobold enemies encountered by [[dungeon crawler carl]] and [[Donut]].
• The entry explains kobold behavior and their mounted units, and it prefaces the ensuing combat with [[Danger Dingoes]].
• A higher-tier kobold variant seen in the center of the cheering group, better dressed (fur coat, spiked boots), and holding the small metal cage that releases [[Ralph]].
