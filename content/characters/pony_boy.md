# pony_boy

* A crawler referenced as previously contesting the [[Memorial Crystal: Apito]] alongside [[Osvaldo]] during the events around the [[Butcher's Masquerade]].
* A named individual the group attempts to contact; mentioned when [[Donut]] asks whether [[Carl]] ever heard back from them.
* Companion mentioned as having killed [[an]] elite and currently paralyzed; carries [[Bianca]] in a pet carrier in his inventory.
* Responsible for a skipped floor ("That was 100% Pony"); mentioned in party-chat context as [[an]] actor whose choices affected the run.
* Secondary character mentioned as someone [[dungeon crawler carl]] intends to talk with later regarding the group's movements.
* Ally credited with originating the idea that linked to the appearance of the quest; referred to as having insight into how the unstable system might react.
* Combatant credited with having already killed several of the [[naga]] executives or guards prior to the described operation.
* Party member participating in the assault plan to infiltrate [[Club Vanquisher]] and the [[Temple of Khepri]].
* Aggressive crawler allied with [[Carl]], obsessed with the destruction of the [[nagas]]; carries and consumes various potions/sodas and stores allies/pets as needed.
* Crawler companion who casts debuffs and assists in killing through subtle status stacking; has multiple unaltered boer siblings in inventory, is protective, and is sensitive to insults from [[dungeon crawler carl]].
* Mentioned crawler/contact whom [[Carl]] attempted to reach; no reply in this chapter.
* Ally referenced by [[Miriam Dom]] who killed [[an]] elite but became paralyzed in the process; currently immobilized and carrying [[Bianca]] in [[an]] inventory pet carrier.
* Mentioned as someone [[Carl]] will talk to later; referenced in party logistics and communications.
* Ally of [[dungeon crawler carl]] who helped devise the theory that triggered the regional quest activation; referenced as having predicted the quest arrival and as part of [[Carl]]'s planning team.
* Member of [[Carl]]'s group who sings during the judging procession ("In the Air Tonight"), providing [[an]] emotional and disruptive interlude during the parade.
