# odious_creeper

Here is the consolidated markdown list:

• A new, extremely dangerous plant-augmented monster introduced by [[The Creeping Apocalypse]] quest, slow in daylight but able to speed up, replicate, and combine with [[Shambling Berserker]] and other creepers at night.
• Fifty are scattered across the [[Sixth Floor]] and marked on the map for all players.
• Named enemy/mob whose boss box dropped satchel bombs; part of a prior encounter referenced for loot context.
• Responsible for vine-based attacks that crushed several [[crawlers]] and can assimilate body parts to grow larger.
• A recurring undead boss- type mob targeted by the floor quest, with multiple instances tracked by system messages.
• Two remaining Odious Creepers served as the origin points for the [[Gehenna Bramble]] spell and the cascading punishment after the quest failed.
• New, extremely dangerous plant-infused monsters created in the aftermath of [[Miriam Dom]]'s death, slow by day but becoming fast, combinable, and able to replicate at night.
