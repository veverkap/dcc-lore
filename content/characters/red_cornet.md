# Red Cornet

# [[Red Cornet]]
Level 21 mob encountered on Banshee Station 116. A devolved form of the Lepus species: eyeless, skinless, long-eared rabbit-like creatures with an aural echolocation-based nausea attack that causes the [[Queasy]] debuff. Drops coins and common loots (e.g., [[Cornet meat]], used vials).
