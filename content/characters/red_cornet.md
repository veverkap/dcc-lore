# red_cornet

• [[Red Cornet]]: Level 21 mob encountered on Banshee Station 116, a devolved form of the [[Lepus]] species with aural echolocation-based nausea attack causing the [[Queasy]] debuff, drops coins and common loots (e.g., [[Cornet meat]], used vials).
