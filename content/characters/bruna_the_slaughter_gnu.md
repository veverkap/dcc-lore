# bruna_the_slaughter_gnu

• [[Bruna]] the Slaughter Gnu: a named, heavily armored six-legged mount used by [[Team Flamengo]] during the tenth floor, protected from most mount-targeting spells, deceptively quick, and able to scale vertical surfaces.
