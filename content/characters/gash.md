# gash

• Named as a sister of [[Slit]] from the demon harem; mentioned as the one to really look out for after [[Slit]]'s death.
• Named as a meaner demon-sibling (contextual relation to [[Minge]] / [[Slit]]); referenced in conversation by [[Samantha]].
• Inventor of [[an]] automaton kit; [[dungeon crawler carl]] uses components from Dr. Ratchet's kit (door-opening bot, quick-release arm) to modify and send machines into the club.
• The defensive force occupying [[Reaver castle]] and responsible for the minefield and summon traps across the [[Madness]]; central opposing faction in the chapter.
