# duende

• A type of mob encountered around the cemetery perimeter, mentioned as nuisances the party deals with while circling the park.
• Common level-20 mob type in the city, small, gnome-like, rag-dressed creatures responsible for local chaos and car wrecks.
• A different mob type encountered earlier while circling the park, referenced as a nuisance on the exterior and distinct from the [[ghommids]] inside the corrupted town.
