# Ursine

# [[Ursine]]
A dominant sentient bear-like race in the region; this settlement is primarily [[Ursine]]-populated. After cataclysmic events, surviving Ursine skewed clerical, producing strict moral codes and towns with many prohibitions.

## Additional Observations
# Ursine
A local population/species in town (likely bear-like) involved in town defense and complaints about hunter threats; being organized into defenses and walls described by [[Mordecai]].
