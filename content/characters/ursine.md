# ursine

• A dominant sentient bear-like race, the [[Ursine]], primarily populates this settlement.
• After cataclysmic events, surviving Ursines skewed clerical, leading to strict moral codes and towns with many prohibitions.
• The Ursine population is involved in town defense and complains about hunter threats, with organized defenses and walls described by [[Mordecai]].
