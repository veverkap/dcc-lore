# Turkey
The ubiquitous farm birds occupying the [[Turkey Barn]]. One turkey becomes a card-wielding boss (Level 5) that initiates combat alongside the [[Spiked Ogre]]; the chapter highlights both the mass flock and a single boss turkey in the rafters.
