# Gary

# Gary
A high-level player/NPC (referred to as [[Growler Gary]] as an alias) who seeks relationship advice during a pause in [[The Crawl]]. He was revealed to have been on a [[Key hold]] (regenerating on death), had the "Shitfaced" debuff removed, and was observed at level 175 before teleporting away.
