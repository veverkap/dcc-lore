# gonk

• A named swamp yak serving as [[King Rust]]'s mount, described as a "Swamp Yak" and used in parade formations.
• A close ally and companion, often associated with [[Dong Quixote]] and [[Bigs the sluggalette]].
• A large, disgusting beast ridden by [[Dong Quixote]] and enhanced by [[Twinkle Toes]] for high speed.
• A mount ridden by [[Bigs]] and used in combat.
• A pet housed in the [[Pet Stables]], mentioned among the narrator's companions.
• A named member of the marching group in the procession, carrying [[Grigori]].
• A swamp yak used as a size reference for [[Bianca]] and mentioned as a known beast in the party's lore.
• A creature who stayed with the transport and reacted strongly to the chaos.
• A mount ridden by [[Dong Quixote]] at the base of the tower and part of the crowd scene during the operation.
• A yak- like mount used in the assault, carrying riders and responding to commands.
• A blood-soaked yak mount/creature used by [[an]] older stripper in the battle.
• A pet kept in the group's pet stable, being played with by [[Rend]] and [[Mongo]] during the scene.
• A named beast used for comparison when [[Juice Box]] transforms into a swamp yak similar to [[Gonk]].
• A yak/ pack animal present with [[Dong]], snorting at and interacting with [[Bianca]] and the [[hellspawn]] familiar.
• A mount that was reportedly hurt during the floor's nine-tier attack.
• A pet referred to as "Gonk the yak," [[an]] animal resident of the [[guild's pet stables]] who is friends with [[Simoom]].
