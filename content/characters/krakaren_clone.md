# krakaren_clone

• A Level 10 Neighborhood Boss variant of the [[Krakaren]] species, described as a clone.
• Encountered bursting through walls in the chapter.
• Lashes with tentacles.
• Hosts mouths that drip infectious goo.
• One of many clones, with [[Krakaren]] Prime capable of birthing two more for every one killed.
