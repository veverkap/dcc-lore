# Krakaren Clone

# [[Krakaren Clone]]
A Level 10 Neighborhood Boss variant of the communal, multi-tentacled Krakaren species encountered bursting through walls in the chapter. Described as one of many clones (“For every one that is killed, Krakaren Prime births two more”), it lashes with tentacles and hosts mouths that drip infectious goo.
