# fleshmancer_mercenary

* [[Fleshmancer]] (mercenary): [[an]] unnamed tall, thin [[human]] in flowing robes hired by [[Imani]] to be kept hidden in their guild until needed.
* [[Fleshmancer]]: a specialist/role that can combine split bodies, such as [[Corcunda]], and remove obligations.
* A practitioner capable of regluing split bodies, listed as a class/profession.
* A named profession/class required to recombine [[Corcunda]]'s halves, altering team obligations.
* A class or practitioner type identified by [[Rosetta]] and potentially found hidden in a bar.
