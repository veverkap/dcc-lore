# [[Sister Ines Quiteria]]
Crawler #8,199,462; a Cuban-accented cat-girl who identifies as a Discalced Carmelite nun and holds the Poet Laureate class. Carries a glowing trident and explains race/class choices influenced by charisma benefits for the [[Word Weaver]] spell.

## Additional Observations

Named crawler who appears in the [[Quest Update: The Chowder War]] as negotiating with the [[Monk Seal Abbot]] against a crab attack.

A crawler nun encountered in the field, seen riding a giant snake and apparently charming seals and crabs with poetry. Her presence triggers the failed [[Chowder War]] outcome and places her at risk from the incoming [[Demon Eviction]].
