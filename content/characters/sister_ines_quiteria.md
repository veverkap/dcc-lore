# sister_ines_quiteria

• Crawler #8,199,462, a Cuban-accents cat-girl who identifies as a Discalced Carmelite nun and holds the Poet Laureate class, carrying a glowing trident.
• Named crawler who appears in the Quest Update: The Chowder War, negotiating with the Monk Seal Abbot against a crab attack.
• Encountered in the field, riding a giant snake, charming [[seals]] and [[crabs]] with poetry, and triggering the failed Chowder War outcome and Demon Eviction.
• Cast a powerful poetry-themed charm spell using the Book of Rhymes and Word Weaver skill to control the level-125 boss Madre de Aguas and nearby mobs.
• Level 47 cat-girl who identifies as a Discalced Carmelite (nun) and Poet Laureate class, pacifying presence in her trio, claims not to worship a god.
• Presence on the beach (riding a giant snake) triggers quest updates and danger, warning [[seals]] about [[crabs]] earlier and central to rescuing [[Imani]] and preventing demonic possession.
• Used a level-15 Book of Rhymes bard skill to power a Word Weaver charm, controlling the giant boss Madre de Aguas and surrounding mobs before her spell was broken.
