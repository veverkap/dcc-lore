# the_eulogist

• The [[Eulogist]] is [[an]] ancient, slumbering deity-like entity referenced by agents such as [[Agatha]] as the object they hope to return to sleep.
• The [[Eulogist]] is the central [[AI]] that governs extraction and is implicated in the situation with [[Lucia Mar]] and the trapped children at the [[kinder facility]].
• The [[Eulogist]] is a referenced greater system entity distinct from the speaking [[AI]] and is part of the mythology behind the system's origins.
• The [[Eulogist]] is a deity-like entity whom agents invoke ("May the [[Eulogist]] ever sleep"); freeing or returning the [[Eulogist]] to slumber is [[an]] explicit goal motivating [[Agatha]] and her people.
