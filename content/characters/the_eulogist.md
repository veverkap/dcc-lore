# The Eulogist

# The Eulogist
An ancient, slumbering deity-like entity referenced by agents such as [[Agatha]] as the object they hope to return to sleep. Opposed by the so-called traitor and central to the ideological stakes driving recruitment and sabotage.

## Additional Observations
# [[The Eulogist]]
The name given to the central AI (the "Eulogist") that governs extraction and is implicated in the situation with [[Lucia Mar]] and the trapped children at the [[kinder facility]]; its connection to the center system makes [[Lucia Mar]] a potential dead-man’s switch.

## Additional Observations
# The Eulogist
A referenced greater system entity distinct from the speaking [[AI]]; mentioned as an identity the AI denies being, and as part of the mythology behind the system’s origins.

# The Eulogist
A deity-like entity whom agents invoke ("May the Eulogist ever sleep"); freeing or returning the [[Eulogist]] to slumber is an explicit goal motivating [[Agatha]] and her people.
