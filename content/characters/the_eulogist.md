# the_eulogist

* The [[Eulogist]] is [[an]] ancient, slumbering deity-like entity referenced by agents such as [[Agatha]] as the object they hope to return to sleep.
* The [[Eulogist]] is the central [[AI]] that governs extraction and is implicated in the situation with [[Lucia Mar]] and the trapped children at the [[kinder facility]].
* The [[Eulogist]] is a referenced greater system entity distinct from the speaking [[AI]] and is part of the mythology behind the system's origins.
* The [[Eulogist]] is a deity-like entity whom agents invoke ("May the [[Eulogist]] ever sleep"); freeing or returning the [[Eulogist]] to slumber is [[an]] explicit goal motivating [[Agatha]] and her people.
* A higher entity or deity-liked figure worshiped by the [[nebulars]].
* Described as the most important remnant of the [[Primals]] and the central system where many people live.
* The original source of the historical [[Nine-tier attack]].
* A deity-liked entity central to the agenda of [[Agatha]] and her kind.
* A major remnant of the [[Primals]]' civilization, serving as a cultural and systemic core.
* Tied to the origin and expansion of the dungeon systems.
* [[An]] entity or [[AI]] responsible for providing memory-echoes.
* The final-form Macro Aggregate Intelligence created during the ancient wars, now asleep and monitored by a smaller instance.
