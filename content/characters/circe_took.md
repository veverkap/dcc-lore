# circe_took

• A mantis minor hive queen and businesswoman who runs [[an]] amusement park, and is the mother of [[Xindy]] and [[Vrah]].
• Moderator of the CrawlCon panel "Crawling Through the Ages" and a powerful, angry figure implied to be mobilizing in response to recent events involving [[Vrah]] and other drama in the inner system.
• Named as the sponsor associated with the summoned god [[Diwata]] and is referenced in relation to player-family connections.
• A massive mantis-liked panel moderator and mantid matriarch, described as the mother of several warrior daughters, including [[Vrah]].
• Demonstrates lethal capability (slicing a table) and reacts violently to perceived slights against her offspring.
• A powerful figure referenced as being enraged by recent events surrounding [[dungeon crawler carl]] and planning to travel to the Earth system.
• Implicated in planned interventions against [[dungeon crawler carl]] and has been communicating with her daughter against the rules.
• The true identity behind the deity [[Diwata]] in [[human]] form, a monstrous mantis-woman when stripped of soul-armor.
