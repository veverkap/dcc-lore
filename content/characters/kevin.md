# kevin

• [[Kevin]] is a lizard newscaster-style commentator who appears in the unexpected broadcast overlay during the [[Special Event: Boss Battle Stream (Beta Opt-in)]] and provides live commentary about the battle and introduces the carrier image for [[Lusca]].
• He is [[an]] on-screen announcer/commentator visible in a floating window during the encounter, providing running commentary on tactics, depth charges, and events.
• Kevin is [[an]] orange- hued, four-eyed lizard announcer who appears in live broadcast windows to cover the battlefield alongside [[Magnificent Troy]].
• He is a squadmate/commentator in the combat encounter, calling out action and laughing about past events, and acts as part of the protagonist's immediate party banter during the battle.
• Kevin is a studio-based crew member remaining in orbit during [[Chaco]]'s field coverage, mentioned in passing as part of the broadcast setup.
• He co-hosts the live recap/special event broadcast, introducing the fight and mocking absent participants while the battle is streamed to subscribers.
• Kevin is [[an]] announcer/commentator who provides play-by-play of the action during the [[Lusca]] fight, frequently alongside [[Magnificent Troy]], and supplies in-world commentary that the party hears but cannot control.
• He broadcasts the Survival Royale Special Event alongside [[Magnificent Troy]], explaining the battlefield context to viewers.
• Kevin is a commentator/teammate who narrates combat events and mocks opponents during matches, sending combat commentary and reacting to plays.
