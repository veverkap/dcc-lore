# rory_the_shamanka

• A pierced shamanka-type crawler who previously gave information that affects [[dungeon crawler carl]]'s changing plan.
• Hands the protagonist a bag of [[Dynamite]] and other explosives and helps trigger the goblin sortie.
• Warned about the dungeon rule that causes bodies to dissolve when descending certain stairwells, explaining the fate of mobs forced down the [[stairwell]].
• A name spoken aloud by [[Gary]] ("Rory? Where am I?") when regaining awareness, possibly referring to [[dungeon crawler carl]] or indicating alternate/real-world identity or confusion upon teleporting.
• One of the builders of the bespoke [[Goblin Copper Chopper]] (along with [[Lorelai]]), contrasting with [[Carl]]'s improvised [[1965 harley davidson flh panhead electra glide]].
