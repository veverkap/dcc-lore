# bugbears

• [[bugbears]]: hostile creatures encountered before entering the portal to [[Hungry Eyes Vendor Village]].
• [[Bugbears]]: a race/species of large, furry, goblinoid engineers common in the [[Kapok district]] of the [[Hunting Grounds]], known for involuntary explosions, mechanical tinkering, and volatile temperaments.
• [[Bugbears]]: monstrous humanoid creatures previously encountered by [[Carl]] and mentioned as part of the group's recent activities before entering [[Hungry Eyes Vendor Village]].
• [[Bugbears]]: allied creatures/crew members referenced as part of the plan for the race, supporting the narrator's strategy.
• Bugbears: a group/class of racers (or [[mercenaries]]) who initially engage but then flee to their van during the boss fight, present on the hilltop and retreating when the situation turns dire.
