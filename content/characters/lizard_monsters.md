# lizard_monsters

• Reptilian mobs generated inside the abyss/[[Abyss]], behaving like spawnable mobs similar to [[ghouls]].
• Reptilian mobs impede crawler movements and cause collapses of search routes in the interior gangways of the [[Abyss]].
