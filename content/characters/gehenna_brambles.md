# gehenna_brambles

• Spreading plague-like bramble zone moving slowly from the north toward the region around [[Zockau]], with trees suffering and casting magic to summon aid.
• Notable invasive plant mob species from the sixth floor, related to the [[Avernus Creeper]] and used as a comparative hazard in lore.
• A spreading, corruptive plague/zone moving slowly from the north across the map, threatening regions like [[Zockau]] and prompting a potential intervention by [[Diwata]].
