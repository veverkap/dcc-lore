# adad

• [[Adad]] is a hulking storm deity, initially summoned alongside [[Dodola]], described as [[AI]]-controlled and larger than [[Dodola]].
• He is teleported away early in the confrontation after [[Dodola]] stabs something into his leg.
• [[Adad]] is identified as [[an]] [[AI]]-controlled deity rather than a sponsored one.
