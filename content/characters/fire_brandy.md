# Fire Brandy

# Fire Brandy
A non-combatant Lesser Demon NPC (labeled "Lesser Demon MILF. Level 75") who is stoking the boiler of the [[Nightmare Express]]. Teaches Carl how to operate the train controls, warns about pressure and the train's boiler hazard, and provides a small emergency box of fuel ([[Sheol Brick]]s) and the [[Steam Engineer’s Key]].
