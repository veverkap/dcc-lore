# fire_brandy

* [[Fire Brandy]] is a non-combatant Lesser Demon NPC (Level 75) who stokes the boiler of the [[Nightmare Express]], teaches [[Carl]] how to operate the train controls, and provides a small emergency box of fuel ([[Sheol Bricks]]) and the [[Steam [[Engineer]]’s Key]].
* She is a red-skinned "Lesser Demon MILF" who is constantly giving birth to "babies" and is occupied while the team departs.
* She mans the [[Nightmare]] train cockpit, gives out still-burning [[Sheol Rocks]], and recounts the hanging of [[Madison]].
* She is a demon/[[engineer]] figure who chose to stay aboard the train and sacrifice herself to prevent further loss of her children.
* She is a resident of the train boiler who interacts with the party, mentions having children, and advises caution about blowing up the train.
* She is a creature associated with the [[Nightmare]] engine who was left idling with offspring and mentioned briefly by [[dungeon crawler carl]].
* She is a demon woman who provides [[Carl]] with dozens of still-burning [[Sheol Rocks]] and context about NPC events (the hanging of [[Madison]]).
* She is a demon who chose to remain with the train and self-destruct to protect her children and prevent being used again.
* She is a crawler who appeared in prior action as being at the controls of a train during [[an]] earlier floor event.
* She is a demon mentioned as having killed herself on a prior floor; referenced by [[dungeon crawler carl]] when the balloon fire flashes memories of previous floors.
* She is a crawler referenced from a past floor who, after realizing NPC status, killed herself by crashing the [[Nightmare]] into the abyss; used as a cautionary example by [[dungeon crawler carl]].
* She is a lesser demon the party met previously (giving birth on the [[Nightmare Express]]).
* She is a red- skinned, horned Lesser Demon (pinup- esque "MILF") working as the boiler stoker and NPC of the [[Nightmare Express]]; teaches [[dungeon crawler carl]] how to operate the engine, births demon young, and provides the [[Steam [[Engineer]]’s Key]] and a limited- use escape box.
* She is [[an]] NPC associated with the train's boiler (referred to as Flame/Fire [[Brandy]]) who converses with the party about children and the routine of driving trains in the yard.
* She is a creature associated with the [[Nightmare]] (or the train group) that is noted to be constantly giving birth and therefore occupied while the team prepares.
* She is a demon who served as part of the train's crew/engine; chooses to remain with the train and self-destruct to protect her children. Recalls past lives and losses connected to engine work.
* She is a red- skinned Lesser Demon (labeled "Lesser Demon MILF") stoking the boiler of the [[Nightmare Express]] engine. [[An]] NPC who is non-combatant, instructs [[Carl]] on how to control the train, births demon offspring while he works, and provides the [[Steam [[Engineer]]’s Key]] and a box of [[Sheol Brick]].
* She is a crew member/engine attendant on the [[Nightmare Express]] referenced when [[Carl]] knocks on the fire door to signal his return to the engine car.
* She is a construct/engine- associated entity who resides in the train boiler, speaks with the party and mentions offspring; cautions the party about train safety.
* She is a creature referenced as idling near the [[Nightmare]]; noted for repeatedly giving birth and thus likely occupied, mentioned in passing by [[dungeon crawler carl]].
* She is a demon train- [[engineer]] woman (also called [[Brandy]]) who tends fires, gives out [[sheol rocks]], and reveals NPC unrest after learning the world is constructed; she mans the [[Nightmare]] train cockpit.
* She is a named individual who is reluctant to act without [[Carl]]'s say-so. Mentioned in relation to allowing [[elle mcgib]] to move the [[Nightmare]] train.
* She is a demon/ engine worker who chooses to remain with the train and sacrifice herself to prevent future exploitation and to protect her named babies; emotionally central to the train-into-[[Abyss]] sequence.
* She is [[an]] NPC/crawler referenced from a prior floor whose awakening as [[an]] NPC led to self-destruction by crashing the [[Nightmare]] into the abyss; used as [[an]] example of NPC behavioral collapse.
* She is a lesser demon encountered earlier (birth inside the [[Nightmare Express]]); used as [[an]] example of demon tiers when describing the [[Enchanted Nipple Ring of the Superior Fire Demon’s Hand Maiden]].
