# filipe

• Companion to [[Osvaldo]] standing outside [[Garage Three]] during the scene.
• Crawler in the observing group outside the garage, present during the rescue planning.
• Teammate of [[Osvaldo]] who rides [[Bruna]] and is part of the group considering the elevator shaft option.
• Teammate/competitor who finished the race alongside [[Osvaldo]] and influenced the [[Heat Six]] results.
• Brazilian crawler who agreed to take a deal and entered the [[Outreach Guild]]; jockeyed the sixth heat and arranged secrecy with [[Donut]].
