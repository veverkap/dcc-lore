# kimaris

• A named demon entity that formerly inhabited a collectible figure in the party's possession, described as a Unique summoned creature when its tag is pulled.
• A unique demon figure summoned from a beanie, noted as Unique rarity and distinct from the Legendary items [[daniel bautista]] possesses.
• A named summoned entity/god referenced when the totem inspects [[dungeon crawler carl]]'s inventory (associated with a [[Summoning Totem of Kimaris]]).
• A being described as "one of the brothers of [[Sheol]]" with a summoning duration noted as "the remainder of the floor", central to warnings about summoning limits.
• A unique summoned figure (a demon) originally from a stuffed figure, noted as unique and the same type as the creature [[Mordecai]] is currently shape-shifted into, more valuable than common [[Grulke]] variants.
• A named entity referenced as a summon linked to a [[Summoning Totem of Kimaris]], mentioned during [[Shi Maria]]'s inventory inspection as a potential summon tied to [[dungeon crawler carl]]'s items.
• A possible target of a summoning totem in [[Carl]]'s inventory ("you have a summoning totem of [[Kimaris]]?"), referenced by [[Shi Maria]].
