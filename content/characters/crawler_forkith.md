# crawler_forkith

* Contributor/annotator in 20th edition of [[The Dungeon Anarchist’'s Cookbook]], confirming and expanding on [[Crawler Sinjin]]'s notes about infused smoke and bombs.
* Earlier edition author of notes in the crawler book (20th Edition), [[an]] Urgyle Sapper, who warned about backpack stability and dedicated a personal note; cited by [[dungeon crawler carl]] for research on bomb satchel behavior and backpack utility.
* Confirmed and expanded on [[Crawler Sinjin]]'s notes about infused smoke and bomb effects on undead.
* Appended a note to the chapter ("Note added by [[Crawler [[Forkith]]. 20th Edition]]") and acted as [[an]] in-world annotator/commentator on the layout and behaviors of the [[Hunting Grounds]] floor.
* Confirmed and expanded on [[Crawler Sinjin]]'s note about infused explosives in the cookbook.
* Author of 20th edition notes in the crawler book, [[an]] Urgyle and Sapper who documented backpack behavior and personal loss (his sister [[Barkith]]), and whose notes influenced [[dungeon crawler carl]]'s backpack experiments.
* Confirmed and expanded on [[Crawler Sinjin]]'s notes about infused explosives, noting effects against invisible [[Swamp Wights]].
* In-world chronicler referenced in the chapter header ("Note added by Crawler [[Forkith]]. 20th Edition"), providing observational commentary on the layout and political landscape of the [[Hunting Grounds]] floor.
* Crawler author of the 20th edition notes in a widely read book; [[an]] Urgyle Sapper who reached at least the 11th floor and left warnings and anecdotes about explosives and backpacks.
* A small red demon who tends to [[Louis Santiago]] after arrival in [[Sheol]], applies a [[Leech]] to him, and claims friendship with [[Carl]] and [[Pontiff]].
* [[Forkith]] is another named former Crawler referenced alongside [[Herot]] in the narrator's overview of factions.
