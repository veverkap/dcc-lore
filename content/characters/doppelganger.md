# doppelganger

• A shapeshifting enemy type that forms itself like clay to imitate others (exemplified by [[Katia]]); compared and contrasted with [[Changelings]] and [[Mimic]] behavior.
• One of the three basic shapeshifter archetypes in the dungeon (alongside [[Changeling]] and [[Mimic]]).
• A shapeshifting creature type (example: [[Katia]]) that forms itself like clay to mimic others.
