# baroness_victory

* [[An]] orc judge and attorney, formerly of the [[skull clan]] and half-sister to [[Queen Ugloo]], presented as a stern and experienced candidate for adjutant.
* Adjutant and warlord advisor who manages the flag room and explains the season's rules and map of [[Larracos]].
* [[An]] armored orc adjutant and self-described non-combatant observer for the [[Princess Posse]]; intervenes to stop [[Herman the Fleet]] and enforces rules about lawful engagement.
* A higher-ranking ally/contact of [[dungeon crawler carl]] who instructs him to stay put while [[an]] adjutant/ quorum is convened; acts as a stabilizing, authoritative presence in the chapter's communications.
* Adjutant seated at the table during the adjudication; interjects on procedure, offers rulings, and defends the rules; has specific knowledge about communal [[Primal]] traits and the duality involving [[Shi Maria]].
* High-ranking figure who teleports into the battle, inspects defenses, and provides commentary on the nature of the [[Gurgles]] infection and battlefield rules.
* Sponsor/adjudicator figure who provided input on the smaller trebuchet loadouts and allowed certain trap modules (like [[Dazzlers]]) to be used; assists in strategy.
* Somber orc adjutant who appears inside the enemy headquarters and comments on the battle's moral and magical implications; present in the throne room during [[Carl]]'s intrusion.
* Present in the [[Guildhall]] observing the battlefield; recognizes [[King Rust]] and provides commentary about faction participation in the approaching army.
* Enforces rules and explains the prior adjudication regarding working with unaffiliated [[crawlers]] like [[Prepotente]]; advises [[Carl]] on limits and suggests recruiting [[Prepotente]] into the [[Princess Posse]] to circumvent restrictions.
* Large orc NPC who appears in the throne room, observing the group's interactions and referenced by [[dungeon crawler carl]] during conversation.
* Public-facing member of the party who conducts live updates/interviews; acts as a spokesperson during the chapter's broadcast segments.
* Commander who explains range limitations on the [[Paint the Target]] effect and provides tactical clarification about target painting mechanics during combat.
* Adjudicator/authority figure who interjects via message when warnings and potential foul play are called during the operation.
* Strategic ally advising stall tactics for the trial; instructs the group to prolong the engagement to influence the [[AI]].
* Named warlord who reports failed attempts to delay events in the system messages; appears in post-battle communications.
* Adjutant who monitored financial feeds during adjudication and coordinated a transfer used to buy ships; has past dealings with [[Porthus]] and is involved with the [[Open Intellect Pacifist Network]]'s plan.
* NPC (baroness) and adjutant figure who appears beside [[Carl]], provides political and tactical commentary, and helps interpret unusual rule interactions.
* Authority figure who reports not knowing [[Warlord Akuma]]'s whereabouts and states that the defeated War Mage team is no longer under her authority.
* Orc judge and former member of the [[skull clan]], presented as a candidate adjutant; half-sister of the late [[Queen Consort Ugloo]] and [[an]] attorney who claims impartiality despite family ties.
* Adjunct/judge and the [[Princess]] Posse's adjutant who posts orders in the Warlord Chat; suggests operational splits (flag room oversight and recruitment in [[Larracos]]) and is aunt to [[Prince Maestro]] and [[Crown Prince Stalwart]].
