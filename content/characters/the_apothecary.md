# the_apothecary

* A trans-dimensional entity used as [[an]] analog by [[Houston]] to explain the creature seen at the meeting, suggesting scientific/otherworldly parallels.
* The [[Apothecary]], a [[Primal]], was born near the end of the Decision and became part of the resistance.
* The [[Apothecary]] survived the great attack in a cocooned state, later emerging physically altered with defensive behaviors similar to the [[Security Guard]].
* A named participant who egged on [[an]] altercation during the panel, tied to the controversy around [[dungeon crawler carl]].
* A mysterious, collective-minded entity allied with [[Porthus]], advocating for shutting down the center system, and having significant influence and goals that intersect with the dungeon's meta-politics.
* A party member or ally aware of [[justice lights team]]'s plan and part of the group left to handle ensuing complications.
* A figure in the myth believed to have tempered or stopped the historical [[Nine-tier attack]], sacrificing loved ones to control the [[all tree]].
* A named individual present at the panel, allegedly egging on events or confrontation involving [[dungeon crawler carl]] and [[Circe Took]].
* A mythic figure credited with tempering the final attack of the [[Nine-Tier Attack]], associated with the [[princess]] who kills the [[All-Tree]] and later replants another.
