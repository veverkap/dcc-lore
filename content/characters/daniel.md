# daniel

* [[Daniel]] is a real-world contact ally who messages about meeting people in the Desperado Club and relays warnings about threats hunting the leaderboard bounty.
* He is a teammate who owns a stuffed animal called a [[Back Alley Mouser]] that [[Katia]] intends to repurpose as a prop for a disguise.
* Daniel is defensive and secretive about [[Katia]]'s condition, implying prior knowledge of her drug problem.
* He is a party member mentioned in team chat, and [[Katia]] offers to support him as she attempts to intervene in the goddess confrontation.
* Daniel has escaped the locked lobby while others remained trapped, and is part of the team referenced during the lockdown.
* He is someone [[Katia]] cares about and worries over, and fears he might "do something stupid" if a situation with [[Huanxin]] Jinx fails.
* Daniel is a contact who messages [[Carl]] from the Desperado Club with information about meeting people like [[Imani]] and warns about bounty [[hunters]] hunting leaderboards.
* He is one of the allies who agreed to support [[Katia]]'s group on the next floor.
* Daniel is the owner of a stuffed animal called a "back alley mouser" mentioned by [[Katia]] as a potential prop for their ruse.
* He is part of the raid team that covers the back door along with [[Gwen]] during the assault on the temple.
* Daniel is [[Katia]]'s partner who accompanies rescue/healing logistics briefly and exchanges emotional farewells with her before she goes into combat against [[Eva]].
* He is [[an]] acquaintance whose reaction to [[Katia]]'s condition hinted at familiarity with her drug problem.
* Daniel is [[an]] ally mentioned by [[Katia]] as someone to be supported while she attempts to stop the goddess.
* He is a member of the guild who would be the target of [[Linus]] the tourist's efforts to make tea, described as lacking [[an]] outreach manager and thus vulnerable to being copied.
* Daniel is a crawler who bows and speaks about honoring friends and service, expressing peace and the honor of serving others before departure.
* He is a named crawler referenced in the casualty/list count.
