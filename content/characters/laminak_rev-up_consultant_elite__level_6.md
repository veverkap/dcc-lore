# laminak_rev-up_consultant_elite__level_6

* Higher-tier Rev-Up mob: small, fat winged fairies (manager-class) that run filling-room operations.
* Immune to most health-seeping attacks and valued for their essence as a disease shield.
* Enforce quotas and repurpose underlings into production roles.
* The small, winged "soccer [[mom]]" fairies on the second floor, known as Laminak, can be charmed by [[Donut]]. They speak Syndicate Standard, can cast spells like [[Magic Missile]], and drop items such as gold and brochures when killed.
