# huanxin

* [[Huanxin]] is [[an]] NPC/ally who promised to help [[Katia]] if [[Carl]] secures the orchid and obtains [[Odette]]'s participation.
* [[Huanxin]] is a powerful operator/goddess figure who made deals with [[Odette]] and is blamed for brutal consequences.
* [[Huanxin]] is a wealthy CEO who may be planning moves in the crawl and is referenced as a rival to [[Odette]].
* [[Huanxin]] is [[an]] antagonist who used the [[spider]] quest chat to send a threatening message and manipulated the [[Spider]] quest outcome.
* [[Huanxin]] is a named antagonist referenced by [[Katia]] as a rival who would react violently to certain actions.
* [[Huanxin]] is a contact who tipped [[Carl]] off about [[Odette]]'s intention to ask for [[an]] Adjutant and is referenced in planning for the Faction Wars event on the Ninth Floor.
* [[Huanxin]] is [[an]] ally who has promised to help [[Katia]] if the team secures the orchid and whose aid is contingent on that timing.
* [[Huanxin]] is a player/agent driving the goddess [[Eileithyia]] and communicates plans for what will happen to [[Katia]] after she acquires the orchid.
* [[Huanxin]] is a powerful external sponsor/ally interested in who becomes [[Carl]]'s team Adjutant and has a contentious relationship with [[Odette]].
* [[Huanxin]] is a sender of a message via the [[spider]] quest chat that plays a role in the resolution of the Spiders quest and issues threats tied to revelations and enforces agreements.
