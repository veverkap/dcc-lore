# jasha

• [[Jasha]] is a Level 65 bugbear NPC and member of [[Free Love]], providing information and commentary to [[Donut]] and [[Dungeon Crawler Carl]].
• He is a friend of [[Dungeon Crawler Carl]] who was previously killed earlier in the crawl.
• He is a companion bugbear who sleeps in a kiddie pool and is noticeably intoxicated by beer.
• He is the driver of the [[bugbears]]' van, seen smoking and waving at [[Carl]]'s group.
• He is a member of [[Free Love]], providing radio updates to [[Dungeon Crawler Carl]] during the race and helping after the crash.
• He is a named bugbear NPC from [[Team Free Love]], helping fight the van fire and explaining the origin memories and motivations of many NPC racers.
• He is [[an]] awakened NPC bugbear teammate traveling with [[Carl]]'s group, providing information about other teams and helping with vehicle operations.
• He sings and plays violin as part of the bugbear ensemble.
• He is a bystander/crawler present with [[Radoslav]], [[Osvaldo]], and [[Filipe]] observing the situation outside the garage.
• He describes the [[Unicorn]]'s unusual route, cited by [[Osvaldo]] as the source of the cheating claim.
