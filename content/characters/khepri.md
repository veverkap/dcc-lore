# khepri

• [[Khepri]] is a god of light in the celestial ascendency pantheon, providing a version of Travel the Path and a recruitment quest that transforms prospects into beetles.
• The deity's temple, the Temple of Khepri, houses targets and residents the assault aims to box in, and is central to the mission objective inside Club Vanquisher.
• Khepri manifests in the temple as a bug-headed humanoid, triggering temple system notifications, granting temporary immortality, and marking trespassers for death.
• The god is also known as the [[Scavenger]]'s Daughter and is described as invulnerable on this floor while inside his own temple.
• Khepri's temple is described as "endless" with a never-ending banquet featuring hundreds of [[bugs]].
• The bug god's flaming, immortal insects swarmed the chamber, and he appears through the portal, driven off by another god's voice.
• Khepri is a god encountered and fought inside his own temple during the Club Vanquisher raid, banished by [[Hellik]] during the fight.
• The deity is also referenced in a message to [[Donut]], mentioned as allegedly forming a "death cult" in [[Donut]]'s name.
• Khepri is a bug god with a bug-headed appearance, called into the realm by dungeon crawler [[Carl]]'s provocation, and implicated in the dispute over who killed [[Emberus]]'s son.
• The deity's effect granted ninety seconds of invulnerability to [[Penny]] and is present, confused, in the arena during the battle with [[Emberus]].
• Khepri is a deity present during the confrontation, briefly interacts, and is ordered away by [[Taranis]] ("Go home").
• The deity is referenced as the "weird bug god" from a prior floor, sends a message to [[Donut]] about forming a "death cult" in her name, and is a deity-level entity associated with prior encounters.
• Khepri is a deity with a bug-headed appearance, summoned as part of [[Carl]]'s staged evidence, and named by [[Carl]] as one person responsible for the death of [[Geyrun]] during the confrontation with [[Emberus]].
• The deity's effect granted temporary invulnerability to [[Penny]] in the arena, and appears confused after the explosion.
• Khepri is a named god present in the scene who angrily gestures at [[Carl]] and [[Donut]] before being ordered to leave the realm, earlier providing invulnerability that has expired.
• The deity is associated with feast-table prizes and worship in Club Vanquisher, with several beetles described as types taken from Khepri's feast table in his temple.
