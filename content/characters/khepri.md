# Khepri

# [[Khepri]]
An obscure god of light in the [[celestial ascendency]] pantheon who provides a version of [[Travel the Path]] and a recruitment quest that transforms prospects into beetles that must be swallowed by a female adherent, who is then transferred to the [[Temple of Khepri]] (depicted as an endless buffet of insects).

## Additional Observations
# Khepri
Deity whose temple (the [[Temple of Khepri]]) houses targets and residents the assault aims to box in; central to the mission objective inside [[Club Vanquisher]].

## Additional Observations
# [[Khepri]]
Deity who manifests in the temple (described as a bug-headed humanoid). Presence triggers temple system notifications, grants a temporary immortality effect, and marks trespassers for death. Referred to as "The Scavenger’s Daughter" in the chapter.

## Additional Observations
# [[Khepri]]
God of Rebirth and the Sunrise, presented as a deity with a bug-like head. Displayed in-system as "Level 250." He is a locked deity (no sponsorship), described as invulnerable on this floor while inside his own temple, and is a retainer of Adrasteia. His temple is described as “endless” with a never-ending banquet featuring hundreds of bugs.

## Additional Observations
# Khepri
The bug god whose flaming, immortal insects swarmed the chamber; he appears through the portal and is driven off by another god's voice, with a system notification "Khepri has left the realm."

## Additional Observations
# Khepri
A god encountered and fought inside his own temple during the Club Vanquisher raid; banished by [[Hellik]] during the fight. Fighting him triggered permanent hostility.

## Additional Observations
# [[Khepri]]
A bug god referenced in a message to [[Donut]]; mentioned as allegedly forming a "death cult" in Donut's name. Identified as the weird bug god the narrator and Katia fought on a previous floor.

Deity with a bug-headed appearance called into the realm by [[dungeon crawler carl]]'s provocation. Described as a "fake sun god" and implicated in the dispute over who killed Emberus's son.
