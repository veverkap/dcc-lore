# colonel_boomer

• A grizzled, veteran elf and Elven Legate (Level 62) who commands the [[106th Bloody Leeches]] and is a colonel in the [[Princess Posse]].
• Has war gauntlets similar to the [[Grull gauntlet]] and a loud, abrasive personality.
• Coordinates assault plans with [[Tran]] and the narrator [[dungeon crawler carl]].
• Calls out tactical observations during battles (e.g., "Hitting the craters").
• Receives control of the assault and issues tactical orders and time limits for operations.
• Reports tactical developments to [[Carl]] during engagements, urging timely action.
• Officer under [[dungeon crawler carl]] who receives and executes orders in the throne room.
• Coordinates movements such as looting and advances toward the [[Blood Sultanate Castle]].
• Military organizer outside the [[the castle]] who coordinates defenses and warns the guild of approaching enemies.
• Field commander leading the Reaver assault, requesting air-dropped bombs and coordinating the timing of [[Louis]]'s run and the rest of the ground operation.
