# colonel_boomer

* A grizzled, veteran elf and Elven Legate (Level 62) who commands the [[106th Bloody Leeches]] and is a colonel in the [[Princess Posse]].
* Has war gauntlets similar to the [[Grull gauntlet]] and a loud, abrasive personality.
* Coordinates assault plans with [[Tran]] and the narrator [[dungeon crawler carl]].
* Calls out tactical observations during battles (e.g., "Hitting the craters").
* Receives control of the assault and issues tactical orders and time limits for operations.
* Reports tactical developments to [[Carl]] during engagements, urging timely action.
* Officer under [[dungeon crawler carl]] who receives and executes orders in the throne room.
* Coordinates movements such as looting and advances toward the [[Blood Sultanate Castle]].
* Military organizer outside the [[the castle]] who coordinates defenses and warns the guild of approaching enemies.
* Field commander leading the Reaver assault, requesting air-dropped bombs and coordinating the timing of [[Louis]]'s run and the rest of the ground operation.
* Grizzled elf contact and pilot who coordinates docking and assault logistics with [[York]].
* Former crawler turned commander leading the [[106th Bloody Leeches]] during the southern-front engagement.
* Commander who criticizes not taking the [[Naga]] castle and reports incoming flyers to the command net during the attack.
* Grizzled elf commander conducting battlefield debriefs at base, reporting on fronts, looting, unit statuses, and advocating for [[an]] assault on the [[Operatic castle]].
* Commander of the [[106th Bloody Leeches]] reserve force positioned to smash any [[Naga]] army that attempted to exploit a weakened southern border.
* Leader assigned to engage the [[Reavers]] and directs [[Samantha]] to participate in his offensive against that faction.
* A voice in [[Carl]]'s party giving tactical commands, directing movement toward the [[Madness castle]] and communicating during the raid.
* Member left behind to help repair the city after lava damage, part of the support forces not on the assault transport.
