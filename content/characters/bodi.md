# bodi

• Uses a boomerang-enhanced spear and provides decisive battlefield actions.
• Leader of the 103rd, carries out seeding of the field, and coordinates early retreat.
• Part of a prior plan to retreat and lure enemies into the field of corpses.
• Former season champion and member of the support group repairing defenses.
• Teamed with [[Imani]] and [[Elle]] McGib in the APV for demolition-derby style combat.
• Companion present and dazed after the arena events, sitting near [[Elle]] McGib and others.
• Quiet, deadly colonel who previously dominated Land War and currently plays Forza with [[Louis]].
• Driver of [[Imani]] and [[Elle]]'s APV, trapped in wreckage and pulled into the arena fight.
• Crawler companion sitting dazed next to [[Elle]] and [[Jurgen]] following the arena fight.
• Excellent driver referenced in radio chatter, allegedly entered Hungry Eyes and consumed many buffs before a run.
• Character asked by [[Carl]] to commit to a race for the next heat and potentially serve as a jockey for [[Zhang]].
• Driver riding [[an]] octopus and hoped to know how to ride it by [[Zhang]]; appears on another heat/track during the event.
• Member of the group handling the Betrayal float, assigned to drive while others rode atop their float.
