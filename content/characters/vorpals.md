# vorpals

• A condition-turned-mob described by [[Mordecai]]: deputized/celestial foot soldiers that act like suicide bombers when proximate to [[demons]].
• Named summoned creatures [[Astrid]] claims to have "unleashed" in response to the Nothing breach; referenced as a type of combat force meant to contain threats inside the [[desperado club]].
• A special condition/adelphic mob described by [[Mordecai]]: not a species but a deputized/conditioned celestial guard that seeks [[demons]], can draw demon aggro, and will explode if losing a fight.
• Monstrous creatures that tear through the lower level of the [[desperado club]] and bathe in demon blood; source of danger and gore in the chapter.
