# sluggalos

* Infectious slug-type creatures spawned by the slug disease, some large and weaponized, used/recruited as infantry
* Mob-type group of slug-like [[crawlers]], mostly levels twenties to thirties, producing [[an]] orange slime trail and semi-controllable by the party
* Malleable creatures used for trap-testing due to their tolerance for accidental triggering and skewering
* Species/group allied with [[Carl]]'s force, reacting violently to insults against [[Imani]]
* Mob/creature type used as disposable frontline assault force by Group Three, intended to swarm the [[naga]] castle and draw enemy attention
* Group of slug-like mobs who have captured a cart near the castle and are described as doing donuts in a field
* Crowd of creatures that gather around [[Dong]] Quixote; presented as [[NPCs]]/mobs in the field near the tower
* Group/creature used by [[Justice]] Lights Team to assist in retrieving the Antenna from the Midnight Market
* Species/race of [[NPCs]] present in the safe room and garage, interacting socially with the group and occasionally climbing on party members
* Aggressive pet/creature class used by [[Imani]] and others, attacking [[demons]] and playable in tactical situations
* Chaotic squad of hatchet-wielding slug mobs that charges into the demon horde, causing temporary disruption before taking heavy casualties
* Group of [[NPCs]] engaged in gaming with Doctor Bones, depicted as distracted and off- course during play
* Category of mobs used as test subjects by [[Mordecai]] to trial the experimental effect
* Collective term for many of the slug-like NPC participants, some moved over to [[Florin]]'s group for the parade
* Blood-soaked slug-themed attackers present in the truck and arena, shouting and operating a side server/deep fryer
