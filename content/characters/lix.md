# lix

• Named as the grandpup of the older gnoll guard who questions [[dungeon crawler carl]] during the security transfer.
• Won the art contest at [[CrawlCon]] with a perfect score for a simple but striking 3D drawing.
• Presented as innocent-looking and favored by one of the judges.
• Granddaughter of a head-of-security figure within the venue.
• Grandpup of the Shade Gnoll, referenced as part of the gnoll's attempt to solicit favor and leniency for [[dungeon crawler carl]]'s future actions.
