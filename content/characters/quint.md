# quint

• A level 75 half-Djinn pharmacist and non-combatant NPC working at the [[desperado club]], providing pharmaceuticals, starter gear, and potions like [[Hair of the Dog]], and warning about the tenth floor.
• Supplies items like the [[Starter Kit]] and [[Blitz Stick]] to characters, including [[dungeon crawler carl]].
• Minor NPC contact tied to consumable items, often off-screen.
