# Quint

# Quint
A level 75 half-Djinn pharmacist and non-combatant NPC working the [[desperado club]]. Floats while spinning like a cyclone, trades pharmaceuticals and starter gear (the [[Starter Kit]]), provides potions like [[Hair of the Dog]], and warns about the tenth floor.

## Additional Observations
# Quint
A possum-faced pharmacist NPC remembered by [[dungeon crawler carl]] as one of the many named inhabitants of the floor.

## Additional Observations
