# waldrip_chris

• [[Waldrip Chris]] is a white-tagged male [[gremlin]] NPC applicant for garage management, described as a Level 30 animal [[trainer]] and non-combatant.
• He is a named crawler who was detonated/explooded in the garage, with their remains splattering when the vehicle-selection wheel pops into place.
• He is a prior example of [[an]] exploding-on-death notification, used to contextualize the on-screen notification that appeared over [[an]] on-fire opponent before it exploded.
• He is a white-tagged [[gremlin]] NPC applicant and Level 30 animal [[trainer]], serving as a non-combatant garage manager option for biological/flesh-based vehicles.
• He handles biological care for mounts but is vulnerable and cannot enter safe rooms.
• His exploded remains are visible in the garage, used as [[an]] ominous example of what happens to losers on [[Don't come in last]].
• He is referenced as having exploded earlier, used as a comparison for how [[an]] on-fire rider in this heat behaves when killed/exploded.
