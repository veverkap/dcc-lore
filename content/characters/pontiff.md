# pontiff

• A [[Taurin]] mercenary and sound-wave-based mage who wields a comically large glowing mallet that generates a protective vibration.
• Recognized by [[Louis]] Santiago from [[an]] earlier visit to the mercenary market and positioned among the group holding down [[Chris]].
• A level 83 [[Taurin]] listed as a "[[Taurin]] Rhythm Rider" hired as a mercenary for One Fine Pig.
• Participates directly in the fight against Prison Pocket, swinging a massive mallet.
• Recognized by [[Louis]] Santiago from a previous visit to the mercenary market, standing near [[Britney]] when [[Louis]] Santiago arrives in [[Sheol]].
• A bull-headed [[Taurin]] mercenary who uses sound-wave magic and holds a comically large glowing mallet that vibrates to deflect hail and rain.
• A powerful ally in the run, swinging a massive glowing mallet that lands a delayed-impact, heavy blow used tactically during the boss fight.
• A crawler who was on a different team than [[Corky]] ([[Corcunda]]), per [[Bucket]] Boy's report.
• A bullheaded [[Taurin]] mercenary who speaks with [[Bucket]] Boy about making amends, present at the garage and acknowledged by [[Carl]] before departure.
• A driver at the starting line with large horns, coordinating with the team and issuing tactical prompts like "Shields up."
• A mercenary crawler handling vehicle modifications and maneuvers, commenting on upgrades and guiding the team's approach toward the ring.
• A member of the racing party who pilots/maneuvers the truck, times rocket boosts, and navigates walls and ceilings to clear gates and avoid hazards throughout the floors.
• The driver/pilot controlling the vehicle the team uses to move between rooms and floors, following directions from [[Carl]] and reacting to containment and gate announcements.
• A crawler and commentator who recognizes the [[Damien]] voice, pragmatic and interacting with [[Carl]]'s team in the garage scenes.
• A crawler agent operating inside venues to gather intelligence, reporting about the guards, the Wheel of Fortune, and the mysterious "Nothing" to [[Carl]].
• A mercenary who volunteers to test a portal-examination spell and relay results, philosophically motivated and willing to risk being removed from [[Carl]]'s employ to gather information.
• [[An]] NPC who may have been pulled into the Pineapple Cabaret or the Nothing holding area, conveying warnings to [[Herot]] about worshippers and gods.
• A crawler who previously used the Wheel of Fortune Nothing spot, his earlier disappearance and mysterious fate central to the group's concerns about the spot.
