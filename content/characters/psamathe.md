# Psamathe
A banished lesser deity (identified by [[council]]) who was apparently duplicitous in Ghazi’s summoning. Associated with a sand/ooze familiar that regenerates from individual grains of sand and is occupying the necropolis’s ghost, [[Queen Quetzalcoatlus]].
