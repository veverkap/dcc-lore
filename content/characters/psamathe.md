# psamathe

* A banished lesser deity who was duplicitous in [[Ghazi]]'s summoning and uses a sand/ooze familiar.
* Associated with a jealous familiar (the ooze) and implicated in prior deceptions that affected the Mad Dune Mage.
* Minor deity whose essence was split; one half inhabits the [[Lika]] Love Doll Head as a withering spirit, while the other half exists as a sand-ooze familiar.
* Banished to The Nothing by her father; implicated in tricking [[Ghazi]] into opening The Nothing.
* A false identity line [[Samantha]] was given to incite confusion: "I am Psamathe, long-lost daughter of the confederacy."
* A distant, faint voice heard from the dark chanting the name "Psamathe" as The Nothing opens; implied to be [[an]] external entity or calling connected to the tear.
* A named individual invoked during the fight, implied to have plugged a hole in the casino earlier; connected to [[Samantha]]'s circle or actions.
* A demon/ entity voiced from above during the encounter; called upon by [[Ysalte]] in the scene.
* Entity that tricked and possessed a sex-doll body during the sand castle encounter; tied to the events involving [[Ghazi]] and the Gate.
* A goddess mentioned by [[Samantha]] whose baby was referenced as dead in the recap and who figures into [[Samantha]]'s fragmented emotional memories.
* A banished lesser deity mentioned in the Letter from the Council who duped [[Ghazi]]'s summoning and uses [[an]] ooze/sand familiar.
* A patron/ greater being connected to sand ooze minions, cited by the [[AI]].
* A feral god referenced as escaped/free in the necropolis; associated with a familiar called the ooze and implicated in larger divine politics that the Winding Box can affect.
* A minor deity whose essence was split during [[an]] escape attempt from The Nothing; half of her spirit inhabited a naiad-like vessel and half was trapped in a [[Lika]]-themed sex doll, which became the [[Lika]] Love Doll Head.
* A false identity [[Samantha]] is instructed to speak ("I am Psamathe...") to attempt parlay with the leaders of the Confederacy.
* A distant, hollow voice/entity heard from the dark invoking the name "Psamathe" and signaling [[an]] approaching presence from the void when [[Signet]] is pulled into The Nothing.
* A demon (or named entity) who cries out from above during the encounter and whose appearance is acknowledged directly by [[Ysalte]].
* A divine or god-adjacent figure mentioned in the recap; her baby is noted as dead, affecting emotional beats in [[Samantha]]'s narration.
