# sekhmet

• A goddess whose court-style accessories (e.g., [[Assassin's Veil]], [[Wrap of Sekhmet's Compassion]]) carry factional implications; enemies of [[Sekhmet]] will react hostilely to wearers of her items.
• Items tied to Sekhmet, such as the [[Assassin's Veil]], [[Wrap of Sekhmet's Compassion]], and [[Nemes of Pestilence]], can provoke hostility from her enemies when worn.
