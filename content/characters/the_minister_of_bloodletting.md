# the_minister_of_bloodletting

* Named NPC on the Yokai side who intervenes in the bar fight to defend Yokai teammates like [[Yuto]].
* A level 98 skeleton performer who wears pope-ike garb and a necklace of live bats, leader of the [[Bleak Congregation]] and a memorable karaoke act at [[The Lollipop]].
* Wears pope-like attire and a live-bat necklace, member/leader of the faction [[Bleak Congregation]].
* Onstage skeleton performer who finished singing "White Wedding" before [[Tipid]] took the stage.
* Leader of the skull-faced group associated with the [[Wienermobile]], claims to practice singing to join the [[Unholy Choir]] and promised not to blood-sacrifice [[Carl]]'s team to [[Baal]].
* Creepy pope-like skeleton figure who travels with the Wienermobile and is seen carrying a [[bucket]] of a suspicious white liquid, bows to [[Donut]] as they pass.
* NPC or player-styled character present at the karaoke event, noted for dancing with skeletons and being part of the floor's grotesque pageantry.
* NPC present in the bar who comforts [[Dwight the unicorn]] by patting him on the shoulder and is part of the immediate scene.
* NPC present in the bar who aids [[Dekoki]] by pouring beer into her head-bowl, demonstrating local social interactions among the patrons.
* Named crawler who, with his Wienermobile, constitutes one of the opposing teams in a final-heat matchup against [[Elle]] and [[Imani]].
* Named NPC formerly associated with the [[Bleak Congregation]] who was accepted into the [[Unholy Choir]], mentioned in banter by [[Donut]] and [[Elle]].
