# infiltrator

• Described as [[an]] especially insidious possession-style creature, distinct from [[shapeshifters]] and discussed by [[Mordecai]] when explaining enemy types.
• A parasitic race that takes full control of living bodies over time; central to the chapter because a single [[Infiltrator]] has annexed [[Chris Andrews 2]]'s body and is manipulating events.
• Parasitic race (brain-worm cousin) that takes full control of living hosts over days; central antagonist type in the chapter responsible for [[Maggie My]] inhabiting [[Chris Andrews 2]].
