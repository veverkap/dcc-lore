# hunter_iota

• [[Hunter Iota]]: a fallen level 50 crafter, introduced as "Hunter [[Iota]] – Crafter. Level 50. Coin Tinker", with a severed leg and drunk when encountered.
• Teleported into the clearing, badly injured, and provides exposition about the mantis, [[kua-tin]], and the crawl's legal problems.
• Part of the [[crafters]], described as a pale, hairier humanoid similar to a [[Soother (Common Soother)]].
