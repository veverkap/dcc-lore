# kappa_onstage

* A [[kappa]] performer was onstage singing, pausing to glare when the party caused a disturbance in the Lollipop.
* A monster species encountered in the dungeon: turtle-shelled humanoids with water-filled head bowls that immobilize them if the water spills.
* The kappas include performers like [[Dekoki]] and can be killed via freezing methods.
