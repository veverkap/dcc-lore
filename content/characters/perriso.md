# perriso

• A species of large-mount mammal, likened to [[an]] oversized tapir, with the species of the named mount [[Sweety]].
• A creature species in the dungeon ecosystem, described as a large mammal similar to a tapir but large enough to serve as a mount.
• A large mount species used as a monstrous, fast mount in the race, described as a fat gray creature with a caged saddle and a glowing secondary shield, praised by [[Dong]] for unexpected speed.
