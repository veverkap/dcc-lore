A species of large-mount mammal in the game world, likened to an oversized tapir; the species of the named mount [[Sweety]].

A creature species in the dungeon ecosystem. Described as a large mammal similar to a tapir but large enough to serve as a mount (example: [[Sweety]]). Classified in the chapter as a large-mount-sized mammal.

Large mount species used as a monstrous, fast mount in the race; described as a fat gray creature with a caged saddle and a glowing secondary shield. Praised by [[Dong]] for unexpected speed.
