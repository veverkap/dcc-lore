# basquiat_the_mongolian_death_worm

• A massive totem/tameable monster summoned in battle, described as bus-sized with three [[face]] flaps.
• Devours multiple totems (rabbit, pig, turtle).
• Vomits gore.
• Kills [[Chuluuna]].
• A turning-point threat in the capitol engagement.
