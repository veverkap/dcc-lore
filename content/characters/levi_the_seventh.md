# levi_the_seventh

* [[Levi]] the Seventh is a combined creature, a Troll [[Flesher]] and Hobgoblin Skellie [[Symbiote]], Level 7.
* He is the central NPC in the system announcement quest "New Quest. [[Levi]] is On the Menu!".
* He is a terrified [[engineer]] on the front of the Dismemberment Limited.
* He begs [[crawlers]] not to let [[Fleshers]] devour him.
* He is at risk of being devoured into [[Fleshers]] if the party fails to complete the active quest "[[Levi]] is On the Menu!".
* [[Dismemberment Limited]]
* [[New Quest. [[Levi]] is On the Menu!]]
* A teammate or nearby crawler who reacts loudly and jubilantly when [[Dismember]] is killed, present during the train encounter and subsequent station departure.
* Travels with [[dungeon crawler carl]]'s party on the train leading up to Station 281.
* Travels with the party on the Mauve line.
* Companion present during the train fight and subsequent departure; reacts vocally to [[Dismember]]'s death.
