# annie

• [[Annie]]: a small child referenced in [[Katia]]'s dream/memory, connected to [[Katia]] and the traumatic incident with [[Shi Maria]], and a key concern in the chapter and the failed [[the bedlam bride quest]] quest.
• [[Annie]]: a crow attached to [[Katia]], appearing in [[Katia]]'s dream sequence and reacting angrily when [[Shi Maria]] appears, and named in the dream as part of [[Katia]]'s symbolism.
• [[Annie]]: a character left alone in the room with [[Shi Maria]] in the dream/replay, connected to the personal aftermath of the possession event.
• [[Annie]]: a totem companion of [[Katia Grim]], participating in the fight and momentarily disappearing in a puff of smoke.
• [[Annie]]: described as "the skull-headed bird" on [[Katia]]'s card, depicted itemized among the possessions left on the table.
• [[Annie]]: [[an]] ally reported down by [[Katia]] and the team during the battle.
• [[Annie]]: a level 80 totem described with grotesque [[infant]]-skull features and used offensively in the battlefield.
• [[Annie]]: [[Katia]]'s card/animal companion in bird form with the skull of a child that perches on her shoulder and provides comfort, directly tied to [[Katia]]'s memories and frequently present during her dream sequence.
• [[Annie]]: the little girl in [[Katia]]'s memory, a terminally ill patient in the children's hospital whose death became a defining trauma for [[Katia]], and the emotional center of the sequence.
