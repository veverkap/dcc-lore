# Swordfish Interlopers

# [[Swordfish Interlopers]]
Level-50 bipedal swordfish-headed bodyguard mobs used by the [[Reef Explorer]]. Described as penguin-like legs and flippers, capable of spitting water to disrupt thrown items and launching high, spear-like missile attacks. Could be buffed with [[Precision]] utility cards.
