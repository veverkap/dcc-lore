# swordfish_interlopers

• Level-50 bipedal swordfish-headed bodyguard mobs used by the [[Reef Explorer]].
• Have penguin-like legs and flippers, capable of spitting water to disrupt thrown items and launching high, spear-ike missile attacks.
• Can be buffed with [[Precision]] utility cards.
• Can be spawned as totems with countdowns.
