# astrid

• Astrid – Bloodlust Sprite, Level 125; Assistant Manager of the [[desperado club]]. Imbued with Cardiovascular Magic, capable of deadly circulatory attacks.
• Married to the [[Night Wyrm]] (aka [[Hamed]]) and targeted by the assassination plan involving [[dungeon crawler carl]], [[Katia]], and [[Samantha]].
• Powerful fairy NPC in charge of nightclub security with [[an]] Intelligence of 150, a lethal spell arsenal, psionic control abilities, and the skill [[Cosmic Sense]].
• Target of the Guild Job assigned by the Night Wyrm: "Kill Astrid, level 125 Bloodlust Sprite, Assistant Manager of the Desperado Club / owner of the Naughty Boys Employment Agency."
• Target of the murder plot arranged by [[Hamed]] earlier in the story.
• Former patron of [[Doctor Bones]].
• Late character whose death led to shifts in control of the Desperado Club and the rise of [[Hamed]]'s influence.
• Implicated in the family conflict – her death is a catalyst in the chapter and tied to payments and guild intrigue involving the [[Guild of Suffering]] and the Night Wyrm.
