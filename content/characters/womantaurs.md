# womantaurs

• Introduced as two womantaurs and a [[half-mantaur]] companion occupying garage number three.
• Fast, combat-capable allies who use arm chains to retrieve a dead [[joey]] and assist in the assault on [[Prison Pocket]].
• Named enemy group: two womantaurs and their [[half-mantaur]] gimp, opposing competitors in the race zone.
• Female centaur-like fighters who move swiftly to retrieve a dead [[joey]] and assist in the plan using arm chains.
• Group of beast-like humanoids who likely hold grudges against [[team Sparkles]] and pose a potential threat to allies such as [[Corky]] during the race.
