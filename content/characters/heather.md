# heather

• A transformed circus performer appearing as a monstrous, undead bear-clown hybrid, with roller skates, tutu, exposed skull, and worm-like front paws.
• Identified as the sacrifice whose blood [[Signet]] wishes to use for summoning; currently asleep under the [[Water Lily]] spell.
• Defeated by [[dungeon crawler carl]], dropping items like [[Hobgoblin Pus]] and other explosives.
