# alevtina

• Alevtina, a bugbear teammate of [[Radoslav]] and [[Jasha]], died in the crash inside the overturned red Pontiac minivan, motivating the survivors' resignation and discussion of the race's literal stakes.
• She was a member of the bugbear group, playing bongos and tambourine, and was honored by [[Radoslav]] in a toast after her death.
• Alevtina died in the crash, breaking her neck, and her death is central to [[Radoslav]] and [[Jasha]]'s mourning, explaining the emotional stakes for the team.
• She was mentioned as a fallen comrade by [[Radoslav]], implying prior death.
