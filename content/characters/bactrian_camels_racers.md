# bactrian_camels_racers

* Bactrian [[camels]] (racers) are a trio of racing mounts/competitors that block the portal to the [[Cul-de-sac]] on arrival, described with old-school racing helmets and aggressive banter.
* Bactrian [[camels]]
* Racer [[NPCs]] encountered at the portal to [[Hungry Eyes Vendor Village]], described as wearing old-school racing helmets and jostling for access to the sole portal back to the cul-de-[[sac]]
