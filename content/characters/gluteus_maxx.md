# gluteus_maxx

• [[Gluteus Maxx]]: Weird, hairy dancer in the [[Desperado Club]] who danced with [[Donut]]; characterized as a bit of a jerk but a good dancer.
• [[Gluteus Maxx]]: Short, hairy, muscle-bound mercenary who trains with twin war gauntlets in the [[Mercenary Arcade]].
• [[Gluteus Maxx]]: Named entity who helps by tossing small [[Sluggalo]]s toward [[demons]] as part of [[Imani]]'s tactic to manipulate demon responses.
• [[Gluteus Maxx]]: Fuzzy competitor playing Luigi in one of the Mario Kart races; noted as "pretty good" at the game.
• [[Gluteus Maxx]]: Loud, boisterous racer who drives for [[Carl]]'s team in the heat; noted for wearing only a Speedo and a hockey helmet, revving his horn, and using stimulant drugs before the race.
