# eileithyia

* [[Eileithyia]] is a goddess of Childbirth and Female Pain, Level 250, and invulnerable while in a temple.
* She is sponsored by [[Huanxin]] Jinx of the [[Grixist]] Swarm and CEO of Icon Industries.
* She grants party boons (protection from spiritual possession, healing speed, constitution) and issues the system quest "Spiders".
* She provides the three possible permanent boons tied to the Orchid of Eileithyia's [[Grace]]: triple Constitution, Celestial Attendant status to the Twelfth Floor, or forced pregnancy with export to the Kinder Facility.
* She offers options for [[Katia]] when she consumes the Orchid: constitution buff, impregnation (boots out of the dungeon), or transfer to the 12th floor as [[an]] intern/adjutant.
* She is tied to the [[Spider]] quest and requires a temple visit to receive a boon.
* She is a goddess who, under the influence of [[Huanxin]], has options for intervening with [[Katia]] (buffing constitution, impregnating, or transferring to the 12th floor).
* She is a deity/temple entity tied to the completed "Spiders" quest.
* She is a goddess sponsored by [[Huanxin]] Jinx and mentioned in relation to [[Katia]]'s potential promotion to a Floor 12 attendant.
* She is a named goddess mentioned in the conversation as [[an]] example of a deity potentially manifesting on the surface near [[Katia]].
* She is a deity mentioned in the pantheon lore around [[Diwata]]'s origins and relationships.
* She is a friend of [[Yemaya]] who will grant each squad member a single boon if [[Yemaya]]'s shrine is repaired and [[Yemaya]] ascends.
* She is a deity whose arrival in the realm is announced at the chapter's end alongside [[Yemaya]].
* She is a goddess whose patronage is required to unlock expanded descriptions of the boons from the Orchid of Eileithyia's [[Grace]].
* She is a goddess in the setting who, under the influence of [[Huanxin]], has options for intervening with [[Katia]] (buffing constitution, impregnating, or transferring to the 12th floor).
* She is a deity/temple entity tied to the completed "Spiders" quest.
* She is a goddess sponsored by the rich CEO/sponsor (likely [[Huanxin]] Jinx) mentioned in relation to [[Katia]]'s potential promotion to a Floor 12 attendant.
