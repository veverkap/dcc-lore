# hunters

• Generic group of external adversaries referenced by the upcoming [[Hunter Release]]; their impending release drives party preparations and secrecy.
• Players or NPC opponents who are being targeted or will pursue [[Carl]] and the group; the party estimates numbers and notes a mass release of [[Hunters]] in about thirteen hours.
• Adversarial player-hunters that descend on cities and roads when the dungeon opens; referenced both as individuals and as a collective threat that clears mobs around population centers.
• Hunter adversaries/groups that earlier engaged the party and are currently scattered; their absence or dispersion affects the group's tactical options.
• Group present in the room observed by [[Signet]] and the narrators.
• Faction-ike group of armed [[NPCs]] or agents responsible for mass NPC deaths encountered by [[Porthus]].
• External attacking force scheduled for release later, referenced as [[an]] adversarial group the party must prepare for.
• Player adversaries scheduled to be released in approximately thirteen hours; [[dungeon crawler carl]] and company discuss that many hunters will be coming for them.
• Group term for outworlder player-like combatants trapped on specific club levels, often at odds with [[Crawlers]] and restricted in where they can go by club/ten-floor rules.
• Hostile forces referenced in the chapter that "killed every single NPC in that city" (or at least every one they found).
