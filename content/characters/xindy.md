# xindy

• Identified as "Hunter Xindy – Mantis. Level 61. Blood Tracker." and affiliated with [[the hive]].
• Previously killed by [[Carl]], a severed claw from Xindy appears among [[Carl]]'s Hunter-Killer box loot.
• Child of [[Circe Took]] and sibling to [[Vrah]], died during a hunt involving the protagonist, making [[Circe Took]] hostile.
• A mantis sponsor/mob that [[Carl]] killed earlier, its appendage appears in Hunter-Killer reward boxes.
• The deceased child of [[Circe Took]] and sibling to [[Vrah]], her death is referenced as a reason for tension between mantis factions and [[dungeon crawler carl]].
• Mentioned as [[Vrah]]'s sister and a successor in mantis hierarchy, referenced in [[Vrah]]'s dialogue about breeding and rank.
