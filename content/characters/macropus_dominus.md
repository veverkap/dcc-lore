# macropus_dominus

• The species designation for giant, kangaroo-like province bosses, characterized by fur, dorsal spikes, expansive pouches, and a caustic pouch secretion.
• Adapted to low-gravity, oxygen-rich plains, with pouches that shelter joeys and can cause starvation deaths in prolonged storms.
• Secretion from pouches is caustic and harmful to other species.
