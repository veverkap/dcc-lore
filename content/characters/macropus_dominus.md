# Macropus Dominus

# Macropus Dominus
The species designation for the giant, kangaroo-like province bosses encountered (e.g., [[Prison Pocket]] and [[Party Foul]]). Noted for fur, dorsal spikes, expansive pouches that can contain joeys, and a caustic pouch secretion.

## Additional Observations
# Macropus Dominus
The species/formal designation tied to the bosses (used alongside the name [[Prison Pocket]]). Described as large, kangaroo-like marsupials adapted to low-gravity, oxygen-rich plains; they have pouches that shelter joeys, can cause starvation deaths in prolonged storms, and secrete caustic oil harmful to other species.
