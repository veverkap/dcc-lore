# clint

• A small, nervous man traveling with [[Signet]] and the party, warning of emergent threats from the river during heavy rain.
• A bald, one-legged were-castor NPC who accompanies [[dungeon crawler carl]]'s group back to [[Point Mongo]], using a mace and exhibiting high constitution and aggro-drawing behavior in battles.
• A were-beaver ally who fights alongside [[Carl]], present during the artillery bombardment and assists in extinguishing flames on the captured [[Akland]].
• A companion tracker who shifts into a fuzzy humanoid form to scout and run ahead, participating in the group's reconnaissance and operations.
• A bald member of the group who finds [[Carl]] and helps regroup after the ambush, providing exposition about the area and the protection of the [[all tree]] outcroppings.
• A deceased character referenced by [[Holger]] as having been ripped apart during the conflict, his death contributing to the camp's mourning and tension over the failed southern shore defense.
• A remembered individual whose younger/older forms appear in [[nadine]]'s animated tattoo, recognized by [[Holger]] and others.
• A small, kindergarten-aged companion with a complicated backstory tied to [[Scolopendra]], [[Signet]], and [[nadine]].
• A child under [[Holger]]'s protection mentioned as a reason he stayed behind, cited when [[Dong Quixote]] explains the teleport swap.
• A small man in the group with a nervous demeanor, riding atop a large caterpillar alongside other warriors.
• A character represented in [[an]] animated tattoo on [[Signet]]'s wrist, interacting with a butterfly and a fish in the animation.
