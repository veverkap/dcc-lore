# tummy_acher

• A [[meatball]]-like, two-legged mob with a mohawk that can be found among pet choices, named on its tag in the reward room.
• A round creature with a mohawk that is friendly, popular with viewers, and becomes [[an]] excellent melee tank when mature.
• A small, round, mohawk-bearing pet species recommended by [[Odette]] as [[an]] alternative to [[Mongo]].
• A pet-class mob species also known as Belly Achers or Colon Worms, with biology, regeneration mechanics, and special attacks.
• A creature that can bond with characters and change size and behavior after resurrection.
• A pet/creature that can be kept or boxed in inventory, with interactions with undead and player characters.
