# grull

Here is the consolidated markdown list:

* [[Grull]] is a war god figure referenced in item flavor text and [[Mordecai]]'s warnings; worshipped by equine-class creatures and invoked by certain mobs.
* Grull is a sanctum-tier war god mentioned in summoning lore; level ~250, invulnerable on most floors, and deadly when involuntarily summoned.
* Grull is a deity encountered and summoned in the dungeon, triggering achievements like [[Let There Be Chaos]] and [[Divine Epiphany]].
* Grull is a massive centaur-like, black-skinned minotaur with a double-headed axe, involuntarily summoned into [[trainyard]] and engaging the floor boss [[Mimic]] and [[crawlers]].
* Grull is a war god worshipped by some Tangle employees and followers, invoked by [[Gore-Gore the Mantaurs]] during combat.
* Grull is a deity option shown on [[Dungeon Crawler Carl]]'s god tab alongside [[Emberus]] when choosing worship.
* Grull is a sponsored deity who might be summoned and disrupt plans if provoked, [[an]] external divine wildcard in the chapter's strategic concerns.
* Grull is a mythic figure referenced in the origin tale of the masquerade, son of [[Taranis]] and [[Apito]].
* Grull is a body or avatar associated with [[Prince Maestro]] in past events, connected to the bolt-backstory and [[Princess [[Formidable]] of the Skull Empire]]'s plans.
* Grull is a mount associated with [[Maestro]] mentioned as [[an]] indicator for faction participation.
* Grull is a god who inexplicably added [[Carl]] to a friend list, a mystery to [[Carl]] given his objectives.
* Grull is a war god referenced by [[Odette]], using the gauntlet against worshipers can summon him, expected to become relevant on the [[Fourth Floor]].
