# valtay

* A race or polity credited with wiping out the H'lene and [[orcs]], referenced to explain historical map elements.
* A specific type of parasitic actor, identified as a type of [[Gondii]] that reactivates recently dead bodies and reads memories.
* A parasite that secretes a liquid to keep host bodies alive, contrasted with the [[Infiltrator]] and other worm-types.
* A race referenced in the chapter, their neural enhancement pills and upgrades are sought-after items.
* A [[Gondii]]-type creature that takes over dead bodies and can maintain them for long periods by reading old memories.
* A parasitic variant that keeps bodies alive via secretions, mentioned by [[Mordecai]] as related to other brain-worm types.
* A species/citizen race, [[Orren]] the Liaison's glass head contains a parasitic [[Gondii]] identified as a citizen of the Valtay.
* A named authority figure mentioned alongside [[Borant]] Corporation as potentially controlling the licensing and broadcast of elite-related events on the floor.
* A strange allowed entrant to the adjutant lineup, his inclusion surprises the narrator and others.
* A race/species referenced in connection with neural enhancements and prior upgrades distributed by sponsoring groups.
* A corporate facility referenced in the neural enhancer warning as a recommended place to discuss upgrade options and interface installations related to Valtay products.
* A controlling parasite/agent referenced as having the crawler [[Drick]] "under the control of a Valtay worm."
* A named species of brain parasite referenced as the type of worm inside certain controlled individuals.
* A small worm-like creature visible inside [[Orren]] the Liaison's swirling hood-liquid.
* A parasitic faction-like threat that seized control of a crawl, leading to political upheaval and promotions among [[crawlers]].
* [[An]] explicit set of dungeon/production rules requiring a liaison to be present whenever a crawler might be alone with a sponsor.
* A specific ship/vessel operated under Valtay that has been cleared and pressurized to Earth standard and is hosting the production environment and CrawlCon events.
* [[An]] alien/parasite faction/species referenced alongside Intellect [[Hunters]] and the [[Scree]] to illustrate off-world intelligences inhabiting hosts.
