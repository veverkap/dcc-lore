# lazarus

• Named achievement received by [[dungeon crawler carl]] for surviving [[an]] offensive spell above level 15, granting the reward [[Platinum Lucky Bastard Box]].
• Achievement received by [[Donut]] and others for surviving near-death scenarios, with associated rewards varying.
• A combined totem/construct used by [[Donut]], appearing with a bazooka and consumed by [[Basquiat, The Mongolian Death Worm]].
• A combatant who wields [[an]] RPG and reacts angrily to the presence of a massive demon.
• A combined card-creature resulting from other card interactions during the Floor 8 card fights.
• Armed ally who wields a large [[RPG]] and reacts emotionally to the massive demon.
