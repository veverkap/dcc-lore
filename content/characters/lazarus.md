# Lazarus
Named achievement received by [[dungeon crawler carl]] for being hit with—and surviving—an offensive spell above level 15. Grants the reward [[Platinum Lucky Bastard Box]] and is explicitly presented as an Achievement mechanic.

An achievement referenced as received by [[Donut]] and others for surviving near-death scenarios; associated rewards may vary (some get boxes, some merely the title).

A combined totem/construct used by [[Donut]] (was combined with [[Skylar Spinach]]). Appears with a bazooka and is consumed and partially destroyed by [[Basquiat, The Mongolian Death Worm]]; reverts into constituent cards when killed.
