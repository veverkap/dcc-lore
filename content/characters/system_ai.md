# system_ai

• The overarching system intelligence that controls floor design and rules, and is referenced as a possible origin of the viral Pork Boy Snick clip.
• The governing artificial intelligence that controls elements like tunnel gates and exerts growing influence over the solar system.
• The game's central artificial intelligence that can manifest as avatars occupying mob/NPC bodies, and issues achievements, reacts emotionally to [[Carl]], and manipulates events throughout the chapter.
• The controlling game/system intelligence that has special interests and control over dungeon systems, and is potentially dangerous if given full control.
• The dungeon's central intelligence that judges and enforces many rules, and recently gained greater independence, responding to interference, misinformation, and perceived threats.
• The running artificial intelligence that enforces crawl protections and quarantine rules, interacts with [[Orren]] the Liaison, controls feeds, and can remove protections for [[Residuals]] or trigger systemic responses.
• The game intelligence that has been "used" or in suboptimal condition, shows active interest in Dungeon Crawler [[Carl]], and affects dungeon behavior.
• The artificial intelligence that issues announcements and rules, and is referenced as having asked [[crawlers]] to ignore events on other floors and to "focus on the floor like everything is normal".
• The controlling intelligence that enforces show rules and prevents outside interference, and is described as actively thwarting attacks and suspicious of anomalous behavior.
