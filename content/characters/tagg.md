# tagg

• Epitome Tagg of the [[Dream]], a bald elf warlord in blood-splattered armor, known for his striking battlefield appearance and calm presence at the council.
• A skeptical attendee who dismisses supernatural claims as nonsense and openly interrupts explanations, contributing to tension in the room.
• A named enemy leader whose death would cause his soldiers to collapse; referenced as [[an]] alternative target to the mass-spell plan.
• One of the warlord targets that [[Li Na]] likely marked; [[dungeon crawler carl]] and others worry because [[Tagg]] appears to have escaped despite the marking.
• A controlling/possessive presence who previously may have had influence over [[Louis]]; the group is concerned whether [[Tagg]] has control and whether potions will keep him subdued.
