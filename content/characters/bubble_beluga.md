# bubble_beluga

• A dolphin-like corpse, "Bubble Beluga. Level 29," was encountered underwater, pierced by a spear of glass.
• [[Donut]] used [[Second Chance]] and [[Clockwork Triplicate]] on this corpse to create clockwork copies that attacked a shark.
