# Bubble Beluga
A dolphin-like corpse encountered underwater ("Bubble Beluga. Level 29. Killed by getting her brain pierced by a spear of glass"). [[Donut]] used [[Second Chance]] and [[Clockwork Triplicate]] on this corpse to create clockwork copies that attacked a shark.
