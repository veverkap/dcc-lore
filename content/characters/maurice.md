# maurice

• Chieftain of the northern cannibal tribe, named target of the mandatory quest [[Pasty Inferno]] after the death of [[Great Rusty]], and a threat to [[Upano]].
• Quest target to be found and killed, also pursued by [[Elle]] and [[Imani]] as the "Red Menace of Tinker Town".
• Named race boss encountered in the River of Sloth, a large, hairless, ogre-like man who is overwhelmed and killed by a wave of [[Zipper Shrews]] summoned via [[Summon Ye, Vermin]].
