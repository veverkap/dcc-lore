# Maurice

# Maurice
Chieftain of the northern cannibal tribe. Named target of the mandatory quest [[Pasty Inferno]] after the death of [[Great Rusty]]; his remaining power is a threat to [[Upano]].

## Additional Observations
# Maurice
A quest target the narrator’s group must find and kill; another iteration of this target is pursued by [[Elle]] and [[Imani]] as the "Red Menace of Tinker Town."

## Additional Observations
# [[Maurice]]
The named race boss encountered in the River of Sloth. This chapter's Maurice is a large, hairless, ogre-like man who is overwhelmed and gruesomely killed by a wave of [[Zipper Shrews]] summoned via [[Summon Ye, Vermin]].
