# damascus

• Mysterious [[naga]]-voiced figure in the Desperado Club who interrogated [[dungeon crawler carl]] about loyalty and taught a cover story (e.g., limp). Has influence over club matters.
• Encountered by [[Carl]] in a dark room, claimed to have knowledge of secret passageways in the [[desperado club]] and enforcer-like methods (threatened [[Carl]] to feign injury).
