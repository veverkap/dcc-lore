# sir_ferdinand

• A level 100 province boss cat and familiar of [[Queen [[Imogen]] of the High Elves]], granted magical privileges (can cast a level 10 lightning spell, go invisible, and phase-jump).
• Target of the group's charm attempt led by [[Donut]].
• A cat minion described as "Sir [[Ferdinand]], minion of [[Princess]] [[Donut]]", sitting among the judges and reacting to [[Princess Donut]]'s performance.
• Co-warlord to the Home Team (city of [[Larracos]]) for Faction Wars, part of the system-enforced team protections and structure.
• [[An]] orange Level 100 cat titled "Warlord on High" and co-warlord of [[team retribution]], sitting on [[Drick]]'s head and possessing a prized turban and a rhinoceros mount ([[Simoom]]).
• Warlord of [[team retribution]] who proposed emergency action items during the Faction Wars.
• The familiar/pet cat associated with [[Princess Donut]], often on [[Donut]]'s shoulder and notable in conversation about court status.
• Wears a conspicuous magical turban hat that grants a level 10 lightning spell, invisibility, and the ability to phase jump.
• Responds strongly to high charisma and is a physical threat to [[Dungeon Crawler Carl]] and others.
• [[Ferdinand]] the cat, minion of [[Donut]], sits at the judges' table and cheers onstage, noted for the ridiculous hat and wagging tail.
• NPC co-warlord paired with [[Juice Box]] and assigned to lead the Home Team base in [[Larracos]] during [[Faction Wars]].
