Four-armed naga representative of the [[sultanate]] present at the meeting; notable for a courteous but guarded demeanor and a promise of fealty under certain conditions.

A temple-guard naga mentioned as the spouse of one of the two titled assistants ("Ikbal") in the [[sultanate]] succession; encountered by the narrator at pre-production meetings.

Identified as the husband of Vinata (the small, black-scaled naga’s husband). Not present in the room; his absence is noted.

## Additional Observations
# [[Rishi]]
A large green-and-purple naga formerly tied to the [[sultanate]], later claiming the title "Sultan Rishi the Usurper" of the [[nagas]]. Captured and rendered armless and encased in containment by the Princess Posse, he offers a signed treaty granting Earth rights and five [[Naphil-class generation ships]] in exchange for naga settlement rights. Marked by the protagonist and noted for high Dexterity.

## Additional Observations
# [[Rishi]]
[[Rishi]] is the Naga referenced as having had multiple wives (including Chandra) and whose sequence of marriages and deaths is part of the background legal justification Chandra uses (Widow's Rights) to claim Carl. He is discussed in relation to family dynamics and palace intrigue.

## Additional Observations
# Rishi
A named participant in a referenced GreaserBall tournament (placed 8th), whose damaged participation trophy is found in the junk from the [[Legendary Fan Box]].

Representative of the Blood Sultanate; a four-armed naga with a coiled snake body. Interacts politely but with underlying menace and honor-bound obligations. Mentioned as important to eliminate early in Faction Wars by Carl.
