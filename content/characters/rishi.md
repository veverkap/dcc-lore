# rishi

• Four-armed [[naga]] representative of the [[sultanate]] with a courteous but guarded demeanor, promising fealty under certain conditions.
• Green- and purple [[naga]] with a muscled, armor-covered upper torso, previously appeared at a council meeting.
• Husband of [[Vinata]], not present in the room.
• Large green- and purple [[naga]], formerly tied to the [[sultanate]], claiming the title "[[Sultan]] Rishi the Usurper" of the [[nagas]], captured and rendered armless and encased in containment.
• Had multiple wives, including [[Chandra]], and whose sequence of marriages and deaths is part of the background legal justification [[Chandra]] uses to claim [[Carl]].
• Participant in a referenced GreaserBall tournament, placed 8th, with a damaged participation trophy found in the junk from the [[Legendary Fan Box]].
• Representative of the Blood Sultanate, a four-armed [[naga]] with a coiled snake body, interacting politely but with underlying menace and honor-bound obligations.
• Character whose execution is referenced earlier in the narrative, with a damaged GreaserBall participation trophy appearing among the fan-box loot.
