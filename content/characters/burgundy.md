# burgundy

• A young [[human]] woman with heterochromia (one pale blue eye and one brown) was encountered on the stairs at the [[Skyfowl]] administrative building, carrying a blue folder and making a dismissive comment when told they're going to see the magistrate.
• [[An]] assistant to the magistrate and a former or partial [[krasue]], found alive but half-charred after the fireball, provided crucial testimony, confirming [[Miss Quill]] as the true magistrate, explaining [[krasue]] behavior, and pointing to the location and nature of [[remex the grand]].
• The same young [[human]] woman was encountered rushing up and down the magistrate's stairs, commenting on the rarity of non-eagles up there.
• [[An]] assistant to the magistrate, recognized from earlier scenes, was found partially restored after the fire and revealed crucial exposition: [[Featherfall]] was already dead and turned into a fetish, Miss [[Quill]] runs the city, and [[remex the grand]] is the hidden, necromantic threat.
