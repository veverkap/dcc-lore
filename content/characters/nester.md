# nester

• A rare Zebani mercenary and level 65 Cartographer hired for the race, possessing gravity spells and a Radar skill.
• Surveys rooms, identifying non-apartment utility rooms, and casts abilities to manipulate movement and enemy positioning during runs.
• A crawler present in the garage, reacting emotionally to the group's survival and the apartment building looming in the background.
