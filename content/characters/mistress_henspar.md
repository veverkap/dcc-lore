# mistress_henspar

• Mistress Henspar, [[Mistress Henspar]], was [[Volteeg]]'s [[human]] caregiver before the dungeon.
• She loved and protected [[Volteeg]], and her death on the seventh floor becomes a central trauma motivating his later actions.
