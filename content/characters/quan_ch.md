# quan_ch

• A level-15 crawler (Imperial Security Trooper) who survived the magical blast and opened a Celestial Quest Box, triggering a rare double-upgrade sequence.
• A high-level crawler (Half Elf – Imperial Security Trooper – Level 38) who outranks [[Katia]] Grim in level and is a source of annoyance for [[Donut]].
• A crawler who received a Celestial Quest jacket granting flight and lightning attacks, appears on the recap, and rises rapidly on the leaderboard.
• A named crawler accused of sabotaging trains and farming train drivers, causing multiple lines to be disrupted and leaving parties stranded.
• A high-level crawler (Half Elf – Imperial Security Trooper) who popped his bubble early and is farming mobs aggressively, appearing on the leaderboard.
• A top-ten crawler (rank 10) – Half Elf who changed to Sergeant-at-Arms, visible on the broadcast, and noted by the group.
• A one-armed rival crawler who ambushes the fight from above, level 70, and steals both keys via card play.
• A crawler who uses a robe granting flight and a blue bolt spell (possibly Disrupter), appears on the top-ten leaderboard.
• A named crawler cited in [[Odette]]'s recap as instrumental in shifting outcomes on earlier floors.
• A crawler who received a Celestial Quest box and uses a flight-enabled magic jacket to attack trains, appears on the leaderboard as a level 31 half-elf Imperial Security Trooper.
• A named crawler mentioned as having been in the same "bubble" with some of the newcomers on the fifth floor and having had conflicts, part of inter-player history.
• A lone combatant encountered by [[Imani]] who attempted to initiate a card battle, identified as [[an]] opponent in the Cuba church saferoom incident.
• A character that [[an]] orc cosplayer imitates, complete with prosthetic bloody stump, referenced as part of the fan cosplay present at the signing.
• A named crawler added to the narrator's Book of Voodoo during the chapter, a crawler or NPC entry relevant to the book's lore index.
