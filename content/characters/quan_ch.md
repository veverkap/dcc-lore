# Quan Ch
A level-15 crawler (Imperial Security Trooper) shown surviving the magical blast and later opening an upgraded reward. His survival triggered a rare double-upgrade sequence that converted a [[Platinum Quest Box]] into a [[Celestial Quest Box]], making him a focal point in the legal/financial dispute between [[borant corporation]] and the [[Syndicate]].
