# queen_imogen_of_the_high_elves

* Queen [[Imogen]] of the High [[Elves]] is a newly introduced "country boss" character, expected to appear in the high-elf castle during the Butcher's Masquerade event, suspected to be a repurposed [[human]] and a major reveal influencing top-crawler selection.
* She is the High Elf monarch who keeps exotic familiars like [[Sir Ferdinand]]. Her favor grants unusual protections and privileges to her pet, and she is central to the castle's court and politics.
* Ruler of the high [[elves]]; interested in [[Carl]] and staging a Masquerade event, represented in the field by her familiar and guards.
* The elven queen and boss of the castle; the target of [[Signet]]'s plot and the planned assault during (or timed with) the Butcher's Masquerade.
* A Level 145 Country Boss, a cleric-sorceress who hosts the ritual party known as the Butcher's Masquerade. She wears a soul gem pendant ([[Soul Crystal]]), claims the favor of [[Apito]] (the Oak Mother), and enforces the ballroom's [[Protection Seal]].
* The queen of the High [[Elves]], formally presented in the ballroom. She is the central antagonist in the scene, wearing a blood-streaked gown and enforcing [[an]] anti-violence seal that prevents attendees from leaving or breaking the seal without consequences.
* Mean ruler of the high [[elves]] who hosted the Butcher's Masquerade at the [[castle of the high elves]]; a country boss targeted by [[Signet]] and central to several revenge plots.
* Referenced possessively through "Queen [[Imogen]]'s private ballroom." A high-elf figure whose named location anchors the ballroom where the massacre and subsequent adjudication take place.
* The reclusive, pure-blooded high-elf mage and target of [[Signet]]'s revenge quest. Lives deep in the [[castle of the high elves]] and appears at elite seasonal gatherings; killing her is the stated objective of "The Vengeance of the Daughter" quest.
* Ruler of the High [[Elves]] whose court is involved in the creeper purge; has placed [[dungeon crawler carl]] on her programme du bal (Masquerade) and sends messages via her familiar.
* Ruler of the [[elves]] and host of the [[Butcher's Masquerade]] party; guarantees safe passage out of the southern castle entrance after the event.
* The High Elf queen described as "Queen of All That Is and the Queen of All That Will Be," central to the masquerade. She is expected to be present in her private ballroom, and her protection spells (attributed to [[Apito]]) are a major mechanical constraint for guests.
* The ruler expected to visit the Masquerade. [[queen [[imogen]] of the high elves]] is central to the plan to manipulate the party's timing—organizers expected her arrival to be a trigger point for their infiltration.
* The Queen of the High [[Elves]] present at the goodwill ball. Hostile toward [[Carl]] and his group; bound by the [[Peace Seal (anti-violence seal)]] while in the ballroom. Displays powerful magic and interacts directly with [[Ferdinand]] during the scene.
* Mean high-elf ruler who hosted the [[Butcher's Masquerade]] at the [[castle of the high elves]], acted as a country boss, and was the antagonist in [[Signet]]'s revenge arc.
* A previously encountered boss referenced for strategy comparison; [[Carl]] notes that [[The Visitor]]'s approach mirrored tactics used to defeat [[queen [[imogen]] of the high elves]] in [[an]] earlier event.
* [[Princess]] [[Imogen]], the high elf heir who, according to [[Signet]]'s history, carried out purges against half-elf bastards to secure her claim—context for [[Signet]]'s banishment.
