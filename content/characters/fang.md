# fang

• [[Fang]]: crawler whose team got caught in [[Lucia]]'s teleport trap, had a key taken after his death.
• Warlord Fang of the [[Reavers]], appears in youthful [[human]] guise with robotic indicators, present at council alongside other faction leaders.
• Shapeshifting or evasive enemy leader, "worse than a [[changeling]]", difficult to locate; killing him drops his soldiers, but locating him is problematic.
• Warlord alongside [[Tagg]] whom [[Li Na]] likely marked, both slipped away, creating tactical concerns.
• Warlord and cyborg example, known to have survived catastrophic events by existing as small biological material encased in robotics.
• Associated with [[Reavers]] side, previously obstructed brain implant [[Epitome Tagg]] wanted, cited as reason for delaying procedures tied to swap plan.
