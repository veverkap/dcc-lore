# penny_the_pig

* A pig in the pet stables observing [[Mongo]] and acting jealous when the dinosaur plays with [[Rend]].
* Also called [[Penelope]] the pig; a party member who becomes invulnerable from [[Khepri]] for ninety seconds and is healed/regrown by [[Taranis]] after being gravely damaged.
* A dungeon-born pig companion who must visit a [[Safe Room]] to receive a class; interacts affectionately with [[Carl]] and eats supplied [[Splooge]].
* Equipped with the [[Enchanted Spiked Kneepads of the Munificent Goddess Kina]] and a collar with a trap module; in one chapter, she is suffering oxygen deprivation and requires more [[Splooge]].
* A pet carried with [[Rend]] into the garage prior to the race; part of [[Carl]]'s menagerie.
* Addressed apologetically by [[Carl]] amid the chaos of the arena entrance.
