# [[Widget]]
A Level 19 Grease Gremlin and iron-tangle steam engine train technician encountered on the engine. Speaks in a creole-cockney accent, wields an oversized wrench, and assists the party by rigging the train turntable/chain escape. Mentioned as having a wife and a home at Station 60.

## Additional Observations
# Widget
A gremlin who previously mentioned the "Terminus Direct" and is cited by [[Katia]] when explaining the structure of the [[Vermillion Line]] and its stops.
