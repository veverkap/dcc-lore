# chiyome

* A named Razor Fox NPC (Level 80 Mistress of Nunchaku) from team [[wild hunt]] who demonstrates team form magic, drinks beer provided by [[Radoslav]], and identifies competing teams.
* A foxlike member of [[dungeon crawler carl]]'s group who observes and comments on the [[mantaur]] variants, notes that [[Genesis]] and [[Rapture]] practice domination magic, and explains battlefield cautions.
* Ally referenced in the team’s planning, part of the strategy discussion about shattering the windshield and coordinating with the [[bugbears]] for the race.
* Ally of [[dungeon crawler carl]]'s team who fields a giant bear mount intended to smash [[Rapture]]'s vehicle as part of the coordinated attack.
* Racer who attacks the boss pouches, revealing that the bosses’ pouch interiors are the true weak spots.
* Crawler allied with [[dungeon crawler carl]] who speaks at the start of the chapter and participates in the kangaroo boss encounter alongside the group.
* Team ally who communicates with [[dungeon crawler carl]] about tactical adjustments before the race and plays a supporting coordination role in the heat.
* Ally on [[free love]] who reports battlefield conditions and communicates with [[dungeon crawler carl]] during the race.
* Named Razor Fox ninja: "Level 80 Mistress of Nunchaku," one of three from team [[The Wild Hunt]], who performs team-form magic, refuses peach-flavored beer, drinks from [[Radoslav]]'s magical cooler, and advises cooperation against other teams.
* Fox-like team member and ninja who analyzes foes, demonstrates combat skill, and drinks and comments on [[mantaur]] physiology.
* Collaborator in the team's race plan who coordinated tactics with the narrator and the [[Bugbears]] prior to the event.
* Ally of [[Carl]] whose mount is a giant bear intended to smash the womantaur's car and complete the coordinated attack once [[Rapture]] is immobilized.
* Racer who actively attacks the boss pouch, demonstrating that the boss's pouch is the primary weak spot.
* Ally present in the chapter who declares vengeance for [[Ito]]'s death and participates in the boss engagement.
* Teammate or contact who communicates with [[Carl]] during race preparations, asking tactical questions about altering plans.
* Team member coordinating situational awareness and noting battlefield effects, providing tactical warnings to [[Carl]] about veering and obstacles during the run.
