# Fitz

# Fitz
The large, bald level-30 human barkeep and proprietor of The One-Eyed Narwhal. He greets the group and runs the pub's rooms and services.

## Additional Observations
# Fitz
Barkeep at the inn where the party stays. Provides free room-and-board in exchange for potions, supplies oranges on request, and is emotionally affected by GumGum's death (a longtime customer). Witness and minor source of local information.
