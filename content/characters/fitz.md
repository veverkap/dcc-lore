# fitz

• The large, bald level-30 [[human]] barkeep and proprietor of The One-Eyed Narwhal, who runs the pub's rooms and services.
• Provides free room-and-board in exchange for potions, supplies, and oranges on request.
• Emotionally affected by [[GumGum]]'s death, a longtime customer.
• Witness and minor source of local information.
• Raises the alarm about a lich after overhearing conversations, summoning a crowd and the "night patrol" to the warehouse area.
• Interacts with dungeon crawler [[Carl]], [[Donut]], and [[Mordecai]] during the chapter.
• Raised the alarm and gathered [[NPCs]] for the night patrol, indirectly helping [[Carl]] assemble a crowd of townsfolk to watch/retreat during the operation at the [[Swordsmen]] Depot.
