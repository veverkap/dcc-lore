# hold_steady

• [[Hold Steady]] is a [[skyfowl]] party member who maintains the magic required to keep the  [[Puzzle Disk]] primed and within the stairwell circle.
• He casts protective and priming magic that enables the final activation ritual.
• He reacts with outrage when events spiral out of control.
• He protests the sacrificial actions taken against [[Uzzi]].
