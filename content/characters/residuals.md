# residuals

• A mysterious phenomenon related to the unfolding mystery, treated as a game-world anomaly.
• A hyperspatial, collective-mind alien race that occupies other beings' memories and bodies, divided into factions with differing goals.
• Two distinct types of entities exist, with differing goals, and have attempted to influence the System [[AI]] to end or change the crawl.
• A class of dungeon entities present in large numbers, used to justify alarm and defensive measures by caprid protectors.
• The entities can be edited out by Showrunners and include individuals like [[Paulie]] ([[Goff]]) and [[Agatha]].
• They are present in almost every crawl with a [[primal]] engine, move into other beings' bodies to speak with fledgling AIs, and have internal politics and external threats from the System [[AI]].
• The entities are a numerical threat factor driving fear and preparation in the dungeon.
