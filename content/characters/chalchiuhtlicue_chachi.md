# Chalchiuhtlicue (Chachi)
A goddess-like figure referred to as "Chachi" by [[Samantha]] and implicated in extreme, violent acts against children in Samantha’s account. Mentioned as a deity Carl had discussed previously with [[Lucia Mar]] and [[Azra]].

## Additional Observations
# Chalchiuhtlicue (Chachi)
A goddess discussed by the narrator and contacts. Referred to as "Chachi" by some players; Rosetta notes she's a lesser-known water-and-fire goddess associated with childbirth, women, and harvests, and not a commonly sponsored deity in the Ascendency game. Linked culturally to the Aztec flood myth.
