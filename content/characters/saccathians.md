# saccathians

• [[Saccathians]] are tentacle-faced alien [[hunters]], with individual soldiers involved in the trench fight and having mechanical leg enhancements.
• They have cephalopod-like faces, are commonly referred to as "Sacs", and have a trade influence in the [[Prism]] Kingdom.
• They are represented on a panel by [[Princess D'nadia]].
