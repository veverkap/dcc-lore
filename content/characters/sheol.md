# sheol

Here is the consolidated markdown list:

* [[Sheol]]: a high-level demonic zone, infernal realm, underworld, or hellish plane, referenced in various contexts:
	+ Fire/demon resistance and immunity
	+ Demon hierarchies on the fifteenth level
	+ Properties of Self-Immolation and burn-related damage
	+ Baptism by Fire mechanic
	+ Origin of evicted [[demons]] and forced possession
	+ Destination of freed full-power [[demons]]
	+ Realm of fire below the building
	+ Abyssal destination [[demons]] attempt to drag characters toward
	+ Burning, hellish region of the floor
	+ Infernal region invoked by Sheol fire
	+ Fifteenth floor of the Dungeon, [[an]] unbearably hot, cave-like zone
	+ Parent of four sons, one of whom may have been involved in [[Geyrun]]'s murder
	+ Potential destination for the memorial crystal ([[Ysalte]])
