# cici

• One of [[Lucia Mar]]'s rottweilers, depicted as a combat companion that helps slay large monsters, shown alongside [[Gustavo 3]].
• A giant [[rottweiler]] companion of [[Lucia Mar]] that fought in the stairwell incident and is affected by [[Lucia]]'s protective spells.
• The massive, [[pony]]-ized [[rottweiler]] companion of [[Lucia Mar]], aggressive and large enough to decapitate and batter opponents, killed/decapitated by [[Donut]] after being lured through a hole spell.
• A named individual mentioned as having been killed; [[Lucia]] cites revenge against [[Donut]] for [[Cici]]'s death ("Blood for blood. She killed Cici. I kill her.").
• One of [[Lucia Mar]]'s dogs (a [[rottweiler]]) enlarged and modified by [[Lucia]]'s effects, participates in the ghoul-station incident and receives reflected damage during the encounter.
• The larger, [[pony]]-sized [[rottweiler]] companion of [[Lucia Mar]], aggressive and lethal in combat, killed in the chapter when [[Donut]] uses [[Hole]] and removes the [[dog]]'s head.
