# botis

• A satanic-look goat creature serving as the magistrate/host of the [[Plenty]]'s tunnel event, announcing live sentencing and interacting with [[dungeon crawler carl]] and [[Princess Donut]].
• A goat-host caprid who acts as [[an]] on-camera presenter in the studio, associated with the caprid herd that panics during the broadcast and runs through the protagonists when the stampede begins.
• A goat-like demonic magistrate who hosts the tunnel event, calling out the initial death sentence and acting as emcee, interacting with [[Princess Donut]] and [[Carl]].
• A goat-host in the panel, initially asking questions and then panicking with the other [[caprids]], physically present as part of the goat delegation during the broadcast.
