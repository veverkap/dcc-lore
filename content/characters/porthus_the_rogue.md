# porthus_the_rogue

• A High Elf Crawler who received [[The Dungeon Anarchist’’s Cookbook]] (disguised as a blank sketchbook) on the [[Ninth Floor]] during the fifteenth season, and is credited as [[an]] early recipient and part of its provenance.
