Level 70 rhinoceros NPC: a Sai Outpost Guard Mercenary Leader and a Sergeant in the [[Princess Posse]] hired to defend the castle; haunted by failure from the Desperado Club incident and offers to remain and defend the building or be sent to [[Shanty Town]].
