# toyotomi

• Level 70 rhinoceros NPC, haunted by failure from the Desperado Club incident, offers to defend the castle or be sent to Shanty Town.
• Too large to fit in the Party Planner personal transport, part of the new mercenary group sent toward the border with Shanty Town.
• Spearheaded the rapid construction of the synergy tower and coordinates enchantments and defenses.
• Ally present with [[Donut]] and Li [[Na]] at the Nest, helping demonstrate tower synergy mechanics.
• Guard/mage securing the front entrance of the Nest tower alongside [[Bomo]], reports a crawler woman with a [[dog]] entering the tower.
• Named ally/mage who is killed in the tower defense (described as "dead-dead" after being cleaved by a spell).
