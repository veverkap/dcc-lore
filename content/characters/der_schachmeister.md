# der_schachmeister

• Opponent deckmaster in the chapter: a small tuxedoed boy with round glasses.
• Holds a twenty-card deck including six totems with [[an]] eight-second summoning cooldown.
• Focuses on debuffs and disabling effects, using mystic cards like [[Peekaboo]] and [[Summon lock]].
