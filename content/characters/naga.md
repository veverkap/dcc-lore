# naga
A group/species of snake-like beings mentioned as offering insurance to elites and as an antagonist force the annotator intends to harm; targeted by [[Crawler Drakea]]'s ire.

## Additional Observations
# Naga
A species/monster type on [[Floor 9]]; the recap notes that neither [[Donut]] nor [[Katia]] can leave that floor until all the [[nagas]] are dead, and only one may leave afterward.

## Additional Observations
# Naga
The enemy species/faction defending a castle (the [[Naga Castle]). Many naga soldiers are reported dead early in the chapter; their forces and the [[Naga Castle]] are focal points of the assault.

## Additional Observations
# Naga
Snake-headed humanoid race; [[Manasa]] is presented as a cobra-headed celebrity associated with this lineage (though her on-air presence is maintained by [[valtay]]).

## Additional Observations
# Naga
Serpentine royal members of the [[sultanate]] referenced as the targets that royal members must slay before leaving the [[Ninth Floor]]. Represent a significant combat obstacle tied to the [[crown of the sepsis whore]] condition.

## Additional Observations
# Naga
Monster species that play a gating role on [[Floor 9]] — all Naga must be killed before certain players can leave, and only one of two tiara-bearers can depart afterward.
