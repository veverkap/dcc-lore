# naga

• A group/species of snake-like beings offering insurance to elites and serving as [[an]] antagonist force.
• Found on [[Floor 9]], where neither [[Donut]] nor [[Katia]] can leave until all are dead, and only one may leave afterward.
• Defend the [[Naga Castle]] and are a focal point of the assault, with many reported dead early in the chapter.
• Represent a snake-headed humanoid race, with [[Manasa]] being a cobra-headed celebrity associated with this lineage.
• Are serpentine royal members of the [[sultanate]] that must be slain before leaving the [[Ninth Floor]], tied to the [[crown of the sepsis whore]] condition.
• Play a gating role on [[Floor 9]], requiring all Naga to be killed before certain players can leave, and only one of two tiara-bearers can depart afterward.
