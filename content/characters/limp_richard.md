# limp_richard

• [[Limp Richard]]: Mole Man NPC (Level 36) and non-combatant proprietor of "Limp Richard's Sundries" at [[Traveler Station #83]], selling general supplies and greeting [[crawlers]].
• Sells items like the [[Battery Fabricator]] and small wares, and trades physical books and negotiates prices with [[Donut]] and the party.
• Provides supplies and basic interactions with [[crawlers]].
