# viceroy

• A masked, necromancy-capable representative of the team called the [[Madness]]; appears decapitated during the meeting but is tied to regenerative/illusory mechanics.
• A high-status mercenary identity/armor type referenced by defenders.
• Viceroy masks are used by some attacking forces; genuine Viceroys are rare and recognizable in combat.
• The masked representative/ race associated with the Madness team initially silent at the meeting; revealed to have a bone-white, demonic, tusked [[face]] when the mask is removed and later regenerates.
