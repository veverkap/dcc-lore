# lorelai

• [[Lorelai]] is a level 7 goblin shamanka who participates in negotiations, samples Dungeon-made Meth, and helps build vehicles.
• Lorelai is a named goblin companion who flirts with the protagonist during gear-up sequences and is present alongside Rory and [[Donut]] during assault preparations.
• Lorelai is referenced as a companion paired with Rory, with the narrator wondering if she'll be mad about the boss's handling.
• Lorelai and Rory co-create the custom Goblin Copper Chopper and its sidecar, and the vehicle is used as a point of comparison to [[Carl]]'s motorcycle.
