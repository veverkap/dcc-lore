# Lorelai
[[Lorelai]] is the other level 7 goblin shamanka present in the workshop; she participates in the negotiation, samples the [[Dungeon-made Meth (two baggies)]], and helps order engineers to build the requested vehicle.

## Additional Observations
# Lorelai
A named goblin (or goblinoid) companion who flirts with the protagonist during the gear-up sequence. Present alongside [[rory the shamanka]] and [[Donut]] during the assault preparations.
