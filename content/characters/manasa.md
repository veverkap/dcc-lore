# manasa

* [[Manasa]] is a cobra-headed pop singer who was killed when a pulse struck her production trailer, triggering the show's breaking-news sequence.
* Her death was caused by a brain parasite from [[Valtay]].
* She was previously murdered, and her production trailer was borrowed by [[Dungeon Crawler Carl]] and his crew.
* Her music might be playing in the casino, and her death is referenced as part of prior events that shaped sponsorship and politics.
* She was a [[Naga]] musician whose death became a major plot catalyst in the chapter.
* Her death was referenced as part of the broader club incidents and was tied to the [[Valtay]]'s machinations.
* She was mentioned as a possible source for the casino's intoxicating music.
* Her death was referenced as context for sponsor tensions and was mentioned as a past event that colors [[Dungeon Crawler Carl]]'s memory of similar rental trailers.
* She was used as a visual comparison for [[Eva Sigrid]]'s appearance.
* She was assassinated by the [[Skull Clan]] and her death was referenced as prior history connected to [[Ripper Wonton]]'s program and the protagonists' past.
