# pustule_the_hobgoblin

* [[Pustule]] the hobgoblin, a merchant NPC, sells smoke curtains on the top floor of the [[desperado club]] and provides context for the upgraded [[Troll Smoke Mantle]] purchase.
* The Level 30 hobgoblin proprietor of [[Hobgobs and Boom Sticks]], knowledgeable about explosives and pricing, sells [[hobgoblin dynamite]], [[Hobgoblin Dynamite]], [[hob-lobber]]s, [[Smoke Bomb]]s, and other supplies.
* Sells exotic materials, such as [[hobgoblin pus]], on the [[Silk Road]].
* Previously sold smoke curtains, but is considered a lower-tier supplier compared to [[Troll Smoke Mantle]] sellers at the [[desperado club]].
* Offers pricing and crafting advice, commenting on [[Boom Jug]] composition.
* Sells and values explosive goods, including [[hob-lobber]] and [[hobgoblin dynamite]].
* Named hobgoblin vendor referenced as a source for alternate bomb types and impact-triggered hob-lobbers.
* Sold [[Hobgoblin Pus]] previously used by [[Carl]] to rig a [[Hob-lobber]].
