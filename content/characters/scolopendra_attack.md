# scolopendra_attack

* [[Scolopendra]] attack: a named hostile attack/event referenced in conversation about crystal protections, treated as a hostile mob/attack source related to [[Ysalte crystal]] protection failure, mentioned by [[Carl]] and [[Osvaldo]].
* **Scolopendra**: a massive centipede-like entity, often referred to as a "big bad" at the bottom of the dungeon, responsible for catastrophic attacks and transformations.
* **Scolopendra's Lair**: a location where **Soul Crystals** are mined, related to lore about crystals and power sources.
* **Scolopendra's Club**: a location mentioned as a place where **[[Ugloo]]** intended to visit to meet the king prior to fighting, or a specific area on the 18th floor containing trapped players/[[NPCs]].
* **Scolopendra's Encounter Rules**: a collection of explicit dungeon rules tied to the presence or fate of **Scolopendra**, including killing or abandoning her, triggering catastrophic rule effects.
* **Scolopendra's Nine-Tier Attack**: a devastating attack pattern launched by **Scolopendra**, causing mass deaths and transformations.
* **Scolopendra's Transformation**: the ability of **Scolopendra** to transform creatures, such as **[[Kiwi]]** and **Big [[Tina]]**, into dinosaurs.
* **Scolopendra's Warning**: a system announcement signaling potential activity on the 18th floor and triggering concern in the council.
* **Scolopendra's Lair**: a location where **Scolopendra** is said to be dreaming and prophesied to awaken and "destroy everything and free us all" – a piece of local prophecy/artwork in the ballroom dome.
* **Scolopendra's Attack Preparation**: a system announcement signaling the preparation of **Scolopendra**'s attack, including the number of attacks remaining and the time until the next attack.
* **Scolopendra's Awakening**: a system announcement signaling the awakening of **Scolopendra**, marking [[an]] escalation and immediate threat following the planar breaches.
* **Scolopendra's Club**: a venue/zone tied to **Scolopendra** that receives urgent evacuation advisories when the lair stirs.
* **Scolopendra's Pet Carrier**: a device used to capture and control **Scolopendra**, central to the party's strategy to place her in a **Pet Carrier** and control the Ascendency outcome.
