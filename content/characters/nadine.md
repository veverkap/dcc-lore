# nadine

• Nadine, a Bearded Sage Caterpillar (Level 50), is a mount and guide for the group, formerly a [[Chee]] teacher and actress.
• She chews trails through the jungle and was the instructor for a group of were-warriors.
• Also called Miss Nadine, she shelters [[Clint]] and directs dungeon crawler [[Carl]] toward [[Princess]] Lunette and a Pulpmancer.
• She helps protect [[Clint]] and urges taking the [[Princess]] Lunette construct.
• Nadine is a giant caterpillar summoned/ally present at the riverbank, who becomes alarmed and attempts to flee during the battle.
• She is a large boss-like caterpillar who was killed and exploded during the assault, triggering grief scenes and aftermath decisions.
• Her corpse is mourned by the camp and by [[Signet]]'s [[were-castors]].
• She is represented as a tattoo that [[Donut]] greets while observing [[Signet]]'s chest art.
• [[Holger]] promised to guard her and is cited as a reason he didn't teleport into the current action.
• She is a giant caterpillar allied with the infiltrators, reacting strongly to blood and chaos in the river around Fort Freedom and attempting to flee toward the shore.
• Her corpse provided loot, including the Neighborhood field guide map, and her death sparks mourning among companions like [[Holger]] and the [[were-castors]].
• She is mentioned by [[Donut]] (as a tattoo greeting) and appears via tattoo imagery on [[Signet]].
