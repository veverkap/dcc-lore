# diwata_circe_took

* A Level 250 Immortal deity whose true identity is Circe Took.
* Presents as a god giving birth to mantis offspring.
* Vulnerable when stripped of soul-armor/protection and ultimately reverted to her mortal form and killed when targeted by allied spells like [[Laundry Day]].
* A minor nature goddess whose adherents will attack on sight if [[Carl]] bears the "Enemy of the Church" tattoo.
* Described as a squirrel-like antlered being covered in moss; vengeful and capable of summoning animals and making trees grow.
* Forest protector deity sponsored by Circe Took, able to change form and grow giant; unforgiving toward apostates.
* Can summon animals and make trees grow.
* Can alter form and scale to giant size.
* Warned to be dangerous to apostates.
* Appears as a small antlered squirrel-like god, marks [[Carl]] for death with a pulsing red X, and departs after a short timer.
* Goddess/bug-like entity who can shift sex and is currently in mantis form giving birth to many mantis babies.
* Located beneath the party on the map, reported as not moving during the chaos.
* Level 250 Immortal deity-form, revealed to be the god identity of Circe Took, with extreme durability.
* Mother of [[Vrah]], traveled to Earth in anger and participated in the dungeon events.
* Character referenced in connection with a legendary De-Sleeving Box event.
* Deity worshipped by certain creatures, including mandagots, and triggers automatic hostility from creatures that worship Diwata toward the player.
