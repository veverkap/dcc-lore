# louis_santiago

• Referred to commonly as [[Louis]] in the chapter; titled in-system as “crawler Army Air General [[Louis]] Santiago 2.” Owner and controller of the [[Party Planner]] vehicle and the [[Airship Portal]] access. Can grant or restrict access to the ship and controls use of the hangar/elevator.
• Protagonist of the interlude, waking up in [[Sheol]] instead of the intended [[Pineapple Cabaret]] holding area, with malfunctioning HUD and message access.
• Received kill credit on [[Odious Creeper]]s during the events of this chapter.
• Found cut open and surgically modified inside the [[Reaver castle]], wraith-possessed by [[Juice Box]] for extraction, with inventory including [[no disassemble]].
• Level-22 [[human]] crawler in the class "Pest Exterminator" encountered at the [[Spit and Swallow]] saferoom, part of the duo with [[Firas M]] and displaying drunken, uncooperative behavior.
• Has gills, a malfunctioning HUD, and receives messages from [[Samantha]], interacting with [[Britney]], [[Chris]], and [[Carl]], and is given a [[Leech]] by [[Forkith]].
