# tran

Here is the consolidated markdown list:

* [[Tran]] is a level-28 [[human]] Swashbuckler with a metal-detecting ability who helps locate a ring under the drainage wheel and assists with the long electrical cable.
* [[Tran]] is a supporting contact mentioned by [[Gwen]] who reports on physical hookup work relevant to [[dungeon crawler carl]]'s plan.
* [[Tran]] is a named individual briefly mentioned as the one [[Gwen]] sent into the glass stairwell chamber; part of [[Gwen]]'s team involved in the decrystallization incident.
* [[Tran]] is a [[human]] swashbuckler and Pathfinder skill user who joins the dive into the [[Akula]] and helps navigate internal sub corridors; first to react to nauseating drained compartments.
* [[Tran]] is a member of [[Carl]]'s party aboard the [[Akula]], falls during the submarine's jolt and later assists [[Katia]] and [[Vadim]] at [[Carl]]'s direction.
* [[Tran]] is a member of [[Katia]]'s squad who fights alongside [[Carl]] and [[Katia]] inside the shark, wounded and later rescued by [[Carl]] during the escape from [[Lusca]].
* [[Tran]] is a crawler present when [[dungeon crawler carl]] opens boxes; received loot similar to [[dungeon crawler carl]] and [[Katia]] and shares in the group's reactions.
* [[Tran]] is a party fighter who investigates the damaged electrical line, engages feral mobs, and reports on the status of the lightning towers and wiring that factor into the group quest and interior electrification.
* [[Tran]] is a companion who assists [[Gwen]] with draining the necropolis before returning to a saferoom as ordered by [[dungeon crawler carl]].
* [[Tran]] is a crawler who uses a flying wheelchair module for mobility; interacts warmly with [[Donut]] and the group.
* [[Tran]] is a veteran defender and trench commander who coordinates assaults, wears a mechanical crab-like mobility/body exoskeleton built by [[Katia]], and helps integrate reinforcements such as the [[106th Bloody Leeches]].
* [[Tran]] is a leader of the [[102nd bulwark battalion]] responsible for southern-front defenses and issuing orders to dispersed battalions during the battle.
* [[Tran]] is a member of [[Katia]]'s team who lost his legs but is described as resilient and recovering after the collapse.
* [[Tran]] is a Vietnamese crawler who lost both legs on a prior floor and is learning to use a floating [[Odette]]-style magical wheelchair.
* [[Tran]] is a crawler who uses a flying wheelchair module; hosts [[Donut]] at the scene and is shown smiling and petting her, reflecting a rare moment of levity.
* [[Tran]] is a squad member who observes combat and is assigned to hold positions with [[Britney]] and [[Katia]]'s squad.
* [[Tran]] is a crawler who falls through levels during the fight; reports wheelchair damage and later claims to have damaged a rock creature totem.
* [[Tran]] is a member of [[Katia]]'s squad trapped with [[Imani]] in debris inside the collapsing FOSCA building during the demon assault.
* [[Tran]] is a party member who is passed out and legless after the battle; lost his wheelchair and carries a string of debuffs.
* [[Tran]] is a crawler who is stuck in his garage due to damage to his [[spider]]-legs; reportedly has a hearse for a car and is being repaired by his [[gremlin]].
* [[Tran]] is a crawler whose team is referenced in incoming communications; one of the other competitors at the starting line mentioned in the context of replacements for eliminated teams.
* [[Tran]] is a member of the party who sits at the table in his [[spider]] wheelchair and offers counsel to [[Zhang]] about blame and responsibility following recent killings.
* [[Tran]] is a member of [[Carl]]'s group who reacts with relief when the plan is agreed upon, clasping hands and bowing; present at the planning table.
* [[Tran]] is a member of the crawler group present at the table; leaves with [[Elle]] and [[Zhang]] to attempt contact with [[Na]].
* [[Tran]] is a crawler with mechanical [[spider]] legs who joins those leaving; referred to as a "General" informally and interacts with [[Carl]] and [[Zhang]] at the farewell.
