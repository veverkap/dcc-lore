# the_vinegar_bitch

* The Vinegar Bitch, a named antagonist and goddess, was killed and her removal is part of the floor's aftermath, causing her sponsor to be enraged.
* Her removal is tied to the larger campaign that freed [[Amayon]].
* Colloquial/insulting name used in-scene for the goddess [[Ysalte]]
* Defensive target whose deck must be kept from emptying to avoid catastrophic spells
