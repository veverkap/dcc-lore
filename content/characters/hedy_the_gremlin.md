# Hedy the gremlin

# Hedy the gremlin
A gremlin garage attendant who runs the starting gate in the race garage on the tenth floor. She physically opens the garage door and directs racers with urgent commands (e.g., “Only one person drive!”). Connected scenes include the vehicle-selection sequence and interaction with [[dungeon crawler carl]] and [[Donut]].
