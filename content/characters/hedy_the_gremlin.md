# hedy_the_gremlin

• A [[gremlin]] garage attendant who runs the starting gate in the race garage on the tenth floor, physically opening the garage door and directing racers with urgent commands (e.g., “Only one person drive!”).
• Connected scenes include the vehicle-selection sequence and interaction with [[dungeon crawler carl]] and [[Donut]].
• Organizes the start of the races on [[Don't come in last]], yelling orders to [[crawlers]] to get into vehicles and enforcing that only one person drives per heat.
