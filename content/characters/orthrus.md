# orthrus

• A canine/[[Emberus]]'s "[[dog]]" or "little doggie" associated with the god's search after being trapped in the Nothing.
• A juvenile two-headed Gate Guardian, gigantic, lovable puppy (Level 10), and central NPC of the chapter's group quest, adopted companion linked to [[Emberus]], and objective of the quest is to avoid lightning and shark bites for his survival.
• A massive two-headed puppy (city-scale mob) central to the chapter's events, rescued from the necropolis, attracts group and world quests, and is the target of both a rescue objective and a world quest that encourages killing him for rewards.
• A massive two-headed [[dog]] soldier (puppy quest target) whose health and consciousness drive the chapter's rescue efforts; central to the failed quest and subsequent completion.
• A giant, two-headed puppy (referred to as the giant puppy at the end of the last floor) that chases the [[drop bear]] in the fan photo; notable beast from prior floors.
• A previously unleashed two-headed puppy referenced by [[dungeon crawler carl]] as [[an]] example of a past event that reverberated across the floor; invoked to contextualize the current outbreak's potential scale.
• A two-headed puppy released from the Nothing and tied to the death of [[Geyrun]]; searched by [[Emberus]] for memories relevant to the murder investigation.
• A two-headed puppy referenced indirectly as having a father (discussion of "Orthrus' daddy"); connected to the family lineage that is part of [[Amayon]]'s bargaining and the demon backstory.
• A named beast/companion referenced by [[Samantha]] in banter about breeding puppies with [[Carl]]'s new [[Dachshund]] form; treated as a pet/ beast.
• A named level-10 [[dog]] soldier (two-headed [[dog]]) central to the chapter's quest; target of the "Get Orthrus" quest; is healed by [[Mordecai's Special Brew]] and later removed to the twelfth floor as a reward.
• A giant puppy/ beast referenced in [[an]] animated photo of [[dungeon crawler carl]] and [[Princess Donut]] in the [[drop bear]] being chased; noted as the giant puppy encountered at the end of the previous floor.
• A previously unleashed storyline creature (the two-headed puppy) referenced as [[an]] example of [[an]] event that reverberated across a floor—used in comparison to the current runaway infestation.
• A two-headed puppy released from the Nothing tied to the murder of [[Geyrun]]; searched by [[Emberus]] for clues, but its time in the Nothing made its memory unreliable.
• A named child/ totem referenced in dialogue, discussed in relation to a parental figure and the events witnessed by [[Amayon]].
