# ghazi

• The signatory of the letter looted from [[Commandant Kane]]’s corpse, warning Kane to cease bombing and claiming knowledge of the [[Winding Box]] secret and the ability to open a portal, threatening to end the world.
• A [[human]], Level 43 Glass Mage known as the Mad Dune mage, former researcher from [[larracos]], who summoned a being believed to be [[Yarilo]] and instead brought [[Psamathe]] into the bubble, possessing the [[winding box]] and corresponding with [[council]].
• Owner of the sex doll vessel that housed part of a deity's essence, responsible for bringing the doll into play and connected to the events that opened the Nothing.
• Author of the [[Letter from the Glass Wizard]] looted from [[Commandant Kane]]’s corpse, warning of a truce request and claiming knowledge of opening a portal and cracking the winding box.
• [[Human]] level 43 glass mage known as the Mad Dune Mage, owner/inhabitant of the [[sandcastle]], who attempted to summon a deity (claimed to seek [[Yarilo]]) and instead unleashed [[Psamathe]] and its ooze familiar, keeper of the [[winding box]] and target of interrogation by [[dungeon crawler carl]]’s group.
• Mage whose workshop and lab are the scene of the encounter, owner of the object of [[Mrs. Ghazi]]’s affection (a crystallized love doll) and source of the winding box mystery, present during the fight and possibly killed during the collapse.
• A crawler who possessed the other half of [[Psamathe]] at one point and was manipulated into opening the Nothing, owner of the original doll vessel (the [[Lika]] doll) and implicated in the plot to free the deity half.
