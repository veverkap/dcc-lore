# Ghazi

# Ghazi
The signatory of the letter looted from [[Commandant Kane]]’s corpse. The letter warns Kane to cease bombing and claims knowledge of the [[Winding Box]] secret and the ability to open a portal, threatening to end the world.

## Additional Observations
# Ghazi
Human, Level 43 Glass Mage known as the Mad Dune mage. Former researcher from [[larracos]] who summoned a being he believed to be [[Yarilo]] and instead brought [[Psamathe]] into the bubble. Trapped in the Sandcastle and housing the ooze/familiar; possesses the [[winding box]] and corresponded with [[council]].

## Additional Observations
# [[Ghazi]]
The lab owner/mage tied to the ooze (creator or handler of the love doll). Present in the scene; his workshop houses the boss core. Looted and apparently killed/crystallized during the collapse, leaving a note about the [[Winding Box]].

## Additional Observations
# [[Ghazi]]
Owner of the sex doll vessel that housed part of a deity's essence; responsible for bringing the doll (and the plotline) into play. Connected to the events that opened the Nothing.

## Additional Observations
# [[Ghazi]]
Referenced as the now-dead "Mad Dune Mage" from the sand castle on the bubble floor who had the last part of the [[Gate of the Feral Gods]]; tied into the possession and sand-ooze subplot involving [[Psamathe]] and other characters.

## Additional Observations
# Ghazi
Author of the [[Letter from the Glass Wizard]] looted from [[Commandant Kane]]'s corpse. The letter warns of a truce request and claims knowledge of opening a portal and cracking the winding box; positions Ghazi as a broader antagonist or crisis catalyst in the lore.

## Additional Observations
# [[Ghazi]]
Human level 43 glass mage known as the Mad Dune Mage and owner/inhabitant of the [[sandcastle]]. Attempted to summon a deity (claimed to seek [[Yarilo]]) and instead unleashed [[Psamathe]] and its ooze familiar. Keeper of the [[winding box]] and target of interrogation by [[dungeon crawler carl]]'s group.

## Additional Observations
# Ghazi
Mage whose workshop and lab are the scene of the encounter. Owner of the object of [[Mrs. Ghazi]]'s affection (a crystallized love doll) and source of the winding box mystery. Ghazi is present during the fight and is later looted/possibly killed during the collapse.

## Additional Observations
# Ghazi
A crawler who possessed the other half of [[Psamathe]] at one point and was manipulated into opening the Nothing. He owned the original doll vessel (the Lika doll) and is implicated in the plot to free the deity half.
