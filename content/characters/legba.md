# legba

• Trickster god Legba appears as a fedora-wearing ghost with a rooster, owner of [[legba]], which grants future smite avoidance [[chance]] and the level-15 spell [[Confusing Fog]] when repaired.
• Repairing the Shrine of Legba, located in the thorn room, grants a 50% [[chance]] to avoid future smites and teaches the level-15 spell [[Confusing Fog]].
• Legba actively tempts party members during the thorn room and tries to bargain for repairs to his shrine.
