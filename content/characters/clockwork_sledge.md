# clockwork_sledge

• Clockwork automaton introduced as a summoned/clockwork construct, referred to as [[Sledge]] or "clockwork [[Sledgie]]".
• Two copies are used in [[Donut]] and [[Carl]]'s plan to trigger teleport traps and create chaos outside the saferoom in [[Alucarda]].
• Named construct/mob targeted previously by [[Lucia Mar]]'s teleport technique.
• Named entity affected by [[an]] earlier teleport/spell tactic, contrasting with [[loop de loop]].
