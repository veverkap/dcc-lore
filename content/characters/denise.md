# denise

• The Feral Goose Mother, a Level 53 Borough Boss, originally from the seventh floor, descended from [[Emperor Anser]]'s people.
• Suppresses magic and protects children obsessively, driven nearly mad by the Nothing.
• Defeated by being shoved into a garbage disposal during the timed [[Borough Boss]] fight in [[Bonnie's House]].
• Known as the "murderous, indestructible goose" boss that attacked the house and killed [[Bonnie]]'s father.
• Brought from another world, her grandfather is [[Emperor Anser]].
• Suppresses magic ("No Magic" effect) and exhibits protective behavior toward children while violently attacking intruders.
• Defeated in this chapter via the kitchen garbage disposal.
• The murderous feral goose boss responsible for major destruction in the chapter.
• Temporarily cut off magic functions with her anti-magic aura.
• Killed the father of [[Bonnie]].
