# lexis

* [[Lexis]] is [[Odette]]'s production assistant, appearing in various roles throughout the story, including:
	+ Briefing [[Carl]] and [[Princess]] [[Donut]] on the rules of the interview and the virtual nature of the studio set
	+ Helping manage the studio and guest transitions
	+ Escorting new guests and handling return procedures
	+ Fetching people from the green room and managing on-set logistics during the broadcast
	+ Conducting captures and cleanups on native settlements
	+ Coordinating timing for [[Odette]] and the show
	+ Arranging seating and logistics in the trailer/studio prior to the broadcast
	+ Monitoring system activity and relaying sensitive intel from [[Odette]]
	+ Interacting with [[Zev]] during the broadcast
	+ Assisting with the broadcast and prompting time-limited interactions with [[Odette]]
	+ Briefing the narrator and [[Donut]] about current events and the format of the interview
	+ Providing information about the Open Intellect Action Network and caprid customs
	+ Entering the live broadcast and helping manage aftermath
	+ Recovering a dropped tablet and noting viewer ratings
