# warlord_stalwart

* Skull Clan leader who disputes the leadership of [[Warlord [[Hortense]] Leadership Unit]] during the battle and questions unfolding events.
* Orc commander piloting a massive mechanized scorpion-like tank that leaps into the battlefield and threatens the south flank.
* Referenced as the brother of [[princess [[formidable]] of the skull empire]] who ascended the throne after the death of their father; named as the current ruler of sections of the [[skull clan]].
* Leader of the [[Bone Clan]] and participant at the warlord council, verbally hostile toward other leaders.
* Leader of the [[skull clan]] (referred to as [[Stalwart]]); physically present during the ambush and killed in a gruesome fashion after being targeted by surrounding war mages.
* Wears a glittering golden crown and physically strikes [[Chaco]] during a council meeting.
* Crown prince of the [[skull clan]] who broadcasts a statement claiming that [[dungeon crawler carl]] and [[Princess Donut]] have been executed in retaliation for sedition.
* Later reported as having his yacht destroyed (rumored to have carried the disgraced [[Prince Maestro]] and [[Queen Consort Ugloo]]) in the unfolding conflict.
* A member of the surface royal family who appears in a public statement; his prepared messaging and presence of troops suggest he was part of a setup.
* His actions (or those surrounding him) factor into the political fallout after [[Manasa]]'s death.
* Crown prince of the [[skull clan]] referenced in the succession discussion and possible disowning that could affect [[Princess [[Formidable]] of the Skull Empire]]'s position.
* Named faction leader referenced in discussions about gear acquisition and faction purchasing behavior on higher floors.
* [[An]] orc noble present at the meeting who is shot with a magical arrow during Juice Box’'s interruption; opposes the NPC team proposal.
* Named orc prince/warrior referenced in faction hierarchy alongside [[Maestro]] and [[King Rust]]; interface labels suggest he may still hold the warlord role despite unexpected appearances.
* Crown prince of the [[skull clan]] who issues a televised statement claiming that a pulse execution against [[dungeon crawler carl]] and [[Princess Donut]] is justified under Syndicate law; later a [[Valtay]] dreadnaught reportedly destroys his yacht, escalating inter-faction conflict.
* A royal figure whose prepared statement and public reaction to the assassination incident makes him a villain in public perception.
* His actions trigger political fallout among [[King Rust]]’'s house and allied factions like the [[Valtay]].
* Referenced exemplar of a high-tier champion/deity-like figure ("juiced-up, almost immortal version") used to illustrate what summoning [[Grull]] might produce.
* A referenced powerful figure of the Skull Empire used as a comparison for what a summoned god-like entity (e.g., [[Grull]]) would resemble in power.
* Crown prince of the Skull Empire referenced in succession discussion; his status influences whether [[Princess [[Formidable]] of the Skull Empire]] is next in line.
* Named figure referenced in discussion about who owns marketplace interfaces and gear; used as a foil in explaining faction and town economies (no direct scene interaction in this chapter).
* Named celebrity/godkiller referenced in [[Carl]]'’s memory for a video he made after killing [[Manasa the singer]], used as a clue to the location of a faction army.
* [[An]] orc leader present at the meeting who is targeted by Juice Box’'s impersonation; he is wounded during the meeting (magical arrow to the eye) and is vocally dismissive of the NPC-team proposal.
* A wealthy orc participant on [[Floor 8]] mentioned among the rich players who could potentially die in the upcoming chaos.
* A wealthy orc crawler on Floor 8 cited as one of the rich, high-profile players who "can really die now" under the floor'’s lethal conditions.
* A named figure whose death was caused by [[Akuma]] and others; his murder is part of the backstory that led to the formation of the [[War Mage Rebellion]].
* [[An]] actor praised by [[King Rust]] for building a large army this season, mentioned as a force contributor whose efforts increased the threat to [[dungeon crawler carl]]'s side.
* Named warlord of the [[orcs]], still a factional power despite [[King Rust]]'s death, referenced to explain ongoing orc political structure.
* War Chief of the Bone Clan whose death was announced by a system message, with his corpse destroyed beyond collection.
* Deceased orc noble whose death precipitated succession events in the [[skull clan]] and motivated actions around the yacht transaction.
* Younger orc boss killed by the [[war mages]] during [[Faction Wars]], distinguished from [[an]] older orc killed earlier by [[Rosetta]].
* Named crawler killed by [[Akuma]] prior to the events in this chapter, with his death tied to the stolen [[Gate of the Feral Gods]].
* Younger orc boss killed during [[Faction Wars]] by the [[War Mage Rebellion]].
* Character referenced as having been killed by [[Akuma]] during the events surrounding the [[War Mage Rebellion]] and the theft of the [[Gate of the Feral Gods]].
