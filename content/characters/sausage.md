# Sausage
Bonnie’s pet pig whose capture at the Bactrian settlement triggered retaliatory bombing. Mentioned as dead earlier in the backstory; memorialized by the “Sausage Memorial Fund Lemonade Stand” in [[Bonnie's House]].

# Sausage
The beloved pet pig of [[Bonnie]]. Referenced as having been taken by the Bactrian camels and as the emotional catalyst for Bonnie’s family actions. Sausage's loss motivates Bonnie and figures into the backstory of the scene.
