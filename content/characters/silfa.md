# silfa

• A level-25 Wisteria Fairy and "Holistic Healer" who heals others, including [[Katia]] and [[Hekla]], and flees into the apartment during the fight.
• A fairy healer associated with [[Brynhild's Daughters]] who is accused of betrayal after [[Hekla]]'s death and is interrogated by [[Carl]].
• A crawler-mage who heals [[Hekla]] during the skirmish and is ordered to leave the train car along with other mages.
• A former baker turned healer whose death and the death of her daughters are tied into the manipulations surrounding [[Eva]].
• A fairy healer who hides in the [[ManTauR apartment]] and is accused by others of betrayal after [[Hekla]]'s death.
