# charles_bartender

* [[Charles]] (bartender), the badger bartender at the club, is verbally harassed by [[Samantha]] and later becomes a victim/target during the demon rampage.
* Level-25 [[Porsuk]] (badger) bartender working at the club's badger-run bar, interacts with [[Samantha]] over a drink order.
* Deceased bartender from the Desperado Club, referenced in party banter, died due to prior floor violence.
* Also called [[Charles]] the bartender or "Chuckie," a [[porsuk]] bartender who is gruesomely killed by [[Samantha]]'s neck cavity during the attack on the club.
* The [[porsuk]] bartender in the Desperado Club who is gruesomely killed during the [[Minge]] attack; referenced throughout as "[[Charles]] the bartender" and later by full name.
* A level-58 [[human]] classed as [[an]] [[Armored Mage]] who appears on the casualty list as dead but was encountered actively in the breach.
* Killed by [[dungeon crawler carl]] during a melee.
