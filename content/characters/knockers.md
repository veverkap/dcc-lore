# knockers

• Small round-faced NPC creatures (level 10-15) wearing yellow hard hats, similar to [[gnomes]]/Bopcas, and manufacturers of illicit product [[Toddler Cocaine]].
• Present in swarming numbers across the factory floor and protective of their product.
• Implicated in the creation/defense of the factory area and its elemental experiments.
