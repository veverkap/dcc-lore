# hellik

* Twin brother of [[Emberus]], god of Sun and Life, accused of murdering [[Geyrun]] and targeted by [[Emberus]]'s Kill quest.
* Brother and enemy of [[Emberus]], worshipped by guards like [[Potsy]] and associated with stained-glass iconography in [[Club Vanquisher]].
* Rival deity worshiped by some [[NPCs]], referenced as a superior god in temple bounty splitting and hostility mechanics against [[Emberus]] worshipers.
* God who aided [[Carl]] by banishing [[Khepri]] and issued quests/ hostility after [[Carl]]'s actions in the temple.
* Central target of [[Emberus]]'s divine quest, killing him reportedly grants a Celestial God Box and may have serious unintended consequences for [[crawlers]].
* God of Sun and Life, twin brother of [[Emberus]], and the subject of a dangerous kill quest discussed by [[Carl]] and [[Quemada]].
* Considered extremely dangerous to attack and referenced in dialogue as having superior catering at parties.
* A powerful deity and brother of [[Emberus]] who is the subject of a kill quest and a central figure in the chapter's narrative tension.
* A named antagonist or target that [[Carl]] must ensure is dead before the eleventh floor ends to avoid receiving a smite related to [[Emberus]].
