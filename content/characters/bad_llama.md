# bad_llama

• A level 3 hostile mob that spits lava and has the debuff "Septic".
• Drops loot, including [[Poor Llama skin]], [[Uncooked Llama steaks]], and [[Baggie of trailer park-grade meth]].
• Can be killed by [[dungeon crawler carl]] with [[an]] assist from [[Princess Donut]].
• Has a weakness in its throat, which can be exploited to cause its head to melt off.
