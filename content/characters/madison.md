# madison

* [[An]] NPC [[Human]] Resources Associate and Non-Combatant rescued from the burning substation, providing bureaucratic/ route information about the Iron Tangle workforce and mentioning entities like the [[Station Mimics]] and [[Kravyad]].
* Manages programs (e.g., using [[Kravyad]] to control workers), enforces productivity measures like the armband scheme, and provides exposition about stations, employee portals, and corporate operations.
* A [[human]] NPC (Level 10) identified as [[an]] Iron Tangle [[Human]] Resources Associate, rescued from a flaming substation, and provides limited lore about station operations, the Station [[Mimics]], and employee procedures.
* Provides exposition about the [[Kravyad]], employee portal mechanics, and station layouts; proud of productivity initiatives and indifferent to worker welfare.
* A referenced NPC/ Crawler who previously mentioned transit security forces; cited in [[Carl]]'s speculation about which guards were stationed at certain stops.
* A [[human]] NPC ([[human]] resources woman) whose death and public display (hanging) was described to [[Fire Brandy]]; [[an]] example of [[NPCs]] beginning to grasp the constructed nature of the dungeon.
* Manages benefits for groups like the [[Kravyad]] and the six [[Station Mimics]].
* Offers (confused) directions about Yard A and station logistics.
* [[An]] Iron Tangle employee NPC who manages the [[Kravyad]] program (a [[human]]-resources initiative).
* Provides exposition about worker exploitation, the [[Kravyad]], and the false employee settlement at station 60; quarrels with [[Brandy]].
* A low-level NPC (Level 10) with corporate attitude; directly interacts with [[dungeon crawler carl]] and others in the cab.
* A [[human]] NPC (the [[human]] resources woman) whose death and public hanging are recounted by [[Fire Brandy]], illustrating NPC awareness and conflict.
