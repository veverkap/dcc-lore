# club_vanquisher

Here is the consolidated markdown list:

* A rival, members-only club in the [[Over City]] described as a more exclusive, country-club-style venue compared to the [[desperado club]].
* A parallel social venue that appears at transfer stations ending in "9"; referenced as an alternate club type to [[desperado club]].
* A well-kept, [[church]]-liked building that serves as a club entrance at [[Station 149]]; a ram-headed cleric emerges from it during the chapter.
* A nightclub connected via [[Station 59]]'s [[church]] exit; part of the transfer station complex and local social hub.
* A location/zone on the floor mentioned as hosting a Meat Shields mercenary market (alternative to [[desperado club]]).
* A members-only club location entered by [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]] in the chapter; contains a stained-glass window depicting a deity, robed ram-headed cleric guards, and internal areas including the [[Emberus High [[Temple]] of the Ending Sun]] and guild/market corridors.
* A club location some crawlers access; [[Donut]] cannot currently access [[Club Vanquisher]] and thus cannot easily collect her reward from a [[temple]].
* A [[subterranean]] club complex—includes the buried [[temple]] entrance in some seasons—identified by [[Rosetta]] as the most likely hiding place for [[Princess Vinata]] and her guards during [[Faction Wars]].
* A central location on many floors (notably floors 3-11) that commonly contains the strongest [[temple]] on the floor; many light-god temples, including temples of [[Theia]] and others, are located inside this club, making it a frequent locus for deity-based transfers and the [[Travel the Path]] spell.
* A nightclub in [[Pandinus]] that was the site of a recent violent incident involving [[Prepotente]] and a hellspawn familiar; currently closed for cleansing.
* A location mentioned in backstory as the place where Carl acquired the ring from Pater Coal (the disguised strength ring).
* A strip-club/[[guildhall]] setting where much of the chapter opens and the [[portal]]/[[temple]] exit is located; houses the [[Mercenary Arcade]], a [[portal]] guarded by the wisp [[Yasmine]], and club staff like [[Doctor Bones]].
* A club location containing shops such as the [[Blessed Sun Enchantments shop]]; scene for the initial ring transaction with the [[Satyr Jeweler]] and [[Pater Coal]].
* A location accessed through the [[temple]] of [[Kuraokami]]; functions as a club and a conduit for certain NPCs (ram clerics, mantaur guards) to appear in the area.
* A club located in [[Hungry Eyes Vendor Village]] identified by a black "X" game notification over its entrance; the narrator notes they are permanently banned from this establishment.
* A club/venue adjacent to [[Yemaya]]'s [[temple]]; [[Yemaya]] directs the party to get inside the Club Vanquisher as she departs.
* A members-only entertainment venue and hub encountered by the protagonist; features a robed ram-headed guardian, stained-glass imagery, multiple [[stairwells]], guild desks, market access, and internal temples (including the [[Emberus High [[Temple]] of the Ending Sun]]).
* A club location normally accessible to Donut; currently inaccessible to her in this chapter, complicating access to [[temple]] rewards or gatherings.
* A location referenced as the site of a past battle where [[Zhang]] survived and received the [[Target Cape]] from the system AI.
* A location referenced as the site containing Khepri's never-ending [[temple]] and feast table; source of the beetles used in the floor's design.
* A complex containing the [[Basilica]] and tied to the [[Ascendency]] battle mechanics referenced by characters.
