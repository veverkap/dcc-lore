# magistrates_quarters

• The Magistrate's quarters is a location that was partially destroyed by Carl's explosion.
• It contains the reception, the Magistrate's bedroom (where Magistrate Featherfall's corpse was found), and [[mall]]-level shops below.
• The building is also home to a krasue [[nest]] and a major combat encounter.
• The Magistrate's residence and office building has a reception office, a [[mall]]-level below, sleeping quarters, and a horrific bone hoop room where Magistrate Featherfall was found.
• The building is a [[target]] for the team's objectives and is separate from the Apito Education Center operations.
