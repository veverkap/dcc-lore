# fifteenth_floor

• A [[dungeon]] floor where Reaper Spider Minions are commonly found.
• Inhabited by janitor mobs like the [[Moon Reaper]], which originate from this domain.
• Intended destination of [[Amayon]] once he ascends, with potential fallout if war spills outside the current floor.
• One of the three levels where inhabitants of [[the Nothing]] were unleashed, with limited visibility.
