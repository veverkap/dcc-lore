# Bubble Summoning Patch
System announcement and policy update (driven by legal action and the [[Syndicate]]) that permits the bubble-escape method to continue while blocking some secondary summonings and changes the odds of feral gods summoning corresponding deities (notably altering the probability discussed in the announcement).
