# This is How the Gristle Grinds
A mandatory, no-opt-out team quest (system event) that supersedes other events on the [[ninth floor]]. It announces the arrival of [[Emberus]], sets the stakes (imminent floor destruction), enforces faction-wars as the required activity, and attaches specific rewards for survivors.
