# Danger Zone with Ripper Wonton
Live broadcast panel show (studio) where [[dungeon crawler carl]] and [[Princess Donut]] are guest panelists. The program features a round table, audience Q&A, holographic guest seats (e.g., [[Princess D'nadia]]), and the host [[Ripper Wonton]]. The studio experiences an on-air interruption when [[Manasa]]'s trailer is hit.

# Danger Zone with Ripper Wonton
Live studio talk program where [[dungeon crawler carl]] and [[Princess Donut]] appear as panelists. Hosted by [[Ripper Wonton]] and featuring holographic and tunneled guests (e.g., [[Princess D’nadia]], [[Hekla]]). The show experiences a mid-broadcast interrupt when a pulse attack kills guest [[Manasa]].
