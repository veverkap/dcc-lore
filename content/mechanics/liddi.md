# Liddi
Female host of the [[Veriluxx New Toys Showcase]] (also referred to as "Professor Liddi" during the taping). Dresses in a mismatched superhero costume, claims to have helped bring the [[Robot Donut]] toy to life, and interacts with [[dungeon crawler carl]] and [[Robot Donut]] on-camera.
