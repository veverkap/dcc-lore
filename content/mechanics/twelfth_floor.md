# twelfth_floor

Here is the consolidated markdown list:

* The Twelfth Floor is a significant location in the [[dungeon]], housing behemoths like the [[Divine Guardian]] and serving as a threshold to lower floors.
* It is also referenced as the level where gods become vulnerable, important in deity-summoning rules and Celestial [[Ascendency]] mechanics.
* The Twelfth Floor is a destination mentioned in the quest completion text where [[Orthrus]] will resume training.
* It is a future [[target]] in [[Eileithyia]]'s promise, offering survival and assistance up to the Twelfth Floor and a role in claiming the Celestial Throne.
* The Twelfth Floor is a designated [[dungeon]] milestone referenced in deity quest constraints, and is the next descent [[target]] for crawlers who continue.
* It is also mentioned as the next major threshold where [[Ascendency]] battles begin, representing a larger prize and different dangers for parties that continue.
* The Twelfth Floor is a chaotic floor where outworlders were placed into god-like bodies, described as having non-sponsored gods acting strangely.
* It is the location where the Crawlers have advanced and where the Halls of the [[Ascendency]] and surrounding Cotton Fields are located, central to the [[Ascendency]] broadcast.
* The Twelfth Floor is a high floor referenced as the location of the halls of the Celestial [[Ascendency]], a place of divine and mythic strangeness.
* It is also mentioned as the season-end floor where the Macro AI is typically bonkers and where gods lose [[invulnerability]] during the Celestial [[Ascendency]].
* The Twelfth Floor is a future floor goal referenced by [[Eileithyia]] in her promise, ensuring survival to the Twelfth Floor where [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]], [[Katia]], and the cat will help claim the Celestial Throne.
* It is also referenced as the next major threshold where [[Ascendency]] battles begin, representing a larger prize and different dangers for parties that continue.
* The Twelfth Floor is a problematic floor where the [[Nothing]] has affected outworlders and gods, referenced as having converted many into bodies of gods with strange behavior.
* It is the current [[dungeon]] floor where [[Carl]] and survivors decide to stall [[Ascendency]] wins and where [[Scolopendra]]'s transformation occurs, with the objective of keeping others from claiming victory here.
* The Twelfth Floor is the play-floor where the Crawlers had gone and where the broadcast feed centered on the Halls of the [[Ascendency]] and surrounding Cotton Fields, site of the [[Ascendency]] events.
