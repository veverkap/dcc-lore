# jikininki_ghoul

• An undead mob type encountered at the door, described as tall, skeletal creatures with chattering teeth, identified at level 25.
• Similar to janitor mobs from the [[Iron Tangle]].
• A hazard to flesh-eating pets like [[Rend]].
