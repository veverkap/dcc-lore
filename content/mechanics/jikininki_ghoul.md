# Jikininki ghoul
An undead mob type encountered at the door: described as tall, skeletal creatures with chattering teeth and identified at level 25. Similar to janitor mobs from the [[Iron Tangle]] and noted as a hazard to flesh-eating pets like [[Rend]].
