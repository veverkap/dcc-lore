# Krasue — Death & Damage Rule
System/dungeon rule associated with [[Krasue]] mobs: you may kill the head and trailing organs to fend her off, but the mob is not truly dead until you find and destroy the rest of her corpse; you will not earn experience until the mob is "dead-dead." Warning: ghost-class mobs (like [[Krasue]]) are only injured by magic or magical items.
