# krasue__death__damage_rule

• You can kill the head and trailing organs to fend off a Krasue mob, but the mob is not truly dead until you find and destroy the rest of her corpse.
• You will not earn experience until the mob is "dead-dead".
• Ghost-class mobs like Krasue are only injured by magic or magical items.
