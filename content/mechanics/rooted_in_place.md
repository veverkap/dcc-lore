# rooted_in_place

• A system status/mechanic that immobilizes the caster and is accompanied by a visible countdown timer, making the caster vulnerable, when casting certain spells (e.g., Donut during Minion Army).
• A status/system indicator that appears above a caster (e.g., Donut) to show they are immobilized and vulnerable while finishing a spell cast, treated as a game mechanic that affects movement and vulnerability.
