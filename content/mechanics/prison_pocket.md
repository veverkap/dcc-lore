# Prison Pocket
A named Level 170 province boss introduced as a giant, fur-covered marsupial (scientific/name tag shown as Macropus Dominus). [[Prison Pocket]] sits in the road as a gatekeeper, blocking racers with a pouch full of small, mostly-dead joeys and secreting a caustic pouch oil. It heals when it consumes wounded creatures from its pouch and is paired across the road with the boss [[Party Foul]].
