# nothing

• The [[Wheel of Fortune]] spot previously functioned as a dimensional exit, but is now altered/broken and central to [[Carl]]'s extraction plan.
• The "Nothing" spot on the wheel is a dangerous, lore-heavy void that previously caused the real "Nothing" effect (driving people insane or removing them).
• The term "Nothing" is presented in quotes on the wheel and treated as an explicit game outcome.
• [[Nothing]] is a locale where different terminology is used (e.g., "flap-jacking"), appearing to be an alternate region or plane known to some characters.
