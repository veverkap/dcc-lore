# Nothing
Dangerous, lore-heavy void/spot on the [[Wheel of Fortune]] that previously caused the real "Nothing" effect (driving people insane or removing them). The chapter centers on whether the wheel's current Nothing slice is genuine, a trap, or part of a scam; the term is presented in quotes on the wheel and treated as an explicit game outcome.
