# Per-Quadrant Mob Cap (5,000)
An explicit dungeon rule limiting the maximum number of spawned mobs in a quadrant to 5,000. The chapter states the party has already hit this cap, explaining the massive grub presence and why killing corpses would continue to generate more mobs until the cap is reached.
