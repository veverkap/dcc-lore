# System Announcement: "Congratulations, Murderer..."
The in-game System Announcement that informs the player that the [[Ring of Divine Suffering]] has leveled up, now granting two stat points per kill. Triggers visible progression feedback (Carl gaining stats and a level) and frames the chapter's opening.
