# transit_station

• Small passenger/transit areas on the subway/tracks where the party encounters a swarm of [[Babababoon]] mobs.
• Normally considered safe zones but become unsafe when mobs suffering from [[DTs]] appear.
• A small, enclosed station area with dozens of [[Babababoon]] mobs, a general store with barred windows, and leads onto the [[Nightmare]] tracks.
• Contains a general store with a mole-man proprietor and [[loot]].
