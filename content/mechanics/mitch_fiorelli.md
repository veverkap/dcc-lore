# Mitch Fiorelli
Level 120 croupier for the [[Wheel of Fortune]] in the [[Desperado Club]]; brusque dealer who enforces the game's rules and demands payment (and tips) for letting people jump into the Nothing spot. His in-game description notes "This is an Enforcer for the <Redacted>."
