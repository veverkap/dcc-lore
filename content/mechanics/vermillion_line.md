# vermillion_line

* The Vermillion Line is a subway/[[train]] line that runs across many numbered stations, including [[Station 101]], [[Station 60]], [[station 433 terminus]], and [[station 435 kravyad station]].
* The line is one of the few still with power and is the route the rescue [[train]] will travel.
* The Vermillion Line is the focus of the rescue plan, with Hekla's crew planning to run a reversed-engine [[train]] down the line to pick up trapped crawlers at [[Station 101]].
* The line is also the site of the large trapped group to be rescued, with multiple stops referenced in the chapter, including [[Station 60]], [[Station 72]], [[Station 75]], and [[Station 101]].
* The Vermillion Line is a central part of Carl's plan to run to the end of the line for distribution of [[Escape Hats]].
* The line is also the site of a damaged engine used in [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]]'s bomb plan and where waves of monsters advanced.
* The Vermillion Line is a colored subway line that can run the whole line in about a day, and is central to the chapter's operation, with [[Station 101]] located on the line.
