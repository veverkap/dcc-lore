# Special Event: Boss Battle Stream (Beta Opt-in)
System mechanic that overlays a live broadcast and commentator feed onto the boss encounter with [[Lusca]], opt-ing players into a beta testing program and streaming the fight to subscribers. Introduces new UI elements (hosts [[Kevin]] and [[Magnificent Troy]], sponsor-style commentary) and changes pacing and presentation of the battle.
