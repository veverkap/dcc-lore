# gnomish_throne_room

• The Gnomish throne room is a location.
• It is a small, floating house/structure held aloft after the crashing of the [[Wasteland]].
• The focus of [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]]'s crew is to capture and retrieve a [[Pocket Watch]] piece from the throne room.
