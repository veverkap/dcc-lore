# ghoul

• Generic term for festering, conveyor-bound monsters being loaded into carts in robot rooms and sent along the coaster/conveyor to be disposed of; central to multiple outbreaks throughout the stations.
• Hired mercenary type used by [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]]'s group; several ghoul mercenaries die during the [[heat]], triggering system warnings.
• Common undead mob streaming from multiple stations; central antagonistic force besieging platforms and the pit/[[abyss]] throughout the chapter.
• Mob used by the [[truck]] team: two ghouls are deployed in cages to throw offensive potion balls and create defensive barriers during the race.
