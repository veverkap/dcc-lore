# synergy_tower

• A long-range structure suspected of containing a war mage and generating the green death-ray beam that struck the level's invisible ceiling near [[Party Planner]].
• A [[tower]]-like asset whose effect can upgrade Atrocity spells and increase their area, enabling a wider-apply of [[War Crime]] and part of the tactical calculus choosing [[Bijanbi]] over [[Hamzo]].
