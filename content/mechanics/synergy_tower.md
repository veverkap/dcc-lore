# Synergy tower
A tower-like asset whose effect can upgrade Atrocity spells and increase their area. The chapter references the [[Synergy tower]] as enabling a wider-area application of [[War Crime]] and as part of the tactical calculus choosing [[Bijanbi]] over [[Hamzo]].
