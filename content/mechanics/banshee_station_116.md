# Banshee Station 116
A transfer-station platform on the dungeon's Yellow Line where the party fights waves of rabbit-like mobs and encounters tunnels, waiting rooms, and the passage to the larger tunnel system. The station is the immediate setting for the group's clearing operations and links to other numbered stations such as [[Station 120]], [[Station 127]], and [[Station 131]].
