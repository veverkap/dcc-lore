# banshee_station_116

* The transfer- station [[platform]] on the Yellow Line where the party fights waves of Red Cornet mobs and encounters [[tunnels]], waiting rooms, and the passage to the larger [[tunnel]] system.
* The [[platform]]/transfer station where the chapter's exploration takes place, containing multiple exits, [[tunnels]], waiting rooms, [[train]] [[platform]], and the [[warehouse]] room with two Dwarven Industrial Light-Duty Automatons.
* The station is the immediate setting for the group's clearing operations and links to other numbered stations such as Station 120, Station 127, and Station 131.
* The [[platform]] where the party explores [[tunnels]], platforms, and a boss-like chamber, with multiple exits, frequent monster traffic (notably Red Cornet mobs), nearby [[tunnels]] leading to a cluster of rooms and a separate [[warehouse]]-like chamber with two Dwarven Industrial Light-Duty Automatons.
* The stop is associated with dangerous monsters and was explicitly named in the chapter announcements.
* The [[platform]] where the party fights Red Cornet mobs, discovers the robot [[warehouse]], and clears waiting rooms of Cornets.
* The location of the Prize Case trap incident aftermath and the source of the Cornets that board shortly thereafter.
