# station_48

* Station 48 is identical to Station 24 and Station 36.
* It is a location of a province boss that screams audibly from a distance.
* The boss fight is considered too powerful for current forces and is avoided by most groups.
* The station is associated with a boss encounter where Festering Ghouls aggregate and [[fuse]] into the room-filling Province Boss (Stop 48).
* The boss fight is shown on the recap, where a group of twenty crawlers were overwhelmed by a [[ghoul]] conglomeration.
* The station is a critical stop where Festering [[Ghoul]] clusters [[fuse]] into a giant province boss that completely fills the room, yielding an achievement.
* It is a home to a province-level boss and a [[target]] for proposed raiding teams.
* The station is shown in the recap where a group of twenty crawlers foolishly fought a powerful station boss and were massacred.
* It is a [[stairwell]] [[portal]] destination named in the Iron Tangle System Announcement as an escape option for customers.
