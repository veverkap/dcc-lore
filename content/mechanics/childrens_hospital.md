# childrens_hospital

• The Children's Hospital is a pediatric ward with Icelandic signage, cribs, nurses, and medical machines.
• This is where [[Katia]]'s pivotal memory occurs.
• [[Annie (baby)]] was cared for and later died in one of the hospital's rooms.
