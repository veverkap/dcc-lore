# Warning: This room is now sealed. Nobody will be allowed to enter or leave until the conclusion of the party. You are protected from outside influence or spells.
A System Announcement that activates a protective seal on the [[Queen's ballroom]] (and likely adjacent rooms) during the talent portion. This mechanic prevents entry/exit and blocks outside influence or spells, directly impacting [[Samantha]]'s infiltration, the team's escape plans, and how [[dungeon crawler carl]] deploys items like [[Feather Fall potions]].
