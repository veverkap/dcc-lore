# mad_dune_mage

• Acts as a regional boss in the sand [[quadrant]] and motivates part of the assault planning.
• Named antagonist mentioned as an active threat on the floor, separate from gnomes and changelings, with unknown capabilities.
• A dangerous mage seeking to study and replicate a [[gate]] artifact; allegedly possesses a third of the artifact/[[pocket]]-watch set.
• Primary remaining objective for the protagonists.
• Named enemy whose body turned to sand; left behind a multi-page note concerning the [[Winding Box]], [[pocket watch]], and plans to prevent misuse by the feral gods.
• A named adversary from past events who was tricked by [[Samantha]] during an escape from [[the Nothing]].
• A named adversarial presence on the ground mentioned among threats on the floor; context implies a dangerous NPC or local boss.
• Pursues a portion of the artifact; described as having one-third of the item that relates to the [[Gate of the Feral Gods]].
• Antagonistic NPC whom [[Katia]] fought and looted; author of the note describing the mechanics of the [[Winding Box]] and the dangers of opening [[portals]] to the [[Nothing]].
