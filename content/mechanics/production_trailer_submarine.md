# Production Trailer (Submarine)
The deluxe rental trailer in which [[dungeon crawler carl]] and [[Donut]] are hosted; a submerged, off-planet celebrity trailer with an exterior floodlight, picture window to the ocean floor, kitchen shelves, makeup table, and studio portal.
