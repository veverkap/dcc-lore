# Deity Summoning Rules
Cookbook/system rules summarized in-chat and by [[Mordecai]]: three summoning types (celestial boon, indentured summoning, involuntary summoning), deities' near-invulnerability off the 12th floor, and duration formulas for involuntary summons (vessel level + deity level seconds). Central mechanic explaining risk of summoning gods like [[Grull]].

# Deity Summoning Rules
Cookbook-derived dungeon rules describing how deities behave when summoned: three summoning types (celestial boons, indentured summons, involuntary summons), gods' invulnerability except on the [[Twelfth Floor]] and above, vessel-based summoning durations (summoner-vessel level + god level), growth behavior (deity expands to fill chamber), and notes about removing alien "Soul Armor" with spells like [[Take That Shit Off]] and [[Laundry Day]].
