# deity_summoning_rules

• Celestial boons, indentured summonings, and involuntary summonings are the three types of deity summoning.
• Deities are near-invulnerable off the 12th floor.
• Involuntary summoning duration is calculated as vessel level + deity level in seconds.
• Gods function as "Soul Armor" and can be removed by spells like [[Take That Shit Off]] or [[Laundry Day]] under specific conditions.
• [[Grull]] is an example of a deity that can be summoned.
