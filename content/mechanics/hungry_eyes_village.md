# hungry_eyes_village

• A mapped dot/settlement appearing near the race finish area, visible on the party's GPS as they approach the end of the race.
• A [[Dungeon]] floor/zone entered via a [[garage]] door, described as a suburban [[cul-de-sac]] with nine numbered garages, a painted skull door, blue sky with a red sun, and a [[portal]] to a neon-filled settlement.
• A village label that appears on the team's GPS/map as they exit the cave and approach the race finish, one of the named points plotted near the end of the race.
• The race zone/[[arena]] where the chapter takes place, described as a [[cul-de-sac]] suburb with nine garages (one marked with a skull), tall trees, a red sun, and the signature Team Roundabout area.
• The market/settlement where the chapter's events occur, a street lined with vendors, restaurants, and temples, where the group of racers gather near the [[temple]] leading to [[Club Vanquisher]].
• The village location where the Pineapple Cabaret's farewell gathering occurs, containing the main square, garages, and the guild common room referenced throughout the chapter.
