# the_bubbles

• The Bubbles are a location composed of 1,172 individual bubble worlds, each containing four quadrants and multiple castles.
• The Bubbles are a transparent, ceiling-like enclosure over a bowl containing floating balloons, flying houses, and magical protections.
• The Bubble is a contained play-area/environment that the party inhabits, referenced when observing distant zones and the campaign's geographic limits.
• The Fifth Floor - The Bubbles is the entire floor setting, themed as "The Bubbles", with 1,172 self-contained bubble worlds, each split into four quadrants ([[Land]], [[Sea]], [[Air]], [[Subterranean]]) and containing four castles.
