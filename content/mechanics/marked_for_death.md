# Marked for Death
Skill/mechanic granted by the [[Enchanted Night Wyrm’s Ring of Divine Suffering]]. Allows the wielder to mark 100%‑health crawlers on the map; after 30 seconds the mark becomes active and killing the marked crawler grants permanent stat points (scaling: +1 per three marks increases). Contains explicit floor-based cooldowns and restrictions (e.g., special rules on Scolopendra Lair floors).

# Marked for Death
An infamous dungeon mechanic accessible via the [[Enchanted Night Wyrm’s Ring of Divine Suffering]]. Rules include selecting only full-health crawlers within map range, a 30-second mark formation delay, permanent stat gains when marked targets die, and floor-dependent cooldowns and restrictions (detailed cooldowns and exceptions for certain floors).
