# marked_for_death

• A visible system status marker (an X with acorns) that appears over targets, indicating targeting/priority by bosses or special rules.
• A system-applyed status where a [[Crawler]] is flagged as "marked for death by all deities" following the slaying of a goddess, creating global divine hostility and consequences.
• A skill/mechanic granted by the [[Enchanted Night Wyrm’s Ring of Divine Suffering]], allowing the wielder to mark 100% health crawlers on the map, with killing the marked [[crawler]] granting permanent stat points.
• A [[dungeon]] mechanic accessible via the [[Enchanted Night Wyrm’s Ring of Divine Suffering]], with rules including selecting only full-health crawlers, a 30-second mark formation delay, permanent stat gains, and floor-dependent cooldowns and restrictions.
• A visible system marker above characters (a symbol over [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]] and later above [[queen imogen of the high elves]]), denoting a special targeting/status effect from the [[Dungeon]] System and invoked during boss-state announcements.
• A system-applyed condition described when [[Crawler Paz Lo]] slays [[Ysalte]], indicating he "has been marked for death by all deities" except sworn enemies of the deceased.
