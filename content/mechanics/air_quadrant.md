# air_quadrant

• One of four possible [[quadrant]] assignments for entry to the [[Fifth Floor]] determined by the wheel spin, dictating aerial- themed challenges and the available opponent set.
• Determines environmental traits and likely types of encounters for that portion of the floor.
• The [[quadrant]] where the party spawns in [[Bubble #543]], dominated by high [[towers]], aerial movement, and conflicts with the [[Air]] castle.
• The upper zone of the multi-[[quadrant]] [[Necropolis]] system, under sail-style protection and subject to anti-[[air]] defenses.
• A [[quadrant]] of the [[Wasteland]], referenced in the Bubble Notification, where liberation of the Commandant's quarters grants travel privileges to crawlers.
• The [[quadrant]] assigned to Carl's team by the first wheel, determining entry to the fifth floor via aerial-themed regions.
• The aerial sector of the [[necropolis]]/level, featuring anti-[[air]] defenses, sails, and flying creatures like [[Ruckus]], where [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]] plans and tests missiles.
