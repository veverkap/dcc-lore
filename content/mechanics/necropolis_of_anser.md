# necropolis_of_anser

• The [[Necropolis]] of Anser is a massive tomb structure underlying the bubble and the broader locale, hosting a three-way stalemate between the Dromedarians, Bactrians, and gnomes.
• It is a [[subterranean]] catacomb complex revealed by a map/scroll in the [[Town]] Hall trap room, with sprawling maze-like tomb areas whose plans were added to party maps.
• The complex contains crypts, tomb raiders, gnomes, and the looming need for bombing support, with Carl's operations focused on its scale and elevation gauges.
• The [[Necropolis]] is tied to Shamus Chaindrive's treasure-hunting lore and rumored to contain the [[Gate]] of the Feral Gods.
• It is a regional high-stakes location referenced as the buried site of powerful artifacts and the ghost Queen Quetzalcoatlus, central to multiple characters' quests.
• The [[Necropolis]] includes the flooded water [[quadrant]], the submerged [[Akula]] submarine, and the ruined castle, with distinct quadrants and a final castle as the [[target]] location of the party's current campaign.
