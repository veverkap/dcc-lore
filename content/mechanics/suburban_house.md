# Suburban House
A reconstituted, tilted suburban home that arrived with a magical balloon and housed the family (Jennifer and [[Dale]]). Scene of the boss fight with [[Denise]]; contains master bedroom, garage (with workshop), and Bonnie's room. Will be used as a flying party-house.
