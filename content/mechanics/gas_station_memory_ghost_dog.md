# Gas Station (memory ghost dog)
A roadside gas station on the route north where [[dungeon crawler carl]] earlier encountered a memory-ghost dog; he asks [[orren the liaison]] to be sent there briefly before returning to the floor. Represents a recurring touchpoint from earlier crawl events.
