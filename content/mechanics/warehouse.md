# warehouse

• Site of the assassination/detonation setup tied to [[Remex the Grand]], where [[Hobgoblin pus]] and [[Dynamite]] were used; central to the contested event footage shown on the recap program.
• Building used by crawlers to spin wheels and claim [[land]] quadrants; featured in the recap where [[Lucia Mar]] is assigned a cold, stepped-pyramid [[quadrant]].
• Initial scene of the chapter where the glowing case and [[Carl's Doomsday Scenario]] are found; contains deactivated armor and swordsmen guards after [[Remex the Grand]]'s scream and subsequent EMP-like effect.
• Site of the detonator/dynamite event discussed in the recap where the magical detonator ([[Hobgoblin]] pus) was used and multiple guards were killed.
• Large building used in the televised recap where crawlers spin wheels to claim [[land]] quadrants and where the [[ghoul]]-station and [[stairwell]] scenes (involving [[Lucia Mar]], [[Ifechi]], and [[Florin]]) were depicted; several [[crawler]] [[land]]-quadrants (bubbles) are described emerging from warehouse spins.
