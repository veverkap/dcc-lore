# Town Rules (Ursine settlement poster)
Explicit list of settlement rules posted on the covered bridge that functions as binding dungeon law for the [[Large Ursine Settlement]]. Rules include prohibitions (no fighting, no alcohol, no magic on the streets, bans on Macaques/Naiads/dinosaurs, bugbears must bathe, etc.) and a stated immediate decapitation penalty for violations.
