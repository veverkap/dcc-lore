# town_rules_ursine_settlement_poster

• Explicit list of settlement rules posted on the covered bridge, binding [[dungeon]] law for the [[Large Ursine Settlement]].
• Prohibitions include: no fighting, no alcohol, no magic on the streets, bans on Macaques, Naiads, and dinosaurs.
• Bugbears must bathe.
• Immediate decapitation penalty for violations.
