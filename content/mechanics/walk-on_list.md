# walk-on_list

• A recurring industry practice hiring trackers to abduct and deliver native survivors as spontaneous cast additions, exploited by factions like [[borant corporation]] and operatives such as [[Sadir]].
• A procedural concept mentioned in the broadcast, presenting rules about humans who did not descend into the [[dungeon]] and how non-crawlers are handled by production.
• A controversial roster/listing that allows NPCs or others to "walk on" into the competition, sparking debate about the list's validity and ethics.
• A private list of desired natives or survivors that producers and sponsors covertly contract trackers to abduct from a planet after collection, illegal but widely used to procure drama and specific people for later seasons.
• A named production policy mentioned in the censored video segment, tied to the treatment of humans who did not enter the [[dungeon]] and the illegal practice of [[bounty]] hunters locating survivors to sell them to production.
