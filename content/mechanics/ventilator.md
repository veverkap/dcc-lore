# Ventilator
The [[Ventilator]] is a class-C rapid-attack battle cruiser (a Naga warship) where [[Chandra]] conducts legal work and meetings. It sustained heavy damage in the coup that elevated the [[Sultan Protectorate of the New Reformer]] and serves as the immediate setting for the chapter’s negotiations.
