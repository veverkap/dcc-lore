# ventilator

• The [[Ventilator]] is a class-C rapid-attack battle cruiser (a Naga warship) where [[Chandra]] conducts legal work and meetings.
• It sustained heavy damage in the coup that elevated the [[Sultan Protectorate of the New Reformer]] and serves as the immediate setting for the chapter's negotiations.
• [[Princess Chandra Esquire]] conducts her legal work and meets with [[Princess Donut]] at the [[Ventilator]].
