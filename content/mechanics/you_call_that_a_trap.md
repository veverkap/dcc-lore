# [[You call that a trap?]]
An achievement unlocked by [[dungeon crawler carl]] (and [[Donut]]) for injuring a mob via a deliberately placed dungeon hazard. Reward: a [[Gold Sapper's Box]] containing trap-construction tools and [[Proximity Trigger]]s.
