# Production trailer (dignitary trailer)
Rented, well-appointed trailer used to host dignitaries and non-crawlers; contains advanced cleaning facilities ([[All-purpose cleaner (cleaning machine)]]) and a studio for [[Danger Zone with Ripper Wonton]]. [[D-0NAH]] manages it.
