# soothed

• Reduces debuff effectiveness by 10%
• Lessens pain
• Triggered by a healer's calming touch
• Activated on [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]] in the cart: "You have been Soothed! Debuffs are 10% less effective."
• Applied by [[Miriam Dom]] to reduce the impact of active debuffs
