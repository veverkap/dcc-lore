# three_cheers_for_slaughter

• An in-game achievement awarded to Carl for killing three boss monsters with the same attack, granting a [[Platinum Big Daddy Box]] and appearing as a system announcement.
• The achievement is earned while aboard the [[Nightmare Express]].
