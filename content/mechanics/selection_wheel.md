# Selection Wheel
A giant spinning wheel in the garage on [[Don't come in last]] that randomly presents dozens of vehicle silhouettes (school bus, Hummer, Zamboni, box truck, etc.) and determines which vehicle each team receives for a heat. It visibly interacts with the start timer and the floor's staging.
