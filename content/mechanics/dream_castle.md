# dream_castle

• Home base of the [[dream]] which was left under-protected, a tactical error exploited by forces like [[elle mcgib]] and noted by [[Rosetta]].
• Enemy castle under assault whose shields were reported down and whose remaining elves were beginning to flee; also the site impacted indirectly by the fight between [[the feral god meatus has entered the realm]] and [[Harpocrates]].
• Castle near which [[Epitome Tagg]] was observed fleeing; involved in concurrent fighting and strategic movements.
• Battle site heavily damaged in the conflict; gods and major characters (including [[Eris]] and others) are engaged near or inside this location.
• Location beside which the massive pile of summoned witnesses was formed; many named NPCs and players (e.g., [[Katia]], [[elle mcgib]]) are trapped in the pile near this castle.
• Castle housing a digger/mech asset assigned by [[Juice Box]]; its digger is proposed as a distraction for [[Harpocrates]].
• Location still occupied by NPCs mentioned by [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]]; it houses defenders who are expected to recapture the throne room within hours.
• Remains of the [[stronghold]] referenced as being secured by [[Tran]] and the [[102nd bulwark battalion]] while the rescue proceeds.
• Castle controlled by [[Tagg]]/the Dream that will trigger [[Epitome Tagg]]'s death/forfeiture rules when taken by hostile forces; central to the timer-driven rescue problem.
