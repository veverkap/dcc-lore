# blood_trail

• A tracking-style debuff that causes ongoing health seepage and leaves a magical bleed scent enabling hunters/collectors to track the victim.
• Can be applied to ranged attacks and persists even while healing.
• Causes [[slow]] steady health loss.
• Tracks the [[target]] by continuing to produce open wounds/odor.
• Makes the bearer easy to track.
• Applied to [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]] in the chapter and referenced as an active system effect.
