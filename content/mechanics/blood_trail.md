# Blood Trail
A tracking-style debuff that causes ongoing health seepage and leaves a magical bleed scent enabling hunters/collectors to track the victim. Can be applied to ranged attacks and persists even while healing.

# Blood Trail
A named debuff/status effect described in-system: causes slow steady health loss, tracks the target by continuing to produce open wounds/odor, and makes the bearer easy to track. Applied to [[dungeon crawler carl]] in the chapter and referenced as an active system effect.
