• Referred to as "the [[trainyard]]" by [[Fire Brandy]], a reachable option on the track switch that allows for "Station Repair" and a suggested destination for routing the [[train]] to a repair yard.
• Referenced as the "[[trainyard]]" or repair station option by [[Fire Brandy]].

• The yard [[platform]] referenced by [[Vernon]] where loop cycles begin/and for the crew.
• Stepping off at station 10 is described as putting crew back into the loop's start point.
• The yard [[platform]] where [[Vernon]] regains consciousness after the line resets.
• Part of the crew's looping experience.
• Used as a temporal anchor in his story.
