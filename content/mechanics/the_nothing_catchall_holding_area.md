# The Nothing (catchall holding area)
A pocket-dimension storage area used by floor designers to place problematic entities in stasis. Functions like an inventory for later retrieval and is a fallback for non-escapeable races or NPCs (discussed with [[Rosetta]], [[Mordecai]], and [[Tipid]]).
