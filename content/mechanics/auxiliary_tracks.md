# auxiliary_tracks

• One of the labeled track options at the switching cavern, leading into a tangle of additional switching choices and alternate [[train]] lines.
• A long track that enters the root system on the far side of the [[portal]], allowing jumping onto different tracks without passing through the gateway, but subject to the last-used track switches.
