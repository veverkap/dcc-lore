# martyrs_path

• Eighth-floor-only Path benefit that activates when a player drops below 5% health, engulfing their footsteps in flames and granting martyr-style effects.
• Triggers a fire effect on the user's footsteps when the user drops below a health threshold.
• System-announced as having "engulfed your steps."
