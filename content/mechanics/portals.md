# portals

• A core [[dungeon]] mechanic with many varieties, including two-way [[portal]] carts, scoop portals, [[teleport]] traps, and one-touch floor portals.
• Exhibits inconsistent behaviors, such as size requirements, one-way/two-way, and tangling ropes.
• Can act like doors, require touch or being pulled, and have button-sized openings that lead to monster dens.
• Varies by brand/tuning, such as [[trainyard]] tuning and [[abyss]] selection.
• [[Abyss]] is central to moving trains between yards and the [[Abyss]].
