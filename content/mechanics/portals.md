# Portals
A core dungeon mechanic with many varieties: two-way portal carts, scoop portals, teleport traps, and one-touch floor portals. Explained in the cookbook note (Crawler Milk) and central to moving trains between yards and the [[Abyss]]. Portals exhibit inconsistent behaviors (size requirements, one-way/two-way, tangling ropes) and are treated as systemic rules in this chapter.

# Portals
Dungeon/System mechanic describing many portal types and behaviors (two-way cart portals, scoop portals, teleport traps, size/fit rules, inconsistent pull mechanics). Cookbook excerpt notes portals can act like doors, require touch or being pulled, have button-sized openings that lead to monster dens, and vary by brand/tuning (trainyard tuning, abyss selection).
