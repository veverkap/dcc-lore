# time_to_the_butchers_masquerade

• A System Announcement countdown displayed throughout the chapter, indicating the remaining time before the in-world event called the Butcher’’s Masquerade begins.
• The countdown functions as an active [[dungeon]] timer, structuring party/raid planning and forcing decisions by [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]] and other crawlers.
