# Time to the Butcher’s Masquerade
A System Announcement countdown displayed throughout the chapter (e.g., "Time to the Butcher’s Masquerade: 8 hours" then "5 hours"), indicating the remaining time before the in-world event called the Butcher’s Masquerade begins. It functions as an active dungeon timer that structures the party/raid planning and forces decisions by [[dungeon crawler carl]] and other crawlers.
