# cursed_light_walker

• A debuff that causes a victim's health to slowly drain when away from light.
• Afflicted [[Viscount Fog]] after the spell chain reversed, ensuring Fog's death at sunset.
• Introduced by the spell interactions, causing a vampire's health to seep away when away from light.
• Led to [[Viscount Fog]]'s death at sunrise while he was frozen.
