# Mind Balance Level 16 Achievement
System achievement announced when [[dungeon crawler carl]]'s [[Mind Balance]] reaches Level 16. Grants the skill increase and a system notification ("New Achievement! You now tread in the realm of gods!"), which restores [[dungeon crawler carl]]'s awareness.
