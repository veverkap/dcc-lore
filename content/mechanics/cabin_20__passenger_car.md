# cabin_20__passenger_car

• The party spawns in Cabin #20, a dingy passenger car with plastic seats, graffiti, and a closed metallic wall at the end.
• The car is part of a 20-car [[train]] on the [[Red Line]].
• The party uses this car to regroup, equip traps, and retreat toward the rear.
• The car serves as the party's immediate base while they move between stations.
• The car has interior details such as beige molded seats, poles, scorch marks, and is labeled on the map.
• [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]]'s party spawns in this car.
