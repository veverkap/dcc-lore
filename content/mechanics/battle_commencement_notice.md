# [[Battle Commencement Notice]]
System announcement type messages in the chapter (e.g., "Notice: Battle will commence as soon as all squad leaders finish choosing their allegiances..." and "All allyships have been chosen. Battle commencing...") that dictate when combat begins and stipulate deck usage and alliance rules.
