# [[PVP coupon distribution rule]]
System mechanic: when a player receives a player-killer skull, they can receive a Savage box which may contain a coupon book; that book appears to include a [[PVP Coupon]] for every member of the killer's party. Coupons are nontrivial social/party-disrupting rewards (providing loot and potential level gain) and are resistant to simple destruction (e.g., won't burn).
