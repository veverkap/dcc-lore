# pvp_coupon_distribution_rule

• When a player receives a player-killer skull, they can receive a Savage box containing a coupon book with a [[PVP Coupon]] for every member of the killer's party.
• Coupons are nontrivial social/party-disrupting rewards providing [[loot]] and potential level gain.
• Coupons are resistant to simple destruction (e.g., won't burn).
