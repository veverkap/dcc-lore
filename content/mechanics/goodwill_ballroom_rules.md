# Goodwill ballroom rules
Explicit dungeon/party rules governing conduct inside the goodwill/ballroom spaces (no magic, no weapons/explosives/traps, no inventory use, hunters/Crawlers cannot harm one another inside). These are presented as enforced System-level restrictions for the event.
