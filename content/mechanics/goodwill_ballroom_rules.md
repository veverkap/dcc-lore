# goodwill_ballroom_rules

• Explicit [[dungeon]]/party rules govern conduct inside goodwill/[[ballroom]] spaces.
• No magic, weapons, explosives, traps, or inventory use is allowed.
• Hunters and Crawlers cannot harm each other inside.
