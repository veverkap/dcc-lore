# phase_three

• Begins at midnight on December 26th, with keyholders and their squads teleporting back to their original areas and able to exit to the ninth floor via the opened [[stairwell]].
• Keyless squads transfer to the doorway of a keyholder's [[stairwell]] and can attempt to steal keys by killing occupying squads.
• An explicit [[Dungeon]] Rule/phase of Faction Wars, marked by a countdown ("Time until Phase Three: Two Hours.") that signals a transition to a more dangerous combat state.
