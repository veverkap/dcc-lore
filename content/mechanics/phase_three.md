# Phase Three
A system rule: begins at midnight as the clock turns to December 26th; keyholders and their squads teleport back within original area and may exit to the ninth floor via the opened stairwell. Keyless squads transfer to the doorway of a keyholder’s stairwell and can attempt to steal keys by killing occupying squads.
