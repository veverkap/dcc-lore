# Naga exit rule (Ninth Floor)
A stated dungeon rule for the Ninth Floor: neither [[Princess Donut]] nor [[Katia]] can leave until all the [[Naga]] are dead, and even then only one of them may exit — an enforced restriction with major narrative consequences.
