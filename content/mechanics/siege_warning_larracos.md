# Siege Warning (Larracos)
System announcements appearing in the chapter, including "Entering Larracos. City of Broken Dreams," "You are on the 50th level," "Warning: This city is under siege," and timers such as "Time until the towers are operational: 13 minutes." These are active Dungeon/System messages framing the battle.
