# province_boss_discovery_achievement

• An in-game achievement awarded to party members for discovering the giant frankenmonster at [[Stop 48]] (the province boss), referenced as the [[Province Boss Discovery (Achievement)]] earned by Elle, Imani, and others.
• The achievement is earned by players for discovering the fused, room-filling boss at [[Station 48]] (the [[Province Boss (Stop 48)]]).
• The achievement is explicitly referenced as a reward for identifying the fused [[Festering Ghoul]] boss.
