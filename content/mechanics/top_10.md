# Top 10
A leaderboard mechanic indicating the highest-ranked players. In the chapter [[Top 10]] is referenced as the group from which [[Hekla]] is a member; killing a member yields a bounty and notable recognition.

The game's public ranking system for powerful crawlers; [[Hekla]] is described as one of the [[Top 10]] (number two at the time), making her death notable and bounty-worthy.
