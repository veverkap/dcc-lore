# top_10

• A leaderboard mechanic indicating the highest-ranked players.
• Killing a member of the Top 10 yields a [[bounty]] and notable recognition.
• The Top 10 is the game's public ranking system for powerful crawlers.
• [[Hekla]] is a member of the Top 10, specifically number two at the time of her death.
