# Gehenna Bramble
An explicit dungeon rule/spell effect cast as punishment for failing the world quest. [[Gehenna Bramble]] spreads outward from the Odious Creeper locations, transforming plants into ten-meter-high, thorn-covered bramble walls that boil victims' blood on contact. Cannot be poisoned or permanently killed and will avoid stairwells.

# [[Gehenna Bramble]]
A lethal spell-effect/rule cast from the remaining [[Odious Creeper]] locations after the quest failed. It spreads outward (approx. 4.5 km/h), transforming plants into ten-meter-high, thorn-covered, animated bramble walls whose thorns instantly boil a victim's blood, causing explosion. It cannot be poisoned or directly countered, and it avoids stairwells by five meters.
