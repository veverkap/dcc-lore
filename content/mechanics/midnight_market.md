# midnight_market

• Secret bazaar accessible via certain upgrade patches (like [[Upgrade Patch. Small.]]) reportedly hidden in the bottom regular floor of the [[desperado club]] and tied to traveling merchant encounters and strange goods.
• Market referenced as the trading site where the war mages acquired potions in exchange for the [[Gate]] of the Feral Gods.
• Marketplace location where [[Paca]] trades; where the [[Gate]] of the Feral Gods exchange is referenced by [[Eris]].
• Named in-[[dungeon]] market location where the merchant [[Paca]] operates and where [[justice lights team]] intends to retrieve the [[Antenna]] artifact.
