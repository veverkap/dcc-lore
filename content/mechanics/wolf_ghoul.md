# wolf_ghoul

• A wolf-like [[ghoul]] variant, including flaming variants, attacks NPCs and can be dispatched by spells like [[Magic Missile]].
• Encountered at the burning substation and neutralized by [[Magic Missile]] from Donut.
• Lunges at NPCs, such as [[Madison]], from a burning building.
