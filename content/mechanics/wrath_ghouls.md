# wrath_ghouls

* A [[ghoul]] subtype that can be found at Stop 36, with no soul crystals in their room, and can be held back by focused defense.
* A monster type that advances on stations and platforms, and their formation and transformation into higher-tier threats is central to the chapter's strategic concerns.
* A more aggressive subset of ghouls that travel up from the trainyards and contribute to multi-directional sieges.
* A mob type that can conglomerate into a boss similar to the one parked at [[Station 48]], and when allowed to congregate, they form a province boss-scale threat near [[stairwell]] stations.
* A type/pack of large ghouls observed in the [[trainyard]] during visual scans, presented as dangerous mobs encountered on the lines and part of the threat the interdiction carts are meant to clear.
* A type of mob that can congregate to form a powerful boss similar to the one parked at [[Station 48]] when allowed to gather, and treated as a province-level threat in groups.
* A [[ghoul]] subtype that gathers at station 36, part of the coordinated wave of ghouls threatening multiple stops on the line.
* A named monster type encountered on the platforms and tracks, their presence and transformation are central to station defense planning and to preventing boss formation near [[stairwells]].
* A more aggressive flow of ghouls that travel up from the trainyards, contributing to multi-directional besiegement of crawlers.
* A mob type that, when allowed to congregate, can form a powerful provincial boss similar to the confirmed province boss at [[Station 48]], mentioned as dangerous when grouped at multiple [[Station 24]]/[[stairwell]] sites.
* A referenced subtype of [[ghoul]] that can be transported in cages, their presence in cargo can break systems and contribute to the yard's infestation problems.
* Aggressive mobs that can congregate and form a boss similar to the one at [[Station 48]] when allowed to gather, cited as forming a province-boss-level threat.
* A mob type forming large boss amalgamations (described as tied-together ghouls forming giant monsters) that appear as province/city bosses in certain stations and are noted as deadly in recaps.
