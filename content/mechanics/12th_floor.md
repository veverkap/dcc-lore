# 12th_floor

• The 12th floor is referenced in the system's rule exposition, dealing ceases after the one offered upon entrance if [[Scolopendra]] is killed.
• The [[dungeon]] AI warns no more deals will be offered after the one given upon entering the 12th floor if [[Scolopendra]] is killed.
• The 12th floor is the activation point for Advance Reader Copy content, including the [[Tome of Gatekeeper]] and its spell.
• The 12th floor is the [[dungeon]] floor referenced by the [[arena]] rules, entrances to it are tied to the death of its floor boss.
