# seventh_floor

• Floor at which patrons may begin to transfer patronage at their discretion, according to the announcement rules.
• Typical location where [[Frenzied Gerbil]] mobs are found, alongside the [[Fifth Floor]].
• Previous floor that once centered around the [[Guild of Suffering]]'s activities, used for context about memorial crystals and cult operations.
• [[Dungeon]] floor undergoing a complete redesign due to current [[crawler]] activity and hunter behavior.
• Floor that was skipped in one of [[remex the grand]]'s miscasts, and also used in discussion of floor numbering changes (original seventh becoming eighth).
• Floor implied by hidden impressions of the number seven on sponsored items (the hammer and stick) and referenced by [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]]'s speculation about why such marks would exist.
• Transitional floor referenced just before the unexpected jump to the [[Eighth Floor]] caused by [[Prepotente]]’s actions.
• [[Dungeon]] floor where [[Mistress Henspar]] died, an event that radicalizes [[Volteeg]]’s emotional state.
• Completely redesigned floor due to player actions and upcoming changes to crawl progression.
• Home to the vampiric infestation and the setting for the described events, contrasting local cultural vampire myths with the vampires encountered.
• Future floor referenced by the [[Seventh Floor Pole Position]] boon, part of the [[dungeon]] progression and purchasable boon.
• Floor the party expected to visit but ultimately bypassed when the narrative describes jumping to the [[Eighth Floor]].
