# purple_line

* [[Purple Line]]: a transit line intersecting [[Station 149]] and used by [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]]'s team as an escape/route option, or to reach stop 283 and catch the [[Nightmare Express]].
* One of the transit lines referenced as part of planned multi-line transfers to reach higher-numbered stops, such as stop 283 on the [[Nightmare Express]].
* Named [[train]] line/[[platform]] where players grind monsters coming from other stations; noted as having stopped on some runs in the chapter.
* A named [[train]] line; referenced as having been stopped on parts while the Mauve line remains active.
* A colored transit line intersecting at [[Transfer Station 149]] and used by [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]] and companions for movement; a planned escape route and part of the route to catch the [[Nightmare Express]].
* One of the colored transit lines used by the party for routing and grinding; mentioned in route plans that include transfers to the [[Nightmare Express]] and other named trains.
* A colored transit line the party plans to use for grinding after leaving the station; part of the network that includes the [[Ochre Line]] and [[Mauve line]].
* One of the [[train]] lines referenced; noted as having stopped on some routes while the Mauve line remains moving.
* Specific subway/[[train]] line referenced as the likely origin of some mobs and where a [[train]] had stopped running, linked to increased mob presence on the tracks and stations.
