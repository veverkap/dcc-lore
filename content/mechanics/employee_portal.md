# employee_portal

• The Employee [[Portal]] is a [[dungeon]] rule/[[portal]] mechanic that teleports workers to the [[platform]], explicitly lifebound to the [[Kravyad]].
• It controls who can pass and is used for employee-only movement.
• The [[portal]] is used by [[Hekla]]'s crew to enter the system and reach stations, as well as to access otherwise inaccessible [[train]] infrastructure and platforms.
• It is a backdoor/subspace access route to the [[Vermillion line]].
• The [[portal]] snaps closed if the [[Kravyad]] dies, creating a tactical/trap rule for station 435.
• It is a [[portal]] gateway used by [[Hekla]]'s crew to enter the operational employee line and reach substations like [[Substation H]].
