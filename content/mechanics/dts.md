# dts

• A [[dungeon]] mechanic/condition that progresses monsters through stages, with [[stage]]-three producing massively mutated, fatal forms that can explode or hatch.
• The system issues warnings when mobs are suffering from the [[DTs]].
• The mechanic modifies behavior and stats by stages, with [[stage]] two doubling strength, stripping intelligence/speech, and causing affected mobs to attack non-affected entities.
• In [[stage]] two, DTs double strength and remove speech/reasoning from intelligent mobs, causing them to attack anything not similarly afflicted.
• The mechanic transforms original mobs into massively altered, stronger, and unstable forms at [[stage]] three, resulting in tentacled, round-mouthed monstrosities that eventually die/explode and may hatch other creatures.
• System message warnings annotate mobs suffering from the [[DTs]].
• The mechanic represents withdrawal stages for mobs, altering behavior and stats, with [[stage]] two doubling strength and removing reasoning/speech for intelligent mobs.
• DT-[[stage]] monsters become uniformly grotesque tentacled abominations at [[stage]] three, are temporary, and their death can produce hazardous byproducts (explosions, parasitic hatchlings).
