# DTs
A dungeon mechanic/condition that progresses monsters through stages; stage-three ([[stage-three monsters]]) produces massively mutated, fatal forms that can explode or hatch. The system issues warnings when mobs are suffering from the [[DTs]].

# DTs
A system-status/withdrawal mechanic applied to mobs that shows as an in-game warning (e.g., "Warning: This mob is suffering from the DTs. It is in stage two of three."). The mechanic modifies behavior and stats by stages (stage two here doubles strength, strips intelligence/speech, and makes affected mobs attack non-affected entities).
