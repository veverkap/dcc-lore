# elf_castle

• The Elf Castle is a named structure where a defensive spell is being assembled, with [[Tortoise]] casting outside its walls.
• It is the elven [[stronghold]] and boss location of [[queen imogen of the high elves]], containing a locked [[basement]] [[stairwell]] and serving as the central objective for the assault timed to the [[Butcher’s Masquerade]].
• The castle partially teleported away, taking many NPCs and subbasements with it, leaving the remaining players stranded in midair and then dumping them into a bramble-filled pit.
• The castle is a massive defensive position belonging to the elves that has been brought down to the floor with artillery, mercenary forces, and accompanying troops.
• [[Tortoise]] prepares a large spell near the Elf Castle, which is a focal point of elven hostility toward the party and a strategic site in the coming conflict.
• The [[target]] location of the raid is the Elf Castle, where [[queen imogen of the high elves]] holds court, with an expansive [[basement]] and locked internal [[stairwell]] (confirming boss status).
• Crawlers plan multiple ingress points around the castle.
• The large castle's disappearance drives the pit/[[teleport]] sequence, leaving the crew suspended in midair.
