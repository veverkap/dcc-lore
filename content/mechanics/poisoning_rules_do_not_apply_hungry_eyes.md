# [[Poisoning rules do NOT apply (Hungry Eyes)]]
A posted dungeon/system rule stating that even in designated safe-room areas within [[Hungry Eyes Vendor Village]], poisoning rules are suspended — food and consumables may be hazardous and can inflict poisons without usual safe-room protections.
