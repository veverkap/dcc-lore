# precursor_burst

• Named mechanic: Precursor Burst, describing a sequence of four foreshock pulses preceding the final detonation.
• Each burst has a different effect: 
	+ First: buffs stripped items
	+ Second: reactivated guards
	+ Third: activated magical weapons or destroyed armor
	+ Fourth: unleashed wild-magic effects (lightning, teleportation, potion auto-activation, and poison)
