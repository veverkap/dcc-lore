# Precursor Burst
Named mechanic describing the sequence of four foreshock pulses preceding the final detonation. Each burst has a different effect (first stripped item buffs, second reactivated guards, third activated magical weapons or destroyed armor, fourth unleashed wild-magic effects such as lightning, teleportation, potion auto-activation, and poison).
