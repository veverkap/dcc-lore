# smite

• Divine punitive effect threatened as a consequence if a player kills multiple worshippers of a god within the probation period.
• [[Dungeon]] mechanic/god-effect that penalizes certain choices, such as failing to repair [[Shrine of Ogun]], causing smites against existing worshippers.
• Gods can "smite" worshippers, including automatic smites on leaving or probabilistic smites for missed worship, sometimes causing severe debuffs or death.
• A punitive system mechanic inflicted by gods on players, represented in-game by debuffs and flaming-anvil markers.
