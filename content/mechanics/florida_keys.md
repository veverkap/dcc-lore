# florida_keys

• The regional setting of the spawn point is in the [[Florida Keys]], rather than initially displayed [[Iowa]].
• The characters find themselves in the [[Florida]] Keys, described as the southern tip of [[Florida]], warm with palm trees.
• The relocated [[Key]] marker appears nearby on a mound of [[earth]] rising into the clouds.
• The [[Florida]] Keys are an island chain referenced by [[Louis]] when explaining regional geography and proximity to [[Homestead, Florida]] and [[Miami]].
