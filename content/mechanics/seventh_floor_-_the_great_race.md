# Seventh Floor - The Great Race
Named floor encountered in this chapter. Themed on a fairytale of a maze with sealing tunnels; features include glass crawler pods arranged in a spiral, portal-like stairwell exits to the [[Eighth Floor]], and the race/maze rules announced by [[Cascadia]].
