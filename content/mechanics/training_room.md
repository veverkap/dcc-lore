# training_room

Here is the consolidated markdown list:

• The Training Room is a large specialized room added to the communal personal space, providing combat training modules and dummies.
• It is used by party members like [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]], [[Donut]], and [[Katia]] to [[train]] skills like Bare Knuckles, [[dodge]], and shape-altering.
• The room automatically spawns wooden dummies when training starts.
• It is also used for leveling abilities, with the advanced variant granting six hours per day across multiple skills and additional visualization and bonuses.
• The room is adjacent to the safe room and is used by party members like [[Samantha]] and [[Tipid]].
• It is a controllable interior space used by the party to [[train]] skills and house hireable characters like the [[Lika Love Doll Head]].
• The standard facility for training abilities is distinct from the [[Magic Workshop]], which is single-user and tied to spell training mechanics.
• The room is also referenced as being unavailable to [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]] shortly before his transfer, originally assigned for preparation time.
