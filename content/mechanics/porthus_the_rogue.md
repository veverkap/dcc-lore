# Porthus the Rogue
A High Elf Crawler who originally received [[The Dungeon Anarchist’s Cookbook]] (disguised as a blank sketchbook) on the [[Ninth Floor]] during the fifteenth season. Cited as the book's early recipient and part of its provenance.
