# the_wasteland

• The [[Wasteland]] is a gnome airship/fortress that patrols and hovers over [[The Bowl]], moving towards the edge during daily sandstorms and launching explosive bombardments.
• It is a distant, harsh zone that Carl hopes to reach with improved rockets, beyond standard projectile ceiling limits.
• The [[Wasteland]] is a floating zone or mass above the desert "bowl" that serves as a staging area for airships and flying machines, with vehicles sometimes dropping from it onto the bowl below.
• The name "The [[Wasteland]]" is given to the gnome airship/fortress that hovers over the bowl, its movements driving a major plot event (aerial bombardment on a Bactrian village).
