# Warning: Your blood has been set to boil.
A system/status notification indicating that [[dungeon crawler carl]] has been targeted by a cardiovascular spell cast by [[Astrid]] (a [[Bloodlust Sprite]]). The warning denotes an active harmful status effect (blood-boiling) that rapidly depletes health.
