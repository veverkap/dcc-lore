# fight_countdown_timer

• An active system mechanic that appears upon interaction with the [[trailer]] door or activation devices.
• Displays a warning message: "Warning: The fight will not commence for forty-two minutes and thirty seconds."
• Locks time-limited actions and signals an imminent event within the [[Spliced-in Time Rectangle]].
