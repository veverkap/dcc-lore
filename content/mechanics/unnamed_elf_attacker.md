# Unnamed Elf Attacker
A hostile elf who lures [[Mongo]] as part of a trap; engages [[dungeon crawler carl]] and [[Donut]] in the alley, uses flames/dark magic and utters threats. Quickly defeated when [[dungeon crawler carl]] kicks him and [[Donut]] hits him with Magic Missile; his head is severed and later grabbed by [[Mongo]].
