# unnamed_elf_attacker

• A hostile elf who lures [[Mongo]] as part of a trap; engages [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]] and [[Donut]] in the alley, using flames/dark magic and uttering threats.
• Quickly defeated when [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]] kicks him and [[Donut]] hits him with Magic Missile; his head is severed and later grabbed by [[Mongo]].
