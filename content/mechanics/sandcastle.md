# sandcastle

• [[sandcastle]]: Large structure/landmark on the [[beach]] central to the [[quadrant]] puzzles and referenced as where parts were torn away and where the lightning [[tower]] is connected.
• Location of earlier events on the bubble floor where [[Ghazi]] and the possession by [[Psamathe]] occurred; referenced as backstory for current tensions.
• The sand- based medieval-style fortress built around the [[necropolis]]' drainage [[tunnel]], housing the magical doorway and concealing the buried [[stairwell]] at its center.
• A multi-layered castle zone mentioned by [[Gwendolyn Duet Gwen]] as part of her bubble, inhabited by giant snakes, spiders, scorpion-like humanoids, and other threats.
• Sand-made castle guarding the entrance to the [[Necropolis]], containing the [[stairwell]] and the sand-embedded form of [[Ghazi (Mad Dune Mage)]], with two [[towers]] flanking its entrance and a central magical door.
• Primary location of the chapter: a glass- and-sand-transmuted castle inhabited by [[Ghazi]] with glass halls, fountains, and a [[basement]] boss chamber, partially reverting during sandstorms and containing the boss room leading to the [[Necropolis of Anser]] [[stairwell]].
