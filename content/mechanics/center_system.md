# center_system

• The bloated, comatose central system where harvested elemental yields from planetary crawls are delivered and consumed, expanding and capturing new systems as it is fed.
• The central polity and population hub, associated with the remnant called the [[Eulogist]] and the system whose AIs are implicated in the current crisis.
• The only seemingly safe place and the locus of checkpoints overwhelming Syndicate resources, mentioned by [[Prime Minister Victory]] during the broadcast.
