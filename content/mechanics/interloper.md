# interloper

• A displayed in-game achievement the party triggers for allowing a red-tagged NPC boss ([[Asojano]]) into a [[saferoom]], rewarding the player with a [[Bronze Stranger Danger box]] and serving as a system notification of atypical player/NPC interaction.
