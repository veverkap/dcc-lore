# new_quest_spiders

• A system-announced deity quest from [[Eileithyia]] requiring the party to "Kill five spiders in my name every day" with a reward of another boon.
• The quest is used as a covert messaging channel to [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]].
• The quest is issued by [[Eileithyia]] during an encounter, with a visible warning that the quest comes from a deity the player does not worship.
