# personal_space

• Player-owned private spaces that can be attached to the whole, combined, whitelisted for visitors, and generate temporary situational spaces when linked.
• Instance-bound safe areas attached to individual crawlers, which can be combined or whitelisted for group entry, and generate temporary situational spaces when linked.
• The party's private room/[[apartment]] within the floor, which is dirty from gore until the hired cleaner bot begins removing congealed blood.
• Player-owned instanced living spaces where players interact, with several scenes mentioning movements into and out of personal space and show-related tapings occurring there.
• Mechanic for private player areas that can be upgraded and shared, central to party logistics and one reason Katia plans to reorganize before the sixth floor.
• The private instanced area owned by [[dungeon]] [[crawler]] carl where inventory management, item activation, and quick looting occur, with access window closing with the level timer.
• The party's private living area where crafting, item inspection, and the Prize Carousel fan-box event take place, containing the party's enlarged alchemy table and [[storage]].
• Carl's private home instance/space, which unexpectedly contains around thirty visitors including Hekla and others at the chapter's end.
• Carl's private shared area that functions as an inventory-accessible room, appearing empty in a [[portal]] screenshot but then filling with a crowd of visitors, precipitating Carl's surprise at the chapter's end.
• [[Dungeon]] feature that allows crawlers like Katia to attach private areas to the whole, whitelist visitors, and combine with other personal spaces to create shared safe rooms.
• The party's private living quarters where purchases, crafting benches, and rest occur, site of the chapter's cleanup and strategy conversations.
• Private player-owned areas used as safe rooms, containing NPCs, children watching movies, and [[storage]] where [[loot]] is opened and applied.
• Player-owned [[saferoom]] area containing amenities and used as a private operations center and message-trading spot for Carl's group.
• A private safe-room area used by the party to store items and rest, subject to access restrictions as the level collapse approaches.
• A player-owned room/instance where [[dungeon]] [[crawler]] carl is dumped inside the Medium Dryad Settlement after rescue, functioning as a private indoor area in which NPCs interact.
• Instance-linked rooms attached to the tavern and connected by doors in the situationally-generated [[hallway]], with Edict permitted limited entry by one-time pass into a personal space.
