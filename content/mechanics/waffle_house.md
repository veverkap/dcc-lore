# waffle_house

• A first-floor [[restaurant]] site where a group of crawlers (about thirty) are holed up; used as a staging or waiting area during the first-floor collapse.
• A prior safe room location referenced as a source of additional residents; only a portion of occupants moved to the storm [[shelter]].
