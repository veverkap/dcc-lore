# The fuse
A Mantid-named failsafe trigger embedded in the system's containment architecture. When fully tripped by several required conditions, it drives the star at the system center into a nova, destroying the AI and everyone in the system. Access to the fuse is what the [[neural enhancement]] and [[AI containment]] enable.
