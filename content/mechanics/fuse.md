# fuse

• A [[failsafe]] mechanic displayed on the [[Containment Interface]] with five benchmarks required for detonation.
• Two benchmarks are marked "Conditions Met."
• The menu offers "Override Conditions And Activate Fuse" as a high-risk option that would blow the system's safeguards.
• A Mantid-named [[failsafe]] trigger embedded in the system's containment architecture.
• When fully tripped by several required conditions, it drives the star at the system center into a nova, destroying the AI and everyone in the system.
• Access to the fuse is enabled by the [[neural enhancement]] and [[AI containment]].
