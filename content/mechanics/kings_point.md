# kings_point

• One of the nine petal areas on the ninth-floor map; a pre-built, naturally defensible castle area frequently chosen by previous winners.
• The orc [[headquarters]] and castle targeted by the party's bombing and volcanic plan; visible as the cockpit points to it during the run.
• A pre-built, fortified petal area in [[Larracos]]'s nine-petal layout; historically favored by previous winners due to its natural defenses and resource-saving features.
