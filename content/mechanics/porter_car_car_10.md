# porter_car_car_10

• Designated porter-only car containing the [[Yellow Line]] Reward Room and many suitcases.
• Accessed via a sliding half-door interaction; guarded but provides one [[Prize Case]] per [[crawler]] per line.
• Run by [[Pierre]] and other [[Grapple]] porters.
• Located in the tenth car labeled for porters, where reward distribution occurs.
• Accessed via a small sliding service hatch.
