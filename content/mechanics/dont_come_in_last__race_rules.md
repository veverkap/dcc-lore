# Don't come in last — Race Rules
Explicit dungeon rules and mechanics announced for the tenth floor: teams of up to four, seven heats, vehicle/creature repair between races if garage attendant is alive, upgrade rewards (first place: three regular or one Golden upgrade; everyone receives one Golden upgrade after the fourth heat), audience-influenced upgrades for near-last finishers, prohibition on vehicle theft, ban on movement spells (e.g., [[Teleport]]) affecting vehicles or entering opponent vehicles, and the mandate that driver duties rotate (only one person may drive per heat).
