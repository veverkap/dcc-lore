# dont_come_in_last__race_rules

• Teams of up to four, seven heats
• Vehicle/creature repair between races if [[garage]] attendant is alive
• Upgrade rewards: first place (three regular or one Golden), everyone receives one Golden after the fourth [[heat]]
• Audience-influenced [[upgrades]] for near-last finishers
• Prohibition on vehicle theft
• Ban on movement spells (e.g., [[Teleport]]) affecting vehicles or entering opponent vehicles
• Driver duties rotate (only one person may drive per [[heat]])
