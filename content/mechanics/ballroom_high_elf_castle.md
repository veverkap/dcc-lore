# Ballroom (High Elf castle)
The specific room within the [[castle of the high elves]] where the [[Butcher's Masquerade]] occurs. Magic, weapons, inventory use, and explosives are prohibited here; hunters appear to be co-located but are actually in a mirrored/different staging area.
