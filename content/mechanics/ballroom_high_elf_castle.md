# ballroom_high_elf_castle

• The [[Ballroom]] is a specific room within the [[castle of the high elves]] where the [[Butcher's Masquerade]] takes place.
• Magic, weapons, inventory use, and explosives are prohibited in the [[Ballroom]].
• Hunters appear to be co-located but are actually in a mirrored/different staging area.
