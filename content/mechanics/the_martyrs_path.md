# the_martyrs_path

• Grants +10% Strength
• Marks every outdoor step on the map
• Enables releasing a gout of flame from marked steps when health reaches 5% (activates automatically on death)
• Can be revoked
• Persisted for the remainder of the floor while in [[Emberus]]'s grace
