# The Martyr’s Path
Floor boon/mechanic granted by [[Pater Coal]] and [[Emberus]]: grants +10% Strength, marks every outdoor step on the map, and enables releasing a gout of flame from marked steps when health reaches 5% (activates automatically on death). The boon persists for the remainder of the floor while in [[Emberus]]'s grace and can be revoked.
