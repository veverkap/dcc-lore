# boss_battle_announcement

• The system/AI announcement sequence that dramatizes the encounter, labels the fight as a boss- style [[temple]] match, opens spectator betting, and enforces UI presentation of combatants and timers.
• The "B-b- b-b Boss Battle!" prompt, the "You've enraged an NPC" line, the VS/ portrait/ splash text (e.g., "GORE-GORE THE MANTAUR! ... LEVEL 40!"), and toggles boss mechanics and HUD elements during the fight.
• The [[Dungeon]] AI's vocal/system announcements that declare the encounter format ("Four teams enter! One team leaves!"), play boss music (e.g., AC/DC "Thunderstruck"), and present corner-by-corner introductions.
• The system announcement that triggers on entering a boss [[arena]], presents mugshots, declares "[[Versus]]..." with the boss name and level (e.g., "[[Versus]]... The Juicer! Level 9 Neighborhood Boss!"), and initiates music and UI elements for the encounter.
• The system-announcement/[[stage]] music event triggered by the [[dungeon]] AI (including AC/DC "Thunderstruck") that formally initiates the multi-team boss fight and narrates team corners and introductions.
