# Hills Cyclops
Monster species/NPC type. Described in the in-game tooltip observed for [[Mordecai]]’s new form: "both the weakest and the dumbest" cyclops variant, commonly shepherds and tavern drunks, often non-combatant. Example given: [[Mordecai]] is a Hills Cyclops, labeled "Level 50" and "Manager of Princess Donut."
