# Vox: 'Hello, Crawlers' World Announcement
System-level production announcement delivered to crawlers that functions as both a broadcast and patch-note. Includes policy clarifications (e.g., Borant/Valtay status), patch updates (hot springs, lava tubes), sponsor rules, hunter restrictions, and the new ranking visibility for top 1,000 crawlers.
