# [[Special guest creatures]]
Dungeon mechanic explained by [[Zev]]: when certain mobs are killed they are labeled "special guest creatures," and the home audience may vote to send the creature to the 11th floor or send a preannounced stand-in, affecting how those mobs are handled post-kill.
