# main_restaurant

• The Main [[Restaurant]] is a public dining area where the party interacts with the Bopca and spends gold on [[upgrades]].
• The party can purchase a Cleaner Bot here.
• Katia sits eating a pineapple in this location.
• The area is adjacent to the party's Personal Space.
