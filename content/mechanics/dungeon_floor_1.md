# Dungeon Floor 1
The opening floor of the [[World Dungeon]] where [[dungeon crawler carl]] and [[Princess Donut]] arrive — labeled as the First Floor and featuring the stairway, the hulking carved door, corridors, goblin encounters, and the [[Tutorial Guild]] entrance.
