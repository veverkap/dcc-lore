# red_manseros_land_crabs

• A rival crustacean group/species central to the quest conflict "The Chowder War".
• Their mating migration and resultant "chowder" cause ecological and religious conflict with the [[Monk Seals]].
• Named mob/species engaged in the [[beach]] conflict against the [[Monk Seals]] in the quest [[The Chowder War]].
• Their sneak attack and ensuing attrition drive the new quest narrative.
