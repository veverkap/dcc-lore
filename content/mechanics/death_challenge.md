An in-world system announcement/televised event overlay that dramatizes and broadcasts [[dungeon crawler carl]]'s imminent death. Presented with graphics, music, and announcer text; functions as a live spectacle/announcement mechanic during the encounter.

# Death Challenge
A dungeon mechanic described on the show: a pop-up gambling event where viewers bet on multiple possible outcomes with variable odds (the system AI initially set [[dungeon crawler carl]]'s survival at 50:1 in one instance).
