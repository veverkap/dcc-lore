# mentally_unstable_clothing_hoarder

• An in-game achievement awarded to [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]] for having over 500 of the exact same stackable clothing item in inventory.
• The achievement provides no tangible reward and features flavor text that mocks hoarding behavior.
