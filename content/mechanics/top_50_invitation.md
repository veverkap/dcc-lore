# Top 50 Invitation
A ranking/achievement mechanic referenced by [[Hello, Crawlers!]]: the top 50 crawlers will receive mandatory invites to the [[Butcher's Masquerade]] and may bring a plus one (attendance rules vary). Used as a mid-crawl incentive and social mechanic.
