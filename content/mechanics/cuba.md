# cuba

• [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]] and [[Donut]]'s phase-one region; examples of local folklore and the replica environment are relevant to squad strategy.
• Reference point for the party's previous location; mentioned as where they came from prior to the [[Florida]] Keys.
• An in-game zone/area experiencing the [[Demon Eviction]] event in this chapter.
• The specific zone on [[Floor 8]] where [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]] and [[Donut]] became stuck and collected multiple monster cards.
• Real-world location referenced for comparison when describing the style of tricked-out cars; used as contextual flavor rather than an active scene location in this chapter.
• Nation-region used as a player-selected starting area for the phase-one [[Earth]] memory; associated with the [[El Capitolio Nacional de Cuba]] spawn.
• Geographic origin listed on the [[T'Ghee Card]] for [[Yago]].
• Previous floor/area the squad had been operating in before appearing in [[Monroe County, Florida]]; used as a reference for relative location and climate.
• Real-world geography referenced as a comparative location to explain how surprising the [[beach]] corpses washing up on [[Florida]] are within the [[dungeon]].
• The area explicitly named in the chapter's area message: "Warning: Cuba is currently experiencing a Demon Eviction event."
• Regional zone on the current floor; the scope of the activated quest "Where the Sidewalk Ends" and the area targeted by [[Amayon]]'s [[portal]] and the mass evacuation plan.
• A specific zone/location on [[Floor 8]] where [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]] and [[Princess Donut]] got stuck and collected several monster cards.
• Region under which the heavens opened first; proximity to this region affects the timer counting down to the [[portal]] event.
