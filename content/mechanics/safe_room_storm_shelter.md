# safe_room_storm_shelter

• A large safe-room found on [[Floor Two]], resembling a storm [[shelter]], with televisions, showers, [[restrooms]] (with French signage), and many cots.
• Contains a peculiar [[mailbox]] that dispenses spellbooks and functions as a rest/[[loot]] hub.
