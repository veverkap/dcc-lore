# [[Safe Room (Storm Shelter)]]
A large safe-room found on [[Floor Two]] resembling a storm shelter: televisions, showers, restrooms (with French signage), and many cots. Contains a peculiar mailbox that dispenses spellbooks and functions as a rest/loot hub.
