# Green and tan Y-shaped building (Sister Ines' building)
The Y-shaped, green-and-tan skyscraper where [[Sister Ines]] is located and where the team plans to infiltrate to free [[Amayon]] from his shell. The building is the immediate objective of Carl's assault.
