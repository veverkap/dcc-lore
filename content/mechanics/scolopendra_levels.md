# Scolopendra Levels
A set of specially designated dungeon floors (levels 3, 6, 9, 12, 15, and 18) where [[War Crime]] may be cast according to its constraints. The chapter's spell description explicitly ties [[War Crime]]'s usage to the [[Scolopendra Levels]].
