# scolopendra_levels

• A set of [[dungeon]] floors where [[Zerzura]] is known to skip floors unpredictably, as happened to [[remex the grand]].
• A set of specially designated [[dungeon]] floors (levels 3, 6, 9, 12, 15, and 18) where [[War Crime]] may be cast according to its constraints.
• A set of floors in the crawl where spells like [[Zerzura]] have unusual behavior, such as skipping floors.
• [[Dungeon]] floors and zones specifically tied to [[Scolopendra]]'s influence, with the Nine-Tier Attack primarily affecting these levels and floors that contain crawlers.
