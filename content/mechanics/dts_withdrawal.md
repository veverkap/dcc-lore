# DTs (withdrawal)
System-style status/effect referenced in monster entries. Example: the [[Babababoon]] entry includes a "Warning: This mob is suffering from the DTs. It is in stage two of three." Stage two effects described: strength doubled; intelligent mobs lose speech/reasoning and attack anything not also suffering from the same condition. The mechanic progresses in stages and changes mob behavior and combat threat.
