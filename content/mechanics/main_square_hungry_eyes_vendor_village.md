# [[Main Square (Hungry Eyes Vendor Village)]]
The central plaza of [[Hungry Eyes Vendor Village]]. Explicitly called out by dungeon rules: [[Safe room rules do NOT apply in the main square.]] NPC traffic and a single portal to the cul-de-sac converge here.
