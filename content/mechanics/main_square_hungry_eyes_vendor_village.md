# main_square_hungry_eyes_vendor_village

• The central plaza of [[Hungry Eyes Vendor Village]].
• Explicitly called out by [[dungeon]] rules: Safe room rules do NOT apply in the main square.
• NPC traffic and a single [[portal]] to the [[cul-de-sac]] converge here.
