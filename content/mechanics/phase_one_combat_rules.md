# phase_one_combat_rules

• The initial combat phase rules engage when the [[Ceasefire]] ends.
• Players may enter enemy territory via designated corridors.
• Restrictions apply, including "no offensive magic" and "no [[teleport]]-based spells" during the [[ramp]]-up.
