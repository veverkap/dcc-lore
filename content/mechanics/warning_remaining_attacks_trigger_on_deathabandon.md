# Warning: Remaining attacks trigger on death/abandon
An explicit system warning stating that if [[Scolopendra]] dies or is abandoned on a collapsing floor, the remaining attacks (the second through ninth) will all activate at once with no way to stop them. The chapter specifically notes "Time until attack number 2 of 9: 100 seconds" as part of the countdown and attack sequencing.
