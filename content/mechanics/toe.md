# toe

• A specific area or landmark on the west end of [[Hump Town]] that is damaged when debris from the aerial battle strikes the [[town]].
• Carl's personal space/personal housing (referred to as the "Toe"), where party members return to assign stat points, open boxes, and perform [[upgrades]], containing the cleaner bot and environmental upgrade interfaces.
• A [[bar]]/meeting place where [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]]'s group congregates, including the arrival of [[Louis]], [[Firas]], and [[Juice Box]].
• A named personal-space [[saferoom]] where [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]] instructs [[Skarn]] and other children to flee if bombs start falling.
• A specific landmark or district within [[Hump Town]] referenced as being near where debris landed during the attack.
