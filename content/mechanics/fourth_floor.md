# fourth_floor

Here is the consolidated list of unique factual points about the Fourth Floor:

• The Fourth Floor marks a significant milestone in the game's structure, with area size decreasing, entrances becoming guarded, and progression requiring quests or boss encounters.
• Trainers like Mordecai need a [[crawler]] to reach the Fourth Floor to reduce [[indentureship]] obligations.
• The Fourth Floor is referenced in patronage rules as the next floor whose induction will open patron auction spots for one day.
• Gods begin to appear in the game's structure on the Fourth Floor.
• A previously opened themed [[dungeon]] floor, the "living creature" floor, is referenced as an example of a risky themed floor.
• The Fourth Floor is a [[dungeon]] level that can be reached by a staircase in the [[circus]] park after completing a quest.
• The Fourth Floor is a themed [[dungeon]] floor that features momentum-based mechanics and may host worshipers/summons of Grull due to recent sponsorship activity by the Skull Clan.
• The Fourth Floor is governed by floor-specific rules, such as [[stairwell]] timing, and is referred to as the floor where the Tangle transit system exists.
• The Fourth Floor contains the club and [[casino]] visited by the narrator, with activity levels, bounties, and security dynamics discussed in this setting.
• The Fourth Floor is the current [[dungeon]] floor where events occur, featuring the [[trainyard]], stations with Soul Crystals, and widespread Krakaren infestation.
• The Fourth Floor is referenced as the earlier location where the narrator first encountered mentions of Krakaren and related mob descriptions.
• The Fourth Floor is the place where Carl previously encountered and repeatedly killed the shade [[gnoll]] Growler Gary.
• The Fourth Floor is the floor where Carl had previously encountered a ram-headed creature similar to Potsy.
• The Fourth Floor is the place where Donut encountered "babababoon" enemies and collected floppy hats.
• The Fourth Floor is the floor of the [[dungeon]] where Growler Gary serves as a hyena bartender.
• The Fourth Floor is a specific [[dungeon]] level referenced as a past location for Gary and a point of comparison for his earlier level/status.
• The Fourth Floor is the place where Juice Box's changeling brother had been working to free his people from.
• The Fourth Floor is the place where Luca Di. gave Princess Donut his [[train]] hat.
• The Fourth Floor is the next [[dungeon]] level the party must descend to via the [[stairwell]], with the chapter ending with the group entering through the Entrance to the Fourth Floor as the floor's collapse timers and evacuation rules take effect.
• The Fourth Floor is the next floor opening in less than an hour in the chapter, with Odette warning of deity-summmoning dangers and momentum-based advantages on this floor.
• The Fourth Floor is a previously-run [[dungeon]] floor type referenced by Carl to explain how NPCs like Vernon are generated with false memories.
• The Fourth Floor is the [[dungeon]] floor where the club and dance hall scenes occur, described as moderately busy and home to the [[casino]] and nightlife that host notable crawlers like Elle McGib.
• The Fourth Floor is referenced as an example earlier in lore where a sliding-puzzle area once formed a Syndicate-like logo and flipped players into hidden chambers.
• The Fourth Floor is the current floor where the chapter's events take place, containing transfer stations, the [[trainyard]], and large-scale threats like the [[Mimic]] and numerous Krakaren mobs.
• The Fourth Floor is the earlier [[dungeon]] floor referenced as the source of items and enemies (e.g., "babababoon guys") from which Donut had previously collected similar hats.
• The Fourth Floor is the origin point for some items (e.g., mantaur dropping Rev-up Hair Tonic and a benefactor box that contained the bolt given by Princess Formidable).
• The Fourth Floor is the place where benefactor boxes (including the bolt from Princess Formidable) and mantaur encounters occurred, referenced in [[loot]] context.
• The Fourth Floor is the [[apartment]]-level containing the home of Mackenzie and Oliver with photos of Archie Mu and a hidden [[gate]] in the child's room.
