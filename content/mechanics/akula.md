# akula

* The Akula is a named submarine/capital vessel, referenced as [[Ruckus]]'s original command contact, with its bridge/throne room occupation triggering [[quadrant]]-wide effects.
* The Akula is a giant submarine encountered in the [[Water Quadrant]], where the party fought robots, and its torpedo strike initiated the chain that launched [[Chris Andrews 2]] toward the desert.
* The Akula is a massive submarine 500 meters under the surface, physically attached to the [[necropolis]], and acting as a pump flooding the structure.
* The Akula is a submarine where the chapter's primary action occurs, containing the ornate bridge, captain's stateroom, and escape hatches, and is grabbed, torn in half, and ultimately swallowed by [[Lusca]].
* The Akula is the submarine/boat that Carl and team are coming from, swallowed by [[Lusca]], with its bow ending up inside the shark's throat and being central to the Leatherface escape plan.
* The Akula is a named [[quadrant]] or area referenced as another [[quadrant]]'s easy-win scenario, suspected to involve the octo-shark [[Lusca]], and is part of the structural [[quadrant]] system on the bubble.
* The Akula is a reference vessel from a prior floor, a massive submarine used previously, compared to the current production vessel.
* The Akula is a submarine associated with [[Shamus Chaindrive]], docked to access the [[Necropolis of Anser]], and [[Ruckus]] is described as having lost contact with the Akula until recently.
* The Akula is a giant submarine encountered in the [[Water Quadrant]], where the party broke into it and it later fired a torpedo that contributed to the collapse and displacement events described by [[Chris Andrews 2]].
* The Akula is a massive submarine located 500 meters under the surface and physically attached to the base of the [[necropolis]], acting as a pump, and containing the bridge, fire control, boss chambers, and flooded/dead sections the party must navigate.
* The Akula is a large, opulent submarine (location/room cluster) whose bridge is the primary scene for the chapter, described as flooded, torn in half by [[Lusca]], and containing multiple lootable corpses and [[portals]].
* The Akula is the submarine/boat in which Carl and company are trapped and which is swallowed by [[Lusca]], with interior spaces like the bridge and stateroom being central action sites, and the vessel's bow being later visible inside the creature's throat.
* The Akula is a named [[quadrant]] or boss-referenced region mentioned as analogous to other [[quadrant]] "cheats" (likely tied to [[Lusca]] the octo-shark), part of the floor's larger structure.
* The Akula is a previously encountered massive submarine referenced for scale and comparison to the current [[Production Vessel]]'s size and environment.
