# the_red_line

• One-way transit subway line shown on the station map with stops from station 11 to station 435.
• Station #83 is marked along this line and transit behavior and [[stairwell]] placements are governed by map rules.
• Transit route shown on the station map at [[Station #83]] with stations numbered from 11 to 435; includes marked transfer/[[saferoom]] dots and the local dot labeled "You are here. Station #83."
