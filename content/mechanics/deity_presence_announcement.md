# Deity Presence Announcement
A system announcement block that notifies players of divine presence: "You are in the presence of a deity. The Scavenger’s Daughter has opened her eyes. You are now within range of the god Harpocrates." It also describes the temporary status effects (e.g., [[Pied Piper]]) that apply to nearby players.
