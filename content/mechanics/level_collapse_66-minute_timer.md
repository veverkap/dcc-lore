# Level Collapse (66-minute timer)
The imminent floor-destruction mechanic driven by [[Emberus]]'s threat: the narrative repeatedly references 66 (and subsequently shorter) minute timers until the [[ninth floor]] is melted. This active system condition forces emergency faction and evacuation actions.
