# Wild Goose Chase
An achievement/system notification triggered when [[dungeon crawler carl]] attempts to taste the [[Unknown Potion (Lemonade)]]. The message reveals the potion is ordinary lemonade and serves as a narrative/gameplay misdirection during the [[Denise]] fight.
