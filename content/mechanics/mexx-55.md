# [[Mexx-55]]
A floating, jet-black, metallic disk AI that briefs [[dungeon crawler carl]] and [[Donut]] upon arrival in a small rental trailer. It announces leasing details (Senegal Production Systems, Unlimited), program assignment (the show [[death watch extreme]]), and gives instructions for the holo/segment.
