# safe_room_rules_do_not_apply_in_the_main_square

• Safe room rules do NOT apply in the main square of [[Hungry Eyes Vendor Village]].
• Protections normally granted in safe rooms are disabled within the [[Main Square (Hungry Eyes Vendor Village)]], changing combat and interaction expectations.
• This explicit system rule directly affects player behavior and risk while in the square.
