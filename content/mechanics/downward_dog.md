# downward_dog

• The [[Downward Dog]] is a station [[bar]] where [[Growler Gary]] works, and it's the site of repeated killing and resurrection events.
• It serves as the staging point for harvesting [[gnoll]] hands and planning the interdiction cart operation.
• The [[bar]] is located in [[station 75]] and has a sign "Oi! No hobgoblins!".
• It's not a true [[saferoom]], and it's where [[Growler Gary]] works and where much of the chapter's early events occur.
