# Pustule
The Level 30 hobgoblin proprietor of [[Hobgobs and Boom Sticks]]. Described as large, diseased-looking, and talkative; knowledgeable about explosives and pricing (sells [[hobgoblin dynamite]], [[Hobgoblin Dynamite]], [[hob-lobber]]s, [[Smoke Bomb]]s, and other supplies). Interacts directly with [[dungeon crawler carl]] and [[Donut]].
