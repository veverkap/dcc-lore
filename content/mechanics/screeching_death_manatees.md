# Screeching Death Manatees
Named hostile fauna tied to [[The Caves of the Screeching Death Manatees]] environment. One massive manatee appears in this chapter, dragging a bus-sized creature and crushing a bug-like vehicle, demonstrating their direct threat to racers.

# Screeching Death Manatees
The species name used in-system for the grotesque uplifted manatees (e.g., [[Miss Talulah]]). The species originates from the terraformed planet [[Red Terror Place of Family Adventure]] and is explicitly labeled in the game as hostile, themed fauna.

# [[Screeching Death Manatees]]
Species name and branded monster type introduced in the chapter (originating from a failed terraforming/world uplift program). Described as dangerous mounts/obstacles on the course and exemplified by [[Miss Talulah]]. The species name is tied to the horror-theme branding of the planet [[Red Terror Place of Family Adventure]].
