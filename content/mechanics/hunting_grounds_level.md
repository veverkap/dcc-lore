# hunting_grounds_level

• The [[Hunting Grounds level]] is a restricted floor in the [[desperado club]] where hunters and NPCs are confined.
• It contains a main [[bar]], [[restaurant]], guild hall corridor, and a maze-like layout with enhanced guards.
• The floor is where trapped hunters and NPCs are located during certain events.
• It has bars, [[bathrooms]] with secret [[stairwell]] connections, guild halls (including the [[Explosives Guild]] and [[Rogue's Guild]]).
