# Hunting Grounds level
The [[Hunting Grounds level]] is a distinct floor of the [[desperado club]] where hunters and certain NPCs are confined; access is restricted from the top floor. It contains a main bar, restaurant, guild hall corridor, and is maze-like with enhanced guards. Many trapped hunters and NPCs are located here during the events described.

# Hunting Grounds level
A distinct floor of the [[desperado club]] where trapped [[Hunters]] are held and barred from moving to other floors. It contains bars, bathrooms with secret stairwell connections, guild halls (including the [[Explosives Guild]] and [[Rogue's Guild]]), and a maze-like layout.
