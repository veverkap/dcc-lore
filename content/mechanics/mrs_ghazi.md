# [[Mrs. Ghazi]]
A named sand ooze encountered as a boss in the chapter. Identified by the AI as a "Sand Ooze" and a "Level 52 Borough Boss" and described as a minion of [[Psamathe]]. Obsesses over and protects a human (the love interest of its master), and houses its glowing core under a recliner in [[Ghazi's Workshop]].
