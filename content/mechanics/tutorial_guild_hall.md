# Tutorial Guild Hall
The small, classroom-sized guildhall where the narrator meets [[Mordecai]] and [[Donut]]. It contains a fireplace, bed, shelves with framed photos, chairs, and an oval gray carpet. Introduced to the player via a system notification and serves as a temporary safe hub near the dungeon entrance.
