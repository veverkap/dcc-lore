# tutorial_guild_hall

• The Tutorial Guild Hall is a small, classroom-sized location where the narrator meets Mordecai and Donut.
• It features a fireplace, bed, shelves with framed photos, chairs, and an oval gray carpet.
• Introduced to the player via a system notification and serves as a temporary safe hub near the [[dungeon]] entrance.
