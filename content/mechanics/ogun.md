# [[Ogun]]
God of Blacksmithery and Armor. An old, outsider deity temporarily summoned to the floor; level 250 and invulnerable while present on that floor. Described as a buff, dark-skinned smith with a war hammer, white glowing eyes, and a celestial anvil; famed for forging four-legged armor and for being ruthless in meting out punishment. Interacts with worshippers (removing grace/abilities) and follows summoning rules.
