# college_of_larracos

• A location whose [[saferoom]] was suitable to be designated as an airport for the [[Airship Portal]].
• Meets the [[portal]]'s rule about "open [[air]] above" saferooms.
• Where allies (including [[Rend]] initially) and defending mages were positioned.
• Mentioned as a fallback and casualty point during the [[tower]] defense.
