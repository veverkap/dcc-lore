# Guild Jobs
A newly unlocked quest sub-tab (system UI mechanic) introduced by the "Secondary Gig" achievement. It tracks jobs issued by groups like the [[Guild of Suffering]] and is required to accept/complete contracts such as the assassination of [[Astrid]].
