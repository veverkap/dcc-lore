# pasty_inferno

• System-announced, mandatory quest triggered by killing [[Great Rusty]].
• Quest orders players to find and kill [[Maurice]], chieftain of the northern tribe, with explicit reward text and a mandatory progress lock.
• Killing [[Maurice]] triggers a quest completion announcement: "Quest Complete! Pasty Inferno."
