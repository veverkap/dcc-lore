# Pasty Inferno
System-announced, mandatory quest triggered by the killing of [[Great Rusty]]. Orders players to find and kill [[Maurice]] (chieftain of the northern tribe) with explicit reward text and a mandatory progress lock: it blocks race completion until [[Maurice]] is dead.
