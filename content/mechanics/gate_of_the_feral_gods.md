# gate_of_the_feral_gods

* [[Gate of the Feral Gods]] is a legendary or quest-related [[gate]], part of the broader lore.
* It was opened by [[Katia Grim]] on the [[Sixth Floor]], credited as the reason she received the [[celestial prize box]].
* It was discovered by [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]] and inspired [[justice lights team]]'s trap design.
* It was used by [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]] and [[elle mcgib]] to enter the [[Madness]] territory and initiate the operation to find and kill [[Houston]].
* It is a [[portal]] used to enter the [[Madness]] territory and the [[Nothing]].
* It is a System Announcement New Quest: collect three artifact pieces (two [[pocket]] watches and a winding box) from [[Henrik the Changeling]], [[Commandant Kane]], and [[The Mad Dune Mage]] and assemble them.
* Opening the [[gate]] allows transit between places but rips into the depths of [[The Nothing]] and releases an ancient [[Feral Gods]].
* It is a named [[dungeon]] feature referenced as a potential equalizer in the upcoming play of the crawl.
* It is a named [[gate]] that formerly provided access routes tied to the [[Pineapple Cabaret]]; noted as "gone and broken" in the interrogation.
* It is a teleportation [[gate]] used by the party to travel to [[Architect Houston's Castle]].
