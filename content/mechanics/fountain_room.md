# fountain_room

• A large room inside the castle containing the glass fountain and statue/depiction of [[Lika]], which was shattered by [[Mongo]]’s sniff, and was crystalized during the transmutation.
• The upper-level room where [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]] and [[Donut]] [[stage]] themselves while [[Katia]] and [[Ghazi]] remain near the [[stairwell]], used as the drop point for the distraction and entry to the ooze chamber.
• Adjacent room where [[Donut]] and [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]] [[stage]] while preparing the plan, from which the party watches the ooze move up the [[stairs]].
