# stairwell_rule

• [[Stairwells]] are the only allowed method for moving between floors, with no skipping floors allowed.
• [[Stairwells]] may be gated until conditions are met.
• [[Stairwells]] themselves cannot be removed, destroyed, or placed into inventory.
