# small_bugbear_settlement

• The Small [[Bugbear]] Settlement is a damaged [[town]] near the main city of [[Zockau]].
• The [[town]] is described as having bombed buildings, looted structures, and a disturbed [[graveyard]].
• The [[town]] is also classified as being looted, bombed, and ringed with traps.
