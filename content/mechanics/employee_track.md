# employee_track

• The [[portal cart]] was positioned on a specific track, described as wider with wheels that fit inside the employee track.
• The employee track is a wider track used to position the [[Portal Cart]] and guide movement of equipment in the [[trainyard]] area.
• The employee track is part of the station/yard infrastructure used to move [[portals]] and carts, with wheels that fit on it.
