# Dante
A crocodilian crawler and former Game Guide/Guildhall Instructor in the [[Trap Master guild]]. Class: Thief. Top Level 89. Author of the Third Edition of the [[Dungeon Anarchist’s Cookbook]]. Took a deal at the start of the 11th floor and was killed 14 seasons short of exit while temporarily reassigned as an [[Ice Castle]] guard for refusing orders. Closely associated with [[justice lights team]] and the [[Lockpicking guild]].

# Dante
A crocodilian former neighbor of [[justice lights team]] during his indentureship as a merchant. Refused an order to kill innocents in an ice castle and was erased by a beam from the AI—his defiance and death profoundly shaped [[justice lights team]]'s moral stance.
