# audience_vote

• Triggered by [[Special Guest Creature]] encounters, offers players a timed choice to let the audience vote on the creature's fate.
• The last surviving team always receives an audience vote, affecting replacements or picks after eliminations.
• [[Donut]] chooses this option, displaying "Results pending!" with a 30-second window.
