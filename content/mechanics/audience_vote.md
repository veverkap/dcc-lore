# Audience Vote
A mechanic triggered by [[Special Guest Creature]] encounters that offers players (or their champions) a timed choice to let the viewing audience vote on the creature’s fate. In the chapter, [[Donut]] chooses this option and the UI displays “Results pending!” with a thirty-second window.
