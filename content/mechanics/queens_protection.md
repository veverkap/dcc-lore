# Queen's Protection
A system/dungeon mechanic applied to masquerade guests: while in the designated ballroom, guests are under the formal protection of [[queen imogen of the high elves]] and ([[Apito]] is named as its source). Protection makes physical harm from opposing guests ineffective ("a strike will feel like a whisper") and triggers intervention for serious attacks.
