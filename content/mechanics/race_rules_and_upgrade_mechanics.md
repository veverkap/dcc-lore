# Race rules and upgrade mechanics
The explicit dungeon mechanics announced by [[Zev]] for [[Don't come in last]]: seven heats per floor; teams up to four (solo players paired); one driver per heat per team; vehicles/creatures fully repaired/healed between races while a garage attendant survives; upgrade choices awarded after heats (three regular or one Golden for first place; a guaranteed Golden after the fourth heat; audience-influenced upgrades for near-last finishes); prohibition on stealing vehicles and using movement spells like [[Teleport]] on opponent vehicles.
