# race_rules_and_upgrade_mechanics

• Seven heats per floor in the race.
• Teams can have up to four members (solo players can be paired).
• One driver per [[heat]] per team.
• Vehicles/creatures are fully repaired/healed between races if a [[garage]] attendant survives.
• Upgrade choices are awarded after heats: three regular or one Golden for first place, a guaranteed Golden after the fourth [[heat]], and audience-influenced [[upgrades]] for near-last finishes.
• Prohibition on stealing vehicles and using movement spells like [[Teleport]] on opponent vehicles.
