# Worship system (gods)
Dungeon-system rules governing how players may worship deities such as [[Kuraokami]] and [[Emberus]]: players can opt to worship at gods/temples/scrolls, gain benefits tied to rules (e.g., daily rituals), receive boons after consecutive compliance, suffer debuffs or permanent penalties for missed days, be smited, and receive intrusive messages from gods.
