# worship_system_gods

• Players can worship deities such as [[Kuraokami]] and [[Emberus]] at gods/temples/scrolls.
• Worshiping grants benefits tied to daily rituals.
• Consecutive compliance earns boons.
• Missed days incur debuffs or permanent penalties.
• Failure can result in smiting.
• Gods may send intrusive messages.
