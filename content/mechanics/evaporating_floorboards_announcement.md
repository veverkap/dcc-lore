# Evaporating Floorboards Announcement
A System Announcement broadcast over the city loudspeaker describing a druid spell causing supposedly indestructible wooden floorboards to evaporate, creating sinkholes and players falling off the map. Presented as an active dungeon/system event impacting local gameplay and safety.
