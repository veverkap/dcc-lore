# Devotion to Emberus Notification
System announcement that appears when a player becomes an adherent of [[Emberus]], listing obligations (daily blood offering, 5% tithe, Hellik ban, church quests) and benefits (temple access, map indicators, Burn immunity/infliction, Club Vanquisher access).
