# city_of_zockau

• Northern city on [[The Hunting Grounds]] where hunters are temporarily sequestered during the chapter's grace period; identified as particularly dangerous and linked to alien attackers per [[Ian]]'s warning.
• Major city on the sixth floor; all hunters are temporarily transferred there during the grace period and many unguarded exits to the seventh floor are said to be located nearby.
