# weird_shit_alley

* A northeastern alley in [[Hump Town]] pointed out by [[Clay Dromedarian]] as the place for odd or illicit goods and services.
* Contains the [[town]]'s single public [[saferoom]] and areas people avoid, referenced as a destination for wounded teammates.
* Houses the Acrotomophilia Oasis (A.O.) tavern.
* Becomes a fallback defensive line during the gerbil onslaught.
* Named alley containing a [[saferoom]] where [[Katia]] intended to open the [[emergency]] silver benefactor box from [[Princess Formidable of the Skull Empire]].
* Ruined street/ [[town]] area containing a safe room and [[Dungeon [[Crawler]] Carl]]'s current personal space; the place where the [[Emergency Benefactor Box]] was opened and the [[Enchanted Xistera Extension Slot. Head-throwing attachment.]] obtained.
* A neighborhood housing the single public [[saferoom]] for those without personal space; noted as a risky area but the only formal [[saferoom]] available to some characters.
* A defensive street area inside Hump [[Town]] to which defenders are pushed back during the gerbil assault.
* Ruined urban area containing a [[saferoom]]/personal space and the [[pub]] where Carl briefly shelters to open the [[Emergency Benefactor Box]].
