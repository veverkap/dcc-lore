# [[Apartment]]
The residential room/apartment into which [[dungeon crawler carl]] and the party crash after the floor beneath them evaporates due to [[Self-Immolation]]. The building's interior burns and inhabitants are present (including [[Li Na]], [[Li Jun]], and others).
