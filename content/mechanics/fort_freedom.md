# fort_freedom

* The former [[river]] palace of the Naiad, now a fortress and the seat of the [[Naiad Confederacy]] leadership.
* The primary objective for [[Tsarina Signet]]'s assault in the quest [[The Vengeance of the Daughter]].
* A fortress where the leaders of the [[Naiad Confederacy]] are holed up.
* The party plans to assault with [[Signet]] and allies; mostly submerged and central to an upcoming operation.
* The ruined naiad castle/island fortress sitting off the [[river]], [[target]] of [[Signet]]'s assault.
* The castle under assault in the chapter; its alarm system, defensive layout (front-[[gate]] convergence), throne room, and degraded governance are central to the assault plan.
* The castle/fort location referenced (including its throne room) where the boss incursion and prior fundraiser took place; setting for the encounter and backstory.
* The former naiad palace targeted for assault in the chapter; largely submerged and a strategic objective for [[Signet]]'s team.
* The ruined castle [[target]] of [[Signet]]'s assault; sits partially submerged offshore with broken ramparts, missing bridge access, and numerous traps and alarms guarding approaches from the [[forest]] and [[river]].
* The castle and primary location of the chapter's assault; features a [[river]] moat, defensive alarms ("ah-luga, ah-luga"), a [[Filter Vent]], a [[Throne Room]], and multiple access points (main [[gate]], rear [[air]] area).
* Named location referenced by [[Samantha]] (the "lightning-shooting [[river]] monster that has taken over the throne room of Fort Freedom"), implying the boss or similar monsters have invaded its throne room.
* A location referenced as recently liberated from the [[naiad confederacy]] prior to the chapter's events; used as backstory to explain the narrator's absence from saferooms.
