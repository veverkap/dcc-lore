# el_capitolio_nacional_de_cuba

• The rotunda/starting interior location in [[Havana]] where [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]] and [[Donut]] begin exploration on the [[Eighth Floor: The Ghosts of Earth]].
• A marked [[stairwell]]/exit location in the [[Cuba]] zone; an [[Admin Message]] notifies the squad that they hold a key for its exit vestibule, required to unlock the [[stairwell]].
• The capitol building where the main battle takes place, featuring a broken entrance, collapsing tiles, [[stairwells]], and an exit [[stairwell]] whose doorway is later warned to become unusable.
• The named building/location where the action occurs; the system issues a specific [[stairwell]] warning tied to this site and keys for its exits are central to the chapter's dilemma.
• The specific starting building (the capitol rotunda/room) where [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]] and [[Donut]] spawn on the [[Eighth Floor: The Ghosts of Earth]].
• The marked exit vestibule for the [[Cuba]] zone; an [[Admin Message]]-referenced location that requires a key to unlock access to [[stairwells]] leading to the next floor.
• The specific capitol building in [[Havana]] where the primary battle occurs: burning, partially destroyed, and the scene of totem combat, the worm [[Basquiat]]'s rampage, and the eventual death of [[Quan]].
