# mantaur_guard

• An armed mantaur guard was seen admiring himself in a mirror within [[Club Vanquisher]], serving as a notable sentry near the [[portal]] exit.
• Generic mantaur guards enforce shop and club rules, such as limiting one-visit-per-day, and try to force occupants out of the shop.
