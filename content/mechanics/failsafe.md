# failsafe

• An explicit protocol referenced by [[Orren the Liaison]] and the [[Syndicate]] that could catastrophically destroy a solar system's star.
• A system-level mechanism debated by the [[Syndicate]] that can vaporize a star system to prevent AI takeover or catastrophic events.
• An [[emergency]] system-level mechanism proposed by [[Princess Formidable of the Skull Empire]] as a last-resort measure.
• A protective game/system rule mentioned as likely disabled by the [[System AI]] (per [[Orren the Liaison]]), enabling the AI to extend control beyond normal boundaries.
• A last-resort system action that would "kill us all" if pulled during a major crisis, presented as a catastrophic fail mechanism for the floor's systems or production controls.
• A system contingency mentioned by [[Zev]] and attributed to [[Cascadia]]'s prior mention, with unknown function but treated as a dangerous system-level measure.
• An extreme Syndicate/galactic protocol invoked to destroy a star/system to prevent perceived AI takeover, central to the political debate during the [[emergency]] council session and feared for its genocidal, economic, and political consequences.
• The Syndicate-level planetary/system destruction mechanism discussed throughout the council session, invocation of which would vaporize the system and is central to the political debate and existential risk posed in the chapter.
