# hobgoblin

• Playable limited race option with explosive/trap bonuses: automatic +3 to trap/explosive skills, faster progression, access to Hobgoblin Sapper Workshops, stat changes (e.g., -5 Charisma), exclusive requirements: level five Explosives Handling.
• Hostile humanoid mob associated with explosives, repair shops, and the small-scale yard of flatbed cars at [[Station 75]].
• Named corpses found at [[Station 75]], likely transit crash-interdiction personnel, contextualizing the overrun of transit security.
