# [[Valve Activation Mechanic]]
Dungeon rule/interaction referenced during the chapter: a physical valve opens when turned, and electrifying metal contacts may trigger additional releases. The mechanic underlies [[dungeon crawler carl]]'s plan to use outside electrification in concert with in-bubble manipulations.
