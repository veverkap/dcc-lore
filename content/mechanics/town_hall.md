# town_hall

• Multi-level, secured municipal building in the camel [[town]] with exterior balconies, reinforced doors, an office ringed second floor, a [[subterranean]] trap door leading to [[catacombs]], and high guard presence.
• Site of the infiltration, changeling encounters, and the discovery of the [[Necropolis of Anser]] map and [[Wynne]].
• Central, tall building in the [[town]] that is attacked and collapses in the chapter; site of [[subterranean]] basements, buried items, and rescue efforts.
• Collapsed municipal building whose [[basement]] chamber hides [[Wynne]]; its destruction and sealing complicate rescue and the [[town]]'s code with the gnomes.
• High-security municipal building in the camel-run [[town]] that Carl's team infiltrates; contains offices, balconies, a secured vault door, and a trap door leading to the [[subterranean]] area where [[Wynne]] is chained.
• Tallest municipal building in the [[town]] that is attacked, set on fire, and collapses during the chapter; contains a [[basement]] reportedly holding important collateral or items.
• Collapsed municipal building in [[Hump Town]] where [[Henrik]] directs rescue and where the [[basement]]/[[necropolis]] entrance was sealed.
