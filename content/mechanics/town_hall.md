# Town Hall
Multi-level, secured municipal building in the camel town with exterior balconies, reinforced doors, an office ringed second floor, a subterranean trap door leading to catacombs, and high guard presence. Site of the infiltration, changeling encounters, and the discovery of the [[Necropolis of Anser]] map and [[Wynne]].
