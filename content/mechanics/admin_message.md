# admin_message

• An automated system announcement in-universe that communicates regency, sponsor, and court rulings to all participants and viewers.
• Communicates sponsor loss, ownership changes, and auction notices.
• Appears in the chapter declaring a sponsor removal and announcing a time-limited auction to replace the missing sponsor.
• Directly affects participants and production logistics.
