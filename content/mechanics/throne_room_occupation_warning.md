# throne_room_occupation_warning

• Warning: a throne room owned by the player is occupied by [[Madness]] enemy forces, and if not reclaimed within six hours, the castle and associated [[land]] will be ceded to the Dream.
• Triggers a six-hour timer and enforces territorial ownership mechanics between [[Madness]] and [[Dream]].
