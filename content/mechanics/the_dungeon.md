# The Dungeon
The central setting: an 18-level labyrinth that swallowed the world. Described as an urban-to-subterranean conversion of human structures, it contains the [[Eighteen Levels]], monsters like the [[goblin]], loot distribution by [[patrons]] in the form of [[loot box]], and enforces rules such as the [[Level Time Limit]] and [[No Exit Rule]].
