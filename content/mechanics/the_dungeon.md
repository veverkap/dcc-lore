# the_dungeon

• The [[Dungeon]] is an 18-level labyrinth that swallowed the world, containing the Eighteen Levels, goblins, [[loot]] distribution by patrons, and enforces rules like Level Time Limit and No Exit Rule.
• The game environment where crawlers run floors and face mortality, the central setting behind crawl, release deals, and systemic abuses.
• The overarching setting containing floors, creatures, and trans-dimensional phenomena, referenced as the space where changelings and other entities exist.
• The game environment where crawlers operate, containing floors and mechanics governing transfers, adjutants, and [[Ascendency]] games.
• The dangerous location containing many Residuals and an unstable AI that influenced sponsorship dynamics and Harbinger's warnings.
• The larger game/constraint that contains This Floor, governs exits and re-entries, and traps ships in orbit and forces survivors into heats and deals.
