# abandoned_arena

• A variant environment option for the [[Mock Battle Arena]] with fewer crowd effects, described as the same [[arena]] without the screaming crowd and with a cracked dome and blue sun.
• A quieter variant of the [[Arena of the Eulogist]] with a cracked dome and altered lighting (blue sun), used by [[Donut]] and [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]] to reduce crowd noise and alter ambiance during mock fights.
