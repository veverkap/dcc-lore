# crafting_studio

• A major room in the communal personal space, placed by [[Princess Donut]] and upgradable (seen upgraded to [[Enhanced Crafting Studio]]).
• Holds crafting tables and environmental [[upgrades]].
• Can receive [[upgrades]] such as the [[Enhanced Crafting Studio]].
• Size and capacity scale with personal-space level.
• Room adjacent to the [[saferoom]], referenced as a place where crafting and potential pet-related calls (e.g., to [[Mordecai]]) might occur.
• Designated room within the communal space for crafting.
• [[Upgrades]] like the [[Enhanced Crafting [[Studio]] (Upgrade)]] can be applied to this room.
