# wheel_of_fortune_game

• An alternate high-stakes game in the [[desperado club]] where the [[100,000 Gold Coin [[Casino]] Chip]] may be used instead of the roulette table.
• A vertical, spinning carnival-style prize wheel inside the [[Casino]] with many dangerous and valuable outcomes (e.g., "[[Nothing]]!", 500,000 gold, Legendary boxes), operated by [[Tito]] and playable with comp chips or gold.
