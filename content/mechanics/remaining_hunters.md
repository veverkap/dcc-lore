# remaining_hunters

• A dynamic system stat listing how many active hunters remain in the [[dungeon]], presented in the HUD beneath [[crawler]] counts and used to gauge threat density.
• A system counter showing how many independent hunter players are still active on the floor, displayed as a number and used tactically by crawlers to assess risk and timing.
