# Babababoon
Level 17 exclusive mob described as a grotesque, human-faced baboon hybrid that causes chaotic behavior. The mob entry includes a system-style warning: it is "suffering from the DTs" and is in stage two of three (stage two doubles strength and removes reasoning for intelligent mobs). Created by crossing an earth baboon with a violent population; noted as dangerous and idiotic chaos incarnate. Encounters occur in transit stations and on tracks near the [[Nightmare]].
