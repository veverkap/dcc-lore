# floor_12

• A later [[dungeon]] floor where exiting might be worth negotiating, according to [[Mordecai]].
• A higher floor referenced as the destination for a possible celestial attendant position that [[Katia]] could be sent to under her [[deal]] with [[Huanxin Jinx]] and [[Eileithyia]].
• A destination tied to attendant promotion; [[Katia]] hopes to become a [[Floor 12]] attendant to enable [[Princess Donut]]'s departure.
