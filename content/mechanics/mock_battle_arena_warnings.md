# mock_battle_arena_warnings

• Incomplete decks are flagged upon entering the [[Mock Battle Arena]].
• Consumable cards will be lost if used.
• Generated mobs cannot harm players in the [[arena]], but environmental hazards still apply.
• Cautions about self-harm and death in the [[arena]] are presented.
