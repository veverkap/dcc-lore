# Mock Battle Arena Warnings
System rules and warnings presented on entering the [[Mock Battle Arena]]: incomplete decks are flagged, consumable cards will be lost if used, generated mobs cannot harm players in this location (environmental hazards still apply), and cautions about self-harm/death in the arena.
