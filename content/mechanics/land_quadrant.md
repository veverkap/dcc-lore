# land_quadrant

• The [[Land Quadrant]] is a regional division of the [[necropolis]]/level system, where Gwen operates and Carl coordinates support between zones.
• It is a separate in-world zone where [[Katia]] and others operated, and the party plans to reunite and move their house there.
• The region is subject to the circular sandstorm and the Red Equinox changes, and contains terrestrial objectives that the party believes must be secured prior to attempting the [[Necropolis]].
• It is a geographic/ personal-space [[quadrant]] mentioned by [[Katia]] as the destination she attempted to move the house to while [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]] and others were detained.
• One of the primary quadrants in the bubble, it is subject to sandstorms, electrical [[tower]] mechanics, and the location of the [[sandcastle]] and [[necropolis]].
• It is a separate [[quadrant]] of the [[necropolis]] mentioned as an escape destination, with some parties choosing to flee there via escape [[tunnels]] from the [[Akula]].
