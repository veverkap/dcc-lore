# trailer

• The enclosed encounter chamber beyond the [[Vending Machine]] that opens when tokens are used; the [[Motorcycle]] is parked outside it and it's the likely location of the final adversary and the [[Key]].
• The immediate [[battlefield]] where the [[hydra]] emerges (charred husk of a trailer with hospital bed and [[kitchen]] remains); central set-piece for this episode's fight.
• [[Trailer]] is the sealed fight location referenced at the farm edge that opens when tokens are used in the vending machine; Donut expects Ferdinand to be inside.
• The old trailer in the trailer park where the combat begins; burns open to reveal the hospital bed and becomes the central [[arena]] for the fight.
