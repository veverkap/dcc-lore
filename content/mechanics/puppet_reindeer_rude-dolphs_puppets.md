# Puppet Reindeer (Rude-Dolph's Puppets)
The eight gore-covered servant reindeer animated by [[Rude-Dolph the Blood-Nosed Slay-Deer]]. Act as the boss’s puppets and must be killed before the main puppet-master is vulnerable. Include named individuals like [[Blitzen]] and [[Comet]] among them.
