# iglesia_san_hilarion

• A dark, empty Catholic [[church]] where the protagonist and [[Donut]] appear after transfer into the [[Cuba]] zone; contains a [[saferoom]] door and is the local spawn point for that transfer.
• A Catholic [[church]]/ safe room where the narrator and [[Princess Donut]] are transferred in [[Cuba]]; the setting where [[Imani]] is found and where the squad stages a rescue.
