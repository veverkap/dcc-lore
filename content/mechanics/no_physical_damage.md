# No Physical Damage
A system rule applied during the [[Borough Boss]] encounter indicating the party's own physical attacks will not damage the boss; in this fight the players had to use environmental damage (objects in the house) to hurt [[Denise]].

# No Physical Damage
A mechanic stamped on the encounter that prevents players' direct physical attacks from damaging the boss. Combined with [[No Magic]] it forces players to find alternative damage sources (environmental objects) to hurt [[Denise]].
