# no_physical_damage

• A system rule applied during the [[Borough Boss]] encounter prevents the party's own physical attacks from damaging the boss.
• Players had to use environmental damage (objects in the house) to hurt [[Denise]].
• The mechanic prevents direct physical attacks from damaging the boss, forcing players to find alternative damage sources (environmental objects) to hurt [[Denise]].
