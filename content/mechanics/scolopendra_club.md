# scolopendra_club

• A high-value in-system venue where over half the galaxy's wealth has congregated and where many leaders and influential figures are trapped during the crawl, cited as a reason not to trigger the [[Failsafe]].
• A high-value venue where over half the galaxy's wealth is reported gathered and where many important figures (including relatives of leaders) are trapped—cited by [[Prime Minister Glory]] as a reason to avoid detonating the [[Failsafe]].
