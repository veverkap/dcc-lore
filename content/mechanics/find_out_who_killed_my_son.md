# find_out_who_killed_my_son

• A quest update notification tied to Emberus' arrival and his demand to interrogate Samantha regarding the murder of Geyrun.
• Functions as an active system quest trigger that raises the threat level on the ninth floor.
• System quest issued directly by Emberus to Carl as a deity-assigned task.
• Objective: investigate the murder of Geyrun and report to the high cleric at the Emberus Shrine; no time limit but exiting the 18th floor early incurs a [[smite]].
