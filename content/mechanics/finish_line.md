# finish_line

• The Finish Line is a location marked by a black-and-white checked archway where all race tracks converge.
• The System Announcement declares placements at the Finish Line.
• The race terminus is marked by a black-and-white-checkered arch and converging roads/spotlights.
• The Finish Line is partially blocked by a massive [[Kaiju skull]] in this chapter.
• The Finish Line is visible from the starting area and the focus of the migrating [[Sugar Hermit]]s.
• The Finish Line is the [[lobby]] destination of the [[L'Engle Building]] that teams must reach.
• The Finish Line is the end-goal for the [[heat]] and the race objective and exit destination.
• Teams are trying to "get to the finish line" after clearing gates.
