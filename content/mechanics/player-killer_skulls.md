# player-killer_skulls

• A status/mechanic indicating involvement in player-killing, warning of permanent expulsion from the game for repeated behavior.
• Functions as a punitive/reputational rule within the [[dungeon]]'s social systems.
• Marks players as player-killers, attracting hunter attention (especially around the [[Sixth Floor]]) and making them high-priority targets in faction conflicts.
• An in-game status indicator/penalty mechanic applied to players who have killed other crawlers, leading to social ostracization (as seen with [[Mees Koning]]).
