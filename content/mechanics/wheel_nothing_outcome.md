# Wheel Nothing! Outcome
A specific dangerous game mechanic on the [[Wheel of Fortune (Game)]] and other casino games: the "Nothing!" slot triggers horrific effects (e.g., trapdoors swallowing NPCs) rather than truly nothing, and is flagged as a special negative outcome.
