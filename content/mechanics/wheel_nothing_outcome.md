# wheel_nothing_outcome

• The "Wheel [[Nothing]]! Outcome" is a dangerous game mechanic that triggers horrific effects, such as trapdoors swallowing NPCs, instead of truly [[nothing]].
• This mechanic is found on the [[Wheel of Fortune (Game)]] and other [[casino]] games.
