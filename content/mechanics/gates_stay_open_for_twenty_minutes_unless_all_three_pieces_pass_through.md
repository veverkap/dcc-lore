# Gates stay open for twenty minutes unless all three pieces pass through
Explicit dungeon rule revealed in Ghazi's notes: a summoned gate remains open for twenty minutes by default unless all three gate components are transported through it earlier, affecting timing and tactical choices for [[dungeon crawler carl]]'s plan.
