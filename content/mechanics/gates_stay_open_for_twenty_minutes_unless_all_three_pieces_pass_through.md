# gates_stay_open_for_twenty_minutes_unless_all_three_pieces_pass_through

• Gates stay open for twenty minutes unless all three pieces pass through.
• A summoned [[gate]] remains open for twenty minutes by default unless all three [[gate]] components are transported through it earlier.
