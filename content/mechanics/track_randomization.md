# [[Track Randomization]]
A system announcement rule (delivered by [[Zev]]) stating that each track has four heats, the [[Pit Stop]] combines four tracks, and each heat will roll to determine which path it continues on. When the leading team in a heat times out, other teams are reassigned according to this random draw.
