# Boss Battle!
Loud system announcement that initiates the boss encounter. Includes on-screen messaging ("You have discovered the lair of a Neighborhood Boss!") and an announcer-style audio cue that echoes through the chamber.

# Boss Battle!
System announcement that triggers when players discover a Neighborhood Boss lair. The [[Boss Battle!]] message appears in-world, freezes time briefly for the descriptive mugshot and versus text, and signals the start of the encounter with the [[Neighborhood Boss]] UI.
