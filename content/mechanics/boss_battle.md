# boss_battle

• Loud system announcement that initiates the boss encounter, including on-screen messaging and an announcer-style audio cue.
• Triggers when players discover a Neighborhood Boss lair, freezing time briefly for a descriptive mugshot and [[versus]] text.
• Signals the start of the encounter with the Neighborhood Boss UI.
• Appears when the party engages a major enemy, signaling the start of a combat encounter.
• Triggered by the thorn room to emphasize the choice and potential combat outcome.
• Signals the start of a major combat encounter, appearing as the party is immobilized and the vampiric boss attacks.
• System-announced combat mode triggered within the thorn room if certain shrine choices are not taken.
