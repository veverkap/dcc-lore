# guildhall

* A safe indoor location inside the [[dungeon]], classified as a safe area where players can open [[loot]] boxes and rest.
* The location on the floor where [[Donut]] is reported to be ensconced and protected from immediate danger.
* A social/organizational location where allies like [[Katia]] and [[Donut]] position themselves and prepare materials for the plan.
* A common area and gathering place for the [[crawler]] group; contains couches and meeting space where [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]] writes a summons and where characters like [[Louis]], [[Firas]], and [[Mordecai]] observe the aftermath.
* A room adjacent to the treehouse [[pub]] that served as an escape route/goal in the [[pub]] fight; entering it was a tactical objective in the encounter.
* The starting indoor location where [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]] and company prepare and exit toward the [[pub]] and the street on the rescue mission.
* [[Guildhall]] is the building through which [[Katia]] and [[Louis]] enter; serves as an access point adjacent to the [[saferoom]] and [[pub]].
* The larger communal area adjacent to the [[saferoom]] and training room where the party convenes; referenced as the hub for guild [[upgrades]] (e.g., mercenary training arcade) and social interaction.
* The group's communal living area where the cleaner bot, strippers, and various activities occur; central hub for planning and recuperation between transfers.
* The central defensive and supply hub where key characters ([[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]], [[Donut]], [[Rosetta]], [[justice lights team]], etc.) convene to plan, buy [[upgrades]], and prepare the [[the castle]]'s defenses.
* The safe location where [[Donut]] is reported to be "ensconced." It functions as a refuge and hub for guild-affiliated crawlers and is contrasted with the public production areas of [[CrawlCon]].
* Location where [[Donut]] reports being located and where allies are assembling and prepping props for [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]]'s arrival.
* [[Crawler]] common area and [[saferoom]] staging point used by [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]] and allies for the escape/ambush plan; where players regroup after the blast.
* The party's base from which they depart; connected to an attached [[pub]] with hockey paraphernalia. Contains an [[emberus]] at the back of the crafting room.
* The main communal area outside the [[saferoom]]; includes training room access and the main space for party logistics and equipment pooling.
* A room adjoining the [[Safe Room]] where [[Li Na]] and others are waiting; referenced as the place everyone is assembled and where key confrontations are expected to occur.
