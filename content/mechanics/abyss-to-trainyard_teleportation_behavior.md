# Abyss-to-trainyard teleportation behavior
Observed behavior: carts hitting portals at the abyss edge can "punch through" back to trainyards (teleportation routing), sometimes returning intact or flipping into holding areas; routing depends on switching station alignment and can send mobs or entire structures to unintended yards.
