# abyss-to-trainyard_teleportation_behavior

• Carts hitting [[portals]] at the [[abyss]] edge can "punch through" back to trainyards, teleporting intact or flipping into holding areas.
• Routing depends on switching station alignment and can send mobs or entire structures to unintended yards.
