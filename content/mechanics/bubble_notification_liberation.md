# Bubble Notification: Liberation
A system bubble that appears after [[Denise]] is defeated: "The Commandant’s quarters of the [[Wasteland]] have been successfully occupied. The Air Quadrant has been liberated!" Grants travel and status changes (e.g., free travel for crawlers originating in the Air Quadrant).
