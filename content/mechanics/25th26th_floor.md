# 25th26th_floor

• The 25th/26th floor is the section of the collapsing building where Donut and the party are positioned, above mostly-intact lower floors.
• The approximate floor level where Donut, Carl, and others [[land]] on a metal beam above a series of intact lower floors, with the building actively collapsing from this area.
