# Gym
High-ceilinged boss room and setting for the encounter. Contains a reception desk, turnstile, racks of fitness equipment, mirrors, treadmills, squat racks and weight benches used as improvised weapons and hazards in the fight against [[juicer]], [[Troglodyte Basher]], and [[Virtuoso]].
