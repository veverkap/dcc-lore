# gym

• High-ceilinged boss room and setting for the encounter.
• Contains a reception desk, turnstile, racks of fitness equipment, mirrors, treadmills, squat racks, and weight benches used as improvised weapons and hazards.
• Features encounters with [[juicer]], [[Troglodyte Basher]], and [[Virtuoso]].
