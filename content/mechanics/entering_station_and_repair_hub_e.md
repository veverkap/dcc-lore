# entering_station_and_repair_hub_e

• A system notification, "Entering Station and Repair Hub E," appears when the party arrives in the [[trainyard]], marking the transition into the [[Station and Repair Hub E]] zone and framing environmental/game conditions for the scene.
• This notification acts as an active System Announcement, indicating the party's entry into the floor/zone associated with [[The Iron Tangle Substation E]] and the surrounding yard.
• The notification is displayed as a chapter-opening HUD/System message, indicating arrival in [[Station and Repair Hub E]].
