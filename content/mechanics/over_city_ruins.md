# over_city_ruins

• The abandoned, decrepit sections surrounding the villages on the [[Over City]]: rotting buildings, overgrown parks, fog-obscured streets, and the primary area for monster encounters.
• The ruined urban area where [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]], [[Donut]], and [[Mongo]] travel; described with wooden streets, smoke, and the artificial sun.
• Home to shops like those run by the [[Bopcas]] and leading toward the [[Skyfowl settlement]].
