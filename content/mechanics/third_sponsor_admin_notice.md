# Third Sponsor Admin Notice
A system announcement mechanic that notifies [[dungeon crawler carl]] (the crawler) he has received his third and final sponsor. Triggers UI updates (e.g., visible sponsor ads to viewers and Sponsorship Tab entries) and alters sponsorship-related rewards and broadcast behavior.
