# third_sponsor_admin_notice

• A system announcement mechanic that notifies [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]] he has received his third and final sponsor.
• Triggers UI updates (e.g., visible sponsor ads to viewers and Sponsorship Tab entries).
• Alters sponsorship-related rewards and broadcast behavior.
