# station_59

* A prime-numbered transfer station with 27 platforms, featuring a [[restaurant]], general store, and small [[church]], leading to [[Club Vanquisher]].
* A transit station where the party stops, clears ghouls and juvenile [[Krakaren]], and enters a [[saferoom]] themed as a Costa Rican [[restaurant]] called "Soda".
* The party's current station on the zomp line, with [[Katia]] theorizing that digging down from this station might lead to another station 59 on an inverted line beneath them.
* A specific station on the map (on the [[Zomp Line]]) that the party identifies as their current location, used in theorizing mirrored inverted stations below.
* The [[saferoom]] themed as a Costa Rica [[restaurant]] called "Soda" is where [[Katia]] opens fan and sponsor boxes and the party watches the recap.
