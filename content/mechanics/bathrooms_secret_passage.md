# [[Bathrooms (secret passage)]]
The women’s bathrooms in the [[desperado club]] are known to contain at least one secret passage between floors and are a critical tactical route discussed by [[dungeon crawler carl]] and [[Katia]] for luring and accessing [[Astrid]].
