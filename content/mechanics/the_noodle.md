# the_noodle

• [[Crawler]] nickname for the upside-down escape [[tunnel]] connecting rooms through a long, looped passage where "down" is toward the nearest interior wall.
• Physically disorienting; gravity inside favors the nearest interior wall and allows items and people to traverse loop-de-loops.
• Central to the chapter's boss-baiting plan.
• Player nickname for an "inbetween" escape [[tunnel]] that loops and has local gravity toward the nearest interior wall; connects station rooms and forms the opening through which the party peers into the adjacent chamber.
• Nickname for the surprising looped "inbetween space" escape [[tunnel]] connecting two rooms with a curved gravity orientation.
• Central to the chapter's tactics: the group peers through it, drops rocks, and baits a province boss across the way.
