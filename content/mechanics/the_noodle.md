# The Noodle
Crawler nickname for the upside-down escape tunnel / in-between space connecting rooms through a long, looped passage where "down" is toward the nearest interior wall. Site of observation and baiting attempts at province bosses.

# The Noodle
Crawler slang for the upside-down, looped inter-room tunnel connecting paired chambers (the "escape tunnel"). Physically disorienting; gravity inside favors the nearest interior wall and allows items and people to traverse loop-de-loops. Central to the chapter's boss-baiting plan.
