# totem_killed

• System notification that appears when a summoned totem is destroyed or dissipates during battle.
• Triggers subsequent spawn waves or boss mechanics, such as the arrival of [[Rude-Dolph the Blood-Nosed Slay-Deer]].
• Registers when a summoned totem dies or times out in the simulation; triggered in the chapter during the skirmish.
