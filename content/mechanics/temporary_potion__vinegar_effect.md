# [[Temporary potion → vinegar effect]]
System announcement stating: "Temporary effect from [[Ysalte]]: All potions you quaff or physically handle outside your inventory will turn to vinegar. They will return to a more powerful version of themselves upon her banishment." A time-limited world effect tied to [[Ysalte]]'s presence.
