# satans_water_park

• A named race track with unique hazards and theme, mentioned as the location for other competing teams' heats.
• Features high-speed [[tunnels]], slides, and enemies such as gingers falling down slides, with teams potentially moved here by the Track Randomization mechanic.
• The [[racetrack]] for this chapter, featuring dozens of waterslides, ground-level challenges, and an exit guarded by [[Satan]], with branching paths that can accidentally route racers into the boss.
