# [[Satan's Water Park]]
One of the four track themes feeding into the shared [[Pit Stop]]; features high-speed tunnels, slides, and enemies such as gingers falling down slides. Teams, including [[Florin]]’s party at one point, can be moved here by the [[Track Randomization]] mechanic.

# Satan's Water Park
The racetrack for this chapter, featuring dozens of waterslides, ground-level challenges, and an exit guarded by [[Satan]]. Courses include branching paths that can accidentally route racers into the boss.
