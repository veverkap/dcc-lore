# bonnies_house

• The floating home, Bonnie's House, is a well-lit, damaged house in the [[Wasteland]] containing the [[kitchen]], [[stairwell]], and master-bedroom "throne room" leading up to the sixth floor.
• The site of the timed [[Borough Boss]] fight against [[Denise]].
• [[Commandant Kane]]'s corpse was looted here.
