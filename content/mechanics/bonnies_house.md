# Bonnie's House
The floating home and immediate arena for the chapter’s events: a well-lit, damaged house in the [[Wasteland]] containing the kitchen, stairwell, and master-bedroom "throne room" leading up to the sixth floor. Site of the timed [[Borough Boss]] fight against [[Denise]] and where [[Commandant Kane]]’s corpse was looted.
