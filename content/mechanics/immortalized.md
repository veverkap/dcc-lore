# immortalized

• A time-limited debuff (29-hour countdown) applied to velociraptors cured of vampirism, halving strength and mana, and preventing healing in direct sunlight.
• In direct moonlight, the debuff turns into a powerful buff, granting plus strength/mana and double healing.
• The debuff is unaffected in shadow or heavy overcast.
• Explained to [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]] by [[Mordecai]].
