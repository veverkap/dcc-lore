# Immortalized
A time-limited debuff (29-hour countdown in-chapter) applied to velociraptors cured of vampirism: while active they suffer half strength and mana and cannot heal in direct sunlight, but the effect turns into a powerful buff (plus strength/mana and double healing) in direct moonlight; unaffected in shadow or heavy overcast. Explained to [[dungeon crawler carl]] by [[Mordecai]].
