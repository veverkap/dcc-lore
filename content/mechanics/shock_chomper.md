# Shock Chomper
A lesser naga-like, red-maned, snake-headed monster with an electrical attack found on the [[Nightmare Express]] and [[Yellow Line]] trains. Referred to in-combat as one of the two primary mob types alongside the [[Cave Mudge Bonker]].
