# A Parade of Horribles
The historical name (stage two) of the [[Nine-tier attack]] in old translations; characterized as a social movement or panic that follows the initial seizure/control stage.

# [[A Parade of Horribles]]
The proper name of the 11th floor of the dungeon introduced by [[Zev]]. Described as a parade/march to an arena where surviving crawlers will cooperate and where special voting mechanics and rapid progression rules apply.
