# a_parade_of_horribles

• The historical name of the [[Nine-tier attack]] in old translations, characterized as a social movement or panic that follows the initial seizure/control [[stage]].
• The proper name of the 11th floor of the [[dungeon]] introduced by [[Zev]], described as a parade/march to an [[arena]] where surviving crawlers will cooperate and special voting mechanics and rapid progression rules apply.
• The name given in historic translations to the second phase of the [[Nine-Tier Attack]], characterized by rapid social movements and the beginning of societal panic and breakdown.
