# the_hairpin

• A tavern/booze spot in [[town]] where Genesis and Rapture decide to go for "real booze" instead of drinking with the party at the [[cul-de-sac]].
• A loud, neon [[bar]] marked by a giant racing-car sign, advertising a back entrance to the [[desperado club]] and radiating overpowering light that overshadows neighboring signs like [[The Lollipop]].
• A large neon-lit [[bar]] in [[Hungry Eyes Vendor Village]] with a prominent Formula 1-style race car sign, marketing the [[Desperado Club]] entrance in the back, and noted as a place people were avoiding at the time.
