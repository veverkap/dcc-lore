# Macro AIs
Large, repeatedly-built artificial intelligences produced by the [[mantis]] and central systems. These [[Macro AIs]] are the constructs harvested and fed by the center system to expand and maintain a zone of influence; their production is a major target of the factions debating use of the [[fuse]].
