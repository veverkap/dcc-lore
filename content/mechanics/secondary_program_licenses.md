# secondary program licenses
Dungeon/legal mechanic governing which secondary programs are allowed to continue after collapse of the [[Sixth Floor]]. Normally granted by AI decision or lottery; may be extended under narrow clauses (e.g., emergency use/subsection amendments) at a cost from [[borant corporation]].

# [[Secondary Program Licenses]]
Dungeon/system mechanic governing which secondary programs are allowed to continue running after the collapse of a floor (notably the [[Sixth Floor]]). Typically granted by AI decision at floor end and, when multiple programs are eligible, decided by lottery; but exceptions exist for emergency use.
