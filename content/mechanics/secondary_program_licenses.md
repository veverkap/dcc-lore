# secondary_program_licenses

• [[Dungeon]]/legally governed mechanic governing secondary programs after the collapse of the [[Sixth Floor]].
• Normally granted by AI decision or lottery, with exceptions for [[emergency]] use.
• May be extended under narrow clauses at a cost from [[borant corporation]].
