# homeward_bound

• Employee-only [[train]] intended to ferry employees to the residential complex at [[Station 60]], but the associated housing is revealed to be non-existent.
• The [[train]] line claims to deliver workers to a fabricated employee settlement at Station 60, but in practice, the [[platform]] and settlement are a sham.
• The [[train]] allegedly connects staff to a fabricated settlement at station 60, described as an employee-only route that should deliver employees to a common [[platform]]/[[portal]].
• The [[train]] is ostensibly transporting workers to dormitories/settlements at station 60, but the expected settlement at station 60 is revealed to be false/empty.
