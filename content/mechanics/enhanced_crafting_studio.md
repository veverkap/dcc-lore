# enhanced_crafting_studio

• The Enhanced Crafting [[Studio]] is an upgraded form of the [[Crafting Studio]] applied by [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]] to the communal crafting area.
• The upgrade changes the [[studio]]'s title and persists in the common area unless spaces are disengaged.
• Visibly changes the room's title and implies enhanced crafting capabilities while remaining physically similar upon placement.
