# Prepotente (Pony)
Often called Pony, the goat-man closely bonded to [[Miriam Dom]]. He is found paralyzed and fragile (unable to move or use chat) with a timer over his head and multiple rings (including a soul crystal and a used [[Opposite Day]] ring). He cast the scroll [[Community Pool]] that backfired, leading to the current crisis.
