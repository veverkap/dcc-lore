# player-killer_skull

• System mechanic/marker awarded to players who killed another player (or [[crawler]]), linked to issuing of rewards like a coupon book and a [[PVP Coupon]]; a social/achievement mechanic that affects party dynamics.
• Grants the first party member a Savage box (and coupon book) and signals PVP capability; functions as a social/[[loot]] mechanic that can destabilize parties.
• Marks a player as a player killer and carries social/mechanical consequences in the realm.
• An in-game status/achievement indicator applied to players who have killed other players (PK).
