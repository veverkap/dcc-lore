# train

• The Train is a confined multi-car [[transport]] with features including passenger cars, a cab, cargo containers, and engines.
• Key events, such as injury to [[Katia]] and [[Hekla]]'s death, occur inside a passenger car on the Train.
• The Train is used as a delivery vehicle for an explosive payload and is pushed into [[The Abyss]] with crew members like [[Fire Brandy]] and [[Tizquick]] who choose to remain aboard to ensure the blast.
• Additional crawlers gather on the [[platform]] outside the Train.
