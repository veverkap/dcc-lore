# temple_appearance_rules

• System rules apply when a deity appears within their own [[temple]], flagged as reasons to exercise extreme caution.
• Enhanced deity powers and special boons and restrictions are associated with these appearances.
• When a deity manifests within their own [[temple]], special appearance mechanics and powers apply, including heightened authority and abilities compared to normal AI.
