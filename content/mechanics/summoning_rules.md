# summoning_rules

• Summoning mechanics apply to deities and summoned beings in the floor.
• [[Summoning rules]] govern how vessels (like [[Mantaur]]) release gods and how summoning timing can be manipulated.
• [[Dungeon]]/System rules referenced when a sponsored deity like [[Eileithyia]] is temporarily brought to a location (includes [[invulnerability]] and special summoning quirks such as freezing until a choice is made).
