# the_iron_tangle_substation_e

• A three-story concrete substation building outside the railyard gates that is burning and where workers (and ghouls) are congregating; source of some of the flaming ghouls encountered.
• The building is adjacent to the yard and is ablaze during the chapter.
• It housed workers and conductors.
• The front area functions as the main loading zone for trains in this segment of [[Station and Repair Hub E]].
• NPCs and dead workers are present around this structure.
