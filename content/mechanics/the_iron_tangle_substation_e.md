# [[The Iron Tangle Substation E]]
A large concrete substation/building at the edge of the railyard, described as three stories and engulfed in flames during the chapter. The loading area and worker congregation point for many tracks; connected to multiple transit stations and the exit gates leading from the yard. NPCs and dead workers are present around this structure.
