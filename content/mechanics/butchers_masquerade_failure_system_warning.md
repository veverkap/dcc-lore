# Butcher's Masquerade Failure (System Warning)
A System Announcement that states: "Apito has turned her back. The seal has been broken. The Butcher’s Masquerade has failed to cast. The protection of this chamber has stopped. This party is over." It signals the collapse of the chamber's safeguards and triggers disappearances and danger to characters like [[Signet]] and [[Vrah]].
