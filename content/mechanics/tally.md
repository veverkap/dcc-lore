# Tally
A named non-combatant NPC who identifies as a [[bopcas]] and serves as the caretaker of the [[Safe Room]] fast-food restaurant. Tally prepares food, manages rental/sleeping spaces, and moves between floors as the dungeon collapses. He is Level 63 and speaks with a Slavic accent.

# Tally
A furry companion and former resident of an off-world rescue/relocation program who watches the premiere of [[Dungeon Crawler World: Earth]] with the narrator.
