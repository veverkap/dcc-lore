# general_store

• Limp Richard's shop where the party buys the [[Padded Mallet]], inspects the [[Battery Fabricator]], trades books, and sells junk for gold.
• Contains dusty corners and physical media like books.
• Local shop run by [[Limp Richard]] selling a variety of items including a [[Battery Fabricator]] and stacks of physical books.
• Site of bartering and minor trade.
• Stocked with miscellaneous items and curios, including the [[Battery Fabricator]] and tradeable books.
• Offers poor sell prices but valuable local goods.
