# administrative_hold

• An administrative hold is a system or [[dungeon]] effect that can negate or push back against prior actions before a race starts.
• Items under administrative hold are restricted and may be deleted if improperly handled.
• The term is referenced by [[Quasar]] to explain why the narrator should not touch the [[gate]] pieces.
• Administrative hold is a system status or mechanic name that appears among other pre-race debuffs and is pushed back by the AI.
