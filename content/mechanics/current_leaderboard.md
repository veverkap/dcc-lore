# Current Leaderboard
The working/top-ten ranking system shown during Odette’s broadcast; factors include views, favorites, level, and money earned. Used publicly to highlight popular and financially valuable crawlers (e.g., [[Lucia Mar]], [[dungeon crawler carl]], [[Donut]]) and tied to bounty values that other crawlers can claim.
