# tenth_floor

* The floor is a [[target]] destination for rescuing and preserving as many crawlers as possible after surviving the ninth floor.
* It is the place where Carl noticed changes in monster behavior, and the barn encounter takes place on this floor.
* The floor contains the ritual circle, the Summoning Box, the nine-piece Puzzle Disk, and the storm deities Dodola and Adad.
* It is described as a deadly floor where crawlers make deals or die.
* The floor has a theme where every physical, non-natural object has sapience, and objects can speak, consume others to grow, and form behemoths that roam the floor.
* The floor is off-limits to airships and is a distant, difficult objective that characters yearn to reach.
* Volteeg accepted an NPC guard [[deal]] and exited the [[dungeon]] after 26 seasons on this floor.
