# dirigible_gnomes

• Aerial gnome opponents assigned by the wheel to be the team's adversaries on the [[Fifth Floor]]; defenders of the [[Dirigible Gnome [[Wasteland]] Fortress]].
• The opponent faction/mob type randomly selected by the team’s wheel; players must storm their sky castle (the [[Wasteland Fortress]]) to pass the level.
• An aerial opponent faction/species selected by the wheel for the team to face on the fifth floor; they occupy the sky castle known as the [[Wasteland Fortress]].
• The opponent mob group revealed by the wheel; they occupy the sky-castle the team must assault (the [[Wasteland Fortress]]) to clear their assigned objective.
• A named enemy/group referenced in the floor header ("Phase One of Four. The Gnomes.") and described as one of the parties holding the [[Necropolis]] of Anser in stalemate; central to the floor’s thematic conflict.
