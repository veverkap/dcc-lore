# level_collapse_timer_suspension_post-11th_floors

• Level collapse timer suspension occurs post-11th floors.
• Timers are suspended until a [[winner]] is declared in the [[Ascendency battles]].
• After a [[winner]] is declared, [[dungeon]]-closure procedures commence.
