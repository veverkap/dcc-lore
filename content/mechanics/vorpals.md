# Vorpals
A condition-turned-mob described by [[Mordecai]]: deputized/augmented celestial foot soldiers that act like suicide bombers when proximate to demons. In this chapter [[Vorpals]] are held behind a hidden door in the [[desperado club]] and ultimately attack [[Minge]].
