# nest_tower

• The [[Nest tower]] is a specific [[tower]] that hosts [[Donut]] and has multiple defensive enchantments.
• It is a defensive/strategic [[tower]] referenced as a source of [[Dream]] artillery in the [[battlefield]] area.
• The [[tower]] is massive, positioned near the Larracos border, and has a broad view of the city.
• It is targeted by the anti-escape trap net and has interior defenses, a main curving [[stairwell]], and a shield protecting the interior.
• The team's primary defensive [[tower]], where [[Donut]], [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]], [[Rend]], [[Mongo]], and [[Victory]] gather, and its integrity is actively dropping under enemy fire.
• The [[tower]] is undergoing repairs under the direction of [[Ferdinand]] as part of city defenses.
• It is a [[tower]] from which characters watched events and was used as a vantage point in the chapter.
