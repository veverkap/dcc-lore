# Residuals
A hyperspatial, collective-mind alien race that occupies other beings' memories and bodies. Present in most crawls and capable of broadcasting memories to nearby crawlers; divided internally into factions with differing goals. Noted individuals include [[Paulie (Goff)]] and [[Agatha]]. The text warns they are usually ignored by [[Showrunners]] but can be dangerous if their information reaches the wrong listeners.

# Residuals
A hyperspatial, collective-mind alien race that inhabits memories and other hosts; they are present in almost every crawl with a primal engine. They move into other beings’ bodies to speak with fledgling AIs, can be edited out by [[Showrunners]], and include individuals like [[Paulie (Goff)]] and [[Agatha]]. The chapter references internal politics among them and external threats from the [[System AI]].
