# syndicate_rule_independent_system_sovereignty

• Explicit legal/ political rule invoked by factions (e.g., [[skull clan]], [[valtay]]) to justify actions and claims.
• Cited by [[Prince Stalwart]] to legitimize summary executions and by the [[prism]] when filing grievances.
• Basis for claiming extrajudicial strikes against perceived sedition or offenses across systems.
