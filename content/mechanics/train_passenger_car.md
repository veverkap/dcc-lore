# [[Train (Passenger Car)]]
The moving train and its passenger car serve as the immediate setting for the confrontation. Key actions occur in the confined space of the car, including combat, healing by [[Silfa]], and [[Katia]]’s activated [[Rush]] that kills [[Hekla]]. The platform, cargo containers, and other engines are adjacent related locations in the scene.
