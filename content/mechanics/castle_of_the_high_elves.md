# castle_of_the_high_elves

• The Castle of the High Elves is a southeastern [[stronghold]] that houses Queen Imogen and is protected by a mysterious artifact, origin of the elven procession.
• It is a fortified high-elf [[stronghold]] located in the southeast of the map, dispatching elite guards and mounts to [[deal]] with the creeper incursion.
• The castle is a focal point for elven defense and the masquerade transfer, and is expected to be affected by the advancing brambles.
• It is the venue for the Butcher's Masquerade hosted by Queen Imogen, later teleported to the Ninth Floor and used as a staging point and army base for the party.
• The [[stronghold]] was recently assaulted and retaken by Signet's forces, and is in ruin, containing the private chambers of elites like Queen Imogen.
• The castle has a large white square structure with sentry [[towers]], an empty moat, subsurface levels including a [[dungeon]], and a throne room that is restricted, hosting separate staging areas for [[crawler]] and hunter servants.
• It is the location of the Butcher's Masquerade and seat of Queen Imogen, later teleported to the Ninth Floor and used as an army staging ground for the party.
• The castle site from previous-floor conflicts whose remnants and surviving changelings were moved to the Ninth Floor, connected to the changeling rescue and attendant decisions like hiring Ruby.
