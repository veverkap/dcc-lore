# queasy_resistance

• Skill/notification players receive after repeated exposure to [[Queasy]]; represents accrued natural resistance that reduces or removes the debuff's effect.
• A system notification/skill-like resistance achieved after repeated exposure to [[Queasy]]; once earned, the party members become progressively immune to the cornets' nausea attack.
• A resistance/achievement-like notification players receive after repeated exposure to [[Queasy]], described by [[Mordecai]] as a skill notification that grants immunity over time.
