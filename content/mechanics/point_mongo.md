# point_mongo

* A renamed large ursine settlement on the [[Sixth Floor]] owned by [[Donut]], serving as a hub for guard management, quests, and dinosaur incursions.
* A city near which [[Bravvo]] had been scouting and where characters expected [[Mongo]] to be frightened by a yellow caterpillar mount.
* A notable geographic feature mentioned on the [[Hunting Grounds Map]] and referenced as a locational anchor where several crawlers are said to be positioned.
* A settlement or waypoint named for [[Mongo]], where the party returns, uses the bridge checkpoint, and prepares for upcoming assaults and a [[saferoom]] countdown.
* A [[town]] where the personal space and guild activities are located and from where an assault was launched against its defenders.
* A [[town]] the party previously took and set up defenses in, referenced as a staging point and the northern neighbor to a [[jungle]] area with dinosaurs like [[Kiwi the Mongoliensis]].
* A nearby southern landmark referenced to describe [[river]] width and geography near [[Fort Freedom]].
* A riverside [[town]] referenced as having been attacked by dinosaurs linked to [[Big Tina]] and companions, used as a regional point of interest for the chapter's investigative quest [[The Recital]].
* A prior settlement or base referenced as somewhere the party might have returned to but chose not to, used as a travel reference point.
* Earlier ursine settlement referenced as the first [[town]] the party encountered, described as very religious and similar to the newly claimed towns.
* The renamed Large Ursine Settlement on the [[Sixth Floor]] controlled by [[Donut]], a central location for the chapter's events and the starting point for the [[jungle]] encounter.
* A city referenced as being scouted by a hunter using a levitation potion, the hunter who was killed had been scouting the city of [[Point Mongo]] when struck by a missile.
* A geographic point referenced on the hunters' map and used as a positional marker for several crawlers, near ruined city features on the [[river]].
* The settlement and defensive location the party returns to in this chapter, featuring a bridge guarded by [[Mushroom guards]], alarm traps, and naiad triggers.
* The [[town]] where the group is located and where an attack becomes evident, the [[pub]] and common areas are set here.
* The [[town]] where [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]]'s group previously established defenses and training, used as a template for the group's next objective to the south.
* A captured [[town]] where [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]]'s group previously established defenses and training, used as a template for the group's next objective to the south.
* A geographic reference point south of the current site, used to describe [[river]] width and proximity to the assault area.
* A riverside [[town]] referenced as one of the settlements attacked by dinosaurs like [[Big Tina]] or groups associated with the [[Kiwi the mongoliensis]].
* A base/location referenced as a place the party considered returning to but decided they didn't have time to reach before transfers and [[saferoom]] deadlines.
* An earlier ursine settlement referenced as similarly religious to [[Ferdinand Peak]] and [[Nipton]], part of the local region's village cluster.
