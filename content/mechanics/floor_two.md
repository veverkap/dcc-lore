# [[Floor Two]]
The dungeon level currently explored: cinderblock walls, white floors, orange-tinged lichen, and quadrants rather than a single main hallway. Contains mobs like [[Brindle Grub]], [[Danger Dingo]], and [[Mind Horror]], plus safe rooms and a mailbox that supplies spellbooks.
