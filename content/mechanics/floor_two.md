# floor_two

• The [[dungeon]] level currently explored has cinderblock walls, white floors, orange-tinged lichen, and quadrants rather than a single main [[hallway]].
• Contains mobs like [[Brindle Grub]], [[Danger Dingo]], and [[Mind Horror]].
• Includes safe rooms and a [[mailbox]] that supplies spellbooks.
