# station_281

• The party leaves the [[train]] at Station 281, which intersects the Mauve Line, the Puce Line, and the Dismemberment Limited.
• Station 281 contains a safe-room storefront (J. CO Donuts and Coffee) and serves as a staging point before heading to the [[desperado club]].
• Station 281 is a transit stop and the Dismemberment Limited's immediate transfer point in the party's planned route.
• The party intends to disembark at this stop during the short loop.
• Station 281 is an intersection station on the Mauve Line that also intersects the Puce Line and the Dismemberment Limited, where the party prepares and crafts.
• The station/safe-room location includes the [[saferoom]] storefront [[J. CO Donuts and Coffee]].
