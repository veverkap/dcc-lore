# Hell Portal Ascension Mechanic
System rule announced during the interface update: when the timer reaches zero the heavens open and living things/items/structures are sucked upward; positioning under an enclosed stairwell chamber causes the chamber walls to teleport away enabling an ascent into the next floor — a high-stakes escape mechanic described in-system.
