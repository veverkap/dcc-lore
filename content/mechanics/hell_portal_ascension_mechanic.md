# hell_portal_ascension_mechanic

• When the timer reaches zero, the heavens open and living things, items, and structures are sucked upward.
• Positioning under an enclosed [[stairwell]] chamber causes the chamber walls to [[teleport]] away, enabling an ascent into the next floor.
