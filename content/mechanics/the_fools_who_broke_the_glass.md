# the_fools_who_broke_the_glass

• A System Announcement/Group Quest triggered in this chapter, designating nearby crawlers as hosts who cannot opt out.
• Objective: survive an impending large explosion centered on the [[Soul Crystal]].
• Reward: Platinum Quest Box for survivors.
• Includes warnings about [[saferoom]] access and NPC safety.
• Event- style Group Quest system announcement that forcibly enrols all crawlers within a 45 km blast radius as quest hosts.
• Presents an immediate survival objective ("Unfuck yourself. Don’t die.") and a hard sixty-second [[saferoom]] lockout.
• Seven-minute countdown to explosion.
• [[Platinum Quest Box]] reward for survivors.
• Quest specifically names the epicenter at the [[Soul Crystal]].
