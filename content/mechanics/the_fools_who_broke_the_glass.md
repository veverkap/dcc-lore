# The Fools Who Broke the Glass
An event-style Group Quest system announcement that forcibly enrols all crawlers within a 45 km blast radius as quest hosts. Presents an immediate survival objective ("Unfuck yourself. Don’t die."), a hard sixty-second saferoom lockout, a seven-minute countdown to explosion, and a [[Platinum Quest Box]] reward for survivors. The quest specifically names the epicenter at the [[Soul Crystal]].
