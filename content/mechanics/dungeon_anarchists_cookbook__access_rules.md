# dungeon_anarchists_cookbook__access_rules

• The [[Dungeon]] Anarchist's Cookbook is allowed by [[Dungeon]] code, but its hidden contents will erase if the organization running the season suspects misuse or if the owner reveals the book's existence.
• The book disappears from inventory on death or retirement and can be re-awarded to future crawlers.
• Use of the [[scratchpad]] adds content to future editions.
