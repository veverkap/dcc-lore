# Dungeon Anarchist’s Cookbook — Access Rules
Explicit in-book/system rules: the cookbook is allowed by Dungeon code but its hidden contents will erase if the organization running the season suspects misuse or if the owner reveals the book's existence. The book disappears from inventory on death or retirement and can be re-awarded to future crawlers; use of the scratchpad adds content to future editions.
