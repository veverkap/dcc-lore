# castle_tunnels

• A web of narrow passageways built into the castle, used for secret movement between floors, and important for  [[Samantha]] and  [[Sir Ferdinand]].
• A network of small, cat-sized passageways hidden throughout the castle, used as shortcuts, and suggested as the infiltration route for  [[Samantha]] to reach the  [[Security room]].
