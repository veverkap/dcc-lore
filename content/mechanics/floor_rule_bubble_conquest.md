# [[Floor Rule: Bubble Conquest]]
Explicit dungeon rule announced for the fifth floor: each bubble contains four quadrants (Land, Sea, Air, Subterranean) with one castle per quadrant. To open a stairwell to the next floor, all four castles in a bubble must be conquered (throne room occupied or quadrant boss killed). Once all four castles fall the bubble "pops" and crawlers may leave the bubble area.
