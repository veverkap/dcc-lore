# seventeenth_floor

• A dangerous floor that must be traversed en route from the 18th Floor to the 16th Floor; described as deadly and turning travelers into mince.
• Flagged as especially hazardous among the remaining floors that crawlers must pass if they hope to reach the final exit on the 18th floor following the Scolopendra encounter.
• A hazardous passage between floors mentioned as lethal to those attempting to transit from the 18th to the 16th; described as where many attackers stalk travelers.
• A small, hand-built floor created as a front by Herot and Menerva; it serves as the only known entrance to the Pineapple Cabaret and is notable for its enhanced mobs and near-unkillable boss.
• A specific [[dungeon]] floor referenced as the location where Menerva manages NPC construction and where the [[Backstage]] Death Maze exists.
