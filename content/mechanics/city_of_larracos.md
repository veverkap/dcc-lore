# city_of_larracos

• An inverted-cone faction city whose market and lower levels became the [[target]] location for the [[gate]] into the [[Ninth Floor]]; subject to sudden flooding, timed charges, shark mobs, and collateral NPC casualties as part of [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]]'s plan.
• A major overland city referenced in the [[Paired Watches]] notes; many potential [[gate]] coordinates in the mage's letter point to places surrounding this city.
• A submerged-in-part faction city and the primary [[target]] of Carl's [[gate]]-flooding strategy; contains the [[faction market]] and entrances to the [[stairwell]] used for escape.
