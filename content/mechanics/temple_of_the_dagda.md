# temple_of_the_dagda

• Seasonal entrance [[temple]] in [[Shanty Town]] that provides one of only two access points on the ninth floor to [[Club Vanquisher]]; contains NPC clerics, stained-glass windows, and a giant cauldron.
• [[Temple]] area adjacent to and connected by [[portal]] to [[Club Vanquisher]]; staging point for the incursion and entryway used by [[Li Jun]], [[Zhang]], and [[daniel bautista]].
• Previously referenced [[temple]] used as a test case for [[teleport]] behavior and mentioned in relation to [[black nimbus]] functionality.
