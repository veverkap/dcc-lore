# Pox Slug
Small slug creature spawned when a boil from [[SlugPox]] erupts (e.g., from [[dungeon crawler carl]]). Begins at Level 1 and grows in level with subsequent bursts; initially non-hostile to players but aggressive and disgusting in nature.

# Pox Slug
A small monster produced when [[SlugPox]] boils erupt (e.g., the level-1 slug spawned from Carl's boil). They scale in level over time and can latch onto targets; considered a parasitic minion.
