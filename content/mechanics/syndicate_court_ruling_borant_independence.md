# Syndicate Court Ruling: Borant Independence
Planet-wide System Announcement describing a Syndicate court decision that declared the [[borant corporation]] an independent non-governmental entity and granted the [[valtay]] 51% ownership. Specifies legal/regency changes affecting contracts, indenture, and production control of [[Dungeon Crawler World: Earth]].
