# syndicate_court_ruling_borant_independence

• The Syndicate court declared the [[borant corporation]] an independent non-governmental entity.
• The [[valtay]] gained 51% ownership as a result.
• The ruling affected contracts, indenture, and production control of [[Dungeon [[Crawler]] World: Earth]].
