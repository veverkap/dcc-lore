# Trap Activation (alarm/teleport)
Active system messages and dungeon rule events reported in the chapter: repeated alerts such as "Your alarm trap has activated" and multiple "Your teleport trap has activated" notices. These indicate pre-placed defensive devices and their automatic triggering across the map.
