# youth_assistants

• Children below a variable minimum age cannot enter the [[dungeon]] and are taken away; [[Youth assistants]] are the caretakers who handle those children.
• Children or underage entrants are taken away until after the crawl, and "youth assistants" are the ones who remove and care for them.
