# worship_link

• When a player worships a deity for the first time, a physical, whisker-thin tendril of invisible light is formed, linking the god to the worshiper via the strongest [[temple]] on the floor (usually inside [[Club Vanquisher]]).
• Connections can extend to other temples and worshipers and are exploitable by spells like [[Travel the Path]].
