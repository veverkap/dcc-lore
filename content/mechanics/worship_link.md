# [[Worship Link]]
Dungeon rule/mechanic: when a player worships a deity for the first time a physical, whisker-thin tendril of invisible light is formed (transported by a [[Bijou]]) linking the god to the worshiper via the strongest temple on the floor (usually inside [[Club Vanquisher]]). Connections can extend to other temples and worshipers and are exploitable by spells like [[Travel the Path]].
