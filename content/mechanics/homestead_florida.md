# homestead_florida

• [[Homestead]], [[Florida]] is a [[town]] location specified on the benefactor flyer, roughly 40 minutes north of [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]]'s position near the Keys and just south of [[Miami]] and an [[air]] force base.
• The [[town]] is also named on the flyer from the [[Benefactor box]] where the [[Shepherd’s House]] [[shelter]] is located.
• Geographically, [[Homestead]], [[Florida]] is placed near an [[air]] force base south of [[Miami]] in the story context.
