# [[Traveler Station #83]]
Fourth-level transit station where the party disembarks. Features platform details, a sign for the [[Red Line]], stairwells to the [[Yellow Line]] and [[Nightmare Express]], and nearby shops including [[Limp Richard’s Sundries]], a saferoom (Nirula’s), and the "Mace and Bashing Weapons Guild."
