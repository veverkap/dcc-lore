# Troglodyte Pygmy
Small lizard-like humanoid mob (displayed as "Troglodyte Pygmy – Level 2") with venomous bite and high speed. Described visually as upright Komodo-like from the waist up with long, bent legs. Primary low-level enemy in this quadrant.
