# paralysis

• A status-effect/debuff mechanic used by the [[dungeon]] to [[slow]] or stop players.
• Shown as an applied effect that gets negated by AI resolution.
• System debuff/mechanic message that appears during pod pre-start and is negated by AI resolution in the same sequence.
