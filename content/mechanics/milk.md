# Milk
An indentured NPC and guildmaster of both the [[Calligraphy Guild]] and the [[Cartography Guild]] within [[Club Vanquisher]]. Formerly a crawler (birth race: Vesper), current race: Xenopus, class: Cartographer, top level 73. Runs both guilds, author of the sixth edition of the [[Dungeon Anarchist’s Cookbook]], and bound by an [[Indentureship Contract]] that requires her services 333 times (times used so far: 6). Injured by a pet attack in this chapter and enforces hidden entrances and maps for the club.
