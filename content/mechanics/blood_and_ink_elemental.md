# [[Blood and Ink Elemental]]
A summoned minion type created by Signet’s blood-and-ink magic. Presented in the chapter as the three-headed ogre example (Level 50), these elementals are paper-like, semi-translucent, vary in power, and serve as Signet’s frontline attackers.
