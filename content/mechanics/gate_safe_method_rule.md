# Gate safe method rule
Dungeon rule (from Ghazi's notes) describing a safe gate-handling scenario: leaving one [[watch]] on one side, another [[watch]] on the opening side, and placing the [[winding box]] inside the gate prevents the portal from releasing a monster.
