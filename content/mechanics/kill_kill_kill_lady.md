# Kill, kill, kill lady
An unidentified, disturbed voice heard over the loudspeaker early in the sequence, ranting about death and pain before the intercom cuts out. Recognized by [[Donut]] and confirmed by [[dungeon crawler carl]] as the source of the initial outburst that interrupts the briefing.
