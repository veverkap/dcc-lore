# clocktower

• A recently erected [[tower]] near which [[Jun]], [[Zhang]], [[Daniel Bautista]], and [[Sergeant Toyotomi]] stand; reference point for last-minute construction and observation.
• Local landmark and deployment point near [[Shanty Town]] where a manned synergy [[tower]] with dangling wires is sited to extend aura effects across the assault area.
• Source point for the antenna/capacitor wire that amplifies [[Li Na]]'s aura effects inside [[Club Vanquisher]]; part of the team's wire network.
