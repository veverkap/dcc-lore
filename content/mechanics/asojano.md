# Asojano
A Level 130 City Boss Orisha encountered inside the party's [[Saferoom]]. Described as the "Lord of Smallpox," a formerly worshipped deity reduced to a non-corporeal spirit that radiates a disease aura and causes local townsfolk to go mad. Central to the [[Quest Update! Pueblo de los Olvidados]], he requests that [[Paz]] repair a shrine so he can leave town.
