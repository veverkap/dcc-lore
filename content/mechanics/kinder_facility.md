# kinder_facility

• [[kinder facility]]: a surface facility where many trapped children are physically located, linked to [[Lucia Mar]] and the [[Eulogist]].
• The facility is also where [[Heidi]], [[Katia]], and many children tied to [[Lucia]] are held, separate from [[dungeon]] floors.
• It is a contested territory in [[South Asia]] tied to [[Katia]]'s situation, with unclear control between the [[open intellect [[pacifist]] network]] and other parties.
• The facility is mentioned as the off-floor location where [[Heidi]], [[Katia]], and many children are held, referenced in relation to [[Jurgen]]'s wife and other trapped people.
• The [[Open Intellect Action Network]] has not retaken the facility in [[South Asia]], which is protected by a mysterious third party that is "confused".
