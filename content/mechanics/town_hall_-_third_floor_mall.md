# town_hall_-_third_floor_mall

• The opulent third-floor shopping/[[mall]] area serves as the entry to the Skyfowl administrative offices, including Miss Quill's desk and the locked door to Magistrate FeatherFall's chamber.
• Contains upscale shops for Skyfowl.
• Features a red carpeted [[stairwell]].
