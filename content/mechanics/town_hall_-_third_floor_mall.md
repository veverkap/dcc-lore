# Town Hall - Third Floor Mall
The opulent third-floor shopping/mall area that serves as the entry to the Skyfowl administrative offices (including [[Miss Quill]]'s desk and the locked door to [[Magistrate FeatherFall]]'s chamber). Contains upscale shops for Skyfowl and a red carpeted stairwell.
