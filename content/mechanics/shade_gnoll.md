# shade_gnoll

• A [[gnoll]] subtype encountered asleep at the [[bar]] in Station 75, described as hyena-like and present among the station's bodies.
• An older [[gnoll]] security guard with a gray muzzle and quarter-moon shoulder insignia, encountered by [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]] during transfer, who performs scans and enforces transfer protocols.
• A specific [[gnoll]] variant encountered asleep behind the [[bar]] at [[The Downward Dog]], noted for hyena-like appearance and found in the otherwise-empty tavern.
• The bartender in the Downward Dog is a Shade [[Gnoll]].
• An older, gray-muzzled [[gnoll]] security operative who intercepts [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]] at the ship entry, carrying a quarter-moon insignia, mentioning his grandpup [[Lix]], and enforcing the vessel's security screening.
