# Borant Production Headquarters
The off-dungeon corporate facility (production headquarters/boat) where [[Zev]] reports and where Borant’s production staff coordinate shows and administrative tasks. It is the corporate center tied to pressuring on-site admins and enforcing directives from [[bloom]].
