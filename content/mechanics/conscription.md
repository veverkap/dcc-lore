# conscription

• A referenced [[dungeon]]/season rule constraining warring parties, invoked to argue illegality of actions.
• An in-game action item/mechanic referenced in [[Warlord Akuma]]'s chat as one of the "action items" the War Mages had, treated as a rule/operation of the season.
• A game mechanic likened to worship-like obligations, compared to the effects of the [[Champion system]] on [[Donut]].
