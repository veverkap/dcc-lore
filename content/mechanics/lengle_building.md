# lengle_building

• The L’Engle Building is an [[apartment]]-building track for Heats 6 and 7, with the race taking place inside its multi-floor interior.
• The building is identified by the system as the race environment, with teams placed on the seventh floor in [[apartment]] 712 and the finish line in the [[lobby]].
