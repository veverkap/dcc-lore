# stairwells

• Key vertical-transition locations between floors, opening on timers (six hours prior to collapse) and critical for descent.
• Multiple stairwells on the floor, including two in the woods and one locked in the [[Elf Castle]]'s [[basement]], identified as strategically important choke points by [[Bomo]].
• An unclear route taken at the end of [[Floor 8]] by some parties, with access method noted as confusing and not fully explained.
• Generic but tactically important map features, potential movement points to descend floors and escape the spreading brambles, as instructed by Carl.
• Level stairwells are announced as open by the system, with players without keys teleported to a [[stairwell]] location associated with someone else who has a key, setting the [[stage]] for contested battles for key possession.
