# Lizard-class mob warning
System warning displayed for certain encounters: notifies players that a mob is "lizard-class" and, in this chapter, that such mobs "will inflict 20% more damage against you thanks to your [[Extinction Sigil]]." Functions as an on-screen modifier alerting players to extra danger.
