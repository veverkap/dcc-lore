# Quest Update
A System Announcement mechanic that appears in the chapter to narrate the consequences of the warehouse incident: it confirms the party survived, describes a sequence of four precursor bursts leading to a massive wild-magic detonation, gives a twenty-minute survival window, and previews effects (gear stripping, unpredictable wild magic like "marshmallow in a microwave") for players to prepare against.
