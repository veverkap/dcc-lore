# quest_update

• A System Announcement mechanic that confirms the party survived the [[warehouse]] incident.
• Describes a sequence of four precursor bursts leading to a massive wild-magic detonation.
• Provides a twenty-minute survival window.
• Warns of unpredictable wild magic effects, such as "marshmallow in a microwave".
• Issues a warning: "You now have twenty minutes to save yourselves."
