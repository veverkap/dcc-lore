# York
Tenodera captain and former crawler who pilots the starship [[Homecoming Queen]]. Poet and Game Guide, classed as an Advocate (Top Level 71) who exited the dungeon after taking a deal at the end of the 10th floor. Responsible for deploying an NPB that vaporized an orc yacht and later serves as a captain in the resistance [[open intellect pacifist network]]. Frequently broadcasts poetry and struggles with emotional numbness.
