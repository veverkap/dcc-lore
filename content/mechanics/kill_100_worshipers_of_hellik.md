# Kill 100 worshipers of Hellik
Quest issued by [[Emberus]] demanding Carl kill 100 worshipers of [[Hellik]] before the 12th floor collapse (and practically before the 11th floor collapses). Reward: permanent Celestial-quality boon; no explicit failure penalty noted.
