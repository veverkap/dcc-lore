# guanajay

• The small coastal [[town]] where [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]], [[Donut]], and party drive through at night in search of a path to the [[stairwells]].
• Notable landmarks include a pileup of cars and nearby mob activity.
• The [[town]] is mostly empty, with a large pileup of cars and scattered red-dot enemies (e.g., [[Duendes]]) nearby.
