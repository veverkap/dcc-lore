# Tag! You’re it!
An achievement/system announcement that grants the player the Deckmaster role for a limited time ("For the next three minutes, you are now the deckmaster!"). Controls card persistence and grants temporary inventory/spell allowances to allies while active. The reverse announcement returns control when it times out.
