# Death Watch
A live-show game segment mechanic run by the [[Maestro]] in which grabbed crawlers are shown frozen in dangerous encounters (via [[Time Freeze]]), must guess survival, and can earn [[Teleport Point]]s to save themselves or companions. Central rule-set for the episode's dramatic tension.
