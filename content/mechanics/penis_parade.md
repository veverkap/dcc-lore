# penis_parade

* The [[desperado club]] has a top-floor feature called the Penis Parade, which conceals the entrance to the [[Guild of Suffering]].
* The Penis Parade is a male strip club on the first floor of the [[desperado club]], with a [[stage]] for lead dancers and cubicles for private dances.
* The area is also referred to as a "seedier venue" and is listed on the [[Massage coupon]].
* The Penis Parade is the location where the fight spilled during the incident that led to [[Corcunda]]'s split.
* The club is home to dancers like [[Author Steve Rowland]], [[Anaconda]], and [[Damascus Steel]], and serves as a front for access to the secret [[Guild of Suffering]].
* The Penis Parade is also the source of the thirteen male strippers who are hired into [[Meat Shields]] and moved to the [[Barracks]].
* Performers from the Penis Palace (a male strip club on the middle floor of the [[desperado club]]) arrive bloodied and shell-shocked on the top level.
