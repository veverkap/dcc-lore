# ballroom

• Large event chamber or public space in the castle where the [[teleport]] destination and much of the incident occurred; screens showed [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]]'s translucent avatar appearing here and the caterpillar surrounded by Lemig goblins.
• The specific room within the [[Castle of the Lemigs]] where [[Shi Maria]] rampaged, [[Mordecai]] applied his salve, and [[commander [[stockade]] of the lemig sortion]] attempted to fully drag [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]] into her presence.
• The specific rooms within the [[castle of the high elves]] where the [[Butcher's Masquerade]] takes place; crawlers and hunters attend but are in separate, non-harmful spaces; specific restrictions apply while inside.
• Main event room where the prize counter, [[stage]], and pet show are located; hosts hunters, crawlers, and the holographic prize case.
• The larger event space where the talent show takes place; contains the [[stage]] and fills with smoke during [[Donut]]'s performance.
