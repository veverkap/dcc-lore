# The Nightmare Express
A jet-black steam-powered train with numbered route stops (e.g., [[Station 83]], [[Station 283]], [[Station 436]]). Carries monstrous cargo in open-top cars and is the central set-piece of the chapter; Carl boards and takes control of the engine. The train contains specialized parts like the engine cab and runs on Sheol Bricks according to [[Fire Brandy]].
