# the_nightmare_express

• A jet-black steam-powered [[train]] with numbered route stops, carrying monstrous cargo in open-top cars.
• The [[train]] contains specialized parts like the engine cab.
• The [[train]] runs on Sheol Bricks, according to [[Fire Brandy]].
• The [[train]] is the central set-piece of the chapter, and Carl boards and takes control of the engine.
