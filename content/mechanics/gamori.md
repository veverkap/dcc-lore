# Gamori
The lead engineer of the [[plenty]] and the current Matriarch of [[plenty]]. Seated among the caprids on the broadcast, she initially attempts to control information, becomes emotional when exposed, judges [[dungeon crawler carl]] as dangerous to the herd, and disappears after the violence.
