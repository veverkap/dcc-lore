# old_havana

• The immediate replayed starting zone the party exits into is a warm, humid, tourist-filled cityscape of multi-story Spanish colonial buildings, busy roads, vendors, and parks.
• Key traversal area for scouting toward [[Miramar]] and other [[stairwells]].
• The main in-floor city area the squad emerges into ("Entering Old [[Havana]]") features multi-story Spanish- colonial architecture, busy roads and sidewalks, and is the setting for initial exploration, NPC illusions, and the coastal park encounter.
