# hunting_grounds

Here is the consolidated markdown list:

* The Hunting Grounds is a location below the Over City, referenced by Signet and others as a business opportunity.
* It is also the ancestral domain of the High Elves, contrasted with city-dwelling groups like the City Elves and the 201st Security Group – Magical Ops.
* The Hunting Grounds is a deeper-area destination, referenced as a possible goal for club workers and a rung on the descent ladder.
* It is a contested and dangerous area where Signet was exiled from and where the party currently travels, connected to the transfiguration events and occupied territories.
* The Hunting Grounds is a location where parts of Signet's party remain, including Areson, who remained after the progenitor Di-we split.
* It is a broader combat/event zone where hunters and factions clash, the location of disputes, poaching incidents, and where legal/illegal slayings can occur.
* The Hunting Grounds is a game zone referenced in the description of the "Apex [[Predator]]" achievement, where Carl killed many hunters and earned the Celestial [[Predator]] Box.
* It is a referenced area in the game's broader map, used for comparison to Semeru and Larracos, mentioned in cultural context.
* The Hunting Grounds is a deeper, desirable area players hope to reach by descending, with a prospective reward promised to workers at the Desperado Club and a motivation for several characters to come to the [[town]].
* It is a lower-level area accessible via a guarded [[spiral]] staircase in the [[Casino]], implied to contain additional games or activities beneath the main floor.
* The Hunting Grounds is a name for the sixth floor that changelings and NPCs reference as a lost home, sought after but not currently reachable from the base.
* It is a referenced accessible zone that, once known to be reachable, motivated Juice Box to assist her brother, tied contextually to the larger artifact and Quetzalcoatlus hunt.
* The Hunting Grounds is a dangerous, unprotected area associated with the Sixth Floor, used by hunters to collect gear, where hunters can die permanently.
* It is a floor in which the chapter is set, described as a largely [[jungle]] world with a single named metropolis and many smaller cities, with iteration-dependent monster sets but a largely consistent map and political layout.
* The Hunting Grounds is a larger region/floor area containing settlements, districts, roads, bridges, and urban centers crawled by hunters and parties.
* It is a named region tied to Signet's exile and the site of past conflicts and transformations, where the group is operating within or near the Hunting Grounds during the chapter.
* The Hunting Grounds is a region where some of Signet's tattoo-derived progeny, like Areson, remained to live and grow.
* It is a general location/context where hunting conflicts and legal disputes between hunters (and factions like the the hive) take place, referenced as the site of prior violent incidents.
* The Hunting Grounds is a regional zone referenced in the quest description where Big Tina is rampaging and where High Elves might be forced to deploy troops if the situation is not contained.
* It is a region referenced as having been transmuted or devastated in the past nine-tier attack, used to indicate the scale of prior disasters that inform current fears.
* The Hunting Grounds is a zone where hunters operate, referenced in relation to the Apex [[Predator]] achievement earned by [[dungeon]] [[crawler]] Carl for killing hunters.
