# jikininki_janitor_ghoul

* A grotesque enemy type featured in recap footage, decapitated by [[Lucia Mar]].
* A specific [[ghoul]] subtype associated with Station 12 and janitorial roles, encountered sweeping and disposal duties.
* A mob type scavenging the wreckage at the base of the [[Abyss]], described as crowding and swarming over crashed [[train]] cars like ants.
* A named mob type encountered in the subway cars, shown in recap footage being brutally dispatched by [[Lucia Mar]].
* A specific [[ghoul]] subtype that occupied Station 12, with an ecological/occupational flavor name implying a janitorial role among [[ghoul]] types.
* A skeletal, chattering-toothed undead mob, with two level 25 examples standing at the door in the chapter, and also found on [[the tangle]].
* One of the [[ghoul]] subtypes mentioned on the line (reported at stop 12), a potential [[target]] for counterattacks given spawn mechanics.
