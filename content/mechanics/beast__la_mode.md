# Beast à la Mode
Named deceased dancer referenced in conversation; remembered by [[Splash Zone]] and [[Dong]] as having died from foodborne illness (E. and D. coli) after a salad bar incident at a venue called "Bitches." Used as a cultural reference in the group’s banter.
