# coln_cemetery

• The Colón [[Cemetery]] is a large, gothic [[cemetery]]-park with a gothic entrance arch and fence.
• The area is [[shrouded]] in fog and contains red-map-dots for mobs.
• A chapel or spire is located in the center of the [[cemetery]].
• The party recognizes the landmark and notes differences compared to the real-world location, including a new fence and inner city-like layout.
