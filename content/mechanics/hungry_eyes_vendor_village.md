# hungry_eyes_vendor_village

• A bustling vendor [[town]] with neon- cyberpunk aesthetics, featuring multiple streets, temples, vendor stalls, and a central plaza (Main Square).
• Includes establishments such as The [[Hairpin]], The [[Lollipop]], C&W&U, and Club Vanquisher.
• Features NPCs like Gremlins and Draconians, and has specific system behavior tied to food and safety rules.
