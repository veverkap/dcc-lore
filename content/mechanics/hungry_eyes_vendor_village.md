A bustling vendor town encountered on the current floor. # Hungry Eyes Vendor Village
A bustling vendor town encountered by [[Carl]] and companions. Described as a neon, 1980s cyberpunk–styled market full of NPCs (notably [[Gremlins]] and [[Draconians]]), multiple bars (including [[The Hairpin]] and [[The Lollipop]]), temple-front clubs (including [[Club Vanquisher]]), and various storefronts such as [[C&W&U]]. The village has specific system behavior tied to food and safety rules.
