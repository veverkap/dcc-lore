# crawlcon

• A multi-day fan convention held at a physical location in the inner system, featuring panels, booths, vendor areas, and a daycare with an art contest for alien and human children.
• The convention where the panel takes place, featuring audience members, sponsors, and panels discussing crawl history and tactics.
• The convention where the signing takes place, hosting fans, a lottery for line positions, premium spots, press, and sponsor reveal events tied to benefactor boxes.
• A convention event attended by [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]], where he encountered [[Vrah]]'s mother, leading to her enraged involvement in the crawl as [[Diwata]].
• Event where [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]] met the designer of the [[desperado club]] and judged an art contest.
• A convention/ event where crawlers and industry figures meet; the narrator recalls sharing a panel with [[Drick]] at [[CrawlCon]].
• Large fan convention event (virtual and physical) described in the fan box description; [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]] is scheduled for multiple virtual events there.
• An intersystem, physical convention attended both physically and virtually, hosting panels, vendor booths, and a daycare art contest judged by celebrities like [[Hurk]] and [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]].
• The fan convention/event where the signing and bidding for sponsors occur, hosting a limited line of attendees and premium spots, press coverage, and on-[[stage]] presentations including the awarding of a [[Benefactor Box]].
