# secondary_zone

• The Secondary Zone is an above-surface area classification that disables certain [[dungeon]]-specific protections and inventory functionality.
• The Production [[Trailer]] sits in this zone, explaining why items like [[Mongo]] and [[Stinger]] cannot be freely used.
• The Secondary Zone extends an additional five kilometers above the surface, part of the crawl's spatial zoning architecture.
• This zone is also referred to as a [[studio]]/above-surface classification where certain [[dungeon]]-specific items and protections do not function.
