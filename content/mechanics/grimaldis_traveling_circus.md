# grimaldis_traveling_circus

• The Grimaldi's Traveling [[Circus]] is a ruined carnival with tents, mortars, clowns, lemurs, and fortifications.
• The site is magically protected from outside entry.
• It is the location where Heather launched an attack, Signet was summoned, and the Big Top is located.
