# [[Grimaldi's Traveling Circus]]
The circus location and centerpiece of the chapter: a ruined carnival with tents, mortars, clowns, lemurs, and fortifications. The site of Heather’s attack, Signet’s summoning, and the Big Top that is magically protected from outside entry.
