# elf_castle_the_sugar_cube

• The Elf Castle, also known as the Sugar Cube, is a white, cube/rectangular elven castle located on a rocky bluff in the southeast corner of the map.
• It features pencil-thin [[towers]] at each corner, multiple floors (public spaces and hidden peripheral floors), [[basement]] caves, and a central third-floor control room for [[ballroom]] systems.
