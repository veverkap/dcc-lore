# [[Elf Castle (the Sugar Cube)]]
The white, cube/rectangular elven castle where the masquerade is held. Located on a rocky bluff in the southeast corner of the map with pencil‑thin towers at each corner, multiple floors (public spaces and hidden peripheral floors), basement caves, and a central third‑floor control room for ballroom systems.
