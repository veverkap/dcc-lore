# ghazis_workroom_boss_chamber

• The low-ceiling [[basement]] room behind a wooden door in the [[sandcastle]] serves as [[Ghazi]]'s living/working space and the practical boss chamber.
• The room contains a sand-ooze floor, clutter, a TV-like panel, and a small rear room with a level [[stairwell]].
