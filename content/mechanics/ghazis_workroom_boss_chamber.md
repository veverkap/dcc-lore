# [[Ghazi's Workroom (Boss Chamber)]]
The low-ceiling basement room behind a wooden door in the [[sandcastle]] that serves as [[Ghazi]]'s living/working space and the practical boss chamber: contains sand-ooze floor, clutters, a TV-like panel, and the small rear room with the level stairwell.
