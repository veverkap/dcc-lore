# type-a_containment_zone

• A specific containment setting where most of Donut's enhancements are turned off, designed for crawlers with issues with standard containment.
• A zone with most [[crawler]] enhancements turned off, similar to a zero zone.
• Donut is held in a Type-A containment zone aboard the [[Ventilator]] during a meeting with Chandra.
