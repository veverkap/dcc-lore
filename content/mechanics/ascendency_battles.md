# Ascendency battles
A dungeon rule-driven endgame series of fights that begin immediately if [[Scolopendra]] is killed. Winners of Ascendency are crowned and trigger a dungeon collapse 12 hours afterward; these battles are a core part of the post-[[Scolopendra]] rule sequence and affect victory conditions.
