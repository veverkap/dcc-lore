# ascendency_battles

• A [[dungeon]] mechanic that triggers immediately if [[Scolopendra]] is killed: long, decisive fights to crown a [[winner]], after which the [[dungeon]] collapses 12 hours later.
• The suspension of collapse timers hinges on the resolution of these battles.
• Winners of [[Ascendency]] are crowned and trigger a [[dungeon]] collapse 12 hours afterward.
• These battles continue while players try to reach the 18th floor.
• Winning or abandoning key entities (e.g., leaving [[Scolopendra]] in certain states) affects final attacks and collapse procedures.
