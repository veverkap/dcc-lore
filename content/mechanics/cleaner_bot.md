# cleaner_bot

* Automated maintenance bot in the personal space, reacting to messes with trills.
* Present in the tavern, reacting to coin spills and shooed away during the gold drop incident.
* Operates in the [[saferoom]], cleaning up spills, and intervening when the wriggling spell book [[Oozy Form]] escapes.
* Reacts vocally to disturbances, including the arrival of the [[Legendary Fan Box]] contents.
* Disintegrates debris using a ray.
* Acts like an NPC device within the safe room.
* Sorts the room's [[loot]] and shrills while tending to an ill party member.
* Present in the safehouse, following the bear cubs around and beeping with concern.
* Husks up the oily [[Spellbook of Run, Little Günter, Run]] and cleans spilled goo in [[Shanty Town]].
* Cleans up spills, picks up trash, and intervenes when the wriggling spell book [[Oozy Form]] escapes.
* Reacts vocally to physical damage caused by [[Donut]]'s claws.
* Uses a disintegration-like function to zap the broken [[kitchen]] table.
* Reacts to the deluge of fan-box junk with alarm squeals.
* Active during cleanup attempts.
* Present in the [[saferoom]], reacting to messes with angry beeps.
* Cleans up after grooming or feeding events.
* Interacts physically with items and players.
* Becomes overwhelmed by the volume of junk.
* Sanitizes the scrap pile and is confused by the appearance of the [[White cleaner bot]].
