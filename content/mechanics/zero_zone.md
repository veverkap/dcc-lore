# zero_zone

* A type of [[dungeon]]/ship zone where all enhancements are suppressed, temporarily disabling gear effects.
* The specific meeting room environment where Porthus negotiates deals with the Soother Attorney, with augmentations and strength enhancements suppressed.
* A medical/containment setting outside the [[dungeon]] where lingering injuries are present.
* A temporary area created by Orren the Liaison using Orren's Ball, disabling menus and AI oversight.
* A gameplay mechanic restricting player menus and abilities when active.
* A broader containment concept used for crawlers, representing a standard low-enhancement environment.
* A prior location the narrator had been in immediately before arriving at the Tenth Floor.
* An explicit [[dungeon]] rule/zone type where all enhancements are suppressed and players feel weakened.
* A special meeting/room type where enhancements are disabled, imposing limitations and a sensation of weighted helplessness on crawlers.
