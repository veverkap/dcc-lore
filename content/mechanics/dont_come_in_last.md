# Don't come in last
The name of the tenth floor. A race-themed floor consisting of seven heats with teams (nine teams per heat initially), vehicle selection by wheel, and environment/hazard changes between heats. The floor’s title signals the lethal penalty for finishing last.

# Don't come in last
The tenth floor of the dungeon and the racing arena where seven heats are held with roughly 1,800 teams. The floor's gimmick is literal: coming in last has severe consequences. The floor includes a garage staging area and variable tracks designed by the system AI.
