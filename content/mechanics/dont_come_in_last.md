# dont_come_in_last

• The tenth floor is a race-themed floor with seven heats, featuring nine teams per [[heat]] initially, vehicle selection by wheel, and environment/hazard changes between heats.
• The floor's title, "Don't come in last", signals the lethal penalty for finishing last.
• The floor is the tenth floor of the [[dungeon]] and the racing [[arena]], where seven heats are held with approximately 1,800 teams.
• The floor includes a [[garage]] staging area and variable tracks designed by the system AI.
• Coming in last has severe consequences.
