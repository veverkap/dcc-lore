# club_scolopendra

* A venue on the 18th level where wealthy parties and faction members celebrate, with orbital security.
* A named meeting place where Carl instructs Emberus to come to, used as the rendezvous point during the Scolopendra encounter.
* A club built into or inside the anatomy of Scolopendra, functioning as a safe room/resort area for tourists, but vulnerable to certain deity and boss effects.
* A nightlife venue referenced as a destination Ripper Wonton wanted to visit, culturally associated with the name and aura of Scolopendra.
* A location on one of the affected floors where ongoing slaughter and torture occurs after the breach of the [[Nothing]], home to powerful invaders like Kyryap and Krakaren Prime.
