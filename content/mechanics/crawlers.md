# Crawlers
Term used in the [[Syndicate Announcement]] to describe surviving humans who choose to enter and progress through the [[World Dungeon]] levels seeking to reclaim their lost matter and resources.

# Crawlers
The player-characters / contestants participating in dungeon runs and public events like [[CrawlCon]]. The term refers collectively to figures such as [[dungeon crawler carl]], [[Donut]], and others who must comply with vessel security and scheduling.

# Crawlers
Group term for Carl’s faction/race of survivors operating within the dungeon environment. They have claimed many towns and often cannot be mayors except in special cases (e.g., Zockau). The club enforces separation between [[Crawlers]] and [[Hunters]] on specific floors.
