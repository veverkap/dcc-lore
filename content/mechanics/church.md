# church

• The Church is a tall, wide building housing the possessed city boss/demon, with a focal point for the group's current encounter and demon congregation.
• The Church is the immediate compound/area where the group gathers, with a slug feeding, arriving crawlers, and Doggie Door exits.
• The Church is a building referenced by Imani as a previous location for an airborne threat, indicating movement of a rival.
• The Church is a major building on the current floor, housing the [[stairwell]] doorway and the locus of the demon event, surrounded by possessed zombie demons and a giant demon casting a sky [[portal]].
• The Church is a neighborhood landmark and large structure containing obstacles and at least one [[target]] (the nun), part of local tactical considerations and a named point Carl wants to access.
• The Church is the ruined building and battle site where the group fights, later collapses, and whose surroundings are used to judge casualty and escape, focal point of the floor's major confrontation.
• The Church is the physical location on the floor where much of the final action centered, approach point for the party before the heaven [[portal]] ascension.
