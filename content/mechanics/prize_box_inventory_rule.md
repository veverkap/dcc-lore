# prize_box_inventory_rule

• Purchased prizes are delivered as prize boxes to a buyer's box inventory.
• Prize boxes must be opened in a safe room before the floor ends to transfer contents to regular inventory.
• Failure to open prize boxes before the floor collapses means losing the items.
• Items in prize boxes cannot be used while still boxed or inside the [[ballroom]].
