# [[Prize Box / Box Inventory Rule]]
System mechanic: items purchased at the prize counter are delivered as prize boxes into a separate "box inventory." Boxes must be opened in a [[Saferoom]] before floor end to transfer contents to regular inventory; failure to open before floor collapse means losing the items. Items in prize boxes cannot be used while still boxed or inside the ballroom.
