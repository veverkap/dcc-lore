# The Creeping Apocalypse
World quest system announcement that forces all active crawlers and hunters on the [[Sixth Floor]] to participate. Orders the killing of all fifty [[Odious Creeper]] before sunset, grants stat and level rewards for kills, and randomizes creeper locations; explicitly non-optional and carries severe failure consequences.
