# the_creeping_apocalypse

• World quest system announcement forces all active crawlers and hunters on the [[Sixth Floor]] to participate.
• Orders the killing of all fifty [[Odious Creeper]] before sunset.
• Grants stat and level rewards for kills.
• Randomizes creeper locations.
• Failure carries severe consequences.
