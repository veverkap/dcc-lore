# Shanty Town
A newly large ring of improvised buildings and markets that sprang up outside the entrance to [[Larracos]] after flooding. Contains food markets, temples, and at least one hidden [[Club Vanquisher entrance]]. Temporarily populated by displaced NPCs and contested politically (mayors changing, poisoning schemes, and team interventions).
