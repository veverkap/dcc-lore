# shanty_town

Here is the consolidated markdown list:

• A newly large ring of improvised buildings and markets that sprang up outside the entrance to [[Larracos]] after flooding.
• Contains food markets, temples, and at least one hidden [[Club Vanquisher entrance]].
• Temporarily populated by displaced NPCs and contested politically (mayors changing, poisoning schemes, and team interventions).
• Controlled by [[Sir Ferdinand]] and negotiated to be acquired as part of the hat-for-turban [[deal]] by the [[Princess Posse]].
• A separate district with its own defense system; considered a liability if hostile forces can freely enter after a truce is broken.
• A ring- like settlement that now encircles [[Larracos]] and is controlled by the [[Princess Posse]].
• Upgraded and enlarged, creating an additional defensive ring and strategic possession noted on the holographic map.
• A frontline settlement where defensive [[towers]] and bombs are being built and coordinated by [[Li Jun]], [[Li Na]], and others; under threat as the chat goes to code red.
• A nearby settlement referenced as the location of certain SOF units and personnel not at the front (e.g., some of the [[Frothing [[Tunnel]] Moles]] and veterans lodged with Britney).
• A nearby settlement close to the [[Naga castle]] noted for the castle's proximity to the [[town]] edge, affecting tunneling and siege strategy.
• A low-status settlement to which [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]] plans to send [[Toyotomi]] (framed as exile) to aid the frontlines rather than have him remain in the castle.
• A makeshift donut-shaped [[town]] on the campaign map bordered by multiple territories; contains the [[white dragons]] garrison and special defense rules tied to [[Larracos]]'s defense system.
• A nearby area used by some defenders to approach the castle via a doggie door; referenced as part of a detour route into the interior where allies like [[elle mcgib]] were posted.
• A nearby populated zone containing alternative saferooms and serving as fallback access for the defenders if the castle [[Saferoom]] is disabled.
• A nearby fallback location mentioned as a potential corridor of retreat that [[Florin]] and allied units could keep open for [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]]'s forces.
• A location referenced as needing defensive fortification and urgent staffing in the wake of the [[failsafe]] failure.
• A neighborhood where [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]] entered through one of the saferooms and where the [[kitchen]] scene with the strange spellbook occurs.
• A location/zone where Carl returns and where he plans to meet up with [[Li Na]] and [[Katia]]. Teams [[stage]] around its border during the operation.
• A border settlement and staging area where Group Four and the [[white dragons]] set up to execute the critical portion of the operation against the [[sultanate]] [[stronghold]].
• A neighborhood zone where the [[Temple of the Dagda]] is nestled among shacks and where the assault forces assemble prior to entering [[Club Vanquisher]].
• A location connected by door to the [[Temple of the Dagda]] and thus indirectly to [[Club Vanquisher]]; the first of the three-door chain mentioned in the infiltration plan.
• A location adjacent to the club area; Bautista is stationed at the door out to [[Shanty Town]].
• A populated area near the base where loud broadcasts (e.g., [[Donut]]'s PA) could affect inhabitants; mentioned in relation to noise concerns.
• A settlement where missile support operators like [[Bomo]] and [[Sledge]] are based and from which they can run to assist [[Donut]].
• A less-safe area near [[Area 52]] mentioned as not acceptable [[shelter]] compared to [[Larracos]].
• A fallback/escape destination [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]] planned to [[teleport]] back to using a trap after entering the [[portal]].
• A staging/ backline area referenced as where other friendly forces (e.g., [[Bodi]] and [[Prepotente]] with the [[103rd]]) are approaching from to draw defender attention.
• A key settlement on the floor whose defenders are variably affected by [[Eris]]'s ritual; many allied forces (and NPCs) inside structures in [[Shanty Town]] remain unfrozen.
• A district of [[Larracos]] mentioned as the area the [[slow]]-moving lava glob is about to enter, near a giant [[hole]] in the ground.
• A location mentioned as the destination for certain mounts or paths and the place where changelings are holding a threshold that stops gods like [[Harpocrates]] from passing.
• A settlement adjacent to the city edge and a [[battlefield]] entry point; Carl jumps into Shanty [[Town]] and attempts to reach the [[Nest Tower]] from here.
• A ruined district where the gods fight and much of the destruction has occurred; many of the crawl's outlying [[towers]] were trampled during the divine battle
