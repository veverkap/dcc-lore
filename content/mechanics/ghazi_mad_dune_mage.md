# ghazi_mad_dune_mage

• The Mad Dune Mage who turned himself into a sand elemental to search for the [[Gate of the Feral Gods]].
• His castle houses the [[Winding Box]] and his presence complicates entry.
• He is now integrated into the sand of his namesake [[sandcastle]].
