# thorny_devil

• Common desert lizard-class mob encountered in the dunes, dropping materials like a thorny devil liver and teeth.
• Large lizard-class mob encountered outside [[town]], with heavy armor and spikes, and labeled with behavior warnings.
• Hostile desert mob that [[Donut]] smashes through after using the [[Dolores Doesn’t Splat]] potion and impacting the ground with meteor-like force.
