# Grubs (Level-Two Grubs)
Small janitor-type mobs generated by the [[Waste Disposal System]]. In this chapter hundreds-to-thousands swarm hallways; level-two grubs are harmless individually (give no XP) but when corpses exist can level up. Their kills can trigger additional spawns per the [[Corpse-Triggered Spawn Rule]].
