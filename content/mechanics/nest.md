# nest

• Primary [[saferoom]]/base area where mages enchant blocks and key NPCs coordinate.
• Contains the Nest [[tower]] and is adjacent to the brig complex and the Dagda [[temple]] remains.
• A [[tower]] with configurable synergy that enhances either sniper capabilities or Atrocity-class spells.
• Central to strategic use of Donut's abilities.
• A strategic [[tower]]/position that floating soldiers are pulled toward and is connected to the castle defense.
• [[Li Na]] attaches chains to the Nest during the finishing sequence.
• The location shaken by Harpocrates' scream where characters (including [[Rosetta]]) witness divine dismemberment and the surrounding horror.
