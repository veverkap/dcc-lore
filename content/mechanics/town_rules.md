# [[Town Rules]]
An explicit set of civic regulations posted in the [[Large Ursine Settlement]]. These dungeon rules (read by the system) include prohibitions (e.g., no fighting, no alcohol, no magic on the streets, no Macaques unless escorted, mandatory baths for bugbears, absolute ban on dinosaurs) and carry the mechanical weight of in-world enforcement (decapitation penalty stated).
