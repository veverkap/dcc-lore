# town_rules

• An explicit set of civic regulations posted in the [[Large Ursine Settlement]].
• Prohibitions include no fighting, no alcohol, no magic on the streets, no Macaques unless escorted, mandatory baths for bugbears, and an absolute ban on dinosaurs.
• Carries the mechanical weight of in-world enforcement, including a decapitation penalty.
