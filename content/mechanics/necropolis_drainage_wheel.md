# [[Necropolis Drainage Wheel]]
A mechanical panel/wheel on the necropolis wall that, when turned, opens the main drainage pipe and forces water out to the ocean, capable of destroying the sandcastle by turning it into mud. Tied into dungeon puzzle mechanics and sequencing.
