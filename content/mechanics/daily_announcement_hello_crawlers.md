# [[Daily Announcement: Hello, Crawlers!]]
The system-wide daily broadcast that interrupts the saferoom to provide population stats, guild/party counts, hunter activity, prizes, floor redesign notices, explicit dungeon placement rules (e.g., placement penalties for going down within six hours of level collapse), and invitations (e.g., [[Butcher's Masquerade])]. Classified as an active System Announcement.
