# ocean_floor

• The Ocean Floor is a green- hued, silt-filled seabed strewn with blood and wreckage, including the buried [[Buzzsaw (25-foot activated buzzsaw)]].
• [[Dungeon [[Crawler]] Carl]] earned the [[Rock Bottom]] achievement for diving more than 1,000 meters and surviving the ocean floor descent.
