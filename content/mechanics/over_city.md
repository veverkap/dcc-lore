# over_city

Here is the consolidated markdown list:

• [[Over City]] is an urban- level region of the [[Dungeon]] world where Incubi and related beings are found.
• The thematic third-floor region is comprised of hundreds of small villages and vast ruined urban areas (the [[Over City Ruins]]).
• The ruined urban region contains the [[circus]] remnants and taller building facades, origin of many cursed attractions like [[Grimaldi’'s Traveling Circus]].
• The urban setting is where infected [[circus]] acts, elites like [[Signet]], and civic rules (announcements) play out.
• The larger urban zone contains [[Grimaldi'’s Big Top]]; noted as the original setting for the [[circus]] before the cataclysm and the floor surface on which the vine's roots crawl.
• The urban ruin zone is populated by mobs like the [[Street Urchin]], the [[circus]] site, and multiple settlements including the [[Skyfowl settlement]].
• The urban- level setting contains establishments like the [[desperado club]] and rival venues such as [[Club Vanquisher]].
• The larger urban region/setting contains wards, townships, and local power structures (guards, magistrates).
• The urban zone is shown in broadcast footage where [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]] ran nearly naked wearing a [[one-armed leather jacket]].
• The broader game/setting region is referenced as the location of multiple group quest events and recap episodes that influenced [[crawler]] population and AI decisions.
• A separate city is referenced for comparison: its guards are swordsmen, unlike the mushroom-man [[Funeral Bell]] guards of the sixth floor settlements.
• A major urban location is where [[Signet]] once lived with the [[circus]] before Scolopendra's disaster; some characters (like [[Apollon]]) were in the Over City during earlier events.
• A location is mentioned as where [[Signet]] fled and later joined the [[circus]]; part of her exile backstory.
• A major urban location is to which [[Apollon]] traveled and where he worked in the [[circus]].
• A referenced location is used as a contrast to [[Semeru]] and [[Larracos]] (part of broader level geography in the game's cosmology).
• A separate game zone is referenced for contrast with [[Larracos]]; described as having a wooden floor and different environmental rules than the funnel city.
• The larger urban region/setting houses the [[town]] where the events occur, mentioned in lore regarding the [[Skyfowl]], the [[Hunting Grounds]], and the placement of militias like the [[201st Security Group – Magical Ops]].
• The larger city/region is in which this Skyfowl settlement exists; referenced as a multi-level urban area with a ninth-floor capital in the pre-cataclysm hierarchy.
• The broader urban region formerly dominated by the [[Skyfowl]] and the stated [[target]] of [[Miss Quill]]'s heirloom spell [[The Final War]].
• An above- level city environment is shown in the opening visuals; [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]]'s unclothed run across the [[Over City]] was displayed on the broadcast.
• A contrasting place to the Dryad settlement; home to a different style of swordsmen guards and urban environment elsewhere in the game world.
• A major urban area is where [[Signet]] once lived after exile; referenced as the place where some family members (such as [[Apollon]] at the time of attack) were located.
• A place [[Signet]] fled to and later joined a [[circus]] in, referenced in her backstory.
• A distant urban area is where [[Apollon]] traveled and worked in a [[circus]]; referenced as part of regional geography.
• A major city is referenced historically as devastated by the nine-tier attack; used to contextualize large-scale consequences that ripple into smaller events like those in this chapter.
