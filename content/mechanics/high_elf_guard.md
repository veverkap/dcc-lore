# high_elf_guard

• Named white-tag NPCs (level 70) that accompany the high-elven procession; described as elite, lance-wielding guards.
• Elite high-elven armored guards (level ~70) riding [[Moon Sambhur]] mounts or on foot, armed with enchanted lances and bows; part of the royal hunting party dispatched from the [[Castle of the High Elves]].
