# Bravvo
Unaffiliated level-50 yenk hunter who was shot from the sky and found tied across saddles on a massive caterpillar. Severely injured and nearly dead; his presence reveals a trap involving [[Elites]].

# Bravvo
A hunter encountered and killed by [[dungeon crawler carl]] and [[Donut]]. His equipment included enchanted black leather boots, thirty [[Good Health Potion]]s, thirty [[Good Mana Refill]]s, a magical weapon described as a [[Magical Longsword]], and nine [[levitate]]s. His death granted [[dungeon crawler carl]] a single stat point and yielded limited loot.
