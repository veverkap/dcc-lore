# time_until_level_collapse

• A System Announcement timer signals when the floor will collapse and forces progression, displaying times such as "31 hours", "6 days, 22 hours", or "4 Days and 17 Hours".
• The timer indicates floor stability and drives urgency for key collection and reaching the [[stairwell]].
