# the_recital

• A quest update notification issued during the chapter that assigns the party an investigative objective tied to dinosaur attacks, directly leading into the larger revenge arc involving [[Big Tina]], [[Kiwi the mongoliensis]], and threats to nearby settlements such as [[Point Mongo]].
• A system-issued quest for [[Point Mongo]]: investigate or exterminate increasing dinosaur attacks on the settlement, with rewards including a [[Gold Quest Box]] and a [[town]] upgrade.
• A system quest announcement describing increasing dinosaur attacks on [[Point Mongo]] and offering two approaches: exterminate the threat or discover and satisfy the dinosaurs' motive, with rewards including a Gold Quest Box and a free [[town]] upgrade.
• Active system quest message received by [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]]'s party, describing [[Big Tina]]'s rampage, providing objectives ("Tina must be stopped. Kill her or give her what she wants"), and warning of possible High Elf military involvement if left unchecked.
