# zomp_line

• The Zomp Line is a transit line used by the party, featuring interdiction carts ("E" cart and "Q" / Def Leppard cart) and [[portals]] tuned to the [[abyss]] to prevent lateral monster attacks.
• The interdiction carts are named the "E" and "Q" (Def Leppard) carts.
• The [[portals]] are tuned to the [[abyss]] to block monster approach while on track.
• The party uses the Zomp Line to move between stations while protected by the interdiction carts.
• The party is located at [[Station 59]] on the [[Zomp Line]] in their current mapping.
