# [[Flesher]]
A sentient strip of skin created by the War Mage spell "You're Not Done Yet." Fleshers seek bones to cover and will smother and melt victims' bodies; they then cast [[Boned]] to animate skeletons into [[Symbiote]]s. Frequently used by war mages as minions.
