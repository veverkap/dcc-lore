# you_sure_you_wanna_do_that_bucko

• System prompt/warning displayed when Carl begins unzipping Donut's [[Prize Case]], acts as a trap-detection prompt tied to the narrator's Find Traps activation, and precedes the [[Literal Fire Ants]] spawn.
• System confirmation prompt that appeared when [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]] attempted to unzip [[Donut]]'s [[Prize Case]], likely tied to trap detection and the game's interactive confirmation layer, shortly before the [[Fire Ants]] were released.
• System prompt/notification that appeared as the party began to unzip Donut's selected [[Prize Suitcase]], acts as an on-screen warning, and precedes the activation of the contained trap [[Literal Fire Ants]].
