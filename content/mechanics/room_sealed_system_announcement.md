# Room Sealed System Announcement
Active in-text system message: "Warning: This room is now sealed. Nobody will be allowed to enter or leave until the conclusion of the party. You are protected from outside influence or spells." Represents an explicit Dungeon Rule/System Announcement affecting player movement and magic in the [[Queen's Ballroom]].
