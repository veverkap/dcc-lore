# [[Reaver cybernetic upgrade rule]]
A contextual rule-of-thumb presented in the chapter: if a soldier has more than two or three cybernetics, they've likely voluntarily re-upped at least once. The chapter also explains the Reaver path to citizenship (first upgrade free; subsequent upgrades earned via deployments or paid).
