# Sekhmet
Sekhmet is a goddess whose court imagery is attached to several items: the [[Assassin's Veil]] (worn by those in her court), the [[Wrap of Sekhmet's Compassion]], and the [[Nemes of Pestilence]] (themed to her). Wearing items tied to Sekhmet can provoke hostility from her enemies.
