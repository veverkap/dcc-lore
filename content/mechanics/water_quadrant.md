# water_quadrant

* The Water [[Quadrant]] is a regional zone of the [[dungeon]] housing nautical castles, where Elle McGib and Meadowlark operate with a galley crew and probe an orc-run oil rig.
* The Water [[Quadrant]] is referenced via the gravitational gauge and [[sea]]-level readouts, sitting below the party's current elevation and part of the multi-zone [[Necropolis]] layout.
* The chapter ends with a notification that the Water [[Quadrant]] has been liberated and freed for inter-[[quadrant]] travel.
* The region was recently conquered by other teams, triggering flooding issues, and is known for deep horrors and aquatic bosses.
* The [[quadrant]] contains strong currents, pressure, and hostile moving predators attracted to blood, but can be survived with scrolls of water breathing.
* The Water [[Quadrant]] is a flooded sector of the [[necropolis]] where the [[Akula]] sits, and the party must operate underwater to turn off the pump, facing aquatic mobs and environmental hazards.
* The Water [[Quadrant]] is used as a reference point for elevation and gravitational zone [[sea]]-level readings in [[Dungeon]] [[Crawler]] Carl's UI.
* The [[quadrant]] is a major zone of the bubble environment, announced as liberated when the Bridge of the [[Akula]] is occupied, enabling crawlers from that [[quadrant]] to travel freely to others.
* The region's capture preceded internal wall changes and the flooding of the [[Necropolis]].
* The Water [[Quadrant]] is one of the floor's themed quadrants where Chris Andrews 2 reports coming from, notable for the submarine encounter and aquatic hazards.
* The [[quadrant]] was previously cleared by other teams, with deep-ocean horrors reported there, and survivors Vadim and Britney refuse to return.
* The party is dumped into the Water [[Quadrant]] after the engineered collapse, where they must use scrolls of water breathing and encounter sinking corpses and a large [[predator]].
* The flooded section of the [[necropolis]] is home to many drowned corridors, sentinel defenses like concierge sharks, and the origin of the party's underwater operation.
* The Water [[Quadrant]] is the [[quadrant]] of the party's bubble that borders the bubble edge and ocean, used as the takeoff/escape area for the Drop Bear flight.
