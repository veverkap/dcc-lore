# demon_eviction

• A system announcement/event: excess death causes demons to be involuntarily ejected from Sheol and attempt possession of recent corpses or undead, with the dangerous side-effect that possessed hosts can drag survivors down into Sheol.
• Due to excess death, demons are being forcibly ejected from Sheol and randomly possessing recently-fallen or weakened corpses.
• Warned via a System Message and an Area Message for [[Cuba]].
