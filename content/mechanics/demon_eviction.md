# Demon Eviction
A system announcement/event described in the chapter: excess death causes [[Demons]] to be involuntarily ejected from [[Sheol]] and to attempt possession of recent corpses or undead, with the dangerous side-effect that possessed hosts can drag survivors down into [[Sheol]].

# Demon Eviction
A system announcement and dungeon mechanic: due to excess death, demons are being forcibly ejected from [[Sheol]] and randomly possessing recently-fallen or weakened corpses. Warned via a [[System Message]] and an [[Area Message]] for [[Cuba]].
