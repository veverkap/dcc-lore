# cwu

• [[C&W&U]]: a shop in [[Hungry Eyes Vendor Village]] with an on-screen notification reading "C&W&U Location. Illegal Modifications and More. Grand Opening Soon." that promises illicit modifications.
• [[C&W&U]]: a repair/shop location that opens "after the third [[heat]]" and is tied to the gremlin-drawn advertisement on an old invoice paper.
• [[C&W&U]]: a location where the narrator and some companions had been, but [[Tipid]] was not present at the time.
