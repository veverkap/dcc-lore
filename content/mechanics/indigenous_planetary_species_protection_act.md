# indigenous_planetary_species_protection_act

• A Syndicate statute that mandates reclamation opportunities for surviving indigenous species.
• Authorizes the creation of the [[World Dungeon]] and associated rules for survival and reclamation.
• Cited in the [[Syndicate Announcement]].
