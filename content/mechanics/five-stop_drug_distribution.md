# Five-Stop Drug Distribution
An explicit dungeon rule/mechanic: every five stops (stations ending in 5 or 0) contains tunnel doors where mobs queue to receive race-specific sedative vials (the [[Rev-up Vitamin Shots]]) from [[Pooka]] bosses and then transit through one-way portals; interrupting trains disrupts this cycle and causes physical transformations in mobs.
