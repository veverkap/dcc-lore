# art_contest_scoring_130

• The Art Contest: Scoring (1–30) is a judging mechanic used for the [[CrawlCon]] daycare art contest.
• Judges assign scores from 1 to 30 for each entry.
• The contestant's final placement is based on averaged totals across judges.
