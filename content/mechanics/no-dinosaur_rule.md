# no-dinosaur_rule

• An explicit in-world rule that can be toggled on or off to control dinosaur presence in [[town]], mentioned by [[Donut]] and [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]].
• The rule can be turned back on to manage wandering dinosaurs.
• [[Donut]] considers turning the rule back on after observing dinosaur activity, including [[Kiwi]] and other Mongos.
