# prize_counter

• The Prize Counter is an event station at the masquerade where items can be turned in for rewards, with attendants extracting items from inventory.
• The Prize Counter is a glass display case across from the [[stage]] in [[Ballroom A]], containing prize figures and oddities, including [[Chaco]] behind a draped blanket.
• The Prize Counter is a glass-enclosed display area in the [[ballroom]] where holographic miniature prizes rotate on a carousel, purchases are made, and items are delivered as prize boxes into box inventory.
• The Prize Counter is a glass display case on the far side of [[Ballroom A]], marked as the location for prize items, housing figures such as [[Chaco]] behind a draped blanket during setup.
• The Prize Counter is a glass case display in the [[Ballroom]], where holographic items and prizes rotate on a vertical carousel, operated by [[Chaco]].
