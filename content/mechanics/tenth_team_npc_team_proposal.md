# [[Tenth team (NPC team) proposal]]
An explicit rule-change proposal made by [[dungeon crawler carl]] and supported by [[Juice Box]] to create a formally recognized tenth team populated by NPCs, with protections and voting rights equivalent to other teams. This proposal triggers system voting and political pushback.
