# view_counter

• [[view counter]] is a system metric that tracks audience attention, spiking as "the whole universe was watching" the unfolding drama.
• The view counter reflects the audience/viewership pressure on participants, affecting narrator frustration and serving as a real-time metric tied to in-[[dungeon]] spectacle.
