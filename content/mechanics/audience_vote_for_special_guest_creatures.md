# Audience vote for 'special guest creatures'
A dungeon mechanic announced by [[Zev]]: when certain mobs labeled "special guest creatures" are killed, the live/home audience may vote to send the creature itself to the 11th floor or to send a predetermined stand-in; roughly 80% of votes favor stand-ins.
