# switching_station

• Named segment of track operations used by the party; location where the party inspects a massive [[portal]] and observes track switches on the [[portal]]'s far side controlling auxiliary routes.
• Large rail switching area with multiple labeled switches (e.g., [[Auxiliary Tracks]], [[Station Repair]], [[Recycle]]) that can route the [[Nightmare Express]] onto different named lines.
• Station near the [[portal]] where [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]] stops to examine the massive gateway; connects to auxiliary tracks and the [[Pass-Through Two-Way Portal]] root-track system.
• Rail infrastructure hub with numerous track choices (e.g., Auxiliary Tracks, Station Repair, [[Recycle]]) that can be manipulated to route trains onto different named lines or back to base from terminal stops.
