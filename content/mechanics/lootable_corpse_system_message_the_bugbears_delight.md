# [[Lootable Corpse System Message: The Bugbear's Delight]]
System text that appears when a hidden boss-control chest is revealed under a grate in the [[Akula]]. Reads "Lootable Corpse. The Bugbear's Delight. Neighborhood Boss. Killed by System Deactivation." Triggering it populates the full submarine map and reveals corpse markers and enemy positions.
