# Shadow Mimic / Changeling Interaction Rule
A systemic rule discussed on-floor: changelings cannot become or "touch" shadow mimics, shadow mimics do not gain the skills of those they copy, and the presence of both types together is treated as a glitch—important for assessing threats like a potential [[Mordecai]]-related anomaly.
