# temple_of_emberus_loan

• An interest-free loan of 990,000 gold with worship and debt restrictions.
• Cannot renounce religion while under debt.
• Must trade jewelry.
• Accelerated penalties on losing the ring.
• Tied to the [[Enchanted Pauper’s Ring of the Steadfast Emberus]].
