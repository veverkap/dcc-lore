# [[Temple of Emberus Loan]]
System message describing the terms of the loan: an interest-free loan of 990,000 gold with worship and debt restrictions (cannot renounce religion while under debt, must trade jewelry, accelerated penalties on losing the ring, etc.). An explicit game/dungeon rule tied to the [[Enchanted Pauper’s Ring of the Steadfast Emberus]].
