# Abandoned Indoor Swimming Pool (YMCA)
An abandoned, partially-collapsed swimming pool area (former Over City YMCA) where the corpse of the neighborhood boss [[The Divider]] was found. Site of the major ambush and explosive chain reaction that propelled [[dungeon crawler carl]] to level up.
