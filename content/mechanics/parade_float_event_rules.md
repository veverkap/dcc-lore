# parade_float_event_rules

• garages will merge into five parade float groups, each assigned a theme
• teams have ninety minutes to build procession(s)
• route is three kilometers with spectators
• procession culminates at judging stands and an [[arena]] fight against stand-ins and a floor boss to open the [[stairwell]]
• system-announced rules are delivered by [[Chaco]]
