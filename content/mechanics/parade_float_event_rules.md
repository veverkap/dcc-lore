# Parade Float Event rules
System-announced rules delivered by [[Chaco]]: garages will merge into five parade float groups, each assigned a theme; teams have ninety minutes to build procession(s); route is three kilometers with spectators; procession culminates at judging stands and an arena fight against stand-ins and a floor boss to open the stairwell.
