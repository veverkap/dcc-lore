# [[faction wars games of the ninth floor]]
An explicit game-mode rule set on the [[ninth floor]]: teams receive a [[throne seed]] on arrival, must place it within a self-contained structure within a day, and conquest mechanics transfer dominion and armies when a castle is claimed.
