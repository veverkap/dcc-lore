# shattering_train_bomb

• A hostile mechanical/environmental status applied to the [[Nightmare Express]]'s boiler, which will detonate under extreme pressure, presented as an in-world tooltip with a degrading status meter and a time/pressure-critical challenge for the player.
• The [[train]]'s boiler is described as a massive, deteriorating explosive device, displaying a status ("Deteriorating. 75/1000") and warning of imminent catastrophic detonation if pressure is not managed.
• The boiler is a decaying, [[train]]-sized explosive device with a status meter, displayed during [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]]'s attempts to control the engine.
• The boiler is described as a [[train]]-sized grenade that will detonate if pressure isn't managed, shown with a deteriorating status (e.g., 75/1000) and imposing a timed pressure-management challenge on the player.
