# Shattering Train Bomb
A hostile mechanical/environmental status applied to the [[Nightmare Express]]'s boiler: "A steam boiler made with a shocking disregard for safety" that will detonate under extreme pressure. Presented as an in-world tooltip with a degrading status meter and a time/pressure-critical challenge for the player.
