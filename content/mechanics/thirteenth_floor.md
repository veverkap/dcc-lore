# thirteenth_floor

• A famed, difficult floor referenced as a landmark in [[crawler]] history; [[Odette]] and [[Mordecai]]'s stories intersect around attempts to reach or pass the thirteenth floor.
• Mentioned as a deeper floor below the [[Twelfth Floor]] that adventurers aspire to reach; associated with extremely powerful encounters and fire gates.
• Called out as a potentially problematic floor for crawlers who must still progress toward the 18th floor after the [[Scolopendra]] encounter.
