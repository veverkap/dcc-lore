# pub

• Attached tavern with hockey paraphernalia where [[Prudence]] and her cubs hide.
• Public gathering spot where [[Prepotente]] arrives, the group meets, and a benefactor potion is placed on the table.
• The [[bar]] where the group assembles; scenes include [[Prepotente]]'s arrival, conversation about benefactor items, and observation of [[Bianca]] outside.
