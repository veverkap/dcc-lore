# focsa_building

• The FOCSA Building is a prominent location in the chapter, serving as a presumed [[portal]] or home for demons.
• It is a skyscraper-like location with a [[lobby]], elevators, [[stairwells]], upper residential floors, a service [[stairwell]] to penthouses, and a [[roof]] where the snake city boss is located.
• The building is a focal point in the demon eviction patterns described by the crawlers.
• Demons concentrate around the building during the eviction and it figures into local fight dynamics near the [[church]] and the assault staging area.
• The building is also the location of the party's assault, featuring a [[lobby]], stores ringing the perimeter, elevators, and [[stairwells]] leading up to the penthouses and the [[roof]] where the demon and Sister Ines are encountered.
