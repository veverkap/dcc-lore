# lollipop

• A crowded karaoke [[bar]] on the current [[dungeon]] floor where crawlers gather after heats, featuring gremlin bartenders and a [[stage]] used by monster mobs for performances.
• The [[bar]] contains a karaoke [[stage]], gremlin barkeeper, and bingo of NPCs, and hides the entrance to the [[Stage Performance Guild]] via a song-based mechanic.
• The venue contains a DJ stand and karaoke [[stage]] and is the immediate location for events involving [[Carl]], [[Donut]], [[Team Meadow Lark]], and other characters.
• The [[bar]] is mentioned as closed, prompting [[Britney]] to go get a drink elsewhere; part of the local entertainment zone.
