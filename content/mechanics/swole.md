# swole

• A permanent upgrade choice: every one point of Strength gained from level-up stat points counts as two points thereafter (not retroactive; does not apply to equipment).
• An Admin-choice upgrade option granted by the achievement [[What big muscles you have!]]: future Strength gained from level-up points counts double (1 point → 2 points).
