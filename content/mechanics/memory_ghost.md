# Memory Ghost
The persistent spectral remnants of people and animals populating [[dungeon crawler carl]]’s environment (e.g., drivers on the road, the dog [[Lightning Lou]], and sleeping men at the shelter). Memory ghosts are tangible to sight but intangible to touch.
