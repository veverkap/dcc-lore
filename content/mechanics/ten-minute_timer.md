# Ten-Minute Timer
On-screen countdown activated when [[Lusca]] mouths the [[Akula]]; represents the babies' feeding window (roughly ten minutes) during which internal destruction and digestion mechanics threaten the players. Visualized in the broadcast overlay and diagrammed by commentators.

# [[Ten-minute timer]]
Event timer mechanic that starts at ten minutes for the [[Ysalte]]/[[Amayon]] encounter. If [[Amayon]] remains under [[Ysalte]]'s control when the timer ends, both will teleport to the [[celestial ascendency]]; the timer governs the outcome urgency.
