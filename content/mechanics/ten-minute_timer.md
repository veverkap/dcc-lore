# ten-minute_timer

• On-screen countdown activated when [[Lusca]] mouths [[Akula]]; represents the babies' feeding window (roughly ten minutes) during which internal destruction and digestion mechanics threaten the players.
• Visualized in the broadcast overlay and diagrammed by commentators.
• Event timer mechanic that starts at ten minutes for the [[Ysalte]]/[[Amayon]] encounter.
• If [[Amayon]] remains under [[Ysalte]]'s control when the timer ends, both will [[teleport]] to [[celestial ascendency]]; the timer governs the outcome urgency.
