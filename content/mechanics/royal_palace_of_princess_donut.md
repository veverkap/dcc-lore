# royal_palace_of_princess_donut

• The Royal Palace of Princess Donut is a combined personal space and communal base used by [[Princess Donut]], [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]], [[Katia Grim]], [[Mordecai]], and [[Mongo]].
• It is an industrial-[[hangar]] interior transformed into multiple rooms (crafting, training, [[kitchen]], [[restrooms]], [[manager]]'s room) via the game's personal-space UI.
• The palace contains safe rooms and a [[manager]] system with whitelisting mechanics.
• It serves as a staging area before departure to the tracks and a meeting point for the party's interior discussions and potions crafting.
• The palace houses multiple labeled rooms (e.g., [[Crafting Studio]], [[Training Room]], [[Restrooms]]) and personal doors for [[Princess Donut]], [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]], and [[Katia Grim]].
• [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]]'s personal space is a private, decorated living area described jokingly as a palace and used as the meeting point for the party's interior discussions and potions crafting.
