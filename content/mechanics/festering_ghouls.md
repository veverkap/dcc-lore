# festering_ghouls

• A type of [[ghoul]] that aggregates at certain stops to produce wormy frankenmonsters (province bosses).
• Generic [[ghoul]] mobs that move along tracks and are fought by the party, funneling through robot rooms and onto conveyors toward disposal.
• When aggregated in rooms, produce worm-like components that [[fuse]] into a giant frankenmonster (a province boss).
