# ballroom_a

• The main goodwill [[ballroom]] where [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]]'s group performs and is admitted, located on the fourth floor under the [[Protection Seal]] and other overlay restrictions.
• Designated seating/serving area in the masquerade with gold-colored trays, part of the [[ballroom]]'s logistical layout described by [[Theobold]].
• Primary attendees' [[ballroom]] on the fourth floor where [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]]'s group is placed, subject to the banquet's protections: mute, inventory lock, and the [[Protection [[Seal]] (Butcher's Masquerade)]].
• One of the two seating designations at the Masquerade where guests use gold-colored trays as designated by [[Theobold]]'s announcement.
