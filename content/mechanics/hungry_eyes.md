# hungry_eyes

• A [[bar]] where mercenaries for heats can be hired, recommended by [[Hedy]].
• A shop or location where Carl recognizes similar invoices, linking it to repair/shop signage used on the floor.
• A stall or location on the track where [[Bodi]] allegedly ate many things to gain buffs before racing.
• A [[town]]/commons area where volunteers gather to be chosen for the Pineapple Cabaret escape attempt, site of the Linus goodbye party and large crowd activity.
• A named zone/floor area where the [[Outreach Guild]] has opened, and events and announcements take place.
• A zone where crawlers queue and collect [[upgrades]] and food buffs, where [[Prepotente]], [[Donut]], and [[Carl]] gather.
• A meeting/holding location the team intends to regroup at after the rooftop escape, used as a rendezvous point.
• The exit/next-area the party heads toward after leaving the Desperado Club, referenced as the direction they pad back to.
