# [[Five-Stop Tunnel Drug Loop]]
An explicit dungeon-floor rule/mechanic: every five stops (stations ending in 5 or 0) host tunnel portals where monsters line up by race, receive a race-specific vial from a [[Pooka]], enter a one-way portal to consume the dose, and then return to the train. Interrupting trains breaks this loop and causes mobs to physically change when they miss doses.
