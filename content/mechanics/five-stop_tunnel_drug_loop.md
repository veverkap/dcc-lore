# five-stop_tunnel_drug_loop

• Every five stops (stations ending in 5 or 0) host [[tunnel]] [[portals]] where monsters line up by race.
• Monsters receive a race-pecific vial from a [[Pooka]].
• Monsters enter a one-way [[portal]] to consume the dose and then return to the [[train]].
• Interrupting trains breaks the loop and causes mobs to physically change when they miss doses.
