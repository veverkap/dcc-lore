# station_433_terminus

* The [[Vermillion Line]] makes limited stops, including Station 433 (Terminus).
* Station 433 is where the station mimics reside and the red-and-white wooden crossbeam [[mimic]] trap appears across the tracks.
* The [[platform]] is also an escape [[portal]] destination, called out in the system announcement.
* The station is significant as the [[mimic]]'s locus, and the red-and-white crossbeam trap appears there.
* Station 433 is one of the numbered [[stairwell]] [[portals]] referenced in the Iron Tangle System Announcement as an [[emergency]] exit destination.
* The station is where the station mimics lived and where the red-and-white crossbeam [[mimic]] trap was encountered.
* A station [[mimic]] devoured many passengers at this location, referenced in reports from Daniel Bautista and warnings issued by [[Dungeon]] [[Crawler]] Carl.
* The station may provide a route home, as the narrator suspects it corresponds to a place where they teleported a [[mimic]] away.
* Station 433 is a high-numbered [[stairwell]] [[portal]] station included in the system announcement's list of escape [[portals]].
* The station is named Terminus Station (433) and is referenced as an upcoming arrival point where Daniel Bautista is expected to come.
* The station is the last transit station the party expects traffic to reach, associated with incoming reports and other parties.
* Gore-Gore the Manticore recommends Stop 433 as the last recommended disembarkation point, presented as "the last transit station" before unsafe territory.
* Stop 433 is a major transit hub two stops before the end-of-the-line Station 435 Kravyad Station, recommended for transfer and disembarkation by Vernon.
* The stop is identified by Gore-Gore the Manticore as the recommended disembarkation point and the "last transit station" before unsafe areas on the line.
