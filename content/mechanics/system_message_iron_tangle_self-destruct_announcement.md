# System Message: Iron Tangle Self-Destruct Announcement
An active system announcement declaring that the Iron Tangle has initiated a self-destruct sequence, that lines are down, and that emergency escape tunnels and stairwell portals at stations (including [[Station 12]], [[Station 24]], [[Station 36]], [[Station 48]], [[Station 72]], and [[station 433 terminus]]) will open on a timed schedule. Triggered the wide-scale evacuations and combat described.
