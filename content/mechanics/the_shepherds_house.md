# the_shepherds_house

• The Shepherd's House is a homeless [[shelter]] in [[Homestead]], [[Florida]], advertised on the benefactor flyer.
• It promises food, warm beds, fellowship, and a Christmas breakfast at 7 AM.
• No pets are allowed.
