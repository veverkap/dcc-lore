# Carl's Father
The dying man in the hospital bed at the center of the trailer recording. Sickly, gaunt, and lucid, he watches television and is verbally cruel. He is the focus of [[dungeon crawler carl]]'s confrontation and is ultimately injected with a lethal dose of [[Morphine]] by [[Carl's Stepmother]].
