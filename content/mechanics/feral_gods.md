# feral_gods

• Deity-class entities implied by the title of [[Book IV, The [[Gate]] of the Feral Gods]].
• Primordial, banished deities described as immortal beasts driven mad in [[The Nothing]].
• If summoned through the [[Gate of the Feral Gods]], they would enter the world maddened and destructive.
