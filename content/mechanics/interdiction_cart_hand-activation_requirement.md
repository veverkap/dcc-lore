# [[Interdiction Cart Hand-Activation Requirement]]
An explicit activation rule for the [[Interdiction Rapid-Response Railway Repair Cart]]: the cart requires two gnoll hands placed on separated hand-shaped key consoles to start. The system enforces spacing (one operator cannot activate both) and in one instance enforces handedness (a warning indicated "You must use your left hand to activate the key"), complicating human operation.
