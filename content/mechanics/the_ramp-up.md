# the_ramp-up

• The [[Ramp]]-Up: a 180-hour combat phase that triggers after [[The Ceasefire]] ends, characterized by suppression of offensive and [[teleport]] magic, restricted egress through kilometer-wide [[Gates (egress corridors)]], and a focus on non-magical, close-quarters combat.
