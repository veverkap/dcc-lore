# The Ramp-Up
The second combat phase that triggers when [[The Ceasefire]] ends and lasts 180 hours. Rules highlighted in the chapter: suppression of offensive and teleport magic, restricted egress through kilometer-wide [[Gates (egress corridors)]], and a designed emphasis on non-magical, close-quarters combat.
