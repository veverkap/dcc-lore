# kobold

• Mentioned as typical minions or riders associated with [[Danger Dingo]] packs; used as contextual enemy types likely to appear alongside dingoes.
• Small humanoid enemies that ran the fighting pits; a specific kobold held the Master Pen Key and activated the cage controls.
• Common humanoid mobs inhabiting the boss chamber; open cages, chant, and serve as the captors of the dingoes; several are killed by Carl's [[Boom Jug]] during the encounter.
