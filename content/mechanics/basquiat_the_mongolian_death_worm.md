# [[Basquiat, The Mongolian Death Worm]]
A massive totem/tameable monster summoned in the battle (described as bus-sized with three face flaps). Devours multiple totems (rabbit, pig, turtle), vomits gore, and kills Chuluuna; a turning-point threat in the capitol engagement.
