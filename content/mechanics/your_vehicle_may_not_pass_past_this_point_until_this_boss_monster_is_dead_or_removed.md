# Your vehicle may not pass past this point until this boss monster is dead or removed.
An explicit dungeon/system rule displayed as a red notification above each gatekeeper boss (e.g., [[Prison Pocket]] and [[Party Foul]]). This mechanic enforces that mounts or vehicles cannot progress past the boss location until the boss is defeated or removed, creating a forced combat/encounter checkpoint.
