# la_iglesia_de_los_olvidados

• Octagonal [[temple]] at the center of the [[graveyard]] where [[Asojano]] performs his ritual.
• Contains seven shrines (one per wall), an alcove shrine pile, and the forming "Sun of Reawakening".
• Entry point for the party's temporary safety after the exterior gauntlet.
