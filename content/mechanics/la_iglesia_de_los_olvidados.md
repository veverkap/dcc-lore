# La Iglesia de los Olvidados
Octagonal temple at the center of the graveyard where [[Asojano]] stands and performs the shrine ritual. Contains seven damaged shrines and the Sun of Reawakening; party enters here to repair shrines and complete quests.

# La Iglesia de los Olvidados
Octagonal temple at the center of the graveyard where [[Asojano]] performs his ritual. Interior contains seven shrines (one per wall), an alcove shrine pile, and the forming "Sun of Reawakening." Entry point for the party’s temporary safety after the exterior gauntlet.
