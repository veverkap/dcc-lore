# eleventh_floor

• The [[dungeon]] floor where [[Odette]] lost her legs to a [[Fiend Scythe]], an event central to her backstory and subsequent prosthetic usage.
• The [[target]] destination the party needs to reach to gain season bonuses and potential guild access, opened by the [[Key (mote of light)]] forming from the [[Puzzle Disk]] after [[Dodola]]'s death.
• The floor at whose start Porthus is making his [[deal]] choice, outreach guilds generally do not open until just before collapse on this floor, and learning the floor description here would lower the value of the offered deals.
• The [[dungeon]] floor about to begin during the interview, linked to the second [[stage]] of the [[Nine-tier attack]] and the broadcast takeover.
• A higher [[dungeon]] floor referenced as the [[target]] goal for future progression, tied to speculation about class-related mercenary accessories for [[Donut]].
• The [[arena]] destination for summoned stand-ins promised as a reward for completing the quest to reunite [[Corcunda]]'s halves.
• One of the floors where gods lose [[invulnerability]] during the Celestial [[Ascendency]], providing context for lore about god vulnerability.
• The desired destination opened by the [[Key Mote]] and [[stairwell]] [[portal]], reaching it would grant bonuses used for residency and guild access, including the [[Outreach Guild]] spawning mentioned in the chapter.
• The current [[dungeon]] floor where [[Scolopendra]] ([[Dungeon]] boss) and [[Krakaren Prime]] (Floor boss) are simultaneously present and where the chapter's boss battle occurs, containing the massive tree and central [[arena]] described.
• The floor during which [[Porthus]] takes his outreach guild [[deal]], in the chapter it is the current floor under negotiation, menus and enhancements are inaccessible inside certain meeting zones (e.g., [[Zero Zone]]), outreach guilds for the [[eleventh floor]] open shortly before collapse, and choosing options here determines indenture assignments and potential release seasons.
• The destination floor the party aims to reach, successful entry grants significant season bonus rewards and access to guilds like the [[outreach guild]], Odette's targeted floor for freedom.
• The upcoming [[dungeon]] floor whose start is identified as triggering the second phase of the current crisis, referenced as a direct catalyst for the unfolding [[A Parade of Horribles]].
• A higher [[dungeon]] floor referenced as the prize destination, and a strategic objective in the group's planning.
• The specific [[dungeon]] floor where [[Quasar]] and [[Kiki]] observe arrivals and system activity, described as having just received a chicken [[truck]] delivering people and where 203 people have made it to this floor, central to the chapter's setting.
• The current floor of the [[dungeon]], containing the parade event, combined garages, and the [[arena]] that must be cleared to open [[stairs]].
• The current [[dungeon]] floor where the parade, [[Ascendency]]-related displays, and the judgment procession occur, providing context for the chapter's events and NPC rules like those under [[Ascendency Rules]].
