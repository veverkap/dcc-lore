# arena

• The bleacher-filled space surrounding the [[Big Top]] where spectators watch the program, where [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]] interacts with [[Apollon]], and where the producers [[stage]] the drama.
• The main battleground where the fight occurs: contains the stands, entrances, vehicles, and the boss encounters with [[Emberus]], [[Scolopendra]], and [[Krakaren]].
• The battle setting where the kaiju, bosses (including [[Krakaren]] and [[Scolopendra]]), vehicles, and party members clash. Contains stands, entrances, and scattered wreckage.
• The central combat zone where the battle and subsequent collapse preparations take place; contains [[portals]]/[[stairwells]], wrecked vehicles like the [[Abrams tank]], and the coalescing goo of [[Scolopendra]].
• The end point of the parade route where crawlers will enter to fight monsters (stand-ins from the tenth floor) and a floor boss; defeating the boss opens the [[stairwell]].
• The huge arena visible at the end of the [[road]] that the procession is headed toward; mentioned by [[Donut]] while surveying the long line of masked observers.
• The larger contest/viewing complex for the floor's climax; contains the jumbotron, [[Judging Stand]], and the crowds where the parade culminates and the Eulogist-related forms are hinted to be present.
• The massive battle zone with a towering white tree at its center, filled with thousands of mobs, bleachers of observers, and the site of Carl's revolt.
