# Dimensional inventory and AI cataloging system
Seasonal dungeon system that replaces the slot-based inventory with effectively unlimited dimensional storage. Rules noted: items that can be physically lifted for ~4 seconds may be stored; living creatures cannot be stored (they die); storage is time-locked so food doesn't spoil. Interacts with the player's [[Hotlist]] and inventory UI.
