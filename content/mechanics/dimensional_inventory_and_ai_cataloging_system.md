# dimensional_inventory_and_ai_cataloging_system

• Seasonal [[dungeon]] system replaces slot-based inventory with unlimited dimensional [[storage]].
• Items that can be physically lifted for ~4 seconds can be stored.
• Living creatures cannot be stored (they die).
• [[Storage]] is time-locked, preventing food from spoiling.
• Interacts with the player's [[Hotlist]] and inventory UI.
