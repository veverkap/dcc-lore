# pole_position_chamber

• Starting chamber/pod area on the seventh floor where crawlers are held in clear glass rooms before being released into the maze.
• Contains the timer and the [[platform]]/doors that open into the race [[tunnels]].
• Named chamber on the Seventh Floor where the party's [[crawler]] pod starts.
• Glass-walled "fish tank" pod architecture and the site of the race start countdown and system announcements.
