# Ceremony of Chaos
Eris-specific ritual that summons mortal witnesses to a god ceremony and places them in the [[Witness State]] (invulnerable and immobile) so the goddess can claim the Ascendent throne only when no active mortals are present. Can be cast only once by Eris and can have its invulnerability toggled by the goddess.
