# ceremony_of_chaos

• Eris-specific ritual that summons mortal witnesses to a god ceremony and places them in the [[Witness State]] (invulnerable and immobile) to claim the Ascendent throne when no active mortals are present.
• Can be cast only once by Eris and can have its [[invulnerability]] toggled by the goddess.
