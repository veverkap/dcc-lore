# Soul Crystal Chain Reaction
An observed dungeon mechanic: killing certain large mobs or province bosses can cause nearby [[Soul Crystal]]s to pop or shut down, potentially triggering area-wide effects and allowing groups to progress when crystals are destroyed simultaneously.
