# Safe Room Rules
Explicit dungeon rules governing safe areas: no violence or stealing allowed; three strikes penalty system (first strike: ~100 sec freeze and lecture; second: ~1 hour freeze; third: strip gear and teleport into a mob nest). Mobs teleport away when they attack in safe rooms; sleeping crawlers appear translucent.
