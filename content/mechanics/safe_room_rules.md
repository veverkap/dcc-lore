# safe_room_rules

• No violence or stealing allowed in safe areas.
• Three strikes penalty system: first strike (~100 sec [[freeze]] and lecture), second strike (~1 hour [[freeze]]), third strike (strip gear and [[teleport]] into a mob [[nest]]).
• Mobs [[teleport]] away when they attack in safe rooms.
• Sleeping crawlers appear translucent.
