# Lootable Corpse (system message)
A system announcement label that appears when inspecting bodies (example: "Lootable Corpse. Former Magistrate Featherfall. Dark Cleric. Level 32."). Used by the system to denote corpses that can be interacted with/looted and to show class/level metadata.
