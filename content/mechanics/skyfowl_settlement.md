# skyfowl_settlement

• A distant [[town]] destination roughly thirty kilometers from the party's current location.
• Destination for [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]] after receiving warnings about a regional battle event.
• A medium-sized [[town]] populated in part by Skyfowl NPCs (eagle-headed humanoids).
• [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]] and [[Donut]] stop to visit a tutorial guild and observe local life.
• The [[One-eyed Narwhal]] is located here.
• Used as a regional reference for meeting and travel within the chapter.
