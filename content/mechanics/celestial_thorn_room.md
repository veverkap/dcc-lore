# celestial_thorn_room

• A rare room type that forces the party into a moral and mechanical choice: seven broken shrines and one Sun of Reawakening; repairing a single shrine triggers divergent outcomes for gods, party boons, and the floor's fate.
• Presents seven shrines and forces a single choice with mixed pros and cons; announces rewards and consequences and triggers a major decision point for the party.
