# Celestial Thorn Room
The specific thorn-room variant discovered during this chapter. A rare room type that forces the party into a moral and mechanical choice: seven broken shrines and one Sun of Reawakening; repairing a single shrine triggers divergent outcomes for gods, party boons, and the floor’s fate.
