# system_message_a_deity_has_fallen

• A deity has fallen, declaring the damnation of the deity to the [[Nothing]] and warning that the heavens are enraged.
• The announcement is triggered by the death of [[Yarilo]], [[Emberus]], or [[Dodola]].
• The system announcement line is "A deity has fallen. The heavens tremble with rage."
• The announcement signals the in-world consequences and prestige of slaying a god, and may accompany rewards such as level jumps and [[achievements]].
• The benefit from [[The Scavenger’s Daughter]] fades when a deity falls.
