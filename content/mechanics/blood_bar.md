# Blood Bar
A new status bar introduced by the system announcement governing saferoom access for the remainder of the floor. The [[Blood Bar]] fills by killing enemies; when full it grants limited safe time, and if it hits zero while in a saferoom the player is randomly teleported outside (and cannot enter saferooms if empty).
