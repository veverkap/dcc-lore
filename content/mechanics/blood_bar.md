# blood_bar

• The [[Blood Bar]] fills by killing enemies and grants limited safe time when full; if it hits zero while in a [[saferoom]], the player is randomly teleported outside.
• The Blood [[Bar]] determines access to safe areas and must be filled for ejected hunters to reenter saferooms.
• The [[Blood Bar]] is a measurable patron resource/entry requirement for the [[desperado club]] and other clubs; patrons must top off their blood bars to gain entry.
• The Blood [[Bar]] is a [[crawler]]-specific resource that must be refilled through combat or other means.
• The Blood [[Bar]] governs [[saferoom]] access: when full, players get a timed allowance; if empty, players cannot enter saferooms.
• The Blood [[Bar]] is retroactive and enforces active participation on the floor.
• Empty Blood Bars prevent access to saferooms and require refilling.
• The Blood [[Bar]] can be refilled by killing creatures and is announced via system warning at safe-room entry.
