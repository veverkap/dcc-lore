# reaver_castle

* [[Stronghold]] of the [[Reavers]] from which missiles and [[air]] defenses originate and which [[Party Planner]] skirts during the mission.
* A castle targeted for raids by allied forces (e.g., [[Boomer]] initiating barrages).
* Heavily trapped [[target]] fortress roughly five kilometers away from the party's landing point, home to the Reaver defenders and the Dream throne room; central objective for Colonel [[Colonel Boomer]]'s assault.
* Enemy/neutral [[stronghold]] targeted by allied operations (bomb runs by [[Louis]]); referenced as part of the broader multi-castle engagement.
* Enemy [[stronghold]] that receives incoming combatants and is the location where some players (like [[Louis]]) are about to [[land]] and face immediate threat and tracking by Reaver security.
* Flat castle where [[Louis]] had landed and was running while collecting unstable items during the battle.
* Location where [[Louis]] is trapped and being stalked by robot dog automatons; [[Samantha]] is searching this complex in an attempt to rescue him.
* Enemy castle held by the [[Reavers]] that the allied teams plan to assault once the [[college]] and synergy [[towers]] are ready; currently guarded by robots and possibly empty of Warlord Fang.
* [[Target]] location in [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]]'s plan where the party intends to go rescue [[Louis]] and seize another throne room after stabilizing current fronts.
* Location Carl and others plan to assault to rescue [[Louis]]; described as having changing walls and robot dogs controlling it.
* Ruined, box-liked fortress the team approaches to rescue [[Louis]], noted for honeycomb-lined interior corridors that shift and open passages to the throne room.
* Damaged [[stronghold]] and primary battleground in the chapter, featuring shifting honeycomb walls, a throne room reachable via a top-center honeycomb, and housed Reaver defenses like sentry dogs and bosses including [[Warlord Fang of the Reavers]].
* Reaver-controlled fortress proposed as the place to finish a brain-implant procedure in [[Epitome Tagg]]'s plan; referenced as part of cross-faction logistics.
