# demon_eviction_event

• An ongoing global system event that forcibly ejects demons from shells, causing freed demons to return to realms like [[Sheol]] and leaving behind dangerous consequences on the surface.
• Represents a coordinated system- driven purge affecting multiple zones and engagements, with a system notification: "A demon eviction event is occurring over multiple locations on the [[battlefield]]."
• A global/system event active during the chapter that forces demons out of weak-minded shells and back toward Sheol, central escalation mechanic causing zombies to be transformed into full demons.
• A system-driven event triggered when the AI treated crab sperm as a mob and many crabs "died," breaking Sheol, forcing a large-soul requirement for [[Amayon]]'s return and dictating unusual quest rules.
