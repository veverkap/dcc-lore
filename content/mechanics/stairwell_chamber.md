# stairwell_chamber

• Enclosed [[stairwell]] chamber mechanic: positioning directly under an inaccessible [[stairwell]] when the [[portal]] opens causes the chamber walls to [[teleport]] away, allowing crawlers to be pulled up into the next floor's [[stairwell]].
