# wasteland

Here is the consolidated markdown list:

• A floating region above the bowl that serves as a staging area for aircraft and other vehicles, and is the source of [[drop bear]] deployments.
• The hostile region called the [[Wasteland]] that lies over the water [[quadrant]], referenced as a continuous threat beyond the [[town]]'s immediate defenses.
• A red-glowing zone at the edge of the bubble visible from the party's dune vantage, a game-world environmental feature that shifts position with the sun.

• A hazardous zone/chunk that broke apart and rained debris, including [[Live Ammo Ball]]s, down onto the [[battlefield]], and its destruction triggers ongoing effects and NPC displacements.
