# warning_your_oxygen_levels_are_low

• An in-game/system notification that appears during the burial/collapse sequence, alerting the player to dwindling oxygen, critical to the party's decision to use [[scroll of water breathing]]s and other survival consumables.
• System announcement displayed during the underwater sequence, alerting players of diminishing breath and prompting the use of water-breathing scrolls and escape decisions.
• System announcement issued to players in the submerged environment (displayed during the [[Akula]]/Lusca sequence) alerting characters to dwindling oxygen and prompting use of scrolls or escape.
