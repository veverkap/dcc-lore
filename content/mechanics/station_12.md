# station_12

* [[Station 12]] is filled with [[jikininki janitor ghoul]] and part of the [[ghoul]]-infested corridor.
* Station 12 has a [[ghoul]] generator and is referenced as a comparison to the hazard expected near [[Station 72]].
* Teams planned to attempt to kill [[ghoul]] generators at Station 12 as part of coordinated defense efforts.
* Station 12 is too dangerous for most groups to attempt at this time.
* One of the [[stairwell]] [[portal]] destinations listed in the system announcement, later associated with an explosion potentially tied to overloaded [[Soul Crystal]]s.
* Station 12 contains a [[Soul Crystal]] and had been blown during the chapter; specifically referenced as one of the dangerous soul-crystal stations to avoid until the coordinated plan.
* A station referenced in reports about [[ghoul]] generator positions and [[battlefield]] layout.
* A [[stairwell]] [[portal]] destination listed in the [[Iron Tangle System Announcement]] and later referenced as the site of a possible soul-crystal explosion; a potential fallback point.
* A transfer station type noted in the chapter as one of the stations that possess floating [[Soul Crystal]]s (station 12 specifically blew during events described).
* A station noted for holding staircases and as a boarding point for certain ghouls; named in [[Vernon]]'s description of crew behavior and boarding patterns.
* A station where teams planned to attempt to destroy [[ghoul]] generators.
* Rumored to have a blown generator like some other stations, cited among transit rumors.
* A station type with a soul crystal (mentioned as "station 12 is definitely the one that blew") near an employee line; site of heavy fighting and crystal-related effects.
* A station noted for containing staircases where certain ghouls board; referenced in [[Vernon]]'s description of the crew rotation.
* A candidate [[target]] for destructive action; reported to have [[jikininki janitor ghoul]] presence and potential for an old-fashioned assault.
* A station listed among those that crawlers were avoiding due to occupation/danger; not actively engaged by the present rescue groups.
* One of the [[stairwell]] [[portal]] stations listed by the system announcement as an escape destination and also a locus of explosions affecting nearby soul crystals.
* A transfer station with a [[soul crystal]] (mentioned as blown and tiny) and a burnt-out generator; one of the types of station crawlers are warned to avoid when near others.
