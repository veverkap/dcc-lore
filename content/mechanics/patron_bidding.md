# patron_bidding

• Allows patrons (sponsor groups) to bid during the level.
• Produces notifications when bidding concludes.
• Active on the [[Fourth Floor]].
• Allows external patron bids on players or events.
• Players are notified when their bidding concludes.
• Ties into floor economy and rewards.
