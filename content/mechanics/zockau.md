# zockau

Here is the consolidated markdown list:

* [[Zockau]] is a city/zone referenced as the setting for club entrances and external threats.
* It is a named metropolis/capital on the Hunting Grounds floor, containing a central park, rows of pubs, city guards, traps, and the border leading out to the [[Selva]].
* The city is near the frontier of the spreading Gehenna Brambles and is expected to be threatened by the brambles' advance.
* It is a [[town]]/zone on the current floor where brambles attacked, and later turned into a near-ghost [[town]] after teleports during the Butcher's Masquerade.
* The city is a major city on the floor and staging point, mentioned repeatedly, and its opening triggers hunters to spread outward and impacts strategic movement decisions.
* It is a [[town]]/zone referenced as the location of a mantis representative and as the place to which outside contacts were tuned.
* The city is a refuge location mentioned in the announcement where some hunters are reportedly hiding after abandoning their hunts.
* It is a [[town]]/zone on the map bordering the "noob" [[plains]] and near several creeper spawn points, a focal area for hunter activity.
* The city is a [[target]]/location that was raided earlier in the campaign and mentioned as a turning point that changed expectations about the season's profitability.
* It is a named settlement referenced by the System as home to some crawlers/hunters, used rhetorically in the world quest announcement to urge participants into action.
* The city is a [[town]]/zone referenced as the location of a [[saferoom]]/zone where some hunters are hiding, and as the place to which outside contacts were tuned.
* It is a [[town]]/zone near which the party currently travels, and the Gehenna Brambles are likely just reaching its borders.
* The city is a [[town]]/zone undergoing bramble encroachment and the site of coordinated hunter raids, earlier saw the brief summoning of [[Emberus]] and subsequent legal and social fallout.
