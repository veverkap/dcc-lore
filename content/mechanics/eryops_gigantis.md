# Eryops Gigantis
Species/mob class: large, bottom-dwelling amphibian that can shoot lightning and root in place. Notoriously difficult to move or kill; lore notes that inviting one into your home means it never leaves. Associated aliases: River Squatter, Mud Kato. Related mechanics include a rooted constitution bonus and lizard-class interactions.
