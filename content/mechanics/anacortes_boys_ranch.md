# Anacortes Boys' Ranch
The institutional home where [[dungeon crawler carl]] lived from roughly age fifteen to eighteen. The chapter describes dorm photos, a sparse three-page report on [[dungeon crawler carl]], chore assignments (kitchen work, rewiring, cleaning), and weekly communal stew nights tied to [[Milk's Cookbook]] recipes.

# Anacortes Boys' Ranch
Residential home for teenage boys where [[dungeon crawler carl]] lived from about age fifteen until eighteen. Records include photos and a three-page report; the facility provided food, shelter, chores such as kitchen work or maintenance, and a high school equivalency credential for residents.
