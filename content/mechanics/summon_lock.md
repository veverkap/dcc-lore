# summon_lock

• A mystic-card-derived system effect announces a 30-second countdown during combat, preventing summoning by placing ethereal chains on a deckmaster's cards.
• An announcer/system effect places an ethereal summon lock on a player's cards, visible as a floating lock with a countdown, preventing summoning for the lock's duration.
• Used by [[Der Schachmeister]] in the chapter.
