# conductors_quarters

• A private room within car five of the Yellow Line [[train]] where [[Vernon]] sits, stores personal items, and is a locus for gathering lore about the [[train]] loop.
• Located in the employee break car (Car 5) and separated from the engine, maintained as an employees-only space.
• Contains personal items such as drawings, coins, moonshine, and gold.
• Where the party interrogates the conductor about the loop and stations.
