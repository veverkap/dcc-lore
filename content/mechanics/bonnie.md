# Bonnie
A juvenile dirigible gnome (Level 5) who runs a lemonade stand and is the daughter of [[Commandant Kane]]. Obsessed with her pet [[Sausage]], spoiled, and apparently under a spell that dilates her pupils. She lives in a floating house (the setting of the [[Borough Boss]] encounter with [[Denise]]) and is central to the chapter's events.
