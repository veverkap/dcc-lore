# abyss_station_-_436

• The station where Carl stops to investigate, marked by the sign "[[Abyss]] Station - 436", features a steep [[stairwell]] leading up into the catwalks and the massive pit called the [[Abyss]].
• The [[platform]] serves as the access point to the catwalks and [[portals]] that feed the flaming pit below.
• The station contains a bare [[platform]] with a steep [[stairwell]] leading up into the infrastructure above the [[Abyss]] and to catwalks overlooking the massive pit filled with crashed [[train]] cars.
