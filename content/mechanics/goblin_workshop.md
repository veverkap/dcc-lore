# goblin_workshop

• [[Goblin Workshop]] is the large engineering room filled with a massive steam engine, engineers, tools, parked [[Murder Dozers]], and barrels of explosive material.
• The room contains tools, coils of wick, explosives, a giant [[Steam Engine]], barrels marked "XXX", stacks of coal, and a cart used to [[transport]] explosives into the [[Boss Room]].
