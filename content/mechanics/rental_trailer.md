# [[Rental Trailer]]
A small, windowless rented production trailer provided by [[Senegal Production Systems, Unlimited]] where [[dungeon crawler carl]] and [[Donut]] are transferred for their interview. The interior is cramped, plastic-ceilinged, smells of salt water, and contains two chairs and a ceiling-mounted AI ([[Mexx-55]]).
