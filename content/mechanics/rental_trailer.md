# rental_trailer

• A small, windowless rented production [[trailer]] provided by [[Senegal Production Systems, Unlimited]].
• The [[trailer]] is cramped, with a plastic ceiling, and smells of salt water.
• It contains two chairs and a ceiling-mounted AI, [[Mexx-55]].
• [[Dungeon [[Crawler]] Carl]] and [[Donut]] are transferred to the [[trailer]] for an interview.
