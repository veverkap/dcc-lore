# [[Saferoom & Stairwell Rule]]
Dungeon system rule displayed on station maps: "Saferooms appear at all transfer stations. Stairwells appear at stations numbered 12, 24, 36, 48, and 72 on any colored line." Governs the distribution of saferooms and stairwells on the floor and affects route planning.
