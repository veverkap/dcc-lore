# baptism_by_fire

• Grants the rank "Devotee" to [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]].
• [[Upgrades]] burn immunity to environmental [[heat]] immunity.
• Provides the daily [[Self-Immolation]] spell (level-10, once daily).
• Increases tithe percentage from 5% to 10%.
