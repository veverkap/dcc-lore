# Baptism by Fire
A system/achievement notification from the [[temple of emberus]] granting [[dungeon crawler carl]] the rank "Devotee." Effects include upgraded burn immunity (environmental heat immunity), access to the [[Self-Immolation]] spell (level-10, once daily), and increased tithe percentage.

# Baptism by Fire
System shrine/system-announcement awarding [[dungeon crawler carl]] increased rank ("Devotee") with the god Emberus. Grants upgraded burn immunity (environmental heat immunity), the daily [[Self-Immolation]] spell, and increases the tithe from 5% to 10%. Labeled as the highest obtainable rank for non-Clerics/Paladins.
