# protection_seal_butchers_masquerade

• The Protection [[Seal]] is invoked by Apito (Oak Mother) and cast by Queen Imogen of the High Elves, creating a no-violence mandate for the Butcher's Masquerade.
• Breaking the [[seal]] results in immediate banishment to The [[Nothing]] and prevents guests from leaving until the ritual concludes or is broken.
