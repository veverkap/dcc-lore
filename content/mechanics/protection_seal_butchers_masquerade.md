# [[Protection Seal (Butcher's Masquerade)]]
The celestial ritual invoked by [[Apito (Oak Mother)]] and cast by [[queen imogen of the high elves]] that creates a no‑violence mandate for the masquerade. Breaking the seal results in immediate banishment to [[The Nothing]] and prevents guests from leaving until the ritual concludes or is broken.
