# [[Unknown Male Voice]]
An internal opposing voice that speaks to [[dungeon crawler carl]] during the possession by [[Shi Maria]]. Presents as a male presence telling [[dungeon crawler carl]] to hold on and resisting [[Shi Maria]]'s dominance. Identity remains unnamed in the chapter.
