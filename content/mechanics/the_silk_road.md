# The Silk Road
A long, mostly-empty market room on the current floor with a single row of stalls and room for additional rows; mirrored on the next floor. Hosts booths such as [[Hobgobs and Boom Sticks]] and other merchants selling items like [[Spider Shield]]s and various explosives.
