# scolopendras_nine-tier_attacks

• Scolopendra's nine-tier attacks are a floor/boss attack mechanic that releases a sequence of nine-area effects.
• The mechanic includes a visible system wheel and multiple distinct attack outcomes that can affect safe rooms and mounts.
