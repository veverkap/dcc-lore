# Emergency Action Item (AI decision)
A special Warlord/Faction mechanic allowing limited (three) proposals that bypass regular votes and are auto-decided by AI. [[dungeon crawler carl]] successfully used one to send pending viewer-facing votes to the audience for rapid resolution.
