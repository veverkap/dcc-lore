# The Ladies Love It
A loud, mid-sized pub on the same block in [[Zockau]] as [[desperado club]] and [[The Scuttlebutt]], notable for music and crowd noise. One of the row of seven pubs noted in the chapter.

# [[The Ladies Love It]]
A large pub adjacent to [[desperado club]] in [[Zockau]] described by its wooden sign depicting a grinning bald man. One of several bars on the street that house hunters and contain entrances to personal spaces.
