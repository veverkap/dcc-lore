# the_ladies_love_it

• A loud, mid-sized [[pub]] on the same block as [[Zockau]], [[desperado club]], and [[The Scuttlebutt]], notable for music and crowd noise.
• Located adjacent to [[desperado club]] in [[Zockau]], with a wooden sign depicting a grinning bald man.
• One of several bars on the street that house hunters and contain entrances to personal spaces.
