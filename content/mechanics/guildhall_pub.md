# Guildhall Pub
Attached pub to the [[Guildhall]] described as empty, with unlit torches and hockey paraphernalia. Scene of the encounter with [[Prudence]], her cubs ([[Randy]] and [[Todd]]), and discovery of the [[Glamoured Fragment – Ursine Claw (Left). Level 5.]].
