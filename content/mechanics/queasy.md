# [[Queasy]]
Debuff caused by the [[Red Cornet]]'s auditory/echolocation attack. Temporarily incapacitates affected anatomies (nausea/vomiting, double vision, immobility). Not affected by earplugs; natural resistance builds with exposure.

# Queasy
A debuff inflicted by the cornets' aural/echolocation attack that causes nausea, double vision, and vomiting. It functions as a physical (non-magical) incapacitating effect that affects certain anatomies and can be trained off by repeated exposure.
