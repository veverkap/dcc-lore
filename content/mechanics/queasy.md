# queasy

• Debuff caused by the [[Red Cornet]]'s auditory/echolocation attack, temporarily incapacitating affected anatomies (nausea/vomiting, double vision, immobility).
• Not affected by earplugs; natural resistance builds with exposure.
• Causes nausea, vomiting, temporary incapacitation, and ringing ears.
• Affects certain anatomies.
• Can be trained off by repeated exposure.
