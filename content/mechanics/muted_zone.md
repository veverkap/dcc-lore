# muted_zone

• Spells from the player's menu and scrolls will not cast while inside a muted area.
• Magical items, potions, and pre-cast spells still function.
• Items already cast or non-spell actions remain usable.
• [[Mordecai]] and the bone-room are referenced as examples.
