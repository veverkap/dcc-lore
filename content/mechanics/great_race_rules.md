# great_race_rules

• Pods open in order.
• [[Tunnels]] contain nodes and branches.
• No backtracking.
• Zero tubes only once.
• Path-closing after usage.
• Pitstop saferooms exist.
• Path dangers/requirements (math/puzzles) are displayed.
