# Great Race Rules
The explicit system-announced rules for the seventh-floor race/maze: pods open in order, tunnels contain nodes and branches, no backtracking, zero tubes only once, path-closing after usage, pitstop saferooms exist, and path dangers/requirements (math/puzzles) are displayed.
