# trainyard

Here is the consolidated list of unique factual points:

* The trainyard is a staging area where the party positions the [[Nightmare]] and [[portal cart]] before sending the [[train]] into the [[abyss]].
* The trainyard includes the edge where the [[train]] departs.
* The trainyard is also where allies say goodbye and where the [[train]] launches into the [[abyss]].
* The trainyard is a facility where the [[train]] is prepared and launched toward the [[portal]] and [[Abyss]].
* The trainyard contains engine cars and [[ghoul]]-like creatures inside the fence, with dwarves waiting outside.
* The trainyard is a peripheral location dotted along the railway exterior, referenced as a source of wrath ghouls and an escape [[target]] for those using [[portal]] hats.
* The trainyard is an exterior network of yard hubs distributed around the railway's spirograph-like pattern, common escape destinations from [[Escape Hats]] and focal points for [[ghoul]] flows.
* The trainyard is an exterior node around the railway spirograph where trains and larger mobs congregate, a destination of [[portal]] escapes from the pit and source of some [[ghoul]] spawns.
* The trainyard is a large exterior/auxiliary zone visible through the [[portal]] and on screenshots, populated by zombies and multiple tracks, with the [[portal]] connecting the subway tracks into this yard's root system.
* The trainyard is a burned-out trainyard visible in [[portal]] scans, containing dangerous mobs like [[Wrath Ghouls]] and representing one of the possible destinations for interdiction scoops.
* The trainyard is a specific trainyard instance where a cart playing "Physical" by Olivia Newton-John appeared and got routed into a dead-end parking spot.
* The trainyard is another trainyard destination connected to a [[portal]], used as an intended safe destination for some crawlers.
* The trainyard is a specific trainyard identifier that one rapid-response cart is tuned to, with Carl able to return to the [[Nightmare]] from this yard and keep one cart tuned to it as an escape option.
* The trainyard is a large enclosed floor chamber with named engines, [[portals]], and wreckage, the site of the confrontation between [[Grull]] and the [[mimic]] and where key events ([[portals]], the [[portal]] cart, and rescue attempts) occur.
* The trainyard is a massive cavernous trainyard containing the wrecked fence, administrative building, multiple tracks, the massive [[Mimic]], and hordes of ghouls and minions.
* The trainyard is a specific trainyard referenced as the arrival point for [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]]'s team when they entered the [[dungeon]] space, used as a landmark for finding personal space [[portals]] and access points.
* The trainyard is a trainyard destination used repeatedly as a [[teleport]] sink for crawlers and mobs.
* The trainyard is a massive cavernous trainyard region the party must cross to reach the employee line, populated with corpses, ghouls, the giant [[Mimic]], tracks, and multiple cave entrances.
* The trainyard is a specific entry point mentioned as where [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]] and company expected to enter the world, used as a navigational reference to reach combined spaces via substations.
* The trainyard is a destination trainyard where several groups and an entire [[station mimics]] were accidentally teleported, central to rescue logistics and subsequent danger.
* The trainyard is a yard/station (also called station E) containing the [[mimic]] and referenced by [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]] as an area squeezed by monsters and [[mimic]] attacks.
* The trainyard is a named combat zone where the chapter takes place, with major events including the public "Death Challenge!" between [[Grull]] and [[mimic]], the arrival of the [[portal]] cart and [[Nightmare]], and movement of several crawlers and NPCs (e.g., [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]], [[Donut]], [[elle mcgib]]).
* The trainyard is a yard destination reached by [[Imani]] and crew that contained a breached [[gate]] and a large [[ghoul]] presence, prompting [[shelter]] in the nearby [[iron tangle]].
* The trainyard is a yard reached by [[Imani]] and the [[meadowlark]] group that contained breached gates and swarming ghouls, adjacent to the [[iron tangle]].
* The trainyard is a specific trainyard that one of the carts had been tuned to, mentioned in the sequence explaining how carts rode the tracks and interacted with [[portals]].
