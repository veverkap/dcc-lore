# 30-kilometer_downhill_racetrack

• 30-kilometer downhill [[racetrack]]: a single-path, wide asphalt-paved track with a steep decline spanning 30 kilometers.
• Waves of generated mobs will be featured along the route.
• The race has a 20-minute time limit.
