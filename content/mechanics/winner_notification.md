# winner_notification

• A system announcement indicating the fight's conclusion and the [[winner]](s); appears immediately after the boss is defeated and freezes the world momentarily.
• System message appearing after a combat conclusion that marks victory and grants post-battle status (displayed when [[gore-gore the mantaur]] is defeated).
• Post-combat system announcement that celebrates the party's victory over [[Ruckus]] and indicates the end of the encounter.
• Achievement/notification mechanic displayed after [[Ruckus]] is defeated, signaling victory and accompanying bubble/world events.
