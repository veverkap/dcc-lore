# ochre_line

* The Ochre Line is a colored transit line in the network that intersects multiple major stops, including Station 149.
* It is the site of new mobs, unique third-rail hazards that destroy Clockwork Mongo constructs, and part of the team's routing strategy.
* The line is color-coded, with ochre keys only opening ochre trains.
* [[gore-ore the mantaur]] is identified as an Ochre Line [[Train]] Engineer.
* The line's trains contain unfamiliar mobs and the environment includes the problematic Third Rail.
* The full neighborhood map of the Ochre Line shows stops from station 11 to 435 and positions of trains, conductors, engineers, and monsters.
* The line is used for grinding and testing derailing techniques.
* The Ochre Line is a major transit line and primary location for the chapter's events.
* It intersects with other lines like the Purple line and Mauve line.
* The line is referenced in the chapter, with [[gore-ore the mantaur]] being an Ochre Line [[Train]] Engineer and keys being color-coded to specific lines.
