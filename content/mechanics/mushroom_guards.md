# mushroom_guards

• Stationary or patrol guard mobs placed by [[Mordecai]] at the approaches to [[Point Mongo]]; serve as local defensive NPCs.
• Hostile fungal mobs whose spore attacks and aggro mechanics complicated the team's assault on the [[church]].
• Stationary guard-mobs placed by [[Mordecai]] at both ends of the bridge to [[Point Mongo]]; act as sentries and are part of the [[gate]] defenses encountered by the party.
• Hostile mob creatures encountered guarding the [[church]]/[[temple]] area; their aggro and spore attacks create area hazards; casters like [[Li Na]] can incapacitate them.
