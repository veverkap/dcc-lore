# skyscraper_roof

• The combat [[arena]] setting is the [[roof]]/top floors of a collapsing skyscraper.
• The five-[[versus]]-two confrontation takes place here.
• Players must balance offense against [[Amayon]] and defense against [[Ysalte]].
