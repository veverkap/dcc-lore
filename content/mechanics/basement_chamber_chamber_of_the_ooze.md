# [[Basement Chamber (chamber of the ooze)]]
The specific basement room containing [[Mrs. Ghazi]]'s body, core under the recliner, tables, and the glowing small doorway to the level stairwell room. The collapse here traps the party and triggers the flood into the [[Water Quadrant]].
