# basement_chamber_chamber_of_the_ooze

• The [[Basement]] Chamber (Chamber of the Ooze) contains Mrs. Ghazi's body, a core under the recliner, tables, and a glowing small doorway to the level [[stairwell]] room.
• The collapse in this room traps the party and triggers the flood into the Water [[Quadrant]].
