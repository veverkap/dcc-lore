# razor_foxes

• A group of ninja-like fox enemies previously encountered in the [[cul-de-sac]], referenced as having performed a dance where [[Genesis]] and [[Rapture]] later stand.
• Small, aggressive fox-like mobs participating in the encounter; several somersault down a [[hill]] to engage the bosses.
• One razor fox is pulled into a pouch and later is the source of a regeneration event when eaten by a boss.
• A group of ninja-like fox mobs encountered earlier; their presence and behavior are referenced when [[Genesis]] and [[Rapture]] pause in the [[cul-de-sac]].
• A mob type encountered on the nearby [[hill]]: small, agile fox-like racers (one set wears black ninja suits); they engage [[Party Foul]]/[[Prison Pocket]] and suffer casualties when pulled into a boss pouch; one missing fox is later produced and eaten by [[Prison Pocket]], triggering its full heal.
