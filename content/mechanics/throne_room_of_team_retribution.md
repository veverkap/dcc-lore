# throne_room_of_team_retribution

• A named throne room occupied by forces from the [[war mages]], granting them temporary control over the city defense system.
• A contested strategic location whose control flips between [[team retribution]] and [[Princess Posse]]; system messages tie its occupation to the six-hour condition for winning [[Faction Wars]].
