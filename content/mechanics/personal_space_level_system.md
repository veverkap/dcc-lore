# personal_space_level_system

• Private rooms are governed by the Personal Space Level System, which includes levels 1–5.
• Each level has a specific size and available [[upgrades]], including crafting, [[kitchen]], and training.
• [[Upgrades]] can be combined into communal halls.
• Level thresholds affect [[manager]] room placement and shared upgrade behavior.
