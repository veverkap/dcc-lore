# large_ursine_settlement

• A well-maintained ursine [[town]] with manicured streets, shops, a row of temples (including the [[Temple of Hellik]]), and strict enforcement by [[Funeral Bells]] and Mayor [[Elmer]].
• The [[town]] has strict civic [[Town Rules]], funeral bell mushroom guards, shops, guild entrances (e.g., [[Club Vanquisher]]), and a notable [[Temple of Hellik]] at its edge.
• The settlement was originally named before being renamed [[Point Mongo]] by [[Donut]].
