# no_hand_to_hand_until_the_spiders_are_detonated

• No hand to hand until the spiders are detonated: Explicit raid rule used by Carl's team during ambushes, forbidding melee engagements until [[Spider Stalkers]] have detonated to coordinate timing and prevent friendly interference.
