# [[Negative ROI / Separation Anxiety]]
A visible debuff/system status (displayed as "Negative ROI") afflicting [[Dong Quixote]], later explained by [[Mordecai]] as the curse known as [[Separation Anxiety]]; requires the sock and all lost coins to cure.
