# Fire Ants
Trap monster spawned from a reward [[Prize Case]] (suitcase) in the [[Yellow Line Reward Room]]. Level 1 insect swarm that is literally on fire, rapidly spreads flames, and overwhelms the carriage—classified in text explicitly as a trap.
