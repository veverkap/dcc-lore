# warning_you_have_exited_the_current_playing_field_without_a_registered_pass_if_you_do_not_immediatel

• You have exited the current playing field without a registered pass.
• If you do not immediately return to the playing area, you will be considered to have left the game.
• Please see <Error>.
• An explicit system announcement is shown on [[Louis Santiago]]'s HUD when he is detected outside the registered playing area.
