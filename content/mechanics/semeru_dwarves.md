# Semeru dwarves
Dwarven group credited with building and maintaining the inverted castle at the center of [[Larracos]]; portrayed as non-combatant caretakers and pub workers.

The dwarf population native to the ninth floor who worship a single goddess ([[Ysalte]]). Mentioned as having unique cultural beliefs and rituals tied to the end-of-battle appearances at [[Larracos]].

The dwarf people of the ninth floor noted for unified worship of [[Ysalte]]. They maintain the single temple of that goddess in the dungeon and appear as a distinct cultural group in local lore and combat narratives.
