# semeru_dwarves

• Credited with building and maintaining the inverted castle at the center of [[Larracos]], portrayed as non-combatant caretakers and [[pub]] workers.
• Native to the ninth floor, worship a single goddess [[Ysalte]], and have unique cultural beliefs and rituals tied to end-of-battle appearances at [[Larracos]].
• Unified worship of [[Ysalte]], maintain the single [[temple]] of that goddess in the [[dungeon]], and appear as a distinct cultural group in local lore and combat narratives.
