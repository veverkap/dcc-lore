# entering_security_checkpoint

• A system announcement/ mechanic triggered during the transfer sequence: "Entering Security Checkpoint."
• Marks the transition into a gameplay-like screening area with [[slow]]-motion physics, scanner pat-downs, and an encounter with the [[Shade [[Gnoll]] Riot Forces]].
• Signals the start of the automated security screening sequence that places [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]] and [[Donut]] into the checkpoint chamber and triggers interactions with the [[Shade [[Gnoll]] Riot Forces]].
• Frames the moment before Carl meets a security [[gnoll]] and precedes the transfer into the [[Production Facility]].
