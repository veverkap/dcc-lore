# third_floor

Here is the consolidated list of unique factual points:

• The Third Floor is a significant location in the game, where players can select their race and class, and shop/trading mechanics unlock.
• The Third Floor is also a point of temptation, where players may be advised to remain a cat, and warnings are given about the potential consequences of changing race/classes.
• The Third Floor is a planned destination for certain parties, including [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]] and [[Brandon]].
• The Third Floor is a recurring location, referenced as part of a recurring floor pattern (third, sixth, ninth, etc.).
• The Third Floor is the current [[dungeon]] level in the chapter, featuring a golden [[hallway]], illusionary sky, and the point at which class selection and floor-specific mechanics begin.
• The Third Floor is a location where many key events occur, including character introductions, [[saferoom]] dynamics, and integration transition issues.
• The Third Floor is the origin of a past reward (the [[Celestial box]]), used to place prior events in series chronology.
• The Third Floor is a previously visited floor used as a reference for safe-foraging behavior in [[town]] and environmental expectations.
• The Third Floor is a named [[dungeon]] floor referenced for licensing rules (elite events filmed on this floor are owned by the elite's program).
• The Third Floor is a lower floor of the [[World Dungeon]] referenced as the place where [[Donut]] originally lost the [[crown of the [[sepsis]] whore]].
• The Third Floor is a prior floor of the [[dungeon]] where the party met [[Signet]] and where events like the show "Vengeance of the Daughter" occurred.
• The Third Floor is a previously referenced [[dungeon]] floor where past controversies over found [[loot]] occurred.
• The Third Floor is a location where [[Volteeg]] first chose a gargoyle form and began to develop higher cognition and strategy after the pet biscuit transformation.
• The Third Floor is a milestone referenced in the transformation rules: if an enriched biscuit is consumed after the Third Floor begins, the newly transformed pet must find a safe room and choose a class.
• The Third Floor is a referenced location in the setting, used as a comparison by [[Donut]] when recalling "city elves on the third floor".
• The Third Floor is a [[dungeon]] floor where crawlers originally enter character creation and meet their initial game guides.
• The Third Floor is a location where important character transformations occurred for [[Katia Grim]] and [[Eva]].
• The Third Floor is a previously-encountered [[dungeon]] floor referenced in passing, used for contextual history about characters' survival and progression.
• The Third Floor is a past level where undead lemurs attacked, used as a point of comparison for current creatures' behavior.
• The Third Floor is the floor where [[Donut]] originally lost the [[crown of the [[sepsis]] whore]], after which the crown regenerated and may have been awarded to another [[crawler]].
• The Third Floor is a castle level between the party's [[ballroom]] and the hunter [[ballroom]], central to tactical movement and where a soul-crystal explosion occurs that damages floors/ceilings between levels.
• The Third Floor is an earlier floor where the party first encountered [[Signet]] and where the show "Vengeance of the Daughter" originated.
• The Third Floor is a [[dungeon]] floor referenced as a milestone after which stricter rules apply to pet transformations (see [[Post-Third-Floor Pet Transformation Rule]]).
• The Third Floor is a location- level trigger for System constraints.
• The Third Floor is mentioned in passing as the location of the "city elves" who reacted to [[Donut]] in a prior event.
• The Third Floor is a [[dungeon]] level where [[Giulia DiMarco]] received a mind-restoring intervention from a [[Benevento]] after being wheeled into the [[dungeon]] with [[Luca Di]].
