# Marking mechanic (successful mark / 30s formation)
System mechanic enacted when Carl forces [[Warlord Empress D’Nadia of the Prism]] to full health: a mark is placed ("You have successfully marked...") and completes after thirty seconds, enabling further actions against the marked target.
