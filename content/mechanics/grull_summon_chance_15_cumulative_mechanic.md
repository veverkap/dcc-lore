# Grull Summon Chance (1.5% cumulative mechanic)
Combat mechanic tied to Carl's gauntlet: each successful punch has a 1.5% chance to summon [[Grull]]; the chance shown as incremental numeric feedback (e.g., "1.5", "3.0"... "10.5") and can trigger a deadly god-summon if it reaches threshold during combat.
