# Outreach Guild Offers
The explicit dungeon negotiation mechanic described in the chapter: outreach guilds present timed indenture offers with defined season lengths (e.g., "fifteen seasons", "thirty seasons", "one hundred seasons"), stipends, level adjustments (e.g., given level 50 or half current level), restrictions on items (no weapons allowed), and options to take pre-collapse or sentimental inventory items. These offers are governed by the guild rules and affect crawler fates and return prospects.
