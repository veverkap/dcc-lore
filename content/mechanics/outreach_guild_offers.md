# outreach_guild_offers

• The Outreach Guild presents timed indenture offers with defined season lengths, stipends, level adjustments, and restrictions on items.
• Offers can include options to take pre-collapse or sentimental inventory items.
• Guild rules govern these offers, affecting [[crawler]] fates and return prospects.
