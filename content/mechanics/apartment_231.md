# apartment_231

• The location of Carl's team's mandatory ring, assigned by the late rule announcement, is on the second floor of [[Apartment]] 231.
• [[Apartment]] 231 is a deceptive [[apartment]] that morphs into a domestic scene resembling the team's own building interior.
• The [[apartment]] contains an NPC tableau with Marjory Williams, Alicia, and the cat Gravy Boat.
• The [[apartment]] also has a utility room with multiple gates, including a disintegration trap and a [[teleport]] trap.
