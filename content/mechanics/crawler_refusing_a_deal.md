# [[Crawler Refusing a Deal]]
An explicit interface/system entry Chandra selects during the meeting with [[Princess Donut]]. Presented as a current status/mechanic that registers Donut's decision not to accept offers, this functions as an in-world system flag affecting how deals and media visibility proceed.
