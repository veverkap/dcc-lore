# zombie_turkey

• Reanimated turkey corpses created by the [[Worm Fulcrum]] effect, slower than living turkeys but hostile and dangerous.
• Behave like typical [[slow]] zombies and remain hostile after many living turkeys revert to NPC status.
• Spread into farm animals and created a persistent threat on the farm.
• Undead mob encountered by [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]] and [[Donut]] on the north side of the floating chunk of [[Iowa]].
• One of several zombie farm animals populating the area.
• Infected livestock (e.g., pigs) and present a lingering environmental hazard on the floor.
