# Laminak Rev-Up Consultant Elite – Level 6
Higher-tier Rev-Up mob: small, fat winged fairies (manager-class) that run filling-room operations. Immune to most health-seeping attacks and valued for their essence as a disease shield; enforce quotas and repurpose underlings into production roles.
