# Knockers
Small round-faced NPC creatures (level 10–15) wearing yellow hard hats. They are a gnome/Bopca–like race and the manufacturers behind the chapter’s illicit product [[Toddler Cocaine]]. Present in swarming numbers across the factory floor and protective of their product.
