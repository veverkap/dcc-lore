# [[Tower integrity warnings]]
System announcements that report tower/structure health (examples: "Nest integrity at 97%", "Warning: This tower’s integrity is at 74%") and offer management cues (e.g., "You may manage this tower in the Warlord menu"). They reflect active structural mechanics during siege.
