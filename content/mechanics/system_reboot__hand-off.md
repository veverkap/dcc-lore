# System reboot / hand-off
An explicit system-level process announced in the chapter ("System will reboot in ten seconds"; "the hand-off. The system is transferring") describing the transfer of control from one authority to another (mudskipper control to [[Valtay]] central control) and temporary suspension of sniffers.
