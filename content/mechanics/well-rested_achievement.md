# Well-Rested (Achievement)
Achievement triggered when a crawler sleeps exceptionally well (as Carl did in the [[Ultra-Stabilized Bed]]). Reward grants the Good Rest bonus: +10% base stats and +10% experience and skill training for 30 hours.
