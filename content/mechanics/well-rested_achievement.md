# well-rested_achievement

• Achievement triggered when a [[crawler]] sleeps exceptionally well, such as Carl in the [[Ultra-Stabilized Bed]].
• Reward grants the Good Rest bonus: +10% base stats and +10% experience and skill training for 30 hours.
