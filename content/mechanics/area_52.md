# area_52

• A logistics/load area managed by Louis, referenced in troop and equipment movement procedures.
• A woodland location where remote missile batteries are physically positioned and triggered from.
• A safe base/zone for evacuees and [[saferoom]] access, where the Party Planner heads to repair and reload.
• A docking/repair location where Louis returned and unloaded passengers before repairing the Party Planner.
• A [[teleport]] destination that would place travelers near a safe room and a doggie door exit.
