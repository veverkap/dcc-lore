# [[Inventory: Break Magical Connection]]
A system restriction message encountered when attempting to put the Wheel of Fortune into inventory: "You may put this in your inventory, but it will break the magical connection." Explicit dungeon/item mechanic limiting inventory actions to preserve enchantments and portal function.
