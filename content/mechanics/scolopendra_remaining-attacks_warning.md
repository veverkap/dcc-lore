# Scolopendra remaining-attacks warning
Explicit dungeon rule announced during the encounter: "If [[Scolopendra]] dies or is abandoned upon a collapsing floor, the remaining attacks will trigger all at once." It is a hard-coded, unavoidable system mechanic with a nine-attack sequence (the chapter shows time until attack 2 of 9).
