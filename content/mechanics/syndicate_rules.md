# syndicate_rules

• The Syndicate rules govern outcomes such as the handover of kidnapped survivors and jurisdictional decisions.
• Examples of governed outcomes include the processing of captured pirates and the determination of a guest's citizenship status.
• The Syndicate rules are cited in the interview narrative as determining a guest's fate.
