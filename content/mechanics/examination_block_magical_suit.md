# Examination Block: Magical Suit
System-style message/mechanic that prevents players from fully examining creatures wearing certain magical equipment (e.g., [[Ninja Suits (Magical)]]). In this chapter the system displays: "You are unable to examine this creature. But it's a ninja. You can see that much."
