# [[PETA Enthusiast!]]
Achievement awarded to [[dungeon crawler carl]] for removing hostility from an aggravated, non-sapient enemy (feeding the [[Danger Dingoes]]) such that the enemy fought on the player's behalf. Includes a humorous flavor reward line in its description.
