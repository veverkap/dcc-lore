# Sugar Hermits
Migrating mob creatures that swarm the track during the race. Described as female, skull-wearing, tentacled creatures that feed on brains and bones. They attach skull-like shells to their bodies and can latch onto racers and mercenaries; designated "Female Sugar Hermit. Level 11" in the narrator's assessment.
