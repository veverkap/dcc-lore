# Fortress of Unyielding Power and Authority
Primary stronghold (castle) the group enters; medieval-style layout with outer ring tall enough for larger flesh-golems, a central throne room, and linked antechamber/flag room areas. Nicknamed "FUPA" by the players and the source of system status messages.
