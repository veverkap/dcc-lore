# Tenth Floor Rules
A cited set of club/dungeon rules referenced by [[Clarabelle]] that govern entry requirements and behavior for the "tenth floor" (and its applied standards at club doors). These rules affect who may enter (e.g., blood bar requirements) and the separation of [[Crawlers]] and [[Hunters]].
