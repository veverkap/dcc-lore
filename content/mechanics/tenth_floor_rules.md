# tenth_floor_rules

• "Tenth floor rules" are explicit club/ [[dungeon]] rules that control interactions at the [[desperado club]] entrance, enforcing mechanics for entry and behavior.
• The rules govern entry requirements and behavior, affecting who may enter and separating [[Crawlers]] and [[Hunters]].
