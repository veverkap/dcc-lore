# city_defense_system

• The automated defensive mechanism of [[Larracos]] prevents simple tactics from succeeding, influencing battle outcomes.
• The City Defense system controls [[towers]] and city defenses in areas like [[Larracos]], and its status directly affects [[battlefield]] dynamics.
• The system can go inert, as mentioned in a system message ("The City Defense system has gone inert!").
