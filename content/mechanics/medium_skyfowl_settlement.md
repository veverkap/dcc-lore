# medium_skyfowl_settlement

• The Medium Skyfowl Settlement is a location where the chapter takes place.
• System ownership was briefly awarded to [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]] after the magistrate's death via the [[Platinum Tyrant's Box]].
• The settlement contains the magistrate's building, [[mall]] level, and adjacent shops, including an entrance to the [[desperado club]].
