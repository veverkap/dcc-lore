# [[Race Restriction (Equipment Use)]]
An equipment-use mechanic: certain items (notably some [[Power Armor]] and [[Guns]]) are race-restricted, preventing some crawlers from equipping them and influencing scavenging decisions in locations like [[Zockau]].
