# summoning_rules_apply

• Summoning rules apply when an external deity is temporarily summoned into a location, governing behavior, duration, and restrictions.
• Summoned beings, like [[Grull]], follow the [[dungeon]]'s summoning mechanics and restrictions.
• [[Invulnerability]] while on the floor and behavior constraints apply to temporarily summoned deities, such as [[Ogun]].
