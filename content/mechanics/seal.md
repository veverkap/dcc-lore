# Seal
An active dungeon rule in play during the masquerade; breaking the [[Seal]] is a risk that could trigger the contest. [[dungeon crawler carl]] and [[Imani]] discuss using the presence of [[Signet]] to gain extra seconds if someone tries to break the [[Seal]].
