# [[Gods as Soul Armor]]
Explicit cookbook note (from [[Crawler Tin]]) asserting that gods can act like "Soul Armor" inhabited by aliens, and that spells like [[Take That Shit Off]] and [[Laundry Day]] might remove off-world influences—an applied dungeon mechanic/hypothesis about handling deity-hosted aliens.
