# paramour_benefit

• Raises non-sapient pets' Intelligence/Wisdom
• Resets non-sapient pets to level 1
• Assigns [[crawler]] status and a [[crawler]] number to [[dungeon]]-born pets
• Enforces a physical transformation, changing the recipient's body to a subtree with a two-B structure
• Imparts a permanent, incurable charm making the recipient fall hopelessly in love with the feeder
