# Paramour benefit
An explicit dungeon-effect rule described in the chapter: when granted (via an enriched biscuit like [[Enriched Pet Biscuit: The Paramour]]), it (1) raises non-sapient pets' Intelligence/Wisdom, (2) resets them to level 1, (3) can assign crawler status and a crawler number to dungeon-born pets, (4) enforces a subtree two-B physical transformation, and (5) imparts a permanent, incurable charm making the recipient fall hopelessly in love with the feeder.
