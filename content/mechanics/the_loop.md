# the_loop

• A broader transit/location system referenced as a destination for named trains and [[portals]].
• A massive [[loop-de-loop]] structure dominating the park's lower half used by racers attempting a shortcut or stunt.
• Named engines and [[portals]] are used to return to [[The Loop]].
• [[Satan]] topples the structure during the chase, crashing it across the course and disrupting multiple teams.
