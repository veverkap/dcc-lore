# Top 10 Bounty Rule
An explicit dungeon rule in [[Dungeon Crawler World]]: killing one of the game's top 10 players yields a monetary bounty. In this chapter [[Katia]] kills [[Hekla]] (a top-2 player) and claims a 500,000 gold bounty as a result.
