# pueblo_de_los_olvidados

• The compulsory system quest triggered on entry to Pueblo de los Olvidados, which locks the party inside until they eradicate the source of corruption, awards the ability to leave and a Silver Quest Box.
• The infected [[town]] where the chapter's events occur, with its central [[temple]]/shrine being the objective to repair to free the [[town]] from the influence of Asojano and the surrounding ghommid.
• Quest location and settlement liberated when Yemaya is repaired, with the quest completion message announcing the settlement liberated and the [[deck]] reset.
• System-issued compulsory quest that locks the party into the corrupted [[town]] until they eradicate the source of the sickness, with rewards including exit, [[town]] reversion, and a Silver Quest Box.
• A Quest Update/System Announcement stating that Orisha Asojano is the source of the ghommid infection and instructing players to repair his shrine or remove him to free the [[town]].
• The quest/settlement that the party liberates by completing the thorn-room sequence, with system messages declaring the settlement liberated and ghommids cured.
