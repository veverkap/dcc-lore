# leaderboard__top-10__bounty_system

• The system-maintained ranking of top crawlers (top-10) displays names, classes, levels, and bounties.
• Ranked players are vulnerable to player hunting ("hunting season").
• Awards [[loot]] boxes and percentages of bounties on survival/collapse.
