# Leaderboard / Top-10 & Bounty System
The system-maintained ranking of top crawlers (top-10) that displays names, classes, levels, and bounties. The mechanic makes ranked players vulnerable to player hunting ("hunting season") and awards loot boxes and percentages of bounties on survival/collapse.
