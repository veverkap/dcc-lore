# the_prize_carousel

• A live segment/system event in [[Dungeon]] [[Crawler]] World that manifests as a spinning carousel of selectable prizes when a fan box outcome allows player choice, hosted by [[Chaco]] and governed by a countdown.
• Triggers a timed choice sequence and can involve system enforcement.
