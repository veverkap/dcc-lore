# The Prize Carousel
A live segment/system event in Dungeon Crawler World that manifests as a spinning carousel of selectable prizes when a fan box outcome allows player choice. Hosted in-world by [[Chaco]] and governed by a countdown; tied to fan-box prize mechanics.

# The Prize Carousel
A dungeon event/system segment and fan-box prize selection mechanic hosted by [[Chaco]] that materializes a spinning carousel of selectable prizes for the player. Triggers a timed choice sequence and can involve system enforcement (as happened with [[Mordecai]]’s [[Naughty]] time-out).
