# [[Temporary Combination With a Totem (System Warning)]]
System announcement that informs the player they have been temporarily combined with a totem and warns that death while combined will prevent regeneration. Gives a timeout (180 seconds) or ends when the battle ends. Triggering mechanic in this chapter that explains the constraints of [[Shi Maria]]'s attachment to [[dungeon crawler carl]].
