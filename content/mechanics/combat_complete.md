# combat_complete

Here is the consolidated markdown list:

* A system announcement that ends an [[arena]] encounter, resets or modifies the [[cards]], and prompts the player whether to run another match.
* System message declaring the end of the encounter and despawning summoned totems and transient effects in the simulation.
* System announcement indicating the end of the encounter and declaring the [[winner]]; appears as the [[Reminiscence [[Hydra]] of Malicious Compliance]] is defeated.
* System notification observed in-scene indicating combat resolution and that the players' "[[deck]] has been reset."
* System notification that concludes the mock battle when all combatants are resolved; causes summoned totems like [[Shi Maria]] and [[Geraldo]] to disappear.
* System announcement marking the end of a combat scenario. In the chapter it follows the failed simulation and precedes a reset of decks.
* System announcement signaling the end of the encounter and the resolution of the boss fight.
* System announcement indicating the end of the encounter (noting [[deck]] resets and declaring the fight's conclusion).
* System messages that mark the end of the thorn-room encounter, announce quest completion (e.g., [[Pueblo de los Olvidados]]), liberation of the settlement, and the reset of the [[deck]]/game-state.
* System announcement signaling the end of the encounter with [[Yago]] and the automatic reset of combat/[[deck]] mechanics. Occurs immediately after the flagging/defeat of the mob.
* System announcement that declares the end of combat and resets the enemy [[deck]] UI; observed after [[Jola (Yule Cat)]] kills [[The Visitor]].
* System announcement that ends the match state, resets summoned decks (e.g., Donut's), and allows post-combat actions like looting and movement.
* System announcement that signals the end of a battle and a reset of whatever "[[deck]]" combat mechanic the game uses.
* System announcement marking the end of an [[arena]] encounter; resets players' [[deck]] state and offers the option to retry the [[arena]].
* System announcement indicating the end of the encounter and automatic [[deck]] reset; occurs after [[miss sharp elbows]] is defeated.
* System notification indicating the conclusion of an encounter and that the party's [[deck]] or combat state has been reset; appears immediately after [[elle mcgib]]'s unexpected, massive [[Graupel]] cast.
* System announcement observed after the [[Reef Explorer]] fight indicating the end of combat and the reset of the [[deck]] mechanics for the encounter.
* System announcement signaling the end of the skirmish after entering [[La Iglesia de los Olvidados]]; includes the note "[[Deck]] has been reset" that resets certain player resources.
* System message indicating the end of nearby encounters; triggers player movement and situational updates (e.g., allowing [[Donut]] to [[land]] on [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]]'s shoulder and signaling a lull before the infiltration).
