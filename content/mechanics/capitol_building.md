# capitol_building

• The Capitol Building is a location where the party's [[saferoom]] and exit [[stairwell]] are located, with Carl surrounding the exit [[stairwell]] with traps to control access.
• It is a strategic exit location where allies are told to hold position to stop Quan Ch from escaping with the keys.
• The building is a major urban landmark 15 kilometers from the party and is used as a navigation reference by [[Dungeon]] [[Crawler]] Carl.
• The keys for a door in the Capitol Building are time-limited and central to the escape scenario that concerns Carl and Princess Donut.
• Carl deployed [[teleport]] traps in the Capitol Building, which were later triggered by Ren's team, leading to the "Lord of the Flies" achievement.
• The building is a landmark location near an exit in the current floor, and Carl asks allies to hold position by the exit in the Capitol Building to intercept Quan Ch.
• The location is also referenced as the site where Carl left [[teleport]] traps, triggering the "Lord of the Flies" achievement when another team set them off.
