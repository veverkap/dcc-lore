# all_tree

• A massive metaphysical tree that grows through multiple worlds, connecting realms and characters.
• A monumental tree whose roots host the castle of the dwarves near Larracos, part of larger regional geography tied to divine attention.
• A vast metaphysical root system discovered beneath Larracos that connects everything; the dwarves' castle and monuments were built on these roots and the discovery shaped the region's history.
• A mythic or physical root-tree referenced as the dwarves tunneled down to the gods and only stopped when they reached the roots of the All Tree.
• A deep- floor system of nodes and maps that can be revealed by the spell all tree, specifically functioning on deeper floors.
• A massive cosmological tree referenced by [[Dungeon]] [[Crawler]] Carl and tied to [[circus]] performers like Grimaldi, connecting worlds and relevant background lore.
• A monumental tree whose roots host Semeru Dwarves' castle, referenced when describing where divine attention typically concentrates around battles.
• A magic-infused, floor-spanning tree whose outcroppings protect [[forest]]-kin from magic by absorbing spells and redirecting them back to the caster, with roots running beneath the area between the disputed lands and the celestial realm, and outcroppings that regrow after being destroyed.
