# cockblock

• Achievement triggered when [[Low Thi]] fatally interrupts [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]]'s quest to free [[Wynne]] by killing the gnome.
• Achievement awarded for killing an NPC required for another [[crawler]]'s quest (Miss Quill – Skyfowl, Level 30), granting a [[Silver Quest Box]] and warning repeat offenders will be exposed to affected questers.
• In-game achievement earned by [[Low Thi]] for destroying the catacomb map and preventing the party from progressing on the [[Wynne]] rescue quest.
