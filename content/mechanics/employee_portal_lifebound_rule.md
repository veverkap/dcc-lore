# [[Employee portal lifebound rule]]
Dungeon rule described by [[Madison]]: employee portals are lifebound to the station's [[Kravyad]] — if the Kravyad dies or is incapacitated, the portal snaps closed. Explicit dungeon mechanic affecting travel options (e.g., Station 435 employee exit).

# [[Employee portal lifebound rule]]
Dungeon/system rule: employee portal functionality is explicitly lifebound to the controlling [[Kravyad]]—if the creature dies, the portal snaps closed. This imposes a tactical constraint: to use the portal exit safely, the controlling boss must remain alive.
