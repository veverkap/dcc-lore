# employee_portal_lifebound_rule

• Employee [[portals]] are lifebound to the station's [[Kravyad]]: if the Kravyad dies or is incapacitated, the [[portal]] snaps closed.
• The [[portal]]'s functionality is explicitly lifebound to the controlling [[Kravyad]]: if the creature dies, the [[portal]] snaps closed.
• This imposes a tactical constraint: to use the [[portal]] exit safely, the controlling boss must remain alive.
