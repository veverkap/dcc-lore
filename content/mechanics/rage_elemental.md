# rage_elemental

• Lethal elemental mob that can kill the offender and their entire party, described as plucked from the [[thirteenth floor]].
• Level 93, almost-indestructible smoke-and-fire monster with six legs and horned skull, capable of self-healing, gravity reversal, and requiring 666 souls to dissipate.
• Persistent, door-bashing summoned mob that screamed and smashed at the safe room door, later dissipating after claiming 666 souls and devouring a mass of grubs.
• Horned, high-level (level 93) pursuit mob encountered in the halls, using claw attacks and a [[Reverse Gravity]]-type effect, teleporting out of safe rooms when doors open, and ultimately forced down the [[stairwell]] and dissolved.
• High-level elemental encountered and damaged during the escape sequence, with its death triggering an on-screen "Experience Denied" result, suggesting special rules or traps.
• Enemy that previously cast a spell flipping hallways upside-down, invoked as precedent for inverted/rotated spatial tricks on the floor.
• Hostile elemental mob referenced as having caused Yolanda's death while defending others.
