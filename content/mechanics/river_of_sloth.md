# river_of_sloth

• The [[River]] of Sloth is a location in [[Satan's Water Park]], featuring a lazy-[[river]]-style route with a ginger base [[camp]] near its halfway point.
• The attraction/zone includes an onboarding raft area and a [[river]] filled with inner-tube [[Gingers]].
• The party must traverse this [[river]] to reach the finish line while fighting the boss [[Maurice]].
