The mantaur species/race in this area. Males are described as having a secondary torso with four arms; female variants (womantaurs) can present as four-legged or with a secondary torso and hands. Most mantaur typically worship [[Grull]], though [[Genesis]] and [[Rapture]] worship [[Enyo]].
