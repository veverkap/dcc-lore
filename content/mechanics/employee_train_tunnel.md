# employee_train_tunnel

• A suggested on-foot route between areas, offering a way up to a transfer station, used or recommended by protagonists and allies such as [[Imani]].
• A staff-access [[tunnel]] suggested by Carl as a safer route between areas/stations, referenced as an alternate path to transfer stations.
• A suggested escape route for the [[meadowlark]] survivors to reach the nearest transfer station on foot after encountering ghouls.
