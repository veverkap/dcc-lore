# Throne Room Occupation (6-hour victory condition)
A system announcement mechanic stating that occupation of a throne room (e.g., the [[Throne Room of the Madness]] or the [[Throne Room of Team Retribution]]) by a faction (such as the [[Princess Posse]] or [[war mages]]) for six hours will declare that faction the season winner.
