# throne_room_occupation_6-hour_victory_condition

• Occupation of a throne room (e.g., the [[Throne Room of the Madness]] or the [[Throne Room of Team Retribution]]) by a faction (such as the [[Princess Posse]] or [[war mages]]) for six hours declares that faction the season [[winner]].
