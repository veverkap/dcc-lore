# car_15

• Mysterious, non-passenger car on the [[train]] initially labeled with a question mark, later revealed as the [[Janitor's Lair]] location, containing corpses, broom/dust buckets, and hosting the first encountered [[Jikininki]] pair.
• Inhabited by [[Jikininki]] and a pile of bones, brooms, and dust buckets.
• Accessible via gangways and doors, a focal point for exploration and combat as the [[train]] stopped at [[Sirin Station (81)]].
