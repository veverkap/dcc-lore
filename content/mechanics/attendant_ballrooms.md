# attendant_ballrooms

• Items placed on attendant stages can be interacted with from the corresponding main [[stage]] (subject to on-[[stage]] limitations).
• The two servant/attendant ballrooms mirror [[stage]] and item interactions with the main ballrooms.
• Key to staging because items placed on attendant stages can be interacted with by performers on main stages (subject to overlay rules).
