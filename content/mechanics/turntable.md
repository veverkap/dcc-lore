# turntable

• A baseball-field-sized circular rail [[platform]] that rotates to align trains with different service bays.
• Operated from a nearby watchtower.
• Central to the party's escape plan from [[Service Bay 32]].
• Controlled from a nearby [[tower]] (accessed by a chain).
