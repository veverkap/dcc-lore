# No Magic
A system rule stamped onto the party during the [[Borough Boss]] encounter: offensive and defensive magic (spells, scrolls) are disabled against the boss while the rule is active (a signature of [[Denise]]’s anti-magic environmental effect).

# No Magic
A mechanic stamped onto player portraits at the start of the boss encounter indicating that offensive and defensive magic cannot be used against the boss. In this chapter it disables spells, scrolls, and some gear effects during the [[Denise]] fight.
