# mimic_mass-shedding_rule

• If more than 50% of a large [[mimic]]'s mass is removed from the main body at once, the main body can no longer heal or transform, becoming vulnerable.
• Separated pieces will grow legs and attack; they only die when the main [[mimic]] dies.
