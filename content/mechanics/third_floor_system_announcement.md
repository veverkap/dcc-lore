# Third Floor System Announcement
The in-game broadcast welcoming crawlers to the [[Third Floor]] (e.g., "Welcome, again, Crawlers to the third floor!"), detailing rules, sponsorship information, and warnings about violence toward non-combatant NPCs—an active system announcement affecting player behavior.
