# Post-Third-Floor Pet Transformation Rule
Dungeon rule stated in the Enriched Pet Biscuit description: if an Enriched Pet Biscuit is consumed after the third floor begins, the transformed former pet is required to find a [[Safe Room]] and choose a class. This is an explicit dungeon constraint affecting survivability and legality of transformations.
