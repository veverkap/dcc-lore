# subterranean_quadrant

• The [[Subterranean]] [[Quadrant]] is a location beneath the party, containing tomb-like [[tunnels]] and traps, with forcefield-like wards blocking access.
• The [[quadrant]] became flooded due to prior sequencing, with survivors holed up in safe rooms at the top, relevant to drainage and pump mechanics.
• The [[Subterranean]] [[Quadrant]] is one of the four quadrants of the bubble/[[necropolis]], its liberation a pivotal event triggered by completing objectives, including destroying the guardian of [[Anser's Tomb]].
• The [[quadrant]] is flooded, with surviving crawlers remaining in saferooms, connected to the [[land]] [[quadrant]] via drainage systems, and impacted by a pump left running by [[Maggie]].
• The liberation of the [[Subterranean]] [[Quadrant]] enables travel for crawlers originating there, celebrated by the bubble system.
