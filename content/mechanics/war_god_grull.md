# War God Grull
A level 250 deity involuntarily summoned into [[trainyard]]. [[War God Grull]] is a centaur/war-god figure (son of [[Taranis]] and [[Apito]]), invulnerable on the floor per system warnings, wielding a massive double-headed axe and causing massive environmental devastation.
