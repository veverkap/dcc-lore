# well-rested

• An in-game achievement unlocked by sleeping exceptionally well, granting the Good Rest bonus: +10% base stats and +10% experience/skill training for 30 hours, and including a flavor text effect about monster mothers crying more.
• The achievement is unlocked by [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]] for sleeping so well he felt fully rested despite only sleeping two hours, granting the Good Rest bonus (10% to base stats and 10% XP/skill training for 30 hours) and flavor text about making "monster mothers" cry more.
• The achievement grants the [[Good Rest]] buff for subsequent benefits.
