# Well-Rested!
An in-game achievement unlocked by [[dungeon crawler carl]] when he slept in the [[Ultra-Stabilized Bed]] and felt fully refreshed. The achievement grants the [[Good Rest]] buff for subsequent benefits.

# Well-Rested
An in-game achievement unlocked when a player sleeps exceptionally well. The [[Well-Rested]] achievement grants the Good Rest bonus: +10% base stats and +10% experience/skill training for 30 hours and includes a flavor text effect about monster mothers crying more.

# Well-Rested!
An achievement unlocked by [[dungeon crawler carl]] for sleeping so well he felt fully rested despite only sleeping two hours. Reward: grants the Good Rest bonus (10% to base stats and 10% XP/skill training for 30 hours) and flavor text about making "monster mothers" cry more.
