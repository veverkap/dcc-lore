# eighteenth_floor

* [[Eighteenth Floor]]: a floor containing a club inside the awakened [[Scolopendra]] where guests cannot truly die; leaked monsters and forgotten gods are attacking the trapped tourists and resetting them in the club.
* Attempting to exit the 18th floor before completing the god's task will incur a [[smite]].
* The 18th floor is the relay point for illicit communications and the location of [[Nurse Yugoslav of the Madness]].
* The final boss is expected to awaken on the 18th floor, and it is currently flagged by a [[Scolopendra]].
* The three surviving marked harem members returned to the 18th floor, placing them out of [[Li Na]]'s reach.
* The destination for Syndicate adjutants is the 18th floor, and [[Victory]] mentions not going there with others.
* The door on the 18th floor is now open, making it an objective/exit destination for players.
* The 18th floor is a transfer destination available via the [[Cosmic Lounge]] during [[ceasefire]] under certain conditions.
* The 18th floor is a dangerous floor where guests in a physical club (inside the Scolopendra) are [[immortalized]] by the system AI and where leaked horrors are attacking non-lethal-but-traumatized guests.
* Krakaren Prime was last seen on the 18th floor after escaping banishment to the [[Nothing]].
