# naga_territory

• The [[battlefield]] sector containing enemy [[trenches]], artillery hills, and the [[Naga castle]]; the immediate objective of the planned assault described by [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]], [[Tran]], and [[Colonel Boomer]].
• A neighboring zone the mercenary convoy deliberately bypasses to enter [[Shanty Town]] via [[Posse]] territory; a contested area on the campaign map.
• Region where [[Edgar the tortoise]] is scouting a route and performing reconnaissance.
