# flag_room

• Room adjacent to a faction's [[stronghold]] and [[Throne Room]], referenced as a place to oversee final [[stronghold]] preparations and recruit swearing-in.
• Strategic interior location in the [[stronghold]], called out by system warnings for viewing incursion details and coordinating strategy.
• Special chamber in the fortress that contains the full list of active enchantments and serves as a control/administrative area, adjacent to the [[antechamber]] and throne room.
• Chamber within a faction's [[stronghold]] adjacent to the [[throne room]], used for overseeing [[stronghold]] preparations and managing incoming recruits as advised by [[Baroness Victory]].
