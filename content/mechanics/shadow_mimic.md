# shadow mimic
A subtype of intelligent mimic that copies nearby shapes and can gain some powers; [[Mordecai]] identifies himself as this type. Hostile to [[changeling]]s and prone to "glitching" when near new races.

# Shadow mimic
A highly intelligent, generally evil mimic subtype that can copy the shape of anything nearby. [[Mordecai]] identifies as a shadow mimic (hence his glitching between forms). Shadow mimics are mortal enemies of [[Changeling]] types.
