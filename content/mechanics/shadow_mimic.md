# shadow_mimic

• A subtype of intelligent [[mimic]] that copies nearby shapes and can gain some powers.
• Hostile to changelings and prone to "glitching" when near new races.
• Highly intelligent, generally evil [[mimic]] subtype that can copy the shape of anything nearby.
• Mortal enemies of Changeling types.
• A type of hostile mob/species infesting the Pineapple Cabaret and contributing to its dangerous state.
• An encountered mob whose early detection awarded Donut a legendary box for seeing it before it revealed itself.
