# Levi is On the Menu!
An active system quest triggered aboard the [[Dismemberment Limited]]: "Do not allow [[Levi the Seventh]] to be devoured by the Fleshers." It is a pass/fail quest with a reward (a [[Silver Quest Box]]) and a catastrophic failure state (every crawler on the train turned into a [[Flesher]]).
