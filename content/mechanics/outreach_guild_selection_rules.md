# Outreach Guild Selection Rules
Explicit rules and constraints enforced during outreach-deal meetings:
- Menus and HUD access may be disabled in meeting zones (e.g., [[Zero Zone]]), preventing inventory/healing use.
- Learning the full description of a floor prior to selecting a deal usually increases the season cost of offers (commonly by one or two seasons).
- Pre-collapse item allowance: crawlers may choose a few pre-collapse items and sift current inventory, but weapons are explicitly disallowed.
- Deals are time/availability sensitive: outreach guilds generally reopen only just before floor collapse, and offers may become less generous if you return to negotiate later.
These are procedural dungeon rules affecting crawler decisions during deal selection.
