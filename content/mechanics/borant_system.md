# borant_system

• The Borant system is a star system mentioned as the location where the Valtay had a fleet ready to enforce collections before the season started early.
• The Borant system is under threat of Valtay collection actions prior to the season-start intervention by the Kua-Tin.
• The Borant system is at the center of a debt/sovereignty dispute involving the Valtay and other factions.
• The events of the chapter occur in the Borant system, where the system/ entity must decide whether to lower the [[trailer]]'s shield to save Loita or preserve commercial assets and crawling stars.
• The Borant system is embroiled in civil war, and its collapse and refugee/massacre news are influencing in-[[dungeon]] stakeholders and emotional states.
• The Borant system is referenced during the broadcast as the locus of a debt/bankruptcy dispute involving the Valtay, part of the larger political and economic conflicts discussed on the show.
• The Borant system is the location where political decisions (e.g., border closures) would be enacted by factions like Bloom, and the chapter references choices made by Borant authorities during the [[trailer]]/forcefield incident.
• The Borant system is a governing/regency system referenced in planetary/regional lore, depicted in the Upgrade Patch (Small) as planet [[Earth]] under the regency of the Borant System.
