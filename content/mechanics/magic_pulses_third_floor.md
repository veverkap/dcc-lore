# magic_pulses_third_floor

• Magic pulses (third floor) are environmental events that activate magic on a floor, serving as a precedent for traps that can trigger spells and items.
• Mordecai warns that similar pulses/triggers on lower floors could interact catastrophically with items like the [[Ring of Divine Suffering]].
