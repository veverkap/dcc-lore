# halls_of_ascendency

• The Halls of [[Ascendency]] is a celestial/afterlife locale where gods reside, including [[Ysalte]].
• It is also a higher-plane or system destination where deities return after being defeated or stripped of their soul-armor, such as [[Diwata (Circe Took)]].
• The location is referenced in the origin story of the Butcher’s Masquerade, where Taranis, Apito, and their children held an ill-fated feast that led to the ritual.
• [[Halls of Ascendency]] is associated with the [[celestial ascendency]].
