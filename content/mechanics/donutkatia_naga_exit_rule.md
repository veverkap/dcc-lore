# Donut/Katia Naga exit rule
Explicit dungeon rule described in the chapter: on [[Floor 9]], neither [[Princess Donut]] nor [[Katia]] can leave until all [[Naga]] are dead, and once the Naga are killed, only one of the two may leave. This gating mechanic drives the political dealmaking around attendant promotions.
