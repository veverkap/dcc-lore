# sin_of_consumption

• Named result on a spinning "wheel" attack mechanic deployed by [[Scolopendra]]; causes worms to burst from affected targets and devour them, with a stated 30% chance to hit players.
• Causes a 30% chance for players to be afflicted, with worms spawning to devour affected characters.
