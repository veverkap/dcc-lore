# inner_system

• [[Inner System]]: a spatial destination and off-planet setting, where [[Beatrice]] is en route and standing by to appear after being removed from the planet by [[Odette]].
• The [[Inner System]] refers to the socio-political/astral region where residency berths and citizenship are granted, with [[Odette]] aiming to earn enough credits to buy a berth and obtain guaranteed [[shelter]] and services.
