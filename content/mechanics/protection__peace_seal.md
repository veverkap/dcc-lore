# Protection / Peace Seal
An enforced dungeon rule/field that prevents immediate combat or banishment; referenced when [[Zabit]] warns [[dungeon crawler carl]] about nearly angering [[Vrah]] and a protection/peace seal being in effect. Breaking it triggers consequences and movement by some hunters.
