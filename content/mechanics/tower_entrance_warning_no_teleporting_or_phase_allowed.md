# Tower Entrance Warning: No teleporting or phase allowed
The explicit dungeon rule posted on the tower door: "Warning: If you enter the tower maze, you must proceed to the top before you'll be allowed to go back down. No teleporting or phase allowed in the tower." This is an enforced mechanic restricting movement options inside the [[Tower Maze]].
