# sixth_floor_announcement

• The Sixth Floor Announcement system broadcast opens the sixth floor, declaring it the [[The Hunting Grounds]].
• The announcement notes the hunter count and level bumps, with hunters starting at level 50.
• The exploration window is 17 days.
• There are exits to the seventh floor.
• Final sponsorship bidding is opened.
