# Sixth Floor Announcement
System broadcast that opens the sixth floor, declaring it the [[The Hunting Grounds]], noting the hunter count and level bumps (hunters start at level 50), the 17‑day exploration window, the number of exits to the seventh floor, and the opening of final sponsorship bidding.
