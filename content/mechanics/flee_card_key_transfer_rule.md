# Flee card key transfer rule
An explicit dungeon rule change introduced in the system announcement: if a [[keyholder]] uses a [[flee card]], they will automatically lose one random [[Key]] to their opponent(s) upon fleeing (keys transfer to the ultimate victor); keys cannot be voluntarily dropped or transferred by non-battle means.
