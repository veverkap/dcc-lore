# stairwell

Here is the consolidated markdown list:

* The Stairwell is a physical [[dungeon]] feature that appears in streets and serves as an entrance to lower levels.
* It is the only certain way down to deeper levels in the borough structure and is tied to the Stairwell rule (borough bosses) mechanic.
* The Stairwell is an access point between floors, and clearing a route to it is a key tactical objective in some chapters.
* It becomes a [[ramp]] following prior groups' descent and is a staging area where crawlers must time their descent to avoid losing hours.
* The Stairwell is a primary locus of movement between floors and transforms into a [[ramp]] briefly as Agatha appears and the group moves down.
* It is a crucial stairwell removed from the house and left half a mile east of [[town]], guarded by Langley's crew and necessary for progressing between quadrants.
* The Stairwell is a common vertical escape route between bubbles/floors, and multiple groups flee into it as Emberus rampages outside popped bubbles.
* It is a physical game object that serves as the only permitted [[portal]] between [[dungeon]] floors and is effectively indestructible.
* The Stairwell is the geometric [[portal]] connecting floors, and the party must get the Key Mote into and keep the Puzzle Disk within the Stairwell circle to open and descend.
* The Stairwell Dissolution Rule causes entities that descend certain [[stairwells]] to be dissolved/killed by the [[dungeon]] itself.
* The Stairwell Chamber is a special room type with stricter rules about traps and teleportation.
* The Stairwell Station is a type of station layout used as defensible meeting points and fallbacks.
* The Stairwell travel rule states that the only way for a [[crawler]] to move from one floor to the next is via a Stairwell.
