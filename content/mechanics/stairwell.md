# [[Stairwell]]
A physical game object that serves as the only permitted portal between dungeon floors. The chapter reiterates that [[Stairwell]]s are not removable or allowed to be placed into inventories and are effectively indestructible (survive things like the [[Utter Annihilation wand]]). Many plot beats revolve around damaged stairwell doors and stairwell chambers being inaccessible despite the [[Stairwell]] itself remaining on the playing field.
