# alucarda

• The village of Alucarda contains treehouse buildings, a central [[temple]], and a [[saferoom]]/[[pub]] called [[The Aloe Mana]].
• Alucarda is the [[town]]/area where the chapter's confrontation occurs, featuring a [[saferoom]] tavern, [[guildhall]], a [[temple]] (the [[diwata]]), treehouses, and streets containing traps, NPCs, and [[teleport]] ambushes.
• The village already has an assigned name and is where the [[saferoom]] is located.
• Alucarda is a [[dungeon]] [[town]]/zone entered at the chapter start, where the [[saferoom]], [[guildhall]], and [[temple]] conflicts occur.
• The [[town]] hosts the damaged [[pub]] that [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]] and companions use, with the [[pub]] in Alucarda specifically referenced after the coin spill.
