# station_and_repair_hub_e

• The massive railyard/repair hub, also referred to as [[Station E]], is filled with thousands of ghouls, multiple service bays, watchtowers, and turntables.
• The entry area features a massive fenced railyard with watchtowers, service bays, turntables, and thousands of ghouls.
• The system notification "Entering Station and Repair Hub E" triggers when [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]]'s group arrives with the [[Nightmare]].
