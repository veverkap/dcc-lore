# tutorial_guild

• Starting in-guild room where the protagonist receives HUD/menu tutoring from [[Mordecai]].
• Small guild building and safe-space where [[Mordecai]] sleeps, and new crawlers like [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]] and [[Donut]] can rest, open [[loot]], and learn basic [[dungeon]] mechanics.
• Introductory in-[[dungeon]] facility where new crawlers receive basic information (inventory activation, tutorials).
• Nearby guild destination Carl plots a path to after looting the boss chamber; a location point of interest on the same borough floor.
• Early-game gathering/tutorial area shown in the recap where the floating [[Mind Horror]] guide instructed new players and where [[frank]] and [[Maggie My]] began their violent path.
• Guild instance in the [[Skyfowl settlement]] used for training and player services; [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]] and [[Donut]] head there for guidance and [[upgrades]].
