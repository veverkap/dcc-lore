# hump_town

Here is the consolidated markdown list:

* A dromedarian [[town]] built against a curved wall in the [[Air Quadrant]]; contains the marketplace, inns like [[The Toe]], the desparado establishments on [[Hump Street]], and the central [[City Hall]].
* The [[town]] where [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]] and party operate; single-floor buildings, a [[town]] hall with a guarded secret, and neighborhoods including the [[desperado club]] and Weird Shit Alley. Overlooks the badlands and the [[Wasteland Castle]].
* A corrugated-mental settlement (Bactrian community) near the southern edge of [[The Bowl]] from which flares and anti-aircraft fire originate.
* The bowl-[[town]] under threat of bombardment; site of the collapsed [[town]] hall, the [[Toe]] [[pub]], and the Desperado Club where residents and crawlers gather.
* The village below the descending dreadnaught and balloon where the party is operating; referenced as being at risk of bombardment and the reason for the hostage-negotiation ruse.
* The settlement near the [[necropolis]] and primary [[target]] of the bombing run. [[Hump Town]] sustains partial damage from falling debris and fires but is largely spared by the protagonists' intervention.
* The settlement (located in a bowl) that [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]] and company return to; site of the gerbil assault, repairs, personal space, and children watching [[Toy Story 3]].
* The bowl-shaped [[town]] being repaired and threatened by a sandstorm; the site of the bronze quest to procure deflated balloons and where Carl's group prepares to assault the floating gnomish house.
* The [[town]] at the edge of the bowl where Carl's group is based. Serves as a home for orphans (e.g., [[Bonnie]]) and a staging point for operations ([[storage]], shows, and repairs).
* The district in which [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]] and [[Donut]] are based; the group remains in Hump [[Town]] when they regroup after the meeting with [[orren the liaison]].
* A settlement near where the balloon/house landed; referenced as a landmark when the characters arrive at the [[beach]]/landing area.
* Settlement near the [[necropolis]] used as a staging area; referenced as a point of relocation for the [[uprooted]] house and party operations.
* Settlement where [[Donut]], [[Louis]], and [[Firas]] are reported to be; used as a staging/relay point during the escape and coordination.
* A neighborhood/area referenced as the origin point for [[Donut]]'s separate mission; the text indicates she entered the [[saferoom]] from Hump [[Town]] earlier.
* Landing destination for refugees exiting Desperado gates; the extraction sequence deposits survivors just outside the [[desperado club]] in this locale.
* Local settlement near one of the landing zones where [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]] and [[Donut]] [[land]] and are greeted after a rescue run.
* Settlement near the [[the bowl]] where dromedarians and other survivors work to repair structures; near where the final preparations and confrontations occur.
* A patchwork, brothel-centric [[town]] built against a metal wall within [[Bubble #543]]. Features include nested semi-circular streets, fabric awnings that deploy for storms, [[Hump Street]], [[Weird Shit Alley]], a City Hall, and inns such as [[The Toe]].
* The [[town]] where Carl visits [[Skarn]]; a single-floor city in a bowl-like area with the [[desperado club]], [[town]] hall, and nearby dunes. It is protected from gnome bombing by an unknown deterrent in [[town]] hall.
* A settlement at the bottom of the bowl where survivors gather. Home to orphans and dromedarians and the staging area where [[Bonnie]] is taken and cared for by [[Juice Box]].
* The personal-space neighborhood where [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]] and [[Donut]] return after the liaison meeting; referenced as their current location in the aftermath.
* Settlement near which the flying house landed; local [[town]] referenced when the balloon settled in front.
* A [[town]] used as a staging area; referenced as the location near which a removed [[stairwell]]/chamber was placed so that teleportation and [[portal]] maneuvers could be conducted.
* A settlement where [[Donut]], [[Louis]], and [[Firas]] regrouped in a safe space; mentioned as a staging and refuge area.
* A named [[town]] referenced as the origin point for [[Donut]]'s separate mission and the [[saferoom]] she entered earlier.
