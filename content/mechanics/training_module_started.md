# training_module_started

• An explicit system announcement indicates the start of a guided training sequence in the advanced training room.
• The event is logged as a system-level event in the chapter.
• The announcement appears when [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]] initiates a training sequence in the tier-four training room, signaling the beginning of an automated multi-hour instruction module.
