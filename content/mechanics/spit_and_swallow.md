# spit_and_swallow

• A brothel [[bar]] [[saferoom]] in Hump [[Town]] where [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]]'s group meets other crawlers, noted for incense and a changeling musician.
• Named local [[saferoom]]/tavern that [[Flint]] reportedly tells kids to use, though [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]] considers it unsafe.
• Local tavern mentioned by [[Juice Box]] as a place to discuss events and party afterward.
• A [[bar]] in Hump [[Town]] where Carl, Juice Box, and others sit and where the exploding toy scorches the front of the establishment.
• A brothel [[bar]] [[saferoom]] in [[town]] (logo: bird skewered on a stick) where the [[crawler]] duo [[Firas M]] and [[louis santiago]] were found, featuring dromedarian patrons and serving as a recruitment stop.
• Named [[saferoom]]/tavern referenced by [[Skarn]] and associated with [[Flint]]'s guidance, mentioned as an incorrect destination for safety compared to the [[Toe]].
• A local establishment ([[pub]]/tavern) mentioned by [[Juice Box]] as intact and suggested as a meeting place to discuss local matters and the changelings.
• A tavern/[[bar]] in the [[town]] where [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]] and [[Juice Box]] converse and where debris from an exploding toy scorches the front, a scene hub for planning the floating-house assault.
