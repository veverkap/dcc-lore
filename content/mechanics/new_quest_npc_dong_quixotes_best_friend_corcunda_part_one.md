# New Quest: NPC Dong Quixote’s best friend, Corcunda (Part one)
The system announcement quest that appears mid-chapter. It declares that [[Corcunda (Corky)]] has been located (part one) and explains that finishing part one will trigger part two, which requires locating [[Porky]] and a fleshmancer. The reward promises two stand-ins to be sent to the [[Arena on the 11th floor]] if completed.
