# area_message_amayon_arrival

• A large-scale system announcement informs players that the [[Madre de Aguas]] has succumbed and that [[Amayon]] has entered the realm.
• Includes global effects and sets up a related quest opportunity.
• The announcement announces that [[Madre de Aguas]] succumbed to zombism, triggering the involuntary arrival of the Demon Prince [[Amayon]] and granting associated temporary effects and rewards.
