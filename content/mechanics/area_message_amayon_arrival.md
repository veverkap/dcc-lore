# [[Area Message: Amayon arrival]]
A large-scale system announcement informing players that the [[Madre de Aguas]] has succumbed and that [[Amayon]] has entered the realm. Includes global effects and sets up a related quest opportunity.

# Area Message: Amayon Arrival
An in-world system area message announcing that [[Madre de Aguas]] succumbed to zombism, triggering the involuntary arrival of the Demon Prince [[Amayon]] and granting associated temporary effects and rewards.
