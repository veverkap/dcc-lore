# Flee card
An explicit dungeon rule and in-battle card whose behavior is modified by a system announcement in this chapter. Normally allows non-keyholders to exit combat and teleport to a nearby random location; under the new rules, if a [[Key]]-holder uses the [[flee card]] they automatically lose a random key to their opponent(s). Keys cannot be voluntarily dropped or transferred except via this mechanic.
