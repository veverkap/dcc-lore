# main_hallway

• A primary corridor that serves as the staging area for the [[Parade Train]] and becomes a focal point when grub swarms converge.
• The route where the [[Rage Elemental]] teleported and pursued the party, and where bombs and grubs were encountered.
• Leads to the [[Stairwell]] and other exits.
