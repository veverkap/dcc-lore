# festering_ghoul

• A Level 18 mob born from withdrawal-induced [[DTs]], not undead, and immune to anti-undead attacks.
• Has two primary drives: consume organic matter and recruit/lead others to this area.
• Present in large numbers in the yard and responsible for the infestation.
• Not true undead, but a reborn lifeform born from addiction-driven [[DTs]].
• Resists anti-undead attacks, seeks organic material, and recruits more of its kind.
• Can produce worm-like creatures that combine into a massive, room-filling frankenmonster at Stop 48.
• Moss- and bark-skinned variants among the yard's creatures, seeking organic material and more of their kind.
• Key component of the floor's escalating threat.
