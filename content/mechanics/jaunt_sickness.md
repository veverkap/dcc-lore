# Jaunt Sickness
A dungeon affliction delivered by the [[Moon Reaper]] "Reaper Gaze" that traps the victim in subjective time (each second perceived as ~30 years). Described as one of the most insidious attacks in Dungeon Crawler World.

# Jaunt sickness
A debilitating condition inflicted by [[Moon Reaper]] gaze attacks: victims are "trapped in time" and perceive each second as decades, leaving them fully conscious but temporally distorted. Cited as one of the dungeon's most insidious status effects.
