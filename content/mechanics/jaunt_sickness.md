# jaunt_sickness

• A [[dungeon]] affliction delivered by the [[Moon Reaper]] "Reaper Gaze" that traps the victim in subjective time (each second perceived as ~30 years).
• A debilitating condition inflicted by [[Moon Reaper]] gaze attacks: victims are "trapped in time" and perceive each second as decades, leaving them fully conscious but temporally distorted.
