# station_435_kravyad_station

* Station 435 (Kravyad Station) is a terminal station on the Vermillion Line, listed after Terminus 433 in the route description.
* It is reported as the employee exit point, with a rumored [[portal]] to [[headquarters]] beyond the zombie fence and a boss fight against [[The Kravyad]].
* The station is two stops from the [[mimic]] incident and hosts a [[Kravyad]] boss and an employee [[portal]] that can be used as an exit if the Kravyad remains alive.
* It is associated with risk, including possible [[Kravyad]] boss fights and memory/time loss effects.
* The station is specific to a location where a [[Kravyad]] boss must be fought to use the employee [[portal]] exit.
* It is a transit stop where Tangle employees disembark to take the [[portal]] back to the depot, central to the chapter's mystery about employee return and memory loss.
* It is the end-of-the-line stop where all passengers must disembark, central to [[Vernon]]'s loop mystery.
* The station is key to understanding employee return mechanics and is associated with the [[Abyss Gate]].
* It is reported by [[Vernon]] as the terminal stop on the loop where anomalous events occur, including memory fuzziness, deaths beyond the line, and the [[train]] resetting to yard/station 10 for certain crew members.
* The station is also reported as the terminal station for the looped Yellow Line/Nightmare Express run, with [[Vernon]] claiming everyone must disembark and strange post-435 events occur, including apparent deaths and resets, with anecdotal links to station 10 and the yard.
* The Kravyad is also the name of a [[target]] that [[Sadie]]'s [[Glass Prison]] was used against.
