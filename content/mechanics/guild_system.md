# guild_system

• New mechanic activating on the sixth floor that allows organized parties, shared personal space [[upgrades]], and structured group formation (information available at sixth floor [[Bopcas]]).
• Allows crawlers to form guilds via purchasable [[Guild charter]]s (starting at 500,000 gold), combining multiple parties/personal spaces into guilds, adding guild-only [[upgrades]], and including rules (e.g., parties limited to 30 unless in a guild).
