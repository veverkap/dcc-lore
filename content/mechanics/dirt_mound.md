# dirt_mound

• The massive pile of [[earth]], visible as a towering, crumbling wall of dirt, holds the exit key somewhere on or near its summit.
• The large pile of [[earth]] at the end of the island blocks the [[road]] and covers a bridge to the next island.
• The top (~700 feet up) houses the next deckmaster.
• The base is guarded by [[Reef Explorer]] in this chapter.
• The pile is approximately 700 feet high in places, partially eroding into the ocean.
• The site of the chapter's major confrontation and the location of the next deckmaster encounter.
