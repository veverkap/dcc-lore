# The cul-de-sac
The residential circle of houses referenced in the chapter where teams had their homes. It now contains ten houses (previously nine) with empty, abandoned houses arranged in the order teams were lost; the closest empty home belongs to [[One Fine Pig]]. Nearby garages like [[Garage Three]] and [[Garage Four]] open onto this cul-de-sac.
