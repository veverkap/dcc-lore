# the_cul-de-sac

• The [[cul-de-sac]] is a residential circle of houses with ten houses, previously nine, arranged in the order teams were lost.
• The closest empty home belongs to [[One Fine Pig]].
• Nearby garages like [[Garage Three]] and [[Garage Four]] open onto this [[cul-de-sac]].
