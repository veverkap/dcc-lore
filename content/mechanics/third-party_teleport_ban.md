# Third-Party Teleport Ban
Punitive dungeon rule implemented by the [[Syndicate]] as a result of the investigation: no crawlers may be teleported away by third parties for the remainder of the [[Fifth Floor]]. This ban cancels scheduled show appearances and affects mobility until the next floor opens.
