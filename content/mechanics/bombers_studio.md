# bombers_studio

• [[Bomber's Studio]] is the workspace where the narrator modified smoke-curtain devices and tested dispersal with water; location of crafting and device testing.
• A location belonging to a "bomber" mentioned by [[Mordecai]] when instructing [[Dungeon [[Crawler]] Carl]] to go there; implies an external workspace tied to the group's activities.
• A workspace within the [[saferoom]] district where the wheeled bomb/cart is prepared and where [[Dungeon [[Crawler]] Carl]] retrieves the cart for the operation.
• A location referenced by [[Mordecai]] when instructing [[Carl]] to go there; implied to be linked to one of Carl's associates and potentially to post-battle actions.
