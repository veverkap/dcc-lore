# Shambling Berserker
A Level 12 undead abomination (also called the Mini Grinder or the Shrilling) built from sewn-together body parts. Quadruples in power in darkness, slow until enraged, and described by the in-world bestiary entry encountered during the pool fight. Killed by [[Mongo]] and the party when daylight returned.

# Shambling Berserker
Also referred to as a "Shrilling" or "mini-Grinder" in the quest text; mindless forest undead used as fodder in the mass outbreak described in [[The Creeping Apocalypse]]. Numerous and variable in size, they are less threatening individually but dangerous in quantity.
