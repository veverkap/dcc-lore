# zockau_saferoom

• A [[saferoom]]/safehouse location in the [[guildhall]] where the group gathers, opens [[loot]] boxes, and posts notices.
• Contains the [[emberus]] and the small vocal coach training room rewarded from a box.
• Players post notes and receive replies.
• [[Bomo]] is sent there to await a response.
• Several characters gather nearby in the [[guildhall]]/common area associated with it.
