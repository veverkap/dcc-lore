# vahana

• The wicker-basket balloon craft commandeered by [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]] and crew contains the control mechanism, farseer, and serves as the launch point for their counterattack and eventual leap to the [[necropolis]] [[roof]].
• A decorated Gnomish Legate Balloon encountered beneath the [[Wasteland]]'s formation, carrying a wicker basket with three gnome officers (pilot, negotiator/[[Leon]], and sniper) and functioning as the immediate boarding/ negotiation [[platform]].
• The hot-[[air]] balloon/basket vehicle hijacked and used by [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]], [[Donut]], and [[Katia]] to assault the aerial fleet and approach the [[Wasteland]].
