# forest

• The dark, rain-soaked Forest is where the party tracks Miriam Dom's signal and encounters Sierra - Northropi Vampire in a "Special Forest Encounter."
• The Forest is a region where dryads and trees are in pain, casting a spell to call Diwata for protection against the Gehenna Brambles.
• The Forest cries, and elves hunt the party, according to Signet.
