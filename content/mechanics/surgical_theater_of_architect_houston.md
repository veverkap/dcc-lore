# Surgical Theater of Architect Houston
An octagonal, stark-white surgical room in the castle housing multiple operating tables, the [[Grease Monkey]] apparatus, and the scene of the confrontation where [[Architect Houston of the Madness]] was attached to [[Namico]] and where [[Burcu]] and [[Zeynep]] were found.
