# warning_level_collapse_is_imminent

• A system announcement mechanic that indicates imminent structural failure of the floor, serving as a ticking constraint on the party's actions and tied to a countdown timer.
• The countdown timer forces rushed decisions and drives the party's rush to complete a puzzle.
• Variants of the countdown, such as numeric displays or unit ticks, are used to convey the remaining time.
• The mechanic is used to signal the imminent floor collapse and drive the urgency for the party's ritual and sacrifice.
