# dilapidated_farmhouse

• The Dilapidated Farmhouse is a ruined, wooden house near a cliff.
• Its [[roof]] is blown apart early in the chapter when Carl's [[Quarter Hob-Lobber]] detonates, causing terrain collapse and additional hazards.
