# the_goddess_ysalte_has_entered_the_realm

• [[The Goddess Ysalte has entered the realm.]]: A high-level system announcement triggered by the killing of [[Sister Ines Quiteria, The Reaper of Havana]] or the death of [[Sister Ines]], warning players that the celestial Patron (the Goddess Ysalte) has arrived and is coming to seek revenge.
