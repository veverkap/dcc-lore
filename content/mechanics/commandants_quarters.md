# Commandant's quarters
The house/home setting (the Commandant’s quarters) where the chapter's encounter occurs. Contains the kitchen, upstairs bedrooms, and the sealed "throne room" leading to the sixth floor; the scene of [[Commandant Kane]]'s death and the [[Denise]] boss fight.
