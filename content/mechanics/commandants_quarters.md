# commandants_quarters

• The Commandant's quarters is a location where the chapter's encounter takes place.
• It contains a [[kitchen]], upstairs bedrooms, and a sealed "throne room" leading to the sixth floor.
• The throne room is the scene of Commandant Kane's death and the Denise boss fight.
