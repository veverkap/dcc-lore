# concierge_sharks

• Predatory aquatic mobs shown devouring trapped players and officials in the submerged parts of [[Larracos]].
• Used by event organizers to dispose of ejected participants.
• Predatory creatures used by operators in the submerged sections of [[Larracos]] (e.g., the Desperado Club) to devour ejected players and mercenaries.
• Featured on Odette's broadcast footage.
