# dungeon_crawler_world

Here is the consolidated markdown list:

• The [[Dungeon]] [[Crawler]] World is a seeded, interstellar game-world constructed and operated by corporations like [[borant corporation]] under [[Syndicate]] oversight.
• It is the primary location for the season's crawl, composed of multiple floors with evolving rules, themes, and entrance mechanics.
• The game-world is referenced in various contexts, including the live show, system messages, and achievement rewards.
• It includes floors, leaderboards, sponsorships, and system rules enforced by [[borant corporation]] and overseen by the [[Syndicate]].
• The game-world has a ranking system (e.g., [[Top 10]]) and bounties such as the [[500,000 Gold Bounty]].
• The game-world is also referenced in exposition, historical source of classic monsters like the [[hydra]], and governs season rules and spectacle.
• The game-world is the setting for the crawl, and its architecture and zoning determine how player interfaces and [[upgrades]] behave on different terrains and in rental environments like the [[Deluxe Rental Trailer]].
• The game-world is the overarching system/world in which the crawl takes place, and its environment/setting is referenced in various contexts, including the broader setting of the series and the historical context for how dangerous certain creatures are.
