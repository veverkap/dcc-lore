# Premature Questjaculation!
An achievement/System notification triggered when a quest was effectively completed before it could be assigned. Mentioned in-camp after the battle; it mocked the party and offered no reward beyond a taunt. Connects to the broader quest flow around [[The Vengeance of the Daughter]].
