# Ascendency Rules
Dungeon/System rules referenced when describing diminished divine power: when a deity loses worshippers or is subjugated, their level and powers are reduced (e.g., [[Lamashtu the Donkey]]'s level being cut in half). Governs interactions for subjugated gods in the floor mechanics.
