# studio

• The broadcast set where the interview with [[Odette]] takes place, with props simulating deeper space and hiding the true location of the boat's far end.
• The on-[[air]] set where [[Odette]] hosts the show, interacts with the audience, and where censorship and broadcast delays are managed by admins like [[Loita]].
• The on-[[air]] broadcast space where the caprid herd appears via holos, the confrontation with [[Gamori]] and [[Harbinger]] occurs, and where the violent eruption takes place.
• The televised set where [[Odette]] hosts, audience interaction occurs, and the preview footage for the next floor is shown.
• The broadcast space for [[Odette]]'s show; site of audience interaction, censorship delays, and on-[[air]] commentary about the crawl.
• The on-camera environment where [[Odette]] conducts the tunneled program and where the interview with [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]], [[Princess Donut]], and [[Beatrice]] takes place.
• The larger production space containing the panel [[stage]]/[[arena]] and the audience, connecting to the green room and including the panel room where [[Circe Took]] moderates and the crowd watches the discussion.
• The live broadcast space where the goat panel, [[Carl]], [[Donut]], and production staff are present; the primary setting for the stampede and its aftermath.
