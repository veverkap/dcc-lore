# [[Hands (Currency)]]
Currency unit used to purchase prizes at the counter (e.g., 1, 3, 5, 10, 20, 30, 50, 100 hands). Hands are earned by killing crawlers or other means; used to buy upgrades, potions, and rare boons like [[Memorial Crystal]] and floor boons.
