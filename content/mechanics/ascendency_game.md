# ascendency_game

• A referenced meta-gameplay mechanic where players awaken or re-ascend gods via quests and shrine activity, driving global resurrection events.
• A battle where the [[winner]] becomes a true god of the playground, with rules remaining "as written" and a "[[winner]] takes all" approach.
• A cascading system triggered by a systemic collapse among crawlers, with the mission aiming to "kick off" the [[Ascendency]] game early to enable allied assets to react.
• Notably, the twelfth floor is involved in the [[Ascendency]] game.
