A transformed circus performer appearing as a monstrous, undead bear-clown hybrid (roller skates, tutu, exposed skull, and worm-like front paws). Identified as the sacrifice whose blood [[Signet]] wishes to use for summoning; currently asleep under the [[Water Lily]] spell.
