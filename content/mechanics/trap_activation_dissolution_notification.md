# [[Trap Activation (Dissolution Notification)]]
A system notification reporting that a player's trap has activated and dissolved a creature (e.g., "You have dissolved a [[Tigran]]"). Also used to report repeated activations and team eliminations (e.g., notification that [[Team One Fine Pig]] was eliminated).
