# pet_stables

• The pet stables are a location for housing large animal mounts and pets, intended to hold them during evacuation.
• The party reroutes [[Dong Quixote]] away from the pet stables to [[Shanty Town]] for a better exit.
• The guild location houses pets such as [[Rend]], [[Gonk]], [[Simoom]], and [[Penelope]], and is just behind the storefront.
• The secondary guild add-on room, the pet stables, is used for group meetings and has a bug that prevents gods from overhearing certain discussions.
• [[Upgrades]] can raise the pet stables to level 5 with the [[Stablemaster benefit]].
