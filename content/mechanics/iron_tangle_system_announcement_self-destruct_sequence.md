# iron_tangle_system_announcement_self-destruct_sequence

• The loud system broadcast declares the [[the tangle]] broken, warning customers that all lines are down.
• The self-destruct sequence is initiated.
• Evacuees are instructed to use [[emergency]] escape [[tunnels]] and specific [[stairwell]] [[portals]].
• The [[emergency]] escape window is open for 6 hours, with the last opportunity to escape in 18 hours.
