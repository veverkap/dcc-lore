# Iron Tangle System Announcement (Self-Destruct Sequence)
The loud system broadcast declaring the [[the tangle]] broken, warning customers that all lines are down, announcing a initiated self-destruct sequence, and instructing evacuees to use emergency escape tunnels and specific stairwell portals (opening in 18 hours for a six-hour window).
