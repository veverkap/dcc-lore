# fourth_floor_-_the_iron_tangle

• The Fourth Floor, also known as "The Iron Tangle", is a location with a vast, intertwined rail/subway system.
• The floor contains the Red Line and multiple stations, including [[Sirin Station - 81]], [[Mora Station - 82]], and [[Traveler Transfer Station - 83]].
• The party has a 10-day time limit to complete the chapter.
