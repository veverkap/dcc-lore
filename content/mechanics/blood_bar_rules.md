# blood bar rules
Dungeon/Game rules affecting entry and persistence in certain locations: a full blood bar is required to enter places like [[Club Vanquisher]], and in some zones blood bars don't drain once inside—critical to [[Princess Vinata]]’s ability to hide and to restrictions on safe-room camping.
