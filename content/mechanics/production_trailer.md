# production_trailer

* The Production [[Trailer]] is a safe-room-like space with its own rules, a [[bar]], bathroom with a porthole, and a couch where guests relax before being ushered onto the [[Studio]] Set.
* It is a [[backstage]]/production space implied during the Maestro's broadcast, referenced when attacks onstage passed through an invisible boundary.
* The [[trailer]] contains a snack table, the green room, and is a secondary zone where inventory restrictions are enforced.
* It is a [[studio]] space where the team meets before being transferred to the [[Warehouse]]-like Room for [[quadrant]] selection.
* The [[trailer]] is a secondary zone where some [[dungeon]] protections and inventories are restricted.
* It is the [[studio]]/ [[trailer]] where Loita was killed by the exploding toy, containing the Mexx unit surveillance camera and a forcefield that influenced the explosion's behavior.
* The [[trailer]] is a scheduled in-world meeting location where [[Dungeon]] [[Crawler]] Carl and Donut will meet Odette and have a chance to speak freely with staff.
* It is a smaller living/interview space inside the Production Facility where crawlers are housed for events, containing a bench, small bathroom, counter, and an exit with an alien symbol.
* The [[trailer]] is a dignitary-grade, rentable onboard [[studio]]/quarters used by the show, containing advanced cleaning facilities and a [[studio]] space.
* It is the [[studio]] space above the surface where the interview took place and where inventories behaved differently due to being in the secondary zone.
* The [[trailer]] is the on-surface [[studio]] area where the team meets before broadcast, containing the table where Loita stands and is designated as being in the "secondary zone."
* It is the enclosed, submarine-like [[trailer]] rented by Veriluxx for the commercial taping, functioning like a [[studio]] with a green room and a picture window looking into the dark ocean.
* The [[trailer]] is the set/room where Loita was killed by the explosion triggered by the Robot Donut's Toy Back Panel failure and where surveillance (a Mexx Unit) captured limited footage.
* It is a meeting/ location referenced where Zev schedules visits and scrutiny, where characters expect to meet NPCs such as Odette and receive additional interactions.
* The [[trailer]] is an on-site production space referenced at the start of the chapter where Lexis seats the guests and from which the interview is conducted, part of the [[studio]] environment and behind-the-scenes setting for the segment.
* It is an individual compartment-style room aboard the Production Facility used to house crawlers during events, contrasting with previous underwater trailers and noting altered surveillance and containment.
