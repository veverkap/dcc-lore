# [[Temple of Theia]]
Temple dedicated to [[Theia]], typically located inside [[Club Vanquisher]] in the described floors. Uses Theia's prospect mechanic (motes of light) and serves as a hiding/wooing place where prospects are courted and can be teleported away if they decline.
