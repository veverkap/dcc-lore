# intellect_hunter

• A finger-sized parasitic race that can take over bodies of slain creatures, causing rapid rot.
• Takes over bodies briefly, causing rapid rot and constant hopping between hosts.
• Reanimates or controls bodies, hopping between hosts and causing rapid rot, part of the taxonomy of possessive/undead entities.
