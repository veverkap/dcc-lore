# Yellow Line Reward Room
Porter car reward chamber guarded by porters like [[Pierre]]. Presents the system prompt "You’ve discovered the Yellow Line Reward Room!" and enforces the rule that players may only collect one prize per crawler, per train line.

# Yellow Line Reward Room
A special reward room on the [[Yellow Line]] accessed by knocking in the porter car; players can select one [[Prize Case]] (suitcase) number between 1 and 200. The room is associated with a system discovery message when first found.
