# yellow_line_reward_room

• Presents the system prompt "You’ve discovered the Yellow Line Reward Room!" and enforces the rule that players may only collect one prize per [[crawler]], per [[train]] line.
• A special reward room on the [[Yellow Line]] accessed by knocking in the porter car; players can select one [[Prize Case]] number between 1 and 200.
• The porter-operated reward cupboard accessible via the [[Porter Car]] door where [[Pierre]] dispenses [[Prize Case]]s and suitcases to eligible crawlers.
