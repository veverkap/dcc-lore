# Saferoom and Stairwell Rule
System message text on the station map: "Saferooms appear at all transfer stations. Stairwells appear at stations numbered 12, 24, 36, 48, and 72 on any colored line." An explicit dungeon rule governing saferoom and stairwell distribution.
