# graveyard

• Nighttime battleground surrounding the octagonal [[temple]], filled with wailing [[ghommids]], and the scene of the gauntlet run toward [[La Iglesia de los Olvidados]].
• Fog-covered [[battlefield]] surrounding [[La Iglesia de los Olvidados]], full of night-empowered [[Ghommids]] and the gauntlet path cleared by [[Asojano]] to the [[temple]].
• Section of [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]]'s inventory where collected corpses (including war mage bodies) appear; described as nearly empty after recent pickups.
• Prior location where [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]] and [[Donut]] tested a [[phase through walls]] potion earlier in their departure; used as contextual lore for potion mechanics.
