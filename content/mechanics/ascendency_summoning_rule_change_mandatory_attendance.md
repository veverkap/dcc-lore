# Ascendency summoning rule change (mandatory attendance)
An approved emergency action item altering ascendency rules: ascendency players must now attend and pilot their sponsored deities when summoned off the [[twelfth floor]]; summoned deities will appear as designed and be piloted by sponsors, removing prior opt-out options.
