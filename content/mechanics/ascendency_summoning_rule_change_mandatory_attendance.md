# ascendency_summoning_rule_change_mandatory_attendance

• [[Ascendency]] players must attend and pilot their sponsored deities when summoned off the [[twelfth floor]].
• Summoned deities will appear as designed and be piloted by sponsors, removing prior opt-out options.
