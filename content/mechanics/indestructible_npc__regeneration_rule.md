# [[Indestructible NPC / Regeneration Rule]]
A dungeon/system rule described in the Crawler lore: some NPCs cannot be permanently killed and regenerate within minutes. Such NPCs often possess important knowledge or items required for progression (e.g., passwords or keys). Cited by [[Crawler Azin]] and illustrated by [[Growler Gary]].
