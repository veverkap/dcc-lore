# system_message_an_odious_creeper_has_fallen

• [[System Message: An Odious Creeper has fallen.]] is a recurring system announcement used to log kill credit for [[Odious Creeper]]s, track remaining creepers, and assign credit to specific crawlers.
• The announcement is used throughout the chapter to notify players of kills and display remaining creeper counts.
