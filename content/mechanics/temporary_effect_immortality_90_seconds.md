# [[Temporary Effect: Immortality (90 seconds)]]
A temporary deity-granted effect from [[Khepri]] upon presence in the temple. The chapter explicitly reports "You are immortal for the next 90 seconds," a mechanic that can alter the immediate combat outcome.
