# Quest: Reunite Corcunda (Corky) and Porky
The system announcement quest that appears mid-scene. Text: "NPC [[Dong Quixote]]’s best friend in the world, [[Corcunda (Corky)]], has been located ... Reunite them." Part one is easy; completing it triggers part two which requires locating [[Porky]] and finding a [[Fleshmancer]]. Reward: two stand-ins of the player’s choosing will be sent to the [[Arena (11th floor)]] on the [[11th floor]].
