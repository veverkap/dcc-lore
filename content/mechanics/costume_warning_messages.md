# Costume Warning Messages
System announcements and warnings emitted when [[Enchanted Juvenile Macropus Dominus Skin]] is equipped (examples in-chapter include notices negating boxer/pedicure effects and timed-item warnings such as "This is a 12-hour timed item"). These are presented as active system messages that affect player status while the costume is worn.
