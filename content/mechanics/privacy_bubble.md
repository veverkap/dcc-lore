# Privacy Bubble
A club-provided spell/mechanic that creates a translucent bubble around a user's head to filter loud music and enable normal conversation; multiple tiers exist and it is commonly available in clubs like the [[desperado club]].

# Privacy Bubble
An active player/system skill/mechanic used by the protagonist to partially mute nearby audio and obscure visibility; it is applied while in the [[Penis Parade]] and removed automatically when entering the [[Guild of Suffering]].
