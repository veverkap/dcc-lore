# privacy_bubble

• Creates a translucent bubble around a user's head to filter loud music and enable normal conversation, with multiple tiers available in clubs like the [[desperado club]].
• An active player/system skill/mechanic used to partially mute nearby audio and obscure visibility, applied while in the [[Penis Parade]] and removed automatically when entering the [[Guild of Suffering]].
