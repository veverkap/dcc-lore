# [[Transit Map Rules (Saferooms & Stairwells)]]
Map-system rule box displayed on the station map: "Saferooms appear at all transfer stations. Stairwells appear at stations numbered 12, 24, 36, 48, and 72 on any colored line." Governs safe locations and stairwell placements on the floor.
