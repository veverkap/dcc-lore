# Unnamed Dog
An off-screen animal entity described as capable of remotely controlling [[Lucia]] in short bursts. [[Florin]] stresses that retrieving this dog is the priority because it can exert control from afar; it is implicated in the presence of multiple people "in there" and linked to [[Alexandro]].
