# shunned

• Status label applied to players who have incurred divine displeasure, visible to others and affecting social/combat consequences.
• Visible status effect indicating social or divine disfavor after killing an [[Emberus]] worshiper; public marker that affects interactions and is tied to other penalties like [[Penitence]].
