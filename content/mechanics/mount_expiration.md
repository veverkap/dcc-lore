# [[Mount Expiration]]
An implicit race/dungeon rule referenced in the chapter: teams can be eliminated if their mount expires (as stated for the [[Trauco Triplets]]), and rules treat vehicles/mounts differently than living participants. This mechanic drives Carl’s urgency to prevent another team elimination.
