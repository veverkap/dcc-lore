# shit-faced

• A debuff/mechanic caused by consuming [[rev-up moonshine]], producing intoxication effects such as chance to Blind, chance of instant death, and possible permanent stat increases.
• Applied to [[Dwight the unicorn]] as a status effect indicating extreme drunkenness and altered behavior, affecting NPC behavior in dungeons.
