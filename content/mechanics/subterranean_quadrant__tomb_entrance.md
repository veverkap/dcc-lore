# subterranean_quadrant__tomb_entrance

• A cave-like opening beneath the party leads into carved brick [[tunnels]] protected by a forcefield.
• Traps are present, triggered by a rolled [[Banger Sphere]].
• The forcefield blocks entry until [[quadrant]] conditions are met.
