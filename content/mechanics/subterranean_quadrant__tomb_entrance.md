# [[Subterranean Quadrant / Tomb Entrance]]
A cave-like opening beneath the party that leads into carved brick tunnels protected by a forcefield. Traps are present (triggered by a rolled [[Banger Sphere]]), and the forcefield blocks entry until quadrant conditions are met.
