# desperado_club

Here is the consolidated list of unique factual points about the Desperado Club:

• The Desperado Club is a nightclub and [[casino]] destination on the floor that is always open.
• It contains games like High Roller's Roulette Table and Wheel of Fortune.
• Mordecai has connections to the club.
• The club exists across multiple club locations on the same plane.
• It allows local [[crawler]] access and contains many bot NPCs.
• The club has a neon knife logo and multiple themed rooms.
• It is a social hub where crawlers gather and can appear at transfer stations.
• The club is a meeting point for workers and a route to deeper floors.
• Victims and workers were lured to the club, and Burgundy reports being promised opportunities there before being trafficked into the krasue scheme.
• The club is a named locale reachable via quests and is cited as a goal tied to certain quest lines.
• It is a destination the party plans to hit after the recap episode and before sleep/training.
• The club is a social venue where crawlers gather and can be found.
• It is a meeting point for teams to plan and share intelligence.
• The club is a major pleasure district [[pub]] and social hub with game machines and gathering spots.
• Access to the club can be removed if entry points are destroyed, affecting survivors' privileges.
• The club is a location where messages can be left for the changeling group.
• It is a location with a contested second floor occupied by hunters.
• The club is a strip club that serves as a front for the Guild of Suffering.
• It contains a hidden entrance used for guild access and houses the [[Naughty]] Boys front.
• The club is a multi-level entertainment complex where the chapter's events unfold.
• It contains a [[casino]], [[kitchens]], [[restaurant]], bars, strip club levels, secret halls, and a hidden door housing Vorpals.
• The club is a location referenced in the [[ceasefire]] discussion as a transit point some players can use.
• It is a prominent club on a lower floor of Larracos that has just reopened under new management.
• The club is a named club referenced as an important venue in the region.
• It is a location in the city that Ferdinand and Drick return to.
• The club is a location associated with drug dens and quests tied to Glory Bound.
• It is a violent location where Minge escaped and many rhinoceros guards died.
• The club is a destination/location invoked by the transfer notification at the end of the chapter.
• It is a location mentioned as the place where a turned family patriarch became [[manager]].
• The club is a well-trafficked club area distinct from Club Vanquisher.
• It is a location referenced for its magical mass-ejection defense.
• The club is a nightclub location where missing strippers [[Anaconda]] and Damascus Steel were last sought.
• It is home to guildmasters like Milk.
