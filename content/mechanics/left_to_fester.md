# left_to_fester

• A debuff triggered by the [[Ring of Divine Suffering]], preventing normal healing until the marked [[target]] is killed.
• Applied to [[Miriam Dom]] as a consequence of magical backlash and marking.
• Inflicted on the wearer of the [[Ring of Divine Suffering]] after marking a [[target]], preventing healing and clearing only when the marked [[target]] is killed.
• A named debuff/status that appeared on [[Prepotente]] after a spell backlash, preventing normal healing and interaction.
• A poisonous debuff afflicting [[Li Na]] that worsens over time and may be cured by reaching the [[Pineapple Cabaret]] or the [[Earth]]'s surface.
