# Left to Fester
A debuff mechanic triggered when a target is marked by the [[Ring of Divine Suffering]]. The afflicted cannot heal normally until the marked target is killed; the chapter shows both Carl and [[Xindy]] infected with [[Left to Fester]] after marks are applied.

# [[Left to Fester]]
A persistent debuff inflicted on the wearer of the [[Ring of Divine Suffering]] after they mark a target. The debuff prevents healing (and possibly other restorative actions) and does not clear until the marked target is killed. Explicitly presented as a system effect in the chapter.
