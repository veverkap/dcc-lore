# [[Symbiote]]
The combined creature formed when a Flesher animates bones using [[Boned]]; exemplified by the hobgoblin-skellie component of [[Levi the Seventh]]. New iterations can grow in power with repeated resleeving.

# Symbiote
The combined organism formed when a Flesher bonds to skeleton/boned remains and casts [[Boned]]. Symbiotes can grow more powerful through repeated resleeving cycles; Levi the Seventh is an example of a Symbiote state described in this chapter.
