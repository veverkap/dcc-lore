# dirigible_gnome_wasteland_fortress

• The Dirigible Gnome [[Wasteland]] Fortress is a sky castle and the chosen opponent location from the [[quadrant]] wheel.
• The gnomes occupy this fortress and the team's objective is to storm and clear it from the [[air]].
• The fortress is the primary [[stronghold]] of the gnomes and the specific opponent location the team must storm to pass the fifth floor.
• The Dirigible Gnome [[Wasteland]] Fortress is the full named opponent location revealed by the [[quadrant]]/ opponent wheel, corresponding to the Dirigible Gnomes' castle in the sky and becoming this team's assigned opponent area.
