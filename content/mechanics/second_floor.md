# second_floor

* The Second Floor is a location in the [[Dungeon]] [[Crawler]] World where live viewers can tune in to individual crawlers, mobs begin dropping gold, and creature difficulty escalates.
* The entrance population to this floor is gated by the season's broadcast timing.
* The Second Floor is where contestants' social numbers can spike, and reaching it will dramatically increase public attention for certain characters.
* The floor is home to the Rev-Up moonshine room, laminak fairies, clurichauns, bicycle blenders, and the sudden appearance of the Second Floor Boss.
* The Second Floor contains pre-seeded mobs and has atypical Waste Disposal System behavior, producing mass grub swarms.
* The floor is referenced as having a record-setting, highly watched battle involving certain characters.
* The Second Floor is where most of the chapter's action occurs, including pet acquisition, grinding, and preparation for the Third Floor.
* The collapse of the Second Floor triggers the announcement and countdown for the Third Floor.
* The Second Floor is referenced as part of an incident sequence and a multi-floor event recap.
* Creatures like the Brindled Grub were encountered on this floor, and mob-creation mechanics for janitors were noted.
