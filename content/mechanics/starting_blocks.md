# starting_blocks

• The race starting area is where the opposing team heads towards with [[Corky]], and contestants cannot be attacked while on the way there.
• The staging area from which racers depart is referred to as the starting blocks, and was referenced by [[Carl]] as a location where the AI allowed them to leave despite a transformation event.
