# Predator
A coordinated move used by [[dungeon crawler carl]] and [[Donut]] where both consume invisibility potions and Donut switches to infrared vision; the pair go silent and Donut scouts from Carl's shoulder. Used to infiltrate [[Zockau]] undetected.
