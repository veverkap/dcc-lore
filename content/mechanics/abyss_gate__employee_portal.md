# Abyss gate / employee portal
Teleportation portal mechanic used by Tangle employees after [[station 435 kravyad station]] to return to depot. Causes time displacement and memory side effects for passenger cars ("deep-cleaning") and is guarded or prepared by entities like the [[Kravyad]].
