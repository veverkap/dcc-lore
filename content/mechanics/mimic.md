# Mimic Mass Removal Rule
An explicit combat mechanic for large mimics: if attackers remove more than 50% of the mimic's total mass at once, the main body can no longer heal or transform and becomes vulnerable; separated pieces become independent attackers and only die when the main mimic dies. This rule is the key to fighting the [[station mimics]].
