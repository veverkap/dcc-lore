# mimic

* A massive, semi-building-sized monster in the [[trainyard]] whose minions (giant mouths with centipede legs and a long tongue) cause havoc. A persistent environmental threat the party tries to avoid while moving the cart.
* A shapeshifting monster whose corpse produced significant gore and dropped the [[Map of the Stars]]; the death of a mimic triggered further generator explosions.
* A creature type that physically alters outward from the mouth to imitate objects or beings; contrasted with doppelgangers and changelings.
* A broad shapeshifter category in the [[dungeon]]. Regular mimics take shapes of objects; shadow mimics are a dangerous, intelligent variant mentioned in the safe room.
* A city- scale monster that masquerades as part of the environment (building-like). Controls minions (giant mouths with centipede legs) and a long tongue; looms over the [[trainyard]] and drives much of the surrounding chaos.
* A level 90 city boss impersonator and primary monster opponent in [[trainyard]]. Uses massive tongue attacks, mouth-minions, and summoned mouth-creatures to assault [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]] and the area.
* A city-boss-level mimic on [[trainyard]]—presented as a level-90, apex [[predator]] and the floor's "[[crawler]]-killer champion." Drives the "Death Challenge!" headline and spawns large mouth minions; fights and nearly collides with [[Grull]] during the chapter.
* A massive city boss described as a building-like monster with enormous red eyes and a long tongue; its minions are giant mouths with centipede legs. Present in the [[trainyard]] and a major environmental threat the group must avoid.
* A specific combat mechanic for [[station mimics]]-type monsters: if more than 50% of the mimic’s mass is removed at once, the main body can no longer heal or transform and becomes vulnerable; severed pieces grow legs, attack independently, and only die when the main mimic dies.
* A creature that takes shapes of objects or creatures, with shadow mimics being a specialized, intelligent variant; changelings and doppelgangers are related archetypes.
