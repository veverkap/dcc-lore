# barracks

• The Barracks is a guild housing area where newly hired mercenaries are transferred upon hire, with capacity affected by guild [[upgrades]].
• The Barracks allows mercenaries to [[train]] and gain levels in the Mercenary Arcade, with a base capacity of 20 and an upgrade option to 100 for a high cost.
• The Barracks is a location where mercenaries can be retained and upgraded, as planned by [[Dungeon]] [[Crawler]] Carl.
• The Barracks is a newly built, mural-covered living and staging building adjacent to the castle entrance, where attackers mass and a key mural marks enemy positions.
• The Barracks is guild housing that receives newly hired Penis Parade mercenaries and is referenced as an upgradable facility for Meat Shields.
• The Barracks is the communal housing location for mercenary squads, with a base capacity of 20 and an upgrade option to 100 for a high gold cost.
• The Barracks is referenced by Elle McGib as a location where mercenaries live, and by Bigs as a comparison to their earlier living conditions.
