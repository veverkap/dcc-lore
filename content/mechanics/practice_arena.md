# practice_arena

• A paid facility associated with saferooms where crawlers can summon totem cards in a limited, mock combat environment.
• Opponent monsters summoned by the system lack decks and cannot directly harm players; consumable cards used here are permanently expended.
• Designated zone where players can test and play with their cards and decks.
• Facility included in [[saferoom]] locations where squads can, for a fee, summon totem cards in limited mock battles.
• Opponent monsters have no decks and cannot directly harm players, but consumable cards used will be lost permanently.
• Designated combat/training zone where players might test their decks and cards.
• [[Zev]] limits time for entry before meetings.
