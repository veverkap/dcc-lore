# Colonel Boomer
A grizzled, veteran elf and Elven Legate (Level 62) who commands the [[106th Bloody Leeches]] and is a colonel in the [[Princess Posse]]. Noted for war gauntlets similar to the [[Grull gauntlet]] and a loud, abrasive personality; coordinates assault plans with [[Tran]] and the narrator [[dungeon crawler carl]].
