# lacuna

• The Lacuna is an egg-crate or sheet of bubble wrap-like world outside individual bubbles.
• [[Orthrus]] runs through the Lacuna while the party pursues from the [[air]].
• The Lacuna is a named floor/area, referenced as the ground origin for some crawlers firing spells at [[Orthrus]].
• The Lacuna is a fog-filled expanse between bubbles on the fifth floor, collecting popped-bubble gore, summoned feral gods, janitor mobs, and dangerous terrain (water, dark fog).
• The Lacuna is a void-like space surrounding the bowl and bubbles, where feral gods and summoned deities clash, and is used as a vantage point to observe lights, roars, and beasts.
• The Lacuna is the in-between world outside the bubble-domed worlds, described as a spread of sunken, egg-carton-like bubbles and the zone through which [[Orthrus]] bounds and the party flies.
• The Lacuna is a named floor/zone referenced as the origin point of some ground-based crawlers who attacked [[Orthrus]].
• The Lacuna is a dark, fog-filled basin area between bubbles, where spilled gore and hazards accumulate, and is traversed by [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]] and [[Donut]] during rescues, home to summoned feral gods and janitor mobs.
