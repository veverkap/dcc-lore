# Saferooms and Stairwells Map Rule
Map-level dungeon rule displayed on the Station #83 map: "Saferooms appear at all transfer stations. Stairwells appear at stations numbered 12, 24, 36, 48, and 72 on any colored line." Explicit dungeon mechanic that governs saferoom placement and stairwell locations across transit lines.
