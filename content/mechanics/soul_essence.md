# soul_essence

• A new interface resource [[bar]], Soul Essence, is introduced when the [[Scavenger’s Daughter Patch]] is equipped.
• The [[bar]] stores charged essence from melee kills and powers the [[Soul Reaper]] effect.
• The [[bar]] increases max capacity each time it is topped off and emptied.
• The [[bar]] is tied to divine item behavior and represents a persistent [[dungeon]] mechanic.
• The [[bar]] is revealed when [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]] equips the [[Scavenger's Daughter Patch]].
• The [[bar]] stores essence collected via the [[Soul Reaper]] and scales in capacity each time it's filled and drained.
• The [[bar]] is tied to item effects and carries implicit gameplay warnings about accumulation.
