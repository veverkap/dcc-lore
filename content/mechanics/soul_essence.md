# Soul Essence
A new interface resource bar introduced when the [[Scavenger’s Daughter Patch]] is equipped. It stores charged essence from melee kills; filling and draining the bar powers the [[Soul Reaper]] effect. The bar increases max capacity each time it is topped off and emptied. It represents a persistent dungeon mechanic tied to divine item behavior.
