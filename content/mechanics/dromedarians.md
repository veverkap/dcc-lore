# Dromedarians
A camel-like, NPC race common to arid regions. [[Dromedarians]] are described as strong, water-storing warriors who in this locale are sedentary and form town society (e.g., Hump Town), and who are in a stalemate with the [[Bactrians]] and [[gnomes]].
