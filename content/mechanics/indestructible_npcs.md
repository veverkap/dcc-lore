# indestructible_npcs

• Some NPCs regenerate minutes after death and are tied to quest-critical information or items, explaining why [[Growler Gary]] repeatedly resurrects.
• Certain NPCs regenerate or resurrect quickly and are functionally indestructible for quest reasons, often indicating they possess important knowledge or items.
