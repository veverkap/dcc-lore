# starting_line

• The position where teams line up before the race begins, including teams like [[Lady Dominators]], [[Free Love]], and [[Osvaldo]]’s group.
• The race start area where teams are lined up and begin the [[heat]].
• The immediate race/[[heat]] beginning point where transformations occur, the starting lights change from red to green, and the teams launch into combat.
