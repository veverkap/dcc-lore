# self-destruct_mechanism

• Active destructive system built into the [[Robot Donut]] that detonates when the [[Toy Back Panel]] dislodges.
• Its activation normally triggers a [[Interface Lock-Out Self-Destruct Warning]] unless an [[Interface Lock-Out]] prevents the alert.
