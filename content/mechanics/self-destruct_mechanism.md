# Self-Destruct Mechanism
Active destructive system built into the [[Robot Donut]] that detonated when the [[Toy Back Panel]] dislodged. Its activation normally triggers a [[Interface Lock-Out Self-Destruct Warning]] unless an [[Interface Lock-Out]] prevents the alert.
