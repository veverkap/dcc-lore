# fursona_skill

• A system skill that increases embodiment of the corpse/kangaroo suit as it levels, announced in-chapter with "Your Fursona skill is now level X!" and tied to unlocking behaviors and sensations.
• Integrates the wearer with the suit's sensations, tracked by the interface.
• Unlocks related content, such as [[Yiffing is unlocked!]], with level increases.
