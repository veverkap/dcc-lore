# mercenary_arcade

• The Mercenary Arcade is a faction-owned training [[arena]] where new hires [[train]] and faction members visit.
• It's a large training room attached to a club/guild where mercenaries and strippers spar against AI trainers and each other, featuring an AI drill sergeant and a magical healer with limits.
• The [[arena]] was purchased as an upgrade for the Meat Shields and is where hired mercenaries practice.
• It's also attached to the Club Vanquisher, where mercenaries practice to the death with AI trainers, spar, and use specialized healers and drills to gain levels.
