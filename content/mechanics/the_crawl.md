# the_crawl

• The Crawl is a season-long [[dungeon]]/collection event that categorizes populations, scrambles implants, and drives in-universe rules and exploitative systems.
• The Crawl is an overarching [[dungeon]]-run/game environment that continues to run "unabated," persisting despite in-scene events.
• Stopping the crawl or tripping the [[fuse]] would disrupt economic and political systems that rely on it.
• The Crawl is a planetary [[dungeon]] phenomenon that generates death, harvestable elements, and funds macro-AI production.
• The Crawl is a central location in which floors, levels, factions, and AIs operate, influencing conflicts about AI behavior, legal rulings, and the fate of crawlers like [[Hunter Iota]].
