# No-teleportation punitive rule (third-party teleport ban)
A Syndicate-imposed rule implemented as a punitive measure after [[loita]]’s death: no crawlers will be allowed to be teleported away by third parties until the next floor opens. Explicit dungeon-level restriction affecting show appearances and rescue mechanics.
