# pass-through_two-way_portal_gated

• Type: Pass-Through two-way [[portal]], gated by conveyance type and key holders.
• Can be passed with a player response of "Yes".
• Requires a gated conveyance and/or a key equipped or held outside of inventory, depending on type.
• Compatible keys are marked in the player's inventory.
• Connects local tracks to the distant [[train]] yard/root system.
• Switches on the far side alter track routing.
