# Pass-Through Two-Way Portal (gated)
System announcement describing a portal type: "Type: Pass-Through two-way portal. Gated by conveyance type and to key holders." Includes rules shown in the UI: a player "Can you pass this portal? Yes*" and explicit gating requirement: "You must be on a gated conveyance and/or a key must be equipped or held outside of inventory, depending on type. Compatible keys have been marked in your inventory." Controls connectivity between local tracks and the distant train yard/root system; switches on the far side alter track routing.
