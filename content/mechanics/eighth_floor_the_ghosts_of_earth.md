# eighth_floor_the_ghosts_of_earth

• The Eighth Floor is currently occupied by crawlers and features a memory replay of [[Earth]]'s surface on a given date, with ghostly people and tangible non-biological objects.
• The floor hosts regional folklore mobs and special bosses, and is subject to the [[Eighth Floor Rules (Cascadia Announcement)]] and [[Eighth Floor System Announcement]] rules.
• Non-biological objects are tangible, while recorded people and animals are ghostly.
