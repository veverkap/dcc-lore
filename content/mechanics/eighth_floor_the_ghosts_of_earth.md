# Eighth Floor: The Ghosts of Earth
The floor currently occupied by crawlers; its central concept is an exact replay ("memory") of Earth’s surface on a given date with ghostly people and tangible non-biological objects. Core subject of the [[Eighth Floor Rules (Cascadia Announcement)]].
