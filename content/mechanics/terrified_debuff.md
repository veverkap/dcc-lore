# Terrified (Debuff)
Debuff that roots affected characters in place (prevents movement) for a duration or until the source monster is killed. Applied to [[Paz]] repeatedly early in the escape and to [[dungeon crawler carl]] briefly, triggering the "Crybaby" achievement text.
