# [[Diwata (Circe Took)]]
A Level 250 Immortal deity whose true identity is Circe Took. Presents as a god giving birth to mantis offspring; vulnerable when stripped of soul-armor/protection and ultimately reverted to her mortal form and killed when targeted by allied spells like [[Laundry Day]].
