# Ysalte
A goddess worshiped exclusively by the Semeru dwarves and known by several epithets in the crawl: the [[Ysalte]] is also called the Downward Spiral, The Inevitable, and more vulgarly the Vinegar Bitch. An Earth-and-Water deity described as scheming and a trickster, she appears unpredictably near the gates of [[Larracos]] and is feared by crawlers. She has a single temple in the dungeon and is implicated in myths about [[Scolopendra]]’s attack pattern.
