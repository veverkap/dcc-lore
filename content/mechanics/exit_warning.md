# Exit Warning
A System Announcement encountered by [[Louis Santiago]] during the anomaly. Text: "Warning: You have exited the current playing field without a registered pass. If you do not immediately return to the playing area, you will be considered to have left the game. Please see <Error>." An explicit dungeon/system rule notifying players of an out-of-bounds state.
