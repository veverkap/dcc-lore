# explosion_imminent

• System notification that appears over volatile objects (e.g., [[Moonshine]]) when the environment ignites, counting down to an explosion.
• Timed system notification that appeared over [[Vernon]]'s moonshine bottle after the [[Fire Ants]] ignited the car, counting down to detonations that spread flaming liquid throughout the [[Employee Break Room]].
• Status mechanic displayed on [[Carl’s Doomsday Scenario]] and other in-game UIs indicating an imminent detonation, with timers driving the urgency of the chapter's events.
• Timed system warning displayed above volatile objects (e.g., [[Moonshine]]) when they are about to blow, with the [[Explosion Imminent]] countdown in the porter car signaling imminent area-wide damage and escalating the trap into a catastrophic event.
• Time-sensitive system notification displayed above [[Vernon]]'s [[moonshine]] jug during the [[Literal Fire Ants]] event, counting down to an explosion that affects the environment and players.
