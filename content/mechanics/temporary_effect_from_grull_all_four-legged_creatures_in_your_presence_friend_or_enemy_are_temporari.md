# Temporary effect from Grull: All four-legged creatures in your presence, friend or enemy, are temporarily given 2× power to their most powerful attack.
A system-style effect applied by [[Grull]] that temporarily doubles the power of four-legged creatures' strongest attacks in his presence; presented as an in-world effect notification.
