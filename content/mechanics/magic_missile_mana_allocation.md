# Magic Missile Mana Allocation
Explicit spell mechanic observed in-chapter: the [[Magic Missile]] spell (level five) allows the caster to allocate mana per casting in a range (three to six mana points), which affects output (number and power of missiles); caster intelligence can modify missile count at lower mana settings.
