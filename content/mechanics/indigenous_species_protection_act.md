# indigenous_species_protection_act

• Mandates awarding hobby potions to domestic-class crawlers (subsection 1256-C).
• Affects [[loot]] distribution and is an institutional rule.
• A systemic law/policy that influenced tactics and political decisions in the crawl's development.
• Mandates implant scrambling and other protections for natives once a crawl begins.
• Enforces post-collection lockdowns that Walk-On operations circumvent.
