# Miss Quill
Elderly Skyfowl NPC receptionist for the magistrate's office (displayed as "Miss Quill – Skyfowl. Level 30. Assistant to the Magistrate."). Guards access to [[Magistrate FeatherFall]]'s chamber, refuses to disturb the magistrate for non-Skyfowl victims, and reacts dramatically when presented with the [[Dead Hooker (Quest Clues)]].
