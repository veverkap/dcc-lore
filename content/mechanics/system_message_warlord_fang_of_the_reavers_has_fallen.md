# System Message: Warlord Fang of the Reavers has fallen
A system announcement that credits the death of [[Warlord Fang of the Reavers]] to a combination of actions (detonation by [[dungeon crawler carl]] via a bomb dropped by a wraith-possessed [[louis santiago]] under control of [[Juice Box]]). Specifies experience splits (51% to [[dungeon crawler carl]], 1% to [[louis santiago]], 40% to [[Juice Box]]).
