# crafting_room

• Room where [[Mordecai]] works on potions and crafting projects; mentioned as his likely location during the chapter.
• Adjacent room where [[Mordecai]] is working at his upgraded alchemy table analyzing potions and recipes.
• Location to which [[Mordecai]] fled during the commotion over [[Rend]]'s death and revival.
• Room adjacent to the [[saferoom]] where [[Mordecai]] uses his upgraded alchemy table and crafting gear (including the [[autoclave]]) to analyze and produce potions.
