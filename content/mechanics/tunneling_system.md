# tunneling_system

• The interconnected infrastructure controlled by [[plenty]] enables rapid travel across the galaxy.
• The Tunneling System is the only mechanism that can be shut off to stop the spread of the rogue AI and the deities.
• It was invented by [[plenty]] and is credited as a [[dungeon]]/engineering mechanism.
• The system is part of the collaborations between [[plenty]] and sponsor [[apothecary]], implying systemic importance to [[dungeon]] infrastructure and sponsor interests.
