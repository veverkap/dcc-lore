# court_of_the_ascendency

• The Court of the [[Ascendency]] is a location on a green [[hill]], where the transferred city will [[land]] and NPCs will settle.
• It is central to the Celestial [[Ascendency]] event.
• The Court is located on the Twelfth Floor.
