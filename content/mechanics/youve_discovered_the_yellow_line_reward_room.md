System announcement triggered when the party contacts the porter door; indicates discovery of a line-specific reward room and enforces the one-prize-per-crawler-per-line rule.

# You’ve discovered the Yellow Line Reward Room!
A system announcement that appears when players successfully locate the [[Yellow Line Reward Room]]. It indicates one-per-crawler, per-line reward rules and triggers the suitcase-selection interaction with the porter NPC ([[Pierre]]).
