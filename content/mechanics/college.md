# college

• The location housing the librarian elves who were kidnapped during the Faction Wars [[portal]] incident and had previously helped crawlers survive.
• Institution in [[Larracos]] tied to plot threads: [[Ghazi]] wrote letters to it, and [[Tish]] works there. Mentioned in backstory related to the [[Gate]] and possession events.
• A local surface institution threatened by [[Samantha]] as the [[target]] for the stolen [[nuke (stolen from Buster)]]; referenced as part of Samantha's revenge plan.
