# taint

• A status-effect game mechanic inflicted by certain mobs, such as [[Unvaccinated Clurichaun Rev-Up Consultant – Level 3]].
• While active, the afflicted cannot heal their health by any method.
• The Rev-Up smoothie recipe grants immunity to the [[Taint]].
• The [[Taint]] affects crawlers on deeper floors.
• The [[Taint]] is a timed detrimental status effect, with approximately one minute remaining.
