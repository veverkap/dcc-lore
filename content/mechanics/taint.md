# Taint
A status-effect game mechanic inflicted by certain mobs (e.g., [[Unvaccinated Clurichaun Rev-Up Consultant – Level 3]]). While active (timed countdown), the afflicted cannot heal their health by any method. Appears as a timed debuff and is treated as part of the dungeon's hazard rules.

# Taint
A dungeon debuff/disease mechanic feared by [[dungeon crawler carl]] and [[Donut]]. The Rev-Up smoothie recipe grants immunity to the [[Taint]], which affects crawlers on deeper floors.
