# field_hospital

• The newly-built medical area at the guild base where [[Katia]] and [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]] are prepared and monitored for memory-drug procedures.
• A medical facility near the front that becomes overrun during the incursion, prompting urgent rescue and triage attempts by medics like [[Bucket Boy]] and defenders like [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]].
• Medical facility the group intends to bring [[Louis]] to for further examination of the invasive [[Epitome Tagg]] presence and biomechanical organs.
