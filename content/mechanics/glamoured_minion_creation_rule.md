# Glamoured Minion Creation Rule
Explicit dungeon rule stated in-chapter: a glamoured minion can only be created by a vampire two sunsets after the victim was originally killed. The rule explains timing and mechanics for reanimated body-part minions and their potential to reunite into more dangerous constructs (e.g., [[Shambling Berserker]]).
