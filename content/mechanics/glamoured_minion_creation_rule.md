# glamoured_minion_creation_rule

• A glamoured minion can only be created by a vampire two sunsets after the victim was originally killed.
• The rule explains timing and mechanics for reanimated body-part minions and their potential to reunite into more dangerous constructs (e.g., [[Shambling Berserker]]).
