# dodge_room

• The [[Dodge]] Room is a guild training room in the newly-opened row of guild doors.
• It is currently locked and requires an access requirement to enter.
• The room is designated for the [[Dodge]] skill and is only accessible to Crawlers with a [[Dodge]] skill of Seven or better.
