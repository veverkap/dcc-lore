# abyss

Here is the consolidated markdown list:

• The massive pit/zone into which many colored lines empty; destination used by the team as part of a two-[[train]] tactic.
• A massive chasm/zone receiving trains, monsters, and debris via tuned [[portal]] carts; destination of Carl's [[train]]-trap operations and the place where many monsters are dumped or fall into due to [[portal]] routing.
• The dimensional/transit destination that [[portals]] can be tuned to; Carl keeps the Abyss [[Portal]] tuned to the Abyss as a safe routing option and to intercept mobs on the tracks.
• A doom-like [[teleport]] destination used when the [[Portal]] sends hostile mobs away; summoning destination referenced as the place gods or banished mobs are sent.
• The vast pit/zone the party targets by sending the Nightmare into it; central battleground whose collapse and generator overloads shape much of the chapter's action.
• Massive circular pit revealed above Abyss Station - 436; lined with catwalks and hundreds of glowing [[portals]]; filled with thousands of crashed [[train]] cars, fires, and jikininki janitor ghouls scavenging the wreckage.
• Final destination mentioned for trains that carry ghouls for disposal; referenced as where ghouls are sent.
• The perilous zone beyond the [[trainyard]] [[portals]]; used as a [[portal]] tuning option and as the major hazardous zone into which the abyss cart interacts.
• The downward void destination of the [[Portal]] to the Abyss, where Krakaren were being sent and where ghouls and other abyssal fauna like Wall monitor lizards were speculated to reside.
• [[Teleport]] destination used by the [[portal]] to remove enemies and bosses from the room; invoked strategically to clear threats or permanently exile summoned entities.
• The deep pit/[[dungeon]] hazard the group aimed for the Nightmare and [[train]] to detonate in, intended to kill masses of monsters and overload the [[ghoul]] system.
• Massive circular pit beneath the station, filled with wrecked [[train]] cars, fires, and swarming jikininki janitor ghouls; dozens or hundreds of glowing [[portals]] open into it.
• Central pit at the heart of the railway's spirograph pattern where thousands of [[portals]] open to trainyards; crawlable interior gangways are dangerous and collapse under repeated combat.
• The massive pit into which many colored lines empty; [[portals]] can be tuned to send crashed trains and mobs into this chasm; it is a central hazard used tactically in the party's plan.
• The massive pit/void into which [[portal]]-tuned carts and creatures are being dumped; central to the chapter's tactical operations, collection of hands, and the [[portal]]/black-[[hole]] effects that interfere with ropes and flying crawlers.
• The bottomless void the interdiction cart [[portal]] was tuned to; Krakaren and other monsters are funneled into the Abyss to remove them from the local stations.
• A [[portal]] destination used in Carl's summoning plan – the intended drop site for the mantaur so the summoned Grull would form on the far side and be unable to reach crawlers in the [[trainyard]] in time.
• The central pit at the center of the railway spirograph; hosts [[portals]] that require Hats to use and is the location where many crawlers become trapped.
• The [[teleport]] destination the party tunes their cart [[portals]] to in order to send intercepted monsters away; both of the party's interdiction carts are tuned to Abyss [[portals]] for protection.
• The void destination to which the suspended [[portal]] was tuned, used to funnel Krakaren monsters away from the station.
• The major vertical zone/floor-feature targeted for the sacrificial [[train]] run; site where the Maestro was teleported and where mass monster deaths were intended to collapse the [[ghoul]] system.
