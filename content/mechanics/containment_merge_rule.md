# Containment Merge Rule
Explicit dungeon/system rule in this chapter: "Any continuous contact for more than five seconds, and our containments would merge." Drivers must avoid continuous vehicle contact to prevent containment merging; a gray mount/caged creature intervenes once to break contact before five seconds elapse.
