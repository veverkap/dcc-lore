# [[Hurk]]
A gleener (fish-like alien) and veteran CrawlCon panelist. Floats in a virtual tank, speaks with a formal accent, designer of the Desperado Club and a perennial panel presence. Serves as a judge in the daycare art contest and mentors the narrator briefly.
