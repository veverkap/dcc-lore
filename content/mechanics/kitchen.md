# kitchen

• A basic, initially empty kitchen area in the communal personal space, showing a note "Food [[Upgrades]] Not Yet Purchased" until [[upgrades]] are applied.
• The safe-room kitchen where the spell book [[Oozy Form]] scurries, bangs on cabinets, and oozes slime; the [[Cleaner Bot]] retrieves the book from here.
• A featureless kitchen area in the communal space where party members place food and plates; marked in the interface as "Food [[Upgrades]] Not Yet Purchased."
• A compartment of the safe room where the wriggling [[Oozy Form]] spell book scurries into and bangs against cabinets; the [[Cleaner Bot]] retrieves the book here.
• Interior area inside the [[Truck]] that houses the engineered pulley-and-lever system for deploying [[ghoul]] cages and other defensive gear.
