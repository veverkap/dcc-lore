# Hello Crawlers System Announcement
The floor-wide broadcast recapping events and issuing rules and updates (including the patch banning storage of uncontained liquids and notices about party/entrance behavior and new viewer features) that governs player behavior before the level collapse.

# Hello Crawlers (System Announcement)
A broadcasted system message to all crawlers that serves as a second-to-last floor announcement: it previews the next floor’s scatter mechanics, party formation rules, introduces a paid viewer feature for subscribing to private messages, and announces an immediate inventory patch restricting liquids.
