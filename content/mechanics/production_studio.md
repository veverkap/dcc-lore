# Production studio
The on-set space where [[rosetta]] conducts interviews, including adjoining green-room areas where [[dungeon crawler carl]] and [[Donut]] sit. It contains floating screens and a spotlight, and is where the autograph signing and earlier incidents (e.g., with the [[Popov brothers]]) occurred.
