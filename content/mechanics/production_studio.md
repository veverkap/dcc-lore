# production_studio

• The on-set [[studio]] space where interviews take place, adjoining a green room and ringed by floating screens showing screencaps and footage.
• The space where [[rosetta]] conducts interviews, including adjoining green-room areas where [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]] and [[Donut]] sit.
• It contains floating screens and a spotlight, and is where autograph signing and earlier incidents (e.g., with the [[Popov brothers]]) occurred.
