# Eileithyia
Goddess of Childbirth and Female Pain appearing as a sponsored alien; Level 250 and invulnerable while in a temple. [[Eileithyia]] is sponsored by Huanxin Jinx of the [[Grixist Swarm]] / CEO of [[Icon Industries]]. She grants party boons (protection from spiritual possession, healing speed, constitution) and issues the system quest "Spiders" while privately communicating covert instructions to [[dungeon crawler carl]].
