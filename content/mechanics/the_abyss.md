# [[The Abyss]]
Massive, circular flaming pit reached via catwalks above [[Abyss Station - 436]]. Filled with thousands of crashed train cars and fires; numerous glowing portals ring the interior and disgorge train cars to the pit. Populated by scavengers such as [[jikininki janitor ghoul]].
