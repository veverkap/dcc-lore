# the_abyss

* The [[Abyss]] is a massive, circular flaming pit reached via catwalks above [[Abyss Station - 436]]. It's filled with thousands of crashed [[train]] cars and fires, and is populated by scavengers like [[jikininki janitor ghoul]].
* The [[Abyss]] is a one-way [[portal]]-connected dimension/region containing massive zombie-infested areas.
* It's a deep, dangerous zone featuring an abyssal [[portal]] and surrounding catwalks, where crawlers plan to travel up to reach [[Station 436]] and access named trains like the [[Nightmare]].
* The [[Abyss]] is a huge downward void accessed by the [[Portal to the Abyss]], where thousands of Krakaren are funneled and ghouls and other bottom-dwelling creatures live.
* It's the central pit of the railway network, the end point of the spirograph-like system, where crawlers trapped at the edge can escape only if they have the right hat or key.
* The [[Abyss]] is used in Carl's plan as a dumping destination for crashed trains and mobs via the interdiction cart's [[portal]] when tuned to [[abyss]] mode.
* It's a massive chasm/void destination used by tuned [[Rapid-response Cart]] [[portals]], where objects and creatures are being dumped (trains, monsters).
* The [[Abyss]] has black-[[hole]]-like effects near the center and is the [[target]] of the party's interdiction strategy.
* It's a distant [[dungeon]] plane used as a [[target]] destination for summoned entities, where the party attempts to send a summoned god ([[War God Grull]]) to distract or consume him.
* The [[Abyss]] is a major crawl landmark featuring an [[abyss]] [[portal]] and interior catwalks, where Carl directs [[daniel bautista]]'s group to head to and seek access to [[Station 436]].
* It's a major interstitial zone used for [[portal]] routing, where carts are tuned to and from the [[Abyss]], and it serves as an alternate traversal route.
* The [[Abyss]] is a [[teleport]] destination for the cart's [[portal]], used to remove summoned bosses from the local area but also the [[target]] of involuntary deity summonings.
* It's the deep pit zone/floor feature into which the [[train]] and [[Nightmare]] are sent to explode, home to Krakaren monsters and [[ghoul]] systems targeted by the group's plan.
* The [[Abyss]] is a colossal, nearly perfectly round flaming pit a mile across and thousands of feet deep, filled with thousands of crashed [[train]] cars, fires, and scavenging [[jikininki janitor ghoul]].
* It's used deliberately in Carl's two-[[train]] disposal/rescue plan, where objects are teleported over the pit and fall a great distance.
* The [[Abyss]] is the sink Carl's team uses to clear monsters and send resources away, and it's a [[target]] destination for [[portal]] carts and trains.
* It's a dangerous void connected by [[portals]], frequently tuned to the [[Abyss]] as a routing option, and used as an alternate transit route during the rescue.
* The [[Abyss]] is a lethal [[teleport]] destination used by the [[portal]] to remove mobs (or unwary crawlers) from play.
* It's a distant, hostile zone used as a [[target]] destination for summoned entities, where characters plan to send summoned [[Grull]] to engage local monsters (e.g., Krakaren).
* The [[Abyss]] is referenced as the place to dump mantaur vessels during summoning maneuvers.
