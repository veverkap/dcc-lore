# dwarven_industrial_light-duty_automaton

• Industrial contraption/robot found in the "boss" room, large and bulldozer-sized, in Sleep Mode; not intended for combat but can be dangerous when active.
• Two were found inactive and later destroyed for scrap, yielding scrap metal, damaged [[dwarven battery]], cogs, and wheels used for crafting.
• A model name for the pair of large, bulldozer-sized [[factory]] robots found in the [[warehouse]]/ boss-area at [[Banshee Station 116]], appearing inactive ("Sleep Mode") until destroyed and yielding scrap and components when blown apart.
• Designed for labor (pushing bins/ore) but salvageable for materials and cogs after being destroyed with [[Hobgoblin Dynamite]].
