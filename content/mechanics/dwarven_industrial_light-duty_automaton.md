# Dwarven Industrial Light-Duty Automaton
A pair of inactive, bulldozer-sized industrial robots found in the warehouse-like room. Labeled as contraptions in "Sleep Mode," not initially hostile; when destroyed, they yield scrap metal, damaged [[dwarven battery]], cogs, and wheels used for crafting.
