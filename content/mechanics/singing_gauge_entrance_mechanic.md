# Singing Gauge Entrance Mechanic
A dungeon rule: certain songs in the [[Karaoke Song Binder]] bear a secret mark that engages an entrance sequence to the [[Stage Performance Guild]]. A magical gauge (red→yellow→green) appears above singers, and only those who keep the needle in the green for the entire song can enter the guild.
