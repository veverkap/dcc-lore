# madness_castle

• [[Madness castle]]: strategic castle referenced as a rendezvous point, destination linked to the [[gate]]'s spot number, and targeted by Carl's raid.
• Located within [[Madness territory]], focal point of missile bombardment and opposing host's mustering.
• Primary enemy [[stronghold]] toward which Carl's force is marching; back side factors into corpse/Atrocity-magic strategies.
• Strategic [[target]] proposed by party members (Dong and Jefferson) to seize while enemy forces are distracted and absent from the field.
• Small rectangular [[stronghold]] containing [[barracks]], porous foundation, hidden rolling [[garage]]-style door, and operating [[theater]] where Architect Houston and others are located.
• Named [[dungeon]] location from which Carl has been freed, referenced in the current tactical situation.
