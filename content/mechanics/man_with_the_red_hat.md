# Man with the red hat
An older man in a bright-red trapper hat who works at the homeless shelter breakfast line and speaks directly to [[dungeon crawler carl]], opening a crucial interaction at the chapter’s end. He is identified by the graffiti message that instructed visitors to look for “the red hat with the flaps.”
