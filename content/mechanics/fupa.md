# fupa

* The base of operations and protective [[stronghold]] where [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]] and his allies gather, shielded by a semicircular barrier and hosting the hospital tent.
* The internal castle area/region accessed via the bait shack/ safe-room corridor, with a long corridor of multiple doors due to heavy [[crawler]] traffic.
* A base area associated with [[Donut]] where several allies, including [[Tipid]] and [[Rosetta]], are located, with access to saferooms closed off from the protagonist.
* A location where prisoners, including [[Milk]], are held, mages operate, and serves as a defensive and logistical hub for the protagonists.
* A vehicle or [[transport]] used to move prisoners and allies after the raid, part of the escape route back to the [[saferoom]].
* Primary [[headquarters]]/base that [[Tipid]] defends, targeted by [[Dreamer]] teleportation and mentioned as the seat of operations and staging ground for the offensives.
* A local [[battlefield]] area experiencing heavy fighting and demon eviction operations described in troop reports.
* A named site referenced in troop movements and defense, part of the operational map.
* A location referenced as a past drop [[target]] for a Reaver airship, used as contextual history for the looted troop [[transport]]'s origin.
