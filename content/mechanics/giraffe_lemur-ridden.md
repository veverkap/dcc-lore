# Giraffe (lemur-ridden)
A large beast encountered in the chase, ridden and swarmed by multiple [[Former Circus Lemur]] attackers who fling lemurs toward [[dungeon crawler carl]] and [[Donut]] and use the giraffe as a mobile platform in the assault.
