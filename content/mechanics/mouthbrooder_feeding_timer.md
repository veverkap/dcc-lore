# Mouthbrooder Feeding Timer
An explicit encounter rule revealed in the broadcast graphics: [[Lusca]] is a mouthbrooder and grants roughly ten minutes for her babies to feed on swallowed prey, represented by an on-screen ten-minute countdown that directly affects survivor outcomes.
