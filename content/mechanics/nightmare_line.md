# nightmare_line

• A dark-sounding transit line suggested by [[Princess Donut]] as a path to explore; explicitly named as a route on the [[the tangle]] that some characters fear to ride immediately.
• One of the [[train]] lines on the [[the tangle]] floor discussed by the party as a dangerous route to explore.
• One of the named transit lines on the fourth floor; [[Donut]] suggests riding the [[Nightmare line]] to explore its route despite its ominous name.
