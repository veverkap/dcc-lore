# employee_line

• A secret secondary route providing alternate movement and supply access from certain stations to [[trainyard]] or other lines.
• A segmented track line used by staff/employee carts, containing different station types and guarded entrances, targeted for routing the Def Leppard cart to deeper levels.
• A parallel rail route used for movement between stations, referenced as being guarded and difficult to access during the breach.
• A rail line that leads to deeper levels, [[target]] location where the Def Leppard cart must be placed facing deeper floors to manipulate encounters and access employee tracks.
• A secret service/access line discovered by Carl's party, providing an alternate route and resupply/refresh opportunities from inside stations like Station 36.
• A restricted track segment leading to deeper levels, objective for the party to get the Def Leppard cart onto, requiring reorientation and gauge-matching under heavy enemy presence.
• A secret escape route accessed via a hatch discovered near the [[stairwells]], used to resupply defenders holding Station 36.
• A specific set of tracks intended for deeper-level access, the group's [[target]] location for orienting the Def Leppard cart to send the [[portal]] toward deeper floors.
• The path used to return to Station 36 after the explosion, synonymous with the service route for maintenance/[[portal]] operations.
