# ultima_corp_dungeonwerx_industrial_subspace_portal

• [[Ultima Corp DungeonWerx Industrial Subspace Portal]]: a gated, one-way subspace [[portal]] with explicit system rules, displaying warnings about facing the incorrect side and [[portal]]-compatibility checks.
• Requires being on a gated conveyance or holding a compatible key (e.g., [[Ochre Key]], [[Steam [[Train]] Key]], or [[Conductor's Souvenir Hat]]).
• System analysis screenshots reveal the fenced [[Trainyard (engine-car [[storage]])]] on the other side.
• Classified as a [[dungeon]] mechanic with enforced traversal conditions.
