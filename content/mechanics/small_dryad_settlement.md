# small_dryad_settlement

• A [[town]] encountered by [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]]'s group, described as a dryad community with a designated [[saferoom]] and visible dryad guards that can become hostile.
• The [[town]] includes a [[saferoom]], dryad inhabitants, funeral bell guards, and the [[Y-Not Lounge]].
