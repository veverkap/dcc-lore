# Jacobus
"The Reverse Tooth Fairy," a formerly Legendary card summon now permanently active due to Carl's [[Scavenger’s Daughter]] patch. A Level 90, tiny but muscular Danish fairy mob with currency-demanding tooth-stealing behavior; he was an infamous terror in past Faction Wars and is now a permanent summoned entity hovering around [[Imani]].
