# no_mans_land

• The approximately 250-meter-wide contested plain between the opposing [[trenches]], strewn with traps, illusions, and artillery-impact craters.
• The exposed [[battlefield]] between opposing [[trenches]] that the [[Princess Posse]] must cross during the charge; filled with fog, walking explosions, and the path of the [[Party Planner]] craters.
