# blister_ghoul

• A Level 20 undead mob type spawned by [[ghoul]] generators, described as tenacious and produced continuously by soul- crystal-powered devices on the floor.
• Spawned continuously by a [[Ghoul Generator]] when non-undead mobs die on the floor.
• Noted for tenacity and being created in large numbers, contributing to the [[tunnel]] horde.
• Created by a [[Ghoul Generator]] for every non-undead mob that dies on the floor.
• A specific variety of [[ghoul]] mentioned in the daily announcement as having its running speed nerfed.
• Seen swarming the tracks around station 72.
