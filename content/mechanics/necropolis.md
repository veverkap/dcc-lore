# necropolis

* The Necropolis is a [[beach]]-side fortress/structure under siege by Gwen's team, beset by mobs and a borough-boss-level bird that harasses siege efforts.
* The underground [[catacombs]] below [[town]] begin to flood, threatening Morris' team and prompting a scramble for water-breathing scrolls and safe rooms.
* The Necropolis is a location where changelings (including some principals) might be searching for a ghost tied to Henrik.
* The massive tomb/[[volcano]]-shaped structure forms much of the chapter's backdrop, carved with bird-like motifs and forming the adjacent wall to the [[sandcastle]] and drainage systems.
* The flooded Necropolis zone is adjacent to the [[Akula]], referenced as the location the submarine was pulled out of and where earlier encounters (including changeling principals) took place.
* The partially submerged [[dungeon]]/region is tied to the plan to summon the feral god, the [[target]] area and broader risk zone Carl's team is trying to avoid endangering.
* The drained area on the [[land]] [[quadrant]] is where Orthrus was rescued and where the party operates, with Carl ordering Gwen and Tran to drain it before returning to saferooms.
* The fortress-like structure on the [[beach]] is being besieged by Gwendolyn Duet's team, containing sand and seashell walls and being harassed by a borough-boss bird.
* The tomb complex beneath [[Town]] Hall is being flooded and whose exits have been sealed following prior castle captures, home to trapped tomb-raider crews.
* The tomb complex and burial structures are the site of the aerial battle, including [[temple]] roofs and a [[quadrant]] border that affected the fall of the protagonists.
* The Necropolis is a nearby zone where changelings and principals search for a ghost tied to earlier story beats, with water flooding noted as a complication.
* The [[subterranean]] catacomb system is a dangerous objective referenced as a likely next [[target]], with players debating whether to tackle it or clear the [[Land]] [[Quadrant]] first.
* The massive tomb/[[volcano]]-shaped structure at the edge of the [[land]] [[quadrant]] has exterior carved with bird-like motifs and is home to nests for the buzz-ards.
* The Necropolis is an overarching locale referenced as having interior debris mixed with the castle's wreckage during the collapse, providing environmental context for the falling bodies and ruins.
* The tomb complex/zone is connected to the [[Akula]] (the submarine was pulled free of a [[hole]] in the side of the Necropolis), origin of the initial mission and source of the ghostly threat.
* The half-submerged [[dungeon]]/[[tower]] is a central destination central to the story's larger objectives, with Carl intending to [[teleport]] near the Necropolis entrance and noting the Map of the Stars shows bosses' positions within it.
* The [[temple]]/tomb area on the island is the site of the boss Quetzalcoatlus and the lightning [[tower]] mechanic.
* The drained [[land]] [[quadrant]] on the party's bubble is where Gwen and Tran attempt to fully drain via the drain mechanic before returning to a [[saferoom]].
