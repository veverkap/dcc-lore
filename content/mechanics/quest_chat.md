# [[Quest Chat]]
A special in-game communication channel (distinct from regular chat) used during the event; displays group messages from affected crawlers (e.g., an angry message from "Quan Ch") and shows the list of participants.

# Quest Chat
A special system chat room that appears during group quests limited to all players inside the bubble; used for coordination and publicly displays player statuses (e.g., [[Maggie My]], [[Chris Andrews 2]], possessed/enslaved markers).
