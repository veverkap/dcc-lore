# Loot Boxes
The loot distribution system governing item drops, tiers (e.g., [[Gold Box]]), and season-influenced outcomes. The chapter emphasizes that loot boxes are not truly random and can be manipulated by show and sponsor incentives — a written rule and a practical mechanic in the crawl.
