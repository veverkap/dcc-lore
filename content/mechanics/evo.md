# Evo
Pale, drifting program director who greets guests before the live broadcast of [[Danger Zone with Ripper Wonton]]. Identifies himself as a member of the [[Forsoothed (Soothers)]] and briefs panelists about other guests, including [[Princess D'nadia]] and [[Ripper Wonton]].
