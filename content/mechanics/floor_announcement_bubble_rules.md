# floor_announcement_bubble_rules

• 1,172 bubbles, each divided into [[Land]]/[[Sea]]/[[Air]]/[[Subterranean]] quadrants
• Four castles per bubble
• [[Stairwells]] unlock only after all four castles in a bubble are conquered
• Distribution of crawlers per bubble
