# Floor Announcement: Bubble Rules
System announcement that explains fifth floor mechanics: 1,172 bubbles, each bubble split into Land/Sea/Air/Subterranean quadrants, four castles per bubble, stairwells unlock only after all four castles in a bubble are conquered, and distribution of crawlers per bubble.
