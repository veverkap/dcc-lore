# literal_fire_ants

• Trap monster released from a [[Prize Case]] (Level 1), unleashing thousands of flaming ants that spread fire rapidly and overwhelm occupants.
• Triggered from a [[Prize Case]] (the ant trap inside Donut's suitcase), the ants burst forth in enormous numbers, spread flame, and cause cascading explosions when igniting nearby volatile items (e.g., [[Moonshine]]).
• Released from a [[Prize Case]] suitcase, the swarm of flaming ants spreads fire through the [[Employee Break Room]] and triggers secondary explosions.
