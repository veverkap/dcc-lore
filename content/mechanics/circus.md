# circus

• A visible destination several streets away containing large tents, music, and roaming giraffes; the party's exploration [[target]] and potential source of clowns and other threats.
• The ruined location where the prior quest took place, with a hidden [[stairwell]] to the [[fourth floor]] appearing in the park after quest completion.
