# Level Gain / Skull Indicator
System mechanics signaled in this chapter: players display level changes and special skull indicators when leveling up (e.g., [[Katia]] rises from level 24 to 37 and shows a golden skull). These are in-game rules/indicators of experience and rank shifts within [[Dungeon Crawler World]].
