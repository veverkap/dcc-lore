# Wasteland Castle
A large floating gnome island/fortress (also called the gnome castle) visible from [[Hump Town]] using the [[Enchanted Gnome FarSeerer]]. Covered with red-hatted gnomes, balloons, flying machines, and attached weapons and bombs like the [[Gnomish Knock-Knock]].
