# wasteland_castle

• A large floating gnome island/fortress, also called the gnome castle, visible from [[Hump Town]] using the [[Enchanted Gnome FarSeerer]].
• Covered with red-hat gnomes, balloons, flying machines, and attached weapons and bombs like the [[Gnomish Knock-Knock]].
• A massive floating gnome island/fortification observed via the [[Enchanted Gnome FarSeerer]].
• Held aloft by colossal balloons, bristling with weapons, moored flying craft, and gnome activity.
