# station_283

• A transit station that appears multiple times on the [[the tangle]] map, where different colored line intersections occur; referenced as a possible transfer point for backtracking using the Nightmare Express.
• The landing/station where Carl, Donut, and the party wait for the [[Nightmare Express]]; labeled in the chapter header and is the immediate location from which the attempted boarding occurs.
• A high-priority transfer stop on the Nightmare Express and Mauve line; referenced as a waypoint for the party’s faster routing to investigate stop 436.
• The landing point called out in the chapter header ("The Nightmare Express Landing - Station 283") where [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]] and company wait for the [[Nightmare Express]] to arrive.
• The specific [[platform]] and station location where Carl and company wait for the [[Nightmare Express]]'s second pass; the chapter opens at this landing.
• The specific station location where Carl and his party wait for the [[Nightmare Express]]; displayed in the chapter header and used as the staging point for the boarding attempt.
