# Holy Crusade
Event/quest-type mechanic referenced by NPC dialog that modifies bounty splitting rules and imposes temple/god-aligned distribution of rewards for kills (context: bounty on Prepotente interacts with worship of [[Hellik]] and [[Emberus]]).
