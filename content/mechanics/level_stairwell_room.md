# level_stairwell_room

• The glowing doorway/entry adjacent to the ooze core leads to the level [[stairwell]] and grants access deeper into the floor progression when cleared.
• The doorway/entrance next to the TV leads into the chamber and provides access to the ooze boss area and the room that can be opened by defeating the boss.
