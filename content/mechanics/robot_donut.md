# Robot Donut
A robotic companion (sometimes called "Princess Donut the Queen Anne Chonk") owned by [[dungeon crawler carl]]. Central to the commercial segment and the accidental explosion that mortally wounds [[Loita]]. Modeled as both a real crawler device and a marketed toy on [[Veriluxx New Toys Showcase]].

# Robot Donut
Carl’s popular robotic pet/streaming personality. Central to the commercial appearance on [[Veriluxx New Toys Showcase]] and the target of a toy replica (the Veriluxx Donut doll). She provides comic relief, interacts with the hosts [[Gravo]] and [[Liddi]], and is present during the trailer explosion.
