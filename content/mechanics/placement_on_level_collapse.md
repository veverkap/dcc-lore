# Placement on Level Collapse
An explicit dungeon rule announced in [[Hello, Crawlers!]]: if a crawler goes down within six hours of level collapse they receive no head start but will get placement; going down "too early" results in being placed at the back of the pack. Affects spawn/placement timing after floor collapse.
