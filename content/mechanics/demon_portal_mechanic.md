Dungeon rule/mechanic describing demon portal attacks: lesser (zombie) demons can banish themselves by touching one soul and opening a short portal; full-strength demons open larger temporary portals that often require two souls to return and can suck up surrounding entities.

# [[demon portal mechanic]]
A dungeon mechanic revealed in the chapter: lesser "zombie demon" variants require one corporeal soul to return (one touch) and depart immediately, while full-powered high-level demons (e.g., level 140 princes) produce longer-lasting portals and require two souls to send them back to [[Sheol (Floor 15)]]. This explains differing portal durations and lethality.
