# demon_portal_mechanic

• Lesser "zombie demon" variants require one corporeal soul to return (one touch) and depart immediately.
• Full-powered high-level demons (e.g., level 140 princes) produce longer-lasting [[portals]] and require two souls to send them back to [[Sheol (Floor 15)]].
