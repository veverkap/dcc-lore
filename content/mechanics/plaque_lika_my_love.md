# Plaque: Lika, my love.
An in-world readable inscription on the fountain display (rendered by the system for the player). The system text explains that the glass wizard’s research facility was transmuted, includes the dedication “Lika, my love,” and functions as a lore-bearing System Announcement encountered during exploration.
