# Sponsored Advertisement Announcement ('Never trust your life to Gordon Waystation Outfitters')
A system announcement read aloud when [[Everly]] picked up the paper tent; the ad revealed the tower was a sponsored trap and included the line "Never trust your life to Gordon Waystation Outfitters," marking the structure as sponsored by [[Dictum Waystation Controls, Limited.]].
