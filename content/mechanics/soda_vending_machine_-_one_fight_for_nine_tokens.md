# Soda Vending Machine - One Fight for Nine Tokens
A branded vending machine that enforces a dungeon rule: “One Fight for Nine Tokens.” Its small-print warning states that if you leave after activating it you must deposit nine more tokens to reactivate, and that only nine tokens exist in the area. It functions as the trigger and rule-setter for the trailer encounter and prevents re-entry in certain conditions.
