# soda_vending_machine_-_one_fight_for_nine_tokens

• A branded vending machine enforces the [[dungeon]] rule "One Fight for Nine Tokens".
• Its small-print warning states that if you leave after activating it, you must deposit nine more tokens to reactivate, and that only nine tokens exist in the area.
• It functions as the trigger and rule-setter for the [[trailer]] encounter and prevents re-entry in certain conditions.
