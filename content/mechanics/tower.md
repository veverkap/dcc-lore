# tower

• A defensive structure of the castle referenced by [[Sledge]] when trying to gain access; a focal point for external fighting and collapsed rubble blocking reinforcements.
• The location of an earlier attack by [[Donut]] that caused the narrator to develop a persistent hand tremor; part of past traumatic events influencing current behavior.
