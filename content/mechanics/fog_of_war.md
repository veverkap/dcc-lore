# Fog of war
A map mechanic that hides details of areas the player hasn't visited. Safe rooms still appear on the map when the player is near them despite the fog. Mentioned by [[Mordecai]] as part of navigation mechanics.

# Fog of War
A dungeon mechanic described in-chapter: dot visibility shrinks with speed (the faster the [[goblin copper chopper]] moves, the larger the blind zones), causing mobs like [[Scat Thugs]] to remain undetected until close-range and affecting navigation and tactical choices.
