# fog_of_war

• A map mechanic that hides details of areas the player hasn't visited, except for safe rooms which remain visible when the player is near them.
• Mentioned by [[Mordecai]] as part of navigation mechanics.
• Dot visibility shrinks with speed, causing blind zones and affecting navigation and tactical choices, as seen in the movement of the [[goblin copper chopper]].
• Mobs like [[Scat Thugs]] remain undetected until close-range due to the fog.
