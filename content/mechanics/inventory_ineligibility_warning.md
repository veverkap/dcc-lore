# Inventory Ineligibility Warning
An explicit system announcement that appears when an item becomes ineligible for inventory: "Warning: An item in your catalog is no longer eligible to be held in inventory. It will be forcibly removed in five seconds." This announcement triggered the removal/de-crystallization sequence that caused the [[Lika Love Doll Head]] to be ejected from Carl’s inventory.
