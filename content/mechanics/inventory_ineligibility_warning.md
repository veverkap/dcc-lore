# inventory_ineligibility_warning

• Warning: An item in your catalog is no longer eligible to be held in inventory. It will be forcibly removed in five seconds.
• This announcement triggers the removal/de-crystallization sequence, causing items to be ejected from inventory.
• The [[Lika Love Doll Head]] was forcibly removed from Carl's inventory due to this warning.
• The warning is an active system announcement that interrupts communication and enforces inventory rules, attributed to [[borant corporation]]'s updates.
