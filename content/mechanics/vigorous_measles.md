# vigorous_measles

• A named disease mechanic tied to the Rev-Up immunity smoothie, granting protection from Vigorous Measles and [[Taint]].
• Declares an infection status when a tentacle emits a green mist, which can be negated by immunity.
• Broadcasted as a prominent System Announcement in the fight.
