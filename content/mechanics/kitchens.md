# kitchens

• The club [[kitchen]] area where [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]] hides, deploys automatons, and where [[Charles Ellis]] is killed; connected to secret [[stairwells]] and back rooms.
• The back-of-house area in the [[desperado club]] where [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]] hides, readies [[Spider Automaton]] units, and where much of the gore and NPC sheltering happens.
