# System Announcement: Third Phase
The official broadcast (by [[Cascadia]]) declaring the start of the third and final phase of the floor. Announces that the [[level stairwells]] are open, describes placement behavior for non-keyholders, states battles will be card battles, and closes with the explicit directive to engage other crawlers.
