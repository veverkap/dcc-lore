# Iron Tangle System Announcement
The official system message broadcast that declared the [[the tangle]] system broken, stated the self-destruct sequence, opened emergency escape tunnels, and ordered customers and employees to proceed to specified stairwell portals (stations 12, 24, 36, 48, 72, or 433) with an 18-hour open window followed by six hours before charges blow. This announcement triggered mass movement and defensive actions.
