# iron_tangle_system_announcement

• The official system message declared the [[the tangle]] system broken, initiating a self-destruct sequence.
• [[Emergency]] escape [[tunnels]] were opened, and customers and employees were ordered to proceed to specified [[stairwell]] [[portals]] (stations 12, 24, 36, 48, 72, or 433) within an 18-hour window, followed by six hours before charges blew.
