# emberus_high_temple_of_the_ending_sun

• Secret [[Emberus]] [[temple]] within [[Club Vanquisher]] featuring a burning, roofless field, a boiling fountain, and charred stump used in clerical sacraments.
• Where [[Pater Coal]] performs rites on [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]] and where the [[Martyr’’s Path]] boon is granted.
• The secret, roofless [[temple]] interior accessed from [[Club Vanquisher]] where [[Pater Coal]] conducts rites.
• A fiery, open-field shrine environment that grants the [[The Martyr’’s Path]] boon and restricts divine eavesdropping during sacraments.
