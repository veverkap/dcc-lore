# Emberus High Temple of the Ending Sun
Secret [[Emberus]] temple within [[Club Vanquisher]] featuring a burning, roofless field, a boiling fountain, and charred stump used in clerical sacraments. Where [[Pater Coal]] performs rites on [[dungeon crawler carl]] and where the [[Martyr’s Path]] boon is granted.

# Emberus High Temple of the Ending Sun
The secret, roofless temple interior accessed from [[Club Vanquisher]] where [[Pater Coal]] conducts rites. A fiery, open-field shrine environment that grants the [[The Martyr’s Path]] boon and restricts divine eavesdropping during sacraments.
