# abyss_gate

• The enormous [[portal]], [[Abyss]] [[Gate]], is located just after [[station 435 kravyad station]] and is used by engineers to [[teleport]] back to the depot.
• The [[portal]] is associated with time-loss and memory side effects for passenger cars handled by the [[Kravyad]].
