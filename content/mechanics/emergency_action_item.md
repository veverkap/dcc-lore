# Emergency Action Item
Special, limited-use proposal processed directly by the [[System]] without a participant vote; teams have a small allotment and such items can delete NPCs, disallow participation, or target teams and crawlers—examples proposed by [[Warlord Empress D’Nadia of the Prism]].
