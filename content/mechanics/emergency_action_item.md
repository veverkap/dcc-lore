# emergency_action_item

• A special, limited-use proposal processed directly by the [[System]] without a participant vote; teams have a small allotment and can use them to delete NPCs, disallow participation, or [[target]] teams and crawlers.
• A system-level provision giving teams limited, high-impact options (teams receive three [[emergency]] requests).
• A formal, rule-enforcing system mechanic used by teams to enact immediate [[dungeon]]-level policy changes, such as bans on two-way tunneling and feed access; can be proposed and accepted during council sessions.
• A special, limited type of [[Action Item]] that the system can resolve without a vote; teams have only a few of these and they can perform sweeping changes (e.g., deleting NPCs or removing teams).
• System mechanic allowing warlords to propose up to three unvotable [[emergency]] items decided automatically by AI; used by [[Princess Posse]] to force immediate resolutions and present votes to viewers.
