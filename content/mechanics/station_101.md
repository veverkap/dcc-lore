# station_101

• A station where [[Frank]] initially disembarked and claims to have remained near the [[desperado club]] since.
• A Vermillion Line station with a thousand crawlers boxed in and being swarmed by mobs and ghouls, the primary rescue [[target]].
• A station near the line where crawlers were concentrated before being diverted toward the [[stairwell]] platforms.
• The [[platform]] on the [[Vermillion line]] where a thousand-plus crawlers are boxed in by mobs and where [[Hekla]] plans to pick up trapped survivors, including [[Li Jun]]'s crew.
• The destination toward which the [[train]] is racing, holding the main horde and a site where many crawlers are trapped.
• Origin point for some of the party (Imani, Li Jun, and others) and part of the network feeding into [[Station 36]] fortifications.
• Another subway station referenced as where [[Frank]] says he got off before staying at the [[desperado club]].
• The crowded and besieged stop on the [[Vermillion line]] where approximately a thousand crawlers are boxed in and being overwhelmed by mobs; [[target]] of the rescue mission.
• Destination toward which the party is accelerating; a focal point for a large approaching horde and strategic timing in the chapter.
• A station mentioned as earlier being a focal point for [[ghoul]] movement before survivors redirected mobs toward [[Station 36]].
• A station mentioned as where [[Frank Q.]] disembarked and where the [[desperado club]] is located; Frank claims he has not left this station since.
• Primary crisis site on the [[Vermillion line]] where roughly a thousand people (including known crawlers like [[Li Jun]]'s crew) are boxed in and fighting swarming mobs; the objective of [[Hekla]]'s rescue [[train]] plan.
• A central station targeted by the approaching main horde; many non-player survivors are trapped here, and the group speeds toward it.
• A station with trapped crawlers that ghouls initially targeted before survivors were led to [[Station 36]].
• Origin point for part of the group; mentioned as the station where Imani, Li Jun, and others came from before fortifying [[Station 36]].
