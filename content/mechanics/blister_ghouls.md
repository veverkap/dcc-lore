# blister_ghouls

• Blister ghouls are a specific [[ghoul]] variant with their running speed nerfed in daily balance changes.
• They are common aggressive mobs that pour into [[tunnels]] from other stations, such as [[Station 72]], and are used as fodder for training and a persistent threat to stations like [[Station 36]].
• They can appear mixed with Krakaren waves, and killing living Krakaren can generate ghouls via nearby [[ghoul]] generators.
• They are a named variant of ghouls that appear mixed with Krakaren waves, noted in frontline combat descriptions.
• They converge on stations, notably moving from [[Station 72]] and mixing with other threats at [[Station 36]].
