# Temporary Situationally Generated Space
Dungeon system mechanic describing a generated transit space used when multiple non-attached personal spaces are combined and made accessible to visitors (how [[Hekla]]'s headquarters becomes reachable from [[dungeon crawler carl]]'s personal space). Mentioned by [[dungeon crawler carl]] as a known system note.
