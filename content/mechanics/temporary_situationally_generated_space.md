# temporary_situationally_generated_space

• A generated transit space used when multiple non-attached personal spaces are combined and made accessible to visitors.
• Example: [[Hekla]]'s [[headquarters]] becomes reachable from [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]]'s personal space.
