# liana_district

• The Liana District is a region on the elf side of the [[river]], home to a changeling village.
• It is accessible via the bridge and northern [[gate]], and is the primary access point after bridge destruction.
• The district is also the location of an ambush around the covered bridge.
• The high-elf familiar mentions the Liana District as the elves' primary concern.
• The district is south of the infested [[river]] and is referenced as the location of a changeling village where Bonnie and the changelings traveled.
• The [[town]]'s bridges are currently constrained due to destroyed bridges, making the [[town]] a choke point and access point to the Liana District.
• The high-elf speaker references the Liana District as the area of concern for the elves' hunt.
