# system_passage_closure_announcement

• A system message announced the closure of a system passage, stating "You may now make your way downward. The path you used upward is no longer available."
• This altered the [[dungeon]] connectivity, trapping [[Everly]] and her party inside the [[Eighth Floor]] configuration.
