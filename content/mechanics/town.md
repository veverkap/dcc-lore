# town

• The settlement where the party shops, searches for mushrooms and items, and is told to avoid alleys and the surrounding [[jungle]].
• The beleaguered settlement under artillery fire, featuring a cracked entrance wall, leveled buildings, a small park, and nearby [[river]] access.
• The raid locality (village/map node) where the assault, traps, and the appearance of [[Diwata]] occur.
