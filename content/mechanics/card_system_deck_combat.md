# [[Card System (Deck Combat)]]
Dungeon rule and system announced by [[Cascadia]] that introduces deck-based combat. Defines deck composition (minimum six totems), card types (Totems, Utility, Snare, Mystic, Special), mechanics for summoning, timing, discard rules, squad leader restrictions during deck combat, and practice constraints in the [[Practice Arena]] and [[Saferoom]].
