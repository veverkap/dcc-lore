# card_system_deck_combat

• [[Dungeon]] rule and system announced by [[Cascadia]] that introduces [[deck]]-based combat.
• Defines [[deck]] composition (minimum six totems), card types (Totems, Utility, Snare, Mystic, Special).
• Introduces mechanics for summoning, timing, discard rules, squad leader restrictions during [[deck]] combat.
• Practice constraints in the [[Practice Arena]] and [[Saferoom]].
