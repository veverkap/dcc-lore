# hell_portal__ascension_portal

• The Hell [[Portal]]/Ascension [[Portal]] is a system-driven [[portal]] event triggered by a timer reaching zero.
• Living things and items are sucked upward when the [[portal]] opens.
• To transition to the next floor, positioning below enclosed [[stairwell]] chambers is required.
