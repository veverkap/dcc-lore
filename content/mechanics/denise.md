# Denise
"The Feral Goose Mother," a Level 53 Borough Boss originally from the seventh floor (descended from [[Emperor Anser]]'s people). An environmental boss that suppresses magic and protects children obsessively; driven nearly mad by the Nothing. Defeated by being shoved into a garbage disposal during the timed [[Borough Boss]] fight in [[Bonnie's House]].

# Denise
Known as the Feral Goose Mother, a Level 53 Borough Boss and an "enviromental" type creature. Brought from another world (her grandfather is [[Emperor Anser]]), she suppresses magic ("No Magic" effect) and exhibits protective behavior toward children while violently attacking intruders. Defeated in this chapter via the kitchen garbage disposal.
