# warning_you_have_at_least_one_consumable_card_in_your_deck_this_card_will_be_lost_if_used_in_this_ar

• Consumable cards used in the [[Mock Battle Arena]] are permanently consumed.
• System announcement reminds players of this upon entering the [[arena]].
• Players are notified about consumable card loss upon [[arena]] entry, affecting decisions about using [[Combo]] or [[flee card]].
