# Hunting Season / Bounties
A mechanic activated on the leaderboard: top-10 Crawlers each have bounties displayed and can be hunted for loot boxes. Survivors receive a percentage payout of their bounty at floor collapse; the mechanic explicitly opens reward-based player-versus-player incentives.
