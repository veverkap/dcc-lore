# earth

• Home world of [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]] and origin of many cultural references in the chapter; location of the production [[trailer]] and the surface event that causes the broadcast interruption and subsequent political fallout.
• The planetary body shown on the [[Containment Interface]]'s top-down solar system map, marked with a blinking/red dot under "Primary Zone Influence" that begins to grow after [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]] disengages containment.
• The protagonist's home planet and the subject of [[Rishi]]'s petition claiming the protagonist as a de-facto native leader with rights under [[Syndicate]] law.
• Referenced as having a controller system and protections (e.g., implant scrambling) under laws like the [[Indigenous Species Protection Act]] that affect how walk-on trackers operate.
• The human planet undergoing an early season initialization by the Borant System Government; the [[emergency]] call orders population movements, roofed-structure evacuations, and warns of edifice collapse during the season timer.
