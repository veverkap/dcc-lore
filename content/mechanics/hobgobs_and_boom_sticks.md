# Hobgobs and Boom Sticks
A merchant booth/shop at the back of [[The Silk Road]] run by the hobgoblin proprietor [[Pustule]]. Sells explosives, smoke devices, detonators, and related alchemical supplies.

A single booth-style shop in the Silk Road market run by [[Pustule]]. Specializes in explosives and related materials, including cases of [[hobgoblin dynamite]], [[Hobgoblin dynamite]], [[hob-lobber]], barrels of [[Gunpowder]], and various smoke devices.
