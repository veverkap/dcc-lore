# hobgobs_and_boom_sticks

• A merchant booth/shop at the back of [[The Silk Road]] run by [[Pustule]], selling explosives, smoke devices, detonators, and related alchemical supplies.
• Specializes in explosives and related materials, including cases of [[hobgoblin dynamite]], barrels of [[Gunpowder]], and various smoke devices.
