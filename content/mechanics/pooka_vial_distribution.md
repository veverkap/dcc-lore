# Pooka vial distribution
Explicit dungeon mechanic describing the five-stop cycle: at every stop ending in 5 or 0, monsters queue by race to receive a race-specific vial from a [[Pooka]] through a slot; vials (the [[Rev-up Vitamin Shots]]) are addictive and time-limited, and failure to receive one causes physical transformations and escalates floor instability. This rule governs monster behavior across the [[Ochre Line]].
