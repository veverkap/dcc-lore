# pooka_vial_distribution

• Monsters queue by race to receive a race-specific vial from a [[Pooka]] at every stop ending in 5 or 0.
• Vials (the [[Rev-up Vitamin Shots]]) are addictive and time-limited.
• Failure to receive a vial causes physical transformations and escalates floor instability.
• This mechanic governs monster behavior across the [[Ochre Line]].
