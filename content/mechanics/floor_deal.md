# Floor Deal
The standardized contractual offer repeatedly presented to crawlers on floors (notably the [[Tenth Floor]] and [[Eleventh Floor]]): one season indenture as a game guide, full medical, a life stipend, and a gag order. A systemic pavilion of the dungeon economy that many accept.
