# phase_two_rules

• Automatic [[teleport]] at Phase Two start
• Sixty-second squad leader location choice
• Immediate transfer
• Loss of non-active totems
• Six-totem limit enforced
• Marked key location 10–15 km away with 48 hours to retrieve a key
• Keyholder teleports in Phase Three
• Option for keyless squads to block/steal keys from others
