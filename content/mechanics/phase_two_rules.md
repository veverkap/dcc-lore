# Phase Two Rules
System announcement content (delivered by [[Cascadia]]) defining Phase Two and Phase Three behavior: automatic teleport at Phase Two start, sixty-second squad leader location choice, immediate transfer, loss of non-active totems, enforced six-totem limit, a marked key location 10–15 km away with 48 hours to retrieve a key to open an adjacent stairwell, and Phase Three teleport/exit mechanics including keyholder teleports and the option for keyless squads to block/steal keys from others.
