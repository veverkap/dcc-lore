# Safe Room Mob Teleport Rule
Dungeon rule/behaviour where mobs are ejected/teleported out when a [[Safe Room]] door is opened, relocating them a short distance away and resetting their pursuit; exploited by the party to manipulate the [[Rage Elemental]]'s position.
