# the_bar

• The [[Bar]] is a crowded club/ [[bar]] where the performance and most of the chapter's action take place, featuring a [[stage]], DJ booth, tables, and entrance.
• It is a chaotic tavern/ karaoke venue where the fight erupts, the DJ and booth are located, the [[stage]] is set, and the secret door in the side [[bar]] begins glowing after the performance.
