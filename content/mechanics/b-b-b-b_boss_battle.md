# b-b-b-b_boss_battle

• A system notification that freezes the world and spawns the boss encounter UI, including the "[[VERSUS]]" splash and the boss title card for [[gore-gore the mantaur]].
• A loud system/judging announcement that marks the activation of boss music and spectacle mechanics for the encounter.
• A system/announcer callout used to hype the boss phase of the encounter (audible/ music cue).
• In-game boss-battle sequence notification heralding an encounter with [[Ruckus]], accompanied by music and the floating portraits UI.
• UI mechanic announcing the start of a boss encounter sequence; triggers music, floating portraits, and altered descriptions for [[Ruckus]] during the engagement.
• System mechanic that freezes the world and displays the enraged-NPC boss interface when [[gore-gore the mantaur]] turns hostile, showing portrait, title, and battle start.
