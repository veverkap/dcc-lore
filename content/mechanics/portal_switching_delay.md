# portal_switching_delay

• Toggling a cart's [[portal]] destination requires about ten seconds, during which the [[portal]] is off and the cart is vulnerable, affecting tactical routing (e.g., choosing [[Abyss]] vs. [[Trainyard]]).
• The [[portal]] switching delay is about ten seconds, during which the [[portal]] is effectively off and the cart is vulnerable, affecting tactical choices when routing carts.
• [[Dungeon [[Crawler]] Carl]] accounts for this delay when changing routes between [[The Abyss]] and trainyards.
