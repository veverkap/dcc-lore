# Portal Switching Delay
Dungeon/system rule observed in the chapter: toggling a cart's portal destination requires about ten seconds, during which the portal is off and the cart is vulnerable. This delay affects tactical routing (e.g., choosing [[Abyss]] vs. [[Trainyard]]).
