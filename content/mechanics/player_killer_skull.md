# player_killer_skull

• A [[dungeon]]/system mechanic indicating player-[[versus]]-player killing penalties.
• A visible in-interface status marker above characters indicating player-killer status or [[bounty]], appearing as skull icons and carrying social/mechanical weight in interactions.
• A punitive game mechanic applied for player-[[versus]]-player killing, referenced in the context of [[Carl]] killing [[Quan]] without receiving the "player killer skull".
