# Entrance to the fourth floor
The stairwell doorway leading down to the [[Fourth Floor]], marked by an oversized Kua-Tin carving and labeled "Entrance to the fourth floor." The party intends to pass through it with seconds remaining on the bomb timer to escape the impending magical detonation.

# Entrance to the fourth floor
The door at the bottom of the stairwell with an oversized Kua-Tin carving. Displays in-game properties to [[dungeon crawler carl]] ("Entrance to the fourth floor. This is where the real fun begins. Mind the gap.") and is the portal the group enters as the chapter ends.
