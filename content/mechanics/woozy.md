# woozy

• A debuff type that can induce comatose or woozy status from projectiles, used by opponents and can be enhanced by spells.
• A debuff afflicting [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]] after a fight, impairing consciousness and requiring removal before submerged activities.
• A debuff effect induced by a crossbow bolt that put [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]] into a coma-like state, enhanced by the ring/Pony's spell.
• The debuff currently affecting Carl, making him prone to passing out, and must be cured before he dives into water to recover submerged corpses.
