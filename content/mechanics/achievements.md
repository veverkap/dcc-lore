# achievements

• Game system awards achievements with rank indicators like bronze stars, displayed as part of a large wall of notifications following the boss's death.
• System announcements trigger achievements during chaos, flooding Carl's HUD and contributing to cognitive overload.
• System-awarded recognitions, such as [[Katia]] earning an achievement for invention, are displayed to players and part of the active system interface.
• Gameplay mechanic records milestones, with characters like [[Louis]] and crew receiving achievements for flying the magical balloon.
• A game mechanic category referenced when [[elle mcgib]] and others receive multiple awards and level gains after enhanced spellcasts, triggering notifications and progression effects.
• System mechanic used to track and reward in-game accomplishments, with [[Carl]] receiving half a dozen achievements after the fight with [[Prison Pocket]].
