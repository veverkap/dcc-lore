# the_club

• The venue where the [[Two Changelings]] were ejected, with prior disruptions (blocked door, cracked sink) referenced by [[Orren the Liaison]].
• The main club area where the team staged before entering the [[temple]], with Li Na's aura filling the space and the team's other doors remaining propped open into the club during the encounter.
• A venue where non-protected guests blinked out and then regenerated during the broadcast, with some civilians trapped and stand-ins taken.
