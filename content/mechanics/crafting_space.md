• A large, empty room repurposed by the party into a [[workshop]] with the [[Alchemy Table]], [[Sapper’s Table]], and [[Engineering Table]] for item creation and experimentation.
• The room is filled with deployable workbenches, including the [[Alchemy Table]], [[Sapper’s Table]], and [[Engineering Table]], where the party crafts and experiments.
• The room is converted into multiple workstations, including the [[Alchemy Table]], [[Sapper’s Table]], and [[Engineering Table]], where the party assembles gear and experiments with system changes.
• The room is outfitted by [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]] with the [[Alchemy Table]], [[Sapper’s Table]], and [[Engineering Table]] for item creation and modification; where [[Mordecai]] studies the crafting system.
