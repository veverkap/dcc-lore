# Phase Two
A system rule described by [[Cascadia]]: begins when timer hits 12:01 AM on December 24th; all players are teleported to memory-simulation rooms, squad leaders choose locations within 60 seconds, non-active totems are lost, deck size constrained to six totem cards, a map key location spawns 10–15 km away and must be retrieved within 48 hours to open the adjacent stairwell.
