# Divine Epiphany
An achievement for seeing a deity; grants the option to worship [[Grull]] and unlocks an interface tab. Common among long-surviving crawlers.

# Divine Epiphany
Achievement: "You have seen a deity." Marks a crawler’s sighting of a god and grants the option to worship that deity (in this chapter, grants the option to worship [[Grull]]).
