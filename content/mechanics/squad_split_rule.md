# squad_split_rule

• System mechanic that automatically splits parties larger than five into squads of five or fewer for the [[Eighth Floor]] starting process; squad membership remains but certain challenges are squad-limited.
• Parties with more than five members are automatically split into squads of five or less (remain in party but may be constrained for certain challenges); squads cannot be voluntarily broken on the floor.
