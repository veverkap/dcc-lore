# Employee Portal (Abyss Gate)
An explicit dungeon rule/device described by [[gore-gore the mantaur]]: the train's engine pushes through a large portal past stop 435, teleporting the engineer and engine car back to the depot while the passenger cars are processed. The portal causes time loss and memory side effects for passengers, and the returned cars undergo "deep cleaning." Presented as a direct-game mechanical effect (transport + side effects) and guarded/managed in part by the [[Kravyad]].
