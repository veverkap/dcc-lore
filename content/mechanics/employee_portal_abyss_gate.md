# employee_portal_abyss_gate

• The Employee [[Portal]] ([[Abyss]] [[Gate]]) is a mechanic that allows the [[train]]'s engine to [[teleport]] back to the depot while the passenger cars are processed, causing time loss and memory side effects for passengers, and resulting in "deep cleaning" of the returned cars.
• The [[portal]] is described by [[gore-gore the mantaur]] and is guarded/managed by the [[Kravyad]].
