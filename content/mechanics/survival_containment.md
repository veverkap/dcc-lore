# Survival Containment
A system-announced rule for the heat: an indestructible translucent bubble that surrounds a vehicle or mount to protect from toxic air. It cannot be destroyed (for this heat) except through specific conditions (e.g., vehicle destruction or mount death), and its loss triggers survival consequences and potential race permutations.
