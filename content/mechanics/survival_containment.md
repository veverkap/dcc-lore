# survival_containment

• A system-announced rule for the [[heat]]: an indestructible translucent bubble surrounds a vehicle or mount to protect from toxic [[air]].
• The bubble cannot be destroyed (for this [[heat]]) except through specific conditions (e.g., vehicle destruction or mount death).
• Losing the bubble triggers survival consequences and potential race permutations.
