# action_item

• A system proposal submitted before a floor opens that can change rules; can be voted on by participants and is publicly visible when rejected by the system (examples appear on Odette’s show).
• Allows participants to vote on rule changes before a floor opens; items can be voted until the moment the floor opens and are visible when rejected by the system after voting.
