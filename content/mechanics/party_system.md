# [[Party system]]
The game's ownership and minion mechanics governing how minions like [[Bomo]] can be assigned, converted to personal minions, or duplicated as clockwork copies (as with examples [[Clay-ton]] and [[Very Sullen]]), and which determine which generated doors and saferoom behaviors apply.
