# party_system

• The Party System is a mechanic that determines minion affiliations and behaviors based on ownership and control.
• Minions like [[Bomo]] can be assigned, converted to personal minions, or duplicated as clockwork copies.
• The system governs generated doors and [[saferoom]] behaviors.
