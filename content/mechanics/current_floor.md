# Current Floor
The floor on which [[dungeon crawler carl]], [[Donut]], and [[orren the liaison]] are located and which is described as collapsing soon. [[orren the liaison]] plans to leave upon its collapse, and the floor change is connected to procedural uncertainty and the [[Litigation Freeze]].
