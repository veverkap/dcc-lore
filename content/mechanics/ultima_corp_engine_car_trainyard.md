# ultima_corp_engine_car_trainyard

• The destination of the [[Ultima Corp DungeonWerx Industrial Subspace Portal]] is the [[Ultima Corp Engine Car Trainyard]], a massive yard containing only engine cars behind high fences.
• [[Ghoul]]-like creatures are inside the engine cars, while dwarves wait outside.
• The yard contains a small swirling [[portal]] and equipment such as rolling cages.
