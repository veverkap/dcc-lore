# Pied Piper Spinner Outcome: Dick will fall off!
System spinner mechanic presented by [[Eris]] with two possible outcomes ("Dick will fall off!" and "Level-15 Pied Piper"). The spinner determines temporary or permanent effects tied to the random encounter.
