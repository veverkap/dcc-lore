# [[Meat (vendor stall)]]
A food stall in [[Hungry Eyes Vendor Village]] labeled simply "Meat." Sells mystery skewers that cannot be inspected due to the system restriction [[No food in Hungry Eyes may be examined. Good luck.]]; commonly patronized by racers like [[Jurgen]] and [[Prepotente]].
