# meat_vendor_stall

• A food stall in [[Hungry Eyes Vendor Village]] labeled "Meat" that sells mystery skewers.
• The skewers cannot be inspected due to the system restriction [[No food in Hungry Eyes may be examined. Good luck.]].
• The stall is commonly patronized by racers like [[Jurgen]] and [[Prepotente]].
