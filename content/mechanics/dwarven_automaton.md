# dwarven_automaton

• A massive construct that patrols the exterior of [[Larracos]] and the [[Halls of the Ascendency]].
• Dwarven automatons are engineered constructs used for digging or siege tasks, associated with resupply and fortification logistics.
• They are presented as massive constructs, with one example being a patrol automaton that guards the exterior of the [[Halls of the Ascendency]] and surrounding fields.
