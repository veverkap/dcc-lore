# Scavenger's Daughter System Announcement
An active system announcement triggered when [[dungeon crawler carl]] fills his [[Essence Bar]]: "The Scavenger’s Daughter has been fed. Unleash her wrath." This announcement unlocks [[The Daughter’s Kiss]] and signals a dangerous, game-level effect tied to the filled resource.
