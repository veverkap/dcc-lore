# Where the Sidewalk Ends
New regional quest (system announcement) that adds all crawlers in the [[Cuba region]] and designates the local squad as host. Outlines three options—do nothing, free [[Amayon]] from his shell, or kill him—and includes a visible timer that starts counting down two hours.
