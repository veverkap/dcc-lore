# where_the_sidewalk_ends

• New regional quest in the [[Cuba region]] that designates the local squad as host.
• Presents three options: do [[nothing]], free [[Amayon]] from his shell, or kill him.
• Includes a visible timer that starts counting down two hours.
• Rewards include the [[Celestial Hell Boon]].
• Creates a World-quest escalation mechanic if certain options are chosen.
