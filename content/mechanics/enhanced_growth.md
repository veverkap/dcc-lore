# Enhanced Growth
A buff/condition applied to [[Princess Donut]] that altered her race stats: an initial boost to four random stats, then on each level-up grants +1 Strength, +1 Intelligence, and +2 Charisma; no other stat points on level-up. Also enforces fixed low Constitution (2) and fixed Dexterity (8) unless changed by gear or a race/class change.
