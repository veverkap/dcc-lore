# Warlord Empress D’Nadia of the Prism
The empress and primary antagonist in the chapter: a Saccathian ruler with tentacles, multiple built-in shields, and water/ice-themed magics. She is incapacitated, keeps her health artificially lowered to deny certain effects, and is the target of Carl's marking and interrogation.
