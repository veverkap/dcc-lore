# New Achievement: Sex Pervert!
An in-game achievement system announcement triggered when [[dungeon crawler carl]] pierces his nipple to fit a magical nipple ring. The chapter presents the achievement text and its ironic "Reward: Whores don't get rewards." as part of the system's feedback to player actions.
