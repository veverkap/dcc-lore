# simulation_failed

• System announcement triggered when practice bout ends in a loss condition, providing feedback and ending combat, with [[deck]] resets.
• Announcement indicates simulation would have resulted in real deaths, triggered by certain conditions, such as a player or ally being struck.
• Example: Triggered after [[Donut]] is slammed by a wolf and the simulation terminates.
