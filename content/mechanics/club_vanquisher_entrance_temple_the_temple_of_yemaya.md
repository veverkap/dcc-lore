# club_vanquisher_entrance_temple_the_temple_of_yemaya

• The in-game [[temple]]/club location becomes accessible after [[Yemaya]] is re-ascended.
• Carl can enter as a worshipper of [[Emberus]], while some companions (like [[Donut]]) cannot.
• The [[temple]]/location is entered after [[Yemaya]]'s shrine is repaired.
• It acts as a club/[[temple]] hybrid with entry gated by worship status.
• The location contains services such as the "All Healer."
