# Eleventh Floor Arena
A mysterious arena located on the eleventh floor referenced by the system/AI. Certain mobs killed on that floor may be sent there (via audience vote) or replaced with stand-ins from outside the dungeon ecosystem (e.g., from the [[Eighteenth Floor Resort]]).
