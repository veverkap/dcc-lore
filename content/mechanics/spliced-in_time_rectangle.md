# Spliced-in Time Rectangle
A roughly twenty-foot rectangular patch of the yard that has been transplanted from a different temporal instance (summer) into the winter setting of the rest of the floor. Contains half-cut objects, labeled flower pots (alliums, daffodils, etc.), and the [[One Fight for Nine Tokens]] vending machine to force spectators to view a specific event.
