# spliced-in_time_rectangle

• A roughly twenty-foot rectangular patch of the yard transplanted from a different temporal instance (summer) into the winter setting.
• Contains half-cut objects, labeled flower pots (alliums, daffodils, etc.).
• The [[One Fight for Nine Tokens]] vending machine is present.
