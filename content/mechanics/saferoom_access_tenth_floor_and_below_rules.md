# [[Saferoom Access (Tenth Floor and Below Rules)]]
A dungeon rule change activated after the quest failure: all saferoom/personal-space access on the floor is governed by older "tenth floor and below" rules tied to the [[blood bar]]. It enforces timed occupancy and teleports or denies access if timers expire, pressuring players to remain active.
