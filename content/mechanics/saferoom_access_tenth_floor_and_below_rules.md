# saferoom_access_tenth_floor_and_below_rules

• A [[dungeon]] rule change activated after the quest failure: all [[saferoom]]/personal-space access on the floor is governed by older "tenth floor and below" rules tied to the [[blood bar]].
• Timed occupancy is enforced, and access is teleported or denied if timers expire, pressuring players to remain active.
