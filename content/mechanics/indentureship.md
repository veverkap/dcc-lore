# Indentureship
A contractual mechanic determining trainers' obligations and freedom across seasons. The chapter states that a trainer like [[Mordecai]] only reduces his indentureship if one of his crawlers reaches the fourth floor; otherwise he remains bound until a future [[borant corporation]]-sponsored season.
