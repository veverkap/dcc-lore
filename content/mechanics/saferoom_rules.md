# saferoom_rules

• [[Dungeon]] rule subset active during the [[ceasefire]]: allows spell use but restricts hostile actions against other living players within city limits and provides temporary protections in safe zones.
• Saferooms are integrated into strongholds, the blood [[bar]] system reactivates limiting hiding, and mercenary access to guildhalls is restricted during final phases (only a small staff of up to nine allowed).
• Floor-specific safety mechanics active during the [[Ceasefire]]: restrict PvP within city limits, allow limited spellcasting without freezing by the AI, and provide access to personal mailboxes and [[saferoom]]-exclusive items.
