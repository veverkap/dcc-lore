# Temple of Emberus loan rule
System-enforced debt rules revealed when the protagonist takes a faith-backed loan: no renouncing of religion while under debt, requirement to trade long-worn jewelry, accelerated payment if the ring is lost, curse activation if expelled from the church, and restrictions on selling the item.
