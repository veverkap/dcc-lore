# temple_of_emberus_loan_rule

• System-enforced debt rules revealed when taking a faith-backed loan: no renouncing of religion, trading long-worn jewelry, accelerated payment if the ring is lost, curse activation if expelled from the [[church]], and restrictions on selling the item.
