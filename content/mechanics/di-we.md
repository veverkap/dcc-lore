# Di-we
A three-headed nodling and the progenitor ogre who died protecting [[Signet]] from agents of her sister. His original body is used as an ogre tattoo on [[Signet]] and his knowledge is distributed among the offshoots [[Areson]], [[Apollon]], and [[Herman]].
