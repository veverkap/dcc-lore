# goblin_bomb_bard

• [[Goblin Bomb Bard]] is a [[goblin]] mob type encountered in the chapter, specializing in explosives and ranged explosive attacks and flagged as particularly dangerous.
• They ride [[Goblin Copper Choppers]], wear spiked metal helmets, and wield lit explosives like [[Dynamite]].
• They fuel or arm a local conflict and lead assault sorties in the scene.
