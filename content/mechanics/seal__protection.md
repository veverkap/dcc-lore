# [[Seal / Protection]]
Active protective/dungeon mechanic around [[queen imogen of the high elves]] that prevents her from leaving the ballroom; a strategic constraint that affects [[dungeon crawler carl]]'s plans and the timing of Signet/Sledge operations.
