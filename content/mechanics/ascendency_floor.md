# ascendency_floor

• A higher-level floor where many NPCs, including the city of [[Larracos]], were transferred during the events of [[Faction Wars]], delaying their return until later floors.
• A named floor/zone where NPCs were transferred after a spell, mentioned as the likely place they won't be seen again until the [[Twelfth Floor]].
