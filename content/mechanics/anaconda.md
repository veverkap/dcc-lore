# anaconda

* A Level 75 Quarter Naga lead dancer at the [[Penis Parade]] with dark purple skin and snake-like features, mentioned as one of [[Donut]]'s favorites.
* A purple- skinned quarter- naga dancer at the club who weeps over events, one of the named performers on the top level.
* A dark quarter- naga stripper and brother to [[Damascus Steel]], part of the Penis Parade crew and involved in the family narrative around [[Astrid]] and the club's politics.
* Child of [[Hamed]] and sibling in the [[Guild of Suffering]] lineage referenced in connection with the Desperado Club.
* Sibling of [[Damascus Steel]] and child of [[Hamed]], mentioned as a stripper purportedly searching the club for their father.
* [[Crawler]] and brother to [[Damascus Steel]], together they went to hunt their father, who became [[manager]] of the [[desperado club]].
* Named missing stripper from the Desperado Club, being searched for by party members in the chapter.
* Named performer/ dancer referenced as the reward for a free dance after a set number of visits to the [[desperado club]], part of local entertainment culture.
* Lead dancer at the Penis Parade in the [[desperado club]] mentioned by Sister Ines, part of [[Donut]]'s colorful social anecdotes.
* A named NPC/performer who converses with [[Donut]], cited in a contested clip that contributed to the newcomers' suspicion.
