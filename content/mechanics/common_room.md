# common_room

• A communal space adjoining the [[Guildhall]] and [[Safe Room]]; the cleaner bot is pressed against its door listening to the events inside.
• The central room of the [[Guild]] where the meeting and the pet-carrier procedure take place, containing the shop-interface terminal and adjacent [[Pet Stables]].
