# standard_subspace_portal

• A Standard Subspace [[Portal]] is a [[dungeon]] UI/[[portal]] mechanic identified by the [[Valtay Corporation Neural Enhancer #544. Variant 32.c]] upgrade, appearing as a tooltip over doorways with the option to analyze ("Standard Subspace [[Portal]]. Analyze? Yes/No.") and can reveal passability, environment compatibility, gating, and visual feeds.
• The [[portal]] can be analyzed via the interface, showing type, gating, and environment compatibility, and is unlocked for viewing after [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]] installs the upgrade.
• The analysis provides a visual snapshot of the [[portal]]'s far side, such as showing the coffee shop and [[Nodd]].
• The [[portal]] is a two-way, gated type, and its analysis is unlocked after [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]] installs the neural enhancer.
