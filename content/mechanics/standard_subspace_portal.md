# Standard Subspace Portal
A dungeon UI/portal mechanic identified by the [[Valtay Corporation Neural Enhancer #544. Variant 32.c]] upgrade; appears as a tooltip over doorways ("Standard Subspace Portal. Analyze? Yes/No.") and can be analyzed to reveal passability, environment compatibility, gating, and visual feeds (enabled for [[dungeon crawler carl]] after installing the upgrade).
