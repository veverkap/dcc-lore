# Inle
Deaf and mute sea-associated god whose image appears at [[Shrine of Inle]]. Repairing his shrine grants constitution boosts and immunity to many health-seeping conditions; lore notes he will end up back in the Nothing and will not ascend.

# Inle
An Orisha, deaf and mute and partially mad from time in the Nothing. Repairing his shrine grants immunity to many health-seeping conditions and +50% Constitution; he will not ascend and is destined for the Nothing.
