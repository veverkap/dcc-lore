# sirin_station_-_81

• Station on the [[Red Line]] where the party first sees mobs pouring onto the [[platform]] (Dreks) and where cars are disembarking/arriving.
• Labeled on [[train]] signage when the party enters the car.
• A crowded station where the [[train]] stops and swarms of [[Drek]] attack the cars.
• Labeled on the [[train]]'s display and marked as the immediate danger point that forces the party back into the [[train]].
• [[Train]] stop where the party first notices heavy mob presence (hundreds of [[Drek]] on the [[platform]]).
• Labeled on station signs and announced over the [[Red Line]] loudspeaker.
