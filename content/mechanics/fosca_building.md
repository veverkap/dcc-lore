# fosca_building

• A location referenced by [[Sister Ines]] as a meeting place; [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]] does not know its position and is warned to avoid direct engagement with the nun.
• A multi-floor structure in which [[Sister Ines]] is located several floors below a serpent shell, identified as the site beneath the snake and part of the neighborhood Carl's group is approaching.
• A specific building location where [[stairwell]] collapse trapped [[Imani]] and [[Tran]]; the northern façade and several floors are destroyed, exposing multiple levels and beams that serve as combat arenas.
• A building cited by [[Sister Ines]] as a meeting point; Carl does not know its location when asked to come.
• A specific building in the neighborhood where [[Sister Ines]] is located, several floors below a large snake/demon, a focal point for rescue and assault plans.
• The multi-level urban structure where much of the combat occurs; its [[stairwell]] and many floors collapse during the demon assault, creating trapped pockets (where [[Imani]] and [[Tran]] are stuck) and exposing multiple battle zones on exposed girders and debris.
