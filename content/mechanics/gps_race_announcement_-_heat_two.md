# gps_race_announcement_-_heat_two

• An active [[GPS]] system announcement provides the official race rules for the upcoming [[heat]].
• The [[heat]] distance is 10 km, with an asphalt track featuring rolling hills.
• There are two randomized paths with gatekeeper monsters.
• Vehicle path restrictions are in place.
• Extreme weather conditions include torrential rain and giant hail.
• The time limit for the [[heat]] is three hours.
• Task requirements include killing gatekeepers to resume racing.
• The announcement governs allowed actions, crew/mercenary rules, and upgrade/reset mechanics.
