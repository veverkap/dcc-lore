# GPS Race Announcement - Heat Two
An active [[GPS]] system announcement that provides the official race rules for the upcoming heat: distance (10 km), track type (asphalt with rolling hills), two randomized paths with gatekeeper monsters, vehicle path restrictions, extreme weather (torrential rain and giant hail), a three-hour time limit, and task requirements (kill gatekeeper to resume). This announcement governs allowed actions, crew/mercenary rules, and upgrade/reset mechanics for the heat.
