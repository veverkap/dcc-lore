# System Protection
A dungeon rule and protection package that begins on higher floors: managers and certain NPCs receive system-based protections (different from saferooms) starting on the fourth floor, meaning death can result in being kicked from the game rather than permanent removal and affects incentives for managers like [[Mordecai]].
