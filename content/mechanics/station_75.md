# station_75

* A sprawling industrial-style station featuring a small-cale yard of flatbed cars, warehouses, workshops, a [[bar]] called [[The Downward Dog]], and an intact [[saferoom]].
* Location on the Vermillion Line with unspecified trouble and a [[ghoul]] generator at [[Station 72]].
* Stop where [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]] intends to detach the engine car before driving it back toward [[Station 36]].
* Location where [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]] retrieves two interdiction carts before delivering one to [[Station 36]] (the Def Leppard cart).
* Nearby station with "something going on" and contributing to the complexity of the situation around [[Station 101]].
* Transit station on the line where many corpses and ghouls were concentrated and where the party observes dead hobgoblins and gnolls on platforms.
* Origin point where Carl gathered interdiction carts and high-strength crawlers before transporting the [[Def Leppard cart]] to [[Station 36]].
* Node on the [[Vermillion line]] identified as having additional complications in the line's defense and mob activity near the trapped group.
* Station with many corpses on the tracks; noted for wreckage and the presence of transit security casualties such as [[Hobgoblins]] and [[Jackal-faced Gnolls]].
* Planned stop where Carl intends to detach the engine car to then drive back toward [[Station 36]].
* Major railway station and small-scale yard explored by Carl's party in this chapter, featuring flatbed cars, industrial caverns, warehouses, a [[gnoll]] armory, a [[hobgoblin]] repair shop, a [[saferoom]], and the [[bar]] [[The Downward Dog]].
* [[Platform]] pairing with other colored lines (used when switching carts); part of the transit infrastructure for the floor.
* Station on the line noted for additional complications near the path from [[Station 101]] toward [[stairwells]] and safety.
* Transit stop where an early pile of corpses and ghouls obstruct the track; the narrator notes corpses on the [[platform]] and multiple nearby switches/ports.
