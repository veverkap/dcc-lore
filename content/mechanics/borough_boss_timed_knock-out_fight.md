# Borough Boss (Timed Knock-out Fight)
A system-encounter mechanic: discovery of a borough boss triggers a timed, high-stakes boss battle (displayed with music, countdown timer, and rules). In this chapter it manifests as the [[Denise]] encounter inside [[Bonnie's House]] with a three-minute collapse timer.
