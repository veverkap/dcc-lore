# borough_boss_timed_knock-out_fight

• A system-encounter mechanic: discovery of a borough boss triggers a timed, high-stakes boss battle displayed with music, countdown timer, and rules.
• The encounter is triggered by discovering the [[Denise]] encounter inside [[Bonnie's House]] with a three-minute collapse timer.
