# havana

• [[Havana]] denotes the opening major locale or narrative arc and functions as a primary Location node connected to the subsequent chapters in that section.
• City where [[El Capitolio Nacional de Cuba]] is located and where the party's starting area is placed on the [[Eighth Floor: The Ghosts of Earth]].
• The larger [[town]]/zone in which the [[temple]] and transformed [[graveyard]] sit; [[Sister Ines]] leaves the [[temple]] and disappears back out into Havana.
• City-zone on the [[Ninth Floor]] where [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]] and company travel southwest through neighborhoods, roadside freeways, and saferooms; contains landmarks such as a [[cemetery]] and [[zoo]].
• The Cuban location ([[beach]] and surrounding areas) where the charm, zombie/demon emergence, and battle take place.
• The broader city zone entered at the chapter's start; multiple buildings are on fire and the capitol is the focal point of the combat encounter.
• The burning city/setting where the group moves through downtown streets, battles swarms of zombie crabs and seals, and where major set pieces (like the tall building with [[Amayon]] and the [[church]]) are located.
• A previously visited zone characterized as flooded and populated by ghostly NPCs; used as a comparison to the living, inhabited feel of [[Larracos]].
• City region chosen as a starting area for [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]]'s group; subject to the floor's [[Earth]] memory mechanics and seeded with region-appropriate mobs under [[Eighth Floor System Announcement]] rules.
• City/setting in this chapter; the party moves out into Havana after leaving [[Yemaya]]'s [[temple]]; NPC behavior and shops are described in the transformed [[town]].
• Urban zone/city on the [[Ninth Floor]] where most of the chapter's travel occurs: includes suburbs, a [[cemetery]], a [[zoo]], [[freeway]], and neighborhoods with saferooms and NPC populations like the [[Ghommid Village]].
• The city/zone in which the chapter takes place; the party enters [[Havana]] and fights at its capitol building.
• The burning city and downtown setting for this chapter where Carl, [[Donut]], and [[Samantha]] move through zombie-infested streets and collapsing buildings.
