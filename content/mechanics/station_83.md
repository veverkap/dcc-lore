# station_83

* A station referenced on the Nightmare Express map that appears in different color-line configurations; one of the numbered transit nodes relevant to route planning.
* One of the recurring stations on the Nightmare Express's loop; referenced as an origin/return stop in the [[train]]'s route history.
* A [[subterranean]] transfer station where the party disembarks: concrete [[platform]], signage, [[stairs]] to upper area, adjacent shops (including Limp Richard's Sundries) and access to saferooms and other lines.
* A station on the Nightmare Express's loop; referenced as the first station the party had previously traveled to and part of the [[train]]'s repeating route.
* A [[subterranean]] transit station where the party disembarks. Features a [[platform]], [[stairs]] to a small circular room, connections to the Red Line, the Yellow Line and the Nightmare Express, shops like Limp Richard's Sundries, a [[saferoom]] (Nirula’s), and [[stairwell]]/map details.
* A [[train]] transfer station mentioned by Katia as the location of the Daughters; described as a conjunction of the Orange and Indigo lines and a useful landmark for [[stairwell]] searches.
* Referenced earlier as a smaller station; used by the party as a prior point of comparison before arriving at [[Station 131]].
* A station on the looping route of the Nightmare Express (referred to as the first station hit on the loop and the original station the party visited).
* A [[subterranean]] transfer station on the Red Line where the party disembarks. Features a narrow concrete [[platform]], a stairway to an upper circular room with [[saferoom]] access, three shops (including Limp Richard's Sundries and a "Mace and bashing weapons guild"), and connections to the Yellow Line and the [[Nightmare Express Platform]].
* One of the stops on the Nightmare Express loop and a station the party had visited previously. Mentioned as part of the [[train]]’s route.
* A transfer station on the Fourth Floor where [[daughters]] are reported to be located; described as a conjunction of the Orange and Indigo lines.
