# prize_carousel

• A system event/Game Show mechanic that materializes in the [[crawler]]'s personal space when a [[fan box]] is opened, forcing a timed prize selection hosted by [[Chaco]].
• Failing to choose within the timer causes forfeiture.
• The Prize Carousel is the reward-area on the [[Fourth Floor]] where the motorcycle was previously offered to Carl.
