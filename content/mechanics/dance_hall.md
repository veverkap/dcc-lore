# dance_hall

• The Dance Hall is a large, bouncing room in the club where crawlers gather, dance, and violent incidents occur.
• The room is moderately busy and policed by rock monster bouncers.
• The narrator meets Elle McGib, Imani C., and uses hired security (Bomo and Sledge) in the Dance Hall.
• The scene culminates with an on-floor killing and a skull appearing.
