# Special Guest Creature
An explicit system designation in the chapter: certain monsters (like [[Miss Talulah]]) are flagged as a [[Special Guest Creature]] for specific floors (the [[tenth floor]] and [[eleventh floor]]). The system provides additional UI choices and audience interactions for these creatures.
