# Quest Failed: The Creeping Apocalypse
A system announcement that triggers when the world quest fails. Declares the quest failure, scolds players, and initiates floor-wide punishments including the activation of the [[Gehenna Bramble]] effect and the new [[Blood Bar]] saferoom rule.

# [[Quest Failed. The Creeping Apocalypse.]]
A system announcement triggered when the world quest to stop Odious Creepers failed. The message announces the imposition of punishments (including the [[Gehenna Bramble]] spread) and introduces a new status mechanic (the [[blood bar]]) on the floor.
