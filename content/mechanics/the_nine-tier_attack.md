# the_nine-tier_attack

• The Nine-Tier Attack is a system quest/announcement initiated by [[Scolopendra]].
• It is a nine-part assault sequence affecting Scolopendra levels and floors with crawlers.
• The attack sequence is presented as a timed, multi-[[stage]] [[dungeon]] mechanic.
• Rewards are revealed only if the ninth attack commences.
• The announcement governs timing, scope, and consequences of subsequent attacks.
