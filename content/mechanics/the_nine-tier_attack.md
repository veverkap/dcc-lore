# The Nine-Tier Attack
System quest/announcement initiated by [[Scolopendra]]: a nine-part assault sequence affecting Scolopendra levels and floors with crawlers. Presented as a timed, multi-stage dungeon mechanic with rewards revealed only if the ninth attack commences. The announcement governs timing, scope, and consequences of subsequent attacks.
