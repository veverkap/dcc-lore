# wheel_of_fortune

• Vertical prize wheel game in the [[desperado club]] and [[Casino]], operated by [[Tito]], offering a mix of outcomes (500,000 gold, Legendary boxes, and "[[Nothing]]!" horror outcomes).
• Interacts with comp chips to alter wheel composition.
• Hitting the "[[Nothing]]" slot can open a [[portal]]/exit to a [[Holding Area (Dimensional Holding Area)]] according to [[Akuma]] and [[Pontiff]]'s reports.
• A central scene for [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]]'s risky spin and reward.
