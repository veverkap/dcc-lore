# [[Wheel of Fortune]]
Vertical prize wheel game in the [[desperado club]]. Offers a mix of dangerous and valuable outcomes (from "Nothing!" horror outcomes to 500,000 gold and Legendary boxes). Operated by [[Tito]]; interacts with comp chips to alter wheel composition.

# Wheel of Fortune
Vertical, prize-filled gambling wheel in the [[Casino]] run by [[Tito]] with a mix of positive and dangerous red outcomes; a central scene for [[dungeon crawler carl]]'s risky spin and reward.

# Wheel of Fortune
A casino game used as a dungeon mechanic; hitting its "Nothing" slot can open a portal/exit to a [[Holding Area (Dimensional Holding Area)]] according to [[Akuma]] and [[Pontiff]]'s reports.
