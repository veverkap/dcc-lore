# the_iron_tangle

• The Iron Tangle is the fourth floor of the [[dungeon]], themed around a subway/[[train]] system with lines like the [[Red Line]], stations, and [[train]] cars (e.g., [[Cabin #20 – Passenger Car]]).
• The centerpiece of the system announcement and primary exploration area is [[the tangle]].
• The floor is characterized by the sprawling [[Red Line]] rail system, moving cars, stations, transfer hubs, and hidden [[stairwells]].
• Crawlers must navigate the rail system to find [[stairwells]] and saferooms.
