# prize_case_rule

• Prize cases must be opened before they can be added to your inventory.
• This system rule governs how [[Prize Case]] items are handled and prevents instant looting without interaction.
• The rule is displayed when attempting to move a [[Prize Case]] into inventory, enforcing on-[[train]] opening and leading to the suitcase trap encounter.
