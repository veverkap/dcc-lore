# Muted (Spell Restriction)
A system announcement/mechanic that mutes attendees in [[Ballroom A]]: "You’ve been muted! You may not cast spells in this room." It is part of the ballroom overlay protections enforced by the [[Control Chamber]] and [[Soul Crystal]]s.
