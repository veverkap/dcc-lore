# ochre_station_platform

• The Ochre Station [[Platform]] is the site of a [[train]] derailment/crash, containing the Club Vanquisher, mangled cars, and a gangway/engine car entry.
• The [[platform]] is smoky, with mangled cars, fires, and a damaged track.
• The boss fight with [[gore-gore the mantaur]] takes place here.
