# beach

• A specific area on the current floor where hostile monk seals gather and where Sister Ines Quiteria requests help capturing creatures.
• The immediate [[battlefield]] where [[dungeon]] [[crawler]] Carl lands and finds corpses, dead [[sea]] life, and memory ghosts.
• The scene of Raul the Crab's reluctant ritual and the effects of the ongoing demon event.
• Shoreline in [[Florida]] where thousands of corpses and [[loot]] washed up, serving as a grim warning set up by the game.
• The corpses- strewn coastal zone where [[dungeon]] [[crawler]] Carl and allies [[land]], containing dead wildlife, memory ghosts, and the immediate site of Raul the Crab's required action for the event.
