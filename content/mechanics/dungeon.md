# dungeon

• The Dungeon is a conceptual location referenced by the narrator, serving as the central setting motif for the series and the narrator's creative focus.
• The larger game/dungeon environment is a source of Residuals, instability, and threats, and is cited by the Harbinger as the locus of danger that motivated his warnings.
• The Dungeon is connected conceptually to [[Dungeon [[Crawler]] Carl]] as the series' primary environment and thematic anchor.
