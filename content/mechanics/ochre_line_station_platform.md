# Ochre Line Station Platform
The derailed transit station where the chapter's events occur: a smoky, multi-platform chamber with a central magical sign, mangled third rail, and multiple crashed cars. The station serves [[station 433 terminus]]–[[station 436]] on the ochre route and contains points of interest like the entrance to [[Club Vanquisher]].
