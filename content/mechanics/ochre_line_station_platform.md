# ochre_line_station_platform

• The Ochre Line Station [[Platform]] is a derailed transit station where the chapter's events occur.
• The station has a central magical sign, a mangled third rail, and multiple crashed cars.
• The station serves [[station 433 terminus]] and [[station 436]] on the ochre route.
• The station contains points of interest, including the entrance to [[Club Vanquisher]].
