# Lootable Corpse
UI/system mechanic displayed when players can access a fallen enemy's inventory (e.g., "Lootable Corpse. Henrik. Level 50 Changeling Principal..."). Drives in-world looting of items like [[pocket watch]] and [[Torn Book Page]].
