# Inventory liquid restriction patch
An explicit dungeon rule change announced in the system message: effective immediately, players may no longer store liquids in their inventory unless those liquids are contained, which directly triggers the red-blood explosion/paradox event when [[Katia]] had blood stored unpackaged.
