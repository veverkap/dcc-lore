# pox_slugs

• Level-30 to 35 large slug mobs, wolf-sized with white dots, capable of being organized and weaponized, observed eating a ram cleric's corpse.
• Some pox slugs equipped with improvised weapons, such as a hatchet.
• Large hostile mob-creatures, size of wolves, encountered eating a corpse near a [[church]].
• Exhibit strange behaviors, observed at level range 30–35 in this chapter.
