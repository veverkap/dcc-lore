# the_twister

• The Twister is a flying/parked house, originally called variations like [[Tiddy Twister II]], and is a local vehicle/location parked outside [[town]], worrying Louis and the party.
• The Twister is also an airship/vehicle serving as a meeting and staging point where characters like [[Samantha]] and others are already aboard, used as a fallback/escape option in plans.
