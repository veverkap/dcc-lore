# monsters_disembark_every_five_stops

• Monsters disembark every five stops.
• This behavior structures combat cadence and player planning on the Nightmare Express.
• [[Dungeon]] rule observed and reported by [[Vernon]]: monsters consistently leave the [[train]] at five-stop intervals (one-way stations).
