# bring_forth_the_witnesses

• Bring Forth the Witnesses: a System announcement and follow-up ritual from [[Eris]] that causes a magical backlash across the entire floor, disabling long-range [[teleport]] spells for one hour, and triggers the ascension of frozen combatants.
