# System Announcement: Floor & Mob Adjustments
An in-world system message ("Hello Crawlers...") that updates dungeon rules: non-sapient mobs on stairwells will no longer be disintegrated, [[Brindled Vespa]] population halved, their acid damage reduced, and [[Brindle Grub]] pupa progression set to a 50% chance. This announcement affects pacing and encounter design on the [[Third Floor]].
