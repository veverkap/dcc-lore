# Portal to the Nothing
The transient exit created by placing synchronized [[pocket watch]]es in the [[Winding Box]] and winding it; lasts roughly twenty minutes or until the artifacts cross. The open portal lingers and can allow entities from the [[the nothing]] to pass through.
