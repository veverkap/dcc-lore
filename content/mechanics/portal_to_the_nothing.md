# portal_to_the_nothing

• The transient exit is created by placing synchronized [[pocket watch]]es in the [[Winding Box]] and winding it, lasting roughly twenty minutes or until the artifacts cross.
• The open [[portal]] can allow entities from the [[the nothing]] to pass through.
