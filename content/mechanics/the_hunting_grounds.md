# the_hunting_grounds

• A distinct area accessible via [[stairs]] in the [[desperado club]] that opens fully once crawlers reach the sixth floor.
• The sixth floor of the [[dungeon]], a [[jungle]]-overgrown variant of the third floor where crawlers have 17 days to explore before hunters are released.
• Contains cities like [[Zockau]] and ruins such as [[Larracos]].
• A regional zone under high-elf influence, targeted for liberation from [[elves]] control.
