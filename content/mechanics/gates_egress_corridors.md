# Gates (egress corridors)
Kilometer-wide fixed corridors through which non-scouts may enter enemy territory during [[The Ramp-Up]]. These [[Gates (egress corridors)]] are an explicit seasonal restriction that concentrates enemy approaches to predictable locations.
