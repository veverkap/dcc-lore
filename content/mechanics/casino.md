# casino

• A planned gaming area within the [[desperado club]] that was not yet open at the time of visit; guarded and tied to the club's higher-security areas.
• The gambling area inside the [[desperado club]] with card tables, roulette, a vertical Wheel of Fortune game, and other games; distinct atmosphere and guarded staircases to lower levels.
• A large, open area adjacent to the [[restaurant]] within the [[desperado club]]; initially the site of the forced demon's appearance and later on fire and damaged by [[Minge]].
• The launch point mentioned in the chapter from which players were supposed to jump toward a [[Holding Area]] and be retrieved by contacts at the [[Pineapple Cabaret]].
• A gambling area on the fourth floor that is noted to be open and tied into club promotions and spending-based security coupons.
• Gaming area inside the [[desperado club]] with card tables, roulette, a vertical [[Wheel of Fortune]], and guarded staircases leading to lower levels like the [[Hunting Grounds]] and [[Larracos]].
• A public location on the current floor that contains a [[The Nothing]] [[gate]], referenced as an alternative disposal point for the [[Lika Love Doll Head]] if the party wanted to eject the artifact into the [[Nothing]].
• A large open area adjacent to the [[Restaurant]] that becomes chaotic when [[Minge]] breaks through and where the initial demon activity and NPC panic occur.
• Location of the [[Wheel of Fortune]] game that [[Carl]] planned to take over to hit the "[[Nothing]]" spot and trigger a [[portal]] or extraction.
• The immediate location of the ambush in this chapter, where furniture and machines revealed themselves as mimics and combat ensued.
