# System Announcement (Damien)
A longer system broadcast delivered by a male voice later identified as [[Damien]]. The announcement opens the [[Outreach Guild]] signups, warns about the [[Pineapple Cabaret]] exploit, details transfer rules (garage contents and hired NPCs moving to the [[11th floor]]), notes safe room access timing, and mandates a 90-minute parade-float objective on arrival at the [[11th floor]].
