# system_announcement_damien

• A longer system broadcast delivered by a male voice later identified as [[Damien]].
• Opens the [[Outreach Guild]] signups.
• Warns about the [[Pineapple Cabaret]] exploit.
• Details transfer rules: [[garage]] contents and hired NPCs moving to the [[11th floor]].
• Notes safe room access timing.
• Mandates a 90-minute parade-float objective on arrival at the [[11th floor]].
