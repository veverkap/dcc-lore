# your_deck_has_been_exhausted

• System announcement indicating the party has no remaining cards in hand; in the chapter it triggers the appearance of the totem card into the empty slot.
• System message indicating that a player's current hand and [[deck]] have been used or empty, often triggering the need to summon a totem (like [[Monk [[Seal]] totem]]) or discard a card to continue.
