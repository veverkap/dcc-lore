# Nine-tier attack
A named, multi-stage magical/psychological assault from the myth of [[Scolopendra]] described as nine rapid, devastating effects. Framed as a repeating historical pattern with stages including control of the [[Tunnel network]] and subsequent social upheavals.

# Nine-Tier Attack
The mythic/systemic multi-stage assault associated with [[Scolopendra]] that combines physical and social/psychological waves of destruction and upheaval. Described as having nine distinct phases, the first being control of infrastructure (the [[Tunnel System]]) and the second named [[A Parade of Horribles]].
