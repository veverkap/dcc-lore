# nine-tier_attack

• A named, multi-[[stage]] magical/psychological assault from the myth of [[Scolopendra]], described as nine rapid, devastating effects.
• The assault combines physical and social/psychological waves of destruction and upheaval, with nine distinct phases.
• The first phase is control of infrastructure (the [[Tunnel network]]).
• The second phase is named [[A Parade of Horribles]].
