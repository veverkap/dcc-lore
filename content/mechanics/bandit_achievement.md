# bandit_achievement

• A rare in-game achievement that triggered a double-upgrade on survivors' [[loot]] boxes in the broadcast incident.
• The notification text appears in the chapter; the reward upgraded a [[Platinum Quest Box]] twice into a [[Celestial Quest Box]].
• Later patched/disabled by [[borant corporation]] after the controversy.
• An achievement awarded for completing a quest in an unusual manner.
• Triggered a double upgrade (Platinum -> Celestial) for survivors like [[Quan Ch]].
• Can be rewarded multiple times.
