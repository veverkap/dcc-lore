# Bandit Achievement
A rare in-game achievement that triggered a double-upgrade on survivors’ loot boxes in the broadcast incident. The notification text appears in the chapter; the reward in the cited instance upgraded a [[Platinum Quest Box]] twice into a [[Celestial Quest Box]]. Later patched/disabled by [[borant corporation]] after the controversy.
