# holding_area

• The intended destination for crawlers jumping from the [[Casino]] and where extractions are normally performed.
• The place where [[Louis Santiago]] expected to arrive instead of [[Sheol]].
• A conceptual location referenced in [[Pontiff]]'s analysis, where stepping through the [[portal]] could move a mercenary from the holding area into an area outside the active playing field.
