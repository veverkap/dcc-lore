# the_castle

* The War Mage Rebellion controls the submerged/occupied [[stronghold]], which must be liberated within a timed window.
* The castle's defenses and [[towers]] play a pivotal role.
* The were-castors and allies use the castle for dam-building, traps, and other activities.
* The larger zone/facility contains the [[ballroom]] and other floors, lacking saferooms in some sections.
* The performance chamber is located within the castle.
* The central fortified base features a single main entrance, interior saferooms, and hallways.
* The [[hangar]] door on top is a key feature.
* The castle is the command hub for Colonel Rosetta and site of internal defenses.
* The [[stronghold]] contains internal structures such as the bait-shop [[saferoom]], [[hangar]], crafting rooms, and the [[Guildhall]] common area.
* The party is preparing to enter the castle, where Elle McGib and Imani are searching for traps.
* Several events and tracks lead back to the castle.
* The castle is the center of the Masquerade event, with a secret underground entrance and sentinel [[towers]] for docking the Twister.
* The entire [[stronghold]] is the scene of the siege, where mantises, hunters, crawlers, and gods converge.
