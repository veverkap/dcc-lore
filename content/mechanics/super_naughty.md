# super_naughty

• A system status/announcement that flashes above [[Edict]]'s head after attacks, used by the [[dungeon]] to mark disallowed behavior before enacting penalties (e.g., ejection).
• A visible system announcement that flashes above a character's head after prohibited aggression, triggering punishment ([[teleport]] to nearest monster [[nest]]) when repeatedly violated.
