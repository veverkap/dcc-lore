# seattle

• The city where [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]] experiences a sudden transformation and the appearance of brightly lit [[dungeon]] entrances, with the first [[Syndicate Announcement]] about the [[World Dungeon]].
• The former metropolitan region, referenced as the planet's city, with ruined infrastructure, used as a setting for scenes involving [[Sadir]] and the New Queen Anne settlement.
• The pre-collapse metropolis, once called "Seattle", where the [[camp]] and ruined neighborhoods, including the airport and apartments, are located, setting for key survivor interactions.
• A metropolis on [[Earth]], mentioned as the intended relocation [[target]] for [[Agatha]] before the [[emergency]] accelerated the season.
