# Abandoned Practice Arena (Demon Tamer's Guild)
Sealed room at the end of the secret corridor with a dust-covered warning sign and a cluster of fifteen red-tagged, outrageously dangerous monsters. Previously targeted by failed clearing attempts and considered extremely hazardous by the [[Night Wyrm]].
