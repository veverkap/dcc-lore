# soul_poisoning

• Harmful status effect afflicting [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]] due to accumulated souls on the [[Scavenger’s Daughter back patch]]; causes pain, worsening condition, and has additional pending effects unless dispersed.
• A system message prompts activation of [[Daughter’s Kiss]] to cure the effect.
• A progressive status effect announced by the system ("Your Soul Poisoning is worsening") which inflicts worsening penalties; in this chapter it triggers the additional condition "Post Coital Blues."
• Debuff afflicting [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]] caused by excessive stored soul energy in the [[Scavenger’s Daughter back patch]]; produces jolts of pain, worsening effects over time, and has additional consequences if not treated; partially mitigated by the [[Pauper’s ring of the Steadfast Emberus]].
