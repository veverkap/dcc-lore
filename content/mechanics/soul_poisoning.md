# Soul Poisoning
Debuff afflicting [[dungeon crawler carl]] caused by excessive stored soul energy in the [[Scavenger’s Daughter back patch]]. Produces jolts of pain, worsening effects over time, and has additional consequences if not treated; partially mitigated by the [[Pauper’s ring of the Steadfast Emberus]].
