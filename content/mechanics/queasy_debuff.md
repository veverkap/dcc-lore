# queasy_debuff

• A nausea-producing debuff inflicted by the [[Red Cornet]]’s auditory/echolocation attack, producing vomiting and incapacitation effects for affected anatomies.
• Explained by [[Mordecai]] as a physical/auditory attack that the body builds resistance to.
