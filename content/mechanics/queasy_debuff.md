# Queasy (Debuff)
A nausea-producing debuff inflicted by the [[Red Cornet]]’s auditory/echolocation attack. Produces vomiting and incapacitation effects for affected anatomies. Explained by [[Mordecai]] as a physical/auditory attack that the body builds resistance to.
