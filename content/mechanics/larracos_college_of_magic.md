# larracos_college_of_magic

• A major campus complex in the city of [[Larracos]], occupying a quarter of a funnel ring, featuring [[towers]], observatories, dorms, and a [[saferoom]] where crawlers meet NPCs like [[Arief]].
• A [[college]] campus on the [[50th Level]] of [[Larracos]], housing professor dorm [[towers]], a cafeteria [[saferoom]] exit, and serving as the focal point of the defenders as [[towers]] power up and mobs press the attack.
• An institution tied to the city of [[Larracos]], referenced during the recap, where [[Samantha]] borrowed a carpet from.
