# larracos_level

• The [[Larracos level]] is a lower floor of the [[desperado club]] that has been flooded or partially drained, containing trapped outworlders, a hotel, and a main [[casino]].
• Access and evacuation are impacted by the mayoral politics of [[Zockau]].
• The nightclub level is home to [[Astrid]]'s security forces, has floors (top, middle), shops like [[Meat Shields]], and a sealed practice [[arena]] of the [[Demon Tamer's Guild]].
• A lower level is referenced as a destination below the club, with NPCs moving upward instead of down towards it during the escape.
• The [[Larracos level]] is also mentioned as a possible reassignment destination for displaced club workers, describing punitive or worse redeployments.
• Hunters and outworlders were trapped on this level, which is also referenced as flooded and containing hotels and the [[main casino]] in prior events.
