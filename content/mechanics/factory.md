# factory

• A location mentioned by [[Prepotente]] as the place where he will see the narrator and others to continue plans after the meeting.
• The massive industrial-themed race entrance and primary [[arena]] for the [[heat]], featuring molten- metal flooring, the [[Receiving Bay]], and the [[Shipping]].
• A building on the race course that the [[Unicorn]] bypassed by going over it to reach the [[roof]] and then the finish line.
