# station_60

Here is the consolidated markdown list:

• [[Station 60]] is a residential/employee loop stop where lower employees live, a proposed destination for depositing the rescued NPC [[Madison]], and a staging area for the party.
• The station is an employee substation used as a junction and transfer point, identified as a place the team can reach on the way to and from the [[Vermillion Line]].
• It is a hub where a dwarf [[Tizquick]] sits at the entrance to colored-line [[portals]], and Carl suggests visiting [[Station 60]] to learn color-routing information for finding other stations like [[Station F]]/[[Yard F]].
• The station is a planned waypoint in [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]]'s evacuation route, where crawlers can choose final destinations.
• It is an employee station and refuge mentioned as a fallback point for several groups, including those evacuated from [[Station 72]], and specified as an escape destination in the system announcement.
• The station is a destination to check for information on reaching [[Yard A]], and is described by [[Widget]] as housing employee quarters/dorms.
• It is a substation and transfer point on the transit network used by [[Hekla]]'s crew to reach the homeward bound platforms and identified as a waypoint on the way to [[Station 101]].
• The station is a transit hub referenced as the place where [[Tizquick]] sits near entrances to colored-line [[portals]], and is suggested as a staging point to reach the F line.
• It is a waypoint in the rescue plan intended as a staging area from which survivors can move to other defended stations like 36 or 72.
• The station is an employee station and rally point referenced in the [[Iron Tangle System Announcement]], and many groups fell back toward this station during the breach.
• It is a higher-level station referenced repeatedly by [[Widget]] as a place where lower employees live and which participates in a loop that lets trains turn around.
• The station is a subway/station node on the [[Vermillion line]] and the waypoint Carl's group passes through, noted as having a small colony of NPCs and the location of the employee [[portal]] switch.
• It is a hub with a dwarf [[Tizquick]] at the entrance to colored-line [[portals]], and the last-known pathway location used to discover line colors and reach other yards/stations.
• The station is a waypoint and decision node in [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]]'s broader plan, where rescued crawlers can disperse to safer [[stairwells]] like 36 or 72.
• It is a transit [[platform]] referenced as a node with platforms at both 60 and 75, and [[Tizquick]] is said to be on vigil here.
• The station is a dormitory/executive-quarters area where [[Widget]] claimed employees (including himself) lived, used as a [[target]] stop for dropping off [[Madison]] and planning a loop.
• It is a substation used as a hub to choose among the 12 Homeward Bound platforms, and is a waypoint for the [[train]] operation to/from the [[Vermillion line]].
• The station is a hub where a dwarf named [[Tizquick]] stands at colored-line [[portals]], and is suggested as a route to reach other yards (e.g., to find Yard F).
• It is a planned waypoint in the evacuation plan where rescued people can choose further destinations (station 36 [[stairwells]], free station 72, or raiding parties).
• The station is a transit station mentioned as having platforms relevant for line switching and where [[Tizquick]] stands vigil to guide crawlers between colored lines.
• It is an employee station and rendezvous point referenced by [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]], and several groups fell back to [[Station 60]] during the evacuation.
