# the_nest

• Planned, very tall and expensive specialized synergy [[tower]] (menu name "The [[Nest]]") to be built at the [[hole]] edge leading into Larracos, designed to operate during the next phase of the assault.
• The defensive [[tower]] complex and staging area where the encounter unfolds; the [[Nest]]'s integrity is tracked and its layout is central to tactics.
• A structure/fortification referenced in combat with an HP percentage; strategic objective near the [[tower]] and [[target]] of area-igniting plans.
