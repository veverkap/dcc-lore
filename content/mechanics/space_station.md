# space_station

• The [[Space station]] is a rotating orbital [[platform]] used to transfer guests before they are sent to the production site.
• It remains over the Indian Ocean during the opening scene.
• The location is also referenced as an environment where guards operate and where ships and nodes interact.
• It is used as an orbital transfer point to move contestants before they are zapped down to the [[Underwater production trailer]].
