# floor_11

• The [[dungeon]] floor Mordecai claims to have reached before becoming a guildmaster, illustrating his backstory and the limits most crawlers face in progression.
• Sister floor to [[Floor 10]], referenced in [[Mordecai]]'s warning about risky exit deals before floor 12.
• A specific [[dungeon]] floor referenced in [[Odette]]'s confession as the level where [[Mordecai]] made his [[deal]] and where key events occurred that shaped several characters' fates.
