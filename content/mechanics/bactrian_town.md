# bactrian_town

• A neighboring [[town]] across the dunes, accessible by a few miles of desert travel from [[Hump Town]], intended to be reached and scouted by [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]]'s group.
• Ruined [[town]] near the battle area, home to local mobs such as thorny devils, with smoldering remains observed from the [[Chariot]]’s perch.
