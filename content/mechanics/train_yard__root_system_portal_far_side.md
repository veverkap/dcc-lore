# Train Yard / Root System (portal far side)
Area visible through the massive portal: a sprawling yard with dozens of diverging tracks (described as root-like) where red trains and hordes of zombies (including zombified [[Cornet]]s) run. Track switches there control which tracks connect to the local network.
