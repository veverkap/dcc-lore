# Comp Chip Wheel Mechanic: remove red spots with larger bets
A gambling mechanic explained by [[Tito]]: using high-value comp chips increases bet size, which removes red (negative) spots from the wheel and replaces them with positive outcomes, changing odds and prize distribution on the wheel of fortune.
