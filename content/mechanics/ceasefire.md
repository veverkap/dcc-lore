# ceasefire

* A time-limited system rule that pauses hostilities, affecting spell/combat permissions and faction interactions in [[Larracos, City of Dreams]].
* Impacts [[crawler]] and NPC interactions, gates, and combat permissions between teams like [[team retribution]] and [[crawler]] forces.
* Temporarily halts open conflict, refreshes in-game resources, and changes which NPCs or players dare to enter areas like [[Larracos]].
* Affects political maneuvering in [[Shanty Town]].
* Governs allowed actions, preventing touching non-possessed players during the ceasefire.
* Central to the adjudication of killings.
* Enforced no-combat period governing the timing of hostilities and relevant to sudden movement alerts.
* Formal in-game noncombat period with codified rules and addenda governing teleportation, penalties, and adjudication.
* Triggers Phase One Combat rules and other system effects when the ceasefire ends.
* Timed protection period (60 hours) during which [[saferoom]] rules apply inside [[Larracos]]: players may not attack other crawlers, and certain protections are in effect.
* Can be shortened via unanimous team vote, voluntary warlord withdrawal, or when all teams finish defenses and mark themselves "Ready".
