# [[Forbidden-Portal Explosion Rule]]
Mechanic explaining portals that lead to forbidden floors (e.g., [[Sheol (Floor 15)]]): crawlers are prevented from passing, but their equipment can be pulled through, sometimes causing the touched crawler to explode as gear is ripped away.
