# back_kitchens

• The [[restaurant]]'s [[kitchen]] area, known as the Back [[kitchens]], is a location where Astrid and several NPCs [[shelter]].
• It is also the site of key combat and Astrid's death.
• The area is guarded by demon and rhino guards.
