# Der Schachmeister
Opponent deckmaster in the chapter: a small tuxedoed boy with round glasses. Holds a twenty-card deck (six totems) with an eight-second summoning cooldown and focuses on debuffs and disabling effects (e.g., mystic cards like [[Peekaboo]] and [[Summon lock]]). Calls attention to faster opponent pull times.
