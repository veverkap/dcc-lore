# Peeling Phase
A system ruleset/phase announced by System Messages that opens the city, sets "first to take the castle wins," and imposes extreme consequences (affiliated soldiers and warlords on a defeated team on the battlefield die). Triggered after [[Architect Houston of the Madness]] fell.
