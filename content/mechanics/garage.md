# garage

Here is the consolidated markdown list:

• The large roll-up-doored space where the party readies its vehicle or mount for the upcoming [[heat]], houses NPC applicants for mechanic roles, and contains the countdown timer, loudspeaker, and garage attendants.
• The starting area on the tenth floor where teams gather, vehicles spawn, and the vehicle-selection wheel appears, contains the countdown timer, loudspeaker, and garage attendants.
• The cavernous garage where [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]]'s team arrives after teleportation, scene of vehicle maintenance, interaction with [[Hedy]], and animal antics from [[Mongo]] and [[Rend]].
• The setting where the crew congregates to find [[Splash Zone]], [[Dong]], [[Mongo]], and [[Rend]] sitting in a circle, and where gremlins crawl over the vehicle and cleanup occurs.
• The enclosed starting location where the team preps the [[Truck]], breaks the [[Tommy gun]], stores the [[Freezer chest]], and receives the race briefing from [[Dr. Metcalf]].
• The team's staging area where the crew arrives after crossing the finish line, characters like [[Hedy]], [[Bucket Boy]], [[Rend]], and [[Mongo]] interact here, and where the audience upgrade vote results are revealed.
• The numbered garage spaces where teams store knocked-out crawlers and gear, described as places where "safe room rules" do not apply and which can contain rival teams' incapacitated members.
• The individual vehicle stalls at the pit stop where teams park and are bound by the [[A two-hour timer starts the moment you park in your garage.]] rule and the prohibition captured by [[Note: Garage doors may not be closed during pit stops. Safe room access is unavailable during pit stops.]].
• The physical location of [[One Fine Pig]]’s operations and the scene of the attack where the APV and gremlins are located.
• The location where [[Hedy]] is being guarded by [[Jamal]] and some slugs while [[Mongo]] is brought to the [[bar]].
• The space where [[Grigori]], [[Hedy]], and [[Jamal]] are left at the chapter’s start, serves as an outer staging area relative to the [[Safe Room]] and [[Guildhall]].
• The physical setting where the group exits the [[truck]] and encounters one another, the entrance to the adjoining safe room is in this space.
• The personal team vehicle/[[storage]] spaces that will transfer to the [[11th floor]] with their contents and may contain hired NPCs.
• The team's home garage visible down the street from the [[apartment]], serving as their staging area and point of departure before entering the [[lobby]] starting blocks.
• The location adjacent to the party where [[Carl]] instructs others to return from, the garage was intended as a staging area before moving people into the [[Safe Room]] but access was limited by the attack’s timing.
• The combined safe-room area where teams’ vehicles, mounts, pets, and gear are stored and where merged float groups prepare.
