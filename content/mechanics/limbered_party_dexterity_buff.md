# limbered_party_dexterity_buff

• Temporary buff granted by [[Donut]]'s singing ("You have been limbered! Your party’s dexterity is increased by 50%!")
• Can be stacked by additional verses
• May be canceled if the performer fails (muted penalty described in-chapter)
