# Limbered (Party Dexterity Buff)
Temporary buff granted by [[Donut]]'s singing ("You have been limbered! Your party’s dexterity is increased by 50%!"). Can be stacked by additional verses but may be canceled if the performer fails (muted penalty described in-chapter).
