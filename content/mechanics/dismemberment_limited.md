# dismemberment_limited

• A named maglev/monorail [[train]] (short loop) that boards at station 281, featuring Levi/ war-mage activity, and is the setting for the Levi quest and Fleshers' attack.
• Carries the war-mage [[Dismember the War Mage]] and triggers the active quest to protect [[Levi the Seventh]].
• Associated with [[Dismember]] and hostile encounters on trains.
• Intersects at [[Station 281]] and is featured in the recap montage.
• A named, short-loop maglev [[train]] line that boards quickly, associated with deadly named-line mechanics and stops that interface with routes like the [[Nightmare Express]].
