# despondent

• Permanent debuff applied by system message to all worshippers of [[Ysalte]] after her death, reducing stats and increasing damage taken from other worshippers.
• Affects NPCs like [[Arief]], reducing their stats (e.g., 15% hit to all stats) and increasing damage taken from other worshippers.
• Classified as a system-level affliction with lasting effect.
