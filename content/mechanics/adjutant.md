# adjutant

• A neutral, third-party observer with access to rules and communications, often a celebrity providing commentary and limited enforcement powers.
• A formal mechanic/role in Faction Wars: a neutral third-party observer assigned to each team with access to rules and inter-adjutant communications.
• A third-party, non-combat referee and consultant assigned per team with access to the expanded rule set, able to clarify rules, call fouls, and deliver daily on-[[air]] recaps while being system-protected on the [[battlefield]].
• Has access to an expanded rule set and can clarify rules and call fouls.
• Provides on-[[air]] recaps.
• Is protected while present on the [[battlefield]].
• May have limited rule-enforcement powers.
• Traditionally symbolic, and may be granted constant [[invulnerability]] while on the surface (per Zev).
