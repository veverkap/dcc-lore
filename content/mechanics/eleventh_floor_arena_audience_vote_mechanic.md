# [[Eleventh Floor Arena Audience Vote Mechanic]]
A dungeon rule tied to the eleventh floor: when certain mobs are killed, players are offered their experience or the option to trigger an audience vote. Depending on the vote, the creature either survives and is sent to the arena on the eleventh floor, or a “stand-in” (often a person from outside the dungeon ecosystem) is sent instead. Stand-ins have included individuals from places like the [[Eighteenth Floor Resort]].
