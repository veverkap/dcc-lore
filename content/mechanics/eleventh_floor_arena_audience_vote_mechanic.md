# eleventh_floor_arena_audience_vote_mechanic

• When certain mobs are killed on the eleventh floor, players are offered their experience or the option to trigger an audience vote.
• Depending on the vote, the creature either survives and is sent to the [[arena]] on the eleventh floor or a "[[stand-in]]" is sent instead.
• Stand-ins have included individuals from places like the [[Eighteenth Floor Resort]].
