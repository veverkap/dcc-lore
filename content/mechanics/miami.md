# miami

• One of the highlighted real-world destination options on the choosing-[[arena]] globe, reached for by [[Donut]] during her selection.
• City referenced via television clues and spawn choices, with multiple in-game messages and media implying a Miami connection for the floor.
• General metropolitan area where [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]] and his guild are based, and where the safehouse party occurs; chosen as the group's newest location.
• City referenced in multiple secret message cues (television shows) and one of the possible location choices players saw before glitches; connected to the reason for relocating the [[Key]].
• City region where [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]] and his group are currently located, referenced in relation to travel distance to [[Homestead, Florida]] and the surrounding area.
