# faction_wars

Here is the consolidated markdown list:

• [[Faction Wars]] is a competitive game mode on the 9th Floor where teams receive a [[Throne Seed]] to found castles.
• It imposes placement rules and conquest mechanics that transfer dominion when a castle is taken.
• The event involves voting on rules, guild [[upgrades]], and the organization of mercenary forces.
• It is a large-scale competition/conflict framing the chapter, with factions contesting for influence and control.
• The event includes voting on action items, [[emergency]] action item mechanics, audience votes, team designations, and rule changes that affect gameplay and the playing field.
• The 9th Floor is a contested floor where guilds, NPCs, and returning outside crawlers fought over a castle and pivotal events unfolded.
• The event is a major set-piece where deities and sponsored teams intervene and where strategic play can alter outcomes for whole regions.
• It is a systemic, large-scale PvP event/game phase the crawlers are entering, with Warlord tabs, action items, warlord teams, audience votes, sponsor protections, and procedural rules.
