# station_149

• A transfer/transit station intersecting the [[Ochre Line]] and the [[Purple Line]].
• The primary operation site where [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]] plants [[Jelly Bomb]]s to derail a [[train]] and where reconnaissance occurs.
• The current station on the [[Ochre Line]] where the group is located at chapter start.
• Key local events and wrecked trains around this stop form the immediate tactical situation.
• Current station where the party is based at the chapter's opening.
• The crashed [[train]] section and surrounding wreckage are described here; the location where the [[neighborhood map]] was obtained.
• The immediate Ochre line station where the chapter's action begins: a crash site with tangled wreckage, gore, and looted items; the party locates the neighborhood map here and assesses [[train]] traffic and monster positions.
• The specific Ochre Line station where Carl and his team board the [[Dismemberment Limited]] and where the earlier wrecked trains and gore are centered.
