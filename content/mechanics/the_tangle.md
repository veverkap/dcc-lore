# the_tangle

• The Tangle is a location containing the Nightmare Express transit network, with public announcements referencing "the tangle" and lines, colored trains, and hubs operating within this domain.
• The Tangle is a broader zone/setting containing the transit system, [[train]] lines (including the ochre line), and is a major locale for the station, clubs, and NPC workforce.
• The Tangle is a large transit network containing colored [[train]] lines (e.g., [[Yellow Line]]), with stations, tracks, and transfer hubs interconnecting.
• The Tangle is the overarching transit network containing the Nightmare Express and multiple color-coded lines, which the party traverses.
