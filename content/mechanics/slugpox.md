# slugpox

• Infected with SlugPox produces successive Pox Slug spawns and progressive infection effects.
• Spawns slugs from boils on a victim's body, causing pain, damage, and potential death if untreated.
• Suppressed temporarily by Pater Coal's sacrament in the Emberus High [[Temple]] of the Ending Sun.
• Causes boils that spawn escalating Pox Slug minions and ongoing harmful effects requiring healer intervention or entry to special facilities.
• Produces parasitic slugs that erupt from boils, [[deal]] increasing damage as new slugs emerge, and require healer intervention or suppression to avoid death.
• A lingering status/ailment referenced by system warnings, affecting characters and not yet cured for Carl.
• Inflicted by Asojano, requiring healer services or entry to special facilities to treat.
