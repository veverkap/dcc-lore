# Ballroom Rules
Explicit event rules enforced by castle staff: no weapons or active magic allowed in the ballroom (with limited exceptions for stage acts), magical storage is inaccessible, security sweeps occur at entrance, and performers have restricted backstage interaction. These rules structure the party’s tactical constraints.
