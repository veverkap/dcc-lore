# the_swamp

• The lair area for [[Shi Maria]], covered in webbing and sticky terrain, located near the southern coast/mouth of the big bay, and flagged as dangerous due to the demi-god's presence.
• Zone on the island where [[Shi Maria]] resides under a sticky web that covers the area; identified by [[Samantha]] as the location where she was swallowed.
