# New Quest: Find Out Who Killed My Son
God-issued quest from [[Emberus]] directing adherents to investigate the murder of [[Geyrun]]. No time limit, but failure to complete before exiting the 18th floor carries a smite penalty. Requires visiting the high cleric at the [[Club Vanquisher]] Emberus Shrine for details.
