# system_message_eris_has_entered_the_realm

• System Announcement: "Eris has entered the realm" indicates the arrival of [[Eris]] in the scene, freezing everything.
• When [[Eris]] appears, a random temporary magical effect or item or aura from someone in your presence (except yourself) becomes permanent.
• The announcement also reveals deity metadata, such as level, sponsorship status, and [[invulnerability]] on the floor, visible to players.
