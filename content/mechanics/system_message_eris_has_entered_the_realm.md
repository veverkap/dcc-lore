# System Message: Eris has entered the realm
A System Announcement that triggers when [[Eris]] appears: "Eris has entered the realm." The message flags the presence of a Deity and describes a Random Effect: when [[Eris]] is present, "a random temporary magical effect or item or aura from someone in your presence other than yourself will become permanent." The announcement also notes deity metadata visible to players (e.g., level, sponsorship status, invulnerability on the floor).
