# Time-Instance Warnings
System announcements triggered when [[dungeon crawler carl]] touches the trailer door: "This area exists in a different time instance than the remainder of this floor" and "Physical interactions in this area act differently than other locations on this floor." They define the spliced temporal rules and constrain player behavior inside the affected area.
