# 11th_floor

• The 11th floor is where [[Scolopendra]] and [[Krakaren Prime]] are encountered, and the site of the critical rule interactions and boss battle.
• The 11th floor is the current [[dungeon]] floor where the boss battle occurs and where Carl and his party attempt their escape/bait strategy.
• The 11th floor is referenced as the destination tied to quest progression, and is mentioned in the quest reward (sending stand-ins to the [[Arena (11th floor)]]).
• The 11th floor is a specific upcoming floor where surviving garages and contents will transfer, and arrival there requires constructing a themed parade float using [[garage]] and safe room resources.
• The 11th floor is referenced as the destination where players will meet [[Group Six]] if they survive the current challenges, used as a milestone location.
