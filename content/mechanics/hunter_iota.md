A level 50 crafter introduced as "Hunter Iota" (Coin Tinker). Part of the [[crafters]] and described as a pale, hairier humanoid similar to a [[Soother (Common Soother)]], missing a leg and drunk when encountered. Teleported out of the [[Saferoom]] for failing to kill; provides exposition about the [[mantis]], [[kua-tin]], and the crawl's legal problems.
