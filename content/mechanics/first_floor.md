# first_floor

• The opening floor/zone of the [[Dungeon]] [[Crawler]] World season, large in scale, not fully interconnected globally, and often off-limits to live viewers during initial episodes.
• The [[dungeon]] level where the party currently operates, with NPCs explaining the mechanics of floor progression and the eventual Floor Collapse ([[dungeon]] rule).
• The [[dungeon]] floor where the [[Goblin]] [[Workshop]] and current Boss Room are located, relatively early/easier [[dungeon]] content compared to deeper floors.
• The initial floor of the [[dungeon]] where heavy casualties were sustained, referenced as having claimed a little more than ten million people.
• The [[dungeon]] floor referenced as having already collapsed once, whose collapse behavior informs current system rules (safe rooms closing prior to collapse).
• The origin point where Donut originally received the butterfly charm, used to indicate historical item acquisition in the party's progression.
• A [[dungeon]] location referenced in past events, used as context for prior actions and crimes.
• The floor of the building where much of this chapter's action occurs, with multiple teams racing across it toward the finish line.
• The early- level analogue to tourists/rats, used for comparative context about low-tier nuisances like rich tourists and Syndicate security.
