# service_bay_32

• [[Service Bay 32]]: a dead-end track/service bay with a corrugated awning, adjacent to a [[turntable]] and a watchtower that fires a chain, integrated with the railyard's maintenance infrastructure.
• Location of the [[Nightmare]]'s routing.
• Site of the initial gremlin contact with [[Widget]].
• Adjacent to a large [[turntable]] [[platform]] and a nearby watchtower.
