# fourth_floor_system_announcement

• "Hello, Crawlers. Welcome to the fourth floor!" introduces the [[the tangle]], states the floor-specific rule about [[stairwell]] timing, announces patron bidding, and activates hunting/[[bounty]] mechanics and the populated leaderboard.
• [[Stairwells]] will only open six hours prior to collapse, and "You may not go down early this level."
• Patron bidding is activated.
• The top-10 [[bounty]]/hunting rules for the floor are announced.
