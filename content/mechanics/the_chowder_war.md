# the_chowder_war

• An explicitly announced [[dungeon]] quest in the chapter revolving around the seasonal conflict between the [[Monk Seals]] and the [[Red Maníseros [[Land]] Crabs]], choosing a side yields a reward (Platinum Quest Box).
• The party's reconnaissance and actions influenced a local quest/conflict between the [[Monk Seals]] and the [[Red Maníseros [[Land]] Crabs]] that initially active but then failed in this chapter.
• The seasonal conflict quest pits [[Monk Seals]] against [[Red Maníseros [[Land]] Crabs]] and rewards a [[Platinum Quest Box]] upon acceptance/completion, shown as a "New Quest" system announcement.
• Players are prompted with choice- driven resolution text in the chapter involving [[Crawler Sister Ines Quiteria]] and the [[Monk [[Seal]] Abbot]].
• The quest/ mechanic introduced and failed in the chapter, describing the escalating conflict between [[Monk Seals]] and [[Red Maníseros [[Land]] Crabs]] and serves as the narrative trigger for [[Hell Comes to [[Crawler]] Town]].
