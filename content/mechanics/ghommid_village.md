# ghommid_village

• Small settlement at chapter start, populated by [[ghommids]], with dissipated fog and a cartoonish atmosphere.
• Location where [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]] worked on anti-undead automatons.
• Nearby village of undead NPCs, unhelpful for demon [[portal]] bait since undead do not count for demon banishment mechanics.
• Small settlement encountered at chapter start, with fog lifted to reveal a cartoon-like vibe and jovial ghommid NPCs, exited to move through [[Havana]].
