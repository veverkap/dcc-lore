# control_sigil

• A visible gating symbol that can be seen by those with the [[Escape Plan]] skill; used by showrunners or secondary programs (e.g., [[the vengeance of the daughter]]) to [[gate]] mobs, control passage, or enforce [[stage]] directions. Blue indicates active; it has no effect on the sigil-user and is considered a sloppy but used piece of programming.
• The symbol is blue when active and can [[gate]] or control mob movement. Only visible to [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]] due to [[Escape Plan (Skill)]].
