# pet_bonding_rules

• Pets must not have active aggro (dot must turn white) to begin bonding.
• Pets acquired as treasure-room "pet-class" start at level one until bonded.
• Bonding deadline is tied to floor collapse (must bond before floor ends).
• Bonded pets begin leveling rapidly.
