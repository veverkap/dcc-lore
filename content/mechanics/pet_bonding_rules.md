# [[Pet Bonding Rules]]
Compilation of explicit pet mechanics described by [[Mordecai]]: pets must not have active aggro (dot must turn white) to begin bonding; pets acquired as treasure-room "pet-class" start at level one until bonded; bonding deadline tied to floor collapse (must bond before floor ends) and bonded pets then begin leveling rapidly.
