# Admin Notice: Administrative lock (Syndicate court)
System-level legal announcement that places items in a player's inventory under an administrative lock pending an appeal in the Syndicate court. Appears briefly when [[dungeon crawler carl]] loots [[Henrik]] and references litigation over pieces of the [[Gate of the Feral Gods (pieces)]], later displaying a court determination that removes the lock.
