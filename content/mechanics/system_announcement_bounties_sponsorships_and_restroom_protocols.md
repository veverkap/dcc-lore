# System Announcement: Bounties, Sponsorships, and Restroom Protocols
An active dungeon system broadcast informing crawlers of doubled bounties for listed targets, ongoing sponsorship bidding, and a protocol for relieving oneself outside (announce out loud, wait five seconds). Sets rules and incentives affecting crawler behavior.
