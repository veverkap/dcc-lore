# nebular_priests

• Robed hunter NPCs, often called "nebs", engage mantis warriors in [[town]] chaos.
• Some are killed during the explosion and berserk events.
• Others are marked by [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]] using the [[Ring of Divine Suffering]].
