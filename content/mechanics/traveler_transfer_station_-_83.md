# traveler_transfer_station_-_83

• [[Traveler Transfer Station 83]] allows access to other lines, including the [[Yellow Line]] and [[Nightmare Express]].
• It is referred to as a potential [[saferoom]]/transfer point, a strategic objective for the party.
• It is located after [[Mora Station 82]] on the Red Line.
