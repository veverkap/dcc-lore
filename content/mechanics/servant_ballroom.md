# servant_ballroom

• A designated holding area where attendants and non-performing guests are secured during the masquerade prior to being escorted to the [[Main Ballroom]].
• Area where attendants and non-performing attendants are to be secured during the masquerade according to [[Theobold]]’s instructions.
