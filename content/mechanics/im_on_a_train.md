# im_on_a_train

• Achievement unlocked on arrival to the Red Line, rewarding the [[Train Conductor’ s Souvenir Hat]].
• Triggered on entering the moving [[train]], awards the [[Train Conductor’ s Souvenir Hat]] and generates a system notification.
• Awarded upon arrival in the [[train]] car on [[the tangle]], granting the [[Train Conductor’ s Souvenir Hat]] and announced by the [[dungeon]] system.
