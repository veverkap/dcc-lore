# race_rules_last_heats_and_team_requirements

• Explicit [[dungeon]] rules broadcast by [[Cascadia]]: two races remain sequentially; the last [[heat]] has three teams per [[heat]] with only the [[winner]] surviving; seventh race must have three teams per [[heat]]; if a teammate escapes via [[deal]], remaining members still must complete the race.
