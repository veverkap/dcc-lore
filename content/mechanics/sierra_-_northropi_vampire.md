# [[Sierra - Northropi Vampire]]
The Level 65 neighborhood boss encountered in the forest: an undead, vampiric pterosaur formerly trained by [[Viscount Fog]]. Described as a massive, vulture/pelican-like flying beast with an enormous beak and deadly talons. The party frees her from undeath by stabbing her heart, triggering the boss death.
