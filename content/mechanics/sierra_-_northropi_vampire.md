# sierra_-_northropi_vampire

• The Level 65 neighborhood boss, Sierra - Northropi Vampire, is an undead, vampiric pterosaur formerly trained by [[Viscount Fog]].
• She is described as a massive, vulture/pelican-like flying beast with an enormous beak and deadly talons.
• The party frees her from undeath by stabbing her heart, triggering the boss death.
• Sierra is a vampiric, undead, pterodactyl-like mount of [[Viscount Fog]] that became corrupted.
• She was defeated by [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]] after stabbing her heart.
