# the_lollipop

• A quieter [[bar]] beside [[The Hairpin]] where the protagonists plan to meet, serving as a rendezvous point for the local racing crowd and contacts like [[Imani]].
• A crowded [[bar]]/karaoke venue on Floor 15 where crawlers and NPCs gather between heats, featuring live acts (including [[Dekoki]]) and a prominent rule override: safe room protections do not apply here.
• A neighboring [[bar]] to [[The Hairpin]] in [[Hungry Eyes Vendor Village]] where [[Carl]] and companions are set to meet, featuring a neon sign with a gremlin and a [[lollipop]], and advertising karaoke.
