# [[The Lollipop]]
A quieter bar beside [[The Hairpin]] where the protagonists plan to meet. Its neon signage features a gremlin waving a giant lollipop; it serves as a rendezvous point for the local racing crowd and contacts like [[Imani]].

# [[The Lollipop]]
A crowded bar/karaoke venue on Floor 15 where crawlers and NPCs gather between heats. Noted for its live acts (including [[Dekoki]]) and a prominent rule override: safe room protections do not apply here.
