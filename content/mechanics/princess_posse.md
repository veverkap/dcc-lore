
# princess_posse

• The Princess Posse [[camp]] is a location where [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]] materializes after teleporting out of the hearing, and is the setting for the immediate post-[[teleport]] combat and the sluggang briefing.
• The Princess Posse forward position is a named forward defensive position occupied by the defenders, currently defended by the [[102nd bulwark battalion]], and includes [[trenches]], periscopes, and fortifications.
