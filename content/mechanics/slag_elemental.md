# slag_elemental

• A large molten-metal gorilla-like boss encountered in the room, known as the "Level 75 Neighborhood Boss".
• Displayed as an infused, regenerating elemental that returns to the molten [[river]] if it falls.
• Throws globs of metal and blocks racers on the ceiling tracks.
