# Slag Elemental
A large molten-metal gorilla-like boss encountered in the room. Displayed as "Level 75 Neighborhood Boss" and described by system warnings as an infused, regenerating elemental that returns to the molten river if it falls. These elementals throw globs of metal and block racers on the ceiling tracks.
