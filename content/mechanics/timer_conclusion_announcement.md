# Timer Conclusion Announcement
System announcement heard at the end of the recording: "The timer has concluded. You may now proceed inside to face the keyholder." Signals the end of the playback window and permission to move to the next objective. Directly addressed to [[dungeon crawler carl]] and [[Donut]].
