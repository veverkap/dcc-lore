# the_toe

* A local [[bar]]/[[inn]] in [[Hump Town]] where the party gathers, eats, and views system messages, employing the changeling [[Juice Box]] and serving as a staging point.
* A meeting point for group recaps and gatherings, referenced by [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]].
* A local [[bar]] where [[saferoom]] protections are discussed, and an example of an establishment that may not be a true [[saferoom]] unless crawlers are present.
* A tavern/[[bar]] where [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]]'s group meets other crawlers, including [[Louis]], [[Firas]], and [[Juice Box]], and serves as a hub for social interaction and planning.
* A tavern where [[Mordecai]] sits and where part of [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]]'s network operates, used as a social hub and source of local rumors.
* A staging area for missions and a social hub where [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]] waits, interacts with locals, and coordinates with party members.
* A small, personally secure [[saferoom]] location recommended by [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]] as the evacuation point for local children.
* A [[pub]]/ personal-space entrance in [[Hump Town]] where [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]] and his party gather children and crawlers, used as a staging area for evacuation.
* A district in [[town]] heavily damaged when a chunk of the [[Wasteland]] fell, still allowing access to the personal space through a front door.
* An [[inn]]/ tavern in [[Hump Town]] run by a Dromedarian proprietor, hosting the incoming floor announcement screens and employing the changeling [[Juice Box]].
* A local [[bar]]/ [[saferoom]] and recurring meeting point referenced as the planned meetup place for crawlers' recap episodes and gatherings.
* A named [[bar]] in the dromedarian [[town]] discussed as not being a true [[saferoom]], used as an example when [[Mordecai]] explains [[saferoom]] mechanics.
* A tavern where several party members, including [[Mordecai]], congregate, a local hub referenced for intel about outside forces.
* A local establishment/ tavern where [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]] waits and interacts with other residents, serving as a social hub and staging area for missions.
* A [[pub]]/ personal-space entry in [[Hump Town]] used by [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]]'s group, observed to be open with children present during the evacuation.
* A neighborhood block mostly destroyed by a chunk of the [[Wasteland]], with its front door to the personal space remaining usable.
