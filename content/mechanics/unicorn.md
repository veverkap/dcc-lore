# unicorn

• The competitor who "cheated" to win the [[heat]] by riding up the wall and going over the [[Factory]] to the [[roof]], then dropping to the finish.
• A figure of opinion in the chat, referenced as "I agree with the unicorn", indicating another voiced perspective within the group.
• A summoned or mounted creature whose magical horn is removed during combat and transferred into [[Donut]]'s inventory.
