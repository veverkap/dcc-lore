# turkey_barn

• The Turkey Barn is a massive, corrugated farm building containing tens of thousands of turkeys.
• The boss [[Turkey]] is located in the rafters.
• The [[Spiked Ogre]] is awakened in the barn.
• The interior is filled with tens of thousands of [[Turkey]] mobs.
• The barn is temperature-controlled with heaters.
• A boss [[Turkey]] is hidden in the rafters.
• The [[Ogre]] is located in the depths of the barn, creating a trap-filled combat zone.
