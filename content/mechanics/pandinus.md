# pandinus

• Coastal [[town]] with half-human, half-scorpion centaur-like pazuzu inhabitants who wear post-apocalyptic gear.
• Smaller [[town]] near the [[necropolis]] with inns, taverns, and clubs (including Club Vanquisher and The Death Stalker tavern).
• Pazuzu [[town]] on the [[land]] [[quadrant]] where Carl's group lands after using the [[Gate of the Feral Gods]].
• Shoreline [[town]] that sheltered Carl's group after they exited the flooded [[quadrant]], populated by punk-styled pazuzu.
• Location of the [[inn]]/personal space where Gwen and others wait.
