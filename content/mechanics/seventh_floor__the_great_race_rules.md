# seventh_floor__the_great_race_rules

• [[Crawler]] pods open to starting chutes
• One-way maze [[tunnels]] with nodes and branching paths (no dead ends)
• Pitstop saferooms
• Path-closure after 10 traversals or a party passes
• Zero tubes usable only once
• Math/puzzle checks on some branches
• Explicit non-backtracking rules
