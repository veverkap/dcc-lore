# [[Seventh Floor — The Great Race Rules]]
System announcement describing the floor’s mechanics: crawler pods open to starting chutes, one-way maze tunnels with nodes and branching paths (no dead ends), pitstop saferooms, path-closure after 10 traversals or a party passes, zero tubes usable only once, math/puzzle checks on some branches, and explicit non-backtracking rules.
