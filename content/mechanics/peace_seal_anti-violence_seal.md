# Peace Seal (anti-violence seal)
An explicit dungeon rule/protection active in the goodwill ballrooms that prevents violent actions while in effect. Central mechanical barrier preventing straightforward assaults and forcing complex plans (e.g., breaking the seal to leave or fight).
