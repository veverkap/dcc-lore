# goblin

• Generic small green humanoid mob encountered on [[Dungeon Floor 1]]; appears at Level 2 in the chapter and attacks [[dungeon [[crawler]] carl]] alongside the [[murder dozer]].
• Operate crude machinery (including the [[murder dozer]]), speak a guttural tongue, and can use small weapons.
• Common humanoid [[dungeon]] mobs encountered and charmed by [[Donut]]; killing and interacting with goblins yields boxes and [[loot]] such as an [[Engineer’s Table]] and explosive-related [[loot]].
• [[Goblin]] is referenced generically in the author’s anecdote as a creature the author insulted (calling it an incel), which sparked threats from a person named [[Kyle]].
• Represent the fantasy/mob concept invoked for humor in the chapter.
