# warlord_system

• The in-world command-and-communication interface manages platoons, Personal Guards, [[transport]] slots, armor/vehicle limits, and global battle messages.
• The warlord system governs faction leadership, including warlord chat, army size and recruitment, officer assignment, territory spots on the map, truce offers and naming, and status notifications.
