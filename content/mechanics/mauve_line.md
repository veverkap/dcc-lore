# mauve_line

• A named transit line used in the party's transfer plan, connecting with the [[Nightmare Express]] at stop 283 and intersecting with the [[Dismemberment Limited]] at 281.
• A named [[train]] line on the current floor, opening at "Mauve Line - [[Station 281]]" and planned to be ridden two stops to 283 to catch the [[Nightmare Express]].
• A [[platform]] actively used by players for grinding in the chapter, with [[Donut]] describing standing on the Mauve [[Platform]] to attack incoming monsters.
• A colored transit line intersecting with the [[Ochre Line]] network, with the [[Nightmare Express]] stopping on this line two stations down from the [[Dismemberment Limited]] connection.
• A named transit line used in multi-line routing, connecting with the [[Nightmare Express]] at overlapping stops like 283 and stop 436 as an objective.
