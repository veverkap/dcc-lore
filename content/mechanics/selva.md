# selva

• A [[jungle]] biome/zone entered by Carl at the chapter start, characterized by dense foliage, heavy humidity, and tropical wildlife.
• Located immediately outside the city borders of [[Zockau]] on this floor, serving as a relative safe haven for Crawlers fleeing the city's opaque border protections.
• The setting for the encounter near the [[Medium Dryad Settlement]].
• The [[jungle]] region outside the city border in this iteration of the [[Hunting Grounds]], where Crawlers are relatively safer after fleeing [[Zockau]].
