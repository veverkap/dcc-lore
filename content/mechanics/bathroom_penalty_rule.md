# [[Bathroom Penalty Rule]]
Explicit dungeon rule announced: any human-born crawler who intentionally urinates or defecates outside a designated bathroom area will be immediately penalized by having a [[Rage Elemental]] dispatched from the [[thirteenth floor]] to kill them and their party.
