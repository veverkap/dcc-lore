# Outreach Guild Deal Options
A system mechanic describing the contract/offers presented by the [[Outreach Guild]] staff (as exemplified by the [[Soother Attorney]]). Notable offers in the chapter:
- "Low floor undead": 15 seasons, high likelihood of death, subsistence stipend on release, no guaranteed housing.
- "City attendant for the [[Hunting Grounds]]": 30 seasons, labeled "good" with double stipend.
- "Game Guide": 100 seasons (potentially shortened based on crawler progress), grants level 50 or half current level (whichever is higher), relatively safe with greatest wealth potential.
- Option to be a guard at the [[Rogue Club]] is mentioned as an alternative.
This mechanic includes selection timing (offers change if you return to the table or if you fail to descend) and ties outcomes to survival likelihood and stipend/wealth outcomes.
