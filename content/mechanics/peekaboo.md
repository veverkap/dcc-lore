# Peekaboo
A mystic-card effect cast by [[Der Schachmeister]] in the fight that lets an opponent see a deckmaster’s hand for the duration of the battle (floating bloodshot eyes visualize this effect).

# Peekaboo!
An announcer/system mystic-card effect in the simulation that allows the opposing deckmaster ([[Der Schachmeister]]) to see the contents of the player's hand for a duration; presented as an active system announcement during the fight.
