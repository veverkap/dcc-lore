# Premature Descent Stasis Rule
An explicit dungeon rule announced during the premiere: if players descend floors prematurely, they are held in stasis until the collapse. The announcement also recommends descending no earlier than six hours before a scheduled collapse to avoid stasis and to gain a head start.
