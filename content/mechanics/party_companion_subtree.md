# Party Companion subtree
An explicit result tree classification used by enhanced/enriched pet biscuits. Contains branches that produce various party-companion outcomes such as the “Enhanced Growth” result befitting [[Princess Donut]] and the party-companion variants like [[nothing special]] and the [[Paramour benefit]].
