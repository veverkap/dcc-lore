# monk_seal_totem

• A [[totem]] card type that can be summoned from the party's [[cards]].
• Produces a summon described as a "Monk Seal Picket [[Sentry]]".
• Can hold up to two utility [[cards]] (uncommon totems).
• A solid tank and melee fighter, part of [[dungeon crawler carl]] and [[Donut]]'s intended squad composition.
• Summons a monk seal picket [[sentry]] (example: [[Geraldo]]).
• Has rarity-based utility-slot limits (uncommon totems hold two utility [[cards]]).
