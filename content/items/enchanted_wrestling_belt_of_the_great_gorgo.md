# enchanted_wrestling_belt_of_the_great_gorgo

• Legendary [[gold]] wrestling belt obtained from a Platinum Slam Master box, granting +5% Strength, +5% Constitution, and the "Avalanche" benefit (increases impact when moving or body-slaming a living creature).
