# train_conductor_hat

* A souvenir [[hat]] that functions as a [[portal]]-compatible [[key]] when equipped, allowing passage through certain portals at the cost of leaving [[train]] cars behind.
* A tradable [[hat]] used as currency/aid in the chapter, valuable enough to buy a social media board.
* A small [[hat]] given as tribute by crawlers, used as currency/tribute in the chapter.
* A non-magical blue-and-white [[hat]] rewarded by the "I'm on a [[train]]!" achievement with the embroidered text "I rode the Iron Tangle."
* A souvenir/equippable [[hat]] that functions as a compatible [[key]] for the one-way Ultima Corp DungeonWerx Industrial Subspace [[Portal]].
* A widely distributed [[hat]] used as a crowd-control/tribute item and temporary currency.
