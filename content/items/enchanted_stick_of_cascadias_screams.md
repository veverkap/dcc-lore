# enchanted_stick_of_cascadias_screams

• A metal rod/enchanted stick given to the [[popov brothers]] to deliver to [[Donut]], with a small hidden symbol interpreted as the number seven.
• Used by [[Crawler Prepotente]] to push into the glass wall, contributing to its cracking and breaching on the seventh floor.
• A magical baton given to party members, used to pierce the glass pod and play a direct role in shattering the level's construction.
• Has a vague description: "This is a stick. It's enchanted."
