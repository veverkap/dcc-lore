# spider_automaton

• Summoned/mechanical spiders, some armed with [[explosives]] or holy grenades, can be deployed for damage or utility in the corrupted town.
• Small programmable robots (utility and anti-undead variants) deployed by [[dungeon crawler carl]] can apply [[Demon Snare]] and smoke effects, then detonate, and can be outfitted with [[holy goopers]] or healing items.
• Small, rat-sized explosive construct bots, programmed to target "Hunter Not Already Targeted" and set to detonate, can be activated by [[Donut]].
• Deployable mechanical minions carried by [[dungeon crawler carl]] can be armed with [[explosives]] or outfitted with [[holy goopers]] or healing items to tackle undead or corporeal threats.
• A small robotic device deployed by the protagonist can carry a [[teleport]] trap anchor and bump into the snake city boss to activate the trap and prevent the snake from teleporting away while breaking it from its shell.
