# combo_card

• A consumable in-game card used to combine two [[totem]] [[cards]] and consume their utility buffs.
• Used to alter the faction deck, as demonstrated by [[Donut]].
• Behaves differently from common [[cards]] like [[Time Extend card]] and [[Stout card]].
• Not a one-time consumable like some others.
• Used to make room or change the active deck composition by combining problematic [[totem]] [[cards]] and consuming their utility buffs.
