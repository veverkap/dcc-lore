# no_disassemble

• Protective ability invoked by [[dungeon crawler carl]] that interacts with other explosive/system effects.
• Unstable unexploded items collected by [[Louis]] that are highly volatile and dangerous if triggered.
• Inventory explosive devices that function like an EMP/pulse, detonated inside the [[Reaver castle]] to neutralize [[sentry]] dogs, but produced a massive pulse that damaged surrounding structures and allies.
• A specialized missile type referenced by [[Colonel Boomer]] as necessary to disable enemy electronics.
