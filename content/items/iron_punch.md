# Iron Punch
A bare-knuckle combat skill gained by [[dungeon crawler carl]] ("Iron Punch level 3" notification). Provides enhanced fist damage, with each level adding an additional 10% to enhanced-fist damage; synergies with hand protection like gauntlets.

# Iron Punch
Carl's strength-based combat skill that leveled to 10 in the chapter. Its tenth level grants the "Breadbasket" effect: a 10% chance to stun a mob on a solid blow.
