# iron_punch

• A bare-knuckle combat skill gained by [[dungeon crawler carl]].
• Provides enhanced fist damage, with each level adding an additional 10% to enhanced-fist damage.
• Synergizes with hand protection like gauntlets.
• Level 10 grants the "Breadbasket" effect: a 10% chance to stun a mob on a solid blow.
