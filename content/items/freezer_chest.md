# freezer_chest

• A never-ending supply storage unit inside the [[Truck]] containing frozen chicken patties, French fries, and tater tots.
• Items reappear seconds after being removed; questioned by [[Donut]] for its source.
• Managed by [[Donut]] and frequently accessed by team members like [[Mongo]] and [[Rend]].
