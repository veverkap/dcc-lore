# alcohol_cure_potion

• A consumable item used to cure intoxication.
• Given to [[Donut]] by [[dungeon crawler carl]] to clear her inebriation.
• Can cause temporary potion sickness before fully clearing intoxication effects.
• Used to counteract intoxication, with the narrator checking their inventory during a club visit.
• A generic term for [[potions]] that cure drunkenness, with [[dungeon crawler carl]] running out of stock before [[Mordecai]] brewed [[Rapid Detox]].
• Used to cure [[Donut]]'s inebriation, removing alcohol effects but causing temporary potion sickness before the effect is fully cleared.
