# gold_apparel_box

• An apparel reward box granted to [[dungeon crawler carl]] by the [[Why Aren't You Wearing Pants?]] achievement after he enters the dungeon without pants.
• A [[gold]]-tier apparel loot box that produced the [[Enchanted Nightgaunt [[Cloak]] of Stoutness]] and the [[Enchanted Trollskin Shirt of Pummeling]].
• A reward box listed on the [[PVP Coupon]]; contains apparel rewards for the killer.
• A reward item listed on PVP coupons; provides apparel rewards when redeemed.
• An apparel reward contained among the [[PVP Coupon]] redemption items; listed as part of the [[coupon]]'s guaranteed loot.
