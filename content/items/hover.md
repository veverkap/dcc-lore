# [[Hover]]
Skill granted by the [[Enchanted Tiara of the Inebriated Dragonfly]] (level 10). Allows hovering 2–3 feet off the ground for about 90 seconds; five-minute cooldown and limited to five uses per day; can be used to reduce fall damage and traverse shallow water.
