# hover

• Skill granted by the [[Enchanted [[Tiara]] of the Inebriated Dragonfly]] (level 10), allowing hovering 2–3 feet off the ground for about 90 seconds, with a five-minute cooldown and limited to five uses per day.
• Can be used to reduce fall damage and traverse shallow water.
• Allows the user to float 2–3 feet off the ground for about 90 seconds, with a five-minute cooldown and limited to five uses per day.
• Functions like a targeted levitation but does not cancel on damage and helps prevent fall damage.
• Can be used to reach the top of the dirt mound, traverse shallow water, and reduce fall damage.
• Is a five-uses-per-day ability that can be used to stay aloft if the tower collapses.
• Is a personal active skill/spell used by [[Donut]] via a dragonfly [[tiara]], described as a five-uses-per-day skill that enables hovering.
• Is an ability/spell used by [[Donut]] to float and reach the ceiling of the [[Hangar]] to calm [[Jamal]].
