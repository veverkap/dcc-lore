# gangway_chock

• A heavy, rectangular metal wedge fabricated by [[dungeon crawler carl]] from a thinned swordsman breastplate to block inter-car gangways, designed to be pulled into/out of inventory for quick barricades.
• Used to barricade the gangway/door between cars on the [[Dismemberment Limited]] against Fleshers.
• Heavy, portable via inventory mechanics, and used to isolate cars from boarding monsters.
• Several sizes were used; one was placed as an improvised barrier.
• A large, rectangular metal wedge created by [[dungeon crawler carl]] (from a thinned breastplate) used to block [[train]] gangways.
• Used by [[dungeon crawler carl]] and [[Katia]] to jam and brace the sliding gangway door between [[train]] cars to prevent Fleshers from entering.
