# Lucienne's Racing Helmet
The small, cracked racing helmet recovered from [[Lucienne]]'s remains. It is described as gore-soaked and later judged by [[Donut]] to be cursed; its recovery prompts discussion of curses and references to [[Michale Graves’s disease]].
