# Yog's Special Chain
A named scroll/item obtained from a Gold box that produces an extendable, ultra-light chain (usable up to extreme lengths, e.g., 1,000 feet) but with a limited duration (about 30 hours). It is the anchor used to build the 300-foot linkage of the [[Parade Train]].
