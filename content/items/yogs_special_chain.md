# yogs_special_chain

• A named scroll/item obtained from a [[Gold]] box that produces an extendable, ultra-[[light]] chain usable up to extreme lengths (e.g., 1,000 feet) with a limited duration (about 30 hours).
• Used as the anchor to build the 300-foot linkage of the [[Parade Train]].
