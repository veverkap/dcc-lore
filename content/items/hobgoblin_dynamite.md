# hobgoblin_dynamite

Here is the consolidated markdown list:

* [[Hobgoblin Dynamite]]: a more powerful, stable explosive variant found in higher-tier loot, used alongside [[Hobgoblin Pus]] remote detonators.
* Higher-yield/different-quality sticks of [[dynamite]] sold by [[Pustule]] at [[Hobgobs and Boom Sticks]], with example pricing: 20 sticks for 2,000 [[gold]] (noted as expensive).
* An explosive stick used by [[dungeon crawler carl]] (upgraded version), extremely powerful and capable of obliterating shamblers and causing large structural collapse.
* High-explosive demolition material used by [[dungeon crawler carl]] to destroy the two automatons for scrap.
* A more stable, powerful explosive used by Carl to craft a [[train]]-mounted [[bomb]], bundles were attached to the [[Vermillion train]] and detonated with an impact-detonated hoblobber pole.
* Explosive munition sold by [[desperado club]] and referenced as part of station preparations, used by some groups to fortify chokepoints.
* A high-damage explosive used by [[dungeon crawler carl]] to destroy the pair of [[Dwarven Industrial [[Light]]-Duty Automaton]]s, effective at producing salvageable scrap.
* Stable, high-yield explosive used by [[dungeon crawler carl]] to create the [[train]] [[bomb]], more powerful than regular goblin [[dynamite]] and sensitive to assembly context (yields can change on a sapper's table).
* Hand-deployed explosive used by defenders (thrown by [[dungeon crawler carl]]) as an anti-pet [[grenade]] to clear incoming rot sticker behemoths at the trench edge.
* Higher-end explosive sticks sold by [[Pustule]] with greater yield and stability than [[hobgoblin dynamite]], priced significantly higher per-stick in the shop inventory.
* An upgraded, highly destructive explosive stick used by [[dungeon crawler carl]] from a rooftop against shambling berserkers, extremely powerful (near "mini-[[nuke]]" yield) and impractical for close combat.
* Explosive charges used by [[dungeon crawler carl]] to destroy the pair of [[Dwarven Industrial [[Light]]-Duty Automaton]] units and turn them into scrap.
* A more stable, higher-yield demolition explosive used by [[dungeon crawler carl]] to rig a backward-running vermillion engine into a [[bomb]] [[train]], survives multiple impacts better than regular goblin [[dynamite]] and bundle yield affected the explosion size.
* Explosive items bought at the [[desperado club]] for use in planned defenses and crowd control across stations.
* Explosive material found among standard loot, referenced as a traditional explosive alongside [[Hobgoblin Pus]] and [[Troll Boom-Boom Paste]] for [[bomb]]-making.
* A type of explosive used by [[dungeon crawler carl]] for practice and crafting, he slices one into pieces to work on [[explosives]].
