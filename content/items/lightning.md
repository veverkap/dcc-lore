# lightning

• A spell used by a mage in the party that hits and cooks a crawler during one of the magic bursts.
• A level-15, trainable to 20, spell offered as a reward for repairing [[Shango]]'s [[shrine]], potentially useful for future encounters.
• A spell referenced when a lightning [[bolt]] struck a party member during the fourth pulse, leaving a crawling newcomer unconscious.
