# jefferson_the_nickel_sock

• Named sapient weapon looted from [[Quan]], triggering the "Uh oh. It talks." achievement
• Recognized as potentially powerful but also risky and annoying
• Associated with Dong Quixote, current holder
• Sapient, incremental-damage weapon that increases in damage with kills, grants dexterity bonuses, can inflict "Bonked" status, and has a chance to make mobs burst into [[gold]]
• Can be used as a flail-like weapon substitute
• Has a peculiar relationship with its wielder's mind
• An unusual high-value item, a literal [[sock]] filled with [[nickels]] found among Quan's gear
• The grotesque, sapient-[[sock]] left in the garage, sustaining and passively healing Dong Quixote by proximity
