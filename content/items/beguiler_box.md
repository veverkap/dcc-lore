# beguiler_box

• A loot container associated with charm-related rewards, yielding items like the [[Minion Army]] tome.
• A variant of the Beguiler reward crate received by [[Donut]] for charming goblins, containing spells, tomes, and consumables.
• The source from which [[Donut]] originally obtained the [[Minion Army]] spellbook on the first floor.
• A loot container from which [[Donut]] obtained the [[Minion Army]] spellbook earlier in the campaign.
• A reward box that yielded items for charm-related achievements, mentioned as what [[Princess Donut]] received for charming [[Ferdinand]].
