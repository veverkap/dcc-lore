# sense_movement

• Advanced map-making skill used by [[Edgar the tortoise]] that allows sensing movement inside structures, including invisible and non-corporeal movement at high levels.
• Level-15 skill used by [[Edgar]] that detects upward momentum between floors, providing evidence that someone nearby has just worshiped a god for the first time.
