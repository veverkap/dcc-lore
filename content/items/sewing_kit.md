# sewing_kit

• Tool used by [[dungeon crawler carl]] to affix a patch to his jacket, granting a sewing achievement and skill levels; used to repair and apply gear patches.
• Used to stitch a patch onto his jacket, granting an achievement and sewing skill levels; used to affix [[Katia]]'s deliberate patch over a [[bomb]] patch.
