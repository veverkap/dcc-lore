# depth_charges

• Underwater [[explosives]] prepared as a last resort to use against heavy underwater security.
• Explosive equipment set to maximum depth to use against [[Lusca]] while inside the [[Akula]].
• Explosive devices (also called "death charges") deployed against [[Lusca]] with multi-layered barrel designs and impact-detonated hob-lobbers.
• Barrel-launched [[explosives]] prepared as a last-resort option to attack or disable underwater threats near the [[Akula]].
• Explosive ordinance set to maximum depth to attempt damage against [[Lusca]] once the sub is swallowed.
• Also jokingly called "death charges" by commentators; multi-layered explosive barrels used against [[Lusca]]'s outer area earlier in the fight.
