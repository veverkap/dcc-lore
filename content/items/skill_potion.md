# skill_potion

• Consumable item that grants a single level to a specific skill.
• Recognized by dark-blue fizzy coloring in the chapter.
• Offered as a Prize Carousel option and used to grant temporary or permanent skill/stat effects to crawlers.
• Grants points to skills (e.g., Donut drank one to add to Animal Wrangling).
• [[Skill Potion]] is a consumable that grants points to a specific skill (Donut drinks one to raise Animal Wrangling).
• A skill potion obtained from the [[Gold Payload Box]] that expands a player's 10-space hotlist into 20 spaces; directly compared to [[Lucia Mar]]'s inventory.
• A potion consumed by [[Dungeon Crawler Carl]] that expands the hotlist from 20 to 25 spaces and unlocks the [[Contingency skill]].
• A potion reward from the [[Platinum Boss Box]] that expands hotlist capacity from 20 to 25 slots and unlocks the [[Contingency skill]]/automation features tied to [[Hotlist Contingencies]].
