# good_mana_refill

• A slower-acting mana-restoring consumable carried by [[Bravvo]] (thirty found), adding 200 mana points over two minutes.
• Unlike mana [[potions]], these refill magic over time.
• Discussed by [[dungeon crawler carl]] as situationally useful.
