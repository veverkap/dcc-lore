# Good Mana Refill
A slower-acting mana-restoring consumable carried by [[Bravvo]] (thirty found). Unlike [[mana potion]]s, these refill magic over time—adding 200 mana points over two minutes—and are discussed by [[dungeon crawler carl]] as situationally useful.
