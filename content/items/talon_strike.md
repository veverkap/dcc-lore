# [[Talon Strike]]
A melee skill granted by a gem on the [[Enchanted Necklace of the Haute Bourgeoisie]]. Turns the side of the foot into a slashing weapon, scales with skill level, can proc the instakill spell Eviscerate at low chance, and has a cooldown. At level 15 becomes permanently active.
