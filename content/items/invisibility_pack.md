# invisibility_pack

• A higher-tier invisibility item available at the prize counter, distinct from single [[potions]].
• Purchased options include both [[potions]] and packs.
• Prize purchasable from the counter, bought by [[Zabit]] for three hands.
• Included as a case-based item and delivered to box inventory.
