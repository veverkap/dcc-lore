# bris

• A spell in [[raul the crab]]'s kit, identified by [[Donut]] during a Size-Up, which can be spelled like "Brisk" without a k.
• A once-per-fight ranged spell cast by [[raul the crab]] after a short meditation, allowing him to crush and decapitate enemies from a distance, and was instrumental in killing a level-100 wolf in the practice bout.
• A spell (named in-text as "Bris") noted among [[raul the crab]]'s abilities, with pronunciation suggested as like "Brisk" without the k.
• A once-per-fight spell cast by the crab [[totem]] [[raul the crab]] after a short meditation, functioning as a powerful ranged crushing attack capable of decapitating an enemy [[totem]] at a distance.
