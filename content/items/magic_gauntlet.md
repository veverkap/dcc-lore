# magic_gauntlet

• Carl's [[gauntlet]] weapon/equipment provides offensive abilities, such as "Bang Bro".
• Has a special cumulative summon chance that risks summoning [[Grull]] when punches hit.
• Grants the ability to cast or channel [[Bang Bro]].
• Attacks are central to the melee exchange with [[Gore-Gore the Manticore]].
