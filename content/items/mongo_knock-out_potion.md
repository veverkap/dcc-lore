# mongo_knock-out_potion

• A specially brewed knockout potion intended for use on a [[Mongo]], used to sedate and extract blood for a flask.
• Limited supply noted by [[Mordecai]].
• Made by [[Mordecai]] to incapacitate a "[[Mongo]]", distinct from the "[[Mongo]] sleep potion" mentioned as lacking.
