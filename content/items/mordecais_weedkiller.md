A potion provided by [[Mordecai]] that acts as a poison/weedkiller effective against the Odious Creeper when delivered via siege weapon.

# [[Mordecai's Weedkiller]]
A potion/weedkiller created by [[Mordecai]] that, when poured into the trebuchet cup, modifies the delivery to kill creepers; noted to work even in rain and speed up kills on Odious Creepers.
