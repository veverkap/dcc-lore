# wheel_of_fortune

• The Wheel of Fortune is a large, [[gold]]-leafed wheel with multiple reward and penalty wedges, including a two-space "Nothing" slice and a "Dirty Tea Bag" wedge.
• The croupier, [[Mitch Fiorelli]], can physically manipulate the wheel to produce a temporary [[portal]] beneath its "stand here" spot.
• The wheel's correct sliver and button are required to open the [[portal]].
• The wheel maintains a magical connection that prevents safe inventory transfer.
