# Wheel of Fortune
The casino game central to the chapter: a large, gold-leafed wheel with multiple reward and penalty wedges, including a two-space "Nothing" slice and a "Dirty Tea Bag" wedge. It can be physically manipulated by the croupier ([[Mitch Fiorelli]]) to produce a temporary portal beneath its "stand here" spot.
