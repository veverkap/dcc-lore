# smoke_bomb

* A consumable tactical item used to create cover and obscure vision, deployed in the street to impede pursuing mobs; effectiveness can be influenced by wind.
* Consumable stealth/utility items found among [[dungeon crawler carl]]'s recent loot drops.
* Apple-sized smoke [[bombs]] in [[dungeon crawler carl]]'s stash, whose size complicates use with his [[slingshot]]; a common consumable explosive/utility item in the world.
* Generic smoke-producing consumables sold at market stalls, with varieties differing in quality.
* A consumable used to mask movement, producing a lingering smoke curtain that later mixed with black smoke from fires and altered visibility during combat with the krasue.
* Consumable/throwable items prepared by [[dungeon crawler carl]] for [[obfuscation]] and retreat during potential boss/robot encounters.
* A consumable used to obscure the operation, sputtering out after the explosion and temporarily muting visibility and contributing to the smoke-filled environment in the magistrate's building.
* A tactical consumable used to create a smoke curtain during assaults on rooms filled with Cornets.
* A generic, non-magical smoke device Carl wishes for (one that would affect NPCs), used in the chapter alongside magical smoke to obscure vision on the street.
