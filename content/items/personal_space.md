# personal_space

• A purchasable in-dungeon asset that transfers between levels and safe locations; accessories and upgrades for a [[Personal Space]] are affected by when they are purchased (earlier purchases can produce stronger upgrades).
• A short-range displacement spell (from [[Katia]]'s beanie) acting like a miniature [[Protective Shell]] to scatter nymphs and open a path forward.
• A [[coupon]] item redeemable for a free personal space upgraded to level three (with fine-print limits) used by party members to acquire and conjoin a higher-tier [[Personal Space]].
• A purchasable/installable module item that upgrades a crawler's personal space; in the chapter several tier-3 modules were dropped by [[Lucia Mar]] and used to install fast-travel functionality via the Doggie Door.
• A crowd-control spell contained within [[Katia]]'s beanie that pushes nearby mobs away (miniature version of [[Protective Shell]]).
• A physical module item that, when installed into a player's personal space, upgrades room functionality; in this chapter [[Lucia Mar]] drops multiple modules; one is installed by [[Imani]] for party-wide fast-travel use.
• A [[coupon]] item that redeems a free level-three personal space upgrade; used by [[Princess Donut]], [[dungeon crawler carl]], and [[Katia]] to obtain conjoinable level-three rooms; coupons have a maximum value limit and certain usage restrictions.
