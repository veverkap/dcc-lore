# murder_dozer

• [[Murder Dozer]] is a goblin-engineered armored vehicle displayed in the workshop and considered too valuable to sell or part with by the goblin chieftain.
• A heavy, dozer-liked transport used by goblins in combat, prone to explosive incidents if pressure/relief controls are mishandled, and mentioned as having previously exploded in the area.
• A crude goblin-built armored vehicle with spinning front blades, passenger benches, and multiple levers, capable of transporting a goblin crew and driven by a goblin engineer.
• Its imminent failure triggers the system announcement [[Boiler Breach Imminent]].
• A destructive vehicle referenced historically from the first floor blast, serving as inspiration for [[Carl]]’s search for focused [[explosives]].
