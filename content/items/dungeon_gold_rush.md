# Dungeon Gold Rush
A red-and-green scratch-ticket item found inside a [[Platinum Lucky Bastard Box]] (owned by [[Princess Donut]]) with six scratch spots and effects like "Mob drops 5,000 coins" or negative outcomes such as "Mob is invulnerable for 30 seconds."
