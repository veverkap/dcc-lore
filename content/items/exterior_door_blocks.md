# exterior_door_blocks

• Improvised tall wedges made from altered weight benches and crossbars, used to brace and obstruct exterior [[train]] doors, functioning as temporary barricades.
• Field-engineered wedges made from altered weight benches with crossbar supports, designed to jam and brace exterior subway doors, used to slow or prevent monster boarding.
• [[dungeon crawler carl]] fashioned these wedges from altered weight benches with crossbars to jam subway doors, serving as a defensive engineering item for isolating cars.
