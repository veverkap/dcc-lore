# Exterior Door Blocks
Improvised tall wedges made from altered weight benches and crossbars used by [[dungeon crawler carl]] to brace and obstruct exterior train doors. Function as temporary barricades but were noted as imperfect on the Yellow Line door handholds.

# Exterior Door Blocks
Field-engineered wedges made from altered weight benches with crossbar supports designed by [[dungeon crawler carl]] to jam and brace exterior subway doors. They are an improvised door-securing item used to slow or prevent monster boarding.
