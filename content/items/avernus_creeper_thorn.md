# avernus_creeper_thorn

• A physical thorn from an [[Avernus Creeper]] that can grow into a full-sized Avernus plant on other floors, collected by Carl for [[Mordecai]] and explicitly prevented from sprouting on this floor.
• Each thorn can grow into a full-sized [[Avernus Creeper]] off-floor, disabled from sprouting on this floor, and sought by [[Mordecai]] for alchemical use.
