# just_wait_until_your_daddy_gets_home

• System-named wheeled [[bomb]] pushed out of the [[saferoom]] by [[dungeon crawler carl]] and detonated at the doorway, causing massive blast effects even inside the [[saferoom]].
• The system-named, large egg-shaped thermobaric explosive is high-yield, imbues [[Fear]] in its blast radius, and is rated with a fortified status in the simulator.
• An earlier, larger version of Carl's thermobaric explosive was used on the sixth floor, referenced for scale and lethality comparisons with [[waffle maker]].
• The system-named wheeled [[bomb]]/vehicle explosive was kicked out of the [[saferoom]] door by [[dungeon crawler carl]] to detonate and shift the battle, causing massive blast despite being inside a [[saferoom]].
