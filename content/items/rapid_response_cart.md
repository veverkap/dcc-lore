# rapid_response_cart

• The Rapid Response [[Cart]] is a small, raised cockpit vehicle used by [[dungeon crawler carl]] and crew to ride the lines at high speed.
• It is equipped with a windshield, throttle, and a [[portal]] attachment, allowing it to be tuned between destinations such as [[Abyss]] or specific [[Trainyard]]s.
• The [[cart]] is powered by batteries when line power is out.
• It is steerable but lacks brakes.
• The [[cart]] is used to scoop stranded crawlers into trainyards.
