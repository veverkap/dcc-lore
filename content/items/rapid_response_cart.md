# Rapid Response Cart
The small, raised cockpit vehicle [[dungeon crawler carl]] and crew use to ride the lines at high speed. Equipped with a windshield, throttle, and a portal attachment; can be tuned between destinations (e.g., [[Abyss]] or specific [[Trainyard]]s).

# Rapid Response Cart
A small, fast cockpit vehicle used by [[dungeon crawler carl]]'s team to ride the lines (powered by batteries when line power is out). The cart can have portals attached, is steerable but lacks brakes, and is used to scoop stranded crawlers into trainyards.
