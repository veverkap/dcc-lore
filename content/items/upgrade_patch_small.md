# upgrade_patch_small

• Grants +5% Strength and immunity to cloud-based attacks when affixed to eligible garments like the [[Enchanted Anarchist’s Battle Rattle]].
• Depicts a traveling Reaper Spider Minion merchant and grants access to the [[Midnight Market]], a notification for nearby traveling merchants, and +5% Dexterity when affixed.
• Grants "Lucky Shot" (redirects a fatal blow into a party member once per floor) when affixed to eligible clothing and is received inside the Legendary Lucky Bitch Box.
• Increases Strength by +5% and grants immunity from cloud-based attacks when affixed to eligible garments like the [[Enchanted Anarchist’s Battle Rattle]].
• Grants stat upgrades when affixed to eligible garments, such as +5% Dexterity and +5% ranged damage, but is destroyed if removed.
