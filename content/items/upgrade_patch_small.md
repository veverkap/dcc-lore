# Upgrade Patch. Small.
A small upgrade patch depicting Earth (the "Earth Patch") that, when affixed to eligible garments like the [[Enchanted Anarchist’s Battle Rattle]], grants +5% Strength and immunity to cloud-based attacks; removal destroys the patch.

# Upgrade Patch. Small.
Small, die-cut upgrade patch depicting a traveling Reaper Spider Minion merchant (reminiscent of [[Shi Maria]]); when affixed it grants access to the [[Midnight Market]], a notification for nearby traveling merchants, and +5% Dexterity, but is consumed on removal.
