# [[Summon Ally]]
A rare, multi-functional teleport spell central to the incident. Requires chat linkage, grants the summoned choice to accept, and has protections to prevent misuse; used by [[commander stockade of the lemig sortion]] to invite [[dungeon crawler carl]].
