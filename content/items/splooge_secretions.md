# [[Splooge (Secretions)]]
A foul-smelling substance sold by the "bat dudes" and stored in buckets; a potion variant that when sipped (about half a bottle) grants roughly half an hour of environmental protection. Also referred to in dialogue as "splooge" or "secretions."
