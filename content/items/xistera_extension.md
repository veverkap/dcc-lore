# xistera_extension

• A wearable extension device Carl uses to hold and propel [[Samantha]], also used to recall her after missions.
• A mechanical aid in long-range projectiles.
• A device Carl must keep on his arm to retrieve [[Samantha]] after throwing her into the river.
• A gnomish [[farseer]]/[[xistera]] accessory that Carl affixes to improve long-distance observation.
• A summon/teleportation device that Carl dropped to summon [[Samantha]] and [[Sister Ines Quiteria]], resulting in a massive area [[teleport]].
• A handheld equipment/extension used by Carl, functioning as a melee/throwing implement or extension in his loadout.
• A [[launch]]/retrieval device used by Carl to hold and fling [[Samantha]] and recall her on command.
• A mechanical/gear extension used by Carl to securely hold [[Samantha]] during the river-sacrifice operation.
• An arm-mounted extension worn by Carl that must be removed before diving.
• A personal extension tool used by Carl to extend his [[farseer]] or other sensory device, used to deploy and recall [[Samantha]] across the river for recon.
• A removable extension/capability referenced as a potential means to toss [[Samantha]] distances.
• A weapon/extension item used by Carl, functioning as part of his armament and can be summoned/unsummoned.
