# silver_adventurer_box

• Awarded to [[dungeon crawler carl]] under the [[Early Adopter]] achievement for being among the first 5,000 Crawlers to enter the new [[World Dungeon]].
• A mid-tier adventurer loot box.
• Example contents: [[antidote potion]], [[pet biscuit]], and [[Torch]] stacks.
• Contained the [[magic missile]] and pet biscuits in [[Princess Donut]]'s rewards.
• Contained a bracelet that added +2 Dexterity and other standard items in [[Princess Donut]]'s rewards.
