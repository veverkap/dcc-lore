# Black Nimbus
An item-based involuntary abjuration spell (granted by the [[Enchanted Nipple Ring of the Defiler]]). Instantly removes the target from the blessing of their deity and associated buffs, prevents physical entry to churches/temples for the duration, and increases chance of smites at higher levels. Duration and effect scale with casters' stats; cooldown once per hour.
