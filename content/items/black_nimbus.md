# black_nimbus

• Instantly removes the target from the blessing of their deity and associated buffs, prevents physical entry to churches/temples for the duration, and increases chance of smites at higher levels.
• Duration and effect scale with casters' stats; cooldown once per hour.
• Removes protection but not a deity's wrath.
• Prevents target NPCs from receiving divine spells, entering temples, or using god-granted abilities.
• Used to incapacitate [[Mork]] and negate the [[Basilica Guards]]' special effects.
• Can induce status on mantaurs.
• Consumable used during combat.
• Tied to the [[Enchanted Nipple [[Ring]] of the Defiler]] and accessible via the [[ring]].
• Used to remove the benefits of worshipping a god from a target.
• Can be used to remove [[Ogun]]'s protection (but not his wrath).
• Can be used to kick people out of temples, as seen with [[Katia]] and [[Prepotente]].
