# boon_from_emberus

• Beneficial effect granted by [[Emberus]] to [[dungeon crawler carl]]: previous step markers will explode in fire if Carl's health drops low enough.
• Described as dangerous and potentially hazardous to nearby allies like [[Donut]] and [[Mongo]].
