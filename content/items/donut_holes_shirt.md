# donut_holes_shirt

• A magical shirt worn by [[Louis]] that cannot be broken, even when his chest is split open, due to magic.
• The shirt is stained with blood but remains intact.
• An enchanted, hand-drawn prize tee worn by [[Louis]] that provides a mechanical benefit (increases range of his cloud attack).
• The shirt is bootleg merch of the [[donuts fan club]] fan club and is offensive to [[Donut]].
