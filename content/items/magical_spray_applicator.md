# Magical Spray Applicator
A vintage-style handheld spray applicator (magical canister) obtained from a benefactor box; required to properly apply a herbicide potion recipe (fine healing + blessed water + weedkiller) as a fine mist. Sought by [[Prepotente]] to combine with healing potions given by [[dungeon crawler carl]].
