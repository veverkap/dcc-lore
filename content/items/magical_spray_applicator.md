# magical_spray_applicator

• A vintage-styles handheld spray applicator (magical canister) obtained from a benefactor box; required to apply a herbicide potion recipe (fine healing + blessed water + weedkiller) as a fine mist.
• Sought by [[Prepotente]] to combine with healing [[potions]] given by [[dungeon crawler carl]].
• Functions as a magical canister received from a [[legendary benefactor box]].
