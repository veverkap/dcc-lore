# doggie_door_upgrade

• Installed upgrade enabling fast travel between the guild room and the planned [[Headquarters]]; a strategic mobility item referenced for logistics.
• Allows the party to exit via a higher [[ring]] entrance, enabling tactical movement by [[dungeon crawler carl]]'s team.
• Enables passage through [[saferoom]] entrances, used by [[Donut]] and [[dungeon crawler carl]] to return through the bait shack entrance; noted as a potential infiltration risk for off-world sponsors.
