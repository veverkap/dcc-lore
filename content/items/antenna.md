# antenna

• A hot soul-crystal plus [[capacitor]] device used by war mages to generate zarogoths, destroying it would collapse the generated mobs.
• An artifact sought by the Justice Lights Team, said to be inside the stomach of merchant Paca in the Midnight Market, treated as dangerous and desirable by certain characters.
