# rev-up_vitamin_shots

• Race-specific, addictive [[potions]]/drugs distributed via the Pooka tunnels at five-stop intervals.
• Consumed by mobs as "shots" (drank) to keep them looping the line and compliant.
• Absence of doses triggers physical transformations and aggressive behavior.
• Manufactured by [[Krakaren]].
• Distributed by [[Pooka]] units.
• Central to the floor's control system and drug loop.
