# instacot_60

• A bed upgrade item used in saferooms for rapid exhaustion resets, described as an upgrade from the guild used by [[dungeon crawler carl]] for quick rest.
• An in-guild [[saferoom]] item upgrade owned by [[dungeon crawler carl]] that allows rapid reset of exhaustion level (enables one-minute recovery in a [[saferoom]]).
• A bed upgrade that refreshes crawlers after sixty seconds, changes Good Rest to Great Rest, and grants a 15% stat bonus for the day.
