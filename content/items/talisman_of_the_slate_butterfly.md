# Talisman of the Slate Butterfly
A silver collar charm awarded to [[Donut]] from a gold magic item box. Effects: +4 to the [[Light on Your Feet]] skill, +1 Intelligence, and causes winged fairies to no longer be automatically hostile. Flavor text references the sad tale of the Slate Butterfly.
