# talisman_of_the_slate_butterfly

• A silver collar charm awarded to [[Donut]] from a [[gold]] magic item box, granting +4 to the [[Light on Your Feet]] skill, +1 Intelligence, and causing winged fairies to no longer be automatically hostile.
