# doomsday_scenario

• An unstable custom explosive item found in a transformed glass case, created by combining a massively overloaded soul crystal and a [[Sheol Glass Reaper Case]].
• The item is described as "an explosion large enough to rattle the teeth of a god" and has a persistent Status: [[Explosion Imminent]].
• It cannot be stabilized and will detonate the moment it is removed from inventory.
• The item is extremely dangerous and has not been handled at the sapper's table.
• It is a soul-crystal-based [[bomb]] in [[dungeon crawler carl]]'s inventory, created when a soul crystal became overwhelmed.
• It is listed as "Pretty Damn Rare" in [[dungeon crawler carl]]'s inventory and is a volatile unique/rare device.
