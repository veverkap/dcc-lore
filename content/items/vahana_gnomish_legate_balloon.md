# vahana_gnomish_legate_balloon

• The Vahana is a decorated black-and-[[gold]] striped hot-air balloon, a Gnomish Legate Balloon.
• It descends from the dreadnaught [[Wasteland]] carrying the negotiator and other crew.
• Historically a symbol of gnome diplomacy, repurposed into a contraption aboard the dreadnaught.
