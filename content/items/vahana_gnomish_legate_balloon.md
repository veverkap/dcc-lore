# Vahana (Gnomish Legate Balloon)
The decorated black-and-gold striped hot-air balloon (a Gnomish Legate Balloon) that descends from the dreadnaught [[Wasteland]] carrying the negotiator and other crew. Historically a symbol of gnome diplomacy, repurposed into a contraption aboard the dreadnaught.
