# satchel_of_gunpowder

• A consumable explosive material found in the [[Silver Goblin Box]], used with ignition tools for explosive effects.
• The heavy leather sack of [[gunpowder]] the protagonist offers during negotiations with the goblins.
• Proximity-triggered [[explosives]] equipped to enemy [[automatons]] and discovered by the narrator's [[find traps]] skill, causing a massive crater and disruption.
• Explosive charges placed by [[dungeon crawler carl]] at the back of the [[train]] in preparation for sending it into the [[Abyss]].
