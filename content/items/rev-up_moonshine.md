# rev-up_moonshine

• A potent spirit sought-after in the club's economy, traded by [[dungeon crawler carl]] to [[Quint]] for a [[Starter Kit]].
• A higher-grade spirit mentioned for comparison to [[Vernon]]'s regular [[moonshine]], more expensive and distinct from the bottle that exploded.
• An illicit brew produced by some variants of [[Krakaren]] using their bodies and grubs, mentioned as a product of previous encounters with the boss.
• A higher-grade [[moonshine]] referenced by description when comparing the jug in [[Vernon]]'s possession to more expensive varieties.
• A concoction previously produced by a Krakaren using its body and [[brindled grub]]; referenced as a plot detail from earlier encounters and relevant to the nature of the Krakaren bosses.
• A 180-proof Rev-Up-branded [[moonshine]] contained in clay jugs, usable as a valuable tradable item and as a crafting ingredient for incendiaries, granting the [[Shit-Faced]] debuff, causing long Blind, instant death, and permanently raising a random stat by two points.
