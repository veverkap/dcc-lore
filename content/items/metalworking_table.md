# metalworking_table

• A crafting bench purchased by [[Mordecai]] for metal-based item production and future explanations of its purpose.
• Reveals tensile strengths and shaping options; higher levels allow reforging and advanced alloy work referenced by [[Mordecai]].
• Used to analyze tensile strength, load limits, and shaping options for fabricated parts.
• Level-two used to refine chassis and wheel components for [[royal chariot]]; an upgrade to level three would allow melting and reforging of alloys for advanced munitions.
• Part of [[Mordecai]]'s workshop expansion and intended for metal gear production.
