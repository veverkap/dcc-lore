# zentix

• A reactive, tamper-proof polymer material used for the toy's back panel, designed to fail under tampering conditions and provide security features.
• Used for the [[Toy Back Panel]].
• Reacts with residue in the [[trailer]].
• Central to [[Orren the Liaison]]'s explanation of how the panel dislodged.
