# hobble

• A consumable [[snare]] card that immobilizes card-carrying opponents.
• Listed as part of the deck, but of limited use in the simulation.
• Can be used to restrict opponent movement.
• A generic term for small grenades used in crowd-control and [[explosives]].
• A consumable [[snare]] card marked as part of the active deck, designed to impede targets.
• A very rare [[snare]] card that reduces a [[totem]]'s summoning time by 90% when played, for one use.
• Contrasted with the non-consumable [[Hole in the Bag]] card.
• Intended to restrict movement, but of limited use in the simulation due to [[targeting]] card-carrying opponents.
• A [[snare]] consumable card in the Current Active Deck.
