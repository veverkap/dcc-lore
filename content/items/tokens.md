# tokens

• Small quest items taken from corpses that are required to operate the vending-machine encounter.
• Found on corpses and required to operate the vending machine or [[trailer]] to initiate the final fight and obtain the [[Key]].
• Carried by [[dungeon crawler carl]].
• Inserting them is necessary to open the [[Trailer]] and attempt to obtain the [[Key]].
