# moonshine_jug

• Flammable pottery jugs, such as Carl's homemade [[explosives]], are used to keep a persistent fire burning on the hydra's back and to create a sustained [[burn]] for cauterization.
• Pottery jugs of flammable [[moonshine]] are used as improvised incendiary devices to set the hydra aflame and force cauterization.
