# ink

• A bubbling black liquid used to apply a chest [[tattoo]] by the [[totem]]-possessed [[dungeon crawler carl]].
• A substance used in the crawl that can share powers between characters.
• A bubbling, black liquid formed in a [[beaker]] during possession, used with a [[tattoo]] needle to complete the ritual-[[tattoo]] that becomes the [[Eye of the Bedlam Bride]].
