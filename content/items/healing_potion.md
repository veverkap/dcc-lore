# healing_potion

Here is the consolidated list of unique factual points about the "Healing Potion" in markdown format:

* A consumable restorative item that restores a significant portion of health.
* Found in various boxes, such as the [[Bronze Adventurer Box]].
* Can be used to raise health after elemental attacks.
* Used by Carl and other characters to recover health during combat.
* Can be used to stabilize and restore health during self-poisoning plans.
* Has potential adverse effects on cursed NPCs.
* Can be used to recover health after being swallowed and injured by Claude Sludgington the Fourth.
* Can be used to recover health after being struck by the cow-catcher and during chaotic boarding of the engine.
* Can be used to recover health after being ejected from Claude Sludgington the Fourth's body.
* Can be used to recover health after severe impalement by Miss Sharp Elbows.
* Can be used to sustain health during combat.
* Can be used to accelerate healing and recover hearing.
* Can be used to stabilize injuries after the crash.
* Higher-quality healing [[potions]] have wider-healing effects.
* [[Gold]] Standard Healing [[Potions]] are high-tier healing [[potions]] that can be used to [[heal]] the wounded.
* Some [[potions]] have altered effects due to [[totem]] interactions.
* Some [[potions]] can be used to affect undead when detonated as a smoke curtain.
* Some [[potions]] can be used to rapidly restore health mid-transformation.
* Some [[potions]] can be used to demonstrate the contrast between the game world and the real world.
