# Enchanted Toe Ring of the Leprous Bandit
A unique toe ring inspired by the legend of the [[Leprous Bandit]]. Grants two benefits: "Sticky Feet" (walk on non-horizontal surfaces once every six hours) and "Super Spreader" (pass active debuffs onto a target once per hour). Received by [[dungeon crawler carl]].
