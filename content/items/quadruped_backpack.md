# Quadruped Backpack
A special backpack/harness item received on the tenth floor (modified by [[Mordecai]) that allows the wearer to use movement spells while carrying a quadruped. Used by [[dungeon crawler carl]] to carry [[penelope]] into the [[Scolopendra]] arena.
