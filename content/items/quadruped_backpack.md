• A special backpack/harness item that allows the wearer to use movement spells while carrying a quadruped.
• Received on the tenth floor, modified by [[Mordecai]].
• Used by [[dungeon crawler carl]] to carry [[penelope]] into the [[Scolopendra]] arena.
