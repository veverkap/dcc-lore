# Robot Donut Toy
A commercially distributed toy modeled after [[Donut]]; heavier and sturdier in its latest revision, includes a flimsy self-destruct tab and restricted/enhanced features tied to corporate enhancement zones. Used in recap merchandising and a scheduled infomercial.

# Robot Donut (toy)
A battery-operated toy modeled after [[Donut]] (robot Donut) that followed [[loita]] and contained a tamper-sensitive back panel. Its self-destruct sequence detonated when the panel dislodged, causing [[loita]]’s death. Multiple iterations of the toy (robot Donuts) had been destroyed previously by [[Mongo]].

# Robot Donut (Toy)
A consumer toy modeled on [[Donut]] sold as merch; heavier and more durable than earlier versions. It contains a flimsy self-destruct/tab panel, bootleg quote outputs, and limited autonomy. Packaged with a benefactor box linking to [[Veriluxx]].
