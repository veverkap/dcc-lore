# robot_donut_toy

• A commercially distributed toy modeled after [[Donut]], with a flimsy [[self-destruct]] tab and restricted/enhanced features tied to corporate enhancement zones.
• A [[battery]]-operated toy modeled after [[Donut]] that contained a tamper-sensitive back panel, which detonated a [[self-destruct]] sequence when dislodged, causing [[loita]]'s death.
• A consumer toy modeled on [[Donut]] sold as merch, with a flimsy [[self-destruct]]/ tab panel, bootleg quote outputs, and limited autonomy.
