# zerzura

• A high-impact teleportation spell provided to [[Sledge]] (suggested by [[Quasar]]), infamous for producing spectacle and carnage historically (e.g., [[Remex the Grand]]).
• Named spell referenced during negotiations; its promise helped secure concessions from the Borant representative. Treated as a bargaining chip by [[Quasar]].
• A powerful spell that teleports an entire city or town (buildings and NPCs included) one floor down. Cannot move mobs/hostile NPCs or stairwells; randomizes landing location (often in dangerous areas); can only be cast at end of a floor and is taxing on the caster. Casting incidents can create revenant outbreaks ([[Children of Inpewt]]).
• Used by [[Dungeon Crawler Carl]] and allies as the planned method to evacuate trapped towns by teleportation. It must be cast on a town to function and has timing constraints (cannot be cast for two days in-chapter).
• A sought-after spell referenced during negotiations with [[Borant Corporation]]; highly valued by [[Quasar]] and his team.
• A named spell referenced as part of a planned operation that requires Sledge's presence; mentioned in tactical discussions but not cast in this chapter.
• A spell that teleports an entire city or town (NPCs and buildings) one floor down. Cannot move mobs or special rooms, is cast at end-of-floor only, can skip floors on certain level sets (e.g., [[Scolopendra levels]]), and frequently lands towns inside dangerous areas, producing deadly revenants called the [[Children of Inpewt]] when inhabitants die.
• Used by Carl and allies to evacuate towns; it must be cast on an entire town and has a multi-day preparation window (two days until it can be cast). It is central to the evacuation plan for threatened towns.
• A named spell referenced by [[Epitome Tagg]] that would transport the city of [[Larracos]] to the [[twelfth floor]]; part of a larger evacuation/transport plan.
• A spell cast by [[Sledge]] that glows and successfully combines [[Larracos]] with [[Shanty Town]] and triggers their transfer to the [[twelfth floor]].
