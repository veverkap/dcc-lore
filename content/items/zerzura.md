# Zerzura
A powerful spell that teleports an entire city or town (NPCs and buildings) one floor down. Cannot move mobs or special rooms, is cast at end-of-floor only, can skip floors on certain level sets (e.g., [[Scolopendra levels]]), and frequently lands towns inside dangerous areas, producing deadly revenants called the [[Children of Inpewt]] when inhabitants die.

# Zerzura
A powerful spell that teleports an entire city or town (buildings and NPCs included) one floor down.
