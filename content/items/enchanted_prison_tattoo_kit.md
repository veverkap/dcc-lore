# enchanted_prison_tattoo_kit

• Stick-and-poke-style [[tattoo]] kit (needle, [[ink]], implements) that allows artists to inscribe magical tattoos on willing or unwilling targets.
• Tattoos can imbue various magical buffs or notoriety depending on artist skill.
• [[Edgar the tortoise]] used this kit to create a [[Tsarina Signet]] [[tattoo]].
