# Enchanted Prison Tattoo Kit
Stick-and-poke-style tattoo kit (needle, ink, implements) that allows artists to inscribe magical tattoos on willing or unwilling targets. Tattoos can imbue various magical buffs or notoriety depending on artist skill. Carl receives this item but delays using it.
