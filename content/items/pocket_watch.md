# pocket_watch

* [[Pocket Watch]]: Used in daily code ritual with gnomes; changing hands transmits mirrored movement to Commandant's twin [[watch]], signaling collateral status.
* [[Pocket Watch]]: Held by Henrik, Commandant Kane, and others; suspected piece of [[Gate]] of the Feral Gods artifact.
* [[Pocket Watch]]: One of two watches forming three-piece artifact to open [[Gate]] of the Feral Gods; target loot in floating-house/throne-room operation.
* [[Pocket Watch]]: Personal device used by narrator to trigger alarm and coordinate communications with Henrik.
* [[Mysterious Pocket Watch]]: Bronze [[watch]] with bone symbol and mirror-like feature; can display or signal other wearers when wound/set.
* [[Mysterious Watch]]: Carried by Henrik and previously used by Carl to [[ping]] Henrik's location; recovered from Henrik's corpse and tied to tracking and narrative clues.
* [[Facsimile Watch]]: Prop [[watch]] crafted by Carl to satisfy Leon's demand; used to seal deal for passage.
* [[Watch]]: Paired timing devices used with Winding Box to determine [[gate]] behavior; leaving one [[watch]] on one side and one on the other produces different summoning outcomes.
