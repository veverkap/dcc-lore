# amc_pacer

• An old roundish yellow car that tumbles during the initial factory chaos; identified by the narrator through familial recognition and associated with [[Team Yokai]].
• A vehicle used by [[Team Yokai]] that cast a spell and moved through a closed door to escape the lobby, noted as an uncommon successful exit.
