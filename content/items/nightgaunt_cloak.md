# nightgaunt_cloak

• A [[cloak]] worn by [[dungeon crawler carl]] that provides protection against [[poison]] effects, cancels [[poison]] from troglodyte tongue attacks, and grants [[poison]] resistance.
• Removing the [[cloak]] lowers constitution and removes [[poison]] resistance, affecting tactics against [[Ringmaster Grimaldi – Pestiferous Vine]].
• The [[cloak]] can be used to clean [[Princess Donut]]'s fur after she becomes ill.
• It grants [[dungeon crawler carl]] [[poison]] immunity, but its temporary loss during a precursor burst removes that immunity and allows him to be poisoned until he takes an antidote.
• The [[cloak]] can be briefly removed to safely fit through the [[truck]] gap before using a [[Phase Through Wall potion]].
• Wearing the [[cloak]] aggravates Nightgaunt-type mobs, including [[The Visitor]], and issues a warning when it influences their behavior.
• The [[cloak]] is a piece of personal equipment carried by the protagonist, [[dungeon crawler carl]].
