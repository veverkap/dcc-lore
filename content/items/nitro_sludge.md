# [[Nitro Sludge]]
A by-product square block left by [[Mordecai]] from potion work; inert alone but, when vaporized at high temperature, acts as an oxygen/amplifier for explosions. Used as a principal amplifier component inside [[Just Wait Until Your Daddy Gets Home – Wheeled Bomb.]] and other explosive devices.
