# eye_of_the_bedlam_bride

* The Eye of the Bedlam Bride is a cursed/artefactual eye implanted on Carl's chest that drives others mad and is linked to Vinata's descent into frenzy.
* It is a tattooed sigil forcibly inscribed onto Carl's chest by Shi Maria, which blinks once and then closes, granting or tying power to Shi Maria's attachment.
* The [[tattoo]] is sentient and previously communicated with Carl, tied to the floor's quest context.
* It is a distinctive [[tattoo]] on Carl's chest tied to a supply of special [[ink]], requested by Rosetta to reproduce or utilize its effects.
* The Eye of the Bedlam Bride is an artifact [[tattoo]] on Carl that can exert influence or attempt to take control when triggered.
* It is a magical/curious item that has returned to its proper place, affecting Carl's animal urges.
* It is a referenced charm/spell-like item that the [[containment]] [[shield]] was said to protect against, invoked as an example of hidden magical danger prisoners might carry.
* It is a notable final card mentioned in the recap, tied narratively to the mystery of the spider now inside Carl.
