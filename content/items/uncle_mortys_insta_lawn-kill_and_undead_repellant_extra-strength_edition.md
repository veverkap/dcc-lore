# uncle_mortys_insta_lawn-kill_and_undead_repellant_extra-strength_edition

• [[Uncle Morty’'s Insta Lawn-Kill and Undead Repellant, Extra-Strength Edition]] is a jugged concoction produced by Mordecai, a modified weedkiller with anti-undead boosts.
• The concoction is intended to kill odious creepers when properly deployed.
• Each jug can kill multiple Odious Creepers if properly delivered and absorbed.
