# Uncle Morty’s Insta Lawn-Kill and Undead Repellant, Extra-Strength Edition
A labeled set of moonshine jugs prepared by [[Mordecai]] containing a bubbling, yellow anti-odious/anti-undead weedkiller. Each jug can kill multiple [[Odious Creeper]]s if properly delivered and absorbed.
