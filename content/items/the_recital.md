# the_recital

• A spell cast by the recharged [[Wand]] that produces a charm-like effect making the audience happy and desirous to [[watch]] the performance.
• Functions as a staged performer trick.
• Induces happiness and engagement in the audience, making them want to [[watch]] the stage rather than pay attention to other dangers.
