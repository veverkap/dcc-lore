# [[Enchanted Hairbrush of the Beefmaster]]
A celestial-tier hairbrush awarded to [[dungeon crawler carl]] that grants Shining Charisma, Speedster, a constitution bonus, Entourage, and cosmetic hair effects when used on eligible males.
