# bronze_pet_box

• A reward item granted as an achievement drop from the [[Crazy Cat Lady]] achievement, received by [[dungeon crawler carl]] upon entering the [[World Dungeon]] with [[Princess Donut]].
• A tiered loot box (bronze) that can contain pet-related consumables and scrolls, which produced a [[heal critter scroll]] and some [[Pet Biscuit]]s when opened.
