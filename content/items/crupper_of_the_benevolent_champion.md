# [[Crupper of the Benevolent Champion]]
A unique enchanted crupper that is part of a matched set with the [[Cloak of the Benevolent Champion]]. Its verbose description links the item to a brutal historical kill and grants set bonuses (including boosts to constitution, [[Find Weakened]] progression, and a spell level increase) and promises additional rewards if the entire set is completed.

# Crupper of the Benevolent Champion
A unique enchanted crupper awarded to [[Donut]] with a lengthy, self-reflective item description. Part of a matched set with the [[Cloak of the Benevolent Champion]]; grants set bonuses including a 20% buff to the highest base stat (Constitution), forces [[Find Weakened]] to level 10, and allows one spell raised to level 16. Originally awarded in a [[Celestial Slap-Chop Box]] on the [[Ninth Floor]].
