# crupper_of_the_benevolent_champion

• A rare/unique enchanted [[crupper]] that complements Donut's champion [[cloak]] and was awarded in a celestial box.
• Part of a matched set with the [[Cloak of the Benevolent Champion]].
• Grants set bonuses, including boosts to constitution, [[Find Weakened]] progression, and a spell level increase.
• Promises additional rewards if the entire set is completed.
• Matches Donut's champion [[cloak]] and was awarded to her.
• Originally awarded in a [[Celestial Slap-Chop Box]] on the [[Ninth Floor]].
• Grants a 20% [[buff]] to the highest base stat (Constitution), forces [[Find Weakened]] to level 10, and allows one spell to be raised to level 16.
