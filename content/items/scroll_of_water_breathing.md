# scroll_of_water_breathing

* Consumable scroll that grants water breathing for (Intelligence × 3) seconds
* Common on this floor and given frequently in boss boxes
* Does not prevent drowning-by-predator or swimmer's ear
* Used by multiple party members to survive extended submersion
* Critical for the 500m dive into the [[Akula]]
* Consumable magical scrolls that grant water-breathing
* Essential after the castle collapses into the [[Water Quadrant]]
* Multiple scrolls are available among the party
* Used by  [[dungeon crawler carl]] and  [[Donut]] to survive the water quadrant after the collapse
* Consumable item used by  [[Morris]]'s team to mitigate flooding in the [[Necropolis]]
* Supply is insufficient for all affected
* Used by the party to operate underwater during the dive into [[Lusca]]'s mouth and the escape
* Consumable items used by subterranean crawlers attempting to venture into the flooded quadrant
