# rockards_ring_of_sniping

• Amber-stoned [[ring]] named after [[Rockard]], looted from [[Quan]]'s severed arm, granting benefits "Ripe" (marks creatures under 50% health on map) and "Loaded" (marks non-hidden creatures wearing magical gear on map).
