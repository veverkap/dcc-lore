# Rockard's Ring of Sniping
Amber-stoned ring named after [[Rockard]], looted from [[Quan]]'s severed arm. Grants the benefits "Ripe" (marks creatures under 50% health on your map) and "Loaded" (marks non-hidden creatures wearing magical gear). A tool for glory-stealing and target selection.
