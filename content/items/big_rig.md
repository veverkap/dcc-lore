# big_rig

• The Big Rig is a large vehicle used by Chris's team, complete with a [[trailer]], emphasizing movement upgrades for desert racing.
• The Big Rig is driven by Chris and the Erins, and is contrasted with faster animal or specialty vehicles in the heats.
