# sheol_brick

• A fuel-like resource derived from failed Lesser Demon offspring, described in the cab.
• Burns extremely hot and long; used as fuel for the [[Nightmare Express]].
• Made from hardened demon baby corpses, discovered inside the engine cab's chest.
• Identified by [[Fire Brandy]] as the [[train]]'s long-burning fuel source.
• A macabre fuel item, described as the hardened corpses of failed Lesser Demon infants.
• Found in the engine chest and usable as long-burning fuel.
• Made from shriveled corpses of failed Lesser Demon babies, burning much hotter and longer than regular coal.
