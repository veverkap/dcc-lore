# Enchanted Nipple Ring of the Defiler
A small equippable ring (nipple ring) that may only be equipped by a crawler Marked for Death by a god. Grants instant knowledge of nearby religious affiliations, allows destruction of church shrines without deity backlash, +25% damage vs clerics, and the ability to cast the spell [[Black Nimbus]] once per hour.
