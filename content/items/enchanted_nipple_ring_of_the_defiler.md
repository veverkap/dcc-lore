# enchanted_nipple_ring_of_the_defiler

• A small equippable [[ring]] (nipple [[ring]]) that may only be equipped by a crawler Marked for Death by a god.
• Grants instant knowledge of nearby religious affiliations, allows destruction of church shrines without deity backlash, +25% damage vs clerics, and the ability to cast the spell [[Black Nimbus]] once per hour.
