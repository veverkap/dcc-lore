# enchanted_handcuffs

• Item obtained from a Silver Savage Box, magically reinforced fuzzy [[handcuffs]] that lock wrists, require high Strength (200) to break, and are keyed to the owner (no physical [[key]]).
• Securing device used by [[dungeon crawler carl]]'s group to bind [[Akland]] to a pole for interrogation.
• Loot from a Silver Savage Box, described as novelty fuzzy [[handcuffs]] that magically lock wrists, require 200 Strength to break, and can be keyed to a magical safe word.
• Magical restraints used by [[dungeon crawler carl]] to secure captured hunter [[Akland]] to a pole during interrogation.
