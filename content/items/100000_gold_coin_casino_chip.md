# 100000_gold_coin_casino_chip

• A comp chip granted in a [[platinum lucky bastard box]] that cannot be sold or redeemed for [[gold]] directly and must be played at the [[High Roller's Roulette Table]] or the [[Wheel of Fortune game]] in the [[desperado club]].
• High-value comp chip previously obtained by [[dungeon crawler carl]].
• A casino comp chip worth 100,000 [[gold]].
• Can be used on the [[Wheel of Fortune (Game)]] to alter odds by removing red (dangerous) slots.
• A comp chip worth 100,000 [[gold]] that can be wagered at the [[desperado club]] wheel.
• Using the comp removes most negative red spots on the wheel, altering prize distribution.
