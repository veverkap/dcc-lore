# belt_of_buoyancy

• Prevents the wearer (notably [[Donut]]) from sinking while in water; only functions when submerged.
• Awarded to [[Donut]] from the [[Bronze I'm Wet Box]]/loot; keeps the wearer afloat in water and only functions while submerged.
