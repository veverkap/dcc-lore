# magical_chain

• A long, glittering 300-foot unbreakable chain was used to tangle and slow the [[Rage Elemental]] during the escape attempt.
• [[Chains]] created from [[Chain-Making Scrolls]] and handled by [[Imani]] were used to lift and lock the [[Interdiction [[Cart]] (Def Leppard)]] vertically in the chamber, withstanding heavy loads and magic.
• The enchanted chain created from [[chain-making scrolls]] held the [[cart]] aloft and sustained its weight while anchored to the improvised crane.
