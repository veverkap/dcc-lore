# midnight_express

• A consumable card that, when played, makes the player the deckmaster for three minutes, allowing control over [[cards]] and [[totem]] actions.
• Features two large men in tights.
• Given to [[dungeon crawler carl]] by [[Ren]].
• Played by [[Donut]] to manipulate [[Amayon]] and the flow of souls/[[cards]].
• Temporarily makes the card-player's ally the deckmaster for three minutes.
• Allows the caster to act separately and control [[cards]].
• One of the rare [[cards]] [[Ren]] passes to [[dungeon crawler carl]] to be transported to [[Princess Donut]].
