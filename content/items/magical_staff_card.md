# magical_staff_card

• A special, non-consumable [[cards]] found in the ogre's deck that can turn turkeys into zombies and acts like a [[totem]] when imbued with a spell.
• Can be imbued with a spell by [[Donut]] to cast a single spell in the upcoming fight.
• Functions like a [[totem]] while active.
• Can be used to animate turkeys into zombies.
