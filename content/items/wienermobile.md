# wienermobile

• The [[Wienermobile]] is a giant hot dog [[truck]] vehicle used by the [[Bleak Congregation]].
• It is a large hot-dog- themed vehicle used by the [[Bleak Congregation]] in the heats, lacking dignity but functional as the team's race vehicle.
• The [[Wienermobile]] is driven by the skull-faced karaoke group led by the [[Minister of Blood-Letting]].
• It is also driven by pope-skeleton creatures, including the [[Minister of Blood-Letting]], spotted emerging from the city, carrying a bucket of white sloshing liquid.
• The [[Wienermobile]] is used by the [[Minister of Blood-Letting]] as the team's transport in the final race.
