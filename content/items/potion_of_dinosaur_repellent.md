# potion_of_dinosaur_repellent

• Consumable potion that causes dinosaurs to eat the drinker last for 30 hours.
• Appeared among [[dungeon crawler carl]]'s box loot and alarmed [[Mordecai]].
• Makes a pack of attacking dinosaurs eat the imbiber last.
• Effect duration: 30 hours.
• Obtained among the loot in the chapter.
• Part of [[dungeon crawler carl]]'s pre-party staging for animal control.
• Possessed by [[dungeon crawler carl]] and regretted not forcing [[Donut]] to use one before [[Mongo]] attracted the Mongoliensis pack.
