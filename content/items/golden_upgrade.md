# golden_upgrade

• A special upgrade tier awarded for race placement.
• Can be used to select three regular upgrades instead of spending it differently.
• A premium upgrade reward offered to teams, allowing them to choose one Golden upgrade or three regular upgrades.
• Also mentioned as a post-race bonus reward for upcoming heats.
