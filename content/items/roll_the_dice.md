# Roll the Dice
Once-per-hour skill embedded in [[Zhang’s Cape of Odds]] that triggers a random outcome from 333 possibilities, ranging from powerful boons to dangerous penalties and even summoning a god. Considered a high-risk/high-reward celestial mechanic tied to the cape.
