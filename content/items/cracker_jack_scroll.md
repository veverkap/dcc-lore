# cracker_jack_scroll

• A consumable scroll used to bypass user-locked weapons, unlock alien tech items, or activate personal shields.
• Three copies were received by Carl in exchange for the Residual's loot.
• The narrator used two of three to unlock personal shields for themselves and [[Donut]] (or [[Princess Donut]]).
• [[Donut]] and the narrator used Cracker Jack scrolls to unlock personal shields.
• [[Dungeon Crawler Carl]] accepts three copies of Cracker Jack in exchange for the [[Plastic Bin]].
• Cracker Jack scrolls are rare and can be used to free restricted benefactor-box items for re-equipping by other players.
