# toraline_root_vegetable

• A very rare alchemy material called [[Toraline Root Vegetable]], described as a sweet-potato-like tuber that grows in lava-covered dirt.
• Possibly used in specialty healing salves for scaled creatures, exotic [[potions]], or [[explosives]].
• Received from a silver benefactor box and examined by [[Mordecai]] and [[dungeon crawler carl]].
