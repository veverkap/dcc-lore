# enchanted_roll_of_never-ending_duct_tape

• A 50-meter enchanted roll of duct tape that regenerates at one meter per hour until it returns to 50 meters.
• Used to bind [[Wynne]] and in various fieldcraft tasks.
• Regenerates at one meter per hour until it returns to 50 meters.
• Used by [[Dungeon Crawler Carl]] to strongly bind and conceal [[Wynne]] and to replace rope for restraining.
• [[Dungeon Crawler Carl]]'s inventory includes a regenerating roll of magical duct tape.
