# powerful_strike

* A combat skill that serves as a more general fighting technique compared to [[bare knuckles]].
* Upgraded melee skill/ability used by [[dungeon crawler carl]] that allows him to one-shot a charging [[Babababoon]] with a stun effect.
* A combat skill/spell the protagonist trains during the chapter (training did not advance); categorized in-universe as a trainable ability.
* A broader fighting skill related to [[bare knuckles]] that [[dungeon crawler carl]] aims to improve for more general heavy-hitting attacks.
* An upgraded attack effect used by [[dungeon crawler carl]] that delivers dramatically increased damage (demonstrated by instantly killing a [[Babababoon]]).
* A combat skill the protagonist trains in; presented in the training [[interface]] and treated like a trainable "spell/ability" in the advanced room.
* A broader, catch-all fighting skill related to unarmed attacks that [[dungeon crawler carl]] practices in conjunction with [[Bare Knuckle]] to improve his combat repertoire.
* An upgraded combat ability used by [[dungeon crawler carl]] that delivers a high-damage stun effect on impact, effective against the [[Babababoon]] mobs in the transit station.
* A more general offensive fighting skill related to punching and close combat.
