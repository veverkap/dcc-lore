# [[Princess Palette eye makeup kit]]
A high-earning piece of crawler merchandise tied to [[Princess Donut]] and the [[donuts fan club]] sponsorship. The product is cited as the highest-earning crawler merch in the history of the crawl and a revenue source Chandra aims to control.
