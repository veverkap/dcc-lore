# pvp_coupon

* Black ticket item embossed with a [[gold]] skull, redeemable for rewards including boxes and level boosts when used to kill a named crawler/player.
* Grants rewards such as [[Gold Savage Box]], [[Gold Weapons Box]], [[Gold Apparel Box]], [[Platinum Adventurer Box]], and up to +1 player level (limited redemptions).
* Found with [[Katia]] and discarded by [[Donut]].
* Distributed via "Savage box" loot and associated with obtaining a [[player killer skull]].
* One was found with [[dungeon crawler carl]]'s name on the back (Crawler #4,122).
* Consumable items the party possesses (not yet explained to Mordecai), tied to player-versus-player mechanics.
* Reference to [[PVP Coupon]] as part of the party's unspent loot that still needs accounting.
