# resurrect_totem

• A resurrection spell/card effect can bring back a dead [[totem]] like [[Geraldo]] with full reset of timers and removal of buffs/debuffs, associated with [[Uzi Jesus]]'s purported abilities.
• A [[totem]]-cast spell can fully restore a dead [[totem]] as if newly summoned, resetting timers and removing buffs/debuffs.
