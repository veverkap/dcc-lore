# portal_cart

• The [[Portal Cart]] is a vehicle used to transport and deploy portals into the trainyard, allowing allies to transfer into the trainyard and send mantaur bodies through portals.
• It is a wheeled employee [[cart]] used to operate and position the [[portal]], manipulated by Katia, Zhang, and the group during their departure.
• The [[cart]] is used to transport mantaur bodies and team members into the trainyard and to open/close portals, operated by Zhang.
• It is a mobile [[portal]]-bearing vehicle used to redirect the summoning to the [[Abyss]].
• The [[cart]] is used on the employee track to host and activate the [[portal]], positioned and turned by Carl, Katia, and others to access the [[portal]] and [[train]] route.
• It is a named transport/device used to move crawlers and bodies between the loop and the trainyard (and to the [[Abyss]]), operated by Zhang during the chapter.
• The [[cart]] is placed onto the employee track and shut off by Zhang after the [[train]] passed through.
• It is a generic term for carts with an attached [[portal]] engine that teleports people and objects between lines, yards, and the [[Abyss]], central to the incidents described.
• The [[cart]] is used to transfer players and the mantaur summoning vessel into the trainyard, operated by Zhang in the chapter, and used to bring reinforcements and to send the maimed mantaur into [[The Abyss]].
