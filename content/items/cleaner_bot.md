# cleaner_bot

• A personal-space environmental upgrade (frisbee-shaped flying Roomba) purchased for 25,000 [[gold]] to remove congealed blood from the group's room.
• A purchased robotic unit/upgrade that magically removes congealed blood and gore from the party's living area; bought from the [[Bopca]].
• The narrator's inventory bot used for cleaning; interacts/beeps with other recovered units.
• A mechanical device used to transport people; [[Prepotente]] owns one and [[Mordecai]] notes capacity limits (about fifty people per [[Cleaner bot]] for the Pineapple Cabaret plan).
• A prior extraction device contrasted with the dimensional-holding-area method for removing crawlers and NPCs from dungeon space.
• [[Cleaner Bot]] was removed from the [[Personal Space Shop]] due to demand; a crucial component of the plan to clear and secure the [[Pineapple Cabaret]].
