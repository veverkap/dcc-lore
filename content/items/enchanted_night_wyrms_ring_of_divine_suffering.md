# Enchanted Night Wyrm’s Ring of Divine Suffering
Magical ring given to [[dungeon crawler carl]] by [[frank]]. Grants +5% to all stats and imparts the infamous skill [[Marked for Death]]. The ring is powerful but dangerous—its skill prevents healing while a mark is active and scales stat gains with marked kills.
