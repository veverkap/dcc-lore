# sponsor_box

• A loot package delivered by corporate or royal sponsors, containing gear or unique items like the [[Bolt of Ophiotaurus]].
• Also referred to as benefactor boxes, sponsor-provided loot crates that can contain legal upgrades.
• Disputed when used to lead players to in-dungeon loot, and central to legal arguments by [[Quasar]] about ownership.
