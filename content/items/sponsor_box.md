# Sponsor Box
A loot package delivered by corporate or royal sponsors (e.g., the silver benefactor box from [[princess formidable of the skull empire]] or boxes from [[Jaxbrin Amusements, Limited]]). Contains gear or unique items like the [[Bolt of Ophiotaurus]].

# Sponsor Box
Also referred to as benefactor boxes; sponsor-provided loot crates that can contain legal upgrades. Disputed when used to lead players to in-dungeon loot, and central to legal arguments by [[Quasar]] about ownership.
