# Snitch
A skill granted by the [[Enchanted Collar Charm of the Effete Bourgeoisie]] that summons town guards to the activator's location (levels summon increasing numbers and range). Useful for calling guards to aid [[Donut]] or [[dungeon crawler carl]] inside and near [[Point Mongo]].

# [[Snitch]]
A skill attached to the [[Enchanted Collar Charm of the Effete Bourgeoisie]] (Level 10 in the chapter). When activated inside a settlement it summons town guards to the user (level-dependent count and range). Warning: guards may not obey or can become hostile if abused.
