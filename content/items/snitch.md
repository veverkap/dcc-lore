# snitch

• A skill granted by the [[Enchanted Collar Charm of the Effete Bourgeoisie]] that summons town guards to the activator's location, with levels affecting the number and range of summoned guards.
• Useful for calling guards to aid [[Donut]], [[dungeon crawler carl]], or others inside and near [[Point Mongo]].
• When activated inside a settlement, it summons town guards to the user, with levels affecting the count and range of summoned guards.
• Warning: guards may not obey or can become hostile if abused.
