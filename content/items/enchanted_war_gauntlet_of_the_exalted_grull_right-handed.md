# Enchanted War Gauntlet of the Exalted Grull (Right-Handed)
A right-handed transformable wrist bracer that becomes a spiked war gauntlet in Fist Mode; grants +3 Strength (fist only), +1 Dexterity, +2 Iron Punch skill levels, +1 Powerful Strike skill level, and a small stun chance. Flavor text warns about interacting with adherents of [[Grull]].
