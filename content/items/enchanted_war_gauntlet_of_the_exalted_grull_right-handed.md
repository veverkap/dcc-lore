# enchanted_war_gauntlet_of_the_exalted_grull_right-handed

• A right-handed transformable wrist bracer that becomes a spiked war [[gauntlet]] in Fist Mode.
• Grants +3 Strength (fist only), +1 Dexterity, +2 Iron Punch skill levels, +1 Powerful Strike skill level, and a small stun chance.
• Flavor text warns about interacting with adherents of [[Grull]].
