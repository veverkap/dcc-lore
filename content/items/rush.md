# [[Rush]]
[[Rush]] is [[Katia]]’s special daily ability that turns her body into a battering ram, propelling her forward to smash obstacles or enemies. It is unpredictable in distance and angle, exhausts her when used, and in this chapter causes the accidental lethal strike on [[Hekla]].
