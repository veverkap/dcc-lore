# rush

• Katia's one-per-day active skill that turns her body into a battering ram, propelling her forward with variable distance and angle.
• Unpredictably propels Katia varying distances and angles.
• Exhausts Katia when used.
• Inadvertently kills [[Hekla]] and awards a bounty.
• [[Rush]] is [[Katia]]’s special daily ability that turns her body into a battering ram, propelling her forward to smash obstacles or enemies.
