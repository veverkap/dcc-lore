# teleport

• A high-tier teleportation spell, approaching the functionality of [[Puddle Jumper]] at higher levels.
• Instant-movement spell that was temporarily broken during the crisis, limiting rapid response options for rescuing frozen witnesses.
• Teleportation items kept in inventories, used by characters like [[Juice Box]] and others for rapid extraction to the [[Platform]].
• General category of magical movement spells, referenced in the chapter, whose allowance or prohibition affects [[Princess Vinata]]'s ability to vanish and reappear during [[Faction Wars]] finales.
• Another common traversal spell mentioned by [[Carl]], which does not substitute for [[Hole]] in the context of moving between self-contained apartments.
