# heal_party

• A level-10 party-wide healing spell is promised by [[Shrine of Oshun]] if repaired, presented as a mechanical reward in the thorn room choices.
• The Tome of [[Heal]] Party is a spellbook reported as looted by a member of Tserendolgor's group, containing a cleric-oriented spell likely used for group healing.
