# [[Spiderweb Tattoo]]
Distinctive tattoo on Carl’s elbow that throbs when the Engaged Lock Box is revealed and grants him access to the [[Guild of Suffering]]; obtained via artifacts like the [[Ring of Divine Suffering]].
