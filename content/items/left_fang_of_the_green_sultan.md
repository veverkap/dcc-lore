# left_fang_of_the_green_sultan

• Magical saber carried by [[dungeon crawler carl]]; its properties only fully function when paired with its counterpart.
• Originally part of [[Eva]]'s main weapon set; returned into [[Katia]]'s custody for eventual return to its rightful owner.
• One half of [[Eva]]'s saber set, recovered by [[dungeon crawler carl]] from gore on the vermillion line.
• Described as part of a pair that grants magical effects only when both blades are reunited.
• An enchanted saber described as part of a two-weapon set; without its pair it provides no magical bonuses.
• One half of a paired enchanted saber set (non-magical unless both pieces are present); picked up by [[dungeon crawler carl]] in the gore-ful car and used by [[Katia]] in smaller form.
• Used by [[Katia]] to fatally pierce [[Eva]]'s breastplate.
