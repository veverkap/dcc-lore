# Zhang's Shrinking Wand
One-charge magic wand owned by [[Zhang]] that shrinks nonliving objects continuously until stopped (or until it reaches the size of a button). Used to reduce the interdiction cart to maneuver it through tight hallways into [[Station 36]].
