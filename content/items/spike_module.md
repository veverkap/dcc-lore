# spike_module

• A ready-to-go trap module of the "Trap Module" type, obtained from a [[gold]] sapper's loot box, upgradable using a [[Sapper’s Table]].
• Portable trap module that generates 50 cm spikes at two-second intervals in a 1 m² area, can be placed vertically and attacks red-tagged mobs while ignoring friendly NPCs/crawlers.
• Creates 50 cm spikes popping up at two-second intervals in a one-meter square, friendly NPCs and crawlers won't trigger it by default, can be placed vertically against doors or floors.
• Sapper's trap cube that generates spikes in a one-meter square area at intervals, can be mounted vertically against doors to delay or impale boarding mobs on the [[Red Line]].
• Trap-module that produces 50-cm spikes in a 1m² area at two-second intervals once triggered, can be attached vertically to doors/gangways and will repeatedly spike until removed.
