# [[Interdiction Rapid-Response Railway Repair Cart]]
The main hobgoblin interdiction workhorse: a larger, three-axle powered cart with passenger seating, larger engine (three-battery compartment), and a wide front "debris scoop" blade that functions as a mobile portal to clear crash sites.
