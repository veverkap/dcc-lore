# interdiction_rapid-response_railway_repair_cart

• [[Interdiction Rapid-Response Railway Repair Cart]]: a larger, three-axle powered [[cart]] with passenger seating, larger engine, and a wide front "debris scoop" blade that functions as a mobile [[portal]] to clear crash sites.
• Requires a pair of gnolls to start due to hand-[[key]] consoles.
• Can switch debris scoop into "abyss mode".
• Runs faster than passenger cars.
• Features a large front "debris scoop" that creates a tunnel-spanning [[portal]] ahead of the [[cart]] to clear wreckage.
