# rebreather

• A breathing apparatus worn by Zev and Loita, worn around the neck or as part of a suit.
• Part of the production-[[trailer]] environment's equipment and Loita's on-set appearance.
• The device's moisture interacted with disco-ball residue to accelerate degradation of the Toy Back Panel.
• Looted by Dungeon Crawler Carl.
