# pet_biscuit

* Consumable food item for pets, commonly found in pet boxes like the [[Bronze Pet Box]] and [[Legendary Pet Box]].
* Used to calm or bond with caged creatures, as seen with [[Donut]] and [[dungeon crawler carl]] using them on [[Mongo]].
* Consumable training reward that motivates [[Mongo]] and aids in obedience training for basic commands like "attack" and "come".
* Used for sustaining crawler/pet mechanics, distinct from [[Pet Biscuit]].
* Food ration handed out by [[Imani]] to shelter residents.
* Previously received valuable pet-related item referenced in conversation as one of [[dungeon crawler carl]]'s notable past loots.
* Cursed consumable that granted limited intelligence and the ability to speak to animals, but also caused race changes and other irreversible effects.
* Consumable item used to transform or claim pets, with misuse resulting in producing unintended pets.
* Small consumable item referenced when [[Prepotente]] laments "I wish I'd never taken the pet biscuit", implying significant consequences for the character.
