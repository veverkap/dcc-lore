# wand_recharge_scroll

• Represents one of the mundane loot items [[dungeon crawler carl]] accepts to avoid losing the stash outright.
• Restores charges to a [[wand]].
• Can be found among the sponsor boxes.
• Its purpose (to recharge a [[wand]]) is clear to [[dungeon crawler carl]].
• Can recharge wands; accessible to performers on stage if the attendant ballroom stage contains the item.
• A rare consumable in [[dungeon crawler carl]]’s inventory intended to recharge wands.
• Noted as a backup in case [[dungeon crawler carl]]’s [[wand]] runs out of charges.
• Received in trade for the [[Plastic Bin]] or the Residual’s bin.
• Part of the modest compensation arranged by [[orren the liaison]].
