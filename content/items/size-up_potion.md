# size-up_potion

* Consumable potion that reveals specific facts and stats about mobs after ~30 seconds, used for planning against bosses like Sir Ferdinand.
* Used by Donut to reveal that Raul the Crab has high Intelligence and multiple spells.
* Used by Donut to confirm monster stats and details before fighting Shi Maria.
* Used by Katia to scan Astrid's spells and abilities and gather intelligence on the fairy's capabilities.
* Used by Dungeon Crawler Carl to analyze a mob's resistances and produce a timed full report, but an attempted "Size Up" fails when visual contact is lost.
* Affects size (not fully detailed here).
* Loot that alters size or provides Size-Up effects, appears among early box rewards.
* Mentioned in trade negotiations, implied to be a stat/utility potion used by players.
* Used by Donut to assess the boss's stats upon appearance, used for tactical evaluation of dangerous foes like Shi Maria.
* Allows players to examine enemy resistances and stats, used by Carl to examine enemy resistances and stats.
* Affects sizing, useful according to Carl, who received one naturally from loot.
