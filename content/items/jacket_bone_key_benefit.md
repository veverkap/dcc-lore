# Jacket (bone key benefit)
[[dungeon crawler carl]]'s jacket that carries the mysterious "bone key" benefit allowing him to open doors even if he doesn't hold a physical [[key]]. Central to a proposed fallback plan involving use of a [[flee card]] to transfer keys.
