# blessed_chock

• A holy- or holy-coated metal door block (leftover from the [[the tangle]]).
• Used as a repellant to slow undead/ghommids at the temple door, coated with protection oil by [[Mordecai]].
• Used by Carl to partially repel undead/ghommids from entering the temple.
• Later disappears when the [[shrine]] activates.
