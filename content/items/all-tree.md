# all-tree

• The [[all tree]] is a mythic entity tied to the climax of the [[Nine-tier attack]], where the Princess takes control, tempering the final assault but causing massive tragedy.
• The [[all tree]] is a central, legendary artifact in the mythic cycle, linked to the tragedy and cyclical rebirth described in the [[Scolopendra]] myth.
• The [[Map the All Tree]] spell (1 mana) provides a map of the [[All Tree]] and its nodes, usable only on the [[twelfth floor]] and deeper.
