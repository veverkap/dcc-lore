# mute

* A spellbook prize that prevents monsters from casting spells, received by [[Donut]] as a book-of-the-floor prize and noted as useful by [[Mordecai]].
* A spell cast by [[Donut]] that silences a target, applying a one-minute speaker-muted debuff (preventing spellcasting).
* A short-duration silencing spell used by [[Donut]] to interrupt [[Asojano]]'s casting, effective only briefly against city-boss-level enemies.
* A spell that [[Donut]] recently learned, noted as potentially useful against [[Astrid]] during an assault due to silencing effects.
* A silencing spell cast by [[Donut]] to prevent [[raul the crab]] from replenishing mana or chanting spells while being captured.
* A spell used by [[Donut]] to silence a mob's casting, applying a one-minute speaker-with-crossed-icon debuff that prevents spellcasting.
* A short-duration silence spell used by [[Donut]] to interrupt [[Asojano]]'s spellcasting, effective briefly against powerful foes.
* A spell possessed by [[Donut]] that would be tactically useful against [[Astrid]] (prevents casting or speaking-based effects), discussed as an asset in the upcoming plan.
* A spell cast by [[Donut]] to silence [[raul the crab]] and prevent chanting or mana recovery during the capture.
* A silencing spell cast by [[Zeynep]] on [[Emir Akbas]] during his attack, appearing as an in-combat status effect shown by an on-screen notification.

* A spell cast by [[Donut]] to silence [[Akuma]] during interrogation in the [[Upgrades]] room, presented in the text as an active, named effect.
