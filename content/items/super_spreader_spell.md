# [[Super Spreader spell]]
A contagious spell/ability that causes infected mercenaries to leak aggressive "punk rock slugs." [[dungeon crawler carl]] and others refer to it in the context of a training arcade where infected mercenaries continuously regenerate and spread the effect.
