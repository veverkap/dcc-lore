# soul_reaper

• Kills with melee charge the patch's [[Soul Essence]]; once fully charged, the stored essence can be consumed in a single melee attack for a powerful effect that varies with the nature of the essence.
• Capacity grows with repeated fills; warnings indicate prolonged storage can be risky.
• Carrying corrupted essence has implied risks.
