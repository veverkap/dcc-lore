# crowd_blast

• Area-of-effect ability used by [[Katia]] to burst through tightly packed mobs, functioning as a devastating close-range crowd-control tool.
• Grants temporary [[invulnerability]] during its effect, similar to part of the [[Dolores Doesn’t Splat]] potion's ancestry.
• Temporarily grants [[invulnerability]] during a powerful area knockback.
• Used by [[Katia]] to blast through hordes and clear intersections during defensive maneuvers.
• Ready to use if [[Ghazi]] acts suspiciously, part of the party's combat toolkit during the ooze engagement.
