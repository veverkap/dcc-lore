# divine_intervention

• A 60-second aura/[[buff]] granted to a wielder of a [[Celestial Grenade]] if the grenadier worships the summoned deity.
• A 60-second aura granted when a worshiper detonates a [[Celestial Grenade]], described as a temporary divine [[buff]] that accompanies a god's summoning.
• Temporary [[invulnerability]] [[buff]] granted when the [[Celestial Grenade]] summons a god matching the thrower's worship choice.
