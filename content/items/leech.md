# leech

• A biological item/device fitted to [[Louis Santiago]] by [[Forkith]], described as a blood-filtering leech that adheres to skin, allowing a character with gills to survive in [[Sheol]].
• Can be stocked in a [[Pet Carrier]] inventory slot.
• Filters blood and keeps a player alive in the heat of [[Sheol]].
• Treated like a pet/item that can be kept in a pet [[carrier]] and placed in inventory.
• Functions with [[Louis Santiago]]'s gills.
