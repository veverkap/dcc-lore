# Leech
A biological item/device fitted to [[Louis Santiago]] by [[Forkith]]. Described as a blood-filtering leech that adheres to skin, allowing a character with gills to survive the extreme environment of [[Sheol]] and noted as something that can be stocked in a [[Pet Carrier]] inventory slot.

# Leech
A wearable biofilter described as "a type of leech" that adheres to skin, filters blood, and keeps a player alive in the heat of [[Sheol]]. It is treated like a pet/item that can be kept in a pet carrier and placed in inventory, and should function with [[Louis Santiago]]'s gills.
