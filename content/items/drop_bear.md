# drop_bear

• The Drop Bear is a small biplane/aircraft used for flight and rescue, featuring cockpit controls and being pushed to its limits while towing the party and casting spells.
• The biplane is also referenced in fan photos, featuring [[dungeon crawler carl]], [[Princess Donut]], and [[Orthrus]].
• The Gnomish Drop Bear is an early-model, open-air twin-engine attack biplane used by [[gnomes]] flyers, carrying altitude-deployed payloads and escape balloons.
