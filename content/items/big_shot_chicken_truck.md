# big_shot_chicken_truck

• Heavily damaged vehicle used by survivors, containing [[Bigs]] and others, described as smashed but still operable.
• Vehicle used by Carl's group, with a magic missile launcher mounted on its roof, used to ram into the arena.
• Barely operational after the arena fight, used during evacuation attempts.
• Flashy, twenty-foot box/food [[truck]] with a faux-1920s hood, kitchen in back, and decorative tommy [[gun]] on the roof, used as a chosen vehicle on [[Don't come in last]].
