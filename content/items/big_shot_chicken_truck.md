A flashy, roughly twenty-foot box/food truck bearing the [[Big Shot Chicken]] logo that appears as the chosen vehicle for one team on [[Don't come in last]]. It has a faux-1920s hood, a kitchen in back, and a large decorative tommy gun mounted on the roof.
