# silver_goblin_box

• A themed silver-tier box with goblin imagery that produced unusual items, including [[Dynamite]], a [[Lighter]], and the [[Goblin Pass]].
• Reward box granted to [[dungeon crawler carl]] for killing more than ten mobs with an explosive.
• Contains consumables and tools, such as [[dynamite]], [[Lighter]], and [[Satchel of Gunpowder]].
