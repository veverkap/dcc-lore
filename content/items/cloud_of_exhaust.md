# Cloud of Exhaust
A legendary, enhanced spell owned by [[Louis]] that creates a knockout cloud with a high probability to incapacitate mobs for variable durations; awakened mobs take enhanced damage for thirty seconds after waking. Normally has an hour cooldown but can be modified by classes or potions.
