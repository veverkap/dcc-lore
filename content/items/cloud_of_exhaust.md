# cloud_of_exhaust

• Creates a knockout cloud with a high probability to incapacitate mobs for variable durations
• Awakened mobs take enhanced damage for thirty seconds after waking
• Normally has an hour cooldown, but can be modified by classes or [[potions]]
• Boosted by [[Louis]]’s class and gear
