# extinction_sigil_tattoo

• A platinum [[tattoo]] item received by [[dungeon crawler carl]] that is keyed to the [[Wall Monitors]] race.
• Removes automatic hostility from natural enemies of lizard-class creatures.
• Increases incoming damage from lizard-class enemies, including [[Wall Monitors]].
• Visible and only concealable with a cover-up sleeve.
• Received from a [[Platinum Asshole’s Box]].
