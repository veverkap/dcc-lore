# Extinction Sigil Tattoo
A platinum tattoo item received by [[dungeon crawler carl]] that is keyed to the [[Wall Monitors]] race. Removes automatic hostility from natural enemies of lizard-class creatures but increases incoming damage from lizard-class enemies (and greatly from Wall Monitors). Visible and only concealable with a cover-up sleeve.
