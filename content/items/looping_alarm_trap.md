# Looping Alarm Trap
A trap or alarm configuration described as looping; when the main [[Castle Alarm 'ah-luga, ah-luga']] stops, the looping alarm becomes more audible. Used to confuse and misdirect defenders during the assault on [[Fort Freedom]].
