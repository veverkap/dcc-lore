# bubble_buddy

• Personal [[shield]] system used by [[Carl]]'s team, discussed upgrade path from regular to Golden (full Bubble Buddy) to protect against molten steel and environmental hazards.
• [[Shield]]/upgrade used by [[Carl]]'s vehicle, features floating upgrade and [[gyro]] stabilization, with selectable [[Heat Sink]] function to lower ambient temperature and protect occupants.
• Protective device/item that allowed the [[truck]] to roll safely over a massive [[hole]], credited with preventing an immediate fall.
• Piece of equipment or activated effect used by the team that cushions or aids their movement midair, explicitly "activated" during the gliding sequence.
