# poison_cloud_enhancement

• An enhancement on the [[trebuchet]] that allows delivery of [[poison]] effects; its delivery [[poison]] can be changed by adding [[potions]] to the device.
• Attached to siege equipment, applies poisonous effect to delivered projectiles when a potion is poured into the device's cup.
