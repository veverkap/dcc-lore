# [[Poison Cloud enhancement]]
An enhancement on the trebuchet that allows delivery of poison effects; its delivery poison can be changed by adding potions to the device.

# [[Poison Cloud enhancement]]
An enhancement attached to siege equipment (noted on the [[venomous elven rock chucker trebuchet device]]) that applies poisonous effect to delivered projectiles when a potion is poured into the device’s cup.
