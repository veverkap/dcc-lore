# cookbook

• [[dungeon crawler carl]]'s source of clandestine knowledge and outside contact, providing information that enabled him to exploit the [[trailer]] forcefield and disco ball dust.
• Informational guide summarizing rules, features, and mechanics, including community-annotated notes from crawlers like [[Crawler Coolie]], [[Crawler Forkith]], and [[Crawler Tin]].
• Source of knowledge for [[dungeon crawler carl]], including a passage by [[Coolie]] that informed his understanding of the [[Production Trailer]] forcefield and disco-ball residue effect.
• In-inventory source used by [[dungeon crawler carl]] for knowledge on dungeon venereal diseases and their cures, providing background on medical/dungeon lore.
• Guide item carried by many players, containing limited information about [[Club Vanquisher]] and notes on which players have access to multiple clubs.
