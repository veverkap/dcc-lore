# engineering_table

* A general-purpose crafting table in the [[Crafting Space]] used with magical shaping tools to fashion items, used with tools like the [[Goo-inator 3000]] and the [[Gorgon Marital Aid]].
* Functionally similar to the [[alchemy table]] and noted as undamaged after the boss fight.
* A crafting station where [[dungeon crawler carl]] works on items and prepares [[explosives]] and mechanical devices prior to deploying them on [[Station 149]].
* A workshop module used for engineering-related crafting, listed among the tables that could help create complex explosive/weapon constructs.
* A crafting bench used by [[dungeon crawler carl]] for vehicle and suspension design, with higher levels enabling more complex assemblies.
* A general-purpose crafting bench used by [[dungeon crawler carl]] for fashioning items with magical tools, described as a catch-all work surface in the [[Crafting Space]].
* A crafting bench used by [[dungeon crawler carl]] and [[Katia]] to design and assemble complex mechanical devices like [[royal chariot]].
* A workshop station referenced by [[Carl]] as a place to fabricate custom solutions, with [[Hedy]] warning that non-official magic upgrades are not allowed between heats.
