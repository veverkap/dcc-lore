# dazzlers

• Trap-module projectiles loaded into the looted [[Trebuchet]]; function as combined alarm/[[spotlight]] traps that fire multi-colored strobe beams and loud audio (enhanced by [[justice lights team]]) to disorient enemy soldiers.
• Weapon munitions fired as part of the bombardment that land between trenches to disorient and control battlefield movement.
