# Tome of Legionnaires of the Damned
A chained, quivering summoning spellbook obtained from a box. Type: Summoning Spell. Cost: 25 mana; cooldown: one minute; can reanimate recently-unequipped armor/weapons as temporary undead symbiotes. Warning: most items used disintegrate when spell ends. Applied by [[Donut]] in the chapter.
