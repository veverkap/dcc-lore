# shield

* A defensive spell that blocks many attacks, possibly a level-15 Shield on [[Quan]]'s Celestial Robe.
* A standard spellbook spell received by [[Donut]] that can be chained with tech-based shield abilities.
* A defensive spell used by [[Britney]] that blocks magical and physical attacks for thirty seconds and has a four-hour cooldown.
* A defensive spell used by [[Donut]] to protect vehicles, with variants including a dome-like and cube-shaped shield.
* A protective spell that does not cover certain outer-grid entities, such as ghouls outside the [[Truck]].
* A vehicle defensive equipment that repels acid rain and debris, and requires power to resist heavy abuse.
* A protective item that can be activated, such as [[Bruna]]'s shield.
* A shield that can be used to protect vehicles from falling skulls and attacks during the heat.
