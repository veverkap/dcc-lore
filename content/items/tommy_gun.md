# tommy_gun

• A giant tommy [[gun]] bolted to the roof of the [[Truck]] that the narrator attempts to pry off by hand as part of pre-race modifications.
• A large mounted weapon on the roof of the [[truck]] that [[Carl]] attempts to remove by force prior to the race.
• A firearm mentioned as an obstruction that forces [[Carl]] to ask [[Donut]] to crouch lower when lining up a shot at [[Rapture]].
• A removed weapon from the chicken [[truck]] float; originally attached to the vehicle but taken off to install a platform for the diorama.
• A firearm previously present and described as made of the same material as some of the oversized masks; noted as "now-removed" in the scene.
