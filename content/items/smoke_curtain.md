# smoke_curtain

* Consumable item deployed from inventory to obscure vision and provide cover during entry into the [[Throne Room for the Prism]].
* Consumable tactical item purchased at the [[Silk Road]] booth for crowd control and cover; part of the party's planned equipment for future operations.
* Deployable item used by automatons to create holy-water-infused blinding fog; combines with detonations to damage demons.
* Deployed tactical item used to create obscuring clouds and break line of sight during [[Grull]]'s rampage in [[trainyard]].
* Disposable/consumable tactical item used for concealment; mentioned as part of the group's planned toolkit.
* Tactical consumable used to create ground-ling smoke cover, employed repeatedly to obstruct vision during the fight with [[Grull]] and the mimic's minions.
* Defensive equipment mentioned as being procured from the [[desperado club]] to obscure sightlines and help manage mob waves.
* Common tossable item used for concealment; can be infused with other substances to create variants.
* Deployable item used by automatons to create blinding, burning fog against [[Minge]].
* Twin smoke devices thrown like grenades to create cover and signal the escape.
* Consumable area-effect item infused with healing potion that creates a smoke field; hostile to undead.
* Consumable area-effect device used to mass-kill or repel undead while offering healing.
* Crafted item that provides healing effects and was noted by [[Mordecai]] as part of the group's equipment.
* Consumable/placed items that provide healing effects in an area; another piece of [[dungeon crawler carl]]'s manufactured equipment noted by [[Mordecai]].
* Concealment effect from a [[potion ball]] used to obscure [[Bruna]] from sight during the rooftop assault.
