# book_of_lore

• A reference item used by [[dungeon crawler carl]] to lookup monster entries, including the Duendes, containing portraits and brief descriptions of common mobs and their behavior.
• A book about the [[Bahamas]] (and other local lore) referenced by [[Donut]] and [[dungeon crawler carl]] as a source of information for regional folklore and potential [[phase]]-two [[targeting]].
• An informational item consulted by [[dungeon crawler carl]] to identify the Duende, containing entries on common monsters and their portraits.
• A hardback book containing regional lore and monster entries (e.g., the [[Chickcharney]]) and used for planning starting-location strategy, purchased by Carl as an eighth-floor boon.
• A limited [[Future Floor Boon]] in the prize case that reveals a random starting location on the [[Eighth Floor]]; full title and details reveal upon purchase.
