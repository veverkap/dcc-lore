# nemes_of_pestilence

• An enchanted pharaoh-style pet [[hat]] restricted to pets, intended for a pet like [[Mongo]].
• Grants "Eternal Protector of Rage" (temporary [[invulnerability]] and 2× strength when owner is below 10% HP; once per day) and "Bringer of Death" (75% chance to inflict the Cruel Sepsis debuff on pet bites).
