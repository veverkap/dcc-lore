# Nemes of Pestilence
An enchanted pharaoh-style pet hat restricted to pets. Grants "Eternal Protector of Rage" (temporary invulnerability and 2× strength under owner-health conditions, once per day) and "Bringer of Death" (75% chance that pet bites inflict the Cruel Sepsis debuff). Intended for [[Mongo]].

# Nemes of Pestilence
An enchanted pet-only fabric hat in pharaoh-style intended for a pet like [[Mongo]]. Grants "Eternal Protector of Rage" (temporary invulnerability and 2× strength when owner is below 10% HP; once per day) and "Bringer of Death" (75% chance to inflict the Cruel Sepsis debuff on pet bites).
