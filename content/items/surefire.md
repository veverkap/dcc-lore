# surefire

• A crafting item that grants the "Guided" status to projectiles or traps, allowing them to seek a chosen target and adding [[targeting]] options to traps and powered weapons.
• Can be found in a [[Gold]] [[Sniper]]'s box.
• Grants the "Guided" status to projectiles or traps, allowing them to seek a chosen target and adding [[targeting]] options to traps and powered weapons.
• Can assign targets for arrows, bolts, traps, and powered weapons.
