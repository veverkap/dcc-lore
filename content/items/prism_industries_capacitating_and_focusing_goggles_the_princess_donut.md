# [[Prism Industries Capacitating and Focusing Goggles (\"The Princess Donut\")]]
Unique, one-of-a-kind eyewear gifted to Donut by [[Princess D’Nadia]]. Protects vision, enhances dark vision, provides heat-vision and ocular energy-focusing that modifies spells (e.g., transforms Donut's [[Magic Missile]] into a laser and enables multi-shot behavior).
