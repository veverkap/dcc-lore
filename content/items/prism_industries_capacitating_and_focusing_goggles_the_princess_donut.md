# prism_industries_capacitating_and_focusing_goggles_the_princess_donut

• Unique, one-of-a-kind eyewear gifted to Donut by [[Princess D’Nadia]].
• Protects vision, enhances dark vision, and provides heat-vision and ocular energy-focusing.
• Modifies spells, such as transforming [[Magic Missile]] into a laser and enabling multi-shot behavior.
