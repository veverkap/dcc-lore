# jelly_bomb

• A homemade tire-shaped [[landmine]] assembled by [[dungeon crawler carl]] with a [[hob-lobber]] and [[dynamite]], designed to detonate under [[train]] contact and produce shrapnel to derail cars.
• The device is intended to detonate when a spike is depressed by a [[train]] wheel, producing shrapnel and derailing cars.
• The [[landmine]] is built from [[hob-lobber]] components, scored metal for shrapnel, and an impact-detonated plunger.
• The system labels the device as a [[Jelly Bomb]].
