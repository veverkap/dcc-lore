# [[Jelly Bomb]]
An improvised landmine device Carl built (tire-shaped, spike-plunger design with a hob-lobber core and dynamite). Intended to detonate under train wheels and derail carriages on [[Station 149]]’s tracks.

# Jelly Bomb
An improvised landmine-style explosive built by [[dungeon crawler carl]] for the planned derailment: a hob-lobber-stuffed metal tube with impact plungers and scored shrapnel casing. Labeled a [[Jelly Bomb]] by the system and intended to detonate when a spike is depressed by a train wheel.
