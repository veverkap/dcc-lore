# totem

• Equipment/items found on mobs that can be equipped or sacrificed; enemies in this area carry totems and totem-bearing mobs are central to [[token]] mechanics.
• A special equipment/slot concept referenced by [[Donut]]; certain effects (including the [[Magical Staff Card]] when imbued) behave like a totem and occupy or modify a totem slot.
• Special card/item attached to mobs (examples include stuffed animals and named mob companions); required elements of fights and sometimes tied to higher-level encounters.
• A powerful item referenced as having killed a god in a prior sequence; its intervention is credited as a [[key]] turning point that allowed certain escape outcomes.
• A class of powerful card summons that require special handling: players can keep non-totem [[cards]] freely, but keeping totems may force them to ditch existing totems or equivalents.
• An artifact that played a decisive role in a previous encounter by killing a god, enabling a successful escape sequence referenced by [[Tipid]] and [[Rosetta]].
