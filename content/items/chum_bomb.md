# chum_bomb

• Explosive/attraction charge used by [[Katia]] to draw sharks and mobs into the [[City of Larracos]] [[gate]] as part of the flooding/assault plan.
• Triple-ply bags filled with dead bodies and weakened hob-lobbers fused with hobgoblin pus, detonated to distract concierge sharks and draw aquatic denizens away from the party's approach.
• Explosive bait dropped by [[Katia]] near the [[City of Larracos]] [[gate]] to draw sharks through the [[portal]] and maximize chaos during the flooding operation.
