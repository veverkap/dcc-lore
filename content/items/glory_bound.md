# [[Glory Bound]]
A highly addictive, memory-recalling drug used in the dungeon to force subjects to relive favorite or traumatic memories. In this chapter, a modified version (compounded with subdermal gel and [[Tough Love]] and combined for [[dungeon crawler carl]] with [[Removal Order]]) is used to enter both [[Katia]]’s and [[Shi Maria]]’s memories.

# Glory Bound
[[Glory Bound]] is an extremely addictive and dangerous drug that puts the user into a memory-reliving coma; its cure involves forcing the user through a bad, controlled relapse to avoid permanent psychosis.
