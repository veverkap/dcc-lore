# glory_bound

• [[Glory Bound]] is an extremely addictive and dangerous drug that induces coma-like reliving of memories; the hardest to cure and a major narrative threat.
• A highly addictive, memory-recalling drug used to force subjects to relive favorite or traumatic memories.
• Modified versions of the drug, compounded with subdermal gel and [[Tough Love]], are used to enter memories.
• The cure for the drug involves forcing the user through a bad, controlled relapse to avoid permanent psychosis.
