# [[Cat-Sized Ghillie Suit]]
Small ghillie-style jacket that fits a cat-sized companion like [[Mongo]]. Grants the skill [[Blend into the Background]] and a [[Cone of Silence]] benefit; slots as a jacket and stacks with items like the [[Boonie Hat (Floppy Hat)]] for ranged sniping bonuses.
