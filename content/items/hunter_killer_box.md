# hunter_killer_box

• A delivery/container used to send severed hands or trophies to recipients, including a hunter's removed hand sent to [[dungeon crawler carl]].
• A reward box type containing hunter-related loot, credited for kills and hunting trophies, received by Carl 14 times.
• A recurring prize/loot box category, yielding hunting trophies and [[gold]].
