# goo-inator_3000

• A silver shaping tool obtained from a loot box (Silver Mechanic's Box), usable at a workbench to shape materials into other items.
• An engineering/alchemy device already in [[dungeon crawler carl]]'s inventory, stored alongside newly acquired tools like the [[Gorgon Marital Aid]].
• A "shaping" crafting tool used at the [[Engineering Table]] to reshape rigid items (e.g., flattening the [[Massive Breastplate of a Swordsman Guard]] into sheet-like material), with shape presets and a Freeform option, which can reduce item strength if overused.
• A "shaping" magical tool used at the [[Engineering Table]] to deform and reconfigure solid materials (e.g., flattening a [[Massive breastplate of a swordsman guard]]), offering shape presets including "Freeform" but can lower item strength.
• A shaping tool used at the [[Engineering Table]] by [[dungeon crawler carl]] to reshape thick metal armor into new forms, with multiple preset shapes including "Freeform" but can reduce item strength.
• Shaping tool used by [[dungeon crawler carl]] at the [[Engineering Table]] to reshape bulky metal (example: flattening a swordsman guard breastplate), offering multiple shape options including "Freeform" but can lower item strength if overused.
