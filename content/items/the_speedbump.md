# the_speedbump

• The Speedbump is a portable, modular fortress/ redoubt assembled from Goblin Tables, weight equipment, barbell poles, and braces.
• Nickname for the party's portable, modular fortress/redoubt.
• Designed to wedge and slow the Ball of Swine.
