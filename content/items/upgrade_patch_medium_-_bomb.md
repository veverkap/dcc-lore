# Upgrade Patch (Medium) - Bomb
A medium-sized, finely made patch stitched from Spider Reaper Minion thread silk depicting a bomb. Grants +11% Constitution, +6% explosive damage, +2 to Dangerous Explosives Handling, and a +15% discount from Spider Reaper Minion merchants. Fleeting on removal.
