# Airship Portal
A tier-three guild upgrade that functions as an elevator to the ceiling, allowing access to a docked airship when the ship is hangared to a designated saferoom. Can designate up to three personal airport locations on the map (e.g., the saferoom in the [[College of Larracos]]). The portal creates a physical trap-door-style doorway in a room’s roof that is vulnerable to damage; if the doorway is destroyed while an airship is in the hangar the ship is unavailable until repaired. Grants a new fast-travel option tied to the base where the portal is parked.
