# Busch Light (Beer)
Beverage consumed in the chapter. Appears in at least two variants: a regular Busch Light and a peach-flavored Busch Light (the latter is declined by [[Carl]] and [[Chiyome]]). Offered by [[Radoslav]] from a magical cooler.
