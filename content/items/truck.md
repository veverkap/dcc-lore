# truck

• The team's vehicle used for racing and storage, subject to upgrades and temporary modifications, and serviced by [[Hedy]].
• The crew's vehicle found in the [[Garage]], with a [[Giant tommy gun]] on it, serving as the central worksite for repairs and upgrades.
• The team's vehicle being prepped for the race, containing a freezer chest, the [[Donut drive system]], a rooftop [[Tommy gun]], and the installed GPS [[Dr. Metcalf]].
• The team's race vehicle/transport containing the driver and passenger seats, mounted [[Tommy gun]], GPS [[Dr. Metcalf]], a freezer chest, and the installed [[Donut drive system]].
• The vehicle used by the party to arrive at the floor, described as still-hot, and where [[Mongo]] was kept before being released.
