# Peace Symbol Patch
A small, round upgrade patch (black with a white peace symbol) that, when affixed, grants neutral-mob aggro reduction, +10% Dexterity, and +10% bongos skill. It is fleeting and was affixed near other patches on the [[Enchanted Anarchist’s Battle Rattle]].
