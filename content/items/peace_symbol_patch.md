# peace_symbol_patch

• A small, round upgrade patch (black with a white peace symbol) that grants neutral-mob aggro reduction, +10% Dexterity, and +10% bongos skill.
• It was affixed near other patches on the [[Enchanted Anarchist’’s Battle Rattle]].
• Affixable to eligible garments and destroyed if removed.
