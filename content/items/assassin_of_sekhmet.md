# [[Assassin of Sekhmet]]
A selectable class/talent chosen by [[Donut]] during the vehicle/class-pick sequence. It provides accessory options for party pets and various spells and abilities; the chapter treats class selection like an itemized choice that grants mechanics and accessories.
