# legendary_pet_box

• A higher-tier pet reward granted to [[dungeon crawler carl]] via the [[Trailblazing Crazy Cat Lady]] achievement for being the first crawler to enter with a cat.
• A high-tier pet loot box with ornate construction and clockwork motifs, yielding large quantities of [[Pet Biscuit]] and [[enhanced pet biscuit]].
