# fill_trench_scroll

• A consumable scroll used to fill trenches and neutralize enemy artillery positions, used by forces like the [[102nd bulwark battalion]].
• A scroll-type item used to construct a channel running toward a small lake, mentioned as in-use during construction.
• A frequently appearing consumable scroll, common and low-impact, mentioned by [[dungeon crawler carl]] and found in multiple adventurer boxes.
