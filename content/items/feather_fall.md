# feather_fall

• [[Feather Fall]]: A common mid-late-floor potion that grants fall protection; Mordecai lacks the scarce seed pods to brew many of them on this floor.
• [[dungeon crawler carl]] instructs crawlers to get it if they lack a safe escape for [[Escape plan D]].
• A superior falling-damage prevention effect, referenced as preferable to the [[half splat potion]] potion; described as a more reliable solution for fall survival.
• A potion name invoked by [[Mordecai]] as a common later-floor potion that prevents falling damage; materials are scarce until the sixth floor.
• An ability or potion Carl tells allies to acquire before executing the escape plan; referenced as an expected protective measure for descent.
• Consumable intended as an escape aid; [[dungeon crawler carl]] purchased these to hand out during an evacuation if the seal broke.
• A potion consumed by [[dungeon crawler carl]] to protect against falling or sudden spatial shifts during the chaotic encounter.
• Potion sold in cases (25/24-pack variants available) that provides fall protection; [[dungeon crawler carl]] notes the party is short several of these.
• A potion consumed by [[dungeon crawler carl]] to prevent fall damage/mitigate movement risk as the battle escalates.
