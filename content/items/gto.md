# gto

• A heavily armored vehicle that tailgates and engages in the race; collides with the food [[truck]] during the finish.
• The golden-tired car driven by [[Genesis]] and [[Rapture]] that transports [[Corky]] away to the starting blocks/ factory.
• A rival vehicle that rockets ahead early in the run; used as a positional reference during the jump sequence and briefly carries [[Bianca]] in pursuit.
• A vehicle (the Dominator/GTO) that crashes through a trap [[hole]] and lands on its side, losing [[containment]] and having a broken axle and wheel; occupants like [[Rapture]] and [[Genesis]] are trapped inside.
