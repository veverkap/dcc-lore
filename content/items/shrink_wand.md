# shrink_wand

• A [[wand]] item with a limited number of charges (ten charges referenced) that shrinks targets; similar wands have existed previously (e.g., [[Zhang]]).
• Used in combat to shrink a locked [[Snap Collar]] and thereby sever a hydra head cleanly.
• Used for controlled demolition and construction tasks; [[dungeon crawler carl]] lends this [[wand]] to [[Zhang]] for use in creating an escape channel beneath a church.
• Has charges remaining after use; [[Zhang]] used two zaps on [[dungeon crawler carl]]’s [[wand]], leaving the shrink [[wand]] with seven charges.
• Available in the prize case with ten charges (similar to one formerly owned by [[Zhang]]).
• Can shrink objects; used tactically in conjunction with [[Snap Collar]] to decapitate a hydra head.
• Used to manipulate space/objects; [[dungeon crawler carl]] loans this [[wand]] to [[Zhang]] for controlled demolition and channel construction under the church.
• Has remaining charges after [[Zhang]] used two zaps on it during the church battle.
• Returned to [[dungeon crawler carl]] and kept for future use with seven remaining charges.
