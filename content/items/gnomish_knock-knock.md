# gnomish_knock-knock

• A fuel-air thermobaric [[bomb]] used by the [[gnomes]] (Gnomish Bombardier Squads) or gnome bombardiers.
• Highly unstable and adjustable fuse.
• Devastating against populations.
• Typically attached to gnome airships around the [[Wasteland Castle]].
