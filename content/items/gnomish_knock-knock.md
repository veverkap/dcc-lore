# Gnomish Knock-Knock
A fuel-air thermobaric bomb used by the [[gnomes]] (Gnomish Bombardier Squads). Highly unstable, adjustable fuse, and devastating against populations; spotted attached to gnome airships around the [[Wasteland Castle]].
