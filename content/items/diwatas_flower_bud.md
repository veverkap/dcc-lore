# diwatas_flower_bud

• The large red flower pod, Diwata's flower bud, manifested from a green explosion and bush where a church had been.
• It opened to disgorged the reborn hunter and the antlered squirrel-god [[Diwata]].
• Petals and pollen were collectible and potentially useful.
