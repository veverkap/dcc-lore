# chariot

• Player-built, motorized vehicle with a four-chamber missile tube and configurable seat/controls used during boss engagement.
• Powered vehicle using dwarfish batteries, referenced in context of salvage and power sources.
• Ground vehicle/war-[[cart]] used for transport, equipped with a back [[cart]] for archers and Louis during approach.
• Inventory vehicle for rapid traversal across the Lacuna to rescue and retrieve survivors.
• Custom desert vehicle with a four-chamber missile tube, swivel seat, and [[launch]] controls, modified with a mounted clamp for the Gnomish [[farseer]] telescope and counter-weight cargo tactics.
• Transport construct intended to be rebuilt for travel, partially assembled outside the burning pub.
