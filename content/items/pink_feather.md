# pink_feather

• A small, dirty pink feather recovered by [[Samantha]] and inspected by [[dungeon crawler carl]], linking attacks to a pink-feathered dinosaur (identified as [[Big Tina]] or [[Kiwi the mongoliensis]]).
• Used to identify perpetrators of attacks on riverside towns.
• Served as evidence and helped trigger quest intel ([[The Recital]].)
