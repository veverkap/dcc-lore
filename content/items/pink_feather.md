# Pink Feather
A small dirty pink feather recovered by [[Samantha]] and inspected by [[dungeon crawler carl]]. Served as evidence linking the attack to a pink-feathered dinosaur (identified as [[Big Tina]] or [[Kiwi the mongoliensis]]) and helped trigger quest intel ([[The Recital.]]).
