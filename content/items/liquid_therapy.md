# liquid_therapy

• A daily protection potion brewed by [[Mordecai]] that protects against [[fear]]-based debuffs and specific powerful spells until sleep.
• Guards against [[fear]]-based afflictions such as [[Unending Insanity]] and [[Lights Out]] until the user sleeps.
