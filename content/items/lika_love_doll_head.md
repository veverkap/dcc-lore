# Lika Love Doll Head
The decapitated sex-doll head artifact recovered from Carl's inventory after a crystallization was lifted. Item property text identifies it as possessed by the withering spirit of [[Psamathe]]; it is vocal, partially sentient, and requires exorcism or containment. The object is latex-like, hinged jawed, and is the story catalyst for the party's dilemma about containment and recruitment via the game's NPC slots.
