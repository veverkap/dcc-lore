# lika_love_doll_head

• [[Lika Love Doll Head]]: Item formerly crystallized in inventory and forcibly removed to the floor when decrystallized. Displayed in-game as "Decapitated Lika Sex Doll Head," it is a possessed doll-head vessel now containing the withered half of the god [[Psamathe]] and screams/mutters when manipulated.
• [[Psamathe]]: Withered half of the god contained in the [[Lika Love Doll Head]].
• [[Lika]]: Depicted in a fountain statue, also represented in a glass head and a sex doll head.
• Sticky glass head: The mostly intact glass head of the fountain statue depicting [[Lika]].
• Decapitated glass head: The recovered inventory object, the mostly intact glass head of the fountain statue representing [[Lika]] from the in-world play [[Troubadour’s Bounty]].
