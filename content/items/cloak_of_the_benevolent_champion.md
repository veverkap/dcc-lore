# cloak_of_the_benevolent_champion

• Unique Celestial [[cloak]] originally awarded during a third-floor Celestial Quest.
• Raises wearer's lowest base stat to match their highest over 66 hours.
• Grants a level-5 [[Find Weakened]] skill.
• Boosts four random spells toward level 15 (one immediate, others over a week).
• Raises one random skill toward level 15 over 504 hours.
• Requires continuous wear; countdowns reset if another crawler equips it.
• Matched [[cloak]] with the [[Crupper of the Benevolent Champion]], enabling set effects when combined with other pieces.
