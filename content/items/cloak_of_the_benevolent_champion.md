# Cloak of the Benevolent Champion
Unique Celestial cloak originally awarded during a third-floor Celestial Quest. When worn it raises the wearer’s lowest base stat to match their highest over 66 hours, grants a level-five [[Find Weakened]] skill, boosts four random spells toward level 15 (one immediate, others over a week), and raises one random skill toward level 15 over 504 hours. Benefits require continuous wear; countdowns reset if another crawler equips it.

# Cloak of the Benevolent Champion
A unique Celestial-tier cloak originally awarded during the third floor; it customizes buffs to the wearer to raise their lowest base stat up to their highest over 66 hours and grants multiple other long-duration benefits: a level-five [[Find Weakened]] skill, four spells partially/fully raised toward level 15 (one immediate), and one skill raised to level 15 over 504 hours. Requires continuous wear for countdowns; equipping by another crawler resets progress. The cloak was previously worn by [[Quan]].
