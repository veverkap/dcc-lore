# asojano

• A powerful [[totem]] card in [[Donut]] and [[dungeon crawler carl]]’s collection, potentially difficult to control if summoned with other strong totems.
• Used alongside [[Golden Uzi Jesus]] in the battle that defeated [[Quan]].
• Instrumental in taking down [[Quan]] alongside [[Golden Uzi Jesus]].
• A named monster card from [[Floor 8]] that was combined with [[Uzi Jesus]] to make [[Lazarus]].
