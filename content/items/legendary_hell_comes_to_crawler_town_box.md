# legendary_hell_comes_to_crawler_town_box

• Legendary "Hell Comes to Crawler Town" box: provides unenchanted [[bagpipes]], personal space, and town upgrade coupons for castle and town development.
• Legendary Quest box (Hell Comes to Crawler Town): produces multiple items when opened, including a small unenchanted kilt, a black-hued spellbook containing the [[You're Not Done Yet]] spell, and possibly [[Donut]].
