# Enchanted Auto Buckler of the Peach Pit
A small forearm buckler/bracer that auto-deploys to catch physical and magical bolt attacks. Catch chance scales with Dexterity (physical) and Intelligence (magic); caught attacks have a 75% chance to reflect, and reflected attacks have a 25% chance to double in power. Carl removes this to swap gear.
