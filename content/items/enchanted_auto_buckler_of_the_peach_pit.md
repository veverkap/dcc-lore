# enchanted_auto_buckler_of_the_peach_pit

• A wrist-mounted auto-forming buckler that can be summoned via motion and enchanted with [[Shatter]], granting a small chance to disarm opponents with scripted outcomes for disarmed weapons.
• The buckler can catch physical and magical [[bolt]] attacks, with success based on user stats, and has a 75% chance to reflect and a 25% chance for reflected attacks to double in power.
• The item is enchanted with Shatter, offering a small chance to disarm attackers with item-drop/break/transfer effects.
