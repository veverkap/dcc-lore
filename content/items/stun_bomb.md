# stun_bomb

• Round, beachball-sized explosive modified by [[dungeon crawler carl]] and [[Mordecai]] to paralyze non-corporeal monsters.
• Combined with [[Troll Smoke Mantle]]s for extended area and duration.
• Primary tool used to freeze large groups of [[ghommids]].
