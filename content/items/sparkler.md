# sparkler

• Low-damage, low-noise incendiary device made from a hob lobber wick and fuse
• Used by [[dungeon crawler carl]] to create a brief spark show, distracting guards during the breach
• Tossed through a [[Hole]] to draw attention and trigger guard reactions during the breach
