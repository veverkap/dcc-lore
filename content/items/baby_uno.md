# Baby Uno
The first and heaviest wheeled bomb deployed from the [[Mother of All Bombs]] containing three [[Boom Jug]]s, a clay jug of [[Goblin Oil]], a small jar of [[Gunpowder]], and a core of [[Hobgoblin Pus]]; detonated remotely.
