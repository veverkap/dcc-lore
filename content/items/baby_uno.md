# baby_uno

• The Baby Uno is the first and heaviest wheeled [[bomb]] deployed from the [[Mother of All Bombs]].
• It contains three  [[Boom Jugs]], a clay jug of  [[Goblin Oil]], a small jar of  [[Gunpowder]], and a core of  [[Hobgoblin Pus]].
• It can be detonated remotely.
