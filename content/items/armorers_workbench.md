# armorers_workbench

• A specialized crafting station or workbench required to properly resize and create wearable armor from large pieces, such as the [[Massive Breastplate of a Swordsman Guard]].
• Allows items shaped at engineering tables to be designated as wearable, for example, turning a shaped helmet into an equippable [[Item]].
• Used to convert engineered shapes into equippable armor pieces, such as turning a shaped helmet into an equippable [[Item]] that [[Katia]] can wear.
