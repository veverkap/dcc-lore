# [[Prism Industries Capacitating and Focusing Goggles. Special High-Fashion Edition. “The Princess Donut.”]]
Unique benefactor eyewear gifted by [[Princess D’Nadia]] to [[Donut]]. Protects against vision-harming effects, enhances dark vision and heat vision, and focuses ocular energy spells—allowing [[Donut]] to fire multiple concentrated [[Magic Missile]]-type shots and to store and combine shots for combat.
