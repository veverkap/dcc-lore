# splooge

• A viscous consumable given to [[Penelope]] to calm her, presented in a [[beaker]] and also imbibed by [[Dungeon Crawler Carl]]. Acts like a calming/stimulant substance in the scene.
• A food-based potion/[[buff]] that provides oxygenated benefits in some contexts but follows different cooldown rules than regular [[potions]]; used (or required) by [[Penelope]] and treated specially by [[Mordecai]].
• A consumable fluid given to [[Penelope 3]] from a [[beaker]] to calm her; also consumed briefly by [[Carl]]. Named in-chapter as an immediately calming stimulant for the pig.
• A substance that the pig finds delightful and craves, described as a viscous consumable Carl pours into [[Penelope 3]]'s throat to calm her.
• A food-based potion-like substance treated as a [[buff]] rather than a normal potion (per [[Mordecai]]). Used to help [[Penny]] breathe but behaves differently from standard [[potions]] regarding cooldowns and effects.
• A consumable dose referenced by [[Jurgen]] as the only healing/utility he has available to retrieve [[Prepotente]] from a toxic area; presented as a scarce support item.
