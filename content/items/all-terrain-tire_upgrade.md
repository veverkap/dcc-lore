# all-terrain-tire_upgrade

• Tire-based mobility upgrade for the [[Royal Court Truck]] featuring dozens of tendril-like grips and vertical [[climb]] capability.
• Always active, but imposes a 25% speed reduction and has fixed tire spacing that can limit performance on some obstacles.
