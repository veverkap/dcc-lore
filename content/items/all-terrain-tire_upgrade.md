# All-Terrain-Tire Upgrade
Tire-based mobility upgrade for the [[Royal Court Truck]] featuring dozens of tendril-like grips and vertical climb capability; always active but imposes a 25% speed reduction and has fixed tire spacing that can limit performance on some obstacles.
