# interdiction_cart

• [[Portal]]-equipped carts that patrol lines and can block or transport things; one playing Def Leppard was wedged in a yard and used to deter movement.
• Specialized [[cart]] designed to clear lines more easily than manual [[explosives]]; contrasted with the improvised Rapid-response [[Cart]] [[bomb]] tactic.
• A [[portal]]-equipped [[cart]] that can block or redirect other carts; in one instance, it became wedged against a wall and remained upright.
• A cargo- van–sized rapid-response [[cart]] used as a makeshift [[portal]] platform in Station 36; shrunk temporarily to transport through tight tunnels and suspended by magical [[chains]] to aim a [[Portal]] to the Abyss downward.
• A [[portal]]- equipped [[cart]] variant that can become wedged, flip, or remain online and is dangerous because its attached [[portal]] teleports anything in front of it to other trainyards.
• A generic term used for the [[cart]]-type [[portal]] vehicle, describing the same [[portal]]-equipped, track-based transport used to [[teleport]] monsters and traverse the tunnel network.
• A [[portal]]- equipped [[cart]] that can become immobile and act as a hazard or fortification when wedged against walls or flipped.
• The large rapid-response [[cart]] used as a [[portal]] platform; lifted and manipulated to create an inverted [[portal]] to the Abyss.
