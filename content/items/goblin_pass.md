# goblin_pass

• A glyph-shaped pass produced by a [[Silver Goblin Box]] that physically brands the player (or their clothing) on receipt, acting as an access/identification item tied to goblin-themed content.
• A pass [[tattoo]] branded onto Carl's forearm that removes automatic goblin hostility and allows free passage through goblin-controlled territory, but cannot be hidden without a coverup and causes natural goblin enemies (like [[Fairy-class creatures]]) to deal 20% more damage against the bearer.
