# carls_book_of_boom

• The newly segmented second volume of the [[Book of Voodoo]] contains many newly added entries and pages.
• The book contains [[lightning]] magic, with several pages offering [[lightning]] magic that were reportedly consumed or removed for an encounter.
• Carl consumed [[lightning]] magic from the book, removing/consuming pages offering [[lightning]] magic in preparation for the [[Scolopendra]] fight.
• The book is a personal compendium kept by [[Carl]], containing entries from other crawlers and notes relevant to the dungeon and its inhabitants.
• The book contains pages of spells, and Carl eats a page from it earlier to gain access to spells such as [[Gutter Bumper]].
• Carl consumes an edible book-like item, gaining the [[Bumper]] spell, which is referenced when [[Lucia]] attributes a spell to Carl's father's gift.
