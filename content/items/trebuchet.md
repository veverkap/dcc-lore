# trebuchet

• A looted siege weapon (one small trebuchet) taken from the [[elves]] on the sixth floor, used to fling [[Dazzlers]] into Louis' craters during the assault.
• Large trebuchets with long range able to hit deep into territory
• Smaller, more mobile trebuchets used against trenches
