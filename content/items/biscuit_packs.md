# biscuit_packs

• Thrown [[potions]] used by ghouls to create instant-expanding foam explosions that harden into rock-like barriers to impede approaching enemies.
• Thrown potion- type munitions used by ghouls that explode into fast-hardening foam (three-meter diameter) to snag and block waves of [[Sugar Skull]] mobs, functioning like instant polyurethane barriers.
• Small explosive consumables used by [[Carl]] to incapacitate swarms of beetle mobs by freezing them in expanding foam.
