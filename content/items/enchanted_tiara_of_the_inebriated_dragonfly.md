# Enchanted Tiara of the Inebriated Dragonfly
A citrine-encrusted tiara purchased for [[Donut]] that grants multiple effects when equipped: 50% reduction to potion cooldown, +10 Intelligence, protection from poisoning, negation of the dexterity debuff when inebriated, and a level 10 [[Hover]] skill (five-minute cooldown, five uses per day). Replaces or supplements the kept [[Tiara of Mana Genita]].
