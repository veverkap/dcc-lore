# [[Boonie Hat (Floppy Hat)]]
Floppy hat item (referred to as a boonie hat) that grants the [[Targeting]] skill and increases spell range. When worn with the [[Cat-Sized Ghillie Suit]], provides compounded buffs enabling extreme long-range line-of-sight sniping.
