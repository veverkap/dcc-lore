# katias_deck__cards

• Katia's deck-based inventory mechanic enables summoning of totems and shapeshifting effects.
• Active [[cards]] in the chapter manifest as powerful totems (troll and skull-headed crow) and heavily influence combat.
• The card/deck system used by [[Katia]] to summon totems and activate powerful effects, deploying totems that play a decisive role in the death of [[Astrid]].
