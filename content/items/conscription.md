# conscription

• A spell/mechanic that prevents direct attacks on officers and allows command over recruits.
• A battlefield hazard that forces NPCs and crawlers into dangerous situations if detected.
• A spell/debuff used by the Princess Posse that reduces the ability of captured faction members to act against their captors.
• A spell/effect that marks allied soldiers with a blue/ [[light]]-blue glow, indicating they are forced into the [[Madness]] military.
• A system spell that inflicts a no-healing debuff on those who refuse to fight.
• A powerful spell used to bind gods and entities to player control, such as [[Harpocrates]].
