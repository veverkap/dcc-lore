# kimaris

• A plush collectible depicting an armored man atop a black horse, found in a charred, enchanted glass case survived an explosion and was looted by Carl into his inventory.
• A beanie-baby stuffed toy, a horse-riding soldier, belonging to Miss Quill, retrieved from the Sheol Glass Reaper Case inventory, used as a narrative/plot device to contain the Soul Crystal.
• A plush creature, an armored man atop a black horse, found in a charred glass case from Miss Quill's apartment, enchanted and pulled into Carl's inventory.
• A beanie-baby stuffed horse-riding soldier, retrieved from a protective case, used as the medium to close the Sheol Glass Reaper Case around the Soul Crystal during an emergency.
• A unique beanie figure in the party's possession that transforms into a demon when its tag is removed, considered more valuable than common Grulke figures.
