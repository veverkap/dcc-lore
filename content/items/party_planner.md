# Party Planner
The silver tilt-rotor stealth light bomber owned by [[Louis Santiago]] and assigned to [[princess posse]]. Described as roughly fifty feet long, low to the ground, with pop-out propellers and a multi-use bomb bay with a 50% increase in bomb yield by default. Classified as a vehicle and compared to the earlier [[Twister]]; runs on a soul crystal. Can be hangared and upgraded (stealth, anti-flak shield, door guns, etc.).
