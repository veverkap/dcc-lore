# mysterious_letter

• A blood-stained folded note found on [[GumGum]] written in unknown squiggle/triangle script, identified as [[necroscript]] by [[Mordecai]], suggesting undead or necromantic origin.
• The note was used as bait in Carl's original plan to draw the magistrate out and was referenced as part of the pre-raid strategy to lure targets into position.
• The note was recovered from [[GumGum]]'s inventory, written in an unreadable script identified as "necroscript" by [[Mordecai]], serving as a potential clue pointing toward undead/magic-user involvement.
