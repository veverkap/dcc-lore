# gloom_wraith_phase

• Allows non-corporeal bursts during lunge attacks that bypass unenchanted weapons and scramble unarmored biological targets, with mana cost and tiered upgrades.
• Renders Carl intangible to attack, but was suppressed repeatedly by Mind Balance.
• Used to pass through mobs and cause disintegration-like damage; short duration and used to avoid a poisonous aerosol attack.
• Grants Carl the ability to move rapidly through danger, but resulted in collateral magical injury.
• Used to move sideways and avoid tower [[targeting]] while approaching the Nest Tower.
• Allows Carl to move through a creature's flesh rapidly; used to enter the interior of the beast (Scolopendra) and reach scolopendra.
• Forces movement as an offensive action; contrasted with travel-[[phase]] spells like [[Oozy]] Form.
• Used to [[phase]] and escape from inside Prison Pocket's pouch during the detonation sequence.
• Phases through the creature's wall and enters the beast's interior; treated as an equippable/usable ability in the chapter.
• Acts as an attack and forces movement in a direction, making it less useful as a travel spell compared to [[Oozy]] Form or Smoke Form.
• Used in combination with [[explosives]] to escape the boss pouch.
