# enchanted_toe_ring_of_the_splatter_skunk

• An enchanted accessory with a themed name, obtained from a [[Gold Shoe Box]].
• Grants +3 Strength and +3 to the skill Powerful Strike.
• Upgraded via a [[Scroll of Upgrade]] at the [[Casino]], granting +10 Strength and +5 to the skill Powerful Strike.
• Previously provided +3 Strength, upgrade added significant skill level increases.
