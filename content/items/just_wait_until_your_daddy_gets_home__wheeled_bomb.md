# just_wait_until_your_daddy_gets_home__wheeled_bomb

• The Wheeled [[Bomb]] is a large, egg-shaped, clamshell [[bomb]] assembled from a casing, explosive filler, and blocks of Nitro Sludge.
• It is a high-yield thermobaric explosive that also imbues [[Fear]] within its blast radius.
• The [[bomb]] has a system-assigned name of "Just Wait Until Your Daddy Gets Home".
• Its status is shown as "250. Fortified".
• The claimed effective radius is flattening within a third of a mile and lethal/severe injury to a half mile beyond.
