# [[Just Wait Until Your Daddy Gets Home – Wheeled Bomb.]]
The system-assigned name for Carl's large, egg-shaped, clamshell bomb assembled from a casing, explosive filler, and blocks of [[Nitro Sludge]]. Type: high-yield thermobaric explosive; additionally imbues [[Fear]] within its blast radius. Status shown as "250. Fortified." Claimed effective radius: flattening within a third of a mile and lethal/severe injury to a half mile beyond.
