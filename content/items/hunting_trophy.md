# hunting_trophy

• A severed hand/claw extracted from a crawler or mob, used as currency/[[token]] at the Butcher's Masquerade prize counter.
• Can be exchanged for prizes, with the cheapest prize costing a single hand.
• Transferring or selling the item reverts it to an ordinary appendage.
