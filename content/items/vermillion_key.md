# vermillion_key

• A specific [[key]] required to disengage or control engine cars, retrieved (or claimed) by [[Katia]] from [[Hekla]]'s corpse, and critical to the [[train]]'s ability to decouple and maneuver.
• The [[key]] controls access to engine car functions and locking mechanisms, necessary for detaching and operating the [[train]].
• [[Katia]] retrieves the [[Vermillion key]] from [[Hekla]]'s corpse, determining the ability to lock/operate engine doors.
• The [[key]] is attached to [[Hekla]]'s corpse and is required to unlock and disengage the engine car doors or control access, as instructed by [[dungeon crawler carl]].
