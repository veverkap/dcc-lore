# abrams_tank

• A heavy vehicle on the battlefield whose main [[gun]] is manipulated by [[Sarah Hayse]] to fire black-flamed attacks at [[Krakaren]].
• An armored vehicle described as a smoking wreck after being struck by a [[Krakaren]] tentacle, with several occupants killed or injured.
• A heavily armed vehicle used by the Destruction team (with [[Makana]] and [[Sarah Hayse]]), part of their "show up all weapons" approach.
