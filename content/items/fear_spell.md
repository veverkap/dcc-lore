# fear_spell

• A spell upgraded to level 10 via a potion from a [[Platinum Asshole's Box]], with a 2% chance to paralyze targets and causing them to flee.
• Can be cast in a wide area and imbued into [[explosives]] or smoke curtains, with a risk of creating "Dud" items.
• Causes enemies, such as [[Red Cornet]]s, to flee, affecting combat flow and enabling [[Mongo]] to finish targets.
