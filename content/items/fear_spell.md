# Fear (Spell)
A spell Carl possesses that was upgraded five levels to level 10 via a potion from a [[Platinum Asshole's Box]]. At level 10 it has a 2% chance to paralyze targets and can be cast in a wide area; it causes targets to flee and can be imbued into explosives or smoke curtains (with risk of creating "Dud" items).
