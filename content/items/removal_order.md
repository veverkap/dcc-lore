# [[Removal Order]]
A potion/compound intended to allow [[dungeon crawler carl]] to visit and interact with the memory of the possessing entity ([[Shi Maria]]) rather than his own memories. It is presented as the mechanism by which the spider could be locked inside its worst memory.
