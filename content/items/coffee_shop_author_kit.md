# coffee_shop_author_kit

• A magical sheet of paper, quill, and [[ink]] that creates a second [[scratchpad]] tab and allows producing scroll scripts for market sale or printing when proper spells/glyphs are written.
• Used for two-way message exchange between Carl's personal-space stall and his [[scratchpad]], serving as a covert messaging system inside the bathroom stall.
• Received from a [[Gold]] box, allowing players to produce and market scrolls.
