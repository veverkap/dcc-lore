# Enchanted Nightgaunt Cloak of Stoutness
A magical cloak made from a wing-like hide with fingerbones; grants +4 Constitution, resistance to poison and ice, adds Anti-Piercing to armor, and has a visible hood with ear points. Warns that actual [[Nightgaunt]]s may take offense if they spy you wearing it.
