# enchanted_nightgaunt_cloak_of_stoutness

• A [[gold]]-tier enchanted [[cloak]] that appeared from a [[Gold Apparel Box]]; treated as notable apparel with special properties (implied defensive/stats boost).
• Grants +4 Constitution, resistance to [[poison]] and ice, adds Anti-Piercing to armor, and has a visible hood with ear points.
• Warns that actual [[Nightgaunt]]s may take offense if they spy you wearing it.
