# boss_box

• A reward container associated with defeating major enemies, explicitly asked about by [[Donut]] after clearing an area or killing a nearby chieftain.
• A loot container obtained by party members after boss encounters, mentioned when [[Brandon]] receives items and others receive varying rewards.
• An item that [[Donut]] reveals she opened, yielding healing items and a [[poison]] dart trap kit, a typical dungeon loot container.
• A loot container dropped by boss mobs, such as the [[Odious Creeper]], containing items like [[Satchel Bombs]].
• A lootable reward type previously obtained by [[dungeon crawler carl]] from boss encounters, part of the chest/box reward taxonomy mentioned.
• A loot container referenced when [[Donut]] loots a boss box and receives items such as [[scroll of heal]] and a [[Poison Dart Trap Kit]].
• A quality-tier reward container, distinct from adventurer and achievement boxes, which can produce unique items like [[Drakea’]]s Enchanted Kerchief of Disorder]].
• A special loot container dropped by bosses, containing unique items, satchel [[bombs]], and other high-value loot, referenced when discussing previous drops.
• A generic reward container acquired after boss encounters, referenced by [[dungeon crawler carl]] as being among his inventory.
