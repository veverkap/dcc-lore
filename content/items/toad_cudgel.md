# toad_cudgel

• A crude melee weapon resembling a shortened baseball bat, obtained from a [[Bronze Weapon Box]] in the chapter.
• A blunt melee weapon, described in-game as "Big stick for bonking. Bonk toad. Bonk mob. Bonk girlfriend and drag back to cave by hair."
• A blunt melee weapon, described as rounded-edged and awkward for head strikes, attempted to be used against a [[Scatterer Brood Guardian]].
