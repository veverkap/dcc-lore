# gold_pacifists_box

• An achievement reward for completing a floor without harming mobs.
• Contains mid-tier loot, such as skill [[potions]] (one of which provides a skill potion for [[Donut]] and [[Love Vampire]]).
• Awarded when a crawler survives a floor without harming mobs, as part of the [[Pacifist]] achievement.
