# yellow_line_engineers_key

• A [[key]] item required to open the door toward the engine car on the [[Yellow Line]] [[train]].
• Required to open the sealed doorway toward the engine car on the [[Yellow Line]] [[train]].
• Needed to access the lead car and engine; its absence prevents access.
