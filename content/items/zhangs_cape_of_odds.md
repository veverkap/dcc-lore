# zhangs_cape_of_odds

• Unique celestial cape owned by [[Zhang]] that grants passive +15% to all stats.
• Level 10 [[Dodge]] skill (stacking with existing [[Dodge]]).
• Once-an-hour "Roll the Dice" ability with 333 possible outcomes (66 negative outcomes, several epic effects, and manual choice after 19 spins).
