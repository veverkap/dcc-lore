# Zhang’s Cape of Odds
Unique celestial cape owned by [[Zhang]] that grants passive +15% to all stats, a level 10 Dodge skill (stacking with existing Dodge), and a once-an-hour "Roll the Dice" ability with 333 possible outcomes (66 negative outcomes, several epic effects, and manual choice after 19 spins).
