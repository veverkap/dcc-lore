# bomb_squad_tech

• Exclusive Earth class focused on [[explosives]].
• Requires the Boom! Achievement.
• Grants explosive-safety benefits (marked traps, non-degrading handling), skill bonuses, armor effectiveness, and the Limb Regeneration Benefit.
• Includes a Silver Earth Box on selection.
