# [[Bomb Squad Tech]]
Exclusive Earth class focused on explosives. Requires the Boom! Achievement. Grants explosive-safety benefits (marked traps, non-degrading handling), skill bonuses, armor effectiveness and the Limb Regeneration Benefit; includes a Silver Earth Box on selection.
