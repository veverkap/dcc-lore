# Goblin Copper Chopper
A cobbled-together steam-powered assault bicycle used by goblin riders. The frame is welded copper, powered by a [[Permacube]]-fed engine with a coal hopper; it has a detachable sidecar and spring suspension. Frequently called a "copper chopper" or "chopper" in the chapter and issued to [[goblin bomb bard]] and other [[goblin]] as a short-range transport.
