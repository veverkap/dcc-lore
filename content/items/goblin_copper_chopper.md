# goblin_copper_chopper

• A cobbled-gether steam-powered assault bicycle used by goblin riders, with a detachable sidecar and spring suspension.
• Powered by a [[Permacube]]-fed engine with a coal hopper, and frequently called a "copper chopper" or "chopper".
• Used by goblin riders, including the goblin [[bomb]] bard, as a short-range transport.
• Used to tow the Mother of All [[Bombs]], emitting the system warning "Goblin Copper Chopper – [[Boiler]] Breach Imminent" when over-stressed.
• A vehicle referenced as the intended means of travel after finding a safe room and stairs, used as part of the party's planned escape route from the neighborhood.
• The narrator's fuel-powered two-wheeled vehicle used for travel, consuming lumps of coal, and granting experience to the Chopper Pilot skill.
• A transport vehicle referenced as a potential means of escape from the area while the Rage Elemental pursued targets.
• A custom, handcrafted [[motorcycle]] built by Rory the Shamanka and Lorelai, with a personalized sidecar that Donut favors.
• A custom, handcrafted [[motorcycle]] previously used by Carl's group, built by Rory the Shamanka and Lorelai, and contrasted with Carl's current 1965 Harley Davidson FLH Panhead Electra Glide.
