# venomous_elven_rock_chucker_trebuchet_device

• A seized siege weapon with a removable cup for pouring [[potions]], used to deliver [[poison]]-laced projectiles at range.
• Has a side cup allowing different poisoning [[potions]] to be used for projectile delivery.
• Can apply a [[Poison Cloud enhancement]] when loaded.
• Originally taken from Dream hunters, and is approximately eight feet long.
