# heal_critter_scroll

• A consumable item or scroll that heals pets or animal companions.
• Can be used to [[heal]] critters, but failed to [[heal]] [[Donut]] with a "No Valid Target Available" error.
• Consumable scrolls that can [[heal]] pets/critters, noted by [[dungeon crawler carl]].
• A targeted healing spell cast by [[Donut]] to restore [[Mongo]] after explosive damage.
• A healing spell cast by [[Princess Donut]] to fully [[heal]] companions like [[Mongo]] and [[Kiwi]].
• A healing spell Donut has that was indicated as one of the spells improved by the [[cloak]] to level 15.
• A healing spell for critter-type allies mentioned among the spells affected by the [[cloak]]'s bonuses.
• A healing spell used by [[Princess Donut]] to restore health to companions like [[Mongo]] and [[Kiwi]] after they were injured by the high-elf attack.
• [[heal critter scroll]] is a scroll or spellbook received as boss loot by [[Donut]].
• [[heal critter scroll]] is a spellbook containing the healing spell specific to critters/pets.
