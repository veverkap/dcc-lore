# benefactor_box

* A high-tier loot container that patrons can send, containing rare items and goods, distinguished from ordinary Adventurer boxes by rarity and contents.
* A special loot box tied to patronage payments, with higher patron bids improving quantity/quality of these boxes for beneficiaries.
* A gacha/loot container type that can yield unique items, such as the [[Prism Industries Capacitating and Focusing Goggles. Special High-Fashion Edition. “The Princess Donut.”]].
* A box awarded by a sponsor as part of a bidding/reveal promotion at the signing, announced as the item the new sponsor wishes to give Carl during the event.
* A loot container delivered by a sponsor that included [[Pawna’'s Cries]] and a cryptic note about saving the [[Toraline]].
* A sponsor-issued loot/container that delivers targeted gifts and items to players, with multiple characters receiving benefactor boxes in this chapter, some containing items intended specifically for [[Princess Donut]].
* A mystery loot box that [[dungeon crawler carl]] received from a sponsor, containing a crumpled flyer for a homeless shelter in Homestead, Florida; the sponsor and associated charity reportedly disappeared after sending it.
* A mystery loot box type that can contain unique themed items and sponsor gifts, such as [[Prism Industries Capacitating and Focusing Goggles...]] given to [[Donut]].
* A social/loot box [[Donut]] mentions receiving as a benefit from in-game patronage systems and streaming progression.
* A reward to be awarded on-stage by the replacement sponsor chosen in a new bidding event, announced by [[Zev]] as part of the signing program.
* A class of loot box received from benefactors, one in the chapter yielding a variety of potion and scroll drops.
* A high-value loot box that contained a limited-time potion called [[Pawna'’s Cries]] and an attached note, revealing sponsorship ties (notably to [[Circe Took]]) and influencing player decisions.
* A system-delivered box from sponsors that contains gifts or items (benefactor items), with multiple characters (including [[Prepotente]], [[Donut]], and [[Imani]]) receiving boxes, some containing items specifically tagged for [[Donut]].
* A sponsor-provided reward container whose items are often restricted to the recipient hunter, referenced as the source of some gear restrictions (e.g., [[sunglasses]]).
* A mystery [[gold]] box sent by an unknown benefactor, containing a crumpled flyer advertising the homeless shelter [[Shepherd’’s House]] in [[Homestead, Florida]]; the sponsor who sent it later vanished.
* A branded loot container (e.g., from [[princess formidable of the skull empire]] and others) that can contain rare items such as bolts and other floor-changing gear.
