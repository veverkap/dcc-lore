# anti-air_gun

• Hunter-deployed turret designed to neutralize aerial threats such as [[elle mcgib]], mounts like [[Bianca]] under [[Prepotente]], or creatures like the [[Twister]].
• Town defense weapon with an anti-seeking enchantment that can destroy flying decoys like [[Samantha]] and emit massive explosions when triggered.
• Hunter-deployed heavy defense designed to counter aerial attacks and assets such as [[elle mcgib]], [[Prepotente]] on [[Bianca]], and other air-based assaults.
• Often paired with anti-[[targeting]] spells and common in larger hunter groups.
• Heavy weapon in the town with an anti-seeking enchantment that prevents seeker automatons from homing in on it; the raiders used a tactic involving [[Samantha]] to trigger and destroy it.
