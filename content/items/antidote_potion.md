# antidote_potion

• A potion referenced as the remedy for [[poison]] from [[Virtuoso]] attacks.
• Consumable for curing [[poison]], awarded in the [[Silver Adventurer Box]] and other mid-tier rewards.
• A rare consumable received from a silver adventurer's box, used by [[dungeon crawler carl]] to prevent fatal effects and enable the blood-poisoning trick on [[Ringmaster Grimaldi – Pestiferous Vine]].
• Consumable used by [[dungeon crawler carl]] after being poisoned when his [[Nightgaunt Cloak]]'s magic was temporarily stripped by a precursor burst.
• Used by [[dungeon crawler carl]] after being poisoned by an effect triggered during pulses, neutralizing the [[poison]] inflicted when items activated on their own.
• A common consumable on this floor, paired with [[Scroll of Water Breathing]], suggesting the floor has [[poison]]-dealing monsters and traps.
