# club_vanquisher

• An access permission/item granting entry to certain club areas, required to reach the temple via a Club, and possessed by multiple characters.
• A vehicle type named "Club Vanquisher" referenced in the Emberus quest directive as a mode for traveling to the [[Temple of Issitoq]].
