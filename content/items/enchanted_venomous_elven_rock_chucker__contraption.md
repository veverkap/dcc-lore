# Enchanted Venomous Elven Rock Chucker – Contraption
A portable, green-glowing light trebuchet looted by [[dungeon crawler carl]]. Constructed from magically-infused bamboo via the [[Bamboozeled]] effect, it hurls heavy rocks (up to ~300 kilos) imbued with [[Poison Cloud]]. Has a 90-second cooldown and was added to inventory under the new tab [[Siege Equipment]].
