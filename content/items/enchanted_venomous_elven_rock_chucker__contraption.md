# enchanted_venomous_elven_rock_chucker__contraption

• Lightweight, green-glowing [[trebuchet]]-like siege device assembled from magical bamboo via [[Bamboozeled]].
• Hurls heavy rocks (up to ~300 kilos) imbued with [[Poison Cloud]].
• Has a 90-second cooldown.
• Added to [[dungeon crawler carl]]'s inventory under the [[Siege Equipment]] tab.
