# legendary_fan_box

• A rare reward given by the [[Holistic]] achievement, restricted to viewers from the relevant system (naga system).
• The box is shaped like a large cobra head (likened to [[Vinata]]).
• When opened, it spews an enormous volume of scorched, wet, and damaged items (clothing, furniture, makeup, trophies), including the narrator's marriage certificate from [[Princess Chandra]].
