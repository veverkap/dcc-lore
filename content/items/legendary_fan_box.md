# [[Legendary Fan Box]]
A high-tier fan box shaped like a large cobra head (likened to [[Vinata]]). When opened it spews an enormous volume of scorched, wet, and damaged items (clothing, furniture, makeup, trophies), including the narrator's marriage certificate from [[Princess Chandra]].
