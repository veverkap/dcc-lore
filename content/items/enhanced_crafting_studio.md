# enhanced_crafting_studio

• [[Enhanced Crafting Studio]]: A tier-one crafting-room upgrade purchased by [[Mordecai]]'s recommendation for the conjoined personal space; costs significant [[gold]] and improves crafting capability for the team.
• Provides upgraded crafting capabilities and is removable and returned to a player's library if spaces are disengaged.
• Costs approximately 255,000 [[gold]] and is selected by [[Mordecai]] as an essential crafting station for the conjoined room.
• Imparts crafting-related benefits to the party.
• Recommended by [[Mordecai]] for improving party gear.
