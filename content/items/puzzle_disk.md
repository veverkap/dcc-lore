# puzzle_disk

• The Puzzle Disk is a ritual device composed of nine pieces that must be primed and physically touched by a storm god or hit with god-tier storm magic to produce the [[key]] that opens the stairwell to the [[Eleventh Floor]].
• It is kept primed by [[Hold Steady]] and targeted by party strategies throughout the chapter.
• The nine-piece Puzzle Disk must be primed and then physically hit by a storm god or god-tier storm magic to produce the [[Key Mote]].
• It must remain within the stairwell circle and be constantly infused with magic by [[Hold Steady]] to function.
