# Puzzle Disk
The ritual device composed of nine pieces that must be primed and physically touched by a storm god or hit with god-tier storm magic to produce the key that opens the stairwell to the [[Eleventh Floor]]. It is kept primed by [[Hold Steady]] and targeted by party strategies throughout the chapter.
