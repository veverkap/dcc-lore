# lightning_rod

• A bard song cast by [[Chaco]] to draw spells and divine attacks toward the party's chosen target (the [[Puzzle Disk]]), used to manipulate incoming god-tier magic.
• Used by [[Chaco]] to attract and redirect spells and summoned bugs toward the puzzle, improving the party's chance that a god-cast spell will strike the puzzle disk.
• A song/bardic magic performed by [[Chaco]] to redirect spells and attract deity attacks toward the [[Puzzle Disk]], used tactically to manipulate the [[targeting]] of summoned storm magic.
