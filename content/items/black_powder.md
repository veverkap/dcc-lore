# black_powder

• Old-school, coarse black powder looted in large amounts from the [[Goblin Workshop]]. Noted as different from modern smokeless powder and primarily useful for propelling shrapnel rather than producing massive explosions.
• A bulk explosive chemical in [[dungeon crawler carl]]'s stash mentioned as part of crafting challenges for his planned devices.
