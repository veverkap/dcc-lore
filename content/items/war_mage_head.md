# war_mage_head

• The severed head of a war mage (Dismember) picked up and stored in inventory as loot by dungeon crawler Carl.
• The head is looted after decapitating Dismember and is kept in Carl's inventory.
• The head is shown briefly in recap footage.
• The head is referenced among Carl's inventory items by Shi Maria during the internal inspection.
• The head is mentioned among Carl's possessions during the mental inventory search by Shi Maria, implied to be a powerful augment/loot piece.
