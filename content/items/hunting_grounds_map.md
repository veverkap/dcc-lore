# hunting_grounds_map

• A crudely drawn map found on the back of a kids’ menu in [[Hunter Arwick]]’s inventory, segments hunting territory, and lists many crawler names, including [[dungeon crawler carl]], [[Donut]], [[Louis]], [[Katia]], and others.
• Reveals which hunting groups, such as [[The Hive]], [[skull clan]], and [[Filigree]], have claimed specific areas and targets.
