# Hunting Grounds Map
A crudely drawn map found on the back of a kids’ menu in [[Hunter Arwick]]’s inventory. It segments hunting territory, lists many crawler names (including [[dungeon crawler carl]], [[Donut]], [[Louis]], [[Katia]], and others), and reveals which hunting groups (e.g., [[The Hive]], [[skull clan]], [[Filigree]]) have claimed specific areas and targets.
