# superb_constitution_buff_potion

• A stat potion that permanently increases Constitution by a random 1–4 points.
• Limited to two uses per crawler in the dungeon.
• Crafted by [[Mordecai]].
• "Superb" quality variants are the best craftable versions.
• Permanently raises Constitution by 1–4 points per dose.
