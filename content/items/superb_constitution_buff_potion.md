# Superb Constitution Buff Potion
A stat potion that permanently raises Constitution by 1–4 points per dose. Players may drink only two while inside the dungeon; Mordecai can craft them and mentions a rarer "Superb" quality and separate [[Cosmic Buff]] potions.
