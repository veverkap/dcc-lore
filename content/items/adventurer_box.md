# adventurer_box

• Standard loot box distributed through season systems or patron sponsorship, varying by tier and quality.
• Contains assorted starter loot, such as [[potions]], bandages, and occasionally gear like the [[Silver ring]].
• Common loot box containing low-to-mid tier gear, including healing [[potions]], mundane weapons, and scrolls.
• Occasionally yields utility items like [[scroll of water breathing]].
• Can be opened in a safe space, producing [[potions]], scrolls, and other random items.
• Referenced when [[Prepotente]] opens several boxes.
• Common reward container received for many small or humorous achievements, such as bronze and silver Adventurer boxes earned by [[dungeon crawler carl]] and [[Donut]].
