# idolized

• A persistent mystic card/effect cast by [[Tom]] that briefly blinds and rallies turkeys, turning them hostile and enabling additional enemy effects (paired with the [[Ralphie]] mechanic).
• The effect blindingly alters the battlefield, turning all turkey dots red when it activates.
