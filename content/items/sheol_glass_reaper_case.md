# [[Sheol Glass Reaper Case]]
A rare protective artifact forged in [[Sheol]] (level 15 of the [[World Dungeon]]) sold by traveling [[Spider Reaper Minions]]. It protects contained items against most forms of abuse; opening it carries a 1.5% chance to trigger the [[Sheol Fire]] spell. Carl uses this case to contain the floating [[Soul Crystal]] in an attempt to stop the explosion.
