# sheol_glass_reaper_case

• A rare protective artifact forged in [[Sheol]] (level 15 of the [[World Dungeon]]) sold by traveling [[Spider Reaper Minions]]. It protects contained items against most forms of abuse and has a 1.5% chance to trigger the [[Sheol Fire]] spell when opened.
• Used by [[dungeon crawler carl]] to enclose the [[Soul Crystal]] and attempt to prevent the mass-explosion event.
• A magical container referenced as part of the crafting of [[Carl’'s Doomsday Scenario]]. Originally contained the glowing gem and contributed to the device's instability and explosive properties.
• A protective display case used to house high-rarity items, including the narrator's [[Kimaris]] figure and other legendary items.
• Cases can have hazardous triggers (e.g., fire) when opened.
