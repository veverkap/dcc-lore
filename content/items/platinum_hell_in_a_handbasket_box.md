# platinum_hell_in_a_handbasket_box

• Platinum box that contained a small patch depicting a spider (linked to [[Shi Maria]] imagery) and other thematic loot.
• Reward item granted via the Area Message/Achievement when [[Amayon]] appears, handed out as loot/reward funnily described by the system.
• Named platinum-tier loot box (described as demon-head shaped) that provided a spellbook to recipients (received by both [[Donut]] and others for interacting with [[Amayon]] on a prior floor).
