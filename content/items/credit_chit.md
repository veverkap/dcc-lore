# credit_chit

• A credit chit is an unexplained off-world payment instrument, resembling a credit card with no in-game description, looted from [[The Talent]].
• It functions as a valuable item/currency [[token]] in the club economy, used for transactions and transfers.
• It was carried by patrons and looted from bodies, with one recovered from [[Haxor the Destroyer]].
• It was also found in [[Donut]]'s inventory and requested for transfer by [[Rosetta]].
• [[Rosetta]] allegedly used it to move [[King Rust]]'s wealth before sending it off the system.
• It is a transferable monetary instrument, treated as valuable and time-sensitive by the party.
