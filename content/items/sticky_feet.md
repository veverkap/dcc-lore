# sticky_feet

* Allows [[dungeon crawler carl]] to walk on ceilings for (dexterity × 2) seconds once every six hours, used to traverse the ooze chamber upside-down to reach the boss core.
* Used by [[dungeon crawler carl]] to momentarily keep from being thrown off the wing during aerial maneuvers.
* Enables wall-running and anchoring to surfaces, used by [[dungeon crawler carl]] to traverse the temple steeple and spire before it expired.
* Used by [[Sister Ines]] to cling to the snake boss's head; presented as an active ability type in combat.
* Used by [[dungeon crawler carl]] to maintain traction and run up the oil-slicked, transformed ramp inside the [[Nest Tower]] after [[Donkey Kong]] is activated.
* Allows [[dungeon crawler carl]] to walk on the ceiling for a duration equal to his dexterity times two seconds once every six hours, used to approach the ooze core upside-down.
* Used by [[dungeon crawler carl]] to avoid being blown off the biplane's wing mid-manuver.
* Movement/[[buff]] item that allows climbing and sticking to surfaces, used by [[dungeon crawler carl]] to move up buildings and spires.
* Used by [[dungeon crawler carl]] to anchor himself to structures during the sliding barn collapse.
* A [[totem]]/skill effect allowing a character (e.g., [[Sister Ines]]) to cling to surfaces such as the boss snake's head.
* Used by [[Carl]] to anchor himself and [[Penny]] against tumbling and shifting dungeon terrain.
* A spell/ability used by [[Carl]] to adhere to the ceiling and prevent falling while navigating an apartment.
