# two-stage_rocket

• Craftable projectile to reach higher altitudes than commercially available projectiles
• Purchased rocket design to be modified at the sapper's table to increase range and reach the Wasteland
• Hand-loaded rockets fired from the [[Chariot]]'s launcher
• Modified by Dungeon Crawler Carl and upgraded by Mordecai to use a two-stage propulsion system and larger warhead
• Used to lock onto and target Ruckus
