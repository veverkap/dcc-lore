# wand

• An inert wand held by [[Big Tina]] that is recharged by the [[Recharge Scroll]] and used to cast charming sparkles and the spell [[The Recital]].
• Wands are single-spell items in the crawl and rare/valuable.
• Wands can be physically interacted with by performers on the main stage despite existing three floors away (per the ballroom overlay rules).
• Wands are standard magical implements.
• Wands can be used to cast the charm-liked performance spell called [[The Recital]].
• Wands can shrink nonliving targets down to button size and were used to shrink the [[Def Leppard cart]] for transport.
• Wands are a generic magical item type referenced when [[dungeon crawler carl]] receives a wand-related achievement and wand-casting skill increases.
