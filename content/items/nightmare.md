# nightmare

* The party's steam engine [[train]], used as their primary vehicle in the Iron Tangle yard, functions as a mobile shelter, weapon platform, and central transport for the crawler party.
* The Nightmare is a named [[train]] that pulls into the area on a separate track, functions as an important vehicle/object in the operation to move people and the [[portal]] [[cart]], and is mentioned in mission instructions as part of the [[bomb]]/escape plan.
* The [[train]] is used to drive into the abyss to detonate and overload the ghoul systems, central to the sacrificial plan involving Fire Brandy and Tizquick.
* The Nightmare is Carl's steam engine [[train]], used by the party, and is central to the chapter, being maneuvered through the railyard and onto the turntable.
* The [[train]] is used as a platform for riders and is referenced while mounting the [[train]], used as part of the rescue movement.
* The Nightmare is a potential means of travel back to the abyss, considered too risky after interior gangways began collapsing.
* The [[train]] is piloted by Fire Brandy in a nearby line, used to ferry groups between stations.
* The Nightmare is a named [[train]] on the employee track that arrives and stops during the aftermath of the boss fight, part of the mission logistics and intended to be set to explode as part of the plan.
* The [[train]] is used to send into the abyss as a timed explosive to destroy monsters and overload the ghoul system.
* The Nightmare is the party's vehicle, central mobile base in the chapter, boarded at Service Bay 32, unhooked and maneuvered using the Turntable to escape the ghouls and reach other stations.
* The [[train]] is used and returned to repeatedly, acts as a mobile base and staging area for discussions and planning in this chapter.
* The Nightmare is a named [[train]] previously considered as a return method to the Abyss, mentioned as an alternate vehicle that became unusable after interior gangways collapsed.
* The [[train]] is used by the team, arrives on the employee track during the battle, and is referenced as the vehicle for mission operations, including [[bomb]] placement.
* The Nightmare is a named [[train]]/conveyance used by the party, functions as both transport and battlefield set-piece, interacts with portals and tracks in the chapter.
* The [[train]] is used to detach cars and use the turntable, central vehicle for movement through the yard and stations.
* The Nightmare is a large locomotive/engine used as the core of the improvised rescue [[train]], described as idling in the employee line with a visible [[boiler]], and used as the platform many crawlers cling to while the convoy moves.
* The [[train]] is used to rendezvous with Carl's group and assist in moving forces across the trainyard.
* The Nightmare is the name of a [[train]]/vehicle used by the team, arrives on tracks in the trainyard, and is referenced as part of the mission to transport and detonate a [[bomb]].
* The [[train]] is used to send into the abyss to detonate and cause mass enemy deaths, central to the floor-ending explosion plan.
* The Nightmare is a named artifact/weapon that was tossed through a [[portal]] to blow soul crystals during the party's prior encounter, referenced as part of the achievements context.
