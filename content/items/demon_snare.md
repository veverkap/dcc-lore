# demon_snare

• A trap affixed to a spider [[automaton]] intended to attach to a demon and immobilize it.
• Used in the chapter but proved inadequate alone against the large [[Minge]].
• Attaches to a demon, not the intended target.
• Contributed to the Holy-water-based disruption.
