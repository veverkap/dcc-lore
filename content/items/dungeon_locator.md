# [[Dungeon Locator]]
Hidden mapping feature accessible via the [[Escape Plan]]/Escape Plan-like functionality; reveals concealed map legends at major dungeon intersections, pointing toward exits, city bosses, and other landmarks. Described as distinct from Pathfinder.
