# enchanted_necklace_of_the_haute_bourgeoisie

• [[Enchanted Necklace of the Haute Bourgeoisie]]: Artifact received from the Platinum Tyrant's Box, granting bonuses such as +5 Dexterity, Talon Strike (Level 5), and periodic tax income.
• Tracks settlements owned by the bearer via gems.
• Grants periodic tax income (example: +432 [[gold]] every 10 days from a medium settlement).
• Can be upgraded by conquering a Large-sized Settlement.
