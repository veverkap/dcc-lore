# [[Enchanted Necklace of the Haute Bourgeoisie]]
Reward from the Platinum Tyrant’s Box Carl opens. A silver chain charm with a jewel representing settlements controlled by the bearer. Grants benefits (e.g., +5 Dexterity from the attached Poor Sapphire gem, Talon Strike skill) and a recurring tax stipend for maintained settlements. Upgradable by conquering Large settlements.

# Enchanted Necklace of the Haute Bourgeoisie
A leadership artifact received from the Platinum Tyrant’s Box. Tracks settlements owned by the bearer via gems; grants periodic tax income (example: +432 gold every 10 days from a medium settlement) and bonuses (+5 Dexterity and the Talon Strike skill attached as a Level 5 gem). Can be upgraded by conquering a Large-sized Settlement.
