# hole_spell

* A spell learned and trained by [[Donut]] that creates a ~2-1 inch [[hole]] that can be toggled on/off and lasts minutes, used for Surprise maneuvers during the chapter.
* A utility spell practiced by [[Donut]] to create teleporting holes of varying diameter, used in conjunction with [[Clockwork Triplicate]] for tactical room entries.
* A spell learned by [[Princess Donut]] that creates a temporary [[hole]] on a chosen surface, leveling increases thickness and utility, useful for creative bypasses and infiltration.
* A spell used to create an opening between floors to drop effects into the ooze chamber, critical to the plan to funnel [[Burn Gel]] and fire onto the ooze core.
* A utility spell referenced as a reconnaissance tool, useful for scouting confined spaces like the bridge.
* A newly learned spell by [[Donut]] that currently only creates a small, temporary one-inch indentation in walls and does not affect mobs directly.
* A spell practiced by [[Donut]] that creates a temporary aperture, shown at level two to create small holes useful for Surprise tactics.
* A spell used to open portals or breaches, players attempted a [[Hole spell]] on a sealed stairwell chamber without success, indicating the chamber resists typical phasing or [[hole]] magic.
* A spell used by [[Donut]] to escape the collapsing building, classified as a spell/item in the party's arsenal.
