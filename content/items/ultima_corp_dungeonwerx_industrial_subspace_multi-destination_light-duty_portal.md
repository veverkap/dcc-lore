# ultima_corp_dungeonwerx_industrial_subspace_multi-destination_light-duty_portal

• A one-way, selectable [[light]]--duty [[portal]] model mounted on station walls for transporting carts to different lines.
• Compatible environments and defaults to the Orange Line if undialed.
• A one-way, selectable [[portal]] device used at station portals to dial colored lines; compatible with a [[DungeonWerx [[Portal]] Selector]] and defaults to the Orange Line if undialed.
• Appears on the station's [[portal]] racks.
• A branded one-way, selectable [[portal]] device used at the station with a list of 48 color-coded destinations; requires a DungeonWerx [[Portal]] Selector to dial and defaults to Orange Line if not selected.
• A one-way, selectable [[portal]] unit ([[light]]-duty) installed at station portals; requires a DungeonWerx [[Portal]] Selector to dial destinations and defaults to selection one (Orange Line) if not set.
• Visually compatible with station traffic and used by transit carts.
