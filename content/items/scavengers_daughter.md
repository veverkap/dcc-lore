# scavengers_daughter

• A deployable/system ability or item that cannot be activated when certain deities (e.g., [[the feral god [[meatus]] has entered the realm]] or [[Harpocrates]]) are "too distant"; referenced in system warnings.
• A patch/gear item that "opens her eyes" in the presence of a deity and can grant deity-derived effects; linked to distance limitations (cannot be activated if the deity is too distant).
• A back-mounted item storing accumulated soul energy; overaccumulation caused [[Soul Poisoning]] and forced [[dungeon crawler carl]] to disperse stored souls via a destructive action.
• A divine-associated patch in [[dungeon crawler carl]]'s possession referenced during the broadcast; discussed for its potential interaction with other divine items.
• A soul-power-related artifact whose power lingered within [[dungeon crawler carl]]; failing to diffuse it (via [[Daughter's Kiss]]) triggers an internal voice and possible future consequences.
• A divine, full-sized back upgrade patch affixable to the [[Enchanted Anarchist's Battle Rattle]]; grants doubled patch stat benefits, +25 Dexterity, +25 Strength, notification when deities are nearby, and three named benefits: [[Climb Benefit]], [[soul reaper]], and [[bone key]].
• A named soul-power item tied to Carl's inventory and the forgotten activation [[Daughter's Kiss]]; its lingering power was noted as a mistake by a voice in Carl's mind.
• A patch/item Carl possesses that caused the permanent summoning of [[Jacobus]] when Carl interacted with [[The Coin]].
• An artifact reportedly found by the [[War Mage Rebellion]] in [[Larracos]] that is part of their unclear objectives and motivations.
