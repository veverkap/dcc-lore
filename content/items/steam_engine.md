# steam_engine

• A giant steam engine left in the [[Goblin Workshop]] hummed and posed an ignition hazard.
• A massive engine in the [[Workshop]] had its [[Boiler]] ruptured in an explosion, emitting hissing steam and contributing to a destructive shockwave that collapsed the room.
