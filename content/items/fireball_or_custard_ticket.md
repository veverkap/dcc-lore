# fireball_or_custard_ticket

* [[Fireball or Custard ticket]]: Consumable/scratch-off-ike inventory item with limited uses, potentially triggering a powerful or unconventional effect.
* Randomly casts either a level-15 [[Fireball]] or a healing Strawberry Custard on the nearest red-tagged mob when scratched.
* Can be used to cast a damaging [[Fireball]] or a harmless custard effect.
* Has a 50/50 outcome on each reveal: either [[fireball]] (level-15 [[fireball]] attack) or custard (healing custodial effect).
* Used by Carl to [[heal]] Heather and eliminate worms, and to destroy parts of the Magistrate's bedroom and reduce Magistrate Featherfall to ashes.
