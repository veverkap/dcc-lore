# burn_gel

• A crafted flammable gel (recipe provided by [[Mordecai]]) that burns long, hot, and with little smoke.
• Used by [[dungeon crawler carl]] to ignite the core under the recliner and damage [[Mrs. Ghazi]]'s core.
• Crafted flammable paste (recipe credited to [[Mordecai]]) that burns hotter and longer with little smoke.
• Stored in a piping-funnel setup and used by [[dungeon crawler carl]] to ignite the recliner over the ooze core.
