A crafted flammable gel (recipe provided by [[Mordecai]]) that burns long, hot, and with little smoke. [[dungeon crawler carl]] uses a bag/funnel/dispenser of it to ignite the core under the recliner and damage [[Mrs. Ghazi]]'s core.
