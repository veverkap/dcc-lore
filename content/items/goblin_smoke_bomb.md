# goblin_smoke_bomb

• Consumable item used by [[dungeon crawler carl]] to obscure his retreat from aggressive mobs on the catwalks above [[The Abyss]].
• Tactical consumable used by [[dungeon crawler carl]] to obscure pursuit on the catwalks while retreating from hostile mobs near the [[Abyss]].
• Small tactical device rolled by [[dungeon crawler carl]] to create visual cover in hallways; produces smoke distinct from Louis’ cloud spell and used during the [[Town Hall]] defense.
• Small consumable used by Carl to obscure pursuit while leaving the catwalk area above the [[Abyss]] and return to the stairwell.
• Consumable stealth item Carl uses in the [[Abyss]] catwalks to obscure pursuit and cover an exit back to the stairwell.
• Low-quality smoke devices sold by goblin makers; criticized by [[Pustule]] for producing limited, short-ived smoke in a small cone—useful only in enclosed spaces.
• Small smoke device Carl uses to obscure vision in the [[Town Hall]] hallway; creates dense, diesel-smelling smoke that impedes sight.
