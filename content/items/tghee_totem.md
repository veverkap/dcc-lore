# T'Ghee Totem
A reusable playing-card–style item created when a squad plants a [[Squad Flag]] into a living entity at <5% health (or flags a noncorporeal monster). Totems can be added to inventory, traded, and later used to summon or represent monsters.

# T’Ghee totem
Card-like summonable item (a reusable playing-card/”totem”) created when a squad plants a flag into a living entity at <5% health. Summoned creatures become squad minions; totems can be traded, stolen, ripped to dismiss, and only one kept at collapse per surviving squad. Notable in the [[Eighth Floor System Announcement]] rules.
