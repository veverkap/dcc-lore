# tghee_totem

• A reusable playing-card–style item created when a squad plants a [[Squad Flag]] into a living entity at <5% health (or flags a noncorporeal monster).
• Totems can be added to inventory, traded, and later used to summon or represent monsters.
• Summoned creatures become squad minions; totems can be traded, stolen, ripped to dismiss, and only one kept at collapse per surviving squad.
