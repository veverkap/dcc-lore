# utter_annihilation_wand

• A powerful destructive [[wand]] that has no effect on a stairwell during an observed attack, supporting the claim that stairwells are indestructible.
• A destructive [[wand]] used against a [[Stairwell]] without damaging it, demonstrating the [[wand]]'s power contrasted with the apparent indestructibility/ protection of stairwells.
