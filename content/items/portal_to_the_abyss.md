# portal_to_the_abyss

• The interdiction [[cart]]'s activated [[portal]] faces downward and funnels thousands of Krakaren monsters into the [[Abyss]], central to [[dungeon crawler carl]]'s improvised [[containment]] plan.
• The [[portal]] is roughly the size of the marble circle in [[Station 36]] and crackles as Krakaren are sucked through.
• The downward-facing [[portal]] is activated from the interdiction [[cart]] (tuned to the [[Abyss]]) and sucks Krakaren monsters into the void.
• The [[portal]] is central to [[dungeon crawler carl]]'s plan to divert the wave of creatures away from [[Station 36]].
• The activated interdiction-[[cart]] [[portal]], tuned to the [[Abyss]], hangs upside-down over the marble circle in [[Station 36]] to funnel Krakaren into the void.
