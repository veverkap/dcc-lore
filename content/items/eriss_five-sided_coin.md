# Eris's five-sided coin
A magical five-sided coin used by [[Eris]] to determine chaotic outcomes for [[Carl]]. Displays shifting, gruesome imagery and captions (e.g., "RIP," severed feet, piles of dead bodies) and dissolves, leaving a swirling tattoo on [[Carl]]'s palm when the fifth side is chosen.
