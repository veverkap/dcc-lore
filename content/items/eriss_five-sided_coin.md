# eriss_five-sided_coin

• A magical five- sided coin used by [[Eris]] to determine chaotic outcomes for [[Carl]].
• Displays shifting, gruesome imagery and captions (e.g., "RIP," severed feet, piles of dead bodies).
• Dissolves, leaving a swirling [[tattoo]] on [[Carl]]'s palm when the fifth side is chosen.
