# [[Lazarus's bazooka]]
The magical, cartoonish weapon wielded by [[Lazarus-A-Bang-Bang]] that fires varied, often hazardous effects (e.g., beetle swarms, RPG-like explosions). It is referenced in the totem's attack list and has a visible cooldown timer in combat.
