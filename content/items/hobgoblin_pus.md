# hobgoblin_pus

• A C4-like magical explosive found in the [[Goblin Workshop]], used in [[Barrels with XXX]] to maximize destruction.
• Incorporated into [[Baby Uno]] as part of its triggering or explosive matrix.
• Used as remote-controlled explosive detonators, acquired from boss loot such as [[Heather]]'s bronze box.
• Allows up to ten simultaneous detonations per stick, mentioned in the context of the demolition plan.
• A magical detonator used in the warehouse assassination attempt, considered a trigger-item that played a [[key]] role in the event.
• Ingredient grafted onto chum-[[bomb]] components ([[hob-lobber]] carcasses with pus attached) used to increase gore and attract predators.
• Exotic, expensive detonator material bought from [[Pustule]] and used by Carl to alter [[hob-lobber]] timing.
• Used in the warehouse event, central to the failed-assassination sequence and subsequent Syndicate appeals.
• Rare crafting/detonator material sold by [[Pustule]] and used by Carl to replace the fuse in a [[Hob-lobber]] and to trigger wide-area effects in the water.
