# nickels

• Coins scattered throughout the bar after the [[sock]] incident; their movement and recovery drive the immediate cleanup actions by NPCs and crawlers.
• The coins collected by [[Dong Quixote]]; many were scattered and picked up by NPCs, and their retrieval is required to cure the [[Separation Anxiety]] curse.
