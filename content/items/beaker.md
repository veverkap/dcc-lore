# beaker

• A small vessel looted earlier and used in the sequence; filled by the [[totem]] with a bubbling black liquid interpreted as [[Ink]] for tattooing.
• An empty beaker used to hold a sliver of [[Toraline Root]] and later filled with bubbling black [[Black Ink]] after heating/interaction.
• Instrument used by [[Shi Maria]] to prepare [[tattoo]]/[[ink]].
