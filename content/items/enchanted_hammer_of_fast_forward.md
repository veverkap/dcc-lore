# Enchanted Hammer of Fast Forward
A small enchanted hammer given to [[Imani]] (intended ultimately for [[Donut]]). Described as enchanted but seemingly inert; Mordecai notes such enchantments can be useful against incorporeal foes like ghosts. Bears the hidden symbol "7" on its handle.
