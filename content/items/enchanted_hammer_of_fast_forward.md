# enchanted_hammer_of_fast_forward

• A seemingly mundane enchanted hammer given in a silver benefactor box, intended for [[Donut]] but received by [[Imani]].
• The enchantment has no obvious magical function, but may be useful against incorporeal threats.
• The hammer bears the hidden symbol "7" on its handle.
