# quans_cloak

• Celestial [[cloak]] looted from [[Quan]]; credited as a Celestial item that counts toward the "Celestial Loot" achievement.
• Grants special effects and is a shoulder accessory that must be planned for on first wear (discussed with [[Mordecai]] and [[Donut]]).
• Description appears to change based on the wearer.
• Part of the post-battle loot to be sorted.
• Part of the unclaimed rewards at the chapter's end.
• Requires inspection by [[Mordecai]].
• Considered for Donut to wear; reshaping/resizing considerations discussed.
