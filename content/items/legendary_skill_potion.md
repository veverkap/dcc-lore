# legendary_skill_potion

• A high-tier potion gifted onstage by the [[Maestro]] that upgrades [[Find Crawler]] to level 15 (or higher, depending on class), allowing precise [[targeting]] of crawlers across great distances.
• Extremely rare potion worth many millions of [[gold]] and extremely uncommon; can raise or grant legendary skills, gifted to [[Maggie]] by the [[Maestro]].
