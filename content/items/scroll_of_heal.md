# scroll_of_heal

* Consumable scroll used by [[dungeon crawler carl]] to restore [[Anton]], [[Paz]], [[Donut]], [[Katia]], and [[Firas]]' health.
* Can be used repeatedly without countdown between uses.
* Instantly heals and can be acquired from survivor boxes, boss boxes, or loot.
* Used by Carl and others to restore health in emergency situations.
