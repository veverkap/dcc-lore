# kangaroo_suit

• A corpse/suit worn by Carl that grants temporary classes and buffs, causes sensory integration via the [[Fursona skill]], contains a pouch, and prevents certain actions.
• Provides temporary class/skill interactions and causes increasing embodiment sensations via the [[Fursona skill]].
• Affects physical actions (e.g., hopping) and limits normal behaviors (e.g., hair brushing).
• Contains a pouch, causing the [[Slip-’n’-slide]] achievement.
• Prevents certain actions (e.g., resetting foot [[buff]]).
• Central to several achievements.
• Impedes access to pedals and requires removal via the [[Laundry Day]] spell.
• Central to the driving arrangement placing [[Gluteus Maxx]] behind the wheel.
