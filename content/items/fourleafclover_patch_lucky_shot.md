# [[Four‑Leaf‑Clover Patch (Lucky Shot)]]
A small patch Carl sews onto his jacket above the [[Midnight Epicure patch]]; its artwork shows a foot crushing four‑leaf clovers. Imbues the wearer with the "Lucky Shot" benefit: a fatal blow can be deflected into the body of a party member once per floor.
