# sticky_hob-lobber

• A thrown explosive weapon used by [[dungeon crawler carl]] to instantly kill mobs, appearing as a consumable/attack item drawn into an [[xistera]].
• Sticks to targets and deals shrapnel/explosive damage, used repeatedly against hydra heads and totems.
• Used by [[dungeon crawler carl]] to damage and disorient [[Quan]] during the fight.
• Used by [[dungeon crawler carl]] to instantly kill a level-45 mob early in the chapter, appearing as a deck/consumable attack option.
• Used by [[dungeon crawler carl]] (sticky) to damage hydra heads and totems, with various types existing (shrapnel, shredder, etc.).
• Used by [[dungeon crawler carl]] to damage and disorient [[Quan]] (detonated against the back of his head).
