# Unknown Manufacturer Enhancement Zone Subspace Portal
Portal type reported by Carl's neural implant when the [[Wheel of Fortune]] Nothing slice is engaged: a one-way, open-access portal that the implant labels as leading to a "stasis chamber." Noted to have a roughly fifteen-second duration when produced by the wheel.
