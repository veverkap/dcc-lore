# Parade Train
The improvised multi-car transport built by [[Imani]] and [[Chris]] using wheelchair wheels, wood planks, ramps, and the [[Yog's Special Chain]] to shuttle dozens of elderly residents down hallways. It is about 300 feet long, modular (cars locked two-by-two), and too wide to fit through some doors, dictating staging decisions.
