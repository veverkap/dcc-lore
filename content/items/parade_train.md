# parade_train

• The Parade [[Train]] is an improvised multi-car transport built by Imani and Chris using wheelchair wheels, wood planks, ramps, and the Yog's Special Chain.
• It is approximately 300 feet long, modular (cars locked two-by-two), and too wide to fit through some doors, requiring staging decisions.
