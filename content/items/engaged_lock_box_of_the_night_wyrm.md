# Engaged Lock Box of the Night Wyrm
An attuned artifact owned by [[Katia Grim]] (attuned to Crawler #9,077,265). Two of three seals are broken; the final seal requires finding the [[Guild of Suffering]] and completing an assassination quest. The box contains the [[Orchid of Eileithyia's Grace]] and will destroy the prize if the owner perishes before completion.
