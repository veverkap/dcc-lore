# dorota

• The name given by [[Donut]] to her Smart Companion Royal Pet Throne, a commercial product that talks, monitors vitals, dispenses snacks, provides comfort, and manifests a padded bed for the pet.
• Interacts verbally and actively supports [[Donut]] during the race, annoying [[Dr. Metcalf]].
• The personalized name/voice instance of the [[Smart Companion Royal Pet Throne]] that talks to and pampers [[Donut]].
