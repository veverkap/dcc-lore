# Dorota
The name given by [[Donut]] to her Smart Companion Royal Pet Throne. A commercial "Smart Companion Royal Pet Throne — Ultimate Diamond Pampered Pet Princess edition" that talks, monitors vitals, dispenses snacks, provides comfort, and manifests a padded bed for the pet. Interacts verbally (annoying [[Dr. Metcalf]]) and actively supports [[Donut]] during the race.
