# pawnas_tears_potion

• [[Pawna’'s Tears potion]] is a powerful consumable used to temporarily raise skill levels, used by [[dungeon crawler carl]] and [[Donut]] to boost [[Laundry Day]].
• It is a finite resource provided by a sponsor and used at a critical moment.
• [[Pawna’'s Tears]] is a stronger potion that grants +5 to any spell or skill and has no time restriction, reserved for later use.
• It is a legendary potion from a [[Legendary Deity’'s Box]] that grants +5 to any chosen spell or skill.
• It can be saved and applied to any skill at a strategic moment, making it highly valued.
