# barrel_bombs

• Improvised explosive containers packed with [[gunpowder]], hobgoblin [[dynamite]], and delay charges, used by Carl to cause massive destruction.
• Explosive devices used by Carl to damage the [[Small Bugbear Settlement]] and clear blocks, with variable strength.
• Used by [[dungeon crawler carl]] to destroy parts of the town.
