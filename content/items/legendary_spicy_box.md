# legendary_spicy_box

• Legendary Spicy Box: a legendary-tier reward given by the "Unbirthing!" achievement, one of the more ominous/rare boxes Carl received.
• Produces a simple, silver, unadorned toe [[ring]] (later identified as the [[Enchanted Toe [[Ring]] of the Well-Balanced]]).
• Linked to the traumatic [[teleport]]/neck-[[hole]] incidents involving [[Samantha]].
• Awarded by the [[Unbirthing]] achievement.
