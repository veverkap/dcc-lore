# Denial of Service missiles
Missile-type munitions fired from the [[Eight-pack launcher]] that trigger a thick mucous/slime membrane on impact, used to blind and slow shielded teams like [[Team Sparkles]].

# Denial of Service missiles
Non-lethal ordnance fired from an [[Eight-pack launcher]] that creates a thick mucous membrane (green slime) on impact to blind and encase shielded vehicles rather than directly destroy them.
