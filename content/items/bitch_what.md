# Bitch, What?
A berserking spell bound to the [[Enchanted Nipple Ring of the Superior Fire Demon’s Hand Maiden]]. Grants brief invulnerability to blunt damage, induces a raging berserk state with massive area destruction for ~20 seconds and has a long cooldown.
