# stuffed_grulke_infantry_figure

• A collectible stuffed figure manufactured by [[Jaxbrin Amusements, Limited]].
• The figure has a paper tag reading "Grulke Infantry. Jaxbrin Amusements, Ltd." that, when removed, allows the figure to function as a true summoning, temporarily teleporting a real creature to fight for the user.
• The figure can also be used to summon a [[Grulke creature]] into the alley as a diversion.
