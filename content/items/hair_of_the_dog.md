# Hair of the Dog
An anti-alcohol potion provided on-the-house by [[Quint]] that sobers [[Donut]] immediately after she drinks; part of the trade during the [[Starter Kit]] purchase.

# Hair of the Dog
A potion recipe taught to [[dungeon crawler carl]] by [[Mordecai]] (involves a gooey okra-scented weed and rubbing alcohol). Crafting the potion grants [[dungeon crawler carl]] an Alchemy level increase and is traded with [[Fitz]] for lodging.

# Hair of the Dog
An alchemical potion recipe that [[Mordecai]] teaches [[dungeon crawler carl]]. Created from a gooey weed and rubbing alcohol; successfully brewed grants [[dungeon crawler carl]] an alchemy level and is used as a tradable good (Fitz trades room and board for doses).
