# hair_of_the_dog

• An anti-alcohol potion provided by [[Quint]] that sobers [[Donut]] immediately after she drinks; part of the trade during the [[Starter Kit]] purchase.
• A potion recipe taught to [[dungeon crawler carl]] by [[Mordecai]] involving a gooey okra-scented weed and rubbing alcohol; crafting the potion grants [[dungeon crawler carl]] an Alchemy level increase and is traded with [[Fitz]] for lodging.
