# dirt_clod

• A ground-attack spell used by [[Zhang]] to pummel immobilized mobs killed by [[Li Na]]'s [[chains]]; a named spell in the party's arsenal.
• An offensive spell cast by [[Zhang]] that pummels enemies with rocks; used in coordinated combat during the tunnel engagements.
• Used in the chapter to help dispatch Krakaren tentacles and other mobs.
