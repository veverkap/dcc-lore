# ping_spell

• Utility spell used by [[dungeon crawler carl]] to scan the lacuna for NPCs, hidden gods, or small mobs.
• Low-level spell (level 2 in this chapter) used to combine with [[Fear (Spell)]] for item enchanting.
• Reconnaissance-style spell used to help man towers and detect enemy scouts during peacetime incursions, referenced by [[Florin]].
