# gate

• Artifact assembled from three pieces, allowing bubble-world transit operations; handled by [[dungeon crawler carl]] and central to multi-[[phase]] rescue plans.
• [[Teleport]] [[portal]] constructed from gate pieces, opening between locations; time-limited and seized by [[war mages]].
• Portable [[portal]] device assembled from three pieces, used to rescue crawlers from bubbles and open pathways between worlds; pieces handed to [[dungeon crawler carl]] by [[elle mcgib]] during operations.
