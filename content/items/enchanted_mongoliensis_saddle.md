# Enchanted Mongoliensis Saddle
A fan-box reward that magically affixes to a [[Mongoliensis]] like [[Mongo]], converting it into a mount. Grants rider bonuses (+15% offensive spell damage, anti-piercing resistance) and gives the mount +20% Constitution and +20% melee damage while occupied.
