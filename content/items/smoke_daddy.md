# smoke_daddy

• A custom-made smoke [[bomb]] deployed by [[dungeon crawler carl]] to blanket the battlefield and obscure vision, used in coordination with hunter smoke tools and [[Donut]]'s [[Fear]] smoke.
• Deployed to blanket the battlefield in thick smoke and obscure vision during the descent into the pit.
