# dart_of_ophiotaurus

• The Dart of Ophiotaurus is a rare artifact used to forcibly bind a god's summoning to a chosen vessel.
• It was used by [[Chaco]] to strike [[Uzzi]] and summon [[Dodola]].
• Unlike a [[Holy Grenade]], the Dart of Ophiotaurus requires a chosen living vessel.
• It functions similarly to a holy [[grenade]] but requires selecting a living target.
