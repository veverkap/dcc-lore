# Dart of Ophiotaurus
A priceless artifact recovered earlier on the floor. In this chapter it is used by [[Chaco]] to strike [[Uzzi]], forcing the summoned deity [[Dodola]] to manifest in that vessel. The dart behaves like a targeted holy summoning consumable and differs from a [[Holy Grenade]] by requiring a chosen vessel.
