# sunglasses

• Sunglasses: an inventory accessory used by Donut to detect or mask reactions to changelings, and can be employed by the party as part of a strategy.
• Donut's sunglasses: a gear that stores a full-powered Magic Missile shot for a double-shot burst, and an example of personal, stored-offense equipment.
• Benefactor-upgraded eyewear that grants precise surface and subsurface temperature visualization and other sensor overlays, useful for spotting traps, changelings, and weaknesses.
