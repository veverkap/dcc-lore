# greased_lightning

• [[Greased Lightning]] is a speed-increasing utility card used by [[Donut]] to accelerate [[Shi Maria]] and enable split/illusion or rapid attacks.
• It can be used to [[buff]] or speed allies, such as [[Raul the crab]] or [[Skylar Spinach]], during combat.
• The card can increase a mob's speed by 25%, making it potentially game-changing when applied to certain totems.
• It is a tactical consumable in deck encounters, representing a utility card that can be thrown to temporarily speed-enhance and [[buff]] allies.
