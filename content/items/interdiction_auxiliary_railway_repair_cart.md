# interdiction_auxiliary_railway_repair_cart

• A common, flatbed-powered auxiliary [[cart]] used by interdiction teams for backup personnel and supplies, [[battery]]- or third-rail-powered, and contains a dwarven-style [[battery]] compartment.
• Does not require a gnoll operator and is intended as a backup vehicle.
• Has a [[portal]] track selector and is not a primary response unit.
• Used for secondary support and supplies, not meant for primary response.
