# Florin's Shotgun
A heavily modified automatic shotgun wielded by [[Florin]], later granted a magical unlimited-ammo upgrade. Used decisively during the ghoul-station incident that resulted in Ifechi's death and a golden skull bounty appearing over [[Florin]].
