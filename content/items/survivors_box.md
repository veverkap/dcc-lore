# survivors_box

• A loot container that granted items, including [[scroll of heal]]s and materials used in crafting the potion to [[heal]] [[Mongo]].
• A reward box given to [[Princess Donut]] for ending a boss battle with less than 5% health, containing a dozen [[scroll of heal]]s.
• A loot box awarded for surviving boss fights with low health, with Carl receiving one for having less than 10% health when the boss died and getting additional [[scroll of heal]]s from it.
