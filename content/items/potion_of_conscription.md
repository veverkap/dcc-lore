# potion_of_conscription

• A potion used by war mages to forcibly [[conscript]] gods, which can cause the caster to explode if level disparities are lethal.
• A rare potion that acts like a [[Conscription]] potion, affecting most non-affiliated entities and potentially many deities at level 18.
• Brewed by Krakaren Prime and duplicated/used by Li Na and dungeon crawler Carl against Lucia/Harpocrates.
• Considered briefly for recruiting Prepotente into the army, affecting Carl's mindset in the scene.
• Used by Li Na and Carl to [[conscript]] gods, including Yarilo.
