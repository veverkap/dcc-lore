# bone_key

* A specific [[key]]-liked benefit referenced by [[dungeon crawler carl]] as a last-resort option, with costs including removal of a bone, loss of a jacket and its benefits.
* A magical [[key]] with a named "bone [[key]] benefit" that can open doors, but using it has costs and is treated as a risky last-resort option by [[dungeon crawler carl]].
* A [[key]] type required to exit via stairwell benefits, with possession or loss of a [[key]] equivalent to survival or death in the chapter's context.
* A one-time-use benefit from the [[Scavenger’s Daughter Patch]] that creates a [[key]] able to open any dungeon lock, but destroys the patch and its host garment and consumes a real bone from the user.
* A special [[key]]-liked benefit tied to Carl's bones that can open certain doors, with use carrying trade-offs and being a controversial option among allies.
* A [[key]]-type item mentioned as the primary way to exit when stairwells/doors are inaccessible, with obtaining and losing a [[Bone Key]] equivalent to death in terms of escape from the floor.
* A one-time-use [[key]] benefit granted by the [[Scavenger'’s Daughter Patch]] that can open any dungeon lock, but will destroy the patch and the garment it's affixed to and consumes a bone from the user's body.
* A singular-use item referenced as having the ability to allow one person to pass an escape point, with failing to use it forcing an impossible choice.
