# battery_fabricator

• A machine sold by [[Limp Richard]] that converts mana [[potions]] and metal blocks into charged batteries, priced at 75,000 [[gold]].
• Described as taken from a dwarven [[automaton]] factory and priced expensively.
• Consumes mana [[potions]] and metal blocks to produce charged [[Battery]] units.
• Converts mana [[potions]] and metal blocks into charged [[Automaton Battery]]s.
• Expensive and mysterious.
• Listed price: 75,000 [[gold]].
