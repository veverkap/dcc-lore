# turtle

• Defensive ability used by [[Paz]] that grants a powerful temporary [[shield]], roots the caster in place, draws aggro, and has a fixed duration that cannot be prematurely canceled unless the [[shield]] is broken.
• Creates a powerful temporary [[shield]] and roots the caster in place, functionally a spell/skill with tradeoffs (high protection but immobility until the timer expires or the [[shield]] is broken).
