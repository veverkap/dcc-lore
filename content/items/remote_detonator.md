# remote_detonator

• Allows magical remote detonation of [[explosives]] designated by the user.
• Functions with a Sapper's table.
• Grants the wearer the ability to remotely detonate [[explosives]] they have designated.
• Supports multiple physical and [[interface]] triggers, especially when used with a Sapper's table.
• Used to designate and detonate thrown [[explosives]] like the [[Hob-Lobber]].
• Used to mark [[explosives]] and rockets for timed or remote activation.
• Employed to detonate the [[multi-launcher]] salvo and several barrel [[bombs]] in Zockau.
• Used in tandem with Hobgoblin [[dynamite]] to initiate controlled explosions.
• Has a ten-second delay and a ten-kilometer range.
• Can be used to attempt controlled timing against an imminent explosion.
