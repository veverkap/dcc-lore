# laundry_day

Here is the consolidated markdown list:

* A spell that removes armor from targets, trained and leveled by [[Donut]].
* Can remove biological or alien "armor" from occupied divine vessels.
* Can remove Soul Armor in divine/alien occupation.
* Removes armor from an opponent, dropping it to the ground; scales with level to strip more opponents.
* Can strip protections from mortals and remove soul-armor from humans.
* Used to help kill [[Diwata]] during the Butcher's Masquerade.
* Enables tactics like stripping armor from mobs and reanimating it as wailing spirit symbiotes.
* Can remove armor pieces from [[Bomo]], creating Scrapheap bits used in the Legionnaires of the Damned exploit.
* Can remove the kangaroo suit from [[dungeon crawler carl]], but carries significant risk.
* Can remove biological armor from gods or alien drivers, mentioned as a theoretical method for stripping deities.
* A bard spell that strips armor from enemies, scales with spell level, and was recommended for [[Donut]] to [[train]].
* Can remove the soul-armor layer from beings, allowing the underlying person to be targeted.
* Used to violently eject armor pieces from [[Bomo]] and create soapy side-effects, enabling reanimation via Legionnaires of the Damned.
