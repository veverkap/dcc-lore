# [[Ghillie Suit]]
A cat-sized camouflage suit looted/received by Donut. Equips in the jacket slot but magically covers the whole body; grants the skill "Blend into the Background" and a "Cone of Silence" benefit. Synergizes with the boonie hat for extreme long-range sniping.
