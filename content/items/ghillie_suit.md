# ghillie_suit

• Accessory/equipment awarded with certain class choices, noted by [[Donut]] as part of her new class loadout.
• Cat-sized camouflage suit looted/received by Donut, equipping in the jacket slot and granting "Blend into the Background" and "Cone of Silence" benefits.
• Synergizes with the boonie [[hat]] for extreme long-range sniping.
