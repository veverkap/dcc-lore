# key

* Quest item required during [[Phase Two]] to open stairwell adjacent to a squad's starting area.
* Must be retrieved from a key location within 48 hours; only the keyholder's squad may pass through the opened stairwell in [[Phase Three]].
* Required item to access stairwells and progress to higher floors (e.g., the [[ninth floor]]).
* [[Keys]] are non-droppable and cannot be voluntarily transferred; mechanics around [[keys]] are the focus of the system announcement.
* Generic floor/restricted-door [[keys]] carried by crawlers; [[keys]] provide exit options.
* Physical key worn on a chain by [[Tipid]] that functioned as the dungeon exit trigger.
* Single-use key that opens the stairwell location adjacent to a squad's original starting area; only the keyholder's squad may pass through the opened stairwell.
* Exit key required to progress; originally marked in [[Iowa]] but relocated to a mound of earth west of the squad in [[Monroe County, Florida]].
* Quest item the pair need to obtain by winning the final fight; failure to retrieve submerged or buried corpses with [[tokens]] could mean failing to get the key.
* Item required to access level stairwells and proceed to floors (notably the [[Ninth Floor]]); [[keys]] cannot be voluntarily dropped or transferred.
* Generic dungeon key items referenced repeatedly: [[dungeon crawler carl]] holds a key for the door, [[keys]] can be used to exit via staircases, and [[keys]] are assets that players can use to return if things go badly.
* Single-use item that will allow one crawler to pass through the [[saferoom]] exit; central to the squad's escape planning and the reason they intend to detonate and channel beneath the church.
* Important quest item the narrator needs in order to retrieve [[Penelope]]; winning the heat is tied to obtaining this [[Key]].
