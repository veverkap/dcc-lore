# Key
Quest item required during [[Phase Two]] to open the stairwell adjacent to a squad’s starting area. Must be retrieved from a key location within 48 hours; only the keyholder’s squad may pass through the opened stairwell in [[Phase Three]]. Multiple rules govern transfer and theft of keys.
