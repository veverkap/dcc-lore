# satchel_bombs

• Explosive consumable loot from an [[Odious Creeper]] boss box, described as small leather-handled bags with explosive power equivalent to multiple sticks of hobgoblin [[dynamite]].
• Demolition [[explosives]] produced and distributed by [[Mordecai]] (via [[Dungeon Crawler Carl]]'s instruction) to help players clear debris and isolate stairwell chambers.
