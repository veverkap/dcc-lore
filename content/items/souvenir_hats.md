# souvenir_hats

• Collectible items lost when the [[portal]] closed after the failed [[paralysis]] of the [[Kravyad]]; many crawlers sold or lost theirs, compounding the crisis described by [[daniel bautista]].
• Items that allow passage through portals above the abyss; crucial for certain crawlers' ability to reach stairwells and avoid being stranded.
• Collectible items taken or lost during an operation against the [[Kravyad]]; [[daniel bautista]] reports that over 150 crawlers no longer have their [[hats]] after the [[portal]] closed.
