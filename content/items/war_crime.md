# war_crime

* A spellbook retrieved by [[Donut]] from a loot box, described as bleeding and crying, making [[Donut]] uncertain about reading it.
* An [[Atrocity]]-class spell in [[Donut]]'s arsenal, obtained from a [[Legendary 1914 box]], described as massively more destructive than other spells and morally fraught due to its potential for [[atrocity]]-level damage.
* A unique [[Atrocity]] spell owned by [[Donut]] that allows combining two (or more at higher levels) spells into a creative, often horrific effect.
* The high-risk offensive spell [[Donut]] intends to cast to finish off Reaver and Dream forces during the Larracos operation, its casting will reduce the caster to 1% health temporarily.
* A named spell used by [[Donut]] earlier in events, it affected who was captured during the chaos and is referenced as part of why some harem members were not treated as enemies.
* A spell used by [[Donut]] that generated celestial-tier reward boxes, described as a source of the celestial slap-chop and celestial godslayer boxes.
* A named spell used or referenced by [[Princess Donut]], discussed in the tribunal when judges ask what three spells she might combine if she cast the [[War Crime]] spell again.
* A high-impact spell referenced by [[Luca Di.]] when addressing the toll it took on [[Princess Donut]], presented as an offensive magic effect in the lore.
* A spell used by [[Donut]] that produced celestial boxes in this chapter, the spell itself is responsible for spawning multiple celestial reward containers when triggered.
* A named spell used by [[Princess Donut]], discussed in the judge interrogation as a combinable spell choice and restricted until at least the [[Twelfth Floor]].
* A powerful spell referenced in Luca’s note that had significant toll on its casters, mentioned as a grievous ability used earlier in the campaign.
* A powerful named spell previously cast by [[Donut]] (larger than [[Astral Paw]]), noted as having a long cooldown and tiered casting restrictions tied to floor progression.
