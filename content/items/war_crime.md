# War Crime
A unique Atrocity spell owned by [[Donut]] that allows combining two (or more at higher levels) spells into a creative, often horrific effect. Key properties: starts at level 5, increases by 5 per cast, requires chosen spells to be at least level 8, knocks caster's health to 1% on use, and cannot directly kill targets (they become undying and suffer). In the chapter, [[War Crime]] is the central mechanic enabling the plan to mix [[Bijanbi]] and [[Wall of Fire]].
