# sheol_fire

• [[Sheol Fire]]: a dangerous magical effect tied to [[Sheol Glass Reaper Case]] usage, with a 1.5% chance to be blasted with it each time the case is opened.
• [[Sheol Fire]]: a potent, named fiery effect that ignites outside-building footsteps and bathes areas in damaging fire, interacting with the [[Martyr’s Path]] benefit and posing lethal danger.
• [[Sheol Fire]]: a destructive fire type invoked by the [[Self-Immolation]] spell, described as violently transmutative and highly dangerous to flesh.
• [[Sheol fire]]: a dangerous infernal spell/element referenced in a note by [[Milk]], warning that using it against demons makes them exponentially stronger.
• [[Sheol fire]]: a named, infernal fire effect that enhances and damages, inflicting severe heat and [[burn]] damage, and proximity is lethal.
