# torch_spell

* [[Torch (spell)]] is a [[light]] spell cast by [[Donut]] that illuminates pitch-black corridors and eliminates the need for carried torches.
* It can be toggled on/off and was used tactically during the raid.
* It is advised not to use it during the boarding attempt to avoid warning the [[train]]'s engineer.
* It can be used to counter the oppressive darkness of the [[Lacuna]] during rescue movements.
* It produces intense, prism-like blinding [[light]] in the sandglass corridors and has limited dimming capability.
* It is an illusionary evocation spell that [[Princess Donut]] may cast while on stage under the masquerade's restrictions.
* It is a powerful spell that can be amplified using moon rays as a component to amplify a ritual.
* It can be used to illuminate large areas and reveal distant landmarks like the earth mound holding the [[Key]].
* It is a simple [[light]]-providing spell used aboard the racers, similar to [[Donut]]'s [[torch]], to examine distant mounts and terrain.
* It can be used to illuminate flooded corridors inside the [[Akula]].
* It can be used to repel krasue and keep the curse at bay for partially corrupted individuals like [[Burgundy]].
