# combo

• A utility card in the [[cards]] that discards during arena play to avoid unwanted effects; discarding triggers a 30-second discard rule and a 10-second card-replenish timer in combat.
• A consumable card intended to trigger combined effects, present in the deck but discarded in the practice run.
• A consumable tactical card in the deck that can be discarded to manipulate hand composition; the party elects to discard it on easy fights to avoid using it in the mock arena.
• A consumable card kept in the practice deck to mirror real match conditions; discarded during the simulation by [[Donut]].
