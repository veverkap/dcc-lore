# celestial_grenade

• Rare explosive artifact developed by the nuns of [[Enyo]] that summons a deity for sixty seconds.
• Used by [[Maggie My]] and would have summoned [[Algos]] based on her worship choice.
• Sponsor prize used by [[dungeon crawler carl]] that summons a random god.
• When used by a worshiper, it grants a 60-second [[Divine Intervention]] aura to the wielder.
• When thrown by [[dungeon crawler carl]] while worshiping [[Emberus]], it involuntarily summoned the god and granted a system [[buff]].
• An expensive, rare orb-liked artifact that summons a god for sixty seconds when activated.
