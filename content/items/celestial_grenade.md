# Celestial Grenade
Rare explosive artifact developed by the nuns of [[Enyo]] that summons a deity for sixty seconds; in this chapter it was in [[Maggie My]]'s hands and would have summoned [[Algos]] based on her worship choice.

# Celestial Grenade
An expensive, rare orb-like artifact that summons a god for sixty seconds when activated. If used by a worshiper, it also grants a 60-second [[Divine Intervention]] aura to the wielder. Central to [[Maggie My]]'s assassination attempt in the chapter.
