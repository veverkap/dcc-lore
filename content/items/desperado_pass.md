# desperado_pass

• Grants access to the [[desperado club]].
• Prevents holding a [[Vanquisher pass]] simultaneously.
• Affects class options during selection for Carl and Donut.
• Enables Katia to secure perks like regular booths and guest arrangements at the [[desperado club]].
• Grants privileges at the [[desperado club]], enabling extra services like hiring additional bodyguards.
