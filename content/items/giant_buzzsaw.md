# giant_buzzsaw

• A heavy weapon powered by a dwarfish [[battery]], found in wreckage, and scavenged from wreckage that was powered by a [[Dwarfish Battery]] and could operate attached to or detached from a chicken hawk.
• Carried into inventory by [[Katia]].
• Used as part of the "Leatherface" plan, embedding in the seabed after cleaving through [[Lusca]] during the escape.
