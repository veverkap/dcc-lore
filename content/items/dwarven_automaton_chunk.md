# dwarven_automaton_chunk

• A heavy piece of broken dwarven metal used by [[dungeon crawler carl]] as an improvised anchor for his rope system when descending into the ooze chamber.
• Later becomes structural debris during the collapse and damages [[dungeon crawler carl]] during the tumble.
