# arrow_of_enthusiastic_double_gonorrhea

• A poisoned arrow recovered from [[Hunter Arwick]]'s inventory, enchanted to afflict a sexually transmitted disease (described in-world as "Enthusiastic Double Gonorrhea") that is painful and difficult to remove.
• Named ammunition item found on the dead elf, intended for [[Louis]], unusual for its name and implied effect.
• Named arrow weapon used by [[dungeon crawler carl]], tip coated with a debilitating disease called Enthusiastic Double Gonorrhea, which strikes [[Vrah]] in the neck and infects her.
• Magical hunter arrow looted from [[Hunter Arwick]], regular arrow tip dipped in a virulent [[poison]] that inflicts the disease "Enthusiastic Double Gonorrhea", described as non-lethal but horrific.
• Single arrow item found in Akland's inventory, apparently intended for [[Louis]], indicative of the vicious or humorous weapon naming conventions in the dungeon.
• Named poisonous arrow used by [[dungeon crawler carl]] to afflict [[Vrah]] with a grotesque, nonlethal disease, described in-game and applied directly to a mantis's neck during the fight.
