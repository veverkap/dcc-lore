# mind_balance

• A passive/active skill sourced from [[Toe Ring]] that prevents or suppresses outside mental control.
• Grants immunity to certain domination effects, protecting from mental manipulation.
• Governs resistance to psionic effects, unlocking new perception and resistances at Level 16.
• Has been leveling up rapidly for [[dungeon crawler carl]], repeatedly blocking attempts by [[Shi Maria]] to cast or control him.
