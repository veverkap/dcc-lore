# dungeonwerx_portal_selector

• The numeric/handle [[interface]] used to dial destinations on DungeonWerx portals, selecting colored-line numbers like 17 for [[Sinopia Line]].
• A dual-handle selector [[interface]] used to dial specific numbered colored lines on [[ultima corporation]] portals, setting destination numbers like 17 for the [[Sinopia Line]].
• The numeric handle-based device used to dial destinations on DungeonWerx portals, setting two handles to select numbered lines such as [[Sinopia Line]].
