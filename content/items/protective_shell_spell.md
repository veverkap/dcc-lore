# protective_shell_spell

• A spell previously used by [[dungeon crawler carl]] that offers protection but has resource implications for later encounters.
• The spell was used to [[shield]] the party briefly, but its interaction unexpectedly dislodged glass/sand in the boss chamber and altered the room's configuration during a confrontation with [[Ghazi]] and the sand ooze.
• The spell was referenced as unfortunate timing by [[dungeon crawler carl]], who wishes it had not been expended before this encounter.
