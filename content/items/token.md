# token

• [[Poker]] chip–sized coins dropped by mobs, used to progress toward the [[key]] fight; first observed with a "1", later with a "2".
• Required for progression, nine [[tokens]] must be collected before fighting the last guardian for the [[Key]].
• An important, unnamed item that [[Imani]] is unable to find, causing ongoing concerns among [[dungeon crawler carl]]’s acquaintances and the Meadowlark staff.
