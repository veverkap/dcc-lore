# [[BabyBjörn baby backpack]]
A pink quadruped-carrying baby backpack produced from a [[Gold Adventurer Box]]; allows the wearer to have a buckled quadruped passenger [[Phase]] with them when using the [[Phase spell]] or [[Oozy spell]].
