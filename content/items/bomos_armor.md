# bomos_armor

• Massive, non-enchanted suit of armor designed to break into pieces on impact
• Houses missile launchers and seeker tubes
• Designed to maximize carrying capacity and provide mobile firepower
• Retrofitted with six smoke seeker tubes and six anti-undead tubes
• Physically formidable, non-magical
