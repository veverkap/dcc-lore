# half_splat_potion

• [[Half Splat potion]] is a consumable that prevents lethal falling damage but leaves the user at 5% health on landing; commonly found in [[Silver Skydiver's Box]].
• It is a risky healing/utility consumable that greatly reduces the user's health to a low percentage when used.
• [[Dungeon Crawler Carl]] deploys a half-splat while falling toward the boss.
• It can be used to prevent death and is often used in emergency situations, such as fleeing or provoking dinosaurs to attack hunters.
