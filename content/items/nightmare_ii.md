# nightmare_ii

• The gnomish Drop Bear biplane, originally labeled "Death Trap", was renamed "[[Nightmare]] II" by Carl and used to reach the floating house with its emergency balloons.
• The plane detached during the boss fight and slowly descended back into the bowl when its emergency balloon lost power.
• Carl assigned Louis and Firas to secure and store it in the house garage.
• The biplane is stored in the uprooted house's garage and can be lash-downed as a balloon/airship platform for approaching the [[Akula]].
• The salvaged biplane was refitted by Dungeon Crawler Carl and used with emergency balloons to reach the floating house, serving as a one-off vehicle/assault platform in the chapter.
