# Nightmare II
The renamed gnomish Drop Bear biplane Carl and Donut use to reach the floating house. Originally labeled "Death Trap," Carl spray-paints it [[Nightmare II]] and uses its emergency balloons to ascend and skitter along the bubble ceiling.

A named airplane that detached during the boss fight and slowly descended back into the bowl when its emergency balloon lost power. Carl assigns [[Louis]] and [[Firas]] to secure and store it in the house garage.
