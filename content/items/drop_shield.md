# drop_shield

• Disposable temporary protective shell sold at the prize counter, providing a short-lived protective bubble (about an hour) that can be toggled, vulnerable to heavy damage and some explosions, and must be rooted in place to function.
• Can be purchased from the prize counter with a [[token]] bought by [[Zabit]].
• Provides temporary protection for about an hour, must be rooted in place, and can be destroyed; does not stop magic.
• Portable defensive equipment tossed by [[Rosetta]] to block incoming fire, absorbing attacks temporarily but unable to hold indefinitely.
