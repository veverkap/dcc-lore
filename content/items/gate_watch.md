# gate_watch

• The pocket-[[watch]] components of the [[Gate of the Feral Gods]] (first [[watch]], second [[watch]], third/third hand) that Carl and [[Katia]] dial to set [[teleport]] coordinates and time.
• One of the pocket [[watch]] pieces that make up the [[Gate of the Feral Gods]]. Individual watches auto-tick and can be dialed to coordinates/time; Carl and [[Katia]] used three pieces to activate the [[gate]].
• One of the pair of watches referenced as component pieces of the [[Gate of the Feral Gods]]; shown on [[Orren the Liaison]]'s desk alongside the [[Winding Box]].
