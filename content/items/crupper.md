# crupper

• Piece of tack/equipment re-equipped by [[Princess Donut]] after being cleaned in the [[trailer]], part of her show-ready gear.
• Inventory waist accessory referenced by Carl, equippable simultaneously with other shoulder/jacket items, such as cloaks and jackets.
• Waist accessory equippable alongside [[Quan's Cloak]] (shoulder) and the [[Cat-Sized Ghillie Suit]] (jacket) for wearing multiple layers simultaneously.
