# portal

• A magical teleportation aperture used to send mobs to the [[Abyss]]; installed on the [[Def Leppard cart]] and elsewhere.
• Can be turned on/off remotely and is central to bait-and-[[teleport]] tactics throughout the chapter.
• Magic device attached to carts that teleports passengers and objects between lines and trainyards or into the abyss.
• Can be tuned to different destinations, has a switch delay, and can unintentionally [[teleport]] large objects like a station mimic.
• Stable teleportation device embedded in the [[Def Leppard cart]] used to send monsters (including neighborhood bosses and mantaur creatures) to the [[Abyss]].
• Can be turned on/off and presents both tactical opportunities and lethal risks.
• A portable teleportation field mounted in the [[cart]] that teleports contacted creatures to the [[Abyss]]; crucial to the team's tactics (including baiting bosses).
• Can be toggled on/off and has vulnerable interactions when creatures touch it.
• A magical gateway device used to [[teleport]] mobs (and crawlers) to the [[Abyss]].
• Central to the group's strategy: used on mantaur and neighborhood boss, and operated from the [[Def Leppard cart]].
• The active doorway that the [[train]] used to traverse floors; created/controlled via the [[portal cart]] and turned off by [[Zhang]] after the [[train]] departed.
