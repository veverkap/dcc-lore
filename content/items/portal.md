# Portal
A magical teleportation aperture used to send mobs to the [[Abyss]]; installed on the [[Def Leppard cart]] and elsewhere. Can be turned on/off remotely and is central to bait-and-teleport tactics throughout the chapter.

# [[Portal]]
Magic device attached to carts that teleports passengers and objects between lines and trainyards or into the abyss. Can be tuned to different destinations, has a switch delay, and can unintentionally teleport large objects like a station mimic.
