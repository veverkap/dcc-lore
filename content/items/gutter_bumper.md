# gutter_bumper

• A level 9 associated spell contributed by [[Mehmet Or. Crawler]].
• Used by groups and mages in combat as an area-of-effect–focusing spell.
• Deployed by [[Carl]] from [[Mehmet]]'s page in [[Carl’s Book of Boom]].
• Creates [[bumper]] lines that guide the [[Ice Slick]] effect down the river, extending the area of effect.
