# phase_through_walls

• A bypass spell attempted on a stairwell-containing chamber failed, suggesting special materials or rules are used.
• The spell was tried by crawlers to bypass magically sealed stairwell chambers, but it failed against the walls.
• A short-duration potion allows the user to pass through solid, non-biological objects, but duration/cooldown mechanics are dangerous if mis-timed.
• The potion is used frequently by [[dungeon crawler carl]].
• The potion allows passing through walls, but is dangerous in the current structural collapse, as users may "drop like a rock".
• Phasing risks uncontrolled falling if used on the unstable building.
• The potion is discussed as a potential means to bypass barriers and reach [[Imani]] and [[Tran]].
