# Door-Opening Bot
A bot from [[Dr. Ratchet]]’s automaton kit modified by [[dungeon crawler carl]] (quick-release arm, double arm, heavy-duty magnet) to breach the hidden hallway and trigger the release of [[Vorpals]].
