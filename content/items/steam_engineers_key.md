# steam_engineers_key

• Allows access to Iron Tangle employee-only areas and actions on all 400 steam-type trains of the Tangle.
• Grants special operational permissions for steam trains like the [[Nightmare Express]].
• Grants Carl specific control privileges on steam trains.
• [[Key]] retrieved from the engine cab.
• A large bronze [[key]] found in the cab.
