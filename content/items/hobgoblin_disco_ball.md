# Hobgoblin Disco Ball
An infused, unstable variant of a Hobgoblin smoke curtain created by [[dungeon crawler carl]] by soaking a smoke curtain in an invisibility potion. Type: magically-infused deflagrating tossable. Produces multi-hued, photosensitive smoke that conceals movement; caster is not immune. Status shown as unstable and it accidentally detonates in the crafting room.
