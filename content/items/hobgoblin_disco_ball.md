# hobgoblin_disco_ball

• An infused, unstable variant of a Hobgoblin smoke curtain created by soaking a smoke curtain in an invisibility potion, producing multi-hued, photosensitive smoke that conceals movement; caster is not immune.
• A sticky, rainbow- smoke explosive thrown by [[dungeon crawler carl]] that adheres to shields and creates a pulsing rainbow smoke plume.
• A thrown gadget used to create distractions (splattered goo) and interact with summoned butterflies, contributing to chaotic area effects during the Imogen confrontation.
• A magicked, infused version of a Hobgoblin smoke curtain created by soaking a smoke curtain in an [[invisibility]], producing heavy, multi-hued, photosensitive, frequency- responsive smoke that conceals movement but also blinds the caster.
• A thrown special munition used by [[dungeon crawler carl]] that explodes in sticky, sand-like rainbow residue that adheres to [[Quan]]'s [[shield]] and chest and produces billowing smoke.
• A thrown item/device used by [[dungeon crawler carl]] as part of an escape tactic during the dinosaur confrontation.
• An item dropped by [[Carl]] that emits flashing lights and thick smoke when activated, used to disorient and obscure vision in combat with mobs.
