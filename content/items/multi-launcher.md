# [[Multi-launcher]]
A deployable rocket platform used by [[dungeon crawler carl]] in the park. Holds arrays of [[Surefire Homing Missiles]] and can be remotely detonated via the [[Remote Detonator]], programmed to prioritize hunter-class targets.
