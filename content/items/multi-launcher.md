# multi-launcher

• A four-pack missile launcher used by [[dungeon crawler carl]] to fire guided and unguided rockets during the aerial battle against the [[Wasteland]]'s [[battery]].
• A deployable rocket platform used by [[dungeon crawler carl]] in the park, holding arrays of [[Surefire Homing Missiles]] and remotely detonable via the [[Remote Detonator]], prioritizing hunter-class targets.
