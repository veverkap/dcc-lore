# conscript

• A military charm/mind-control spell used by [[Doctor Bones]] to enslave weak-minded targets, potentially permanently against weak subjects and as a known threat on higher floors.
• A spell that converts spawned slugs/mercenaries into controllable infantry, imperfectly changing attitude and potentially producing rebellious units.
• A spell that can force mobs (like sluggalos) into line, mentioned as a warlord menu/control option and a knowledge gap for some earlier players.
• A mind-control/military charm spell cast by [[Doctor Bones]] that can permanently enslave the weak-minded, especially effective against uncovenanted crawlers and a common threat on the ninth floor.
• A spell that can force mobs (like sluggalos) back into line during faction wars, referenced as a war-time control tool some prior authors didn't know.
