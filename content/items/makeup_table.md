# [[Makeup Table]]
A level-based crafting/vanity table (mentioned at level three) that lets a user superimpose faces of any monster, crawler, or NPC they’ve met onto glass for sculpting disguises. Upgradeable (level five grants a full-sized mirror) and used by [[Katia]] to aid the [[Walk in Their Shoes]] ability.
