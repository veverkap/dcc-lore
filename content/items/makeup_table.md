# makeup_table

• A level-based crafting/vanity table that superimposes faces of monsters, crawlers, or NPCs onto glass for sculpting disguises.
• Upgradeable, with level five granting a full- sized mirror and additional saved designs.
• Used by [[Katia]] to aid the [[Walk in Their Shoes]] ability.
• Can show any monster or crawler face to assist sculpting and stores multiple designs at level three.
• Higher levels unlock full-body mirroring.
• Sold by [[Mordecai]] and used by [[Donut]] to superimpose faces and save designs.
