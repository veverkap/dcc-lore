# Enchanted Repeating Crossbow of the Scavenger Mother of Mothers
A unique, female-only repeating crossbow looted from [[Hekla]] by [[Katia]]. Properties described in-game: buffet enchantment (infinite basic ammo), damage and firing speed scale by +25% per female in party (up to 30), strength+level scales 1.5x more than a standard crossbow, +15 Dexterity, +10 Strength, and it casts [[Birth Defect]] on monster types that spawn additional monsters. Flavored with lore tying it to the goddess [[Nekhebit]].
