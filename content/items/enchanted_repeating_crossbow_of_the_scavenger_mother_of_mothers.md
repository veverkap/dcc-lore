# enchanted_repeating_crossbow_of_the_scavenger_mother_of_mothers

• Unique repeating [[crossbow]] looted from [[Hekla]] by [[Katia]].
• Female-only wield restriction; scales damage and speed with number of females in party.
• Grants stat bonuses: +15 Dexterity, +10 Strength.
• Buffet ammunition: infinite basic ammo.
• Damage and firing speed scale by +25% per female in party (up to 30).
• Strength+level scales 1.5x more than a standard [[crossbow]].
• Casts [[Birth Defect]] on monster types that spawn additional monsters.
• Ties to the goddess [[Nekhebit]].
