# knockout_potion

• Knockout Potion: a consumable prepared by [[Mordecai]] or another, requiring 15 minutes to create, intended to incapacitate a target for rescue or capture.
• Knockout: a potent, high-proof dungeon alcohol (Everclear analog) with strong, intoxicating effects, consumed by [[elle mcgib]] and others.
• [[Miriam Dom]] planned to pick up the Knockout Potion en route.
