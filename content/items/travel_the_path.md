# travel_the_path

• A powerful deity spell granted by certain [[light]] gods that allows a caster to temporarily become [[light]] and follow a path at near-[[light]] speed.
• Functions differently from teleportation or phasing.
• Can follow bijou tendril connections to the strongest connected temple.
• Risk of being cut by shadows.
