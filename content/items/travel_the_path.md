# [[Travel the Path]]
A powerful deity spell granted by certain light gods that allows a caster to temporarily become light and follow a path at near-light speed. Functions differently from teleportation or phasing, is dangerous (risk of being cut by shadows), and can follow bijou tendril connections to the strongest connected temple.
