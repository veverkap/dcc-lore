# climate_refuge

• A spell used by [[Mordecai]] to adapt to environmental conditions, referenced during the encounter with [[Dodola]] and [[Adad]].
• Used by Mordecai to survive hazardous weather conditions while moving toward the party.
• Allows Mordecai to traverse storm conditions during the ritual sequence while grounded in a bipedal form.
