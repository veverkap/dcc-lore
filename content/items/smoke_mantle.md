# smoke_mantle

• A thrown concealment item used by the defenders to obscure enemy lines of sight.
• A tactical consumable or equipment used by [[dungeon crawler carl]] to obscure the party from enemy sight.
• A tactical consumable dropped by one of the cousins to obscure vision during their retreat.
• A consumable stealth item used by [[dungeon crawler carl]] to obscure movement and help enter the city/[[ring]] area while under tower fire.
