# primal_engine

• The terrestrial/astral computational substrate into which the [[AI]] was installed.
• The device that brings forced memories and roles to life.
• The planetary machine into which the [[Macro AI]] is installed to awaken NPCs and initialize the season.
• The [[Containment Unit]] monitors the [[Primal Engine]] and its installation is the reason global metrics like [[Earth]]'s status appear in the [[interface]].
