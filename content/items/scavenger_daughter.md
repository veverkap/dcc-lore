# Scavenger Daughter
A totem/item in [[dungeon crawler carl]]’s possession with a back-patch mechanic that stores captured souls. Its special attack "Daughter’s Kiss" scales with purity of stored souls (same-species stacks increase power and residual effects).

# Scavenger Daughter
An owned item/pet mechanic that provides a "patch" to capture souls. Filling and draining the [[Scavenger Daughter]] patch charges up the [[Daughter's Kiss]] special attack; homogenous soul stacks (all one species) greatly magnify the attack’s power and residual effects.
