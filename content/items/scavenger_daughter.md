# scavenger_daughter

• A [[totem]]/item in [[dungeon crawler carl]]’s possession with a back-patch mechanic that stores captured souls.
• Its special attack "Daughter's Kiss" scales with purity of stored souls (same-species stacks increase power and residual effects).
• Provides a "patch" to capture souls, filling and draining which charges up the "Daughter's Kiss" special attack.
• Homogenous soul stacks (all one species) greatly magnify the attack's power and residual effects.
