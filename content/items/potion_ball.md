# potion_ball

• A small, contained potion carried internally by [[Samantha]] to prevent premature events, described as very uncomfortable.
• A throwable item filled with a healing potion, used by Carl to [[heal]] or affect mobs/areas at range.
• Black [[potions]] in ball form stored in the [[saferoom]], intended for use by [[Dungeon Crawler Carl]] during the assault, with god-related consequences and limits on use against religions.
• A single-use, portable potion object physically carried by [[Samantha]], intended to be used at a later time for an unknown purpose.
• A throwable consumable intended to put guards to sleep for thirty minutes, which [[Samantha]] was instructed to drop into the [[Security room]] as part of the infiltration plan but failed to bring.
