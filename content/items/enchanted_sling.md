# enchanted_sling

• An old-school sling wielded by a crawler guard, crackling with purple and black energy, used effectively in the [[trainyard]].
• Used to score hits, including disabling a small trap, and is part of local improvised defenses.
• A crude ranged weapon described as "old-school" but crackling with purple and black energy, used by a guard to score hits on traps and carts.
