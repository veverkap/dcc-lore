# loot_box

• A reward container granted to [[Donut]] after the circus quest, mentioned as part of her post-quest gains alongside an experience bump.
• A generic reward container referenced in the announcement, with patron payments affecting discounts and costs associated with [[Loot Box]]es and [[Benefactor Box]]es.
• In-game reward containers dropped to players by [[patrons]], central to progression and survival, delivered in response to audience engagement under the [[Audience / Follower System]].
• Loot containers explicitly referenced as the one thing crawlers do not drop when they die, with the text noting that "crawlers drop everything but the [[loot box]]."
