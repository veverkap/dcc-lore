# fear-infused_smoke_curtain

• A double-strength smoke [[grenade]] variant fired from [[dungeon crawler carl]]'s [[Xistera]] that creates an extended [[fear]]-smoke field.
• Used to obscure and control visibility during the jungle encounter.
• An enhanced smoke munition that fills an area with longer-lasting smoke and applies [[fear]] effects.
• [[Dungeon crawler carl]] launches one to disrupt the dinosaur pack during the encounter.
