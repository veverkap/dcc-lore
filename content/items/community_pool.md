# community_pool

• A powerful scroll used by [[Prepotente (Pony)]] that transfers an infection from one target to another when the caster is physically touching both parties.
• The scroll backfired due to counter-abilities and interacting rings, causing devastating compounded effects.
• The scroll transfers an infection from one physically touching party to another, requiring contact between both parties.
