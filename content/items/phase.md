# phase

• A general category of travel/stealth spells, mentioned in relation to temporary intangibility and movement.
• A spell that allows both the [[carrier]] and passenger to phase together when used while wearing the [[Baby Backpack (quadruped [[carrier]])]].
• A common traversal spell possessed by many crawlers, including [[dungeon crawler carl]], and is ineffective for certain inter- apartment movement.
