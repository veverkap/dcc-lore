# map_of_the_stars

• A dropped boss item recovered from the mimic gore pile that adds boss locations and descriptions to a large area of the map; discovered alongside the [[neighborhood map]] and [[field guide]].
• A dropped item obtained from the pile of dead mimics that adds boss locations and descriptions to a large area of the map, revealing nearby bosses when used.
• A tactical item obtained from a city boss corpse that lets the party see boss locations (e.g., [[Lusca]], [[Quetzalcoatlus]]) on the map and aids in planning the [[gate]]'s destination.
• A loot item given after city boss fights that shows remaining bosses in an area.
• A dropped item found in the mound of dead mimics that adds boss locations and descriptions to a large area of the map, similar in nature to the neighborhood map and field guide.
• A dropped map item retrieved from the gore pile that adds boss locations and descriptions to a large map area, obtained alongside references to the [[neighborhood map]] and [[field guide]].
• A tactical item recovered from a city boss corpse that allows tracking of boss positions within the [[Necropolis]] and other areas, used to plan the party's movements.
• A city-boss reward item that displays remaining bosses in the area, retrieved by [[dungeon crawler carl]] and team prior to facing the hydra.
