# ring_of_water_breathing

• A glowing blue [[ring]] that grants underwater breathing and amphibious changes like gills and -1 Charisma.
• Received by [[dungeon crawler carl]], [[Katia]], and [[Tran]] from a [[Gold]] "I'm Wet" box.
• Grants the wearer the ability to breathe underwater while still breathing on land, making them amphibious.
• Side-effect: -1 Charisma and gill growth/skin sliminess if worn unnecessarily.
• Awarded from the [[Gold I'm Wet Box]].
• A wearable [[ring]] in [[dungeon crawler carl]]'s inventory that grants gills and underwater breathing when equipped.
• Used by [[dungeon crawler carl]] to prepare for entering the river around [[Fort Freedom]].
