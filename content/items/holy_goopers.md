# holy_goopers

• Potion balls crafted by [[Mordecai]] using [[Emberus]] [[shrine]] water, healing potion, and coagulant, functioning like holy-water grenades when combined with a [[Torch]] spell.
• Mixed at the [[emberus]] by [[Mordecai]]: blessed water combined with healing potion and an air-activated coagulant, effective against vampires when used with [[Donut]]'s [[Torch]].
• Explosive/area-effect ordnance described as "holy" and effective against undead, used by [[dungeon crawler carl]] to clear paths and buy time during the horde assault.
• A sticky holy-water-liked potion item used by [[dungeon crawler carl]] against the boss's talon to sizzle and damage it during the encounter.
