# hob-lobber

Here is the consolidated markdown list:

* A thrown explosive weapon used by [[dungeon crawler carl]] to test corridors, deliver fused [[explosives]], and clear rooms.
* An explosive device used as a trigger on the [[dynamite]] bundle, attached to the end of a pole to detonate when striking a large creature or on collision.
* Explosive ammunition used by [[dungeon crawler carl]] with his [[Xistera]]-launcher, with fuses normally between six and seven seconds.
* A type of explosive device used in the world, deconstructed and repurposed by [[dungeon crawler carl]] to salvage fuses and study stability.
* A thrown explosive device used to break apart the [[Rolling Battle Formation Ball]] and [[key]] to splitting composite mobs.
* A thrown explosive device used by [[dungeon crawler carl]] to clear doors and create secondary explosions.
* A thrown explosive device used to clear carpets of gerbils and control swarms.
* A minor projectile weapon used by [[dungeon crawler carl]] to lob [[explosives]] or [[missiles]] into [[Claude Sludgington the Fourth]]'s stomach.
* A high-damage explosive thrown by [[dungeon crawler carl]] into clustered enemies, causing splatter and level/kill notifications when detonated.
* A class of thrown explosive used by [[dungeon crawler carl]], with variable strength and fuse types.
* A fragmenting tossable [[bomb]] item found in [[dungeon crawler carl]]'s inventory, described as "Fused" and "Fortified" with a lit wick fuse.
* The detonating component removed from [[hob-lobber]] and reused by [[dungeon crawler carl]] as testing fuses and in improvised tools.
* A prototype detonator device on a long pole used to trigger the hobgoblin [[dynamite]] when striking large monsters or impacts.
* A dropped explosive device used by [[dungeon crawler carl]] during the firefight with [[Architect Houston]], an improvised [[bomb]]-type item referenced during the [[teleport]] escape.
