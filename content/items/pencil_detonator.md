# pencil_detonator

• The Pencil Detonator is a small, pencil-like remote trigger given to Katia by Carl as a last-resort mutual-destruction measure.
• It is also referred to as the Pencil-like Detonator, a handheld device given to Katia to keep in her inventory with instructions to press if necessary.
