# fulgurite

• A glass-like inventory material collected when the party shatters glass objects, such as pieces of the castle and statue.
• One such piece is labeled in-inventory as [[fulgurite]].
• Larger intact pieces of the castle's glass are identified as fulgurite in [[dungeon crawler carl]]'s inventory after breaking glass fixtures.
• Collected during exploration of the [[sandcastle]].
