# gnomish_farseer_telescope

• Large magical scope stolen from [[Skarn]] by [[Donut]]; clamps to the [[Chariot]] and provides enhanced long-distance viewing and boss intel.
• Used by [[dungeon crawler carl]] to scout [[Ruckus]] and surrounding terrain, revealing boss descriptions and distant features such as the [[Wasteland]] and the [[Bactrian town]].
