# ghoul_generator

* A device present at certain stations that spawns ghouls and makes those stops particularly deadly.
* A soul-crystal-powered device that births Blister Ghouls and other undead.
* A device that spawns ghouls when living creatures die nearby, tied to soul crystal power sources.
* Mechanical devices in the pit whose overload and explosions drive much of the floor's chaos.
* A device that births Blister Ghouls and other ghoul types using soul crystal power.
* Devices that create ghouls across the floor; several had exploded during the events, contributing to the overload of the ghoul system.
* An artifact/device that births ghouls (notably Blister Ghouls) for every non-undead mob that dies on the floor, powered by soul crystals.
* Mechanical devices in the floor that spawn ghouls; several exploded during the conflict, and the system is described as overloaded after the [[train]] detonation.
* A device that spawns Blister Ghoul mobs, powered by soul crystal and continuously birth ghouls as non-undead mobs die on the floor.
* Devices powered by soul crystal that spawn ghouls; their vulnerabilities and explosions shape defensive decisions across stations.
* Floor devices/artifacts that spawn ghouls; multiple units exploded during the sequence and are a [[key]] part of the collapsing ghoul system narrative.
