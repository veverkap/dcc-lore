# ice_slick

• A spellbook of the Elle school that creates a thick ice covering over a wide area based on intelligence and spell level.
• The special edition obtained by [[dungeon crawler carl]] and [[Donut]] starts at level 5 and trains faster.
• Creates a thick ice covering over ground in a wide area.
• Freezes the River of Sloth, causing it to crack, split, and explode as it spreads along the path defined by [[Gutter Bumper]].
• Slicks the dance floor, sending several fighting NPCs spinning and crashing and helping secure the stage during the performance.
