# loop_de_loop

• A named trap used to swap positions between crawlers, considered for strategic use by [[dungeon crawler carl]] to reposition [[Princess Donut]] or execute a spider swap.
• A [[teleport]] trap that switches positions between the triggerer and another target, programmable with trap skill and used to reposition allies like [[Donut]].
• A trap/[[teleport]] spell used by [[Lucia Mar]] to swap positions with victims or send them to default locations, toggleable when Lucia is in her alternate form.
• A loop/[[teleport]] trap referenced as a strategy option: using the red spider plus the Loop to switch positions between crawlers, considered a last-resort [[teleport]] mechanic.
