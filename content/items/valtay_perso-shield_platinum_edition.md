# valtay_perso-shield_platinum_edition

• [[Valtay Perso-[[Shield]] Platinum Edition]]: Tech-based personal [[shield]] item from [[valtay]] found in [[dungeon crawler carl]]'s History inventory after reviewing past loot.
• Provides a short-duration inch-thick buffer against physical and some corporeal magical attacks; charges over time and does not occupy an equipment slot.
• Resembles a Saturn-like button and provides a short-duration inch-thick buffer that blocks physical hits and some corporeal magic.
• Less powerful than [[Protective Shell]] and has a two-minute active duration with recharge mechanics.
