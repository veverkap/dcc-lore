# Valtay Perso-Shield, Platinum Edition
A small, round, pinback personal shield (resembles Saturn) unlocked for the narrator. Provides a short-duration inch-thick buffer against physical and some corporeal magical attacks; charges over time and does not occupy an equipment slot.

# Valtay Perso-Shield, Platinum Edition
A personal, pinback-style shield item unlocked by the narrator; resembles a Saturn-like button and provides a short-duration inch-thick buffer that blocks physical hits and some corporeal magic. Less powerful than [[Protective Shell]] and has a two-minute active duration with recharge mechanics.
