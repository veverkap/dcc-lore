# All-purpose cleaner (cleaning machine)
Advanced trailer-mounted cleaning apparatus operated by [[D-0NAH]] that washes and polishes both organic guests (e.g., [[Princess Donut]], [[Mongo]]) and equipment. Mentioned as an expensive device available for purchase.
