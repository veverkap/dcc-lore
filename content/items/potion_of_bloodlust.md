# potion_of_bloodlust

• Doubles Strength and Movement for (Constitution) seconds, halves Dexterity, applies "Where the Fuck Am I? Who the Fuck Are You?" debuff, and can increase movement speed further per kill while active.
• Also called the berserking potion; spreads through water and triggers a feeding frenzy among Juvenile Octo-Shark mobs when taped to a Hob-Llobber.
• Temporarily multiplies Strength and Movement Speed, halves Dexterity, applies severe memory/identity debuffs, and scales movement speed with kills.
• Can be used to create chaos among enemies by applying it to a mantis.
• Increases movement speed further per kill while active.
• Applies severe memory/identity debuffs.
• Can be retrieved from a reward box.
• Strongly advised against by Mordecai.
• Can be used to clear enemies from the [[Akula]]'s bridge by spreading a frenzy among Juvenile Octo-Shark minions.
