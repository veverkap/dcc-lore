# Potion of Bloodlust
Dangerous potion recovered in a [[Pacifist's Box]] that doubles Strength and Movement for (Constitution) seconds, halves Dexterity, applies the "Where the Fuck Am I? Who the Fuck Are You?" debuff, and can increase movement speed further per kill while active. Strongly advised against by [[Mordecai]].
