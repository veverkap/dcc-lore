# gold_armband

• Employee-tracking armband used by Iron Tangle HR to mark troublemakers for the [[Kravyad]].
• Presented as a fake benefit to force overtime compliance.
• Introduced by [[Madison]] as a productivity measure, claiming it would instruct [[Kravyad]] to [[teleport]] workers home.
• In reality, the armband flags "troublemakers" to the [[Kravyad]] as acceptable food.
