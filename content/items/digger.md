# digger

• An enormous tunneling machine with a dwarven engine, used by changelings to reach upper tunnels, monitored by the Justice Lights Team's seismic sensors.
• A heavy-use piece of equipment that is dangerous to use near a summoned deity like the Feral God [[Meatus]] Has Entered the Realm, as warned by Rosetta, Britney, and commanders.
