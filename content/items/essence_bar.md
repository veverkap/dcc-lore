# essence_bar

• A player resource that fills when killing mobs; becomes larger after each full drain and must be emptied to avoid danger.
• Filling it triggers the [[Scavenger'’s Daughter System Announcement]] and unlocks the item [[The Daughter’’s Kiss]].
• Fills when [[dungeon crawler carl]] kills mobs (notably [[Duende]]); when full it triggers the [[The Scavenger’’s Daughter]] announcement and enables use of [[The Daughter’’s Kiss]].
