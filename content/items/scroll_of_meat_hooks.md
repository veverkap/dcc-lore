# scroll_of_meat_hooks

• A consumable scroll that emits a rancid stench to attract all carnivorous pet-class mobs to the caster for a duration based on caster Intelligence and a range based on Charisma.
• Used by [[Donut]] to lure [[Orthrus]] off the necropolis.
• A scroll spell that attracts carnivorous pet-class mobs, described as emitting a foul stench that lures pets for 30s + 10s per Intelligence and has a range based on Charisma.
• Instrumental in drawing [[Orthrus]] out of the bubble.
• A named scroll/item that [[dungeon crawler carl]] asks [[Donut]] about, implied to be useful for grappling or movement during the rescue/repair operations.
• An offensive spell/effect used by [[Donut]] during the chapter, mentioned as part of her paw abilities.
• A scroll item in [[dungeon crawler carl]]'s inventory, mentioned at the chapter's end as potentially usable for the unfolding situation.
