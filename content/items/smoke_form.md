A Phase-style spell used by [[Princess Donut]] as part of her Assassin of Sekhmet class kit. Functions as a travel/stealth spell that lets casters slip through obstacles; contrasted with [[Oozy Form]] and the offensive [[Gloom Wraith Phase]].
