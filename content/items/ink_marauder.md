# [[Ink Marauder]]
A named sacrificial spell/ability used by Signet to animate drawings into massive paper‑like minions. The spell animates blood-and-ink creations (2D elementals) and can be boosted via sacrificial blood; its summoned creatures appear flat and semi‑translucent but are combat-capable.

# Ink Marauder
A spell owned by [[Signet]] that requires three participants (caster, blood-source sacrifice, and killer) or can be cast via a single person fulfilling all roles. Key to Signet's proposed ritual to prove her family's reality.
