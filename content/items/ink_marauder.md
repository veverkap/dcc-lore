# ink_marauder

• A named sacrificial spell/ability used by [[Signet]] to animate drawings into massive paper-like minions, which can be boosted via sacrificial blood.
• The spell requires three participants (caster, blood-source sacrifice, and killer) or can be cast via a single person fulfilling all roles.
• The spell is intended to prove the reality of [[Signet]]'s family by using a sacrifice.
• The summoned creatures appear flat and semi-translucent but are combat-capable.
