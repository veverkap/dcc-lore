# enemy_of_the_church_tattoo

• A persistent [[tattoo]] marking the bearer as an enemy of [[Diwata]]. Confers +25% damage against Diwata's adherents (+50% if wearer adheres to [[Emberus]]). Cannot be hidden and can be removed only by killing [[Diwata]] or chopping off the arm.
