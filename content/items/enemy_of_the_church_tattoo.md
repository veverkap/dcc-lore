# Enemy of the Church Tattoo
A persistent tattoo marking the bearer as an enemy of [[Diwata]]. Confers +25% damage against Diwata's adherents (and +50% if wearer adheres to [[Emberus]]). Cannot be hidden and can be removed only by killing [[Diwata]] or chopping off the arm.
