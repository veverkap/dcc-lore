# alpha_carl

• [[Totem]] card recovered from the hydra that uses [[dungeon crawler carl]]'s likeness; presented as a potential [[totem]] choice but ethically rejected by [[dungeon crawler carl]] and [[Donut]].
• A specific monster card encountered on [[Floor 8]] that represented a hyper-stylized version of [[dungeon crawler carl]] (better hair, different voice) used in the floor's card-based combat.
• A powerful card variation of [[dungeon crawler carl]] encountered on [[Floor 8]] — essentially Carl with enhanced aesthetics/voice and stronger stats.
• Summoning-type items summoned by camp members to deploy auxiliary creatures and monsters into the battlefield (deployed en masse during the rooftop fight).
• A card/item from a previous floor mentioned as left behind; noted for causing confusion when duplicate-like creatures appear in the dungeon.
