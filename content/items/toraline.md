# toraline

• A yam-like vegetable used in potion creation, such as removing Maggie from Chris's brain, and explicitly referenced in a benefactor note to be kept/saved.
• A valuable consumable/resource mentioned as being limited in supply, with [[Rosetta]] requesting access to the remaining toraline for her purposes.
• A rare [[ink]]/material historically used to stabilize and preserve memories, enabling borrowing or taking spells/skills via tattoos, similar to but not identical to [[Toraline]]-based [[ink]].
• A crafting ingredient or quest resource, with a benefactor note instructing to "Save the toraline" for future use.
