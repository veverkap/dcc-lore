# pathfinder_skill

• [[Pathfinder skill]] is a navigation/mapping skill that aids in locating stairwells, shops, and guilds, explicitly recommended by Odette for survival on deeper floors.
• Katia's navigation skill that expands map visibility when held open, helps identify car shapes, station locations, and other trains passing on adjacent tracks, and is difficult to [[train]] (level 3 in-chapter).
• The skill is used by Katia to zoom the map and detect car/station types (level three on this floor), required to locate stairwells, saferooms, and trains on [[the tangle]].
