# levitate

* [[Levitate]]: Referenced spell contrasted with [[Hover]]; more powerful levitation that can be negated by taking damage.
* [[Levitate Potion]]: Single-use potion used by [[dungeon crawler carl]] to gain aerial vantage for scouting the extent and density of mobs within the [[Colón Cemetery]] boss area.
* [[Levitation Potion]]: Consumable item used by [[dungeon crawler carl]] to [[hover]] above the ground and scout; one was consumed during the approach to [[Miriam Dom]]'s signal.
* [[Levitation potions]]: Consumable items referenced by [[dungeon crawler carl]] as options for reaching the top of the mound; held in inventory for vertical traversal.
* [[Potion of Levitation]]: Utility potion Carl uses to gain height and scout the corrupted town's mob distribution from above.
* [[Levitation Potion]]: Consumable item Carl carries for vertical movement; referenced as an option for scaling the ~700-foot dirt mound instead of climbing.
* [[Levitate]]: Consumable potion used by [[dungeon crawler carl]] to rise above the funnel and make a rescue attempt; it grants vertical movement but has time/height limitations.
* [[Levitation]]: Potion of Levitation used by [[dungeon crawler carl]] to rise above the treeline and search for signals from [[Miriam Dom]].
