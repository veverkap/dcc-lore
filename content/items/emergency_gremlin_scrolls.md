# emergency_gremlin_scrolls

• Consumable scrolls found among Carl's box loot, part of the routine supplies returned after the battle.
• Used to generate gremlins as distractions, some scrolls were dipped in pouches or eaten, drawing attention from other gremlins.
• Common scroll-type loot appearing in the boxes, majority of scroll rewards in the current batch.
