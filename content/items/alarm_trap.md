# alarm_trap

* A trap referenced as having fallen deep into a vent near the castle entrance, unreachable by some characters during dam-building efforts.
* A trap item used by hunters to generate loud sounds that repel [[mantis]] by exploiting their sensitivity to loud noises, with [[dungeon crawler carl]] keeping one in his inventory.
* A generic category of traps Carl removes from the enemy entrance, used to detect and alert defenders.
* A trap item with a music/recorded trigger option, used by [[dungeon crawler carl]] to play heavy metal and trigger mantaurs' berserk behavior.
* A trap item that can be used as a crowd-control tool (loudness repels [[mantis]]) or as an inventory object [[dungeon crawler carl]] carries, with an initiated alarm trap perpetually playing a song.
* A thrown trap-device that triggered local musical chaos ("Ballroom Blitz") and contributed to disorienting nearby mobs.
* A timed alarm trap duct-taped to [[Samantha]]'s head as a distraction, set for one minute to trigger noise inside [[Fort Freedom]] and draw attention.
* A specific configured [[Alarm Trap]] variant noted as already initiated in Carl's inventory that continuously plays "Kickstart My Heart" – an example of personalization of trap items.
* An alarm-style trap placed by [[dungeon crawler carl]] at a town entrance that plays the Red Hot Chili Peppers song "Give It Away" and notifies the owner if disturbed, used as part of [[Point Mongo]]'s layered defenses.
