# enchanted_nipple_ring_of_the_superior_fire_demons_hand_maiden

• A glowing red accessory prize from a Beguiler's box, equippable only on female quadrupeds.
• Grants cursed-item removal, immunity to natural fire and lava, resistance to fire spells, and no health loss inside Sheol.
• Increases fire spell damage based on Intelligence.
• Grants the once-per-30-hours berserk spell "Bitch, What?" (Fire Berserker effect).
• Originated from a Beguiler's Box in the chapter.
