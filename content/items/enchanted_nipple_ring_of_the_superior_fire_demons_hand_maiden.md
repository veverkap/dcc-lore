# Enchanted Nipple Ring of the Superior Fire Demon’s Hand Maiden
A glowing red accessory prize from a Beguiler’s box. Item restrictions and effects: equippable only on female quadrupeds; grants cursed-item removal, immunity to natural fire and lava, resistance to fire spells, no health loss inside Sheol, increases fire spell damage based on Intelligence, and grants the once-per-30-hours berserk spell "Bitch, What?" (Fire Berserker effect). Noted as applied to [[Donut]] in discussion.
