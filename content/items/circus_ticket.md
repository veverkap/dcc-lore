# circus_ticket

• The Circus Ticket is a physical entry item that allows entry into [[Grimaldi's Big Top]].
• It is carried by [[Dungeon Crawler Carl]] and is used as a demonstrative [[shield]] to pass hostile clowns at the entrance.
• The item is emphasized as essential not to drop during the plan.
