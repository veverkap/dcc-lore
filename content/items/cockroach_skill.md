# cockroach_skill

• Gives a chance to survive fatal attacks, scaling with skill level
• Can be trained only by use or [[potions]]
• Activates to save Princess Donut from a lethal hit, leaving her unconscious for a time
• Possessed by Shi Maria and shared by Donut at level 15, activates to prevent fatal damage and grants a short [[shield]]
• Auto-activation defensive skill possessed by Princess Donut that prevented death when she absorbed the [[lightning]] [[bolt]]
