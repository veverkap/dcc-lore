# tuk-tuk

• Also called an auto rickshaw, a precarious, small three-wheeled taxi-type vehicle.
• Used as a race vehicle by Florin and Lucia's team, characterized as an unfortunate/fragile choice for dungeon heats.
• Florin's tricked-out, movement-focused vehicle that other teams like Chris's follow through the Desert Track.
