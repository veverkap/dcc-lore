# Royal Court of Princess Donut Flag
A small, enchanted flag shaped like heart-covered boxers embroidered with the phrase "The Royal Court of Princess Donut" and a bomb patch. The flagpole is an enchanted metal spike that can stick into creatures (including ghosts under 5% health); multiple such flags are carried by the party.
