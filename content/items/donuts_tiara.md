# donuts_tiara

• Jeweled [[tiara]] worn by [[Donut]] that confers membership in the [[sultanate]] and grants the ability to imbue the [[Septic debuff]].
• Jewelled headpiece with multiple absorption jewels and [[buff]] slots, being drained and managed by [[Katia]] to transfer buffs (including Constitution and [[Dodge]] enhancements).
• Advanced [[targeting]] equipment worn by [[Donut]] that grants exceptional line-of-sight [[precision]] for long-range spells and negates the need for Tower [[sniper]] synergies.
• Personal ornament worn by [[Princess Donut]] that she refuses to remove even when directed to strip other gear for the [[All-purpose cleaner (cleaning machine)]], symbolic of her status on the panel.
