# gold_hunter_killer_box

• A reward container received when a player trap kills a hunter, yielding 35,000 [[gold]] and the trophy hand of [[JayGee]].
• Awarded by the [[The Hand that Claws the Master (x35)]] achievement, tied to permanent kills of non-crawler biologicals on the sixth floor.
• Triggered by [[dungeon crawler carl]]'s trap that killed a hunter traveling near the bugbear settlement, containing 35,000 [[gold]] and the trophy hand of [[JayGee]].
• Awarded repeatedly to [[dungeon crawler carl]] for achievements linked to permanently killing non-crawler biologicals, associated with the "The Hand that Claws the Master" achievement.
