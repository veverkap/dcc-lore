# oozy_form

• Transformation skill/spell that allows the caster to become a slime-like form, enabling movement through tight spaces and seeing in the dark.
• Grants slime-transformation abilities, including slipping through cracks and puddle form, and can be held indefinitely at level 15.
• Allows the caster to turn into a slime, slip through cracks, puddle on floors, and function as defensive/camouflage movement.
• Used by [[Carl]] to change shape, allowing for stealthy entry into [[Club Scolopendra]] and other areas.
• Used to slide into a beer can and replace its contents with an explosive device as part of a concealed failsafe.
• [[Oozy Form]] spell book is slimy, mobile, and classified as a special edition that trains faster.
