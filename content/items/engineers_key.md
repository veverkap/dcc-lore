# engineers_key

• A small [[key]] held by Donut in her mouth, possibly used for [[cart]] or equipment access.
• A [[key]] item that opens engine cars, color-coded to specific lines (e.g., ochre), allowing access to the first car and potentially the Abyss [[Gate]].
• A [[key]] believed to be carried by engineers, used to open the engine car and enable access to the first car.
• A [[key]] obtainable by killing an engineer, teleporting the killer inside a [[train]] car within the Ultima Corp Trainyard.
• A physical [[key]] carried in Donut's mouth, relevant to [[cart]]/engineering access and carried through portals as precaution.
