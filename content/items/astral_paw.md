# astral_paw

* A level-based utility/weapon spell learned by [[Donut]], functions like [[Astral Hand]] but without a thumb, gains claws at level 5, size increases by level 10, and at level 15 the caster's skills map to the paw.
* Used by [[Donut]] for remote interaction to reveal reactions inside rooms without entering.
* Used by [[Donut]] to push [[Elmer]] off the slope, directly resulting in the mayor's fall and death.
* Used by [[Donut]] to try to smack [[Gus]] (the dog) down the chute.
* A massive ethereal cat-paw spell conjured and linked to [[Warlord Princess Donut (The Oak Fell)]]'s second spell slot, captures thousands of soldiers and [[the feral god [[meatus]] has entered the realm]], and is used in a repeated slam finisher against [[chains]] anchored by [[Li Na]].
* A spell-liked ability referenced as a powerful option for [[Donut]]'s enhanced claws, can cut through almost anything and is tied to the effects of the celestial boon on her paw.
* A utility/offensive astral-manipulation spell that creates a paw-like appendage to manipulate and strike at a distance, with level-based growth and claw options.
* Used by [[Donut]] to push [[Elmer]] off the edge of town, initiating the fatal tumble that results in the mayor's death.
* A spell referenced as part of [[Donut]]'s new capabilities tied to the celestial boon, described as being able to cut through almost anything (including ghosts) and making [[Donut]] an exceptionally powerful melee combatant.
* Manifests as a giant spectral paw, used to sweep and clear dozens of cannibals and snap the natural bridge trapping the road.
* A powerful offensive spell cast by [[Donut]] that smashed and obliterated the Russian [[Cadillac]].
