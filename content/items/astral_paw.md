# Astral Paw
A level-based utility/weapon spell learned by [[Donut]]. Functions like [[Astral Hand]] but without a thumb; gains claws at level 5, size increases by level 10, and at level 15 the caster's skills map to the paw. Initial reach ~10 meters at level 1.
