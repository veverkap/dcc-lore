# Dolores Doesn’t Splat
A notorious potion designed to be drunk while falling; it accelerates descent, softens impact, and enables repeated seismic-style impacts on landing. [[Donut]], [[Katia]], and [[dungeon crawler carl]] use it to survive the jump from the [[Vahana]].
