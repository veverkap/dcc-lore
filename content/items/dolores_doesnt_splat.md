# dolores_doesnt_splat

• A notorious potion designed to be drunk while falling; it accelerates descent, softens impact, and enables repeated seismic-style impacts on landing.
• Used by [[dungeon crawler carl]], [[Donut]], and [[Katia]] to survive the jump from the [[Vahana]].
