# youre_not_done_yet

• A spellbook, "You're Not Done Yet", with insidious properties, emitting black tendrils, and compared to [[Second Chance]] but more dangerous.
• A War Mage spell that strips flesh from dead soldiers to create sentient Fleshers, the origin spell for Fleshers encountered on the [[Dismemberment Limited]].
• A spell cast by [[Donut]] to animate corpses as allies, central to the plan to target enemy soldiers inside the ball.
• A black-[[hued]] spellbook obtained in the chapter, with insidious properties, likened to [[Second Chance]] but more dangerous, and stored in [[Donut]]'s inventory.
