# photograph_of_epitome_noflex

• An in-inventory photo kept by [[dungeon crawler carl]] showing [[Epitome Noflex]], used to identify the pale hunters associated with [[dream]] and referenced in an incident with [[Louis]].
• The photograph is a point of conflict when [[Louis]] snatched it, prompting tensions among hunters and crawlers.
