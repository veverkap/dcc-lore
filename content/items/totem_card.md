# totem_card

• A card item subtype, the [[Totem Card]], summons a mob to your squad when activated, with restrictions on activation, ownership, and squad slot behavior.
• [[Totem]] [[Cards]] are collectible objectives in the overarching floor mechanics, with the chapter opening with "Three of Six Totems Collected", and using too many utility [[cards]] lowers the chance of drawing them.
