# Monster Cards
The central [[Floor 8]] mechanic: collectible cards used to fight card-wielding opponents. Many named creatures and variants (e.g., [[dungeon crawler carl]]) appear as cards.

The core Item mechanic of Floor 8: cards collected by crawlers that summon/fight as creatures. Floor rules required collecting and using these cards in battles.
