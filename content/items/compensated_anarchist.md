# [[Compensated Anarchist]]
Rare, exclusive Earth-based class (Monk/Rogue hybrid) Carl chooses. Requires Explosives Handling level five and 500 billion views. Grants many bonuses (trap/bomb skills, Fear spell, Charisma boost, access to Desperado Club and Naughty Boys Employment Agency) and requires a subclass choice on the sixth floor.
