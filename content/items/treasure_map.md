# treasure_map

• [[Treasure Map]] is a persistent loot dropped by a country boss that reveals the location and quality of magical gear in the area.
• It marks locations of valuables across the ballroom area, but remains useless after brambles covered the loot.
• Picking one up can trigger a violent scramble among players.
