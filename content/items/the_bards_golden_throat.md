# The Bard’s Golden Throat
An enchanted headset microphone (face accessory) won by [[Donut]]. Grants automatic pitch correction (the "Golden Throat" enhancement), amplified vocal volume (up to 5x), and +5% Charisma while equipped. Treated as a performance/role item for bards and entertainers.
