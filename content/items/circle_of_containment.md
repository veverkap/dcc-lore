# Circle of Containment
A level-15 spell cast by [[Chaco]] that creates a semicircular shell preventing things inside from affecting those outside (and vice versa). It traps [[Uzzi]], [[Dodola]], and the [[Puzzle Disk]] together long enough for the contained lightning spell to detonate.
