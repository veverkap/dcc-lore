# Enchanted Obsidian Bracelet of the Raggle Rouser
A bracelet looted from the dead elf and immediately equipped by [[Donut]]. Properties described in-game: +10 Constitution, +5 Dexterity, +15% Fire Resistance, grants the wearer a level-10 [[Fireball]] spell usable once every twenty minutes. Item has been upgraded three times and carries a warning about a 50% chance to permanently break if upgraded again.

# Enchanted Obsidian Bracelet of the Raggle Rouser
