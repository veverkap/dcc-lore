# enchanted_obsidian_bracelet_of_the_raggle_rouser

• A bracelet looted from the dead elf and equipped by [[Donut]] or taken from Akland and equipped by [[Samantha]].
• Grants +10 Constitution, +5 Dexterity, and +15% Fire Resistance.
• Allows casting a level-10 [[Fireball]] spell once every twenty minutes.
• Item is upgraded three times and carries a 50% chance to permanently break if upgraded again.
