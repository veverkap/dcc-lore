# glass_prison

• A spell used by [[Sadie]] to facilitate safe passage through the Kravyad, employee [[portal]], and substation H.
• Used to trap or immobilize targets, specifically the Kravyad, to allow safe passage or control of threats.
• Cited as the method used to assist [[Hekla]]'s crew onto the [[train]] route.
