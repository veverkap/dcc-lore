# royal_chariot

* A tracked, portable contraption built by [[dungeon crawler carl]] and [[Katia]], labeled as "Tracked All-Terrain Suicide Machine", used for rapid traversal of the bowl.
* A vehicle in Carl's inventory, used for rapid overland travel, intended to transport Carl and Donut south toward the town across the bridge.
* A deployable vehicle/ item used by [[dungeon crawler carl]] to mount a missile launcher and move quickly through the flooded streets.
* A powerful rideable item in Carl's inventory, considered impractical due to heavy vehicle traffic and risk of destructive chain reactions.
* A flatbed-style transport vehicle used by [[dungeon crawler carl]] to traverse plains, with a narrator attempting to mount a six-pack missile launcher on it during mobilization.
* A vehicle item used by [[dungeon crawler carl]] to travel quickly along freeways when the road is clear, allowing faster movement across [[Havana]] suburbs.
* A large vehicle stored in [[dungeon crawler carl]]'s inventory, considered for use but ultimately replaced with his father's [[motorcycle]].
* A vehicle item used by [[dungeon crawler carl]] to traverse the flooded streets, with the narrator latching onto pieces of the [[Royal Chariot]] to provide [[light]] and weapon platforms.
* A powerful transportation item mentioned in [[dungeon crawler carl]]'s inventory, considered unsafe to deploy on crowded roads due to potential crash cascades.
* A vehicle item used by [[dungeon crawler carl]] to travel quickly along the [[Freeway]] and move the party south through [[Havana]].
* A vehicle in Carl's inventory, mentioned as needing air conditioning for travel in the heat, referenced as part of party logistics outside the [[Town Hall]].
