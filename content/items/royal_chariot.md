# The Royal Chariot
A crude, user-fabricated contraption nicknamed and system-labeled as [[royal chariot]] — a tracked, all-terrain vehicle (appears in-system as "Tracked All-Terrain Suicide Machine"). Built at the [[Engineering Table]] with parts refined at the [[Metalworking Table]] and intended to be portable (can be disassembled into inventory-sized pieces). Piloted/tested by [[Katia]] with [[dungeon crawler carl]] as passenger; intended as a fast, non-armored transport rather than a tank.
