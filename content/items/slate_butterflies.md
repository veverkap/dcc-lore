# slate_butterflies

• Small, stuffed creatures sewn onto [[Britney]]'s jacket that summon a common- rarity, hand-sized butterfly which seeks [[light]] sources and can disable lights before reverting to plush form; special ability is non-magical.
• Hand-sized stuffed creatures used by [[Britney]] that can temporarily become a common- rarity summoned butterfly, flying to a [[light]] source and extinguishing it before returning to stuffed form.
• Classified as a non-magical special-ability item in the chapter.
• A common figure in Jaxbrin's lineup; lore notes a tragic fairytale background.
• Mentioned among dozens of beanie figures that [[Daniel Bautista]] owns and inspects for variants.
