# stick_and_poke_tattoo_kit

• Handheld [[tattoo]] kit used to [[tattoo]] individuals, including [[dungeon crawler carl]], [[Samantha]], and others.
• Contains needles and [[ink]] used to create [[Tattoo: The Eye of the Bedlam Bride]].
• Used by [[Shi Maria]] to force [[dungeon crawler carl]] to [[tattoo]] himself.
• Used by [[Katia]] to mark [[Samantha]] with the [[desperado club]] logo.
• Received kit from the floor used by [[Shi Maria]] to [[tattoo]] [[Li Jun]] and [[dungeon crawler carl]].
