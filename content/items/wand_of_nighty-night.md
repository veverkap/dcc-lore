# wand_of_nighty-night

• A gnarled wooden [[wand]] with 10 charges that casts a level-10 Sleep on a non-boss single target for five minutes (opponent wakes if physically damaged), with multiple casts compounding the effect.
• Used by [[dungeon crawler carl]] to instantly put [[Burcu]] to sleep, allowing him to remove her bindings safely.
• Used by the narrator to instantly put [[Sister Ines Quiteria, The Reaper of Havana]] to sleep, producing a two-minute timer over her and initiating combat mechanics.
• Used by the protagonist to instantly incapacitate [[Sister Ines]] (renders her unconscious and triggers a two-minute timer), enabling the party to attempt a kill without an immediate card battle.
