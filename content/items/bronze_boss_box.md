# bronze_boss_box

• A boss-tier reward box awarded to the killers of [[Neighborhood boss]] encounters, containing low- to mid-tier items.
• Contains assorted gear, including armor, rings, and other useful items.
• A loot container rewarded from boss encounters.
• A reward indicating one of the slain NPCs (either [[Miss Quill]] or [[Magistrate Featherfall]]) was a neighborhood boss.
• A visible reward in the notifications indicating that one of the killed NPCs (either [[Featherfall]] or [[Miss Quill]]) was a neighborhood boss, granting an additional boss-tier drop.
• Contains items such as the spell tome [[Bang Bro]] and other loot.
