# Bronze Boss Box
A boss-tier reward box awarded to the killers of [[Neighborhood boss]] encounters. Contains low- to mid-tier items; in this chapter, [[Donut]] opens one and obtains a spell ([[Torch]]). Tied to the associated boss achievement and bronze star.
