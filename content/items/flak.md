# flak

• Anti-air spell obtained from the [[Spellbook of the Floor]], powerful at high levels (level 10) and potentially valuable if buffed to level 15 by the [[Cloak of the Benevolent Champion]].
• Defensive spell owned by [[Donut]] used in combination with other tower defenses to deflect incoming fire.
• Defensive spell used by the enemy that produces explosive anti-air effects, insufficient against gravity [[bombs]] in the chapter.
• Spell learned from a [[Spellbook of the Floor]] prize, a powerful anti-air spell with strong effects at higher levels (recommended to be leveled before reading without coordination).
