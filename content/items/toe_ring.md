# toe_ring

• The Toe [[Ring]] grants the "Sticky Feet" ability to [[dungeon crawler carl]].
• It is an enchanted [[ring]] worn on Carl's second toe, acquired after being stepped on by [[Grull]].
• The item originally granted the "Mind Balance" skill to Carl, and even if removed, the skill remains trained to a diminished level due to continued use.
• The [[ring]] is linked to Carl's state during possession and must be removed while he is unconscious as part of the procedure to deal with [[Shi Maria]].
• The item can be removed by others, as seen when [[Imani]] removed it while Carl was incapacitated.
• The Toe [[Ring]] is an equippable item that enables the ceiling-walking maneuver used in the chapter.
• It provides a +5 bonus to [[Powerful Strike]] but is impacted by training caps and diminishing returns.
