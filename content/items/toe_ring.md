# [[Toe Ring]]
The piece of equipment that grants the [[Sticky Feet]] ability to [[dungeon crawler carl]]. Described as his "newest toe ring" and central to the ceiling-walking tactic.

# Toe Ring
An enchanted ring Carl wears on his second toe (acquired after being stepped on by [[Grull]]). Grants abilities including "Sticky Feet" and "Super Spreader" (the latter used in the chapter to spread a debuff to nearby hunters visible on the map).
