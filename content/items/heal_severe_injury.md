# heal_severe_injury

• A high-tier potion that heals severe wounds, requiring 33 [[fine healing potion]]s and an [[Autoclave]] to craft properly.
• High-level potion created by combining many [[Fine Healing Potion]]s (requires ~33 fine heals and an [[autoclave]] per Mordecai).
• Used to [[heal]] grievous wounds like [[Mongo]]'s burned wing.
