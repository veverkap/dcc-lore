# goblin_oil

• A combustible crafting ingredient used by [[dungeon crawler carl]] to boost the incendiary effect of [[Carl's Jug O' Boom]] and other fire devices.
• Flammable liquid ingredient used in [[bombs]] like [[Baby Uno]] to increase incendiary effects when detonated.
