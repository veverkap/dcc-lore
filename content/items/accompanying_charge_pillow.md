# accompanying_charge_pillow

• The Accompanying Charge Pillow is paired with the [[Tiara of a Thousand Lights]].
• It drains magical properties from [[hat]] items over ten hours and stores them for transfer to the [[tiara]].
• Items remain physically intact but are magically drained.
