# Celestial Godslayer Box
A celestial box that manifests as a cloud of black smoke; in this chapter it forms the smoky image of [[Meatus]] and produces no physical reward, but its smoke has an unsettling effect on the party and appears tied to a subsequent mysterious effect on [[Donut]].
