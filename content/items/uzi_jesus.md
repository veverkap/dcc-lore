# uzi_jesus

• A Veriluxx Real Pet Companion action figure sold at [[Squirties waystation]] that can speak and animate, providing comic relief and commentary in [[Zander]]'s shop.
• A unique orange [[totem]] card (also called the "T’Ghee Card" / "HeyZoos" [[totem]]) described as an undead ranged healer with numerous skills, level 140, Constitution 200, summoning a powerful [[totem]] named "Roy" (giant caiman referenced separately), auto-occupying squad slots.
