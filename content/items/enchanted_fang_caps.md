# enchanted_fang_caps

• Small enchantments fitted onto [[Mongo]]'s teeth that produce occasional electrical sparks when the pet bites or chomps.
• Equippable cosmetic/weapon caps used on [[Mongo]]'s teeth; provide movement speed and strength buffs and sometimes damage/debuff effects.
• [[Enchanted fang caps]] are equippable items placed on [[Mongo]]'s teeth to add damage effects and movement/strength buffs; Donut's Platinum box repeatedly yields these.
• A fleeting equippable item that converts incisors into ripping bite weapons, increasing melee bite damage by 50% and adding chances to inflict level-five Poisoned (25%) and Paralyzed (10%) statuses; noted as swappable to certain pets like [[Mongo]].
