# landmine

• A removable trap near the castle door that Carl disarms and takes into his inventory during infiltration.
• A hidden explosive placed outside the bait-shack [[saferoom]] door by [[dungeon crawler carl]] to deny enemy use of that entrance.
• Crafting a landmine at the sapper's table changes explosive yield and allows yield tuning.
