# ied

• A directional improvised explosive device (claymore-style with orcish iron BBs) was planted by Carl and triggered during the chapter, used in the ambush against Astrid.
• Improvised explosive devices (IEDs) are constructed by Carl to trigger only for hunters, with tactical considerations for placement and conservation.
• IEDs are designed to be triggered only by hunters and are planned for selective placement along routes and around towns.
