# eight-pack_launcher

• The Eight-Pack Launcher is a missile launcher used to hold and program multiple Denial-of-Service [[Missiles]] for timed deployment during the race.
• It is used by [[dungeon crawler carl]] and [[Carl]] to fire non-explosive [[missiles]] towards rival vehicles and shields.
• The launcher is mounted on a vehicle and can fire eight [[missiles]], used to deploy Denial of Service [[missiles]] towards enemy teams such as [[Team Sparkles]] and [[One Fine Pig]].
