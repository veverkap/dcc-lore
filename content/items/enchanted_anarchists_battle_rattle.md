# Enchanted Anarchist’s Battle Rattle
A sleeveless patch jacket (artifact) that grants +1 to all base stats (scaling with compatible patches), access to the [[desperado club]] and [[Naughty Boys Employment Agency]], and +50% range/accuracy for thrown explosives. Received by [[dungeon crawler carl]] from a Platinum Spicy Box.
