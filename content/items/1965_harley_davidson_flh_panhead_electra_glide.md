# 1965_harley_davidson_flh_panhead_electra_glide

• A 1965 Harley Davidson FLH Panhead Electra Glide was presented as a prize, recognized by [[dungeon crawler carl]] and tied to personal memories.
• The [[motorcycle]] was dented and familiar-looking in the prize reveal.
• It was a collector's [[motorcycle]] found in the [[trailer]] yard with old Georgia plates.
• Carl recognized it as his father's prized possession and successfully picked it up into inventory, storing it under the in-game category [[Shitty Transportation Items]].
• The [[motorcycle]] was described disparagingly by [[Donut]] compared to the bespoke [[Goblin Copper Chopper]].
• Carl rode the [[motorcycle]] through [[Guanajay]] with [[Samantha]] on the handlebars.
• The Harley Davidson was mentioned as a road bike unsuitable for a ten-mile trip across rolling plains, contrasting with the [[Royal Chariot]].
