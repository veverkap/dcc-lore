# pole_position_upgrade

• A purchasable upgrade that grants priority positioning for descending floors, cited by [[Prepotente]] as a reason it "wouldn't matter" whether the party descended immediately.
• Claimed by [[Prepotente]] to affect order or advantage when descending floors, used as justification for immediate descent in the chapter.
