# missile_launcher

• A weapon handed out by [[dungeon crawler carl]] to [[Point Mongo]] guards, including [[Doctor El]] and "Ice Man", for town defenses against hunters and dinosaur threats.
• [[Dungeon crawler carl]] pulls the weapon from inventory and mounts it on the [[Royal Chariot]] to blow apart reanimated fragments, firing multiple [[Missiles]].
• [[Elle McGib]] uses the weapon to strike the first [[Anti-air battery]] during the raid, successfully toppling it.
• [[Dungeon crawler carl]] builds more of the weapon in the crafting room prior to town organization efforts, associated with defensive preparations following dinosaur incursions.
• The weapon is attached to the [[Royal Chariot]] by [[dungeon crawler carl]] and used to fire multiple [[missiles]] into a reanimated body-part pile to disrupt its assembly.
