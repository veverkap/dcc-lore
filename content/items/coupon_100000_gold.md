# Coupon (100,000 gold)
A high-value coupon cited by [[Damascus Steel]] as required to hire the Penis Parade crew for a two-floor mercenary contract; the [[Coupon (100,000 gold)]] is used to trigger immediate approval from [[Rosemarie]].
