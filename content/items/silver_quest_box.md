# silver_quest_box

• A quest reward item promised by the system for completing "The Sex Workers Who Fell From the Heavens" quest.
• Reward item received by [[dungeon crawler carl]] as a result of the "CockBlock!" achievement, granted for assassinating an NPC whose existence was required for another crawler's quest.
• The promised reward listed in the "New Quest. Levi is On the Menu!" announcement for successfully preventing [[Levi the Seventh]]'s devouring.
• Declared reward for successfully completing the [[New Quest. Levi is On the Menu!]] System Announcement.
• A quest reward awarded when the party accepts the "Stay out of city hall" quest.
• Reward promised for completing the system quest to free [[Wynne]]; listed in the New Quest system announcement that appears when the party discovers the chained gnome.
