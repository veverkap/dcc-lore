# nipple_ring

* Grants the [[Black Nimbus]] spell upon acquisition.
* Glowing [[gold]] nipple [[ring]] that grants the [[Quarter Fall]] effect, reducing falling speed to one quarter of normal and improving survivability from falls.
* One of four magical nipple rings recovered as loot, granting +5 to the [[Drink Mixing]] skill and considered sellable for value.
* Magical nipple [[ring]] that allows the wearer to perceive the average selling value of a non-unique item without touching it.
* A set of four distinct magical nipple rings recovered as loot, including variants that grant +5 to [[Drink Mixing]], reveal average selling value, grant the "Quarter Fall" effect, and provide a constitution bonus.
* Small nipple [[ring]] providing a significant constitution boost (+10 points and +5% to constitution for a small [[ring]]).
