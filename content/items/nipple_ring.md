# Nipple Rings (loot)
A set of four distinct magical nipple rings recovered as loot. Variants include: (1) a large ring granting +5 to Drink Mixing, (2) a ring that reveals average selling value of non-unique items, (3) a glowing gold ring granting the "Quarter Fall" effect (reduces falling speed to one quarter), and (4) a nickel-sized ring granting a constitution bonus (+% to Constitution). Central to a bargaining scene where [[dungeon crawler carl]] persuades [[Donut]] to wear one.
