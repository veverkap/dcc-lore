# Baby Backpack (quadruped carrier)
A pink backpack item from the [[Gold Adventurer Box]] that only carries quadrupeds (meant for [[Donut]]); it allows the carrier to use spells like [[Phase]] and [[Oozy]] to transport the passenger.
