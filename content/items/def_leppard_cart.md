# def_leppard_cart

• A named, powered [[cart]]/vehicle used by crawlers to manipulate encounters, central to [[dungeon crawler carl]]'s plan to reopen avenues and bait province bosses into the [[Portal]].
• The specific named [[Interdiction Cart]] chosen and transported into the main chamber of [[Station 36]]; used as the platform for the downward [[Portal to the Abyss]].
• An interdiction/[[portal]]-equipped rail [[cart]] ([[portal]]-facing) that [[dungeon crawler carl]]'s group seeks to retrieve and reposition to the employee line to manipulate encounters and [[teleport]] enemies (or bosses) to the [[Abyss]].
• An interdiction/[[portal]]-enabled [[cart]] ([[train]]-[[cart]]) the party recovers and uses to [[teleport]] creatures via the [[portal]].
• The party's second interdiction transit [[cart]] (formerly called the "Q" [[cart]]) dragged behind the [[E cart]] and used to sandwich the group between two abyss-tuned portals for protection on the [[Zomp line]].
• The specific interdiction [[cart]] chosen by [[dungeon crawler carl]]'s group (named the [[Def Leppard cart]]) that was shrunken with [[Zhang]]'s [[wand]] and hoisted into station 36.
• A steam/track-based interdiction/[[portal]] [[cart]] (also called the [[portal]] [[cart]]) used to position a [[portal]] along tracks, requiring physical manipulation to change tracks or employee-line orientation; core to Carl's escape plan.
