# Vehicle selection wheel
A giant spinning wheel in the garage that randomly selects the vehicle silhouette assigned to each team. Displays many Earth-based vehicle silhouettes (school bus, Hummer, Zamboni, box truck/food truck, etc.) and pauses/resumes during the briefing and countdown.
