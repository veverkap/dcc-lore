# vehicle_selection_wheel

• A giant spinning wheel in the garage that randomly selects the vehicle silhouette assigned to each team.
• Displays many Earth-based vehicle silhouettes, including school bus, Hummer, Zamboni, box [[truck]]/food [[truck]], etc.
• Pauses and resumes during the briefing and countdown.
