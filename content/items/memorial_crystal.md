# memorial_crystal

Here is the consolidated markdown list:

• A cult artifact associated with the [[Guild of Suffering]], similar to soul crystals, tied to divine-suffering artifacts like Carl's [[ring]].
• A rare, high-cost crystal created by fallen gods and demigods, storing god-tier-level spells and knowledge, which can be charged by installing it on gear or wearing it and killing enemies to extract its information.
• A loot item taken from [[Queen Imogen of the High Elves]]'s body by [[Osvaldo]], part of ongoing quest threads and tensions between crawler groups.
• A mysterious item tied to the death of a god, identified by [[Pater Coal]] as necessary to advance the [[Geyrun]] investigation, and carried by another crawler.
• A physical trinket seen worn by [[Osvaldo]] on the broadcast, noted as visible in the [[saferoom]] jukebox footage.
• A keepsake in [[Prepotente]]'s possession tied to the murder of [[Geyrun]] and referenced as part of the interconnected mysteries the narrator is trying to parse.
• A high-value, grape-sized glowing gem in the prize case (cost: 100 hands), created by fallen gods/demigods, containing god-tier spells and knowledge, which can be charged to extract information by installing it on gear and killing things, desired by [[Mordecai]].
• A divine item that forms when a god has died, a specific memorial crystal tied to [[Apito]] (despite her being alive) is a [[key]] object the protagonist needs to advance the Emberus quest.
• A memorial crystal named Apito that fell from [[Crawler Osvaldo]]'s hands and is now in possession of [[Prepotente]], tied to a quest update and containing secrets Emberus wants retrieved.
• A rare artifact item taken from [[Imogen]]’s body by [[Osvaldo]], tied to a quest about the goddess Apito and a murder investigation (Quest: Find Out Who Killed My Son).
• A world-generated loot object created by system announcement after the goddess [[Ysalte]]'s death, promised to be available on a future floor and to contain significant riches.
• A glowing amber crystal named "Emberus" that stores memories and spells, stolen from [[Prepotente]] by a god and described as providing protection against [[Scolopendra]]'s attacks.
• A quest item referenced repeatedly by [[Osvaldo]], multiple memorial crystals were collected but reported corrupted or "wrong" by the god [[Tupa]], fueling a new quest to determine why the crystals aren't functioning.
• An item referenced by [[Prepotente]] that [[Carl]] hopes to see, mentioned in passing near the chapter's end as a likely personal or commemorative object.
• A small memorial crystal associated with the deity [[Apito]] that is supposed to charge when kills occur, it is described as 0% charged in this chapter, worn by [[Prepotente]] and previously carried by [[Osvaldo]], the crystal inexplicably activated during the release of [[Yarilo]].
• An item reported by [[Prepotente]] to provide protection to those in the same building or mount, the crystal is partially charged (11.1% in the chapter) and is cited as the reason some players avoided transformation.
• An artifact looted by [[Osvaldo]] and later stolen by [[Prepotente]], central to multiple quests involving [[Carl]], [[Prepotente]], and [[Osvaldo]].
• An amber-hued glowing memorial crystal labeled "Emberus" that stores memories and spells, stolen by a god from [[Prepotente]] and later recovered by [[Carl]] from the dirt.
• An artifact in [[Britney Proskurina]]’s possession said to contain or be tied to [[Ysalte]], must be placed in a plinth at locations like the [[Basilica]] or [[Vulgar Temple]] to resolve its influence.
• A system-generated loot artifact spawned after the death of [[Ysalte]], promised to be available to looters on a future floor with great riches.
