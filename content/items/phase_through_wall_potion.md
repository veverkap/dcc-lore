# phase_through_wall_potion

• Allows passage through wood, stone, or metal walls
• Considered dangerous and recommended only for extreme emergencies (per [[Mordecai]])
• A risky short-duration potion allowing the user to pass through qualifying solid, non-biological surfaces for nine seconds
• Complex mechanics explained by [[Mordecai]] (duration + cooldown quirks)
