# Enchanted Collar Charm of the Effete Bourgeoisie
A settlement-management charm that marks the wearer as a settlement leader and holds jewels representing owned towns. The charm on [[Donut]] contains the [[Plain Turquoise]] gem (for [[Point Mongo]]) and grants stats, tax income, and the skill [[Snitch]].

# [[Enchanted Collar Charm of the Effete Bourgeoisie]]
A town-management collar charm used by [[Donut]] to exercise leadership over [[Point Mongo]]. Grants settlement-linked benefits: attached gems represent owned settlements, provide stats (e.g., +8 Dexterity, +4 Intelligence for the [[Plain Turquoise]] gem), a tax stipend, and a built-in [[Snitch]] skill.
