# enchanted_collar_charm_of_the_effete_bourgeoisie

• A settlement-management charm that marks the wearer as a settlement leader and holds jewels representing owned towns.
• Grants stats, tax income, and the skill [[Snitch]].
• The charm on [[Donut]] contains the [[Plain Turquoise]] gem (for [[Point Mongo]]).
• Grants settlement-liked benefits: attached gems represent owned settlements, provide stats (e.g., +8 Dexterity, +4 Intelligence for the [[Plain Turquoise]] gem), a tax stipend, and a built-in [[Snitch]] skill.
