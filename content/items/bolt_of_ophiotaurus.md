# bolt_of_ophiotaurus

* A rare [[crossbow]] [[bolt]] (1 of 100) awarded from a benefactor box, functions as a normal [[bolt]] but pauses a deity's [[invulnerability]] for 15 seconds if shot into their eye.
* Received from [[princess formidable of the skull empire]]'s benefactor box.
* Used by [[Katia]] to help kill a god.
* Reusable as long as it remains intact.
* Obtained from a benefactor box tied to [[princess formidable of the skull empire]] of the [[skull clan]].
* Fired by [[Donut]] and pierced [[Emberus]]'s eye socket, helping to remove his [[invulnerability]].
* Used by [[Katia]] to kill a goddess.
* Contributed to [[Paz Lo]]'s fate.
* Recovered from a benefactor box on the fourth floor.
* Loaded into the [[Scorpion's Bite]] and fired by [[Donut]], contributing to pinning [[Emberus]].
