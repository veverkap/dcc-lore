# time_extend

* A utility card that extends the active timer of a [[totem]] summon, adding 60 seconds in the chapter.
* A consumable card that adds seconds to a summoned [[totem]]'s countdown, used on [[Geraldo]] to add 70 seconds to his timer.
* A card that adds extra seconds to a [[totem]]'s countdown, used to give [[raul the crab]] an additional 70 seconds.
* A utility card that alters card timing/draw intervals, affecting [[Donut]]'s card draw cadence.
* A generic utility card found in the hydra's deck used to extend battle durations.
* A consumable card that extends timing effects, used by [[dungeon crawler carl]] to prolong the deckmaster/control window.
* A utility card item that adds time to a summoned [[totem]]'s active timer, with multiple uses stacking to lengthen a [[totem]]'s presence in the arena.
* A utility card type found in piles gifted by [[Katia]], used for deck and [[totem]] management.
* A utility card included in the party's deck to alter battle timing, potentially unnecessary for the mock simulation but retained for realism.
