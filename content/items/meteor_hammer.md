# meteor_hammer

• A melee weapon wielded by [[Maxim]], described as a flaming ball attached to a chain, with significant damage potential.
• Wielded by the [[Popov]] brothers, pictured in an animated autograph photo showing them fighting flaming hedgehog creatures in a derailing [[train]] car.
