# worm_fulcrum

• A sentient necro staff that summons reanimation effects, creating [[Zombie Turkey]]s.
• Functions as both a [[totem]] and persistent mystic card.
• Has its own health and timer.
• Can summon a grotesque root/feather tree.
• Can corrupt and necromantically alter corpses, marked by the inventory label "Touched by the Worm Fulcrum".
