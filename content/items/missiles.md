# missiles

• Explosive projectiles assigned to the [[Missile Launcher]] on the [[Royal Chariot]] and detonated to slow the reassembly of reanimated fragments.
• General ordinance used to bombard the distant [[Madness castle]], including anti-personnel variants and salvo strikes that suppressed the target's [[shield]].
• Ammo loaded into the [[Missile Launcher]] on the [[Royal Chariot]]. Fired by [[dungeon crawler carl]] to fragment and attempt to slow the reformation of reanimated composite minions.
