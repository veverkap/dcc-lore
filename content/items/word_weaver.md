# word_weaver

• A spell referenced by [[Sister Ines Quiteria]] that benefits from her charisma.
• Used by [[Sister Ines Quiteria]] to charm and control the level-125 boss [[Madre de Aguas]] and nearby mobs, amplified by the [[Book of Rhymes]].
• Enhanced by [[Book of Rhymes]] (and the [[nebulars]]) to control a level-125 boss and nearby creatures, central to the beach's mass-charm effect.
