# heat_sink

• A regular upgrade option to mitigate loss of [[containment]] and protect against high-heat hazards during the race.
• A sub-function/button of the [[Bubble Buddy]] [[shield]] that lowers internal temperature (indicated by a gauge), activated to make the factory's heat bearable for the vehicle's occupants.
