# Town upgrade coupons
Consumable items that can be applied to expand or improve a settlement like [[Shanty Town]] or the [[Princess Posse]]’s base. [[dungeon crawler carl]] and [[Donut]] each received one after a quest and discussed using them to upgrade the shanty ring.
