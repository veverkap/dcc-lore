# Gold magic item box
A high-tier loot container awarded to early achievers (e.g., [[Donut]] for reaching level three in [[Magic Missile]]). In this chapter it yielded 25 [[mana potion]] and the [[Talisman of the Slate Butterfly]] for [[Donut]].
