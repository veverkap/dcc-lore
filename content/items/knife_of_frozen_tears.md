# knife_of_frozen_tears

• A small Sylvan crystal blade given by [[Ren]] and used to instantly kill and reset [[Garret]].
• Described as not very sharp but lethal to certain enemies and tied to the [[Sylvan crystal]] material.
• Specifically effective against certain weaknesses, capable of killing [[Garret]] due to its sylvan crystal composition.
