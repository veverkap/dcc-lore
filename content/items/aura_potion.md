# Aura Potion
Yellow, bubbling consumable potion that grants a one-hour "Closed Off" effect, making the drinker immune to external aura effects (both Blessings and Dreads) for its duration; used by operatives like [[Prepotente]] and [[dungeon crawler carl]].
