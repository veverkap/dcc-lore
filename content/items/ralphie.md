# ralphie

• A card referenced as an active persistent effect that mutes ally totems' casting, mentioned in the dispel system announcement during the fight.
• Deployed by [[Tom]] as a persistent mystic card, disabling allied active magic and muting minions/totems on Carl's team while active.
• Its presence flipped the fight dynamics until it was destroyed when [[Tom]] was defeated.
