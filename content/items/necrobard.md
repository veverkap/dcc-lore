# NecroBard
[[NecroBard]] is a hybrid class combining Bard and Necromancer traits, granting instant access to a level-5 entertainment skill, Turn Undead (Lv3), Panty Dropper (Lv3), stat bonuses (+5 Con/Cha/Int), and other necromancy-related effects.
