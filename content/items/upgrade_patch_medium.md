# upgrade_patch_medium

• A black denim-liked patch with a goat-head symbol (Midnight Epicure) grants protection against vampirism and lycanthropy, +25% magical damage vs. sapient undead, and +1 to all stats; removal destroys the patch.
• An embroidered patch depicting a burning book grants +15% Intelligence and the "Book Burner" benefit; removing the patch destroys it.
• A medium patch made from rare Spider Reaper Minion thread silk boosts stats (e.g., +11% Constitution, +6% explosive damage, +2 Dangerous [[Explosives]] Handling Skill).
• A medium upgrade patch (black denim with goat-head icon) awarded by [[plenty]] grants protection against vampirism, protection against lycanthropy, +25% magical damage vs. sapient undead, and +1 to all stats for Carl; removing it destroys the patch.
