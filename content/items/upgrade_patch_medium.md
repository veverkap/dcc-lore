# Upgrade Patch. Medium.
A black denim-like patch screen-printed with a goat-head (the [[Midnight Epicure]] symbol). When affixed to eligible garments it grants protection against vampirism and lycanthropy, +25% magical damage vs. sapient undead, and +1 to all stats; removal destroys the patch.
