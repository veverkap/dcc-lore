# touch_of_maw-loo_snowdrop_curse

• A named spell/curse that previously affected [[Penelope]] and is also known as the [[Snowdrop Curse]].
• It can cause NPCs to fall helplessly in love with the afflicted target.
• Part of a larger backstory involving multiple gods and NPCs.
