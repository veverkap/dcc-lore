# valtay_neural_enhancement_pills

• Neural-enhancer pills tied to the [[Valtay]] race, considered regulated and unfair if obtained as found loot.

• A regulated upgrade item that grants neural enhancements associated with the [[Valtay]].
• Part of the Residual's stash and central to the dispute over unfair enrichment and item seizure by [[orren the liaison]].
