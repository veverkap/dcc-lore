# magical_fervor

• A temporary magical [[buff]]/status that appears over [[Signet]] when she eats the acorn; manifests visibly and strengthens her for ritual actions.
• A temporary [[buff]] that triples mana, increases strength, forces loud behavior, and grants immunity to [[Fear]] effects.
