# upgrade_coupons

• Consumable items used to upgrade equipment or crafting tables, such as the [[Makeup Table]] or [[Alchemy Table]].
• Their expenditure is a point of contention between [[Donut]] and [[Mordecai]].
• [[Katia]] spent two on the [[Makeup Table]], upsetting [[Mordecai]].
• [[Katia]] uses two coupons to reach level three on the [[Makeup Table]].
