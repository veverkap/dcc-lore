# alchemy_table

• A portable inventory item that can be deployed as a clamshell-like cover for protection.
• A placeable personal-space item that is invulnerable in combat.
• A crafting station placed in the [[Crafting Space]] for handling alchemical supplies, providing menu options and upgrades.
• A workstation used by [[Mordecai]] for potion and alchemical crafting, with equipment and supplies that can be scattered when mounts or combat occur nearby.
• A crafting bench used by [[Mordecai]] for potion/alchemical production, with the ability to produce specialized materials and [[potions]].
