# recharge_scroll

• A scroll cast by [[Princess Donut]] to recharge [[Big Tina]]'s [[wand]], allowing the [[wand]] to emit further charm sparkles and cast [[The Recital]].
• Used by [[Donut]] to recharge [[Big Tina]]'s [[wand]] so it could cast its performer spell during the show.
