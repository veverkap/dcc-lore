# homecoming_queen

• The Homecoming Queen is a named starship used by Rosetta and others.
• The ship's captain is willing to follow orders to shoot down an orc yacht to complete a transaction.
• The Homecoming Queen is boarded by families planning to flee towards the [[Plenty system]].
