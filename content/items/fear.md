# fear

* [[Fear]] is a spell granted by the [[Compensated Anarchist]] class, costing three points to cast.
* It is a combat spell recommended by [[Mordecai]] for frequent use in battles on the [[Over City]].
* The spell causes mobs to flee or suffer dexterity penalties, and is central to the duo's initial combat play named "Panic".
* It is less effective against certain upgraded mobs like the Brain Boilers.
* The spell can be used tactically by [[dungeon crawler carl]] to manipulate encounters.
* It is a debuff spell cast by [[dungeon crawler carl]] to try to disrupt [[gore-gore the mantaur]]'s charge.
* The spell can be imbued into items or [[explosives]], and is classified as an enchantment that affects targets within the blast radius.
* It is an offensive spell that [[dungeon crawler carl]] is training, and can be upgraded to level 10 by a potion from a [[Platinum Asshole’s Box]].
* At level 10, it gains a 2% chance to paralyze and can be cast in a wide area.
* The spell can be applied to items (smoke curtains, [[explosives]]) but enchantments risk the "Dud" status.
* It is a spell used by [[dungeon crawler carl]] during combat to influence enemy behavior, and is one of the offensive/support spells seen in the chapter's skirmish.
* The spell affects dexterity and is part of established combat tactics.
* It is a level-11 spell that forces turkeys to flee, and is used multiple times during the barn collapse to control hostile NPCs.
