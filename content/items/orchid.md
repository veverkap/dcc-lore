# orchid

• [[Orchid]] is a quest item that Carl must secure for Katia, and its possession factors into Huanxin's bargain and the team's plans.
• When consumed, the orchid triggers goddess-driven options (transfer to the 12th floor, [[buff]], or impregnation) and is pivotal to the squad's plan to resolve the [[Crown of the Sepsis Whore]] issue.
• The orchid is tied to goddess-driven mechanics on higher floors and determines Katia's fate when eaten.
• The orchid is a quest-related item given to Katia (via a box opening after killing [[Astrid]]) to enable Huanxin to fulfill her promise and aid Katia.
• Eating the orchid retrieved by Katia triggers relocation to the 12th floor, NPC conversion, or other divine interventions by [[Eileithyia]]/[[Huanxin]] depending on choices.
