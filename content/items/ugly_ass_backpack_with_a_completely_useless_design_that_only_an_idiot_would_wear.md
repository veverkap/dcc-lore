# ugly_ass_backpack_with_a_completely_useless_design_that_only_an_idiot_would_wear

• A large, open-top equippable backpack crafted by [[dungeon crawler carl]] that allows [[Katia]] to take on massive transformation mass by equipping the pack.
• When equipped, it contributes mass to [[Katia]] and can hold internal contents recognized by the system.
• The backpack integrates contents into [[Katia]]'s mass and enables massive shape changes.
• It stabilizes and allows a portion of contained metal to become part of [[Katia]]'s transformation mass.
• Critical to the party's temporary mass-exploit strategy.
