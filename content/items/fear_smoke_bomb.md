# fear_smoke_bomb

• A charisma-augmented smoke device used by [[Donut]] to sow terror in nearby foes; corkscrews into the chaos and applies [[fear]] effects.
• Applies [[fear]] and terror effects to enemies in the area; part of the group's crowd-control toolkit during the fight.
