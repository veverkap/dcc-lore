# minion_army

• A tome received by [[Donut]] from a Beguiler-style box, teaching a spell that converts hostile enemies to fight for the caster, costing 50 spell points to cast.
• A spellbook/spell that charms groups of enemies, potentially flipping a percentage of intelligent mobs to fight for the caster.
• A high-level mass-charm spell that targets groups of enemies, turning some intelligent mobs to fight for the caster, with a five-minute cast time, long cooldown, and low initial conversion chance.
• A battlefield sabotage spell that creates temporary minions with a long casting time and limited duration.
• A level-one war spell that converts a small percentage of enemies in the area against their allies, with enhancements from [[Imani]] and [[Samantha]].
• A summoning spell that can be used to turn enemies against their allies.
• A spell that can be used to turn zarogoths against their allies, but would freeze the caster in place for five minutes.
