# Minion Army
Spellbook/spell that charms groups of enemies. Cast on a group to potentially flip a percentage of intelligent mobs to fight for the caster. At level one: five-minute cast time, five-hour cooldown, 30-meter base AoE, two percent chance per mob to convert (base), effectiveness scales with caster Charisma. Requires casting in place and is situational; [[Mordecai]] warned about its limited practical use.
