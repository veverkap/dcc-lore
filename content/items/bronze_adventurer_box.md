# bronze_adventurer_box

• A basic adventurer box given to [[dungeon crawler carl]] as part of the [[Empty Pockets]] achievement.
• Contents include [[healing potion]] X2 and [[fingerless gloves]].
• Contains consumables such as a [[Torch]] and [[Healing Potion]]s.
• Awarded to both [[dungeon crawler carl]] and [[Princess Donut]] after combat.
• Common loot box received by [[dungeon crawler carl]] and many crawlers, containing low-tier rewards and consumables.
• Low-tier box type frequently received among Donut's forty boxes, mostly producing mundane clothing and healing scrolls.
• Common loot box (bronze) given to many crawlers after fights, generic adventurer rewards referenced by [[dungeon crawler carl]] and others.
