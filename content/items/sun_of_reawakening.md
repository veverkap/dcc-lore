# sun_of_reawakening

• A summoned magical ball of red-yellow [[light]], described as a flaming ruby skull, formed during [[Asojano]]'s ritual.
• A glowing red-orange skull-shaped gem at the center of the thorn room, used to trigger reawakening/ascension effects by placing it on a repaired [[shrine]], such as atop [[Yemaya]].
• A temporary magical orb/gemion formed by [[Asojano]]'s ritual inside [[La Iglesia de los Olvidados]], described as a small flaming ruby-shaped skull that will only last a short time and is central to the [[shrine]]-repair ritual.
• A glowing skull-shaped gem used to reactivate shrines in the thorn room, central artifact that the party must place on a [[shrine]] to resurrect [[Yemaya]].
