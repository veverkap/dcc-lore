# Summoning Box
The artifact used to summon the storm deities [[Dodola]] and [[Adad]]. It was infused with a chain-lightning potion to attract a sponsored god and is central to the party's ritual to prime the [[Puzzle Disk]] and open the [[Stairwell]] to the [[Eleventh Floor]].

# [[Summoning Box]]
The [[Summoning Box]] (referred to elsewhere as a summoning cube) is the device used by [[Mordecai]] to call the storm deities to the tenth floor. It was primed and infused with a chain-lightning potion to draw the sponsored god [[Dodola]].
