# summoning_box

• The Summoning Box is an artifact used to summon storm deities, including [[Dodola]] and [[Adad]].
• It was infused with a chain-[[lightning]] potion to attract a sponsored god and is central to the party's ritual to prime the [[Puzzle Disk]] and open the [[Stairwell]] to the [[Eleventh Floor]].
• The Summoning Box was used by [[Mordecai]] to call the storm deities to the tenth floor.
• It attracts sponsored gods like [[Dodola]] and AI gods like [[Adad]].
