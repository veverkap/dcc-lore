# tiara

• Accessory commonly worn by [[Donut]] as part of her persona; noted as absent at times during the chapter and used to signal Donut's on-screen costume.
• Decorative equipment piece belonging to [[Donut]] (previously destroyed on the show) noted in social interactions; mentioned as a conversation point with [[Elle McGib]].
• Sponsored piece of equipment in a benefactor box (one of the clearly-understood gifts mentioned alongside the scroll and sheet music).
• Decorative item given to [[Tina]] by [[Princess Donut]] that helps calm the child's behavior during the caravan escort.
• Headpiece tied to exit/attendant mechanics: both [[Princess Donut]] and [[Katia]] had worn tiaras that affected who could leave floors and who could become attendants.
• Personal accessory mentioned as a potential recipient of drained power from [[Donut]]'s Gurkha [[hat]]; an item of personal equipment in the garage.
