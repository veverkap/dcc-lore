# tiara_of_mana_genita

• A white [[tiara]] that raises [[Donut]]'s intelligence; currently worn by [[Donut]] as her main headpiece.
• Existing [[tiara]] previously kept by [[Donut]]; mentioned as an item the party retained before buying the new citrine-encrusted [[tiara]].
• A worn equipment piece on [[Donut]] that raises intelligence.
• Replaced [[Donut]]'s earlier crown and is central to plans to have a jeweler integrate a [[Flawless Jeweler’s Gem]] into it.
• An existing [[tiara]] held by the party (retained after a recent battle); referenced as a potentially useful enchanted item distinct from the newly purchased [[Enchanted [[Tiara]] of the Inebriated Dragonfly]].
