# find_crawler

* A skill owned by [[Frank]] and associated with [[Maggie My]], locating crawlers within a large radius, with a legendary skill potion to upgrade it to level 15.
* A skill potion that adds the names of nearby crawlers to the user's map and provides sorting/filtering functionality, currently limited in detection capability at low levels.
* A skill/ability used by [[Katia]] to locate hidden or relevant crawlers/loot among corpses, instrumental in finding the nodling yearlings.
* A skill/ability used by [[dungeon crawler carl]] to locate other crawlers, revealing the position of entities like [[Sister Ines]].
* A tracking ability used to locate specific crawlers such as [[Louis]] within the castle interior.
* A consumable skill potion effect (level three in this chapter) that marks nearby crawlers on the map and allows sorting/filtering, does not yet reveal stealth users at low levels.
* A skill/ability used by [[Katia]] to locate hidden or relevant crawlers/loot among corpses, instrumental in finding the nodling yearlings.
* A spell referenced by [[Mordecai]] that locates other crawlers, used as a point of comparison when explaining the effect of the [[Eye in the Sky]] scroll.
* A skill/ability used by [[dungeon crawler carl]] to locate other crawlers, functionally treated like a utility spell in play.
* A legendary skill potion reportedly given by [[Maestro]] that can max out the "Find Crawler" skill and was part of a dispute between [[Frank Q.]] and [[Maggie]].
* A detection skill possessed by [[Katia]] that can reveal sneaking or approaching crawlers, cited as part of the group's defensive planning for pursuing the subterranean chamber.
* A legendary potion mentioned as gifted by the Maestro that can max out the "Find Crawler" skill; [[Frank]] and [[Maggie]] debated using/selling it before entering the dungeon.
* A skill potion that grants the drinker Level Five in the Find Crawler Skill (won't work if the skill is already at or above level five), consumed by [[dungeon crawler carl]] in this chapter.
