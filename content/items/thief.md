# thief

• A mystic card that steals opponent [[cards]]; noted as useless in the mock battle due to simulation constraints.
• A card/item used by [[Donut]] to steal a card from [[Amayon]]'s deck; enables immediate transfer of a [[totem]] into the caster's hand.
• A mystic card used by [[Donut]] to steal an opponent's card mid-summon (used successfully against [[miss sharp elbows]] to steal a [[totem]]).
• A card used by [[Donut]] that steals a non-summoned card from an opponent's deck and adds it to the caster's hand; played against [[Amayon]] early in the encounter.
