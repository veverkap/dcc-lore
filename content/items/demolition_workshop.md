# demolition_workshop

• A menu/tab accessed at the [[sappers table]] that allows examination and deconstruction of [[explosives]], shows schematics, yields, and decay under stress environments.
• Allows [[dungeon crawler carl]] to remove [[bombs]] from inventory, view schematics, yields, and decay rates, and experiment with deconstructing [[explosives]] like hob-lobbers.
• Used by Carl to study and disassemble [[bombs]].
