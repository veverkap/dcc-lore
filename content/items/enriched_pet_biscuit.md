# enriched_pet_biscuit

• A refined, non-random variant of the Enhanced Pet Biscuit that imparts a known, guaranteed result.
• An item fed to a mundane animal that grants sapience and party-companion status, as seen with the Yorkshire pig, [[Penelope 3]], and linking her to [[Carl]]'s party.
• A unique, non-randomified enhanced pet biscuit variant that guarantees the "Paramour" outcome when consumed, received from a [[Celestial Box]] in the chapter.
• A named Enriched Pet Biscuit in [[Prepotente]]'s possession, which effects on non-sapient pets include raising Intelligence and Wisdom to at least 3, resetting level to 1, assigning crawler status to dungeon-born pets, causing a physical Party Companion subtree two-B transformation, and imparting a permanent charm.
• A consumable item used as the payload in the combined projectile that defeats [[Scolopendra]].
• One of the three Enriched Pet Biscuits from the celestial box, which converts a creature into a dungeon pet, reverting them to juvenile form and allowing them to gain levels, skills, spells, and upgrades.
