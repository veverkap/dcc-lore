# Enriched Pet Biscuit
A refined, non-random variant of the Enhanced Pet Biscuit that imparts a known, guaranteed result. In this chapter, three distinct Enriched Pet Biscuits arrive in a celestial box and are contrasted with run-of-the-mill enhanced biscuits.

# The Paramour (Enriched Pet Biscuit)
A named Enriched Pet Biscuit in [[Prepotente]]'s possession. Effects on non-sapient pets include raising Intelligence and Wisdom to at least 3, resetting level to 1, assigning crawler status to dungeon-born pets, causing a physical Party Companion subtree two‑B transformation (upright, mascot-like form), and imparting a permanent charm that makes the consumer fall hopelessly in love with the feeder.

# Nothing Special (Enriched Pet Biscuit)
One of the three Enriched Pet Biscuits from the celestial box. Classified under the Pet subtree; it converts a creature into a dungeon pet, reverting them to juvenile form and allowing them to gain levels, skills, spells, and upgrades.
