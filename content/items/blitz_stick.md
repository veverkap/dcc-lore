# blitz_stick

• A drug item sold by [[Quint]] that temporarily increases Intelligence by +5 if eaten, and has a 15% chance of increasing permanent Intelligence by +1 if smoked.
• A consumable/edible drug item exchanged as a toll in [[Hump Town]].
• A drugged hallucination device used by [[Katia]] to replay alternate outcomes, previously salvaged from a victim and described as addictive and reality-altering.
• A small incendiary/explosive implement used by [[Volteeg]] to ignite an [[Energy ball]].
• A consumable drug item traded in town, sourced from the Desperado Club pharmacist [[Quint]].
