# puddle_jumper

Here is the consolidated markdown list:

* A teleportation spell learned by [[Princess Donut]] that allows her to [[teleport]] herself and up to three others within line of sight; costs 20 mana, has a 10-second delay and a five-hour cooldown, and scales to approach [[Teleport]]'s power at higher levels.
* A teleportation spell used by [[Donut]] to move the party instantly to [[Mordecai's Guild Room]]; has a ten-second cast delay and was used as the final escape.
* A mobility-related spell in [[Donut]]'s list (Level 3), referenced among her active abilities.
* A teleportation/transport spell used by [[Donut]] to quickly traverse between rooftops and to return to the [[Nightmare]].
* A level 17 teleportation spell/item that allows near-instant return to recently visited locations; [[Donut]] used it to [[teleport]] Carl and herself to the arena entrance.
* A spell learned by [[Donut]] (recently hit level five) that teleports the caster and adds support for jumping to higher elevations when the ground above is out of line-of-sight.
* A teleportation spell used by [[Donut]] to whisk [[dungeon crawler carl]], [[Mongo]], and [[Ricky Joe]] safely to a rooftop immediately before the [[Boom Jug]] detonated.
* A movement spell/ability used by [[Donut]] to traverse between rooftops; used to move Carl and Donut over to neighboring roofs after the explosion.
* A teleportation spell used by [[Donut]] to transport party members away (part of Plan Exit).
* A level-17 teleportation spell/item used by [[Donut]] that can return the caster to recently visited locations across great distances; used to exit the arena to the entrance.
* A spell that [[Donut]] chooses to raise to level 16 in this chapter; its higher-level effects are remarked upon with uncertainty and intrigue by companions.
