# magical_cooler_never-ending_beer

• An artifact attached to [[Radoslav]]'s van that magically refills beer and guarantees a supply between races.
• Contains regular and peach-flavored [[Busch Light]].
• Always contains three peach cans, with regular beers refilling repeatedly.
