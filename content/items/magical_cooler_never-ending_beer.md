# Magical Cooler (never-ending beer)
An artifact attached to [[Radoslav]]'s van that magically refills beer and guarantees a supply between races; described as containing regular and peach-flavored [[Busch Light]].

# Magical Cooler (Never-Ending Beer)
A magical cooler present in [[Radoslav]]'s van that refills beers automatically between races. Noted as always containing three peach cans and otherwise supplying regular beers repeatedly.
