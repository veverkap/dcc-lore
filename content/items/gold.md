# gold

• The currency used across the floors is gold, which can be dropped by [[Terror the Clown]] and is priced in gold for [[saferoom]] services, such as renting rooms at [[the belly-rubbed pug]].
• Gold coins can be looted from the remains in kobold fighting pits and appear among the small spoils taken by [[dungeon crawler carl]].
• Gold appears scattered across the shoreline, looted from corpses, described as nearly a million pieces.
• [[Edict]] drops a large quantity of gold coins when auto-ejected, burying [[dungeon crawler carl]] to his ankles and becoming a contested haul in the [[Pub in Alucarda]].
