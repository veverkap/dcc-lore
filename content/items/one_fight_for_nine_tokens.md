# one_fight_for_nine_tokens

• A battered vending-machine-style device in the [[trailer]] yard has a silhouette and the slogan "One Fight for Nine [[Tokens]]."
• It dispenses access/[[token]]-based mechanics: once activated, players can leave the zone but need nine [[tokens]] to reactivate, and only nine [[tokens]] exist in the area.
• Activation prompts the area countdown and forces viewing of an event.
• It interacts with the [[Fight Countdown Timer]] and the [[Spliced-in Time Rectangle]].
