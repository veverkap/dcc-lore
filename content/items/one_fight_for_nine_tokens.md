# One Fight for Nine Tokens
A battered vending-machine-style device in the trailer yard adorned with a silhouette and the slogan "One Fight for Nine Tokens." It dispenses access/token-based mechanics: once activated players may leave the zone but need nine tokens to reactivate, and only nine tokens exist in the area. Activation prompts the area countdown and forces viewing of an event. Interacts with the [[Fight Countdown Timer]] and the [[Spliced-in Time Rectangle]].
