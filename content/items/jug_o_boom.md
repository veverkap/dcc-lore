# jug_o_boom

• A thrown explosive used to clear swarming juvenile krakarens from a platform, treated as a precious consumable [[grenade]]-like item.
• A consumable explosive thrown off the [[train]] to disrupt swarming juvenile krakarens, treated as a precious and limited resource.
• An invented explosive device assembled by [[Dungeon Crawler Carl]] that boosted his [[explosives]] skills and earned him the [[Dungeonpreneur]] achievement.
• An item/weapon associated with [[Dungeon Crawler Carl]], linked to cash-out mechanics in the [[ACCOUNTS]] [[interface]].
• A throwable explosive used to disrupt swarming juvenile [[Krakaren]] as they passed platforms, treated as a precious consumable.
• A consumable explosive tossed off the side of a [[cart]] near [[Station 24]] to disrupt swarming juvenile krakarens, described as "precious" by the narrator.
