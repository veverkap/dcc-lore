# walk_on_air

• Allows short-duration walking on air
• Obtainable via the [[Enchanted Toe [[Ring]] of the Well-Balanced]] at a certain threshold
• Implicated in [[Li Jun]]'s combat style
• Allows ascending through space
• Used by [[dungeon crawler carl]] to reach tree branches and escape the rising skunk mist
• A movement skill previously lost and later recovered by a character
• Previously stolen from [[Li Jun]] and used by [[dungeon crawler carl]]
• Used by [[dungeon crawler carl]] to make long jumps across tables in the [[Surgical Theater of Architect Houston]]
• Used in combination with [[levitate]] to attempt reaching the edge of the city
