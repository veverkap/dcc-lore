# Unknown Potion (Lemonade)
A yellow concoction presented by [[Bonnie]] as "lemonade" (displayed in a pitcher and glasses). Initially appears as an item/potion in the UI but is later revealed by the [[Wild Goose Chase]] achievement to be ordinary lemonade (a misdirection during the [[Borough Boss]] fight).

# Unknown Potion (Lemonade)
An inventory-listed "Unknown Potion" presented by [[Bonnie]] as "Lemonade" and sold at her stand. Initially suspected to be a special potion but revealed by the [[Wild Goose Chase]] achievement/system message to be ordinary lemonade — a deliberate misdirection that influences player choices during the fight with [[Denise]].
