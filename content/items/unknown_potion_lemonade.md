# unknown_potion_lemonade

• A yellow concoction presented by [[Bonnie]] as "lemonade" (displayed in a pitcher and glasses).
• Initially appears as an item/potion in the UI, but is later revealed to be ordinary lemonade.
• Sold at [[Bonnie]]'s stand as an "Unknown Potion" and presented as "Lemonade".
• A deliberate misdirection that influences player choices during the fight with [[Denise]].
