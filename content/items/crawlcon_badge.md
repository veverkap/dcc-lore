# crawlcon_badge

• A physical badge with a red lanyard that [[dungeon crawler carl]] is instructed to wear, which activates a new [[interface]] tab and identifies him as a participant at [[CrawlCon]].
• Enables Carl to see floating names over crawlers’ heads even when menu UI is unavailable.
• A physical badge with a red lanyard and a roaring demon image that [[Zev]] instructs [[dungeon crawler carl]] to wear, which is required for access and ties [[dungeon crawler carl]] to scheduled [[CrawlCon]] events.
• Event credential worn by [[dungeon crawler carl]] and the crawlers at [[CrawlCon]] that allows limited HUD-like information (e.g., floating names) even when menus are otherwise inaccessible.
• Physical/virtual reward item received in the fan box: a postcard-sized badge on a "Titan Entertainment" lanyard marking the recipient as a CrawlCon special guest, includes animated front image, scrolling ads on back, and triggers scheduled virtual events.
