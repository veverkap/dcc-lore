# escape_plan

• A skill possessed by [[dungeon crawler carl]] that allows him to perceive otherwise-hidden [[Control Sigil]]s; tied to his unique [[interface]]/modes for evasion and dungeon manipulation.
• Grants [[dungeon crawler carl]] the ability to see a blue glowing control symbol (a sigil) on the warehouse floor that others cannot perceive.
• Triggers a haptic buzz and displays hidden/additional text on items such as [[The Dungeon Anarchist's Cookbook]], indicating meta or secret interactions with certain items.
• Appends extra text to a benefactor note, revealing hidden sponsor instructions about giving [[Pawna’s Cries]] to [[Donut]].
• Makes certain control markings visible to [[dungeon crawler carl]] (e.g., the [[Control Sigil (Mechanic)]]).
• Reveals hidden text on notes and signs, allowing [[Carl]] to read its message (triggered on the anonymous note).
