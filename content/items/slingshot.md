# slingshot

• A basic ranged weapon looted from [[Unvaccinated Clurichaun Rev-Up Consultant – Level 3]] mobs, used by [[dungeon crawler carl]] to [[train]] and gain minor ranged skills (Slingshot skill, Aiming, Steady Hand).
• Typically uses small bags of rocks as ammo.
• Used by [[dungeon crawler carl]] during the fight to harry a flying laminak fairy; its use increases related skills (Aiming/Slingshot).
• Used by [[dungeon crawler carl]] to ground [[Brindled Vespas]] by hitting wings; contributes to skill progression up to level five.
