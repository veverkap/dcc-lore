# [[Prizefighter]]
Exclusive Earth class (Monk subclass) centered on pugilism and knockout abilities. Requires Pugilism level five and grants large Constitution and pugilism bonuses, plus per-mob punch gold scaling and a Silver Earth Box on selection.
