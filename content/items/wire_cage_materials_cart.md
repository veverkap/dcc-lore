# wire_cage_materials_cart

• A wheeled, chain-link materials [[cart]] found in various rooms, including the warehouse, robot room, and warehouse chamber at [[Banshee Station 116]].
• It can be lifted and carried, and is stored in inventory by [[dungeon crawler carl]].
• The [[cart]] can be used as scrap or to carry materials.
• It is described as heavy and liftable, and can be rolled down hills.
