# scroll_of_build_trench

• A consumable scroll that permits placement of a permanent trench of specified dimensions; can be combined for reinforced structures.
• Found in [[Silver Spam Box]] rewards, allowing permanent placement of a trench and combining for fortifications and front-line accessories.
• Taken from [[dungeon crawler carl]]'s inventory by [[Shi Maria]] during possession and dropped to the ground.
• Briefly manifested in [[Carl]]'s hand while controlled by [[Shi Maria]].
