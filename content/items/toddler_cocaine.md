# toddler_cocaine

• Illicit product described as low-dose, highly addictive cocaine marketed to children, also known as "Daddy's Little Snow Drops".
• Produced and protected by the [[Knockers]].
• Connected to the [[Coblyn Corporation]].
• Piled across the second factory room as a white powder that fouls vision and shields.
