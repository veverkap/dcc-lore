# Toddler Cocaine
Illicit product described as low-dose, highly addictive cocaine marketed to children (also called "Daddy’s Little Snow Drops"). Piled across the second factory room as a white powder that fouls vision and shields; produced and protected by the [[Knockers]] and connected to the [[Coblyn Corporation]].
