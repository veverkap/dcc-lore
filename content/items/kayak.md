# kayak

• Simple portable watercraft retrieved from the floating house's garage by [[dungeon crawler carl]] and used to transport [[Donut]] toward shore after the underwater encounter near the [[Necropolis of Anser]].
• Personal watercraft retrieved from the floating house's garage and used by Carl to ferry [[Donut]] back to shore away from the shark feeding frenzy.
