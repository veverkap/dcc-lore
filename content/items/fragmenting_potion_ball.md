# Fragmenting Potion Ball
A hollow, rubber-like ball that can be filled with a full potion vial and then thrown; when it breaks on impact it applies the potion's effects to mobs (and apparently NPCs), allowing ranged potion delivery in combat as manufactured by [[Mordecai]].
