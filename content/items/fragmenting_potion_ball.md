# fragmenting_potion_ball

• A hollow, [[rubber]]-like ball that can be filled with a full potion vial and thrown; when it breaks on impact, it applies the potion's effects to mobs and NPCs, allowing ranged potion delivery in combat, crafted by [[Mordecai]].
