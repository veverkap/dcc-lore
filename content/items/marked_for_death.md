# Marked for Death
A ring-granted skill detailed with in-game mechanics: allows selecting 100%-health crawlers within map range to "mark" (30s formation), granting permanent +1 points to a target's highest stat on their death (scaling every three marks), but prevents healing while a mark is active. Special floor-specific cooldown and formation rules (notably Scolopendra Lair rules and 6th/9th floor exceptions).
