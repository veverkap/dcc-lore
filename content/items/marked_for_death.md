# marked_for_death

• Allows selecting 100%-health crawlers within map range to "mark" (30s formation), granting permanent +1 points to a target's highest stat on their death (scaling every three marks), but prevents healing while a mark is active.
• Grants a five percent bonus to stats.
• Confers benefits (and risk) to its wearer; activates in ways that draw attention from hunters and factions.
• Has severe penalties (no healing while a mark is active) and complex floor-based cooldown rules.
• Is tied to a [[ring]] and can be acquired by [[dungeon crawler carl]].
• Is a draw for hunters and faction collectors on the sixth floor.
• Can be used to gain permanent stat points when crawlers die, but at the cost of no healing while a mark is active.
