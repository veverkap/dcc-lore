# rubber

• A high-level damage-reflecting spell suspected to be used by [[Lucia Mar]] that reduces incoming damage and reflects it to a chosen target, including pets.
• A damage-reflect effect that [[Lucia Mar]] can use (apparently via an item, possibly a [[ring]]), treating as an item-based defensive spell that reflects damage back to attackers.
• A spell referenced as an example of harmful magic that could have been cast on the captive woman.
• A high-level defensive spell that [[Lucia Mar]] casts to reflect damage back at attackers, functioning similarly to high-level damage-reflect mechanics and applied to both Lucia and her dogs during the ghoul-station incident.
• A damage-reflect effect that [[Lucia Mar]] possesses (likely tied to an item she wears), responsible for prior lethal results and part of the reason attackers are cautious.
