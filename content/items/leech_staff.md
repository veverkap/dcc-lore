# Leech Staff
Ifechi's distinctive weapon: a staff that summons and flings swarms of leeches to quickly drain and kill mobs. The consumed, blood-filled leeches provide edible buffs, which [[Florin]] exploits for temporary strength (synergy with his Crocodilian traits).
