# leech_staff

• A staff wielded by [[Ifechi]] that summons swarms of leeches to drain mobs and create edible, [[buff]]-granting creatures.
• The staff is pivotal in the recap footage.
• Ifechi's distinctive weapon: a staff that summons and flings swarms of leeches to quickly drain and kill mobs.
• Consumed, blood-filled leeches provide edible buffs, which [[Florin]] exploits for temporary strength (synergy with his Crocodilian traits).
