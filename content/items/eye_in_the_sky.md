# eye_in_the_sky

• A powerful detection scroll/spell that pinpoints a crawler's location and level across an area.
• Identified by [[Mordecai]] as existing in levels 5, 10, and 15.
• A level 10 version triggered the warning received by [[dungeon crawler carl]].
• Can locate crawlers (used cautiously and recommended that [[Donut]] use them).
• Classified as a rare scroll-like [[Item]] in-world.
