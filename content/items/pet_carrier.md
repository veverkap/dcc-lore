# pet_carrier

• Item used by Prepotente to [[teleport]] a jar containing sentient blood into secure [[containment]].
• Inventory item used to contain dangerous creatures like [[Scolopendra]] to prevent them from initiating final attacks.
• Inventory container that can hold additional life-support leeches and similar small consumables/items for use in hazardous environments like [[Sheol]].
• [[Carrier]] used by [[Donut]] to transport [[Mongo]] after he collapses.
• Container item used by [[Ren]] to hand the reset [[Garret]] off to [[dungeon crawler carl]].
• Inventory item for holding transformed creatures; chosen by [[Carl]] as the means to contain [[Scolopendra]] to prevent her free attacks.
• Portable [[containment]] Item used by [[Carl]] to keep [[Rend]] secured except during battles.
