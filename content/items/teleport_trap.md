# teleport_trap

* A two-part trap system, deployed by [[dungeon crawler carl]], teleports summoned NPC guards into a locked closet on the top floor to neutralize [[Astrid]]'s "Summon Guard" ability.
* General class of traps that [[teleport]] targets when both halves are correctly deployed, including market variants and rarer anchors used on [[Automaton]] backs.
* A trap-type item referenced by [[dungeon crawler carl]] as a means of repositioning crawlers, considered for moving [[Princess Donut]] or escaping the collapsing area.
* A trap item used to [[teleport]] specific riders or mounts into the field, summoning steeds and allies like [[Gonk]] and riders like [[Katia]].
* Inventory traps referenced as escape redundancies for [[Donut]]'s tower escape hatch in case in-situ traps are disabled.
* A deployed trap designed to instantly [[teleport]] summoned NPC guards to a locked closet on the top floor as a contingency against [[Astrid]]'s "Summon Guard".
* A trap placed on the [[spider automaton]] with both anchor and beacon ends, intended to trigger on the snake/city boss to forcibly break the demon free while keeping it physically in place.
* A trap item in [[dungeon crawler carl]]'s inventory, triggered at the chapter's end to [[teleport]], leaving a trap inside [[Samantha]].
* Portable teleportation devices, [[dungeon crawler carl]] tosses into the utility closet, sending triggering guards to a remote room.
* A rare trap item composed of an anchor half and a trigger half, requiring both to be set by the same user to [[teleport]], with limited market availability.
