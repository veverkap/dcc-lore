# dynamite

* Explosive consumable/utility item granted by the [[Silver Goblin Box]], relating to the [[Boom!]] achievement if used to cause large explosions.
* Thrown by [[dungeon crawler carl]] at the room entrance, causing a large blast that killed many minions and damaged [[The Hoarder]] prior to the formal boss battle.
* Hand-held explosive sticks and related small [[bombs]]/grenades handed to the protagonist by [[rory the shamanka]].
* Used by [[goblin [[bomb]] bard]] and others; readily lightable and highly dangerous.
* High-explosive sticks referenced by [[dungeon crawler carl]] as degradable unless worked on at a [[Sapper's Table]]; part of his [[explosives]] experimentation.
* Used in a suicide clockwork tactic ("The Boomy Boy") during the neighborhood boss fight; [[key]] component of that anti-boss strategy.
* Conventional explosive used in the warehouse assassination plot tied to [[Remex the Grand]]; expected to be triggered by the [[Hobgoblin pus]] and central to the dispute over whether reward criteria were met.
* A stable stick of dynamite carried and shoved into the overalls of a reanimated clurichaun to turn it into a suicide explosive in the chapter.
* Thrown by [[dungeon crawler carl]] when facing multiple [[Brindled Vespas]]; offered as an alternative to the [[Slingshot]] by [[Zev]] for better viewer engagement.
* Explosive item placed in the rat corpse that detonated, seriously injuring and killing attendees—directly responsible for maiming [[frank]] and killing [[Yvette]] in the recap footage.
* Standard explosive sticks Carl begins to receive again while opening boxes after the fight.
