# grulke_infantry_figure

• A common stuffed beanie collectible manufactured by [[Jaxbrin Amusements, Limited]] that summons a temporary real creature when its tag is ripped.
• The summoned creature lasts 15-30 seconds and is disposable once used.
• Variants range from common to unique/legendary (e.g., [[Kimaris]]).
• A stuffed beanbag toy that summons a [[grulke creature]] when tossed, used strategically as a diversion in the alley.
