# Grulke Infantry Figure
A common stuffed-beanie collectible manufactured by [[Jaxbrin Amusements, Limited]] that can be activated by ripping its tag to summon a temporary real creature. The basic variations are common, last 15–30 seconds, and are disposable once used.
