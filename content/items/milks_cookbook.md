# Milk's Cookbook
A circulated cookbook authored/annotated by [[Milk]] that contains recipes, notes on portals and mapmaking, and marginalia that later crawlers (including [[Rosetta]] and [[dungeon crawler carl]]) consult. The book serves as both a culinary and informational artifact in the crawl community.

# Milk's Cookbook
A multipart cookbook manuscript with editioned notes (e.g., "Note added by Crawler Milk. 6th Edition") authored by [[Crawler Milk]]. Contains recipes, notes on portals and mapmaking, and marginal rants; [[dungeon crawler carl]] consults it for clues and recipes (notably stew, potatoes, and root vegetables) relevant to the crawl and sponsor objectives.
