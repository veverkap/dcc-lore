# Drakea’s Enchanted Kerchief of Disorder
A black paisley bandana / kerchief that grants +5 Detect Traps, a level-15 [[Tripper]] spell (item-based, five-hour cooldown), and a [[Remote Detonator]] benefit; tied to the famed crawler/author [[Drakea]].
