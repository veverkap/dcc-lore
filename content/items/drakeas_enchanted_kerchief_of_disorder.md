# drakeas_enchanted_kerchief_of_disorder

• A black paisley bandana/kerchief that grants +5 Detect Traps, a level-15 [[Tripper]] spell (item-based, five-hour cooldown), and a [[Remote Detonator]] benefit, tied to [[Drakea]].
• Can raise the [[Tripper]] spell to level 15 and has item-based cooldown mechanics.
• Grants +5 to Detect Traps, allows casting the level-15 [[Tripper]] spell once every five hours, and provides the [[Remote Detonator]] benefit.
• Can be worn in multiple cosmetic ways and was awarded to [[dungeon crawler carl]].
