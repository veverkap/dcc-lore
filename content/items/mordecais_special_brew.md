# mordecais_special_brew

Here is the consolidated markdown list:

• A unique potion crafted by [[Mordecai]] that combines a [[healing potion]] with the [[Troll's Second Wind]] effect to create near-[[immortality]] for thirty seconds, with severe side effects: increases potion-sickness timer by ~10 hours and a two-dose limit.
• Grants near-[[invulnerability]] for thirty seconds, but enforces a ten-hour cooldown on other [[potions]].
• Raises potion-sickness by ~10 hours and has other dangerous side effects; only two doses can be taken per crawler as crafted by [[Mordecai]].
• Provides accelerated healing and sustained protection during the god's strikes.
• Imposes a potion cooldown timer (potion sickness) visible in the UI.
• Recommended by [[Mordecai]] for emergency healing instead of normal health [[potions]] when certain conditions occur.
• Can be combined into a healing/temporary-[[invulnerability]] concoction and used to save [[Orthrus]].
• Grants massive healing/regeneration for thirty seconds, carrying heavy side effects (e.g., +10 hours potion sickness, two-dose limit).
• Intended as an emergency safeguard during the [[Vermillion line]] operation.
• Used by [[dungeon crawler carl]] to gain near-[[invulnerability]] for thirty seconds ("[[Gold]]-Standard!" effect), with a long cooldown preventing potion use for ten hours afterward.
• Has a four-hour cooldown until next potion after consumption.
• Recommended by [[Mordecai]] as an alternative to health [[potions]] when health is low.
