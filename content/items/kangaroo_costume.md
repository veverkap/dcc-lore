# Kangaroo Costume
A high-level costume piece currently worn by [[dungeon crawler carl]] (noted as level 6) that restricts movement (large joey feet) and emits a strong smell. Using its associated high-level skills (like [[Laundry Day]]) risks physical harm.

# Kangaroo costume
Worn by [[Carl]] in the chapter; described as a tight, skin-attached outfit (a joey corpse), problematic for movement and smell, and tied to a costume skill that can flay the wearer if too high-level. Central to many character reactions and the hazard for the next race.
