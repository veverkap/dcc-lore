# kangaroo_costume

• A high-level costume piece worn by [[dungeon crawler carl]] (level 6) that restricts movement and emits a strong smell.
• Using its associated high-level skills (like [[Laundry Day]]) risks physical harm.
• Worn by [[Carl]] in the chapter, described as a tight, skin-attached outfit (a joey corpse) that is problematic for movement and smell.
• Tied to a costume skill that can flay the wearer if too high-level.
• Central to many character reactions and a hazard for the next race.
