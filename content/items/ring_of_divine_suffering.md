# ring_of_divine_suffering

Here is the consolidated list of unique factual points:

* The [[Ring]] of Divine Suffering is a powerful artifact granted by Frank Q to Carl.
* It grants a stat bonus and the "Marked for Death" skill, which allows Carl to mark targets for kills.
* Marking a target grants +1 to a stat based on the target's highest stat, and the mark persists until the target is killed.
* The [[ring]]'s behavior varies slightly by floor, with mark formation and cooldown rules changing.
* The [[ring]] is considered highly dangerous because it makes Carl a prime target for hunters and factions, especially on the sixth floor and in faction wars.
* Mordecai demands that Carl sell the [[ring]] before reaching earlier hunting grounds.
* The [[ring]] has a charged power-scaling effect tied to kills, granting stat points upon kills.
* The [[ring]] levels up, increasing its yield to four stat points per kill after a certain number of kills.
* The [[ring]] is associated with the Guild of Suffering and memorial crystals.
* Carl uses the [[ring]] to mark targets, including Miriam Dom and Vrah.
* The [[ring]] is a focal item in the chapter's moral tension, with Carl struggling with its power and the consequences of using it.
* The [[ring]] is a significant artifact in Carl's inventory, with multiple instances and upgrades throughout the chapter.
