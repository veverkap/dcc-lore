# blitz_sticks

* Highly-addictive consumable items that can permanently increase intelligence with unspecified side effects
* Smoked like cigarettes, seen in the [[desperado club]], and culturally prevalent among some crawlers
* Grant permanent intelligence increases with unspecified side effects
* Illicit products sold openly through pharmacists in the [[desperado club]] middle floor
* Mentioned as part of the loot carried by the deceased hunter and looted from [[Iota]]
* Can induce non-existent memories and is highly addictive
* Smells like patchouli in the chapter
* [[Frank Q.]] and [[Imani]] have used them
* Available through the [[desperado club]]'s hidden networks and drug dens
