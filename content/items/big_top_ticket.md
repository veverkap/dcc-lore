# big_top_ticket

• A quest/novelty item looted from [[Terror the Clown]].
• Grants "Safe Passage" through public areas of the Grimaldi's Traveling Circus Big Top.
• Admits one adult into the Big Top.
• May be used to enter the interior of the tent, but avoid the three rings.
