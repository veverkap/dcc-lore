# [[Big Top Ticket]]
A magical ticket item that admits one adult into the Grimaldi’s Traveling Circus Big Top and grants "Safe Passage" through public areas of the tent; Signet tells Carl survivors with such tickets may enter the interior as long as they avoid the three rings.
