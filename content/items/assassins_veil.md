# Assassin's Veil
An enchanted, unique Egyptian-style beaded headdress (identified as a "veil") that can only be worn by female Feline-class species. Grants +10% dexterity and a Precognition Benefit (warns of nearby offensive/binding spells or skills). Carries a factional restriction that "Enemies of [[Sekhmet]] will be automatically hostile" to the wearer.

# Assassin's Veil
An enchanted, unique, female- and Feline-class-only headdress described as an Egyptian-style beaded veil tied to the court of [[Sekhmet]]. Grants +10% dexterity and the "Precognition Benefit" (warns of nearby offensive/binding spells or skills). Warning: wearing it makes enemies of [[Sekhmet]] automatically hostile.
