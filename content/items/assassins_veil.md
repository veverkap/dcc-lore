# assassins_veil

• An enchanted, unique Egyptian-style beaded headdress (identified as a "veil") that can only be worn by female Feline-class species.
• Grants +10% dexterity and a Precognition Benefit (warns of nearby offensive/binding spells or skills).
• Carries a factional restriction: "Enemies of [[Sekhmet]] will be automatically hostile" to the wearer.
