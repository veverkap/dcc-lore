# Xistera
A fan-box reward: a long, scoop-like metallic basket (the jai-alai hand scoop) that retracts into a bracer. Equips on the forearm, enables high-speed projectile throws (demonstrated by shattering an orange against the inn's safe-room door). Interacts with summonable items (can accept inventory items into the scoop) and modifies combat/playstyle, prompting [[dungeon crawler carl]] to consider Dexterity investment.
