# red_line_engineers_key

• [[Key]] item required to open the service plate under the gangway floor of cars on the [[Red Line]]; without it, the party cannot access car electrical systems or coupler controls.
• Required to access service panels and coupler controls beneath the gangway floors.
• Special [[key]] item required to access the [[train]] service plate and electrical controls beneath gangway panels.
• Required to open magically locked service plates and access undercarriage controls on the [[Red Line]] trains.
