# Force Play
A mystic/consumable card used by [[Quan Ch]] that sees an opponent's hand and forces a specific card in that hand to be played automatically. It is used to steal a [[flee card]] card from [[Sister Ines]] and turn the tide.

# [[Force Play]]
A mystic/consumable card that lets the caster pick a specific card from an opponent’s hand and force it to play. [[Quan Ch]] uses this card to steal a [[flee card]] from [[Sister Ines]] and later to manipulate [[Donut]]’s hand.
