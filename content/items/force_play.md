# force_play

• A mystic/consumable card used by [[Quan Ch]] that sees an opponent's hand and forces a specific card to be played automatically.
• Used to steal a [[flee card]] from [[Sister Ines]] and turn the tide.
• Allows the caster to pick a specific card from an opponent's hand and force it to play.
• Used by [[Quan Ch]] to manipulate [[Donut]]'s hand.
