# winding_box

* The Winding Box is a crucial artifact required to assemble the [[Gate of the Feral Gods]], alongside two [[Pocket Watch]]es.
* It can be used to open one-way portals to the [[The Nothing]] when combined with the watches, and can also function as a conduit directly to the [[The Nothing]] if charged and wound with the lid open.
* The device hums with magic and can spin watches to keep them ready.
* It is central to the plot, as it can be used to control [[gate]] mechanics and set a [[gate]] countdown.
* The Winding Box is a [[key]] artifact sought within the sandcastle, and Ghazi supposedly possesses one part.
* It is a mechanical artifact that houses the two [[watch]] pieces used to control [[gate]] behavior.
* The Winding Box is one of the three component pieces of the [[Gate of the Feral Gods]].
* It can be used to banish threats, such as the [[Necropolis of Anser]], by "sucking" them away.
* The Winding Box is a sought-after artifact mentioned as being in the possession or interest of the Mad Dune Mage.
* It is a plot-relevant object that [[Dungeon Crawler Carl]] had previously stolen, and [[Ghazi]] is suspected to know about it.
* The Winding Box is a critical artifact used to control [[gate]] mechanics, designed to house two [[Pocket Watch]] devices and set a [[gate]] countdown.
* Its position and movement (if shifted more than five feet) are central to [[gate]] validity and safety.
* The Winding Box is a counterfeit version fabricated by [[Katia]] to trick dirigible gnomes and for use in the [[gate]] plan switcheroo.
* The Winding Box is described as a "blood-soaked winding box" from the [[Gate of the Feral Gods]], and when broken, it formed the permanent [[portal]] into the [[The Nothing]].
