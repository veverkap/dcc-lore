# Donut drive system
A makeshift steering-and-pedal lever device constructed by [[Rend]] or the team (sourced from [[Hedy]]). Janky, goblin-workshop–style equipment installed to allow [[Donut]] to control the [[Truck]] by rocking; placed into [[Donut]]’s inventory and used because the primary driver is inexperienced.

# [[Donut drive system]]
A jury-rigged steering-and-pedal mechanism built by [[Rend]] and installed in the truck. Appears in [[Donut]]'s inventory; allows steering by rocking and uses sticks to actuate pedals. Described as cobbled-together and goblin-workshop–style.
