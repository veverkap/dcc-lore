# donut_drive_system

• A makeshift steering-and-pedal lever device constructed by [[Rend]] or the team, sourced from [[Hedy]], allowing [[Donut]] to control the [[Truck]] by rocking.
• Installed in [[Donut]]'s inventory due to the primary driver's inexperience.
• A jury-rigged steering-and-pedal mechanism built by [[Rend]] and installed in the [[truck]].
• Uses sticks to actuate pedals and is described as cobbled-together and goblin-workshop–style.
