# tiara_of_a_thousand_lights

• Unique, sponsor-made [[tiara]] awarded to [[Donut]] with base +10 Charisma; contains 99 Absorption Jewels and can be permanently charged by draining enchanted [[hat]] items via the [[Accompanying Charge Pillow]].
• Transfers buffs from [[hat]] items into the [[tiara]] (with many warnings/limitations).
• A multi-upgrade [[tiara]] worn by [[Donut]] containing dozens of drained skills and upgrades (35 of 99 at chapter time), including [[Perfect Night Vision]].
• Includes a [[tiara]] (+10 Charisma base) and a red charge pillow.
• The [[tiara]] can be permanently charged with up to 99 absorbed buffs by draining magical [[hat]] items on the pillow (ten-hour drain time; partial removal after one hour still destroys the original item's magic).
• Contains 99 Absorption Jewels and can become an extremely powerful [[hat]] by transferring buffs from other [[hat]] items.
• Warnings include interactions with temporary effects, curses, celestial [[hats]], and items carrying debt.
