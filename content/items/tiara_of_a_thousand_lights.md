# Tiara of a Thousand Lights and Accompanying Charge Pillow
A unique, sponsor-made set given to [[Donut]] that includes a tiara (plus +10 Charisma base) and a red charge pillow. The tiara can be permanently charged with up to 99 absorbed buffs by draining magical hat items on the pillow (ten-hour drain time; partial removal after one hour still destroys the original item's magic). The tiara contains 99 Absorption Jewels and can become an extremely powerful hat by transferring buffs from other hat items. Warnings include interactions with temporary effects, curses, celestial hats, and items carrying debt.
