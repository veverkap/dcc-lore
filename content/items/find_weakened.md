# Find Weakened
A level-five skill granted by the [[Cloak of the Benevolent Champion]] that indicates which target in a group is closest to death. Useful for healers and tactical decisions; Quan used it to locate weak crawlers.

# Find Weakened
A level-five skill granted by the [[Cloak of the Benevolent Champion]] that identifies which member of a group (mobs or crawlers) is closest to death. Useful for healers or opportunistic attackers; explicitly noted as part of the cloak's benefits.
