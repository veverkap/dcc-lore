# twister

* A flying house/vehicle used to ferry changelings between bubbles and the sixth floor, central to transport plans, and at risk from hunters and recklessness by pilots like [[Skarn]].
* A large balloon/airship mechanism limited by ceiling/trees on this floor, with Bonnie modifying garage-opener mechanics to build a ballista that affected its surroundings.
* A named vehicle/structure used by the group for travel, recovered from changeling refugees by [[Louis]] and [[Firas]].
* An airship used by the crawler team for aerial support and [[bomb]] delivery, piloted/controlled from the roof and involved in skirmishes with mantises and elves.
* A vehicle or transport referenced as the party's means of travel, with other characters taking it back south to assist allies off-screen.
* A named spell or effect mentioned as potential aid for the [[changelings]] after they are relocated by [[Zerzura]], presented as a defensive/offensive option to help survivors handle ensuing threats.
* A flying house/vehicle used to ferry changelings between floors and for logistic operations, piloted by community kids (e.g., the [[Skarn kid]]) and central to moving large numbers of survivors across the lacuna.
* A named airship/vehicle used by the crawler group for aerial maneuvers and bombing runs, piloted/controlled by [[Sledge]] and targeted by mantises during the assault.
* A previously mentioned vehicle that [[Louis Santiago]] owns and references when discussing power sources and comparisons, with the [[Party Planner]] being compared to it in functionality and power source (soul crystal).
* The name of an airship referenced as slower than [[Louis]]'s new ship, used as a point of comparison in a conversation about travel and upgrades.
