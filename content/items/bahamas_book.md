# bahamas_book

• [[Bahamas book]]: A bestiary/reference tome that categorizes monsters by rarity and lists regional fauna, used to search for information on monk seals and identify monsters, including [[Shi Maria]].
• Contains entries on [[Shi Maria]] and [[La Ciguapa]], and provides alternate names and indexing for notable creatures.
• Used as a research tool for monster names and alternate aliases, and as a reference for mob information and properties.
• Consulted by [[dungeon crawler carl]] and [[Donut]] to cross-reference and identify [[Shi Maria]] and her abilities and weaknesses.
• Used by Carl to look up monsters, including unique monsters like [[Shi Maria]], and provides indexing for notable creatures.
• Physical book referenced when [[Prepotente]] requests Carl read chapters, and mentioned as a social/comfort item in chat.
