# charm_potion

• A consumable that grants charm or charisma effects similar to casting [[The Recital]].
• Used to enhance [[Princess Donut]]'s charm and influence elves during entry.
• Associated with a [[tiara]] and charm upgrade.
• Must be smuggled into secure rooms when stage access won't allow consumption.
• Produces a minor charm effect to focus attention, part of the performer's toolkit.
• Taken by [[Donut]] (exclaimed "I TOOK THE CHARM POTION. IT WORKED!")
