# enchanted_trollskin_shirt_of_pummeling

• A [[gold]]-tier enchanted shirt awarded from a [[Gold Apparel Box]].
• Described with a combat-oriented modifier ("of Pummeling").
• Grants +7 to Regeneration.
• Negates melee-based damage debuffs (Stun, Knockback, Disarm, Out-of-Breath).
• Non-offensive to trolls, potentially attracting attention from [[Trolls]].
