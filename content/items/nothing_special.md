# nothing_special

• Named variant of an enriched pet biscuit that transforms a creature into a dungeon pet, reverts them to juvenile form, and enables level/skill progression.
• Similar to [[Enriched Pet Biscuit: The Paramour]], but preserves the pet's existing anatomy and does not confer the Paramour mental effect.
• Converts an ordinary animal into a sapient party companion when fed, used on [[Penelope 3]] to create a level 10 sapient pig and automatically links the animal to [[Carl]]'s party and to [[Mordecai]] as guide.
