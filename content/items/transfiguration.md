# transfiguration

• A powerful spell type referenced as having been used by [[Imogen]] on the village and as part of the multi-tiered assault associated with [[Scolopendra]]; tied to historical mass transformations.
• Responsible for mass transformations and listed among the dangerous magics in local lore.
