# seeking_missile

• An offensive item or projectile that can blow a hunter's head off, found at the scene of a killed hunter.
• Small, [[crossbow]]-[[bolt]]-sized rockets designed as part of a [[landmine]] payload, which emerge before detonation, seek nearby companions, and carry a small explosive charge.
• A lethal projectile effect that can blow a hunter's head clean off, implied to be an attack ability or item used during a skirmish.
