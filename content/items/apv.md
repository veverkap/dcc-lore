# apv

• Armored personnel vehicle used by teams like [[One Fine Pig]] in the race; mounted atop an [[APV]] in the starting lineup.
• Large armored vehicle type, referenced in the heat, notable as a potential combatant target that could trigger larger consequences if attacked.
• Armored Personnel Vehicle used by [[One Fine Pig]]; projects a square-shaped [[shield]] that repels attackers and contains [[Penelope]].
• Armored personnel vehicle parked in the garage of the abandoned house, described as having a cracked windshield and present when [[Penelope the pig]] is found.
• Armored vehicle used by the team during the lobby engagement; fired spells at the Russian [[Cadillac]] during the attempted escape.
