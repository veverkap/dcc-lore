# abyss_cart

• Carts that originate from the [[Abyss]] and can appear unexpectedly on lines; sometimes blast music and interact unpredictably with trainyard portals.
• The first-appearing [[portal]] [[cart]] that originates from the [[Abyss]] and often acts as the engine car on the lines; appears with music and can be routed into holding areas or flip, creating hazards and unexpected teleports to trainyards.
