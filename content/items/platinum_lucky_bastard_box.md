# platinum_lucky_bastard_box

• A reward item received by [[dungeon crawler carl]] and [[Princess Donut]] from the "Like a Moth to the Flame" achievement; each contained a scratch-ticket-style item.
• A high-tier loot reward granted to [[dungeon crawler carl]] by the achievement [[Lazarus]] for surviving a level-15+ offensive spell; an example of the kind of powerful reward disbursed after dangerous encounters.
• A special reward box given for surviving particularly unlikely events (e.g., surviving [[Signet]]'s attack); can contain rare items like casino chips.
