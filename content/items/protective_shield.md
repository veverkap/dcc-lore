# protective_shield

• A defensive spell that creates a forcefield, observed at a cave entrance, preventing passage between quadrants.
• A magical [[shield]] effect encountered on the rhinoceros litter, blocking attacks against mounts.
• A defensive spell referenced when Carl interacts with a shimmering forcefield protecting the tomb entrance, resembling his [[Protective Shield]] spell.
• A magical defensive effect applied to the high-elf [[howdah]] and/or mount, causing [[Kiwi]]'s charge to bounce off and mitigating incoming attacks.
