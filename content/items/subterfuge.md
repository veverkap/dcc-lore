# subterfuge

• A stealth/illusion spell cast by [[elle mcgib]] that can be canceled by enemy searches and has a cooldown before recasting.
• Cast by [[elle mcgib]] to conceal approach through a [[portal]], avoiding detection on the other side.
• Cast by [[General Elle McGib]] to hide the platoon's presence, requiring them to stay within the spell area to maintain protection.
