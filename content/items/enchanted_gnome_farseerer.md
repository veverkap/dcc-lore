# enchanted_gnome_farseerer

• A gnomish long-range telescope used to sight targets, increasing gravity-[[bomb]] accuracy and allowing distant inspection.
• Used by [[Skarn]] to view the [[Wasteland Castle]].
• Reveals details about the [[Wasteland Castle]] and its armaments.
