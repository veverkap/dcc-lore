# Enchanted Gnome FarSeerer
A gnomish long-range telescope used to sight targets (invented for bomb runs). Increases gravity-bomb accuracy and allows distant inspection—used by [[Skarn]] to view the [[Wasteland Castle]].

# Enchanted Gnome FarSeerer
A gnomish telescope device used for long-range sighting and bomb run targeting. It increases inspection and information on distant objects and reveals details about the [[Wasteland Castle]] and its armaments.
