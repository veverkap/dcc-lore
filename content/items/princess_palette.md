# princess_palette

• A high-earning eye makeup kit and merch product tied to [[Donut]] and the [[Princess Posse]].
• Cited as the highest-earning crawler merch in the crawl's history and a target of [[Chandra]]'s revenue claims.
• An unlicensed makeup palette found among the fan-box junk that [[Donut]] claims as her idea.
• Labeled with irreverent names like "A Night Out with [[Mongo]]" and "The Butcher's Mascara."
