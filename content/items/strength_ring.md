# strength_ring

Here is the consolidated markdown list:

• [[Strength Ring]]: Carl's usual strength [[ring]], which was not the glowing [[ring]] he wore; Carl also looted unnamed strength rings from corpses, including those of Miss Quill's victims and Nica.
• +1 Strength [[Ring]]: A low-tier magical [[ring]] found on a hunter's corpse, described as the only enchanted gear on the body.
• [[Ring]] (+2 Strength): Unnamed rings looted from Nica's corpse, each granting +2 Strength, which Carl equips to restore his strength pool.
