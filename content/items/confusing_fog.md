# confusing_fog

• A scroll-type spell that creates a fog, effectiveness depends on the reader's Intelligence.
• Used for battlefield control, tactical use, and escapes.
• Can be used to disorient enemies, create a smokescreen, or grant defensive utility.
• Lasts minutes depending on caster intelligence.
• Can be found in silver quest boxes or looted as a consumable scroll.
• Can be used to avoid smites if Legba ascends.
• [[Donut]] and [[Dungeon Crawler Carl]] have used it.
• [[Legba]] teaches a level-15 spell version of Confusing Fog.
