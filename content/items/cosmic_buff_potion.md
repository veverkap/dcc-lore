# cosmic_buff_potion

• A platinum good-shit box potion described as "pink and frothy" that raises a random stat by 10 points, used by [[Donut]] and distinct from standard limited-use [[buff]] [[potions]].
• A high-tier consumable received in a platinum box that grants powerful temporary buffs, sought after by several characters including [[Mordecai]].
• [[Princess Donut]] consumed one, which raises a random stat by 10 points and is distinct from standard limited consumable buffs.
