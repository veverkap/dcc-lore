# legendary_box

• A commonly handed-out reward tier in [[Dungeon Crawler World]], less potent and less regulated than [[Celestial Quest Box]].
• A high-tier reward box referenced generically.
• A commonly awarded, lower-tier box referenced for contrast with [[Celestial Box]] rarity and value.
• Reward item offered by [[orren the liaison]] as part of a deal for signing a confession, containing equipment that could guarantee survival until higher floors.
