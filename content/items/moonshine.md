# moonshine

• [[Moonshine]]: a tainted liquid produced in the Rev-Up room, suspected to be hazardous (possible Taint infection or explosive risk).
• Used to make the [[Rev-Up Immunity Smoothie]] and as a volatile component in fights.
• A flammable jug liquor in Carl's inventory, used to set Heather on fire.
• Present in the [[Employee Break Room]] (regular moonshine, distinct from Rev-Up Moonshine), which explodes when ignited by the [[Literal Fire Ants]] trap.
• Carried by [[Vernon]] (distinct from Rev-Up Moonshine), explodes when ignited and contributed to the [[Explosion Imminent]] sequence during the [[Literal Fire Ants]] trap.
