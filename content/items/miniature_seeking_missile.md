# [[Miniature seeking missile]]
Small crossbow-bolt–sized rockets used as part of [[dungeon crawler carl]]'s landmine payload and later duct-taped to [[Samantha]] as an improvised delivery system. They have short range and a seeking behavior that targets nearby companions.
