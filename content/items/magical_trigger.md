# Magical Trigger
A stick-like detonator with a breakable tip (likened to underarm deodorant). You break off pieces (up to 10 per stick), insert the piece into an explosive, and press a button to start a five-second countdown. Single-use per piece; described as having a range of about ten kilometers. Used to detonate the [[Hobgoblin Pus]] in the [[Cart]].
