# magical_trigger

• A stick-like detonator with a breakable tip, used to detonate the [[Hobgoblin Pus]] in the [[Cart]].
• Break off pieces (up to 10 per stick) and insert them into an explosive.
• Press a button to start a five-second countdown.
• Single-use per piece.
• Has a range of about ten kilometers.
