# sticky_detonator_charge

• Attachment used to affix [[explosives]] (like the [[hobgoblin dynamite]]) to structural members; used by Carl during the setup of the demolition.
• An adhesive detonator used to affix the [[hobgoblin dynamite]] to the building's joists, enabling remote or coordinated simultaneous explosions.
