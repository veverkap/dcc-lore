# war_lord_spellbook

• A high-cost spellbook prize that casts a powerful 100-mana effect.
• Judged by [[Mordecai]] as not worth the cost for mid-tier buyers.
• Available at higher hands, with a 100-mana requirement.
• Deemed not cost-effective by [[Mordecai]] at fifty hands.
