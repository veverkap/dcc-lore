
# size_up

• A potion used by [[Katia]] to scan and get a read on [[Astrid]]'s spells and abilities.
• A scanning or evaluation skill used in the [[interface]] to get enemy information, used by characters like [[Donut]] to read levels and spells of foes such as [[King Rust]].
• A spell/form-magic effect cast by [[Chiyome]]'s team that increases apparent size and reveals capabilities of targets in the area.
• A potion type available in the prize case, increasing physical size when used.
• A named scroll referenced as stolen by [[dungeon crawler carl]] from another character's mother, used as barter/negotiation currency in the chapter.
• A spell cast via team-form magic that increases apparent size/stats of creatures in the area to reveal capabilities, used by [[Chiyome]]'s team to gauge [[Carl]]'s party.
