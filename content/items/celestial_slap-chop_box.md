# celestial_slap-chop_box

• A celestial-tier reward box that manifested as a quivering golden blanket/origami swan, yielding items such as a matching [[crupper]] and the [[Crupper of the Benevolent Champion]].
• The original award method for the [[Crupper of the Benevolent Champion]] on the ninth floor.
• Obtained by [[Donut]], manifesting as a quivering golden blanket and later forming an origami swan, and yielding items such as the [[Crupper of the Benevolent Champion]] and other gear.
• A named loot container/artifact that originally awarded the [[Crupper of the Benevolent Champion]] on the [[Ninth Floor]], described as the delivery mechanism for that prize.
