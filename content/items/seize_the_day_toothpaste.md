# seize_the_day_toothpaste

• [[Seize the Day Toothpaste]]: Reward item from a [[Platinum Big Daddy Box]], labeled "Five Applications." When used in a safe room, brushing grants a 30-hour boss-damage [[buff]]: +3x damage to all Boss Monsters (or +4x to Province/Country/Floor Bosses). Limited to five applications and usable only in safe rooms.
