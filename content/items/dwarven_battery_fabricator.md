# dwarven_battery_fabricator

• A crafted device that produces dwarven batteries, which can be recharged with mana [[potions]].
• Each [[battery]] requires four full mana [[potions]] to fully charge.
• The device is used to power carts and engines.
• Carl acquired the device in trade and used it to maintain a supply of rechargeable batteries for powered carts.
