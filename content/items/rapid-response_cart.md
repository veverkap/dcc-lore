# Rapid-response cart
Two-way portal-equipped carts used to send trains and supplies to predetermined trainyards or the [[Abyss]]. Carl and crew use multiple rapid-response carts to plug tunnels, route people to specific yards (e.g., [[trainyard]], [[yard Q]]), and to cork off passages.
