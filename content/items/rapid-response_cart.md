# rapid-response_cart

• Two-way [[portal]]-equipped carts used to send trains and supplies to predetermined trainyards or the [[Abyss]].
• Carts are labeled with trainyard IDs (e.g., tuned to [[trainyard]]) and can be delayed/trapped (alarm traps) and powered by [[battery]] when main power is out.
• Equipped with a [[portal]] attachment, runs on batteries when line power is out, and lacks brakes.
• Used as makeshift barriers, scoops, and transport for [[explosives]] and players.
• Portable, [[battery]]-powered [[portal]] carts used to create temporary gateways to the [[Abyss]] or trainyards.
• Used as corks, portals, and to funnel monsters or send trains into the pit (some are two-way and labeled with trainyard identifiers).
• Carries a [[portal]] and batteries for power during outages.
