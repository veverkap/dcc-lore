# love_vampire

• Named special ability acquired by [[Donut]] at level 30, causing one enemy (level or lower) to "give me his heart": damage that would apply to the user is instead transferred to the targeted enemy; lasts until the target is dead and can be used at intervals (roughly every couple of hours).
• Converts damage to a targeted enemy (level or lower) by making them "give" their heart—effectively transferring incoming damage to the target for the duration until they die; usable roughly every couple of hours.
• An active skill learned by [[Donut]] that reflects damage back to lower-level mobs.
• A charm skill used by [[Donut]] (activated by high Charisma), normally charms one intelligent opponent and reflects damage to that target; at very high charisma it can auto-charm up to five mobs.
• A spell attempted by [[Donut]] that proves ineffective against pets in the assault described in the chapter.
• Causes one enemy of equal or lower level to "give [her] his heart" — transferring damage from Donut to that enemy until its death; usable every few hours.
• A skill obtained by [[Donut]] that "reflects" or redirects damage to mobs below her level.
• A special ability that forces one enemy (level or lower) to "give her his heart," transferring incoming damage to that enemy until it dies; usable on a cooldown (every couple of hours).
• A skill ability obtained by [[Donut]] that reflects damage back to mobs of lower level than the user.
• A charm skill/spell tied to [[Princess Donut]] that reflects damage to a charmed intelligent opponent; upgraded by a skill potion and enhanced by temporary charisma boosts.
• A charm-like skill that saps energy/mana from victims; given as an example of domination-style effects in the scene.
