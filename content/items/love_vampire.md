# [[Love Vampire]]
Named special ability acquired by [[Donut]] at level 30. Mechanically described as causing one enemy (level or lower) to "give me his heart": damage that would apply to the user is instead transferred to the targeted enemy; lasts until the target is dead and can be used at intervals (roughly every couple of hours).
