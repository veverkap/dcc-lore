# Night Wyrm’s Nasty Little Web of Suffering
A faded, green-tinted spiderweb tattoo that appeared on Carl's left elbow after opening a Gold Junkie's Box. Signifies access to the [[Guild of Suffering]] and is described as a bearer sigil.
