# stock_certificate

• A tradable document representing one share in a publicly traded company (e.g., [[Sigmund Textiles Foundry]]).
• Found in each [[Silver Summary Judgement box]], representing one share in different publicly-traded companies.
• Unexpected credit-value items for crawlers and used by the AI to needle plaintiffs.
