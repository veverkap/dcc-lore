# used_vial

• A broken/empty potion vial dropped by a [[Red Cornet]]; initially contained a tiny amount of golden liquid that behaved oddly (didn't pour, puffed away when glass smashed).
• The vial resisted description (golden liquid stuck like honey) and puffed away when smashed.
• The vial initially appeared to contain a golden liquid but vanished when the glass was shattered.
• The vial clinked as a behavioral cue.
• The vial was found on a [[Red Cornet]] and initially resisted inspection (liquid clung like honey) before puffed away when the glass was smashed.
• Investigated by [[dungeon crawler carl]] and [[Katia]].
