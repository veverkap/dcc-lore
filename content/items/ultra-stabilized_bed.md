# ultra-stabilized_bed

• Tier-one personal-space bed upgrade purchased using coupons, allowing full rest after only two hours of sleep and granting a 30-hour Good Rest bonus (improves stats and training efficiency).
• Semi-transparent bed with a gelatinous-looking mattress placed in [[Princess Donut]]'s room.
• Sleeping in it grants the "Well-Rested" achievement and the associated Good Rest bonus for [[dungeon crawler carl]].
