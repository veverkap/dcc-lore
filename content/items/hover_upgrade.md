# hover_upgrade

• An upgrade option that allows the [[truck]] to [[hover]] off the ground, nearly doubling speed and replacing the old gas engine; does not provide vertical [[climb]].
• Alternate upgrade for the [[Royal Court Truck]] that removes wheels and allows the vehicle to [[hover]], nearly doubling speed and replacing the ancient gas engine; does not provide vertical [[climb]].
