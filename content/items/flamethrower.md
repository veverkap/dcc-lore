# flamethrower

* Deployable defensive equipment used to create a [[ring]] of fire around the [[Portal to the Abyss]] and chokepoints; critical to holding back waves of Krakaren and ghouls.
* Area-denial equipment used in rings around the [[Portal to the Abyss]] and chokepoints to incinerate incoming Krakaren juveniles and ghouls; [[key]] to holding defensive lines.
* Effective against some vampiric creatures when paired with [[Ultraviolet]].
* Ren's weapon that the [[totem]] inspects during its control of [[dungeon crawler carl]]'s body and inventory.
* A weapon taken from [[Tsren]] on [[Floor 8]] and later possessed by [[dungeon crawler carl]].
* An offensive upgrade option visible on [[Hedy]]'s tablet that can be installed on the [[truck]] to attack other teams or mobs during a race.
* Heavy defensive weapons operated by several crawlers (including [[Tserendolgor]]) to create rings of fire around portals and chokepoints.
* A weapon referenced by [[Tserendolgor]] as effective when combined with the [[Ultraviolet]] effect against vampiric mobs, identified as a tactical countermeasure for infected creatures.
* The weapon once wielded by [[Tsren]] that ended up in [[dungeon crawler carl]]'s possession after her death.
* A weapon carried by [[Tsren]] on [[Floor 8]] that later came into [[Carl]]'s possession after her death.
* A weapon mounted on [[Jamal]]'s [[truck]] described as primed to cause "melty inconvenience" to opposing cars during the heat.
