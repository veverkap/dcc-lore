# teleport_to_stairwell

• A level-15 spell given to [[Bomo]] as part of the mercenary upgrade, allowing selection of a stairwell and teleportation, with a very long cooldown.
• Lists all floor exits, making it strategically valuable.
• Has an extremely long cooldown (~25 days).
