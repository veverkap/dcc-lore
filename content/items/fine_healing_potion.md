# fine_healing_potion

• Basic healing reagent used in large quantities to craft the [[Heal Severe Injury]] potion and other advanced healing concoctions.
• Base potion type required in large quantity to craft advanced [[potions]] like [[Heal Severe Injury]].
• Consumable used by [[Bucket Boy]] to stabilize [[Dong Quixote]]'s health in the chapter.
• Consumable healing item used by [[Carl]] immediately after the crash to stabilize injuries; multiple doses can be taken for rapid recovery.
