# Wrap of Sekhmet's Compassion
An enchanted headband restricted to pets. Grants "Eternal Protector of Health" (temporary invulnerability and grants the Heal Party Member skill under owner-health conditions, once per day) and "Bringer of Resurrection" (fatal pet bites have a 75% chance to cast a level 10 [[Raise Bootlicker]] on the corpse).
