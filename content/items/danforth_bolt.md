# danforth_bolt

• One of ten specialized bolts designed to hold a god in place; a [[Bolt]] of Ophiotaurus is described as one of these [[Danforth]] bolts.
• A glowing magical [[bolt]] that locks into Donut's Katia's [[crossbow]] with an ominous click, intended to root Rapture in place when fired.
• Fired from Katia's [[crossbow]], auto-locks onto Donut's mount, intended to root the womantaur driver Rapture in place; ammunition is limited (ten remaining).
