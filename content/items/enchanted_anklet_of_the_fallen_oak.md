# enchanted_anklet_of_the_fallen_oak

• A wooden anklet looted from the tree-liked crawler [[Lea]], granting +1 Dexterity, +1 Constitution, and +3 to the skill [[Double Tap]]. It is small enough to be worn by Donut.
• The anklet is a magical item found on a dead crawler and worn by [[Donut]], providing minor stat boosts and the skill "Double Tap".
• Its oak origin makes it a sacredly abhorrent artifact to [[Apito]]'s followers and triggers extreme reactions in city elves.
• The anklet's presence on Donut prompts caution when interacting with other Crawlers like [[daniel bautista]].
