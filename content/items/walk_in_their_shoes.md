# [[Walk in Their Shoes]]
A racial ability/skill possessed at level-5 by [[Katia]]’s race; allows the user to change appearance and (partially) the name over their head. Duration is short (five minutes) and requires high visual similarity (90% to fully change name display).

# Walk in Their Shoes
A shape/identity-changing ability that allows the user to alter their displayed name and appearance if their current appearance matches the target by a high percentage. [[Katia]] uses it, but is limited by percentage match thresholds.
