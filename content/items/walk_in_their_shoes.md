# walk_in_their_shoes

• A racial ability/skill possessed by [[Katia]]'s race, allowing the user to change appearance and partially the name over their head.
• The ability has a short duration (five minutes) and requires high visual similarity (90% to fully change name display).
• The user can alter their displayed name and appearance if their current appearance matches the target by a high percentage.
• [[Katia]] uses this ability, but is limited by percentage match thresholds.
