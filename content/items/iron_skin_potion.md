• A consumable potion used by [[dungeon crawler carl]] and given to [[Donut]] before they fled the safe room, granting protective effects useful during escapes.
• A temporary [[buff]] potion (x5 in the [[Gold Crowd Control Box]]) that increases physical armor by 100% for five minutes.
