# soul_crystal

Here is the consolidated markdown list:

* A magical crystal inferred to be mounted in the depot ceiling that recharges the Swordsmen guardians each night.
* A C-Grade, amber-hued soul gem (elf technology) that stores soul power harvested within the area and wirelessly powers the town's Swordsmen.
* A powerful item used to power devices and Swordsmen guards; when overloaded, it can become a massive [[bomb]].
* Power source used for Ghoul Generators; susceptible to popping under excessive local stress, causing room-level explosions when overloaded.
* Floating crystals tied to station mechanics and province-boss deaths; glowing indicators that, when destroyed (or when a province boss dies), can pop and affect progression across stations.
* A power source used to run devices like the Ghoul Generator and previously used to power Swordsmen; overloaded soul crystals have become explosive hazards.
* A small, purple-hued gem housed in a sliding clamshell device used to power the Suburban Floating House's balloon.
* A component item used to create Carl's Doomsday Scenario when combined with the Sheol Glass Reaper Case.
* A specific soul crystal unit tied to the ballroom [[interface]]; mentioned as already removed by an unknown agent even though the [[interface]] continued working due to the Goodwill [[interface]] [[capacitor]].
* Small soul crystals that control each of the four ballrooms; each attendant ballroom has its own crystal, plus a larger master crystal.
* The fifth, larger soul crystal that controls the entire ballroom overlay system and inter-room interactions; located in the castle's central control chamber on the third floor and targeted by Carl's team to disable protections.
* A magical resource or soul gem allegedly collected or hoarded by the elves for protective magic; tied into explanations for why some factions survived devastating attacks.
* A power source item referenced when Louis Santiago pulls "a small soul crystal encased in silver"; identified as the running power source for the Party Planner and similar vehicles like the [[Twister]].
* A magical crystal implied to sit in the ceiling above the Swordsmen Depot that recharges or powers the Swordsmen armor each night and anchors their yellow aura.
* A C-Grade, floating, amber-hued gem (also called a soul gem) of Elf technology that stores soul power from local deaths to recharge the Swordsmen nightly; it acts as a wireless soul-energizer and was the epicenter marked for the impending blast.
* Power sources used in nearby Ghoul Generators; Mordecai explains over-stressing them causes them to pop like fuses and produce large local explosions.
* A previously used device mentioned as having been blown by crawlers (those deaths allowed others to descend without fighting); context implies major combat utility or objective.
* A powerful inventory artifact used to power guardians and devices (including Ghoul Generator units); overuse or instability of these crystals is implicated in catastrophic events on the floor, including the creation of Carl's Doomsday Scenario.
