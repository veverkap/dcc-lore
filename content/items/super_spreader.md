# super_spreader

• A spell/ability used by the protagonist to infect or spread slug-like debuffs across a target.
• A spell/effect that infects targets with the slug disease, used by Dungeon Crawler Carl and Doctor Bones, with usage limits and can be applied to mercenaries to produce Sluggalos.
• A spell owned by Dungeon Crawler Carl, similar in concept to [[Rigorous]], used for affliction-spreading mechanics.
• An ability/card cast by Dungeon Crawler Carl to cause an enemy to leak slugs that spawn and damage the host, used strategically to weaken and distract a target heading to an exit.
• Compared to [[Rigorous]] in function, as [[Rigorous]] reflects debuffs rather than spreading them.
