# bolt

• A projectile ([[crossbow]] bolt) referenced by [[Hekla]] and others, which hits [[Katia]] and is central to the dispute over who attacked her.
• Distinguished from "arrow" in dialogue.
• Used in the chapter to describe [[crossbow]] fire, distinguished from "arrows" by [[Donut]] and corrected by [[Hekla]].
• Cited as the cause of [[Katia]]'s wound according to [[Hekla]]'s admission.
• A powerful munition given to [[Katia]] by [[princess formidable of the skull empire]], mentioned as something that could have been used against the [[Maestro]].
