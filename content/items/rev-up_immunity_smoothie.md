# rev-up_immunity_smoothie

• Grants temporary disease immunity at the cost of heavy inebriation
• Can be used to infuse a smoke curtain, but the experiment may fail and produce a "Dud" status
• Provides protection against the [[Taint]] debuff and the [[Vigorous Measles]]
• Requires two items to craft, found with bicycle-powered blending gear in the Rev-Up room
