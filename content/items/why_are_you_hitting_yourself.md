# why_are_you_hitting_yourself

* A humorous offensive spell obtained by [[Donut]] that forces targets to stab themselves.
* A debuff-style spell used by [[Donut]] that causes a target to aggressively harm itself, effectively accelerating its HP loss in combat.
* A spell that can induce confusion or self-directed behavior in a target, referenced in combat and used on puppet reindeer earlier in the campaign.
* A named temporary spell effect that timed out during the interaction with [[Amayon]], part of the sequence of spells cast in the demon negotiation/dramatic beats.
* A [[dread]] referenced as comparable to [[Dark Purpose]], an aura effect that causes disorienting, self-directed behavior.
* A comedic/chaotic spell learned by [[Donut]] that forces targets to stab themselves in the face, part of the [[Psychic]] kit.
* A debuff spell used by [[Donut]] that causes a mob to attack itself, accelerating its health loss.
* A spell used by [[Donut]] in prior contexts and referenced when the puppet reindeer were driven berserk, an effect that can induce confusion or self-harm in targets.
* A spell cast by [[Donut]] intended to disrupt or provoke [[Satan]] during the battle.
* A named spell used on [[Amayon]] that forced him to repeatedly punch himself, the spell timed out during the encounter, producing strong battlefield effects and altering the demon's behavior.
