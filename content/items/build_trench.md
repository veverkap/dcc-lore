# build_trench

• A commonly dropped scroll referenced by multiple crawlers, part of in-inventory scroll spam noticed by [[dungeon crawler carl]].
• A scroll suggested by [[dungeon crawler carl]] to tunnel underneath the [[stairwell]] so parties can fit beneath and avoid needing [[keys]]; explicitly recommended as a demolition/ tunneling tool.
• A scroll pulled from [[dungeon crawler carl]]'s inventory (by the [[totem]]) and dropped to the ground early in the fusion sequence.
• A scroll type commonly found in adventurer boxes; primarily a utility/construction scroll.
• Scrolls collected from previous floors used en masse to create the labyrinthian trench network, bunkers, and fall-back positions that form the defenders’ fortifications.
• A scroll referenced by [[dungeon crawler carl]] that can be used to tunnel underneath stairwell chambers; cited as a required tool for parties that need to shelter beneath an encased stairwell.
