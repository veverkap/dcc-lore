# dungeon_anarchists_cookbook

Here is the consolidated markdown list:

• The Dungeon Anarchist's [[Cookbook]] is a grimoire/[[cookbook]] referenced by [[dungeon crawler carl]] and contains strategies and notes relevant to dungeon anarchist tactics.
• It is a system-generated guide that auto-regenerates under specific conditions, transfers between crawls, and contains hidden text visible to the System AI.
• The book was originally awarded to [[Porthus the Rogue]] on the [[Ninth Floor]] and has been contributed to by various crawlers, including [[Crawler Batbilge]], [[Crawler Allister]], [[Dante]], [[Tipid]], [[Milk]], [[Volteeg]], and [[justice lights team]].
• The book has been authored in various editions, including the 24th edition, which contains recipes for [[potions]], [[explosives]], traps, monster weaknesses, and anonymous crawler notes.
• The book has an editable [[scratchpad]] page and contains hidden sections visible only to the System AI.
• Possesses an enforced rule that its hidden contents erase if its existence is revealed or the owner dies.
• The book has been used as a contextual source for the narrator's reflections and has been referenced in various chapters as a source of knowledge.
• It has been carried by various crawlers, including [[dungeon crawler carl]], who has used it to anticipate the [[Map of the Stars]] and other tactics.
• The book has been tied to various motivations, including [[Porthus]]'s desire to survive, accumulate wealth, and return in later seasons to contribute future editions.
