# doctor_ratchets_automaton_build-it_kit

• A large crafting kit containing parts to build over fifteen automatons, including servants and flame-throwing sentinels.
• Contains many crafting components useful for recipes and production.
• Includes parts to create numerous automatons and sentinel droids.
• Listed parts match recipes in the stolen instructional book and provide crafting materials.
