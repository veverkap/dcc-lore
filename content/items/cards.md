# cards

• Collectible, playable items in Donut's deck used to summon, cast, or modify combats, including the Magical Staff Card and Golden [[Combo]].
• Cards are central to party strategy, binding powerful beings to the party and driving decisions about which entities to capture.
• Enemies and players use cards in fights, with decks resetting after combat.
• Mobs drop card stacks and totems, and decks reset after combat.
• The squad's collection of [[totem]] cards used in combat, with tactical choices about summoning combinations.
• Donut's summoned card deck system contains spells, totems, and [[combo]] cards, such as Golden [[Combo]] and Magical Staff Card.
• Large card manifestations appear before Ysalte and Amayon to represent deck plays.
