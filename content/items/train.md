# train

• The conveyance used to send the [[Nightmare]] and its crew into the [[abyss]]; [[key]] to the sacrificial plan and the site of self-detonation by allies like [[Fire Brandy]] and [[Tizquick]].
• The vehicle used to ram into the [[Abyss]]; operated by characters like [[Tizquick]] and [[Fire Brandy]] and rigged with [[satchel of gunpowder]].
