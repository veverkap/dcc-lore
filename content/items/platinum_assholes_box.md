# platinum_assholes_box

• A loot box received by [[Katia]] that yielded items including a gambling chip for the [[desperado club]] and an upgrade to the [[Find Crawler]] skill.
• A high-tier reward item given for the [[Mass Casualty Event]] achievement, which tracks large-scale civilian casualties caused by the player's actions.
• A high-tier prize box that forced immediate consumption of its potion, yielding a potion that upgraded Carl's [[Fear (Spell)]] five levels.
• A platinum-tier reward for the [[Extinction Event]] achievement; one of Carl's boxes produced the [[Extinction Sigil Tattoo]].
