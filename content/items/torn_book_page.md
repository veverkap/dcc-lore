# torn_book_page

• Loot item extracted from [[Henrik]]'s inventory.
• Contains an extract of a cleric's journal describing the "She Who Wails / Wailing Shrieker" ghost and methods to defeat it (exorcism, destruction of corpse, banishment, electric shock).
• Provides narrative context for why Henrik's team attempted to give a ghost flesh.
