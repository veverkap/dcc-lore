# rolling_battle_formation

• An [[atrocity]] spell effect that forms a gigantic rolling ball composed of people, swine imagery, and even gods, which is remotely steerable and persists until it sustains enough damage.
• A spell that keeps thousands of people in a massive, invulnerable, and immobile ball, preventing exposure until the ball is broken.
