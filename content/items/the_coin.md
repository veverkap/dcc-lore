# the_coin

• The Coin is a mysterious item with four sides that presented the party with catastrophic choices when activated by the goddess.
• Carl grabbed the Coin and unlocked a fifth, random option.
• The Coin is central to the chapter's inciting crisis and Carl's [[fear]] that his intervention may have made things worse.
