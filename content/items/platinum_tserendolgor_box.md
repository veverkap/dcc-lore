# platinum_tserendolgor_box

• Platinum-tier reward tied to the "Chickenshit Exploit" achievement; notable celestial/rare loot container.
• Dog-shaped box containing [[automaton]] parts and XL [[fabricator]] components for the [[Automaton table]] and eventual [[Fabricator]] upgrades.
• High-tier reward box given by the [[Chickenshit Exploit]] achievement and associated with loot from [[Tserendolgor]].
• Named platinum-tier loot box (described as a jeweled, sitting German Shepherd-shaped box) that detonates theatrically and provides a spellbook (the [[Sentry]] spell) when opened.
