# temporary_macaroni

• Associated spell: Temporary Macaroni, casts at level 14.
• Conjures giant tubes of pasta (uncooked cannelloni) to form a protective tunnel, cast at level 14/15.
• Part of [[Luca Di.]]'s Pastamancer kit, presented as an associated spell.
• Cast by [[Donut]] from [[Banquet Fit for a Princess]], creates giant cannelloni tubes that can be chained to form tunnels, costs ten mana, and can be shaped/positioned by the caster.
