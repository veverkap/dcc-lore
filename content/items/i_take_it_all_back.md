# i_take_it_all_back

• A potion given to [[Prepotente]] and intended for [[Donut]], one of several targeted sponsor gifts noted by [[Mordecai]] and [[dungeon crawler carl]].
• A potion deployed by [[Donut]] that instantly transforms [[Kiwi]] from a velociraptor into an ursine form when detonated.
• A named benefactor potion received and presented to [[Princess Donut]], removing effects of [[Scolopendra’s Transmutation]] and labeled "Give this to Princess Donut. Use when the time is right. Do NOT give to Tina."
