# Multi-buzzsaw weapon
Gigantic, tethered cutting apparatus attached to [[Ruckus]]: roughly twenty-five feet long with multiple spinning blades designed to shear through structures and foes. When severed, it continued to spin and impact the terrain as lethal debris.
