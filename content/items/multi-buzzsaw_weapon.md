# multi-buzzsaw_weapon

• Gigantic, tethered cutting apparatus attached to [[Ruckus]].
• Roughly twenty-five feet long with multiple spinning blades designed to shear through structures and foes.
• When severed, it continued to spin and impact the terrain as lethal debris.
