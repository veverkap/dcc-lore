# Enchanted Toe Ring of the Well-Balanced
Legendary toe ring that must be equipped on the left pinky toe, granting Heart Balance, Mind Balance, and Physical Balance at Level 5 and conferring [[Extra Steady Balance]] and [[Walk on Air]] effects when at requisite levels.
