# enchanted_toe_ring_of_the_well-balanced

• Legendary toe [[ring]] that must be equipped on the left pinky toe, granting Heart Balance, Mind Balance, and Physical Balance at Level 5.
• Confers [[Extra Steady Balance]] and [[Walk on Air]] effects when at requisite levels.
