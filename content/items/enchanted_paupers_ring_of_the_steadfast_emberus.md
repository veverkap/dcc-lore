# Enchanted Pauper’s Ring of the Steadfast Emberus
A ring enchanted at [[Blessed Sun Enchantments shop]] and bound to [[Temple of Emberus]] loan terms. Appears as a Strength ring but has Emberus-only true properties: removes many health-seep/disease debuffs, has numbing effects, is cursed if worn by non-worshippers, and carries a debt that restricts selling and renouncing religion.

# [[Enchanted Pauper’s Ring of the Steadfast Emberus]]
Ring crafted by the [[Satyr Jeweler]] for [[dungeon crawler carl]] after trading his strength ring. When working, it removes health-seeping and disease debuffs, provides variable numbing against other debuffs, and is disguised as a Strength ring. Restrictions: wearable only by worshippers of [[Emberus]], carries debt-related clauses, becomes cursed if misused, and infects nearby non-worshippers with debuffs if worn improperly.
