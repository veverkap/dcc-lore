# enchanted_paupers_ring_of_the_steadfast_emberus

• A [[ring]] enchanted at [[Blessed Sun Enchantments shop]] and bound to [[Temple of Emberus]] loan terms.
• Removes many health-seep/disease debuffs, has numbing effects, and is cursed if worn by non-worshippers.
• Carries a debt that restricts selling and renouncing religion.
• Crafted by the [[Satyr Jeweler]] for [[dungeon crawler carl]] after trading his strength [[ring]].
• Removes health-seeping and disease debuffs, provides variable numbing against other debuffs.
• Disguised as a Strength [[ring]].
• Wearable only by worshippers of [[Emberus]].
• Carries debt-related clauses.
• Becomes cursed if misused.
• Infects nearby non-worshippers with debuffs if worn improperly.
