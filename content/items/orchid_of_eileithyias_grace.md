# Orchid of Eileithyia's Grace
The prize inside the [[Engaged Lock Box of the Night Wyrm]]. It can only be consumed by women (or seahorse males) and renders the eater unconscious at 1% HP for one hour. Surviving grants one of three permanent boons from [[Eileithyia]]: triple Constitution, [[Celestial Attendant]] transfer to the [[Twelfth Floor]], or forced return-to-birth-race plus pregnancy and export to the [[Kinder Facility]].
