# orchid_of_eileithyias_grace

• The [[Orchid]] of Eileithyia's Grace is a consumable item that can only be eaten by women (or seahorse males) and renders the eater unconscious at 1% HP for one hour.
• Surviving the effect grants one of three permanent boons from Eileithyia: triple Constitution, Celestial Attendant transfer to the Twelfth Floor, or forced return-to-birth-race plus pregnancy and export to the Kinder Facility.
• The item is found inside the Engaged Lock Box of the Night Wyrm.
• When eaten by Katia, it would render her into a coma for an hour and trigger a deal with Huanxin Jinx about ascent to the Twelfth Floor.
