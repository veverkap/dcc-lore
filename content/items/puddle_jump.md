# puddle_jump

• Teleportation ability/item used by [[Donut]] to instantly move the party, employed to reach [[The Yellow Zone]] [[saferoom]] and avoid swarms.
• Planned method for getting the party far away after using [[Protective Shell]] and [[invisibility]].
• Traversal skill/item referenced as an option to reach the top of the mound of dirt, mentioned alongside climbing and [[levitate]].
• Tactical option for approaching building entrances; protagonist elects not to use it in favor of sprinting through the field of debris.
• Escape/utility card in [[Donut]]'s deck referenced as available for movement/escape.
• Summon/escape card/spell available to [[Donut]] that can move characters but has risk (likely dumping users into Sheol fire if used here).
