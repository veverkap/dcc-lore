# [[Second Chance]]
A necromancy spellbook spell read by [[Donut]] that costs 10 mana and raises a lower-level monster from the dead to fight for a number of minutes equal to the spell's level (the example is level one = one minute). Described as distasteful by [[Donut]] but powerful tactically.
