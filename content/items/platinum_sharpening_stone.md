# platinum_sharpening_stone

• Buffs spiked offensive weapons, increasing damage by (2 x current level)% and adding +1 to existing stat buffs when applied.
• Used on [[dungeon crawler carl]]'s [[gauntlet]] in this chapter.
• A short-shelf-life enhancement applied to spiked offensive weapons, like Carl's [[gauntlet]], that multiplies damage and grants stat bonuses.
