# enchanted_pedicure_kit_of_the_sylph

• A magical foot-care kit granting temporary buffs when used inside a Safe or Personal Space and while barefoot: +15% bare-foot damage, +3 to Smush, Unbreakable (feet only), and Celestially Nimble and Tidy (feet-only trap-delay alarm).
• Obtained from a loot box.
