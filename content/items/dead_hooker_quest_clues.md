# Dead Hooker (Quest Clues)
A corpse recovered by [[dungeon crawler carl]] early in the chapter and labeled as "Quest Clues" in inventory. Used as evidence when presented to [[Miss Quill]] at the magistrate's reception desk, provoking a strong reaction and summoning guards.
