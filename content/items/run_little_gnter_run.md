# run_little_gnter_run

• A summoning spell that brings forth [[Günter]] as a living explosive decoy to attack the god.
• Received by [[dungeon crawler carl]] from the [[Legendary 1914 box]], with a lower destructive capacity compared to [[War Crime]].
• Conjures [[Little Günter]] to act as a distraction against foes like [[Prison Pocket]].
• A summoning spell/scroll that conjures "Little Günter," a kid used tactically in the encounter against [[Prison Pocket]].
