# flee_card

* A consumable special card that provides an escape option, allowing a squad to [[teleport]] or escape from combat.
* Can be stolen and played by opponents, causing [[keys]] to be transferred and squads to [[teleport]] out.
* Required to extract trapped players, such as [[Tran]], and is not carried by all squad members.
* Included in the [[Champion Pack]] and allows escape, with a warning that flee mechanics will change during the final [[phase]].
* Used by [[Princess Donut]] to drop one of her two [[keys]] during a forced card battle.
* Received by [[Team Sister Ines]] as a one-use special card that teleports the whole squad away from combat.
* Can be used by [[Donut]] during a card battle to drop one of her two [[keys]] (randomly).
* Central to the chapter's dilemmas about moving [[keys]] between parties.
* Can be found in enemy drops, such as the defeated mob's twenty-card drop.
* Used by [[Katia]] to note that [[Tran]] does not carry these [[cards]] in his deck and would need one to be evacuated.
