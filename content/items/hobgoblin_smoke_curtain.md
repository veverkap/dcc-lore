# hobgoblin_smoke_curtain

* A superior smoke device compared to goblin variants, mentioned as inexpensive and effective by [[Pustule]], with future supply offered.
* Consumable/utility item used by [[dungeon crawler carl]] to create thick smoke and obscure the [[train]] during the tower- [[climb]] and boarding sequence, buying [[Donut]] time to ascend the chain.
* Smoke- based consumable or trap listed in the [[explosives]] chapter of [[The Dungeon Anarchist’s Cookbook]] that can be infused with reagents to create area effects against undead.
* Thrown inventory item used by [[dungeon crawler carl]] to create thick smoke and obscure the [[train]] from nearby ghouls during [[Donut]]'s ascent.
* Existing delivery devices referenced as the design basis for the improvised aerosol/ dispersion system used to spray the repellant.
* Thrown inventory item used by [[dungeon crawler carl]] to create a smoke screen between the [[train]] and approaching ghouls, enabling [[Donut]]'s [[climb]] and temporary concealment.
* Thrown inventory item used by Carl to create a smoke screen and distract nearby ghouls while [[Donut]] ascends the chain to the tower.
* Specific variant of [[smoke curtain]] used earlier by [[dungeon crawler carl]] to hinder [[Minge]] and help NPCs escape.
* Consumable smoke-producing item used by [[dungeon crawler carl]] to obscure vision, described as powerful and used to conceal the group's movements.
* Listed crafting component/device used as the basis for Carl's modified aerosol delivery system; multiple curtains are combined to build the large spray devices intended to disperse [[Uncle Morty’s Insta Lawn-Kill and Undead Repellant, Extra-Strength Edition]].
