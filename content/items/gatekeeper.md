# [[Gatekeeper]]
Spell described in the [[Tome of Gatekeeper]] that temporarily removes a target's ability to use skills or abilities associated with their class (and for deities, their worshipper‑based spell/skill set), with a duration formula tied to intelligence and target stats. Marked as an Advance Reader Copy effective on the 12th floor.
