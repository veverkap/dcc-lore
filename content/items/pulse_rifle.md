# pulse_rifle

• Fires sustained magic-like pulses, resisted by [[shield]] spells and fragile to disruption.
• Looped from hunters and Goriffs.
• Can melt a target in a few shots at close range.
• Found stained with gore after Harbinger explodes.
• Firing sustained, full-strength magical pulses.
• Extremely dangerous to players, but pulses can be stopped by regular [[shield]] spells.
• Can be shattered.
