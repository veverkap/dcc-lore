# Final Blow
A high-tier skill owned by [[Mordecai]] that can target individuals and (in his plan) transfer a received spell effect to the targeted object—in this case the [[Puzzle Disk]]. It is described as a top-of-list ability and central to [[Mordecai]]'s intended sacrificial tactic.

# [[Final Blow]]
[[Final Blow]] is a high-tier skill possessed by [[Mordecai]] that lets him target specific individuals (previously used to detect hidden mobs). He contemplates using it to link himself to the puzzle and take a sacrificial hit to trigger the puzzle, but the party chooses the dart/containment route instead.

# [[Final Blow]]
A high-level targeting skill possessed by [[Mordecai]] that can mark an individual target so that spells affecting the marked person also affect the targeted object (used here to try to link the puzzle to himself). Presented as one of his core abilities and a catalyst for the crisis.
