# dirty_little_phased_bolt

• A broken, phased [[crossbow]] [[bolt]] that hides its entry point and applies effects including [[Temporary Amnesia]] and [[Suffering Bleed]] to victims.
• The [[bolt]] reduces targets to 10% (one [[bolt]]) or 1% (two bolts) health.
• It prevents normal credit/experience on kills and masks kill attribution.
• Invisible until touched, the [[bolt]] is removed from [[Katia]]'s body.
