# Dirty Little Phased Bolt
An invisible, phased crossbow bolt item that hides its entry wound; described as broken when recovered and able to apply the effects [[Temporary Amnesia]] and [[Suffering Bleed]], reducing victims to 10% (and a second to 1%) and obscuring kill credit. It is explicitly identified and examined in the cab.
