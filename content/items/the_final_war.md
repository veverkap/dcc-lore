# [[The Final War]]
A three-part, heirloom spell prepared by [[Miss Quill]] (and her lineage) intended to reclaim the [[Over City]] for the Skyfowl. It requires massive mana over three nights; the third act would unleash beasts and slaughter most inhabitants except those protected by the spell.
