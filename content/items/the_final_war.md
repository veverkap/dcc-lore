# the_final_war

• A three-part, heirloom spell prepared by [[Miss Quill]] (and her lineage) intended to reclaim the [[Over City]] for the Skyfowl.
• Requires massive mana over three nights.
• The third act would unleash beasts and slaughter most inhabitants except those protected by the spell.
