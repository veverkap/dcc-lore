# gunpowder

• Standard explosive component used within devices like [[Baby Uno]] and [[oh shit baby]] to produce large detonations.
• Consumable explosive material used to reveal an invisible creature by outlining it with spilled powder, allowing visibility of movement and identification of the creature.
• Bulk chemical explosive material sold in barrels at [[Hobgobs and Boom Sticks]] and stocked alongside other explosive components.
• Throwable consumable explosive used by [[dungeon crawler carl]] to reveal an invisible Night Weasel by leaving a dust outline and triggering visible reactions when ignited or struck.
