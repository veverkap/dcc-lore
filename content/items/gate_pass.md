# gate_pass

• A slip of paper found in [[GumGum]]'s inventory, issued by Magistrate Featherfall, which functions like a "get-out-of-jail" authorization, exempting the holder from guard interference within the issuing township.
• Used by [[dungeon crawler carl]] to legitimize possession of [[Dead Hooker (Quest Clues)]] and justify evidence to town guards.
• Stamped with "Magistrate Featherfall" and used to explain how some corpses and movements go unchecked.
