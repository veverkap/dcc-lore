# Self-Immolation
Level-10 Emberus spell granted by the [[Baptism by Fire]] mechanic. Instantaneous sphere of "Sheol Fire" centered on the caster (1.5m radius at level 10), once-per-day cooldown tied to shrine/obeisance resets. Highly destructive and dangerous in enclosed or flammable environments.
