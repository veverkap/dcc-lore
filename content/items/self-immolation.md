# self-immolation

• Level-10 Emberus spell granted by the [[Baptism by Fire]] mechanic, creating a 1.5m radius sphere of "Sheol Fire" around the caster with a 1-foot buffer, once-per-day cooldown tied to [[shrine]]/obeisance resets.
• Creates an area of intense flame that burns allies and burns a [[hole]] through the floor, causing a pocket breach and a fall into lower rooms.
• Can be cast by [[Dungeon Crawler Carl]] while under partial control of [[Shi Maria]] or possessed by her.
• Highly destructive and dangerous in enclosed or flammable environments.
• [[Sheol Fire]] has contact and environmental effects.
