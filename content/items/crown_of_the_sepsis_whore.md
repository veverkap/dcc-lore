# crown_of_the_sepsis_whore

• A unique, Fleeting crown that grants +5 Intelligence and +5 to the Good First Impression skill, and causes attacks to have a chance to inflict the Sepsis debuff.
• Wearing it permanently places the wearer in the royal succession of the [[sultanate]] on the [[Ninth Floor]], and removal does not rescind that status – obligating the wearer to participate in lethal royal succession rules.
• Disappears if removed; originally first loot received by [[Donut]], later found on [[Eva]] and then melds into [[Katia]]’s head on transfer – with a gameplay restriction that only one of Donut or Katia may advance from the ninth to tenth floor while it is on a head.
• Grants stats but will vanish if removed; during the pulses it crumbles into dust for [[Donut]] and disappears from at least one other crawler mid-removal.
• A cursed/important headpiece worn by [[Katia]] during the chapter, pivotal to her predicament and motivates Carl’s choice to repair [[Yemaya]]'s [[shrine]].
• Named artifact referenced by [[Eileithyia]] in private conversation; implied to have authority to be "cleared" by the goddess as part of political maneuvering on the ninth floor.
• Part of the darker, real-world narrative threads referenced by the protagonist.
• A named item previously seen on [[Katia]]’s head; part of the plot item that offers a pathway (via a flower) to the 12th floor; its use requires trust in [[Huanxin Jinx]] and is a [[key]] strategic option for [[Katia]].
• A named squad [[flag]] / expected emblem referenced in relation to [[Katia]]'s usual squad; invoked as flavor/identity for her player-killer [[flag]] imagery.
