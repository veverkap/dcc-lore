# Enchanted Crown of the Sepsis Whore
A Unique, Fleeting crown that grants +5 Intelligence and +5 to the Good First Impression skill, and causes attacks to have a chance to inflict the Sepsis debuff. Wearing it permanently places the wearer in the royal succession of the [[sultanate]] on the [[Ninth Floor]], and removal does not rescind that status — obligating the wearer to participate in lethal royal succession rules.
