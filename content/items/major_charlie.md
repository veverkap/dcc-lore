# Major Charlie
A potion (also referred to as "Brain Juice") brewed by [[Mordecai]]. Temporarily raises intelligence by +10 for thirty seconds and then reduces intelligence by −10 for five minutes as a side effect. Used by [[dungeon crawler carl]] to extend the range of the [[Ping]] spell.
