# major_charlie

• A potion brewed by [[Mordecai]], also referred to as "Brain Juice", which temporarily raises intelligence by +10 for 30 seconds and then reduces intelligence by -10 for 5 minutes as a side effect.
• Used by [[dungeon crawler carl]] to extend the range of the [[Ping]] spell.
