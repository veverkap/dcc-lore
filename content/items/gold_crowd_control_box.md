# gold_crowd_control_box

• Higher-tier reward box granted to [[dungeon crawler carl]] for killing more than fifteen mobs with a single explosive attack.
• Given as loot after the [[dynamite]] kill.
• Contains crowd-control and defensive items like [[Enchanted Spiked Kneepads of the Shade Gnoll Riot Forces]], [[confusing fog]], and [[iron skin potion]].
