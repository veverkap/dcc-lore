# tghee_card

• Consumable [[combo]] card that temporarily combines two monster [[cards]] into a single creature; summoning length equals the shorter summon [[phase]] and both monsters are tagged out for the remainder of combat.
• Requires squad leader to hold.
• Contains the summon information for [[Skylar Spinach]] (the splitter ghommid), including level, constitution, skills, and warnings about automatic squad placement and tearing to remove.
• Auto-activates into an empty squad slot unless torn.
• Collecting auto-activates into an empty squad slot unless torn.
• Can be used to summon [[Lazarus-A-Bang-Bang]].
• Level 140, origin [[combo]], summoning duration, constitution, role tags (ranged/healer/affliction), notable attacks (e.g., bazooka attack, Apocalyptic Plague, Resurrect [[Totem]]), and a note about being frozen via a [[Glow-Up]].
• Dropped by [[Yago]] and examined by [[dungeon crawler carl]].
• Labeled a [[Totem]] Card with mob origin (Cuba), level, stats (e.g., Constitution 125), summon duration (60s), and squad activation/ownership rules (owned by [[Team Sister Ines]] in this instance).
• Unique [[totem]] card type used to summon [[Lazarus-A-Bang-Bang]] in this chapter.
• Used to explain the archetype represented by [[Ysalte]] in interfaith lore.
• Card-based combat mechanic central to fights on the [[Eighth Level]].
• Deck-driven opponents and card effects define the encounters that squads must overcome.
• Orange [[totem]] card depicting a ranged undead healer named “Uzi Jesus” with numerous skills (e.g., Blood into Lead, Bullet Hell, Resurrect [[Totem]]).
