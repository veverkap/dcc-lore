# Gold Junkie’s box
A reward item granted by the [[You Crossed a Line You Didn’t Even Know Was There]] achievement. Part of the loot economy and achievement reward structure in Dungeon Crawler World.

# Gold Junkie's Box
A type of prize box Carl opens (from using the [[Ring of Divine Suffering]]). The box produced a spiderweb tattoo (the [[Night Wyrm’s Nasty Little Web of Suffering]]) in this chapter and otherwise emitted no conventional loot.
