# gold_junkies_box

• A reward item granted by the [[You Crossed a Line You Didn’t Even Know Was There]] achievement.
• A type of prize box Carl opens, associated with using the [[Ring of Divine Suffering]], which produced the Night Wyrm's Nasty Little Web of Suffering [[tattoo]].
• A reward item received by [[dungeon crawler carl]] for using the [[artifact of Divine Suffering]] (earned via the "You Crossed a Line..." achievement).
