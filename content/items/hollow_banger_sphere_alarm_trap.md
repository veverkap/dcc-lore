# [[Hollow Banger Sphere (alarm trap)]]
A bass-heavy, impact-detonated trap Carl crafted and labeled "Bass heavy. For distraction." Plays "Jump Around!" and is sealed in a metal ball to survive the ooze's grasp; used to distract [[Mrs. Ghazi]] during the assault.
