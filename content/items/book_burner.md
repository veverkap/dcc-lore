# Book Burner
An anti-magic ability granted by the [[Upgrade Patch. Medium.]] Once per day, lets the user cause an opponent their level or lower to forget a spell for 30 hours (chance to fail increases vs higher-level opponents). Must specify the exact spell name or target the last spell cast.
