# flag

• Used by [[dungeon crawler carl]] to "flag" an enemy by stabbing point-first, as seen in the chapter where Carl uses a flag as an impaling implement against the donkey-snake ghommid.
• Pulled and thrust into the dying [[Asojano]] to trigger the infection effect that produces [[SlugPox]] (turning the boss into a card and spawning disease).
• Used point-first as a makeshift dagger to impale a lunging donkey-snake ghommid, killing the mob and removing Carl's Terrified debuff.
