# [[Hand-shaped key console]]
The magical sensor plate(s) in rapid-response cart cockpits that require gnoll hands to activate the engine and portal. Each cockpit has two consoles spaced so a single gnoll cannot activate both; the consoles enforce the cart's two-gnoll operator requirement.
