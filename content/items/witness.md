# witness

• A spell that granted [[invulnerability]] to individuals; reportedly removed by [[Eris]] for the ball, but the [[Rolling Battle Formation]] kept [[invulnerability]] intact.
• A debilitating spell that paralyzes targets while forcing them to feel everything, similar to being controlled by [[Maggie]].
