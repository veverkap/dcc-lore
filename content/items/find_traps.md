# find_traps

• A trap-detection skill referenced by [[dungeon crawler carl]] that he wishes to [[train]] further after being caught by a triggered trap; treated as an active player ability in the chapter.
• A detection skill/ability that activated when [[dungeon crawler carl]] inspected Donut’s suitcase, producing the alert text and allowing the discovery of the [[Literal Fire Ants]] trap.
• An active detection skill used by the narrator to reveal multiple trap info boxes and identify armed [[automatons]] rigged with [[satchel of gunpowder]] on the battlefield.
• [[dungeon crawler carl]]’s trap-detection ability used to warn about traps (he mentions needing to [[train]] it more after failing to detect a foot-triggered trap in time).
• An active skill used by the narrator that detects hidden traps and gates (it identified a freeze-trap [[gate]] in the hallway between floors).
