# lighter

• A simple ignition tool often paired with items like [[Dynamite]] and [[Satchel of Gunpowder]].
• Can be used to ignite items such as [[dynamite]] and [[Satchel of Gunpowder]].
• Present as a minor inventory tool, used to [[light]] the [[torch]] before the in-inventory prompt auto-activated the [[torch]] instead.
• Paired with [[explosives]] in the [[Silver Goblin Box]].
