• A newly obtained spell for [[Donut]] tied to her singing ability; part of her kit for casting via voice.
• Increases Dexterity and amplifies party spell power; effectiveness scales with how well the song is sung.
• A singing- based spell that grants a dexterity [[buff]] to allies during combat; described as a vocal spell tied to her new abilities and performance mechanics.
• A party-support spell (song) that increases Dexterity and boosts spell power for allies; one of [[Donut]]'s short karaoke-style songs.
