# mystic_card

• A card subtype in the Crawl system with persistent or gameplay-affecting powers, distinct from utility or consumable special [[cards]].
• Examples include [[thief]] and [[Mystic card]].
• A rare card category that must remain in-hand and grants powerful persistent effects like decreased draw time.
