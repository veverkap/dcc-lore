# 103rd

• A military unit paired with the [[105th]] under [[Florin]] for the northern diversion into the former Operatic territory during the operation.
• A unit moving up from [[Shanty Town]] (with [[Bodi]] and [[Prepotente]]) intended to draw [[Madness]] defender attention as part of the operation.
• The allied battalion positioned opposite the castle, responsible for a feint and planned retreat to draw out [[Madness]] forces.
• Two numbered military units of Carl's forces advancing into former [[Operatic territory]], encountering non-magical traps and probing toward orc defensive lines.
• Recon forces aiding the northern defensive effort alongside the [[101st]] and [[105th]].
