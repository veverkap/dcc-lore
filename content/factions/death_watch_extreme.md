# death_watch_extreme

• [[Death Watch Extreme]] is a broadcast/entertainment sponsor associated with the "Death Challenge!" presentation, hosted by [[Prince Maestro]].
• The show is referenced during the "Death Challenge!" and is introduced as the host of [[Death Watch Extreme]].
• [[Death Watch Extreme]] is a high-profile in-universe show/organization that announced a hiatus while restructuring.
• The show is part of the media landscape affecting public attention.
