# veriluxx

* A corporate group tied to Donut's merchandising and sponsorship, with possible ties to larger financial/ political entities and questionable motives for mass giveaways.
* A sponsor/manufacturer brand that sells collectible robot pets and ties into [[Syndicate]]-era merch distributed in the world.
* A corporation/manufacturer associated with the Robot Donut (Toy) and possibly a front for larger corporate or political interests tied to expansionary lending groups.
* A brand/program behind the "New Toys Showcase" commercial, operating toy lines like Veriluxx RealPet companions and coordinating low-price toy marketing at fueling depots/waystations.
* A possible suspect in remote detonation or secret plans, mentioned by Donut and Dungeon Crawler Carl as a potential antagonist in the broader setting.
* A failed sponsor for Donut, listed among commercial sponsor entities influencing crawler branding and loot.
* A sponsor/company mentioned in discussions about the toy doll's voice and prototype delivery.
* A possible outside party that might have remotely detonated the toy, referenced during Donut's speculation and Orren the Liaison's questioning.
* A commercial brand/manufacturer that produces budget AI toys and benefactor boxes, appearing as a consumer-goods faction.
