# council

• The Council is a faction that triggered the primal engines and shaped the crawl's formation.
• The Council is the governing authority overseeing legal rulings and contracts in the crawl.
• The Council is unwilling or unable to shut down the crawl even if the AI goes "primal" due to financial incentives.
• The Council prioritizes profit and lets the AI act for spectacle.
• The Council is the [[decision]]-maker that typically manages AIs, court rulings, and [[showrunners]].
