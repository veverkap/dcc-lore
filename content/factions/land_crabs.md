# land_crabs

• Collective name for migrating crab mobs, including named individuals like [[raul the crab]], massing in the south to invade the water and attack the [[Monk Seals]] in "The Chowder War."
• A massive migrating faction of armored land/crab mobs moving toward the ocean in a coordinated invasion, organized into martial traditions (e.g., Red Maníseros) and engaged in the event known as the [[Chowder War]] against the [[Monk Seals]]; capable of overwhelming numbers and large-scale environmental movement.
• The opposing faction to the [[Monk Seals]] in the local Chowder War; engaged in a brutal, escalating conflict on the beach.
• A sponsor group tied to [[Hekla]] and many members of Brynhild’'s [[Daughters]]; criticized for providing little or no sponsor gear and thus contributing to the party’'s resource problems.
