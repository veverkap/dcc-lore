# ikea

• [[IKEA]] is a global furniture and home goods retailer that carries wall art similar to the author's designs.
• The retailer is mentioned alongside [[Target]] and [[Home Goods]] as outlets where the author's designs could be sold or seen.
• [[Matt Dinniman]] imagines his art could be sold at [[IKEA]].
• [[IKEA]] is an international home furnishings retailer that carries "weird wall art" referenced by the author.
