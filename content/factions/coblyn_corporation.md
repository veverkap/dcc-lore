# coblyn_corporation

• Corporate entity founded by the [[Knockers]] to commercialize their products.
• Runs manufacturing and retail operations related to the chapter's illicit goods.
• Central to the backstory of [[Toddler Cocaine]].
• Responsible for protections around the area, including cheaply implemented safeguards that resulted in contagious, regenerating infused elementals in the molten-metal room.
