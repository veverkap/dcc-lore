# merc_guild

• A faction on the sixth floor of the [[desperado club]] where mercenaries can be hired, with daily fees and contractual obligations causing friction.
• Used by [[dungeon crawler carl]] to hire mercenaries, with some mercenaries regenerating and others dying for real.
• Owns mercenaries like [[Doctor Bones]], with a purchasable "barracks upgrade" to retain powerful mercs for subsequent floors.
