# mantis

Here is the consolidated markdown list:

• A species/faction studying System AIs and developing controllable systems, impacting residuals' strategies.
• An alien or corporate group mining elements, resurrecting AIs, and grafting them into planetary engines to produce fuel.
• Insectile adversaries swarming the castle, responsible for mass decapitation of guards, and tied to [[Diwata/Circe]].
• Alien insectoid faction discoverers of ancient production facilities and creators/developers of macro AIs, responsible for technological breakthroughs.
• An alien/species faction building the "Frankenstein" system and producing repeated "macro AI" constructs, implicated in harvesting elements from planetary crawls.
• A species/faction that recently declared intentions to leave the [[Syndicate]] shortly before [[Hive Home]] went dark, with their departure being a political complication.
• The Mantis government unexpectedly abandoned their faction-wars stake, tied to the [[Burrowers]] and contrasting with other sponsor withdrawals.
