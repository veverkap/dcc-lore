# bactrians

• A local faction in the Necropolis of Anser, part of regional politics.
• Share a secret artifact in Hump Town's town hall that deters gnome bombing.
• Hold something in town hall that deters gnome attacks on Hump Town.
• Have a different indemnity with the [[gnomes]] involving a favored pet swine.
• Contrasted with the [[Dromedarians]]' watch arrangement.
