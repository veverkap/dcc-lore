# white_dragons

• [[White Dragons]]: a potential source of borrowed magical defenses for a castle low on mages.

• [[White Dragons]]: a rank affiliation for [[Harpocrates]], noted as a Legionnaire.
• [[Li Na's 107th White Dragon Urban Defense battalion]]: an urban defense battalion mixed with [[Semeru]] [[dwarves]] and [[Larracos]] citizens, holding the ring around the lip of [[Larracos]] and [[Shanty Town]].
• [[Li Na's 107th White Dragon Urban Defense battalion]]: the unit containing [[Katia]], [[Daniel Bautista]], [[Li Na]], and others, staged in [[Shanty Town]] as part of Group Four for the raid.
