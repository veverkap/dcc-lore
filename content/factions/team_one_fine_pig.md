# team_one_fine_pig

• A competing team occupying the first spot on the track, described as having a large military-style vehicle similar to those driven by [[Imani]] and [[Elle]].
• Racing/mercenary group present on the hill, traveling in an armored truck that projects a cube-shaped shield, with members including [[Nico]], [[Dario]], and hired mercenary [[Pontiff]].
• A team eliminated due to the expiration of both racers, referenced in the system notification about team eliminations.
