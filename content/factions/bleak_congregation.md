# bleak_congregation

• A team/faction in the heats and races, associated with the [[Minister of Blood-Letting]] and characterized by a spooky aesthetic and unconventional vehicles.
• The team is ahead of [[Imani]]'s group on the course and is referenced in radio communications about positioning.
• A faction whose skeleton members assist in cleaning up the nickels in the bar scene and represent an organized NPC group present at the [[Desperado]] Club.
• A sponsor/group that left the realm before the final heat, causing a reshuffling of teams, and is associated with undead-themed [[NPCs]] like the [[Minister of Blood-Letting]].
• The team is referenced by the presence of a skeleton member who accompanied [[Carl]] onto the stage.
