# sensation_entertainment_incorporated

• Media corporation tied to Carl's prior contractual obligations, recently purchased by a subsidiary of [[Dream]], creating legal pressure on Carl via [[Quasar]].
• Implicated in influencing an elite to commit an assassination attempt, accused of corporate interference by [[Baroness Victory]].
• Handles program contracts and cross-program obligations, enforces participation rules, and references the [[Vengeance of the Daughter]] program.
• Central to bargaining over [[Dungeon Crawler Carl]]'s survival incentives and the proposed continuation of the program [[Vengeance of the Daughter]].
• [[Quasar]] is trying to secure favors for this company, proposed as the recipient of an emergency extension to run the program [[Vengeance of the Daughter]] beyond the sixth floor collapse.
• Negotiates with [[Borant Corporation]] and manages broadcasting decisions affecting [[Dungeon Crawler Carl]] and [[Donut]].
