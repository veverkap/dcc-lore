# The Sapient Mobs
[[The Sapient Mobs]] are a faction that includes intelligent monster groups such as [[Shadow Mimics]], the [[Ogre Imperium]], [[Krakaren Prime]], and the [[War Mages]] — powerful but unlikely to cooperate long-term.
