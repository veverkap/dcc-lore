# transit_security

• In-universe enforcement/response organization that investigates station disturbances and irregularities at employee platforms.
• Implied to investigate missing/empty employee settlements, such as at Station 60.
• Expected to investigate anomalies like fake settlements.
• Represents in-universe enforcement/security bureaucracy.
• Comprises gnolls who normally crew and escort interdiction vehicles on the [[the tangle]].
