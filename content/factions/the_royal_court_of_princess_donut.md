# the_royal_court_of_princess_donut

• [[The Royal Court of Princess Donut]] is a faction including [[Donut]] that finished seventh in Heat One, and references [[Princess Donut]]'s persona.
• The team placed first in Heat Three, led by [[Donut]] and including members such as [[Gluteus]] and [[Carl]], and declared First Place in the official race results.
• The team associated with [[Donut]] finished Fourth Place in Heat Five, representing [[Princess Donut]]'s group.
• The faction/team centered on [[Donut]] and affiliated with [[Carl]] placed second in Heat Six results.
• The team placed first in Heat Four, associated with [[Donut]] and noted in the race results printed at the end of the chapter.
• The team placed seventh in Heat Two, associated thematically with [[Donut]].
