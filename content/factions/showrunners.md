# showrunners

• The meta-authority controlling broadcasts and issuing behavioral suggestions to elites, such as [[Signet]], and coordinating spectacle and compliance.
• Media/production operators who manage public-facing footage of crawls, editing out evidence of [[Residuals]] and controlling public memory of surface groups.
• The production/editing authorities who control footage of surface activity, attempting to edit out evidence of [[Residuals]] and other messy elements to shape public memory and presentation.
• The production/administrative authority of the floor, normally enforcing rules alongside the AI, with [[Tipid]] noting that mention of certain topics would normally draw punishment from the AI and the showrunners.
