# team_sparkles

* Racing team composed of [[Dwight]] (Sparkling Unicorn) and [[Lucienne]] (Mandagot) using an [[Avernus Creeper]] mount, placing sixth in Heat One.
* Team with a vine-covered vehicle, responsible for deploying a one-time-use tanklike cannon that destroys [[Girth the Trouble]]'s car.
* First-place team in Heat Two.
* Rival team with a rocket-equipped tumbleweed vehicle, briefly passed by the protagonists and became coated in slime from [[Denial of Service missiles]].
* Second-place team in Heat Three, represented by [[Dwight]] and [[Lucienne]] in a vine vehicle.
* Team with a rocket device on a tumbleweed vehicle, briefly passed by the truck crew while encased in [[Denial of Service missiles]] slime.
* Second-place team in Heat Four.
* Team with a particular set of booster rockets that [[Carl]] considers copying via regular upgrades.
* First-place team in Heat Five.
* First-place team in Heat Six.
