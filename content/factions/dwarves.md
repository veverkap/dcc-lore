# dwarves

• A coordinated group of dwarves engaged in noisy digging and lava abatement near [[King's Point]].
• The [[Semeru]] Dwarves built a castle along the roots of the [[All Tree]] near [[Larracos]].
• The [[Semeru]] Dwarves will transfer to the [[Court of the Ascendency]] along with [[NPCs]] and [[changelings]].
• The [[Semeru]] Dwarves are a dwarf people from the ninth floor, implicated in foundational disasters in other floors.
• Dwarven allies, known as the Semuru dwarves, can be found in the large tunnel space, where they cast healing spells, fortify the ceiling, and organize defense and counterattacks.
