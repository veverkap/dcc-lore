# elves

* The High Elves are a powerful elven faction with a protected royal court, surviving [[Scolopendra]]'s attack via stolen protective magic or soul resources.
* They are a cultural/ political faction with a protected royal court, mentioned as recipients of tithes from the Confederates, and as a potential external force that may field an army if Big Tina's threat escalates.
* The High Elves are the elven polity and inhabitants of the Castle of the High Elves, organized under Queen Imogen of the High Elves, and treated as a factional power with a country-boss role in the event.
* They are the elven polity ruled by Queen Imogen of the High Elves, whose castle hosted the Butcher's Masquerade and was central to the party's later strategic position.
* The High Elves are an influential faction and ruling class that includes King Finian's former line and Imogen, who enacted transfigurations and political domination that displaced groups like the [[Chee]].
* They are a powerful elven group referenced in lore as recipients of tithes from the Confederate government, used to explain the government's financial troubles that led to the ill-fated fundraiser.
* The High Elves are a regional faction mentioned as a potential external military response if Big Tina's rampage escalates in the hunting grounds.
* They are the race/faction referenced in relation to mining expeditions and ownership of the Memorial Crystal, with the high elf queen said to normally wear the crystal.
* The High Elves are the cultural/ political group that Queen Imogen of the High Elves rules over, with the ballroom being an event of the High Elves.
* The Elves are a political and territorial group whose holdings include the Castle of the High Elves and the Elven Border, expected to contest the encroaching [[brambles]].
* They are an NPC faction of mounted and black-clad fighters allied with Queen Imogen of the High Elves, largely wiped out during the conflict in the Bramble Dome and Basement Pit.
* The Elves are general elven forces around the castle fighting [[brambles]] and mantises, described as coming out of the woods and defending the castle.
* The [[Dream]] Elves are a faction or group referenced as already having been wiped out by Carl's party earlier on the floor (status uncertain in-text).
* The Bush Elves are the local elven populace devastated by [[Scolopendra]]'s attack, contrasted with the Elves who survived under defensive magic.
* The Elven Castle Guards are multiple tiers of elven security personnel who enforce castle rules, perform checks, and maintain the masquerade protections.
