# bloom

* A [[kua-tin]] political party that runs the region of [[borant corporation]] and holds preferential treatment for party members, with [[Loita]] being associated with this party.
* The party is represented by a dahlia-like emblem, which is seen on [[Loita]]'s chest and is deemed important for future floors.
* Members of the party receive better treatment, and the party wields influence over outreach representation on [[Borant]].
* The party is also associated with a symbol worn by [[Loita]], which is noted as important symbology to the mudskippers and potentially significant for future floors.
* The party is involved in the corporate networks behind charitable-appearing campaigns like the toy distribution.
* The party is also associated with a cultural purity movement that seeks to "reawaken" the [[kua-tin]] and is willing to enforce strict boundaries.
* The party is opposed to dry/human culture and is willing to take extreme measures to maintain their cultural purity.
* The party is also involved in the [[Borant]] system's civil war, with their defeat and related casualties being discussed as off-world context that impacts in-dungeon stakeholders.
