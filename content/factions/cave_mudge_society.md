# cave_mudge_society

• The Cave Mudge society is tied to the [[Cave Mudge Bonker]] species, with a once star-faring people now regressed to a war-like, stone-age existence.
• They are mentioned in a short hierarchy note explaining the Bonker's social rank and alleged history.
