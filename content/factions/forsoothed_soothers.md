# forsoothed_soothers

• An alien people to which [[Evo]] belongs, also known as "Soothers" in casual usage.
• Presented as a recognizable race with media presence and history across human cultural records.
• The ruling class of Forsoothed used floating protection platforms to hoard food, historically opposing the human Leprous Bandit.
