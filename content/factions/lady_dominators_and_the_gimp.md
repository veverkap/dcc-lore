# lady_dominators_and_the_gimp

• A rival crawler team whose garage is adjacent to Carl's spot; Dong, Bucket Boy, and Splash Zone are at their closed garage door trying to reach a captive Corky inside.
• A competing team that placed fifth in "Heat Two" per the system results shown at the chapter's end.
• A team that finished Third Place in Heat One and is listed on the race scoreboard at the finish.
• A team that placed third in Heat Three; associated with the pursuing [[Dominators]] mentioned during the race.
• A rival team whose car is pursuing the truck during the race; positioned directly behind the protagonist vehicle in the convoy.
• A team mentioned as being under the impression they were cooperating with the narrator's team for the race; tactical relationships affect race behavior.
