# sultanate

Here is the consolidated markdown list:

• The Sultanate is a geopolitical faction that has been targeted by the [[Syndicate]] and is entangled in events from prior seasons.
• The Sultanate is a faction/nation referenced in historical context, mentioned as a party that lost funds to [[Syndicate]] actions in past seasons.
• The Blood Sultanate is a named crawler family/faction, membership of which is conferred by items like Donut's tiara, and its survival status affects escape from the Ninth Floor.
• The Blood Sultanate is a surface faction mentioned as a [[target]] the Skull Clan could be provoked into attacking.
• The Blood Sultanate is the royal faction that rules the Ninth Floor, with membership in its royal line imposing a rule requiring royal members to slay the Sultan and all other royals before descending.
• The Blood Sultanate is a faction associated with Donut's title "Princess Donut of the Blood Sultanate" and is referenced in the unique goggles description.
• The Blood Sultanate is a sponsoring/external faction referenced in the chapter, attempting to contest the temple summoning but lacking standing under the temple rules.
• The Blood Sultanate is a defeated faction whose assets are redistributed to surviving blood relatives and ultimately to the Princess [[Posse]].
• The Blood Sultanate is the original naga political/military faction referenced as having been defeated on the floor.
• The Blood Sultanate is a faction associated with Princess Donut and is referenced as a gift from [[Prism]] to Princess Donut of the Blood Sultanate.
• The Blood Sultanate is a named faction on [[Larracos]]' ninth-floor petal system, described as a poorer faction that typically brings fewer outside people into its army.
• The Blood Sultanate is a faction whose representative Rishi attends the meeting, connected to royal families and binding honor obligations.
• The Blood Sultanate is a recently eliminated faction that formerly owned sector four, its lands turned into trapfields by the protagonists.
• The Blood Sultanate of the Naga is a historical factional operator known for running a financially disastrous Crawl season that gifted many Celestial boxes.
