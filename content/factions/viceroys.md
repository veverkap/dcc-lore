# viceroys

• Another team with local population voting rights referenced as a potential swing vote in the Faction Wars decisions.
• Territorial authorities referenced as potential claimants to land if [[dungeon crawler carl]]'s party abandons it; part of the broader political landscape.
• A scholarly/exploratory group devoted to understanding life and death; they use the name [[The Beautiful Place]] for the trans- dimensional creature and study its effects.
• Named leadership or ruling faction referenced as likely holding back their armies to defend strongholds during the engagement.
• An interstellar organization referenced as providing medical/ scientific outreach and responsible for devices like the [[Grease Monkey]]; one Viceroy (Houston) was present in the castle.
• A governing/financial faction referenced when [[Tipid]] had to go into debt to them to repair his scrambled brain, indicating a power structure outside the immediate group.
• Faction in the [[Faction Wars]] roster; listed alongside the [[operatic collective]] and [[lemig sortition]] for having citizens who can vote on expenditures.
• A rival team credited with summoning the goddess [[Mazu]] in a past season, an action that altered terrain (razing buildings) and indirectly buried the temple entrance to [[Club Vanquisher]], impacting [[Princess Vinata]]'s escape options.
• Masked, violent actors from faction wars referenced by [[Tipid]]'s memory. Also called "the [[Madness]]," they are associated with brutal surgical cruelties and a notorious chief surgeon who performed horrific brain- afflicting spells.
