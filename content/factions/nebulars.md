# nebulars

• A sponsor/collective called the "Nebulars" backed the god [[Ysalte]] and is involved in higher-tier politics on the floors.
• The Nebulars are a cult-like hunter/sponsor faction associated with hats, implicated in manipulating [[Sister Ines]] and worshipping the thing that runs the center system.
• They are a pacifist faction in the open world but active and strategic inside the dungeon, seeking seats on the [[Syndicate]] crawl [[council]] by winning contests like the [[Celestial Ascendency]].
• The Nebular Sin Patrol is a specific hunting contingent of young male priest-[[hunters]] who focus on exterminating [[crawlers]] and sometimes secure deity sponsorships.
• The Nebulars are organized hunting contingent representing the faction at crawls, funding hunts, training priest-students as [[hunters]], and campaigning for crawl-running rights while targeting certain player archetypes.
• They are a past hunting/team example tied to religious Nebulars who previously performed poorly on the sixth floor.
• The Nebulars are a sponsoring faction/group referenced as the affiliation of [[Pontifex Shine]], and are therefore connected to the patronage of [[Ysalte]].
• They are a sponsor group/faction that has sponsored a god that marked [[Louis]] for death and are portrayed as "weird" in the recap.
