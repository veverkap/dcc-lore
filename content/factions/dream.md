# dream

Here is the consolidated markdown list:

• A named faction of zebra-riding [[elves]] specializing in poison, druid magic, and long-range artillery, with the mother Epitome Noflex of the Dream tied to this faction.
• An opposing faction responsible for heavy artillery bombardment and many "pasty" enemy combatants, operating trebuchet batteries and regular troops on multiple fronts.
• A faction that can cede land and castles if system conditions are not met, one of the competing territorial powers.
• A rival faction that currently holds [[Prism]] castle after a recent flip, part of the contested possession dynamic mentioned by Dungeon Crawler Carl.
• A faction whose Throne Room is being contested by Team Retribution during the Faction Wars.
• A faction associated with the Dream castle and represented by an adjutant Fopsy, whose remains/stronghold are being secured.
• A faction referenced as "the Dream" (zebra-riding [[elves]] who use poison, druid magic, and long-range artillery), identified in conjunction with the plaintiff Epitome Noflex of the Dream.
• A powerful organization/ political entity in the setting, renting out the moon for events like the Fantasy Hunter Camp and exerting influence over hunting grounds.
• A powerful faction/consortium of dream [[elves]] controlling food patents and agricultural interests, represented by Epitome Tagg at the meeting.
• A faction/corporate entity that joined the truce/alliance forming [[Bloc]], also referenced as a corporate buyer of Sensation Entertainment, Incorporated in external contractual context.
• A faction mentioned as preparing long-range artillery batteries opposite the Princess [[Posse]]'s border, one of the nine competing teams in the [[Larracos]] map.
• A faction attacking force responsible for the southern-front artillery barrage, referenced as the group whose artillery pounds the defenders' lines.
• A faction that just gained territory (ceded Former [[Prism]] castle) and was listed as no longer a member of [[Bloc]].
• A faction owning multiple entertainment channels and leveraging feed names as communication channels.
• A named team on the floor described as largely defeated in the current conflict, one of the few remaining factions besides the Princess [[Posse]], [[Reavers]], and War Mages.
• A faction/group associated with adjutants like Fopsy and individuals such as Agatha/Paulie, its representatives are present and influencing events in the tower.
• A large consumer-goods manufacturer that provides food in the galaxy, discussed as a corporate faction possibly allied with the [[Reavers]] and implicated in recent chaos.
• A powerful, galaxy-spanning group of [[elves]] involved in faction wars on the ninth floor, led by Epitome Noflex and implicated in conflicts with [[hunters]] and other factions.
• A hunter faction known for pale "dream [[elves]]" who rapidly construct light siege machinery in the field, described as mounted, fast-attack artillery specialists who use druidic magic and cheating tech to assemble contraptions like the Enchanted Venomous Elven Rock Chucker – Contraption.
• A sponsor faction that abdicated its spot and indicated intent to abandon Faction Wars in the announcement.
• A spellcasting faction controlling Dream territory with powerful magical artillery and castles, many teleport into the battlefield and threaten the [[FUPA]].
• A hostile faction involved in the assault that Donut is meant to help eliminate with her spellcasting.
• A sponsor/team mentioned as part of the faction-vote math, Carl predicts the Dream will vote to change the safety protocols.
• A faction referenced as the original owners of the seized venomous trebuchet device.
• A named Faction that proposed action items (e.g., physics/long-range artillery) and is recorded in the audience voting logs.
• A powerful faction referenced via "the Dream's cruiser" that threatened planetary-scale action (nuking) in response to the AI decisions, part of the broader political pressure on the [[Syndicate]].
• A faction/organization that joined the truce and is also mentioned as a corporate parent in industry dealings affecting Sensation Entertainment, Incorporated.
• A faction team that proposed a pending action item related to physics limitations and artillery range, engaged with audience voting during Faction Wars.
• A powerful external faction referenced by possession of a cruiser and leverage (threats to nuke the planet) used during negotiations with the system AI, cited as pressing for concessions and demonstrating coercive power.
