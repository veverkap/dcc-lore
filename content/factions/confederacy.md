# confederacy

• The Confederacy occupies [[Signet]]'s family palace after the revolution and is one of the "great cancers" that [[Signet]] vows to cut free from the land.
• The Confederacy's agents, the confederates, emerge from the river when it rains.
• The Confederacy's leaders are hiding in the [[Throne Room]] of [[Fort Freedom]] and are the [[target]] of the infiltration and the intended audience for Samantha's Psamathe ruse.
• The Confederacy is a decayed ruling faction that has lost control and allowed the kingdom to regress.
• The Confederacy is a political/military power opposed by [[Signet]] and poses threats to the group and the region.
• The Confederacy's forces emerge from the river during heavy rain.
• The Confederacy's influence has collapsed in the region, and the castle has fallen into disarray.
• The Confederate government is an inept faction in the area's lore, as evidenced by their poor planning allowing [[Claude Sludgington the Fourth]] to enter the castle and take residence.
