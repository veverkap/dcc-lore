# one_fine_pig

* A racing/mercenary team present on the hill, consisting of members including [[Nico]], [[Dario]], [[Penelope]], and the hired mercenary [[Pontiff]]. Their vehicles include an armored truck with a cube-shaped shield.
* A rival racing faction in the competition, finishing first in Heat One and having extra reward choices (Golden upgrades or three regular upgrades).
* A team that placed sixth in Heat Two and Heat Three, and finished fifth in Heat Four.
* A team that owns an APV that rumbles over the finish line.
* A team that occupies an APV next to the narrator's vehicle, features a Draconian mercenary with a heavy machine gun, and competes directly against the narrator's team.
* A team that is overtaken by the truck crew earlier in the sequence and targeted by some of the truck's missiles.
* A team that is involved in the incident that kills [[Finley]], [[Olga]], and [[Quemada]] and then retreats into its garage.
* A team that defends [[Penelope]] and is associated with a house in the cul-de-sac and an armored vehicle.
* A team that has a sponsor/association with a house in the cul-de-sac and an armored vehicle that Carl expects may be inside the house.
