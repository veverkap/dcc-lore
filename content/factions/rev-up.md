# Rev-Up
A dungeon-floor corporation/brand behind a moonshine distribution scheme and recruitment pamphlets. Operates through consultant tiers (e.g., Clurichauns, Laminak elites), has product lines like [[rev-up moonshine]] and attempted spinoffs (e.g., Rev-Up Smoothies), and references managerial figures such as [[Krakaren]].
