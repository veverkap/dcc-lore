# 101st

• A numbered military unit fighting near the base of the mountain alongside [[Florin]], exposed to the planned volcanic eruption.
• A numbered unit referenced by [[Florin]] that has completed a mission and is being repositioned, with some squads assisting with lava abatement while the bulk falls back to [[Shanty Town]].
• A numbered allied military unit referenced in field engagements against enemy armor, with some members of related groups (e.g., slug contingents) deployed with them.
• A unit under [[Florin]] that was pinned in trenches and being helped by allied units during the battle, implicated in defensive deployments around the camp.
• A named military unit that occupies sector two after taking it from [[Operatic Collective]]; part of the three-front disposition for Operation Ruin.
• A faction/[[guild]] providing several members who qualify and wish to go with the departing group, noted as the "101st" in [[Florin]]'s message.
