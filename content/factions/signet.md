# signet

• Organization whose crew assists passage through lower tunnels and secret dungeon-level entrance, allied with the [[Sledge]]'s movement.
• Referenced sponsor/group associated with tattoo artists, mentioned in passing when Donut complains about an item's appearance.
• Crew/faction whose members help guide forces through lower tunnels into the castle's secret entrance, mentioned in coordination with [[Sledge]] and [[were-castors]].
• Team guarding the secret, underground entrance to the castle, part of the plan to admit [[changelings]] and control access to sentinel towers.
• Organized strike group led by Signet undertaking the river approach to Fort Freedom, composed of summoned paper constructs, [[elves]], and various beast companions.
• Circus poster/group associated with Tsarina Signet that has been vandalized and labeled "Traitor," indicating social conflict between Signet and the circus faction.
• Sponsor/company referenced via "Signet's tattoo people" as a cultural/organizational entity in the world, invoked in a joking fashion about item aesthetics.
