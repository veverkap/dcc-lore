# squad

• System-formed player grouping of up to five [[crawlers]] with visual representation and inventory slots.
• Squads receive [[Squad Flag]] sets, must be filled before phase transition, and have a designated squad leader who manages [[T'Ghee Totem]]s.
• A regional squad, possibly using [[Eye in the Sky]] scrolls to locate teammates who had teleported away.
