# valtay

Here is the consolidated markdown list:

* A faction/ally state of the [[orcs]] implicated in the assassination fallout; loss of a powerful ally to Valtay could reshape surface politics and benefit parties like [[borant corporation]].
* A people/faction mentioned in the floor's backstory as having wiped out the H’lene alongside the [[orcs]].
* A people/faction notorious for cruel production choices in past seasons (e.g., using repurposed collected children); cited by characters as among the harshest competitors in crawl politics.
* An external corporate/interstellar sponsor faction that gained majority control (51%) of the [[borant corporation]] and is taking over central system control; depicted as aiming to reshape top-crawler composition and impose new rules on the season.
* A faction mentioned as having influence over crawler operations and as the source of a controlling worm and security practices. Distinct from the [[borant corporation]] in operational control.
* A government/national entity referenced in connection with the legality and charter governing gondii brain-worm mercenaries. Also referenced culturally via "Valtay cheese sticks" (enemy troops).
* A government/administration referenced as the issuer of prior policy (the communications blackout) and as a party whose board and prime minister are being engaged about the unfolding events.
* A ruthless sponsor group or race notorious for cruel production choices in past seasons; invoked as an example of extreme exploitation, such as repurposing collected people as sentinels.
* A faction/ally affected by the assassination incident; loss of an ally could drive them into conflict with other powers and factor into the geopolitical maneuvering discussed by [[dungeon crawler carl]] and [[Mordecai]].
* A named group referenced in regional history (they, alongside the [[Orcs]], wiped out the H’lene). Presented as part of the floor's background lore.
* A powerful corporate entity tied to [[Sydnee Iglacia]]'s publisher. The publisher is a subsidiary of a [[Valtay]] company and enforces predatory contracts (including the life-contract and royalty controls).
* A group/species responsible for certain seasons and security practices in the crawl. Referenced as having different operational control, including "Valtay season" contexts, and as the group whose worm controls [[Drick]].
* A faction/ally mentioned in speculation: the Intellect [[Hunters]] were wondered to be the same entities running the [[Valtay]], implying corporate/factional influence in-lore.
* A interstellar corporation that sponsored [[dungeon crawler carl]] on this floor. Previously tied to assassination and political interference (linked to the death of [[Manasa]] and conflicts with the [[skull clan]] and [[borant corporation]]).
* A corporate sponsor/manufacturer that produces neural interface hardware and benefactor items (logo appears on a box). Associated with outreach centers and legacy/payment plans; responsible for the neural enhancer given to [[dungeon crawler carl]].
* A corporate partner credited with funding and completing the engineering required for the next-floor project called the [[Great Race]].
* A commercial/corporate partner mentioned by [[Mordecai]] and [[Zev]] as stakeholders who have shown indifference to recent events but whose liaison and [[council]] relationships are nervous about ongoing developments.
* A parasitic faction that seized control of the crawl from the [[borant corporation]] and caused political upheaval affecting crawler ranks.
* A named fleet referenced by [[Zev]] during an on-air aside; connected to the appearance of a system-busting device and the entry of the [[Emperator]] through a tunnel gate.
* The authority/organization controlling the landing vessel and broader security measures referenced in the chapter. Responsible for tighter crawler oversight and for sanctioning the Valtay landing vessel used for [[CrawlCon]] events.
* The sponsor that appears in an admin notice at chapter end; a corporate faction whose advertisements will appear to viewers of the crawler's feed. Associated with the system sponsorship mechanic.
* A species-based faction heavily involved in managing surface protections and security; the [[Valtay]] proposed the controversial solution believed to allow an extinction event of [[crawlers]] and surface inhabitants to lift the quarantine.
* A powerful sponsor/government faction implicated in surface protection, showrunning, and controversial proposals (including an extinction-style [[resolution]]); the [[Valtay]] are central actors in [[Syndicate]] deliberations and the plan to recall mercenaries.
* A company/corporate sponsor mentioned as the parent of [[Sydnee Iglacia]]'s publisher; associated with life contracts and exploitative business practices that affect royalties and post-death arrangements.
* A larger organization/sponsor referenced in relation to the [[Mongo Mommies Valtay chapter]]. Suspected in crowd speculation to have planted agents among the survivors.
* A corporate-sounding entity mentioned in the neural implant warning ("Please see your Valtay representative for repair"). Implied manufacturer/repair contact for wetware systems used by [[crawlers]].
