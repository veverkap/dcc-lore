# naiad_confederacy

• The Naiad [[Confederacy]] is a faction referenced in [[Tsarina Signet]]'s lineage, known for hostility toward outsiders and cultural traits affecting [[Signet]]'s backstory.
• The Naiad [[Confederacy]] is the fallen government of a river kingdom central to [[Tsarina Signet]]'s revenge plot, with leaders occupying [[Fort Freedom]].
• The Naiad [[Confederacy]] is the ruling body of the Naiad people, with leaders currently occupying [[Fort Freedom]] and being the [[target]] of [[Tsarina Signet]]'s assassination/assault objective.
• The Naiad people/kingdom is tied to [[Signet]]'s heritage, with their kingdom falling into anarchy and [[Fort Freedom]] being an occupied naiad castle.
• The Naiad Confederates are a faction mentioned in relation to an earlier liberation of [[Fort Freedom]] and are part of the chapter's back-reference to previous floors/events.
• The Naiad Kingdom is an underwater polity mentioned as the setting of the confederate castle's fundraiser, with citizens and underwater dwellers (naiads) being part of the local ecology and politics referenced in the boss's backstory.
