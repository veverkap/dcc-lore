# the_hive

* The Hive is a hostile sponsor/faction responsible for hunting [[dungeon crawler carl]] and his group, and is personally implicated by [[Vrah]] in direct threats.
* The Hive is a hunter faction that has claimed several [[crawlers]], including [[dungeon crawler carl]] and [[Donut]], on the [[Hunting Grounds Map]].
* The Hive is a faction listed on the hand-drawn map that has claimed several [[crawlers]], including [[dungeon crawler carl]] and [[Donut]], and is part of the [[hunters]]' territorial divisions around the city and ruins.
* The Hive is an organization referenced in connection with [[Circe Took]] and is implied to be a faction or group within the broader setting.
* The Dark Hive is a powerful mantid-affiliated faction/sponsor group that exerts control over hunting politics, is feared by many [[hunters]], and claimed [[Lucia]] earlier.
* The Dark Hive is a faction associated with [[Circe Took]] and is implicated in sponsoring a god ([[Diwata]]) and in-game intrigue surrounding the benefactor box.
* The Dark Hive is a named faction claiming territory in the area and is one of the groups that has laid claim to the hunting grounds mentioned by captured hunter [[Akland]].
* The Dark Hive is the mantid-aligned faction mentioned in the panel: Circe's [[daughters]] are described as "Dark Hive" warriors, representing the mantid hive culture and military organization that poses a coordinated threat to [[dungeon crawler carl]].
* The Dark Hive is a faction associated with [[Circe Took]] (Circe Took of the Dark Hive) and is implicated in behind-the-scenes sponsorship and rule-bending communications with her daughter.
* The Dark Hive is a faction referenced by [[Vrah]] as her allegiance, and is positioned as a hostile collective to [[dungeon crawler carl]]'s group and is invoked as the source of Vrah's threats.
* The Dark Hive is a [[mantis]] corporate entity described as essentially a private corporation running an intergalactic amusement-style enterprise, selling gear that has been contracted to [[burrowers]].
