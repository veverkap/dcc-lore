# desperado

* [[desperado club]]: A real-world/ingame venue tied to redeemable items, appears as a social or commercial establishment [[crawlers]] can interact with via consumable tickets.
* A vendor/organization/shop where some groups were sending people to purchase tactical supplies like [[smoke curtain]] and [[Hobgoblin Dynamite]] in preparation for the crisis.
* A local establishment and player hub on [[Hump Street]] referenced as a safe-ish place and containing contacts like the pharmacist [[Quint]].
* A club/faction used as an analogy for party dynamics; mentioned in passing in relation to how factions choose attendee numbers.
* A club/shop referenced by Carl as a place to resupply (for buying holy curtains and other equipment) outside the dungeon.
* A named in-game club/entrance system referenced by the system announcement as a recurring feature across floors.
* A club/faction and rendezvous point where [[Donut]] was supposed to meet [[Imani]], [[elle mcgib]], and other [[crawlers]]; used as a social/operational hub in the floor.
* A named faction/[[guild]] referenced as a social group that some players belong to; [[Prepotente]] is not a member, and [[dungeon crawler carl]] looks for signs of the club while moving through [[Havana]].
