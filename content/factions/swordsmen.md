# swordsmen

• A guardian faction responsible for the suits of armor/guards housed in the depot.
• Their reanimation is tied to a magical recharging crystal.
• They stand in formation like a terracotta army inside the depot.
• A guard/knight faction whose depot holds hundreds of animated suits of armor.
• Their inactive guards stand in formation inside the [[Swordsmen Depot]].
• They recharge nightly via a [[Soul Crystal]].
• Central to the tactical considerations of the chapter.
