# [[Swordsmen]]
A guard/knight faction whose depot holds hundreds of animated suits of armor that recharge nightly via a [[Soul Crystal]]. Their inactive guards stand in formation inside the [[Swordsmen Depot]] and are central to the tactical considerations of the chapter.
