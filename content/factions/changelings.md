# changelings

• Infiltrating faction posing as refugees/[[dromedarians]], suspected of plotting to resurrect powerful guardians and carry out covert operations within the town.
• A covert group hiding in a [[Changeling Hideout Village]] referenced by [[Katia]]; must be contacted quickly to respond to the floor event.
• Race/faction being funneled into the castle by [[Sledge]] and [[Signet]]'s crew to impersonate [[elves]], take sentinel towers, and create docking locations for the [[Twister]].
• A group allied with the party who accompanied [[Bonnie]] to the flag room following their earlier involvement in the Butcher’s Masquerade; referenced as part of the rescue and support network.
• Infiltrator faction impersonating [[dromedarians]]; discussed as an internal threat whose hotter brains might be detectable via heat-signature scans.
• A faction/species of shapeshifters (some living among children and disguised among the [[dromedarians]]) central to the evacuation and the watch/inspection dynamics with the [[gnomes]].
• A group of shapeshifters central to Carl’s plan; one changeling ([[Pearson]]) is masquerading as [[Signet]] to test the castle’s protocols. Carl aims to save and move certain changelings to the next floor.
• A group of infiltrators who can impersonate [[elves]]; planned to ascend the four [[sentinel towers]] and remove guards to create docking locations for the [[Twister]]. They work with [[Sledge]] and [[Signet's crew]] in the assault plan.
• Carl's infiltrators who have made it into the castle in attempts to sow chaos. Referenced as part of the larger attack and as being inside [[mantis]] territory.
