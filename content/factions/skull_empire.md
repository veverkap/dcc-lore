# skull_empire

• Political realm tied to [[Princess Formidable]]; source of benefactor boxes and plot connections to bolts and prizes on the fourth floor.
• A political faction represented on the [[Syndicate]] [[Council]] by Princess Formidable; advocates for extreme measures such as triggering the [[Failsafe]] to destroy a star/system to stop an AI threat.
• The ruling power that consolidated holdings and real estate after the death of [[Empress D’Nadia]], responsible for rapid political changes affecting residents like [[Sydnee Iglacia]].
• A political/economic entity mentioned in relation to extradition and safe harbor for financial criminals; [[Skull Empire]] is the jurisdiction where Harry Tisch resides.
