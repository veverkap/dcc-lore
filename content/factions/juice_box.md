# juice_box

• A group or sponsor actively pursuing [[Louis]] and other targets, expending effort to reach targets on upper floors.
• Previously provided limited support, but lent ground troops to occupy [[Shanty Town]].
• Provided assistance alongside [[npcs]] to back the four offensives during Operation: Ruin.
• Suspected of coordinating attacks to reach [[Louis]] and to hunt a changeling on the [[Ninth Floor]].
