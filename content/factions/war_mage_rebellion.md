# war_mage_rebellion

• A late-arriving team/faction in the [[Faction Wars]] that killed an orc boss and stole the [[Gate of the Feral Gods]].
• Led by [[Akuma]], a magic-focused faction involved in major conflicts in prior floors.
• Formed by [[war mages]], including [[Akuma]], after they killed [[Prince Stalwart]] and stole the [[Gate of the Feral Gods]], occupying [[Larracos]].
