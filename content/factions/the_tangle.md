# the_tangle

* The Tangle: transit organization operating the train network, employees tied to rituals and deities, uses Employee Portal (Abyss Gate)
* Tangle Train Engineers: employees of the transit organization
* Iron Tangle: massive corporate/transit organization, runs stations, employs HR [[NPCs]] and execs, implements productivity programs
* [[Grull]]: deity worshipped by Tangle employees
* [[Employee Portal (Abyss Gate)]]: used by Tangle employees
* [[Madison]]: HR NPC affiliated with Iron Tangle
* [[Rod]]: CFO of Iron Tangle, criticized for exploitative productivity measures
* [[Kravyad]]: labor program implemented by Iron Tangle
* [[Steam Engineer's Key]]: grants access to Iron Tangle employee-only areas and actions on steam trains
* [[The Iron Tangle Substation E]]: station operated by Iron Tangle
