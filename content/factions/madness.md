# madness

* A faction/team known for battlefield spells and necromancy, with a viceroy representative occupying one of the meeting slots.
* Led by Architect Houston, with members and adjutants like Opiee representing them at the hearing.
* Participates in the ceasefire hearing and the dungeon game, gaining assets from the defeated Democratic Sortion and implicated via operatives like Nurse Yugoslav.
* Includes invisible operatives in robes and masks and mercenaries absorbed from the Lemig Sortition.
* Contesting the castle, with forces represented politically by figures like Architect Houston.
* Held [[Prism]] castle briefly before it reverted to the [[Dream]], and is active in the ongoing territorial contest.
* Defends the Madness castle, employing psionic and observation spells that threaten Carl's raid.
* Engaged in battle, with forces including dune buggies, mind mages, and Atrocity casters centered around the Madness castle.
* Recruited troops from the former stronghold, and "Madness troops" are mentioned in the skirmish.
* Known for necromancy and horrific battlefield spells, represented at the meeting by a faceless, masked viceroy whose head is seen decapitated and later replaced.
* Joined the truce/alliance forming [[bloc]].
* Occupies Spots 7 and 8 in the battle roster, noted among the remaining competitors.
* Conscripted several crawler teams, including the team associated with Burcu, and appears in alliance listings.
* Aligned with [[bloc]], engaging in skirmishes and diversions.
* Attempted to abdicate its Faction Wars spot as announced on the broadcast.
* Proposed a pending action item (Celestial Summoning limits) during Faction Wars.
* Included in the truce that formed [[bloc]] against attacking teams in [[Larracos]].
* Original proposer of an action item limiting celestial summoning events per team, with its proposal rejected by audience vote.
* Warns about using illegal and horrific spells if lives are on the line, part of the political and combat landscape influencing the crawl.
