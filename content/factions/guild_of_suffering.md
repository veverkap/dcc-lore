# guild_of_suffering

* A cult-like faction worshipping the [[Night Wyrm]].
* Not a formal pantheon church; has outreach centers and uses memorial crystals tied to divine suffering artifacts (e.g., the [[Ring of Divine Suffering]]).
* Bearers of divine-suffering artifacts receive marks linking them to this group.
* Access is granted temporarily by owning the [[Engaged Lock Box of the Night Wyrm]].
* Issues assassination [[Guild Jobs]] and owns an entrance hidden behind the [[desperado club]] (the Penis Parade entrance).
* Located a floor above the [[desperado club]].
* A hidden, secret [[guild]] accessible via a concealed portal inside a cubicle in the [[Penis Parade]].
* A faction led by [[Hamed]] (the Night Wyrm).
* Tied into the club's internal politics and referenced via familial connections to other named characters.
* A named [[guild]] referenced by the narrator's web-pattern elbow tattoo (granting access).
* Mentioned as part of the protagonist's past and systems interaction.
* A shadowy assassin [[guild]] that issues assassination quests ([[Guild]] Jobs) and controls membership access (sting on non-members).
* The final task to open Katia's box requires finding this [[guild]] and completing an assassination quest.
* The [[Desperado]] Club is a front/entrance for it.
* A [[guild]] and faction tied to the assassination objective; Carl intends to deliver Astrid's body to the [[Guild of Suffering]] entrance, which is located one floor above the [[desperado club]] near the [[penis parade]].
* A player [[guild]] indicated by a tattoo on [[dungeon crawler carl]]'s elbow; referenced in social ties and [[guild]] politics relevant to Astrid and the Night Wyrm.
* A named [[guild]] referenced as having a head who ordered Astrid's killing; receives bodies as payment for jobs.
* Connected to the [[Night Wyrm]]'s commands.
* A named [[guild]] referenced in relation to the Night Wyrm and the accusation that money or [[guild]] politics motivated [[Astrid]]'s death.
