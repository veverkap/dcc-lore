# dictum_waystation_controls_limited

• A sponsor corporation mentioned with disdain by [[Crawler Everly]]; Carl suspects sponsors like this manipulate events for viewership and profit.
• A corporate sponsor advertised during the live broadcast betting; cited as a sponsor of the event coverage.
• The sponsor company that funded [[Everly]] and sent the benefactor box; its name appears on the item label and as the sponsor of the tower trap in this chapter.
• A sponsor corporation blamed by [[Crawler Everly]] for steering her toward a public, tragic death. Representative of sponsor groups whose advertisements and influence persist as long as sponsored [[crawlers]] survive.
• A corporate sponsor named by the announcers as backing the betting and broadcast of the Survival Royale Special Event.
• A corporate entity referenced on a recovered [[Horribles mask]] (worn by [[Gershwin Banks]]), indicating corporate presence among arena participants.
