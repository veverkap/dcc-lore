# outreach_guild

* The Outreach [[Guild]] is a [[guild]] organization that appears on the Eleventh Floor, offering spawning/placement opportunities and post-floor support for successful [[crawlers]].
* The [[guild]] system receives [[crawlers]] who take indenture deals, presenting seasonal indentures, stipends, and rules about what items can be taken.
* The [[guild]] is referenced as a destination or affiliation following quest completion, and is associated with support for former [[crawlers]].
* The Outreach [[Guild]] is a legal/contract hub where options are offered, and enforces item/selection rules for pre-collapse allowances.
* The [[guild]] is a safety/professional network that crawler characters can enter after surviving a floor, and is mentioned as a likely benefit for [[Chaco]] after leveling from the kill.
* The Outreach [[Guild]] is a named [[guild]] referenced in the dungeon rules, and players who avoid death will have access to an Outreach [[Guild]] between the sixth and seventh races.
* The [[guild]] is a sponsor/faction newly available in the Hungry Eyes area, and is central to many [[crawlers]]' [[decision]]-making.
* The [[guild]] offers exit deals to [[crawlers]], and its deals directly shape final-race compositions.
