# sluggalos

• A chaotic faction/[[squad]] of roughly forty hatchet-wielding slug mobs that charge demons in a reckless assault, then retreat in disarray.
• Includes named individuals like [[Rampageous]].
• A group of slug-loving players/creatures, some held in reserve in the [[Guildhall]] barracks, escorted by [[were castors]] and tied to characters like [[justice lights team]].
• A group/race of slug-like [[NPCs]] present in the safe room, playing games, interacting with players, and betting with [[Samantha]].
• Several are described as level 10 and act as background crew/contestants.
• A faction or cultural group characterized by sticky, slimy secretions and a code of "unity, support, family, and kneecapping bitches", with [[Bigs]] as a member, and their biology or substances affecting the scene.
