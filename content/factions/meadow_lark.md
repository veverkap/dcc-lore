# meadow_lark

Here is the consolidated markdown list:

* [[Meadow Lark]] is a faction/group of [[crawlers]], including elderly members, and is referenced in various contexts:
	+ They are an adventuring group highlighted by the show and shown among party listings in the arena HUD.
	+ They are a team/group that includes elderly members, such as [[Dungeon Crawler Carl]] and others, who face the obligation to assist or abandon them.
	+ They are a named survivor group/settlement that arrives in tattered form, with members like [[Brandon]], [[Chris]], and [[Imani]] salvaging and improvising transport to evacuate remaining residents.
	+ They are a team/[[guild]] operating in the transit network, currently advancing through stop 250, and are part of the coordinated plan with [[Dungeon Crawler Carl]]'s group to use intersecting stations on the [[Ochre Line]].
	+ They are a group/society of [[crawlers]] whose former residents are holed up in the [[Iron Tangle]] and who provided status updates to the protagonist.
	+ They are a group/survivor faction whose members, including [[Imani]] and [[Elle McGib]], travel together, shelter in the [[Iron Tangle]] after fighting ghouls, and coordinate next moves.
	+ They are a group/team/building collective working on a cannon and coordinating plans, including using oil to bring down a last castle.
	+ They are a team referenced as approaching the town, with [[Chris]] and [[Clay-ton]] waiting on this group in the bar.
	+ They are a large crawler group/[[guild]] referenced as traveling together, having an early skirmish with other [[hunters]], but being largely left alone afterward.
	+ They are a group/team of [[crawlers]] referred to as former residents, including members like [[Imani]] and sheltering in the [[Iron Tangle]] after a run-in with ghouls.
	+ They are a team of [[crawlers]] encountered by the survivors, mentioned as exhausted but instrumental in holding platforms and stairwells.
	+ They are a faction/group working on unconventional solutions, building a cannon to shoot oil into the sky, to take an air castle.
	+ They are a large traveling group/faction of [[crawlers]] that includes [[Elle McGib]] and [[Eva]], noted for having remained mostly untouched due to their size.
* [[Meadowlark]] is also a sponsored show or group ("the Meadowlark folks") that Katia expects will broadcast or recap the confrontation, representing media/sponsor coverage within the crawl.
* [[Meadowlark]] is a player faction/[[guild]] referenced in the raid, with several members, including [[Imani]], [[Katia]], and others, identified as belonging to Meadowlark and acting in coordinated teams.
* [[Meadowlark]] is the faction/[[guild]] that many of the [[crawlers]] in this chapter belong to, with members including [[Imani]], [[Katia]], [[Elle McGib]], [[Li Jun]], and others, and benefits from shared upgrades like the [[Doggie Door]] when installed in party spaces.
* [[Team Meadow Lark]] is a named escort/party group the protagonists plan to escort into the stairwell, functioning as an allied team/faction in the chapter's immediate objectives.
* [[Team Meadow Lark]] is an allied crawler team led by [[Elle McGib]] operating in the [[Water Quadrant]] with a galley and fish-people rowers, coordinating reconnaissance against nearby castles like the [[Orc-run Oil Rig]].
* [[Team Meadow Lark]] is a crawler team/faction named among the groups meeting in the settlement south of the quarters, part of the allied formation coordinating before hunter deployments.
* [[Team Meadow Lark]] is an allied exploration/team currently progressing through the transit network (noted at stop 250), whose movements and warnings factor into [[Dungeon Crawler Carl]]'s train-derailment and warning plans.
* [[Team Meadow Lark]] is a crawling team that included [[Chris]], [[Imani]], and [[Elle McGib]], used to contextualize relationships and who entered the dungeon together.
* [[Meadowlark Adult Care community]] is a named care community whose staff (Chris, Brandon, Yolanda, etc.) are referenced by [[Dungeon Crawler Carl]] and are part of the wider social connections affected by the events.
* [[Meadow Lark party]] is a named party referenced as one Carl was not part of, used to explain why Agatha was not in their group but how she nevertheless earned XP.
