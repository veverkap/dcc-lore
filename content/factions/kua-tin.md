# kua-tin

* [[Kua-Tin]] are an alien people referenced as water-favoring hosts that halted [[Valtay]]'s collection action by starting the Dungeon season early; they are described as non-human and influential.
* They are a named group/race referenced in- system as being restricted from running serials during game runtime; mentioned in context with production companies and shows that involve [[dungeon crawler carl]] and [[Donut]].
* They are a species or cultural group referenced in the floor's imagery (e.g., the "[[Bloom]] of the kua- tin") and the map decoration at the next-floor doors.
* They are gilled, knee-high amphibious administrators and a named group/race in the chapter; administrators such as [[Loita]] are kua- tin.
* They participate in [[Syndicate]] reporting and are referenced as influential in show decisions.
* They are a named faction/entity referenced by [[Orren the Liaison]] as having an interest in [[dungeon crawler carl]]'s status; part of the political calculus around forcing or retaining [[crawlers]].
* They are another named species/group referenced in-milieu by [[Edict]] as aware of the cultural crisis ("the kua- tin, I think, know this, too"), used for comparative lore about sentient groups in the setting.
* They are a named political/corporate interest referenced during the liaison meeting; involved in debates about how to handle [[dungeon crawler carl]] and the artifact.
* They are the designer/producer race or organization responsible for the season's crawl; mentioned as still running the production.
* They are the production-designating species/organization credited with running and designing the season; referenced as the durable operator behind the crawl production.
* They are an aquatic/gleener race referenced in the audience Q&A; example race that interacts with panelists like [[Princess Donut]] and appears in the show's questioners.
* They are a scientific/exploratory group referenced in the origin story for the [[Escape Velocity]] discovery and the early wormhole explorations that led to [[Syndicate]] formation.
* They are a named underground faction mentioned as likely to erupt into civil conflict amid the political turmoil surrounding the crawl and sponsor interventions.
* They are a named government entity criticized for failing to maintain control over corporate interests tied to the crawl and sponsors like [[Borant Corporation]].
* They are a surface group that initiated sporadic deep-cycle imaging of the planet's surface to detect impending crawls and entrances; they are implicated in the logistics that bring [[crawlers]] to locations where [[Residuals]] try to communicate and are blamed for certain disposal/tossing behaviors.
* They are a group accused of being sold a damaged AI; invoked by [[Princess Formidable of the Skull Empire]] as part of the narrative blaming parties for current system corruption.
* They are an external group alleged to have been sold a damaged AI by the [[Burrower Government]]; mentioned in [[council]] accusations about the system AI's origin and behavior.
