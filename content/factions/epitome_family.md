# epitome_family

• A powerful family whose members are trapped in the [[Scolopendra Club]] and cited by [[Prime Minister Glory]] as part of why detonating the [[Failsafe]] would cause galactic upheaval.
• Highly trained security forces associated with the [[Epitome Tagg]] / [[Epitome]] family; veterans of non-dungeon conflicts present in the combat zone as elite defenders.
