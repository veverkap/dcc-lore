# apothecary

* A sponsor group that provides benefactor boxes containing items distributed among [[crawlers]], with many gifts intended for [[Donut]].
* A benefactor faction that awards a platinum box to [[Donut]] and supplies potions and consumable-related rewards.
* A rival faction whose residual agents populate the planet and order self-termination if they fail to enter the dungeon.
* A sponsor group that provides gear to players, gifting Katia a hat intended to prevent a particular outcome.
* A corporate/sponsor presence within convention and dungeon politics, reportedly working with [[plenty]] on infrastructure projects.
* A faction that seeds agents on planets and enforces suicidal orders for missed dungeon entry.
* A sponsor group that coordinates sponsorships with [[plenty]] and provided the [[Silver Benefactor Box]] containing the [[charm animal potion]] recipe and the photo of [[Mongo]].
* A faction that rivals [[Agatha]]'s group in recruitment efforts and has residual agents and informational feeds.
