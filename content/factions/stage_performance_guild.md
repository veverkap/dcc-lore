# stage_performance_guild

• A hidden guildhall tied to karaoke performance mechanics, accessible by singing marked songs and keeping the gauge in the green.
• The [[guild]] can be discovered and entered by performing the karaoke entrance exam.
• The [[guild]] appears on the map for characters in proximity after [[Carl]]'s successful performance.
• The guildroom is a large theater, and [[Porky]] serves as a [[guild]]-affiliated performer/NPC.
