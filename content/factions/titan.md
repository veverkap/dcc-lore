# titan

• An overseeing authority or corporate overseer referenced by [[Bin]] as pressuring production, exerting top-down influence over the show's continued existence.
• A media/licensing company contracted to produce a video about [[Mordecai]] and associated NPC drama, per [[Zev]]'s briefing.
• An executive overseer mentioned by [[Bin]] as the authority applying pressure on his production, described as having previously let him work with little interference before stepping in.
• A production/entertainment company in-universe, named on the red lanyard of [[dungeon crawler carl]]'s fan badge.
• The private media conglomerate that produces "Dungeon Crawler After Hours with Odette," the heavily tunneled after-show where [[dungeon crawler carl]] and [[Princess Donut]] appear, credited with production assistance from many independent systems.
