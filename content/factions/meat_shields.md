# meat_shields

• Named mercenary market/organization on the floor where the [[Mercenary coupon]] can be redeemed for services.
• Provides mercenary contracts, including the top-floor manager [[Rosemarie]] and the hired Penis Parade members.
• Participates in mercenary services where the [[Mercenary Coupon]] can be redeemed (locations include [[desperado club]] and [[Club Vanquisher]]).
• Referenced as a mercenary market/backup location in the back of the club.
• Used to hire crews, with [[Rosemarie]] managing a top-floor location and [[Donut]] overseeing [[guild]] logistics and upgrades like the [[Barracks]] and [[Mercenary Arcade]].
