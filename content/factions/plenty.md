# plenty

Here is the consolidated markdown list:

• The Plenty is a caprid faction credited with inventing the modern tunneling system and real-time communications.
• They are represented on the floor as the Pooka and are central to political debates about prosperity vs. conspiracy.
• The Plenty is a group of goat-like beings that invented a tunneling system and is linked to the [[Apothecary]] sponsor.
• They are a sponsor group responsible for a Gold Benefactor Box and associated patch rewards.
• The Plenty is an organization implicated in manipulating events, suspected of wanting Miriam dead to further their designs.
• They are a faction granted a seat on the [[Syndicate]] [[Council]], but criticized for inaction during the emergency session.
• The Plenty is a group that performs "hot updates" to feed names on behalf of paying teams, charged in tandem with the [[Syndicate]] for covert feed renaming.
• They are a caprid faction led by Gamori, controlling tunnel networks and having a cultural tendency toward panic when startled.
• The Plenty is a sponsor group that manifested during the on-the-spot sponsorship presentation, announcing the sponsorship and awarding a Gold Benefactor Box.
• They are an intergalactic conglomerate-like group favorably disposed toward [[Prepotente]] (and his goats), implying political or sponsor-level interest.
• The Plenty is a faction or corporate-sounding group collecting their "chest of stardust" and charging for tunnel transfer gates, involved in orbital logistics and fees related to incoming ships.
