# training_guild

• A generic [[guild]] unlocked by reaching level thresholds in skills, providing training facilities and unlocks based on skill level.
• Represents organized training resources available in the setting.
• Unlocks when certain skill thresholds are met (e.g., [[Dodge]] at level seven).
• Provides training options and potentially in-game benefits related to skill progression.
• Generic [[guild]] entities noted on the map and in the surrounding dungeon areas, present but sparser near the dungeon's edge.
