# 104th_naughty_little_piggies

• Tipid's battalion was assigned to defend the castle while other forces were committed elsewhere.
• The 104th Naughty Little Piggies were a unit assigned to base defense in the flat lands around the base, led in part by Tipid.
• They were dispersed on assignments when the main attack hit, leaving the castle undermanned.
• The unit rushed to aid the camp during the attack, mentioned alongside the 106th Bloody Leeches.
• The 104th Naughty Little Piggies' banner was referenced on Dong Quixote's lance, and Dong Quixote carried their banner upon teleporting into the field.
