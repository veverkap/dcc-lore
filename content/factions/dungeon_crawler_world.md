# dungeon_crawler_world

• The Dungeon Crawler World is a branded, sadistic game show/universe that runs the dungeon event, responsible for rules, audience-driven survival mechanics, and reward distribution via [[patrons]] and loot box.
• The production/season under which the World Dungeon operates includes executive staff like Mudskipper [[Cascadia]] and is the meta-organization behind Eighth Level challenges.
• The showrunner/production entity enforces contractual rules about secondary productions, forbids [[Signet]] from interfering with the main event, and influences decisions about allowed entrants and consequences for tampering.
