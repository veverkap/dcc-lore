# crawlers

• Player faction, including [[dungeon crawler carl]], [[Donut]], and allies, cooperating to confront the deity and boss; many are injured or killed in the descent into the pit.
• Collective term/faction for the player/performer group (Carl, Donut, Britney, etc.), operating together to infiltrate and perform at the masquerade and execute escape and sabotage plans.
• Player/allied faction led by [[dungeon crawler carl]], present in the goodwill ballroom, coordinating stage plans and defenses against mantises and [[hunters]].
• NFC organization dedicated to helping former crawlers reintegrate after release, led by [[doctor hu]], securing legal protections and citizenship benefits for new releases.
• Implied faction in the title given to [[Louis Santiago]] ("Crawler Army Air General Louis Santiago 2"), indicating a military-like crawler organization or rank structure within the dungeon society.
