# team_yokai

• [[Team Yokai]] is a faction that includes members such as [[Dekoki]], [[Yuto]], and others.
• They are a racing team identified by their yellow [[AMC Pacer]].
• They are associated with karaoke and have been spotted at the Lollipop.
• They have a presence at the bar and support [[Carl]] during performances.
• They are a player team/[[guild]] that includes the kappa singer [[Dekoki]].
• Their vehicle casts a spell and escapes through a closed door, noted as a rare successful exit in the melee.
