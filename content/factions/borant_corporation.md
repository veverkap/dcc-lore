# borant_corporation

Here is the consolidated markdown list:

• [[borant corporation]] is a sponsor group/planetary faction that backs seasons of the crawl, determining political and contractual outcomes for indentured trainers like [[Mordecai]].
• The corporation is responsible for designing a doomed level and is blamed by [[Valtay]] for rapid level failure.
• [[Borant]] is a polity/system that actively hates the [[valtay]] and is implicated in broader interstellar politics influencing sponsors and show timing.
• The corporation is critical of the [[Plenty]] and their tunneling tech, accusing them of using tunneling tech to addict civilizations and pave the way for the [[Krakaren]].
• [[Borant]] is a faction that frequently criticizes the [[Plenty]] and their tunneling tech, accusing them of enabling [[Krakaren]] influence.
• The corporation is responsible for the dungeon's rules and patches, allowing the bubble-escape method to continue and adjusting sponsorship-related restrictions.
• [[Borant]] is a powerful sponsor/faction involved in governance of the dungeon, with financial and regulatory interests in [[dungeon crawler carl]]'s actions and the handling of the [[Gate of the Feral Gods]].
• The corporation is a governing jurisdiction referenced for legal authority inside the dungeon system.
• [[Borant]] System is the corporate/tech system behind the game's implementation, referenced as the system that might patch or control game mechanics and as the regency over Earth in some item descriptions.
• The [[Borant]] System Government is a governing body contrasted with the [[borant corporation]], referenced in context of a civil war that purportedly will not affect the production.
• [[Borant]] engineers are responsible for a particular level that failed quickly, criticized by the [[Valtay]] and implicated in political fallout described by [[Mordecai]].
• The [[Borant]] Emancipation Front is an organized group referenced in smuggling operations, positioned as part of the underground political landscape surrounding the corporate changes.
