# society_for_the_eradication_of_cocker_spaniels

• Organization whose name appears on [[Jenn'ifer]]'s censored t-shirt; involved in opposition to [[Syndicate]] efforts to preserve certain canine varieties (censored in text).
• Named sponsor organization for [[princess posse]] in [[Faction Wars]], listed with legal styling and as a non-profit corporation backer.
• Group referenced by a fan's t-shirt at the convention; tied to activism and fundraising to influence [[Syndicate]] decisions about bringing canine variations (including the censored "C-word") to preserved biomes.
• Connected to the [[princess posse]] message delivered to [[Princess Donut]].
