# larracos

• The Larracos faction is associated with towers that fired at the Nest, with all towers that could fire being destroyed, indicating their participation in the siege.
• The Larracos level is a guard/contingent or level within the [[Desperado]] Club, associated with Sai/Rhino guards who defend the club.
• The Larracos Defense Force Infantry is an organized defense force commanded by Arief, represented by officer-marked [[NPCs]] protecting the college and city.
• The Larracos College of Magecraft is a magecraft institution referenced as Ghazi's affiliation and source of expertise, where Ghazi misled colleagues about his goals and where scholars/[[council]] originate.
