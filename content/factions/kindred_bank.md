# [[Kindred Bank]]
[[Kindred Bank]] is the financial institution where Chandra has placed multiple interest-bearing accounts holding the redirected earnings of [[dungeon crawler carl]] and [[Princess Donut]]. It functions as the repository for the conservatorship-controlled funds.
