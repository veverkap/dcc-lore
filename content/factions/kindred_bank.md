# kindred_bank

• [[Kindred Bank]] is the financial institution where Chandra has placed interest-earning accounts for the redirected earnings of [[dungeon crawler carl]] and [[Princess Donut]].
• It functions as the repository for conservatorship-controlled funds.
• The institution holds multiple interest-bearing accounts for the revenues earned by [[Carl]] and [[Donut]].
• It is the repository named in [[Chandra]]’s seizure paperwork.
