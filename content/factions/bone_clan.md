# bone_clan

* Member faction of [[bloc]] that joined the formal truce/alliance referenced in the war announcements.
* Occupies pie piece one on the [[Plains of Larracos]] and is associated with the larger [[Skull Clan]], controlling [[The King's Point]] castle.
* A mercenary faction aligned with the larger [[Skull Clan]], fielding [[orcs]] and larger mercenaries in the approaching army described by observers in the [[Guildhall]].
* Faction of former-crawler mercenaries to which many returned [[crawlers]] were allegedly consigned; [[Volteeg]] avoided joining them.
* A mercenary/factional force engaged in the battle, immediately charging at their king's death and later awarded all assets of the defeated [[Operatic Collective]] by system rulings.
* Faction that was announced as no longer a member of [[bloc]] and is now listed as unaffiliated in the alliance update.
* Faction led by [[King Stalwart]] and a major combatant in external and in-dungeon conflicts, confidently claiming continued strength despite recent personal losses.
* A realm/faction entered during the mission (system message), indicating hostile territory encountered by [[Party Planner]].
* A faction whose Throne Room is a contested objective; messages indicate occupation by [[Team Retribution]] could lead to the Bone Clan's defeat if held for six hours.
* A named faction that joined a formal truce with [[Operatic Collective]] and other factions to form [[bloc]] during Faction Wars.
* Combatant force that charged when their leader was killed, precipitating chaos in the battle described.
* A faction that was listed among remaining teams occupying Spots 1 and 2 on the battlefield, appearing in the Faction Wars overview and the post-Club Vanquisher disposition.
