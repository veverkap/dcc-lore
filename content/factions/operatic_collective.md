# operatic_collective

* The Operatic Collective formed a truce with the [[Bone Clan]] and later joined [[bloc]] against attacking teams including the [[Princess Posse]].
* The Operatic Collective is a faction controlling pie piece two on the map, positioned adjacent to the [[Princess Posse]]’s sector and noted as a staging area for enemy forces and river routes leading toward [[Larracos]].
* The Operatic Collective is a faction that entered the truce/alliance forming [[bloc]].
* The Operatic Collective is one of the major Operatic factions fighting in [[Faction Wars]]; [[Volteeg]] served as a mercenary/ guard under its banner during the conflict.
* The Operatic Collective is one of the nine teams in [[Faction Wars]] referenced as politically vulnerable because local populations can influence votes; named as a potential swing voting [[bloc]].
* The Operatic Collective is one of the sponsor factions that issued an offboarding abdication notice in the broadcast; part of the [[Faction Wars]] roster context.
* The Operatic Collective is an eliminated faction; their former sector was taken over by [[Florin’'s [[101st]] Crocodiles]] and later the [[orcs]] according to the chapter.
* The Operatic Bankers’ Federation is a corporate/ faction name given to the reclamation/ vulture group that investigated failed terraforming projects, credited with uncovering the malfunctioning uplift satellite that produced the fauna of [[Red Terror Place of Family Adventure]].
