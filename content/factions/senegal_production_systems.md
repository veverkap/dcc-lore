# senegal_production_systems

• Production company that owns and operates the [[Deluxe Rental Trailer]] (submarine-like studio trailer) and employs the service AI [[Mexx-6000]] to manage guest comfort and security.
• Provides the Mexx-6000 assistant and manages recorded segments for [[Earth Beautiful]].
• Previously owned many rental production trailers, but was removed from the system by [[Valtay]] for security and tracking reasons.
• Their removal explains the changing [[management]] of the [[Production Facility]].
