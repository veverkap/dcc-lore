# hunters

• Organized external combatants brought in to hunt [[crawlers]] on [[The Hunting Grounds]]; 360 are sequestered at chapter time and will be released to hunt crawler gear, with starting levels bumped to 50 by the system announcement.
• A distinct group apart from [[crawlers]]; several hunters are attending [[The Butcher’s Masquerade]] and hunters are a persistent threat on the floor.
• A factional group present on the floor; began with over a thousand and dwindled to a few dozen before most were eliminated during the boss event.
• A collective group/class of players referenced repeatedly (e.g., holed up in [[Zockau]]); often contrasted with [[crawlers]] and directly targeted by the consequences of the failed quest.
• Hunters attend the masquerade in force and are the source of trophies like [[Hunter Hands]]; they are treated as a major threat by [[crawlers]].
• The opposing, larger group present at the Masquerade; the [[Hunters]] cluster near the buffet, avoid going backstage, and hold a tense separation from many [[crawlers]] during the event.
• An opposing human/outsider faction in the castle; includes named hunters and groups led by [[Vrah]]; they are trying to break the seal and interact with both the [[crawlers]] and mantises.
