# the_syndicate

• The [[Syndicate]] is a powerful organization and governing sponsor group.
• It oversees plays and politics, controls emergency deployment bunkers, and is central to [[Syndicate]] policy and the Ascendency game.
• [[Prime Minister Victory]] is a [[Syndicate]] official.
