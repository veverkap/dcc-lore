# posse

• The group of [[crawlers]] who speculate about and critique monster types and floor design, referenced by Donut as a community that discusses desired fights.
• A viewer/fan group or faction that has a stake in [[dungeon crawler carl]]'s actions, with a promise about how [[Quan]] would be fought and referenced in banter.
• The group holding the castle and defensive positions, with territory controlled by the [[Posse]] actively defended against [[King Rust]] and his forces.
• A viewer/community group referenced by [[Donut]] who had a stake in how Carl fought [[Quan]], with a newsletter and wagers mentioned.
• An informal faction/group referenced by [[dungeon crawler carl]] as the larger team that evaluates potential recruits like [[raul the crab]], preferring active combatants over meditators.
