# hobgoblin_interdiction_team

• The Hobgoblin Interdiction Team is a specialist cleanup/repair team that operates rapid-response carts on the [[the tangle]], including portal-equipped debris-scoop carts that require guarded operations.
• They are responsible for clearing damaged track, operating rapid-response carts, and repairing infrastructure, and their vehicles are integrated with portal scoops and require escort by gnolls per corporate policy.
• The team operates alongside gnoll security and specialized interdiction vehicles like the [[Interdiction Rapid-Response Railway Repair Cart]] to clear and repair the Iron Tangle lines.
• They possess specialized tools like the debris scoop and operate the rapid-response carts, often escorted by gnoll security, to clear wrecks on the [[the tangle]].
