# 201st_security_group

• A referenced security organization whose headquarters are considered visiting as part of an investigation.
• A militarized group tied to the city [[elves]] and the Apito Education Center, providing Magical Ops personnel and recruiting Wand Bearers.
• A militia/organization operating in the town, using the Apito Education Center as outreach and recruitment, with members including indoctrinated city [[elves]] who worship Apito and the [[Skyfowl]].
• A patch-bearing gang/militia of City [[Elves]], self-styled as a security/military unit devoted to protecting the [[Skyfowl]].
• Implicated in coordinating undead and cult activities.
• Linked to local incidents and attitudes.
• Uses the Apito Education Center as outreach and recruitment.
• Has members who serve as a support network for local undead activity.
