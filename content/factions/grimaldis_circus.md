# grimaldis_circus

• Circus troupe and organization run by [[Grimaldi]] that was devastated and transformed by [[Scolopendra]]’s poison cloud; currently populated by infected performers, lemurs, and sacrifices under the vine’s control.
• A circus troupe and local faction/attraction in the Over City associated with the [[Mold Lions]] and acts like "Madam Kiki’s Dancing Lions."
