# skull_clan

Here is the consolidated markdown list:

* The Skull Clan is a faction with a strong performance in Faction Wars and claims godly/royal lineage.
* The Skull Empire is a larger antagonist power referenced by Dungeon Crawler Carl as part of the broader conflict and context surrounding the [[crawlers]]' efforts.
* The Skull Clan is also referred to as the interstellar royal polity tied to King Rust and Prince Maestro, central to the off-screen controversy discussed on the show.
* The Skull Empire is a major surface power mentioned in the political fallout, tied to the orc royal family and a likely antagonistic force in surface geopolitics.
* The Skull Clan is a faction/nation involved in prior events on the campaign, tied to political figures like King Rust, Prince Stalwart, Prince Maestro, and Princess Formidable of the Skull Empire.
* The Skull Empire is a powerful faction/corporate empire mentioned as likely to purchase notable prisoners or franchisees.
* The Skull Clan is a hostile faction accused in-[[council]] of tampering or destructive acts.
* The Skull Empire is a faction mentioned as having an allegiance with the war mages and implicated in illegal breaking of allegiances.
* The Skull Empire is a power that has seized assets and authority in the absence of the royal family, responsible for sudden rent hikes and wider political turmoil.
* The Skull Empire is a faction mentioned as having spent money on a deity sponsorship and implicated in potential targetings and sponsorship-driven strategies on upcoming floors.
* The Skull Empire is the sponsoring faction associated with Prince Maestro and appears in the broadcast/announcements as the political/cosmic affiliation behind the sponsored Grull summoning.
* The Skull Empire is a royal polity referenced when discussing sponsorships and the lineage of Princess Formidable of the Skull Empire, Prince Maestro, Crown Prince Stalwart, and King Rust.
* The Skull Empire is a powerful political faction referenced as the sponsor network behind Maggie My (via Prince Maestro and Crown Prince Stalwart) and as the likely source of the Celestial Grenade used in the assassination attempt.
* The Skull Empire is a major faction/nation in the setting, one of the plaintiffs in the [[Syndicate]] lawsuit against Dungeon Crawler Carl's group.
* The Skull Empire is an enemy faction and [[target]] of Dungeon Crawler Carl's planned feral-god attack on the ninth floor.
* The Skull Empire is a hostile faction referenced briefly in discussion of censorship and exploit manipulation.
* The Skull Empire is an orc-aligned faction noted on the map, claimed territories and specific [[crawlers]] (including Katia).
* The Skull Empire is the off-world faction responsible for the trio of orc [[hunters]] attacked near the encampment.
* The Skull Empire is a powerful faction/corporate empire cited as an entity capable of buying out high-profile criminals' warrants and acquiring subsidiaries.
* The Skull Empire is a hostile faction referenced as possibly collaborating with the Caprid Liaison to [[target]] Dungeon Crawler Carl and his group.
* The Skull Empire is a hostile faction implicated in prior plots against Dungeon Crawler Carl and allied with Harbinger during an attempt involving Maggie My.
* The Skull Empire is a sponsor faction/empire tied to Princess Formidable of the Skull Empire and the Maestro, a seat of power among the sponsored gods referenced in sponsor politics.
* The Skull Empire is an imperial faction described in Priestly's diary as brutally sadistic, contextualizing earlier battlefield atrocities and the fall of cities like [[Larracos]].
* The Skull Clan is a hostile faction occupying the only mountainous region with a castle, described as a major threat with strong war-mage forces on the battlefield.
