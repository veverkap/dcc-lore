# blood_hunter

• An in-dungeon television program and branded hunter franchise that features [[Viscount Fog]], with events tying into the vampirism plot on the floor.
• A televised in-dungeon program/event that features [[hunters]] and staged hunts, with [[Viscount Fog]] as a star and the vampirism escalation intended as its climax.
• The [[Sultanate]]-owned studio that fields dramas, including the show "Blood Hunter", representing the [[Sultanate]]'s entertainment assets.
• An in-universe program referenced by [[Prepotente]], presented as an organized entity with producers and stakes in the festivities.
