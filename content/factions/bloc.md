# bloc

• A coalition of warlords and factions fighting in the Faction Wars, with internal fractures and leadership disputes.
• A major opposing force mentioned in the context of a truce that influences whether Shanty Town can be razed after a victory.
• A larger alliance/faction allied to both [[Madness]] and [[Reavers]] in the current battlefield order of battle.
• A major alliance grouping whose membership shifted in the system messages, previously including factions like the Bone Clan and [[Dream]].
• A collective name for the eight factions that formed a formal truce, including Bone Clan, Operatic Collective, [[Sultanate]], [[Prism]], [[Dream]], Lemig Sortion, [[Madness]], and [[Reavers]], which declared war on Team Retribution and attacked teams like the Princess [[Posse]].
