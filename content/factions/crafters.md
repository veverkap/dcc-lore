# crafters

• A named hunter group shown on the [[Hunting Grounds Map]], hunting specific [[crawlers]] such as [[elle mcgib]] and [[Eva]].
• A faction of crafters in the crawl, including [[Hunter Iota]], portrayed as subterranean dwellers and outsiders to major faction wars, often described as rowdy and hard-drinking.
