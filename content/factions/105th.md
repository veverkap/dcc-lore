# 105th

• The 105th is a military formation that engaged the enemy, suffered heavy losses, and later fell back, mentioned in battlefield reports heard in the [[Guildhall]].
• The 105th is a military unit that flanked positions, held open retreat corridors, and was referenced by [[Florin]] with members like [[Katia]] and [[daniel bautista]].
• The 105th partnered with the [[103rd]] in the northern offensive commanded by [[Florin]] to distract naga forces.
• The 105th Scream Warriors are an allied fighting faction whose contingent performs the chapter's Mass Teleportation arrival and are being rallied and organized by leaders like [[Jurgen]] and [[Heidi]].
• The 105th Scream Warriors are a rapid-deploy battalion used for teleportation-based reinforcements, depicted on Bonnie's shirt, and referenced as a key reserve force by [[Rosetta]] and [[Jurgen]].
• The 105th Scream Warriors are a battalion led in part by [[Jurgen]] and coordinated with by Carl to reinforce his assault force.
• The 105th Scream Warriors are backing up the [[101st]] at the northern defense line.
