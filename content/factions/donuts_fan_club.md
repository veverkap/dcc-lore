# donuts_fan_club

• A group of supporters traveling with [[Donut]] and the main [[crawlers]], part of the entourage accompanying the small [[squad]].
• A small supporter group traveling with [[Donut]] and present among the group's companions in the city.
• Alternative fan group name for [[Donut]]'s followers that [[Donut]] disapproves of, presented as a competing factional label among fans.
• An unauthorized fan club/merchandising group exploiting [[Donut]]'s likeness, operating like a sponsor/fan faction within the game's economy.
• An alternate fan-group name for Donut's followers mentioned in the episode as a disliked option compared to the [[Princess Posse]].
• An unofficial fan group rumored to exist, explicitly disapproved of by the narrator as a rival to the [[Princess Posse]] fan club.
