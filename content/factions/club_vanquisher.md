# club_vanquisher

* A social establishment/club located at the ochre station platform, where [[gore-gore the mantaur]] enters and is later seen bursting back out.
* A high-status club whose temples are tied to deity worship (temples of [[Emberus]] appear as entrances to this club), with membership/access being a noted benefit for Emberus adherents.
* A club/organization that hires services, such as a healer, and is used to recruit [[NPCs]] for short-term aid.
* A named group referenced by Carl as a source of information he hopes to extract, appearing to be an organized faction or club within the world.
* A faction/[[guild]] presence in the [[Large [[Ursine]] Settlement]], visible as a club entrance on the town streets and part of the local organizational makeup.
* A sponsor/club referenced as the familiar spire/temple in the center of the corrupted area, a recognizable sponsor-brand presence in the dungeon architecture.
* A faction that has banned [[Prepotente]], referenced as part of the social-structure context for players.
* A recurring entrance/organization listed alongside [[desperado club]] in the system announcement about the next floor.
* A club/organization that supplied a healer for [[dungeon crawler carl]]'s group, mentioned in contrast to the mercenaries from the [[Merc guild]].
* A named group mentioned as a source of intel that [[dungeon crawler carl]] wants information about from his temporary allies.
* A club/[[guild]] presence in the town, an entrance is visible, indicative of organized social or factional structures inside the settlement.
* A group referenced in the "Enemy of the Church Tattoo" description as a protective/affiliated entity, adherents outside of a [[Club Vanquisher]] will attack the tattoo bearer on sight.
* A named faction from which some players (like [[Prepotente]]) may be barred, mentioned as a social/competitive organization on the [[Ninth Floor]].
* A club/store referenced as the place where [[dungeon crawler carl]] obtained a ring with help from [[Pater Coal]], implied to be an in-world organization/sponsor/location.
* A named group or club evidenced by the "Club Vanquisher entrance" to a temple near [[Imani]]'s position, an organized factional presence in the area.
