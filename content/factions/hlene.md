# hlene

• An extinct race referenced in the floor's lore, historically set traps and waystations near the galaxy center, and were later eliminated by the [[Valtay]].
• The [[H’lene]] set up six waystations near the galaxy center as traps, tied to the mythic history of the floor and the station mimics motif.
