# patrons

• Sponsor organizations, usually groups, back [[crawlers]] with purchased loot boxes, sending valuable boxes, including rare Benefactor Box types, and driving the in-game economy through sponsorships.
• The patron bidding system on the floor references sponsor groups as operational participants in floor mechanics.
• Benefactor-like entities or groups watch the show from across the galaxy, dropping loot boxes and influencing gameplay through gifts or interference.
• Commercial sponsor groups provide equipment and items, and occasionally manipulative interventions, for [[crawlers]].
• The meta-organization backing teams and the game is displeased by NPC unrest and motivates characters to restrain Juice Box's actions.
• An anonymous benefactor paid entry fees to attract a record number of [[hunters]] to the season, enabling third-party tourist/hunter participation.
