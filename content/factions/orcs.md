# orcs

• A faction referenced through characters, including [[Baroness Victory]] and her sister [[Ugloo]], listed among notable warlords who prefer front-line leadership.
• Hostile faction assigned to be handled by [[Florin]]; their defenses can intercept some incoming missiles during the aerial run.
• A broad race/faction cited in the historical explanation of the map and the eradication of the H’lene in the [[Syndicate]]'s lore.
