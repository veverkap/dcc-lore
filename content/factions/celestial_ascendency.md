# celestial_ascendency

• The Celestial Ascendency is a divine polity and ruling structure, referenced as the lineage and parentage of Grull.
• The pantheon/organization is linked to godly politics, leading to Psamathe's banishment and strange divine happenings.
• The faction oversees sponsor-related activities and is involved in the broader Ascendency battles, beginning when [[crawlers]] reach the Twelfth Floor.
• The organization is also an outside-the-dungeon sponsor or network used for secured messaging, implicated in the relay of forbidden real-time information.
• The party references its members' bizarre mythological biology and activities as context for Psamathe's place in the divine social order.
• The higher-order governing/afterlife structure and political arena is where deities return and contend for the Ascendent throne, with destinations Ysalte and Amayon.
• The faction is also a background context for sponsorship and the Ascendent Throne, mentioned in Eris's description.
• The Celestial Ascendency is the Ascendency sponsor/organization tied to demigod attendants and sponsor communications, with Armita working as a demigod attendant and using legal/privacy rules to arrange assistance from Huanxin Jinx.
• The organization is an organized sponsor body for demigod attendants, with Armita working for them as a demigod attendant and using legal protections/ loopholes surrounding Ascendency sponsor communications.
