# npcs

• A team in the faction wars led by [[Juice Box]] after the system designated her a warlord to stop her killing spree.
• A generic non-player-character combat group referenced by [[Britney]] and represented on-field by adjutants like [[Drick]]; called in for support during the assault.
• A faction/team in the ongoing faction conflicts; led by the psychotic changeling [[Juice Box]] before system intervention.
• A proposed new team composed of unaffiliated NPCs (advocated by [[Juice Box]] and [[dungeon crawler carl]]) intended to grant NPCs voting rights, regeneration, and protections equivalent to the eight canonical teams.
