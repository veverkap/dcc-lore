# war_mages

* The War Mages control the [[Gate]] and are led by a warlord who is a residual, with unknown motives and a desire to end the crawl.
* They are a faction responsible for magical resources and defenses, and are the source/holders of war-mage supplies, including conscription potions.
* They previously controlled the pilot of [[Eris]] and traded the [[Gate of the Feral Gods]].
* The War Mage rebellion has been defeated, and the group has no assets to award.
* The opposing team holding the castle in the ongoing conflict is the War Mage team, which will win Faction control if not interrupted within hours.
* The War Mages are a newly referenced, apparently independent and "non-team" group of war mages who killed [[Stalwart]] and teleported away.
* The Warlord leadership of the War Mages has changed, with [[Warlord Agatha]] removed and [[Akuma]] appointed.
* The War Mages control synergy towers and war mages acting in the battlefield.
* The faction appeared late in [[Faction Wars]], allied awkwardly with [[Agatha]], and ultimately killed an orc boss and stole the [[Gate of the Feral Gods]].
* The War Mages are responsible for generated mobs (zarogoths), conscription attempts, and occupying the contested castle.
* The group is potentially manipulative and dangerous, and is represented by at least one war mage NPC who offers to let players clear the Pineapple Cabaret in exchange for help.
* The War Mages requested help clearing the [[Pineapple Cabaret]] and have information about [[Herot]] central to their plan.
* The group has knowledge or interest in the sand-ooze offspring tied to [[Samantha]] and is presented as an organized faction with informational claims about powerful entities.
