# tutorial_guild

• A [[guild]] and safe-zone tutorial hub, "Da Tutorial [[Guild]]", indicated by neon, that [[dungeon crawler carl]] seeks on [[Dungeon Floor 1]].
• Operated by [[NPCs]] like [[Mordecai]] and governed by dungeon rules for admitting [[Crawlers]].
• Instructional [[guild]] groups recommended by the [[Syndicate Announcement]] and seeded on early [[World Dungeon]] levels (1–3) to assist new [[Crawlers]].
• [[Tutorial guild]] is a [[guild]]/location marked by a neon sign near the main corridor that the party decides to enter; presented as a tutorial-styled [[guild]] presence on the first floor.
