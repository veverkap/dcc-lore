# dromedarians

• NPC camel-people who defend [[Hump Town]] with anti-air guns and repair work after the assault; include changeling principals among their ranks in other contexts.
• A camel-like people who house the [[changelings]], run [[Hump Town]]'s defenses, and maintain the pocket-watch code with the [[gnomes]].
• Camel-like inhabitants of [[Hump Town]] who host and care for survivors; treated [[Bonnie]] kindly and are involved in community support around the house.
