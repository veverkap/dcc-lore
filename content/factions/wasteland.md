# Wasteland
Massive dreadnaught war machine that serves as a mobile stronghold/air fleet for the gnome forces in this chapter. Hosts multiple aircraft/balloons and is treated as both vessel and authority (e.g., [[Leon]] is Commisar of the [[Wasteland]]).
