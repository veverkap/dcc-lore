# scolopendra

• Referenced historic threat ("Scolopendra attack") in backstory that shaped the region's politics and decimated many fishy/naïad peoples; treated as a past hostile force.
• Group that the [[orcs]] transferred to after the Faction Wars; mentioned in the context of political realignments and past atrocities.
• [[Scolopendra]] is mentioned in passing as part of the corporate/backstory milieu.
• [[Otto]] is registered as a guest at [[Club Scolopendra]].
