# lady_dominators

* A competing team mentioned as a possible shield-upgraded opponent whose upgrade could affect the success of [[Donut]]'s Danforth bolt and [[Chiyome]]'s physical bear attack.
* A sponsor/team whose vehicle is a deep-burgundy late-60s Pontiac GTO convertible, projecting a visible shield and hosting [[Corcunda]] in its back seat.
* A rival crawler team whose garage contains [[Corky (Corcunda)]]; [[Dong]] and others attempt to gain access to their numbered garage.
* A team/faction whose members include several strippers such as [[Dong Quixote]], and from whose ranks reunification of [[Corcunda]] would remove the [[mantaur]] from racing obligations.
* A competing team that [[Carl]] assumes is temporarily allied or cooperative with his own team for the purposes of this race.
* A competing team in the vehicle race, mentioned as having a pursuing car behind the truck crew.
* A rival team at the starting line; notable for consistent appearances and a passed-out Corky in their vehicle.
* A competing team that races close to Carl's group; their vehicle is driven aggressively and includes the rider [[Genesis]].
* A competitor team placed ahead of the narrator's team in the pit-stop lineup, used to determine relative race standing.
* A rival team referenced by [[Carl]] and seen elsewhere on the course, apparently located ahead but also in the basement area during the race.
* A rival faction/team encountered on the track that engages other teams near the loop and is mentioned in Florin's status updates.
* A competing racing team that appears passing the garage and finishes second in Heat Four (listed alongside "the Gimp" in results).
* A team/faction whose female mantaurs use domination/submission magic in races, implicated in the danger threatening [[Corky]].
* A rival team/faction participating in the race, whose car includes the upgraded GPS unit [[Janice]] and they are directly adjacent to [[Carl]]'s team on the starting grid.
* A rival team of racers in the event, angling their vehicle toward the conveyor-belt doorway and using an aggressive driving line across the factory floor. Their vehicle features golden wheels and is targeted during combat.
* A competing faction that was eliminated in the Heat Five results; [[Lady Dominators]] are listed among the race participants.
