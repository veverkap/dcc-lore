# daughters

* A small, primarily female player group led by [[Hekla]] that [[Katia]] once traveled with; holds cultural and recruitment influence within the narrative.
* All-female party/faction led by [[Hekla]]; has recruitment rules and internal trust dynamics referenced by [[Katia]] and [[dungeon crawler carl]].
* Female-led adventuring group in the dungeon (led by [[Hekla]]); they have rules about party composition and guarded secrets; involved in early-floor conflicts and the distribution of player-killer rewards.
* [[Daughters]] share a backstory of being on a train that filled with monsters.
* All-female adventuring/faction group led by [[Hekla]]. Has its own recruitment standards and internal politics; Katia and Eva have ties to this group.
* Crawler team led by [[Hekla]]. Mentioned as an active party in the dungeon crawl; one of its members is reportedly near [[dungeon crawler carl]] and seeking help leveling.
* The collective/faction associated with [[Hekla]], composed of many "daughters" including [[Eva Sigrid]]. They operate as a tight-knit crew used for rescue and combat operations, and they traveled via employee portals and substation routes to reach the Vermillion Line.
* A crawler faction/party that was built around [[Hekla]] and her crossbow; described as having many healers and mages but lacking tanks and dedicated damage dealers. The group disbanded after Hekla's death.
* A healer/mage party led by [[Hekla]] with a core of fairies and mages; the faction collapses after Hekla's death and loses cohesion, equipment, and personal space.
* A named group/faction that was transported previously by the [[Nightmare]] and referenced as part of earlier logistics on the line.
* A named gang/faction referenced as a potential destination if [[Donut]] were to leave [[dungeon crawler carl]]; linked to Odette's contract concerns.
* A all-female crawler party led by [[Hekla]] whose membership collapses after her death. Members include healers and mages; the faction loses cohesion and shared sponsors after the leader's fall.
* Faction and fighting crew led or associated with [[Hekla]]; a large group of diverse [[crawlers]] who rescued [[Katia]] and now plan an operation to rescue crowds trapped at [[Station 101]] on the [[Vermillion line]].
