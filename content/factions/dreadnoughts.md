# dreadnoughts

• Large red-skinned humanoid faction/species whose army was wiped out by [[dungeon crawler carl]]'s prior actions.
• Sponsor faction that dropped out of Faction Wars, losing their slot and funds.
• Potential force or group [[dungeon crawler carl]] considers recruiting or using in his plan, presented as heavy hitters, implying military or mercenary status.
• Faction that also withdrew from [[Faction Wars]], causing financial losses for sponsors.
• A faction/clan involved in faction wars, members include Dreadnought individuals like [[Haxor the Destroyer]], pulled out of the faction war after a faction leader's spouse was injured.
