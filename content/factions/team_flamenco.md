# team_flamenco

• A faction/[[guild]] that attempted to recruit [[Sister Ines]]'s team but was declined, representing typical [[guild]] recruitment pressure in early floors.
• A nearby team/[[guild]] that wants [[Sister Ines]]'s group to join them, but [[Sister Ines]] declines to remain independent.
• A group of [[crawlers]] operating together in the woods, reported as harboring [[Osvaldo]] and others.
• A crawler party mentioned during the pet show, including the crawler who owns [[Gimli]] the stone hawk.
• A rising, min-maxing team referenced by Odette as a noteworthy group from previous floors.
