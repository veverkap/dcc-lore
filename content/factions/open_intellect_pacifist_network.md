# open_intellect_pacifist_network

Here is the consolidated list of unique factual points:

• The Open Intellect Pacifist Network is a sponsor group that provides items to [[crawlers]] and participates in the gifting network around Donut.
• They own the documentary-style program booking [[crawlers]] and require a sponsor liaison to attend the production.
• The faction enforces rules that require a liaison to be present when a crawler is alone with a sponsor.
• They fund cultural programming such as Shadow Boxer.
• The Open Intellect Pacifist Network is a named group whose members' disappearance is puzzling and provides motivation for concerns about broader disappearances.
• They are a sponsor group backing Dungeon Crawler Carl during the crawl.
• They are a named ideological/political group listed among parties concerned with residuals and the System AI.
• They are a financing/pacifist collective that funds evacuation ships and the plan to reach Impetus Point.
• They are a named organization mentioned by Dungeon Crawler Carl as a possible affiliation for the robed sponsor who intervened at the panel.
• They gifted Dungeon Crawler Carl the potion Pawna's Cries to be passed on to Donut.
• They are the sponsor/owner of the Shadow Boxer program, requiring a liaison to be present during production.
• They are a sponsor group that sent an Emergency Gold Benefactor Box to Dungeon Crawler Carl but had the delivery seized by liaison action.
• They are a benefactor/sponsor organization that sent a Gold Benefactor Box to Dungeon Crawler Carl.
• They are a sponsor group that sent the emergency benefactor box and commissioned the Enchanted Xistera Extension Slot used to toss the Samantha head.
• They are a sponsor group that arranged the protagonist's presence in the scene, enabling them to receive the neural enhancement and message from Paulie.
• They are a named group mentioned in connection with Rosetta, implied to be involved in efforts that intersect with why Dungeon Crawler Carl and others were directed to the Miami area.
