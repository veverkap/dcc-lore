# temple_of_emberus

• A church/faith organization that issues faith-backed loans and marks items, imposing worship and debt-related restrictions on borrowers and enchanted gear.
• Provides interest-free loans bound by worship conditions and imposes debt-related restrictions and curses on enchanted items.
• Associated with lore about members having difficulty separating dungeon story from reality after AI interference.
• References the high cleric [[Pater Coal]] at [[Club Vanquisher]].
• Grants ranks (e.g., "Devotee"), buffs, spells (e.g., [[Self-Immolation]]), and enforces tithe mechanics.
• References the [[Baptism by Fire]] message, granting ranks and buffs to [[dungeon crawler carl]].
