# [[Council of Scholars]]
A scholarly authority that authored the [[Letter from the Council]] and maintains artifacts and tomes (e.g., the Tome of Scholars). Advises on banished deities such as [[Psamathe]] and prescribes use of the [[winding box]] as remediation.
