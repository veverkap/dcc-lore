# council_of_scholars

• A scholarly authority that authored the [[Letter from the Council]] and maintains artifacts and tomes (e.g., the Tome of Scholars).
• Advises on banished deities such as [[Psamathe]].
• Prescribes use of the [[winding box]] as remediation.
