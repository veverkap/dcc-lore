# penis_parade

• The [[Penis Parade]] is a strip club troupe and Meat Shields-affiliated crew that offers mercenary contracts and has members hired into [[dungeon crawler carl]]'s faction.
• The [[Penis Parade]] is a named strip-club establishment/sponsor group that served as the former workplace of [[Bucket Boy]] and provides background factional context in the dungeon.
• The [[Penis Parade]] is a named allied group that draws members like [[Gluteus Maxx]] and [[Doctor Bones]] and is present among the teleporting soldiers.
• The [[Penis Paraders]] are an informal group of [[crawlers]] described by [[Donut]] as a ragtag mob of strong fighters used for bashing and crowd control.
• The [[Penis Parade]] is a racing/team faction that [[Corky]] had left to go searching and is referenced as part of the competitive landscape and social ties.
