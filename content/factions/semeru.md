# semeru

• Group or faction controlling the submerged area of [[Larracos]] and enforcing access restrictions around the submerged castle and feeding pits.
• Group placed on standby and awaiting the airship's bomb run, referenced by [[Britney]] asking for confirmation.
• Cultural/factional identifier for the parade-armored [[dwarves]] defending the front gate of the [[Larracos College of Magic]]; they press the attack against the [[Zarogoth]] swarm.
• Group/race gathering to repair a statue from the front of the [[Larracos]] castle; portrayed as organized workers among the survivors.
• Controlling group that enforces access restrictions in submerged areas of [[Larracos]], keeping certain zones and the castle off-limits and overseeing feeding of predators.
