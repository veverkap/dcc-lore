# cichociemni

• A competing team/[[guild]] that clashed with the Popov brothers.
• A sponsor/mercenary group named after a Polish special forces reference, described as brutal and effective, and allied with [[Eva]].
• A named team referenced among [[Eva]]'s former allies and [[daughters]], with some members abandoning [[Eva]] during the creeper fights.
• A faction/group containing former members encountered in the town aftermath, with some of [[Eva]]'s team being former [[daughters]] and members of this faction.
