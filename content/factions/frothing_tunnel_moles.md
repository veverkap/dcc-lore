# frothing_tunnel_moles

• A crawler faction/unit that may be a destination for slug sappers reassigned to surface operations.
• A Special Operations Forces (SOF) group of gnome-sized tunnelers used by defenders to place and detonate underground charges and perform sabotage behind enemy lines.
