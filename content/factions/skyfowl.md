# Skyfowl
The avian ruling species of the Over City settlements in this region. Holds governmental control in the village (e.g., [[Magistrate FeatherFall]]), employs non-avian staff in shops, and treats non-Skyfowl victims with lower priority, per [[Miss Quill]]'s description.
