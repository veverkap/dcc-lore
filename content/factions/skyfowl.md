# skyfowl

• The Skyfowl are an eagle-folk people who inhabit the upper levels of the town and run many shops, with a somewhat xenophobic attitude toward non-winged peoples.
• They are the avian ruling species of the Over City settlements in this region, holding governmental control in the village.
• They employ non-avian staff in shops and treat non-Skyfowl victims with lower priority.
