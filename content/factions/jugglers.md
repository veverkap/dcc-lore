# jugglers

• Racing team associated with a large black dog mount that carries masked riders, positioned near [[one fine pig]] at the start.
• Placed second in "Heat Two" according to the match results announced by the system.
• Eliminated in Heat Three (removed when all team members die during the heat).
• Vehicle includes a large black dog with masked riders seated on its back.
• Competing team in the lineup, referenced as part of the starting order for the heat.
• Faction referenced via a garage ("the garage of the Jugglers"), implying a group with vehicles and a garage presence on the floor.
• Mount was [[Old Shuck]], a large black wolf-dog.
