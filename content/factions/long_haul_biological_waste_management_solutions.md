# long_haul_biological_waste_management_solutions

• The new sponsor assigned to [[Donut]] unexpectedly, with branding concerns for [[Donut]]'s image.
• A [[Crest]]-affiliated corporate waste-[[management]] freighter operator, with one of their largest freighters, the [[Homecoming Queen]], stranded in-orbit and repurposed to smuggle troopers and personnel.
• A sponsor company and [[crest]]-owned firm whose logo appears on a benefactor ship, providing the Celestial benefactor that yielded the [[Tiara of a Thousand Lights]] and [[Accompanying Charge Pillow]].
• A sponsor group/corporate sponsor that purchased [[Donut]]'s sponsorship slot from [[Empress D’Nadia]], referenced humorously as an ill-fitting sponsor name by [[Donut]].
• A corporate hauling concern whose freighter [[Homecoming Queen]] is stranded in orbit, normally arriving after crawls conclude to handle biological remains.
• A [[crest]]-owned sponsor company that awarded Donut a Celestial benefactor box containing the [[Tiara of a Thousand Lights]], with their logo and involvement a notable example of sponsor influence and lore.
• A corporate [[crest]] whose freighter, the [[Homecoming Queen]], is caught in-system and later used to transport troops and covert personnel to the dungeon on behalf of the [[Princess Posse]].
