# monkeywrenchers

• A covert subset of the [[Madness]] faction known for going invisible and infiltrating enemy positions.
• Credited with past rapid assaults that seized the [[Dreadnaught Castle]] and [[Naga castle]].
• A group of invisible tech-based mercenaries attacking the base using high-end cloaking gear and grenades.
• Some wear Viceroy masks and Dreadnaught armor.
• They use tech invisibility that bypasses anti-magic charms.
