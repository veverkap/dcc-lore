# gnomes

* Aerial/dirigible-capable faction that enforces collateral proof on towns and has bombarded towns that fail to comply; responsible for floating gnome devices and collateral demands.
* Operating dirigibles and bombing towns from above; responsible for inventions like the Enchanted Gnome FarSeerer and the Gnomish Knock- Knock.
* Specializing in aerial bombardment using thermobaric devices; their tactics shaped regional wartime history and local fear.
* Associated with air forces and bombing runs; credited with inventions like the Enchanted Gnome FarSeerer and organized into units like the gnomes.
* Named group/race associated with early fast-attack biplanes ("Gnomish Drop Bear" contraptions) and drop attacks with deployment devices.
* Responsible for air superiority, dirigible craft, and technologies such as Live Ammo Balls, bandoliers, and anti-air batteries.
* Many fallen corpses are labeled "Dirigible Gnome."
