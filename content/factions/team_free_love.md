# team_free_love

* The racing team of Radoslav, Jasha, and the deceased Alevtina, driving a crashed, burning minivan with a tie-dye and "hippie"-styled aesthetic.
* Finished eighth in Heat One, third in Heat Two, and fourth in Heat Three.
* Trailing the truck in Heat Four, with their van bouncing along behind the Lady [[Dominators]]' vehicle.
* Finished fourth in Heat Five and second in Heat Six.
* Their van is used by the racers, floats on a cushion of air during the apartment drop, and is involved in the race's vehicle logistics.
* The team is composed of [[bugbears]] that quickly take the lead early in the heat and choose the safe, long path at the split.
* The team is a sponsor/ally team present in the race, with members such as Jasha and Radoslav.
* The team's van is visible on the ceiling tracks, using a rooftop device to travel along the inverted track and weave through hazards.
* The team is eliminated in Heat Six, with their wrecked van mentioned just before the finish line.
