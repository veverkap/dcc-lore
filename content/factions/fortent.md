# fortent

• Named faction/company referenced in an anecdote by [[Orren the Liaison]] about admins mishandling crawler threats.
• Represents a production/administrative sponsor in dungeon governance.
• Member government represented on the [[Syndicate]] [[Council]], arguing against immediate use of the [[Failsafe]] and calling out possible self-interest of other members.
• Represented at the emergency meeting, disputing immediate use of the [[Failsafe]] and suggesting political motives behind the proposal from the [[Skull Empire]].
